<p>A. 20% maximum slope on driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>B. 10% maximum cross slope of a driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>C. 5% maximum slope in any direction in a parking stall.</p>
<p>D. Transition slopes are required when the slope of the driveway or ramp exceeds 10%.</p>
<p>There shall be a maximum change in gradient of 10% in any 10 foot section of the driveway including the driveway approach within the street right of way, except that on a driveway sloping up from the property line the gradient within the first 5 feet from the property line shall not exceed 10%.</p>
<p>Parking stall location</p>
<p>A. No parking stall is to be located so that an automobile is required to back onto a major or secondary highway.</p>
<p>B. No automobile is allowed to back from any lot onto a street unless the parking area or garage and the driveway leading to this parking serves no more than two dwelling units.</p></details></div>

### Page 3 - General Off-Street Parking Standards

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>BT-402</strong> &middot; Topics: parking-curb</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0003.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0003.webp" alt="Burbank Std. Plans page 3 BT-402 General Parking Standards"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans, page 3 (scanned, cleaned and sharpened). Click to enlarge.</figcaption></figure>
<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>General Off-Street Parking Standards.</b> Sets general provisions and minimum parking space widths by land use under B.M.C. 31-1041. Minimum widths are 8&#x27;-6&quot; for residential, industrial and office, and 9&#x27;-0&quot; for retail, banks and medical offices. Also states vertical clearances (7&#x27; residential, 8&#x27;-2&quot; commercial) and allows up to 45 percent small car spaces at least 7&#x27;-6&quot; wide for industrial and office uses.</p><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>GENERAL OFF-STREET PARKING STANDARDS</h5>
<p>General provisions</p>
<p>Parking space dimensions, B.M.C. 31-1041. The following minimum parking space widths shall be provided:</p>
<p>Uses, minimum width</p>
<p>(1) Residential: 8&#x27;-6&quot;</p>
<p>(2) Retail and services commercial: 9&#x27;-0&quot;</p>
<p>(3) Banks and savings and loan institutions: 9&#x27;-0&quot;</p>
<p>(4) Medical offices: 9&#x27;-0&quot;</p>
<p>(5) Industrial: 8&#x27;-6&quot;</p>
<p>(6) Offices: 8&#x27;-6&quot;</p>
<p>The minimum width of parking spaces adjacent to walls, columns or other vertical obstructions shall be determined by standards established by the Public Works Director.</p>
<p>Minimum parking space widths for uses not mentioned shall be determined by the Public Works Director, and every residential parking space shall maintain a vertical height clearance of seven (7) feet and every commercial parking space shall maintain a vertical height clearance of eight (8) feet two (2) inches.</p>
<p>Where banks, savings and loan institutions, and retail and service commercial uses are part of an office building or retail complex, the Public Works Director shall ascertain the number and location of those parking spaces which will be required to have a minimum width of nine (9) feet.</p>
<p>For industrial and office uses (but not including medical offices, banks and savings and loan institutions, and commercial retail uses) forty-five (45) percent of all parking spaces provided may be small car spaces if the spaces are clearly marked for small cars and the plan is approved by the Public Works Director. Parking spaces for small cars shall be at least seven and one-half (7-1/2) feet in width.</p>
<p>The Drachman Parking System, whereby parking layout design discourages the use of small car spaces by large vehicles, may be used where possible.</p>
<p>Minor deviations from all foregoing standards may be authorized by the Public Works Director to accommodate the safe ingress and egress of vehicles.</p>
<p>The following tables (Nos. 1 thru 5) shall be used when determining overall parking space dimensions and aisle width.</p></details></div>

<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>General Traffic Control Requirements.</b> Text-only requirements from the Burbank Traffic Division for contractors working in public streets. They cover traffic control per California MUTCD or WATCH, traffic control plan submittals, lane closure hours, bus stop and parking notices, access to adjacent properties, and notification of the Transportation Management Center. Travel lanes must be open 6:00 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., with one lane each way open at all times between 8:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. The sheet is Revision 8, dated 03/19/2019.</p><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>City of Burbank, Department of Public Works, Traffic Division</p>
<p>General Traffic Requirements</p>
<p>1. It is the responsibility of the Contractor performing work on a public street to install and maintain the traffic control devices according to the latest edition of the California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (California MUTCD) or Work Area Traffic Control Handbook (WATCH) to insure the safe movement of traffic and pedestrians through or around the work area and provide protection and safety to construction workers.</p>
<p>2. The Contractor must submit a traffic control plan for approval when requested by the Traffic Division or when traffic handling cannot use a typical application in the latest California MUTCD or WATCH. Any revisions to the traffic control plans or requirements shall be approved by the Assistant Public Works Director-Traffic.</p>
<p>3. All traffic control devices (signs, delineators, barricades, type K temporary railing, portable changeable message signs, etc.) shall conform to the Caltrans Standard Specifications, Section 12. All devices must be in place and shall be repaired, replaced, or cleaned as necessary until work is complete and traffic control is not required.</p>
<p>4. Flashing arrow boards are mandatory for lane closures on any multi-lane street. If any barricades are to be in place at night, they shall be equipped with flashing/steady yellow warning lamps.</p>
<p>5. On working days, all travel lanes shall be open between 6:00 a.m. and 8:30 a.m. and between 3:30 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. One travel lane in each direction shall be open at all times between 8:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. Any deviation of lane closure hours shall be approved by the Assistant Public Works Director-Traffic.</p>
<p>6. If a bus stop is impacted by traffic control, the Contractor shall notify Burbank Bus or Los Angeles Metro at least 5 working days before start of work.</p>
<p>7. If parking is impacted by traffic control, the Contractor shall post &quot;TEMPORARY NO PARKING ANYTIME&quot; signs at a minimum height of 3 feet and at least 5 working days before start of work. The Contractor may obtain signs from the Public Works Permit Counter. Once posted, the Contractor shall notify the Burbank Police Department Traffic Division for approval and enforcement of posted signs.</p>
<p>8. Vehicular and pedestrian access to adjacent properties shall be provided at all times.</p>
<p>9. The contractor SHALL notify the City of Burbank Transportation Management Center 5 business days prior to installing any traffic control that reduces vehicular capacity on any signalized roadway within the City.</p>
<p>10. Any work impacting any traffic loops or cameras SHALL NOT COMMENCE without prior written consent from the City of Burbank Transportation Management Center via email.</p>
<p>11. Impacted loops or cameras SHALL BE restored within a predetermined amount of time (between the contractor and the Transportation Management Center) or the City will restore them and the contractor will be billed.</p>
<p>12. During business hours, immediately notify the Public Works Department, or after hours, immediately notify the Police Department, if any of the following events occur:</p>
<p>a. A traffic signal is dark, flashing red, or malfunctioning near your work area.</p>
<p>b. A signalized intersection has traffic congestion near your work area.</p>
<p>c. Any traffic signal equipment or loop detector is damaged without planned replacement.</p>
<p>d. Any traffic signs, striping, or markings are removed without planned replacement.</p>
<p>Approved: Jonathan Yee, Assistant Public Works Director-Traffic. Revised 03/19/2019.</p></details></div>
