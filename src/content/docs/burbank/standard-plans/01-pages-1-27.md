<p>3. For compact spaces, the minimums shall be 1&#x27; less than the above requirements.</p>
<p>4. (See sheet 10 for angles of spaces less than 75 degrees.)</p>
<h5>Circulation driveways note:</h5>
<p>Add 9&#x27; to drive for two-way traffic or more than 20 cars in &quot;C&quot; zone or 40 cars in &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone.</p></details>

### Page 2 - Allowable Standard and Compact Parking Width Dimension

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>BT-401</strong> &middot; Topics: transit, parking-curb</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0002.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0002.webp" alt="Burbank Std. Plans page 2 BT-401 Allowable Standard and Compact Parking Width Dimension"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans, page 2.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Under the charts: Where parking adjoining the access aisle is a combination of standard size and compact size automobile spaces, Chart No. 1 shall be used for determination of minimum access aisle width.</p>
<p>Special requirements.</p>
<h5>Driveway widths and locations:</h5>
<p>A. 10&#x27; minimum in all zones.</p>
<p>B. 19&#x27; minimum when serving more than 40 cars in an &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone, or more than 20 cars in a &quot;C&quot; zone, or in lieu thereof there shall be two 10&#x27; minimum access driveways. Traffic Division approval for the location and size of driveways shall be obtained for every parking area or garage.</p>
<h5>Driveway slopes and ramps (see sheet 11):</h5>
<p>A. 20% maximum slope on driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>B. 10% maximum cross slope of a driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>C. 5% maximum slope in any direction in a parking stall.</p>
<p>D. Transition slopes are required when the slope of the driveway or ramp exceeds 10%. There shall be a maximum change in gradient of 10% in any 10 foot section of the driveway including the driveway approach within the street right of way, except that on a driveway sloping up from the property line the gradient within the first 5 feet from the property line shall not exceed 10%.</p>
<h5>Parking stall location:</h5>
<p>A. No parking stall is to be located so that an automobile is required to back onto a major or secondary highway.</p>
<p>B. No automobile is allowed to back from any lot onto a street unless the parking area or garage and the driveway leading to this parking serves no more than two dwelling units.</p></details>

### Page 3 - General Off-Street Parking Standards

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>BT-402</strong> &middot; Topics: parking-curb</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0003.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0003.webp" alt="Burbank Std. Plans page 3 BT-402 General Parking Standards"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans, page 3.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>General provisions. Parking space dimensions, Burbank Municipal Code (B.M.C.) 31-1041. The following minimum parking space widths shall be provided:</p>
<p>Uses and minimum width: (1) Residential, 8&#x27;-6&quot;. (2) Retail and services commercial, 9&#x27;-0&quot;. (3) Banks and savings and loan institutions, 9&#x27;-0&quot;. (4) Medical offices, 9&#x27;-0&quot;. (5) Industrial, 8&#x27;-6&quot;. (6) Offices, 8&#x27;-6&quot;.</p>
<p>The minimum width of parking spaces adjacent to walls, columns or other vertical obstructions shall be determined by standards established by the Public Works Director.</p>
<p>Minimum parking space widths for uses not mentioned shall be determined by the Public Works Director, and every residential parking space shall maintain a vertical height clearance of seven (7) feet and every commercial parking space shall maintain a vertical height clearance of eight (8) feet two (2) inches.</p>
<p>Where banks, savings and loan institutions, and retail and service commercial uses are part of an office building or retail complex, the Public Works Director shall ascertain the number and location of those parking spaces which will be required to have a minimum width of nine (9) feet.</p>
<p>For industrial and office uses (but not including medical offices, banks and savings and loan institutions, and commercial retail uses) forty-five (45) percent of all parking spaces provided may be small car spaces if the spaces are clearly marked for small cars and the plan is approved by the Public Works Director. Parking spaces for small cars shall be at least seven and one-half (7-1/2) feet in width. The Drachman Parking System, whereby parking layout design discourages the use of small car spaces by large vehicles, may be used where possible.</p>
<p>Minor deviations from all foregoing standards may be authorized by the Public Works Director to accommodate the safe ingress and egress of vehicles.</p>
<p>The following tables (Nos. 1 thru 5) shall be used when determining overall parking space dimensions and aisle width:</p></details>

### Page 11 - Driveway Transitions

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>BT-406</strong> &middot; Topics: transit</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0011.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0011.webp" alt="Burbank Std. Plans page 11 BT-406 Driveway Transitions"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans, page 11.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Case 1: garage above street level (no scale). Garage setback minimum (MIN.) 16.5&#x27; from curb face. Top of curb (TC) level, garage floor level, 20% slope, sidewalk slope 1/4&quot; per 1&#x27;. Maximum (MAX.) rise 1.26&#x27; + 0.24&#x27; (see table).</p>
<p>Case 2: garage below street level (no scale). Garage setback MIN. 20.5&#x27; from curb face. Maximum fall 0.38&#x27; + 0.28&#x27; (see table).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. The distances and vertical curves shown hereon are the minimum which can be used with a minimum tangent slope of 20% without causing cars to drag on the paved surfaces.</p>
<p>2. The sidewalk slope of 1/4&quot; per 1&#x27; shall be carried from the curb to the beginning of the vertical curve for Case 1 and Case 2.</p>
<p>3. If the back of the existing sidewalk is more than 5&#x27; from the curb face in Case 1, or more than 11.5&#x27; from the curb face in Case 2, the vertical curve shall begin at the back of the existing sidewalk, and the minimum garage setback increased accordingly to provide desired rise or fall to garage level.</p>
<p>4. Where existing conditions make this profile inapplicable, the applicant shall file a plan and profile of the proposed driveway at a scale of 1&quot; = 2&#x27; with the Traffic Engineer for approval.</p>
<p>5. All driveways which do not conform to City Standard must be processed by special permit. Request for said permit is made with the Traffic Engineer for checking and approval.</p></details>

### Page 27 - General Traffic Requirements (Contractor Traffic Control)

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>TC-REQ</strong> &middot; Topics: transit, signals, parking-curb, striping, signs, intersections</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0027.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0027.webp" alt="Burbank Std. Plans page 27 TC-REQ General Traffic Control Requirements (Rev. 8)"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans, page 27.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. It is the responsibility of the Contractor performing work on a public street to install and maintain the traffic control devices according to the latest edition of the California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (California MUTCD) or Work Area Traffic Control Handbook (WATCH) to insure the safe movement of traffic and pedestrians through or around the work area and provide protection and safety to construction workers.</p>
<p>2. The Contractor must submit a traffic control plan for approval when requested by the Traffic Division or when traffic handling cannot use a typical application in the latest California MUTCD or WATCH. Any revisions to the traffic control plans or requirements shall be approved by the Assistant Public Works Director-Traffic.</p>
<p>3. All traffic control devices (signs, delineators, barricades, type K temporary railing, portable changeable message signs, etc.) shall conform to the Caltrans Standard Specifications, Section 12. All devices must be in place and shall be repaired, replaced, or cleaned as necessary until work is complete and traffic control is not required.</p>
<p>4. Flashing arrow boards are mandatory for lane closures on any multi-lane street. If any barricades are to be in place at night, they shall be equipped with flashing/steady yellow warning lamps.</p>
<p>5. On working days, all travel lanes shall be open between 6:00 a.m. and 8:30 a.m. and between 3.30 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. One travel lane in each direction shall be open at all times between 8:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. Any deviation of lane closure hours shall be approved by the Assistant Public Works Director-Traffic.</p>
<p>6. If a bus stop is impacted by traffic control, the Contractor shall notify Burbank Bus at (818) 238-5359, aemmer@burbankca.gov, contact@burbankbus.org or Los Angeles Metro at (213) 922-4632, guglielmoe@metro.net at least 5 working days before start of work.</p>
<p>7. If parking is impacted by traffic control, the Contractor shall post &quot;TEMPORARY NO PARKING ANYTIME&quot; signs at a minimum height of 3 feet and at least 5 working days before start of work. The Contractor may obtain signs from the Public Works Permit Counter. Once posted, the Contractor shall notify the Burbank Police Department Traffic Division, at (818) 238-3100 for approval and enforcement of posted signs.</p>
<p>8. Vehicular and pedestrian access to adjacent properties shall be provided at all times.</p>
<p>9. The contractor SHALL notify the City of Burbank Transportation Management Center 5 business days prior to installing any traffic control that reduces vehicular capacity on any signalized roadway within the City. Furthermore, 818-238-3953 and email at: rlockyer@burbankca.gov, dwilcox@burbankca.gov, DMcLean@burbankca.gov</p>
<p>10. Any work impacting any Traffic loops or cameras SHALL NOT COMMENCE without prior written consent from the City of Burbank Transportation Management Center via email. Impacted loops or cameras SHALL BE restored within a predetermined amount of time (between the contractor and the Transportation Management Center) or the City will restore them and the contractor will be billed.</p>
<p>11. During business hours, immediately notify the Public Works Department at (818) 238-3915 or after hours, immediately notify the Police Department at (818) 238-3000 if any of the following events occur:</p>
<p>a. A traffic signal is dark, flashing red, or malfunctioning near your work area;</p>
<p>b. A signalized intersection has traffic congestion near your work area;</p>
<p>c. Any traffic signal equipment or loop detector is damaged without planned replacement;</p>
<p>d. Any traffic signs, striping, or markings are removed without planned replacement</p>
