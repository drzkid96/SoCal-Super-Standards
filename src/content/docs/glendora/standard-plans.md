The City of Glendora Public Works Department publishes the unified **Complete Standard Designs & Specifications (April 2024 edition)**. Below are the core transportation and roadway sheets directly extracted from Section 1 (Street Improvements & Traffic Control) and Section 4 (Street Lighting).

:::tip[Governing Hierarchy & Local Practice]
**Governing hierarchy & key local rules:**
- **Traffic Control (`1.17` & `1.18`):** All work in the public right-of-way must comply with Glendora Standard `1.17` and carry the verbatim Standard `1.18` Traffic Control Notes on the plan set, supplemented by the **CA MUTCD** and **WATCH Manual**.
- **Curb ramps & striping:** Glendora adopts **APWA/SPPWC Standard Plans** for curb ramps and **Caltrans / CA MUTCD** for signing, striping, and traffic signals.
- **Arterial trenches (`1.10`):** Utility cuts on arterial streets trigger full-lane grind-and-overlay pavement restoration limits.
:::
