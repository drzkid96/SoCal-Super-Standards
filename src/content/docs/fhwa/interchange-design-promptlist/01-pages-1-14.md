<p>1.0 Design Standards</p>
<p>1.1 What design standards are applicable to the project?</p>
<p>- AASHTO publication &quot;A Policy on Design Standards, Interstate System&quot;: applicable for all projects on the Interstate System.</p>
<p>- AASHTO publication &quot;A Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets&quot; (commonly called the Green Book): applicable to all freeways on the NHS including the Interstate System.</p>
<p>- Additional standards of State DOT for geometric design, standard drawings, and standard specifications that meet or exceed the FHWA&#x27;s adopted standards.</p>
<p>1.2 Any design exceptions to the &quot;13 controlling criteria&quot; proposed?</p>
<p>2.0 Interchange and Ramp Spacing</p>
<p>2.1 Does the interchange spacing (based on crossroad to crossroad spacing) exceed 1 mi?</p>
<p>2.2 Does the spacing between successive entrances and exits meet or exceed AASHTO criteria?</p>
<p>3.0 Approach Alignment to Interchange</p></details></div>
