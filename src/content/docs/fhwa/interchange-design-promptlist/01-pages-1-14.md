<p>Below that is the heading Interchange Design Promptlist with a link to interchange_design_promptlist.pdf (186.42 KB). The start of the form follows, titled Interchange Design (New Construction and Reconstruction) Prompt-List for Assessing Key Geometric Features, with blank fields for Project, Location, Description, Assessment made by, and Date of Assessment.</p>
<p>Section 1.0 Design Standards: 1.1 asks what design standards are applicable to the project, with three checkbox lines. Section 1.2 asks about design exceptions. Section 2.0 Interchange and Ramp Spacing has questions 2.1 and 2.2 with blank answer lines. The page ends at the heading 3.0 Approach Alignment to Interchange, which continues on the next page.</p></details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1.0 Design Standards</p>
<p>1.1 What design standards are applicable to the project</p>
<p>_____ AASHTO publication &quot;A Policy on Design Standards Interstate System&quot; - applicable for all projects on the Interstate System</p>
<p>_____ AASHTO publication &quot;A Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets&quot; (commonly called the Green Book) - applicable to all freeways on the NHS including the Interstate System</p>
<p>_____ Additional standards of State DOT for geometric design, standard drawings, and standard specifications that meet or exceed the FHWA&#x27;s adopted standards</p>
<p>1.2 Any design exceptions to the &quot;13 controlling criteria&quot; proposed? __________</p>
<p>2.0 Interchange and Ramp Spacing</p>
<p>2.1 Does the interchange spacing (based on crossroad to crossroad spacing) exceed 1 mi? __________</p>
<p>2.2 Does the spacing between successive entrances and exits meet or exceed AASHTO criteria? __________</p>
<p>3.0 Approach Alignment to Interchange (heading only, continues on the next page).</p>
