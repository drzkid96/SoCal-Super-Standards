## Caltrans Design Information Bulletins &amp; HDM standards

Bulletins on the [Caltrans DIB list](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/design/design-information-bulletins-dibs) and HDM Chapter 1000. Caltrans says DIBs may supersede the Highway Design Manual. None of these is marked retired.

<table class="btab"><thead><tr><th>Bulletin</th><th>Title</th><th>Issued</th><th>Lane / bikeway scope</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-94/"><b>DIB 94</b></a></td><td>Complete Streets: Contextual Design Guidance</td><td>January 16, 2024</td><td>10.5 to 12 ft for through and turn lanes, by place type and speed (Table 5.3); 11 ft minimum for transit-only lanes and for right-hand and left-turn lanes on large-vehicle routes.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-89-02/"><b>DIB 89-02</b></a></td><td>Class IV Bikeway Guidance (Separated Bikeways/Cycle Tracks)</td><td>February 7, 2022</td><td>Does not set travel lane widths. Notes that lanes or the bikeway may need narrowing at loading zones, transit stops and passenger drop-off.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-hdm-ch1000/"><b>HDM Ch. 1000</b></a></td><td>Bicycle Transportation Design (Caltrans Highway Design Manual)</td><td>December 16, 2024 (eff. January 1, 2025)</td><td>Does not set motor-vehicle travel lane widths (see HDM Index 301.1 or DIB 94). Sets statutory Class I, II, III and trail criteria.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-79-04/"><b>DIB 79-04</b></a></td><td>Design Guidance and Standards for Major Pavement Rehabilitation Projects (2R and 3R)</td><td>October 2, 2019</td><td>12 ft on two- and three-lane conventional highway 3R projects; urban turn lanes may be narrower, right-turn lanes to 10 ft when severely constrained.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-81-03/"><b>DIB 81-03</b></a></td><td>Minor Pavement Rehabilitation Capital Preventive Maintenance (CAPM) Guidelines</td><td>November 8, 2024</td><td>No numeric widths. Restriping that creates nonstandard lanes, including narrowing lanes for a bike facility, needs a Design Standard Decision Document.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-93/"><b>DIB 93</b></a></td><td>Evacuation Route Design Guidance</td><td>December 23, 2020</td><td>12 ft standard on evacuation routes (HDM 301.1); 11 ft acceptable in rural town centers with low speeds and low truck volumes.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-92/"><b>DIB 92</b></a></td><td>Single Point Interchange Guidelines</td><td>November 9, 2018</td><td>14 ft left-turn lanes through the intersection, plus offtracking width where needed.</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-90/"><b>DIB 90</b></a></td><td>Diverging Diamond Interchange</td><td>December 14, 2017</td><td>Wider lanes in every lane through the crossovers for truck offtracking (no single value; HDM 301.1 and Table 504.3).</td></tr></tbody></table>

Related Caltrans design memos: [Bikeway Facility Selection Guidance](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/design/documents/dod-bikeway-selection-memo_06302020_signed-a11y.pdf) (June 30, 2020) and [Complete Streets Decision Document implementation](https://dot.ca.gov/-/media/dot-media/programs/design/documents/csdd-memo20210210finalktyt-mdk--a11y.pdf) (February 11, 2021). The HDM lane width standard itself is Index 301.1.
