## What reviewers check

<table class="btab"><thead><tr><th>Group</th><th>Items</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Drafting (1-14)</td><td>LADOT drafting standards and Geometric Plan Sample format; project title above the title block; street name and limits, sheet number, LADOT signature box; LADOT project and drawing numbers; north arrow; scale 1 in = 40 ft; consultant signature block with Civil and Traffic stamps, signed by the Engineer of Record; civil improvements and streetscape dimensions; land use; property lines, limits and right of way; signalized intersections; existing, proposed and removed striping, markings, signs and posts; parking elements (prohibitions, PMZ, colored curb, stall marking)</td></tr><tr><td>As-built base (15-17)</td><td>Current LADOT and civil base files with field-verified conditions; field-verify anything that affects design; field photos and notes checked</td></tr><tr><td>Design (18-24)</td><td>CA MUTCD, MPP 531, the Supplemental Street Design Guide and LADOT policies; title sheet with notes, legend, abbreviations, details, typical section, key map and vicinity map; sign diagrams and legend showing existing, removed and proposed signs; coordination with signal plans and other disciplines; agency and council coordination; comment resolution matrix after 30%</td></tr><tr><td>Supporting documents (25-27)</td><td>Traffic design documentation (for example the Final EIR); preliminary street improvement plans; dated field photos with a key map (Google Street View is not accepted)</td></tr></tbody></table>
