DIB 82 is Caltrans' accessibility design guidance for the State highway system. Caltrans requires it for every project in the State right-of-way, including encroachment permit work by other agencies, and the project engineer certifies compliance at Ready to List. Where full compliance is technically infeasible, the designer documents it on the DIB's "Exception to Accessibility Design Standards" form.

After U.S. DOT adopted PROWAG for transit stops, Caltrans issued supplemental guidance on January 16, 2025, effective for Caltrans projects on January 17, 2025. Projects already in construction, or with all Ready to List approvals by that date, are exempt.

<table class="btab wide2"><thead><tr><th>Element</th><th>Requirement (supplement to Section 4.3.16)</th><th>PROWAG</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Boarding and alighting areas, and the pedestrian access routes to them and to shelters</td><td>48 in. minimum clear width</td><td>R203.2.1</td></tr><tr><td>Boarding platforms not protected by screens or guards</td><td>Detectable warnings along the boarding edge. Where a curb is at the boarding edge, the detectable warning may sit at the back of curb</td><td>R205.5</td></tr><tr><td>Rail boarding areas at sidewalk or street level</td><td>Same detectable warning rule and curb option</td><td>R205.6</td></tr><tr><td>Fare vending machines</td><td>PROWAG R403 and Section 707 of 36 CFR 1191 Appendix D, except 707.2 and 707.3</td><td>R210, R403</td></tr><tr><td>Shelter lighting and heating</td><td>Proximity actuated</td><td>R309.2.3</td></tr></tbody></table>

Infeasibility for transit stops follows PROWAG R202.3: meet the requirements to the maximum extent feasible and document it on the DIB 82-06 exception form.
