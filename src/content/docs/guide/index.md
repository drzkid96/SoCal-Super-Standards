## Search
Press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>. Search covers every section, figure caption, and page title. Section numbers (`3B.19`), sign codes (`R10-6`), and plain words all work.

## Page numbers and figures
Every section heading shows the printed manual page. Figures and tables sit at the end of each chapter; mentions of "Figure 4I-3" in the text jump to the figure. Click any figure to open it full size.

## Short links
Link straight to any section with `/s/ca/<section>/`, for example [`/s/ca/4I.06/`](/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4I.06/).
