## Four ways in

**1. You're designing something.** Open **Design by topic** in the sidebar. Topics are grouped by the part of the street you're working on: pedestrians, bikes and transit, curb and street design, intersections and signals, signs, and work zones and rail. Each topic page has:

- **The rules.** Every CA MUTCD 2026 section on the topic, its first Standard (or Guidance), a count of its shall, should and may provisions, and the matching federal section. A "CA amended" badge means California changed the federal text.
- **LADOT requirements.** The City standard drawings for the topic, each opening its own drawing page.
- **Figures and signs**, plus a link to every related figure and drawing in the Detail and Drawing Index.
- **Ask AI to compare all sources** opens the AI with a comparison question already filled in.

**2. You have a number or term.** Type a drawing number (`S-481.0` or `s481`), sign code (`R3-17`), section (`3C.05`), Caltrans detail (`Detail 22`), bulletin (`DIB 94`), or plain term (`red curb`, `curb ramp`) in the search bar on the home page or Detail Index and press Enter to jump straight there. Press <kbd>/</kbd> on any library page to focus its filter box, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> anywhere to run full-text search across every page, OCR text included.

**3. You're answering a comment or reading a plan.** Open **Ask the standards**, then paste a screenshot, a plan snip or a reviewer comment. It finds the governing CA MUTCD sections, LADOT drawings and signs, and links each one. It uses your own Anthropic key, which is kept in your browser only.

**4. You're reading the source.** Use the top navigation bar or **Source documents** in the sidebar for the full CA MUTCD 2026, LA28 Supplement, Caltrans 2026 Standard Plans, federal MUTCD 11th Edition Revision 1, bulletins and LADOT documents. **Standards status** and **Sources and versions** list current editions and what has been superseded.

## Finding details

- **Detail and Drawing Index.** Every CA and federal MUTCD figure and table, every LADOT standard drawing, and every figure page from the LADOT design guides. Filter by source, document, type and topic, or type words. Filters stay in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
- **LADOT drawing pages.** Each standard drawing has its own page. It shows every sheet as a sharpened image, the notes text (OCR on scans), the signs on the drawing, the MUTCD sections it cites, and links to the previous and next drawing.
- **Sign Library.** Every standard sign drawn at the same scale, with sizes, CA status, governing sections and the LADOT pages that use it. Hover over any sign code anywhere on the site to see the sign.

## Order of authority

CA MUTCD 2026 is the legal standard in California. The federal MUTCD is the national baseline that California adopts with amendments. LADOT documents add City of Los Angeles requirements on top of the CA MUTCD. Design guides (NACTO, FHWA guides) are guidance only.

On manual pages, provisions are color-coded:

- **Standard** (shall): bold.
- **Guidance** (should): italic.
- **Option** (may) and **Support**: regular text.
- California text is blue with a CA mark.
