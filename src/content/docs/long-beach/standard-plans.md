The City of Long Beach Public Works Department publishes dedicated **300-Series Traffic Striping, Pavement Markers & Traffic Control** and **100-Series General Roadwork** standard plans. For items not covered by a City sheet, Long Beach adopts the **CA MUTCD** and **Caltrans Standard Plans** for traffic striping, markers, and traffic signals, and the **SPPWC (Greenbook)** for general roadwork.

:::tip[Governing Hierarchy & Local Practice]
**Governing hierarchy & key local rules:**
- **Curb ramps:** Design per Long Beach **Std. Plan 122** paired with **Caltrans Std. Plan A88A** slope tolerances (8.33% max ramp, 2% max landing, 10% max flare) and a detailed spot-elevation grading plan.
- **Turn pockets:** Arrows are required at the beginning of all left/right turn pockets, and at **both beginning and end** for pockets ≥ 100 ft (`LB 308`); dual turns require CA MUTCD Detail 40 cat-tracking.
- **Traffic Control Plans (TCP):** Regular work hours are **Mon–Fri, 8:30 AM–3:30 PM**; no work during peak hours (5:00–8:30 AM and 3:30–7:00 PM). Engineered TCPs stamped by a CA PE/TE are mandatory on designated arterials (Long Beach Blvd, Ocean Blvd, Pacific Coast Hwy, Bellflower Blvd, Lakewood Blvd, 7th St, etc.).
:::
