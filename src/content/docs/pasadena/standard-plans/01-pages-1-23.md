### Page 5 - Speed Humps and Speed Cushions

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-707</strong> &middot; Signs: W13-1 <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> &middot; Topics: parking-curb, speed-calming, striping, signs</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0005.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0005.webp" alt="Pasadena Std. Plans page 5 S-707 Speed Hump/Cushion Installation, Channelization and Temporary Signing Requirements, sheet 1 of 5"></a><figcaption>Pasadena Std. Plans, page 5.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Installation details:</h5>
<p>1. Speed humps shall not be placed over manholes, watergates, junction chambers, etc.</p>
<p>2. Edge of speed hump should be 10 ft minimum from edge and uphill of driveway.</p>
<p>3. Height of speed hump shall nominally be 3.5 in +/- 0.25 in at all cross sections.</p>
<p>4. Whenever possible, speed humps shall be placed at property lines instead of mid-lot.</p>
<p>5. Whenever possible, speed humps shall be placed adjacent to street lights.</p>
<p>6. Speed humps shall be marked using thermoplastic material.</p>
<p>7. Speed hump signs shall be installed per Figure A, where noted on plans.</p>
<p>Figure A: Speed hump warning sign (typical). Warning flags (first 30 days). 30 in x 30 in. Black on yellow or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> SPEED HUMP, 6 in Series E letters. W13-1(15) sign. Sign locations: as directed by Engineer.</p>
<h5>Temporary signage details:</h5>
<p>1. The contractor shall furnish and maintain a flashing barricade, 50 feet from each speed hump location in both directions, until the first application of paint has been applied showing the pavement messages and striping.</p>
<p>2. Mount 30 in x 30 in <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> warning sign (black on yellow, 6 in Series E letters) and W13-1 sign per MUTCD with the California Supplement, on each of the barricades at each speed hump location.</p></details>
