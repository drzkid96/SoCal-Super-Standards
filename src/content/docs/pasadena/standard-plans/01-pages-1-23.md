<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Turn Bay Transition - Single or Double Centerline.</b> City of Pasadena standard plan S-702, revision 1, dual units, dated 6/4/01. It lays out a left turn bay transition from the centerline of the street with a 30:1 taper from point A over a 45 m (150 ft) length, and gives a six step procedure to mark points A through J and form the curves.</p><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of the transition: A horizontal street centerline is drawn as a dash-dot line. The transition starts at point A at the left (START OF TRANSITION) where the striping leaves the centerline on a 30:1 taper. The tapered line runs 27 m (90&#x27;) to point B and the overall length to the end of transition is 45 m (150&#x27;). A smooth S curve then moves the line across the centerline to point H at the end of transition. Points B and C, E and F, I and J are used to form two curves, B-J-E and F-I-H. Along the baseline from B to the end of transition the segments are 3 m (10&#x27;), 3 m (10&#x27;), 6 m (20&#x27;), 3 m (10&#x27;) and 3 m (10&#x27;). At the end of transition the lateral offsets are 1.5 m (5&#x27;) from point D to the centerline and 3 m (10&#x27;) between points D and H. Thick segments mark the striping lines at start and end. Dashed construction lines connect A to D and C to G, and points D, G and H are the layout points.</p>
<p>Procedure to install transition: see the numbered steps in the notes.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena, Public Works &amp; Transportation Department, Turn Bay Transition, Single Double Centerline. Drawn by M. Mojica, checked by D. Ortega, submitted by C. Hernandez. Revision 1, Units DUAL, Standard Plan S-702, sheet 1 of 1.</p></details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Procedure to install transition:</h5>
<p>1. Layout points A, D, G, &amp; H.</p>
<p>2. Use points A &amp; D to determine a straight line and mark B &amp; C.</p>
<p>3. Use points C &amp; G to determine a straight line and mark E &amp; F.</p>
<p>4. Mark J by measuring 180 mm (7&quot;) in from C. Mark I by measuring 150 mm (6&quot;) in from G.</p>
<p>5. Form curves B-J-E &amp; F-I-H.</p>
<p>6. Stripe.</p></details></div>
