### Page 17 - Pavement Markings, Bicycle Obstruction Markings

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A24K</strong>, <strong>A20D</strong> &middot; Topics: bikeways, striping</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0017.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0017.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 17 A24K Pavement Markings, Bicycle Obstruction Markings"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 17.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. L=WS, where W is the offset in feet and S is bicycle approach speed in mph.</p>
<p>2. Provide an additional foot of offset for a raised obstruction and use the formula L=(W+1)S for the taper length.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Plan A20D for Detail 39.</p>
<p>4. Use markings only when there is 5&#x27;-0&quot; of clear space.</p></details>

### Page 18 - Pavement Markings, Speed Reduction

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A24L</strong></div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0018.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0018.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. <details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. Exact location for speed reduction pavement markings will be determined by the Engineer.</p>
<p>2. Do not cover pavement markings.</p>
<p>3. Speed reduction markings are white. Contrast treatment is black.</p>
<p>4. Engineer to determine length and spacing based on approach and target speeds in the field.</p>
