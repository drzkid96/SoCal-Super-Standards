### Page 19 - Curb Ramp Details

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A88A</strong> &middot; Topics: crosswalks, transit, parking-curb, accessibility</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0019.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0019.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 19 A88A Curb Ramp Details"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 19.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>1. As site conditions dictate, Case A through Case G curb ramps may be used for corner installations similar to those shown in Detail A and Detail B. The case of curb ramps used in Detail A do not have to be the same. Case A through Case G curb ramps also may be used at mid block locations, as site conditions dictate. For specific site condition configuration, including the conform to existing sidewalk, see Project Plans.</p>
<p>2. If distance from curb to back of sidewalk is too short to accommodate ramp and 4&#x27;-2&quot; platform (landing) as shown in Case A, the sidewalk may be depressed longitudinally as in Case B or C or may be widened as in Case D.</p>
<p>3. When ramp is located in center of curb return, crosswalk configuration must be similar to that shown for Detail B.</p>
<p>4. As site conditions dictate, the retaining curb side and the flared side of the Case G ramp shall be constructed in reversed position.</p>
<p>5. The ramp portion of the curb ramp is a typical rectangle, unless modified in the Project Plans.</p>
<p>6. Side slope of ramp flares vary uniformly from a maximum of 9.0% at curb to conform with longitudinal sidewalk slope adjacent to top of the ramp, except in Case C and Case F.</p>
<p>7. The adjacent surfaces at transitions at curb ramps to walks, gutters, and streets shall be at the same level.</p>
<p>8. Counter slopes of adjoining gutters and road surfaces immediately adjacent to and within 24 inches of the curb ramp shall not be steeper than 1V:20H (5.0%). Gutter pan slope shall not exceed 1&quot; of depth for each 2&#x27;-0&quot; of width.</p>
<p>9. Transition gutter pan slope from 1&quot; of depth for each 2&#x27;-0&quot; of width to match typical gutter pan slope per Standard Plan A87A.</p>
<p>10. The detectable warning surface will be a rectangle as shown at back of curb, unless modified in the Project Plans. Curb ramps shall have a detectable warning surface that extends the full width and 3&#x27;-0&quot; depth of the ramp. Detectable warning surfaces shall extend the full width of the ramp except a maximum gap of 1 inch is allowed on each side of the ramp.</p>
<p>11. Sidewalk and ramp thickness &quot;T&quot;, shall be 3 1/2&quot; minimum.</p>
<p>12. Utility pull boxes, manholes, vaults and all other utility facilities within the boundaries of the curb ramp will be relocated or adjusted to grade by the owner prior to, or in conjunction with, curb ramp construction.</p>
<p>13. Detectable warning surface may have to be cut to allow removal of utility covers while maintaining detectable warning width and depth.</p></details>

### Page 20 - Curb Ramp and Island Passageway Details

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A88B</strong>, <strong>A88A</strong> &middot; Topics: crosswalks, transit, parking-curb, accessibility</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0020.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0020.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 20 A88B Curb Ramp and Island Passageway Details"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 20.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>1. Sidewalk, ramp and passageway thickness &quot;T&quot;, shall be 3 1/2&quot; minimum.</p>
<p>2. For details of detectable warning surfaces, see Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>3. Where an island passageway length is greater than or equal to 6&#x27;-0&quot;, but less than 8&#x27;-0&quot;, each detectable warning surface shall extend the full width and 2&#x27;-0&quot; depth of the passageway length. Where an island passageway length is greater than or equal to 8&#x27;-0&quot;, each detectable warning surface shall extend the full width and 3&#x27;-0&quot; depth of the passageway length. Detectable warning surfaces shall extend the full width of the island passageway except a maximum gap of 1&quot; is allowed on each side of the passageway.</p>
<p>4. The adjacent surfaces at transitions at curb ramps to walks, gutters, and streets shall be at the same level.</p>
<p>5. Utility pull boxes, manholes, vaults and all other utility facilities within the boundaries of the curb ramp will be relocated or adjusted to grade by the owner prior to, or in conjunction with, curb ramp construction.</p>
<p>6. Detectable warning surface may have to be cut to allow removal of utility covers while maintaining detectable warning width and depth.</p>
<p>7. For additional curb ramp details, see Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>8. The detectable warning surface will be a rectangle as shown at the face of curb, unless modified in the Project Plans.</p></details>

### Page 21 - Accessible Parking Off-Street

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A90A</strong>, <strong>A24C</strong>, <strong>A88A</strong>, <strong>A90B</strong> &middot; Signs: R7-8b &middot; Topics: parking-curb, signs, accessibility</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0021.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0021.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 21 A90A Accessible Parking Off-Street"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 21.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>1. Accessible parking spaces serving a particular building shall be located on the shortest accessible route of travel from adjacent parking to an accessible entrance. In parking facilities that do not serve a particular building, accessible parking shall be located on the shortest accessible route of travel to an accessible pedestrian entrance of the parking facility.</p>
<p>2. One in every six accessible off-street parking stalls, but not less than one, shall be served by an accessible aisle of 8&#x27;-0&quot; minimum width and shall be signed van accessible. The R7-8b sign shall be mounted below the R99B(CA) plaque or the R99C(CA) sign.</p>
<p>3. In each parking stall, a curb or parking bumper shall be provided if required to prevent encroachment of vehicles over the required width of walkways. Parking stalls shall be so located that persons with disabilities are not compelled to wheel or walk behind parked vehicles other than their own. For more parking bumper requirements, see the Standard Specifications.</p>
<p>4. Parking spaces and access aisles shall be level with surface slopes not exceeding 1.5% in all directions.</p>
<p>5. Table A shall be used to determine the required number of accessible parking stalls in each parking lot or garage.</p>
<p>6. Where Plaque R99B(CA), Sign R99C(CA) or Sign R7-8b are installed, the bottom of the sign or plaque panel shall be a minimum of 7&#x27;-0&quot; above the surrounding surface.</p>
<p>7. Curb ramps shall conform to the details shown on Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>8. Blue paint, instead of white may be used for marking accessibility aisles in areas where snow may cause white markings to not be visible.</p>
<p>9. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;, shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials. See Standard Plan A90B for details of the &quot;NO PARKING&quot; pavement marking.</p>
<p>10. A R100B(CA) sign shall be posted in a conspicuous place at each entrance to off-street parking facilities or immediately adjacent to and visible from each stall. The sign shall include the address where the towed vehicle may be reclaimed and the telephone number of the local traffic law enforcement agency.</p>
<p>11. Where a single (non-van) accessible parking space is provided, the loading and unloading access aisle shall be on the passenger side of the vehicle as the vehicle is going forward into the parking space.</p>
<p>12. Where a van accessible parking space is provided, the loading and unloading access aisle shall be 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide minimum, and shall be on the passenger side of the vehicle as the vehicle is going forward into the parking space.</p>
<p>13. Accessible Parking Only Sign shall be Sign R99C(CA) or Sign R99(CA) with Plaque R99B(CA).</p>
<p>Legend: ISA - International Symbol of Accessibility.</p></details>

### Page 22 - Accessible Parking On-Street

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A90B</strong>, <strong>A24E</strong>, <strong>A88A</strong> &middot; Topics: transit, parking-curb, signs, accessibility</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0022.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0022.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 22 A90B Accessible Parking On-Street"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 22.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>1. Parking spaces shall be so located that persons with disabilities are not compelled to wheel or walk behind parked vehicles other than their own.</p>
<p>2. Surface slopes of accessible on-street parking spaces shall be the minimum feasible.</p>
<p>3. Where Plaque R99B(CA) or Sign R99C(CA) are installed, the bottom of the sign or plaque panel shall be a minimum of 7&#x27;-0&quot; above the surrounding surface.</p>
<p>4. Curb ramps shall conform to the details shown on Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>5. Accessible on-street parking spaces shall not be smaller in length or width than that specified by the local jurisdiction for other parking spaces, but not less than 20&#x27;-0&quot; in length and not less than 8&#x27;-0&quot; in width.</p>
<p>6. Blue paint, instead of white may be used for marking accessibility aisles in areas where snow may cause white markings to not be visible.</p>
<p>7. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;, shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high on a contrasting background and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials. See Standard Plan A24E for square foot area for painting the words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;.</p>
<p>8. There shall be no obstructions on the sidewalk adjacent to and for the full length of the parking space, except for the ISA parking sign shown.</p>
<p>9. The Conventional detail should be the primary choice of accessible on-street parking. However, if the sidewalk lacks adequate space to construct a standard curb ramp, the restricted right of way detail should be used.</p>
<p>10. If the Restricted Right of Way Width detail is selected and it conflicts with a bus stop or other uses, this detail may apply to the other end of the block.</p>
<p>11. Accessible Parking Only Sign shall be Sign R99C(CA) or Sign R99(CA) with Plaque R99B(CA).</p>
<p>Legend: ISA - International Symbol of Accessibility.</p></details>
