### Page 11 - Pavement Markings, Words

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A24D</strong> &middot; Topics: bikeways, signals</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0011.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0011.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 11 A24D Pavement Markings, Words"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 11.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. If a message consists of more than one word, it must read &quot;UP&quot;, i.e., the first word must be nearest the driver.</p>
<p>2. The space between words must be at least four times the height of the characters for low speed roads, but not more than ten times the height of the characters. The space may be reduced appropriately where there is limited space because of local conditions.</p>
<p>3. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>4. Portions of a letter, number or symbol may be separated by connecting segments not to exceed 2 in. in width.</p>
<p>Table, Word Markings (area in sq ft): RUNAWAY 43, VEHICLES 42, YIELD 24, SCHOOL 35, WAIT 19, SLOW 23, AHEAD 31, TURN 24, HERE 26, XING 21, SIGNAL 32, LEFT 19, RIGHT 26, STOP 22, PED 18, COMPACT 10, BIKE 5, LANE 6.</p></details>

### Page 12 - Pavement Markings, Words

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A24E</strong>, <strong>A90A</strong>, <strong>A90B</strong> &middot; Topics: bikeways, transit, parking-curb</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0012.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0012.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 12 A24E Pavement Markings, Words"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 12.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. If a message consists of more than one word, it must read &quot;UP&quot;, i.e., the first word must be nearest the driver.</p>
<p>2. The space between words must be at least four times the height of the characters for low speed roads, but not more than ten times the height of the characters. The space may be reduced appropriately where there is limited space because of local conditions.</p>
<p>3. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>4. Portions of a letter, number, or symbol may be separated by connecting segments not to exceed 2 in. in width.</p>
<p>5. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot; pavement marking is to be used for parking facilities. For typical locations of markings, see Standard Plans A90A and A90B.</p>
<p>6. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot; shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high on a contrasting background and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials.</p></details>
