### Page 4 - Pavement Markers and Traffic Lines, Typical Details (Channelizing, Bike Lane, Extensions and Recessed Markers)

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>A20D</strong> &middot; Topics: bikeways, parking-curb, striping, intersections</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0004.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/p0004.webp" alt="Caltrans Std. Plans 2026 page 4 A20D Pavement Markers and Traffic Lines, Typical Details"></a><figcaption>Caltrans Std. Plans 2026, page 4.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Note: Pavement markers shown off the solid line may be placed on the line.</p>
<h5>Recessed notes:</h5>
<p>A. See typical traffic line details for pavement marking patterns.</p>
<p>B. See Standard Specifications for recess depth and recess striping material thickness.</p>
<h5>Recessed marker notes:</h5>
<p>1. See typical traffic line details for marker patterns to be used with recessed pavement markers.</p>
<p>2. The retroreflective pavement markers shown for recessed installations are not to be used for non-recessed installations.</p>
<p>3. The top of pavement markers installed in recesses shall be 0 in. to 3/16 in. below the pavement surface.</p>
<p>4. Use Type 1 recess for pavement markers with one-way retroreflective face. Use Type 2 recess for pavement markers with two-way retroreflective face.</p>
<p>5. For exit ramps, use Type 1 recess for Type R markers with one-way retroreflective face. Reverse the orientation of the recess and place the marker at the upstream end of the recess.</p>
<h5>Legend:</h5>
<p>Type C red-clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Type G one-way clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>6 in. yellow line.</p>
