<p>Option:</p>

<p>08 The LA28 sign panel, including if it is combined with the word message PERMIT ONLY, may be used on any existing or new Advance Turn (G22(CA)) sign, where engineering judgment determines the need to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games. Support:</p>
