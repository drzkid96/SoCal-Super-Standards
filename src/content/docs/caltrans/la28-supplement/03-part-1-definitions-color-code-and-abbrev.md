<p>CHAPTER 1C. DEFINITIONS, ACRONYMS, AND ABBREVIATIONS USED IN THIS MANUAL</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1C.02/">Section 1C.02</a> Definitions of Words and Phrases Used in this Manual The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Standard:</p>
<p>03 The following words and phrases, when used in this Manual, shall have the following meanings: 92a. Games Route Network (GRN) – Network of existing roads connecting Los Angles 2028 Summer Olympics and Paralympics competition and key non-competition venues involving a wide range of traffic management measures. As per California Vehicle Code Section 21655.10, vehicles with a permit only will be allowed to use the dedicated lanes on roadways in GRN. 97a. GRN Lane(s) - Specific preferential lanes that are used as dedicated lane(s) for Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues and are included in the established GRN.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1C.03/">Section 1C.03</a> Meanings of Acronyms and Abbreviations Used in this Manual The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>02 The following list of acronyms are related to traffic control devices and provided for ease of use and as a handy reference: 25a. GRN Games Route Network 104a. LA28 Los Angeles 2028 Olympics and Paralympics</p>
<p>03 The acronym “LA28” refers to the name of the event, named by the host city plus the 2-digit year (e.g. Paris24). Abbreviating the year allows the word message to be as brief as practical while keeping it simple, allowing for easier accommodation when used on the plaque, panel or as word message pavement marking.</p></div>
