<p>CHAPTER 2B. REGULATORY SIGNS, BARRICADES, AND GATES</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2B.01/">Section 2B.01</a> Application of Regulatory Signs The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>07 The LA28 sign plaque and the LA28 sign panel combined with the word message PERMIT ONLY in black legend on a white background shall only be used on signs in GRN dedicated lanes, on which the GRN permit requirements and restrictions are applicable. These signs with the combined LA28 and PERMIT ONLY sign plaques or sign panels shall be installed at or near where the restriction applies.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2B.03/">Section 2B.03</a> Size of Regulatory Signs The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>01a Except as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.07/">Section 2A.07</a>, the minimum sizes for any new installations of regulatory signs to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways shall be as shown in Tables 2B-1 and 2B-1(CA) of CA MUTCD 2026.</p>
