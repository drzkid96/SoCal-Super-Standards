### Page 20 - CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement

<div class="pmeta">Topics: parking-curb, signs</div>
<div class="dtext"><p>CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement Page 20</p>
<p>Introduction July 15, 2026</p>
<h4>CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement</h4>
<h4>INTRODUCTION</h4>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>01 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and Paralympics (LA28), which includes the Summer Olympics (July 14-30, 2028) and Summer Paralympics (August 15-27, 2028), will establish a Games Route Network (GRN) with dedicated lanes for Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues and ensure athletes and Game personnel get to their events and venues on time. The GRN will establish a temporary network of roads, freeways, expressways, highways, arterials, local roads, and managed lanes to provide a safe, fast, and reliable transportation system.</p>
<p>02 During the Games, in order to facilitate the movement of athletes and Games personnel, some of the existing traffic control devices on roadways included in the established GRN will need to be changed temporarily, to allow identification of the priority lanes that have been dedicated in GRN for athletes and Game personnel travel. This will be accomplished by using “LA28” white legend or black legend on light blue background as a sign plaque or sign panel and using it as a word message pavement marking. National MUTCD has not assigned the meaning of light blue color, currently, it has reserved it for future designation. Any use of light blue color on a traffic control device will not be in compliance with the MUTCD, requiring permission to experiment. At some locations, in addition to the LA28 sign plaque or panel, the “PERMIT ONLY” black legend on white background will also be used as a sign plaque or sign panel and in white legend as a pavement marking.</p>
<p>03 To save costs, expedite implementation and for fiscal responsibility, since the change is needed temporarily, instead of removing existing signs and installing new or special traffic signs, most of the changes will be limited to changing existing traffic signs. New signs will be used only when existing signs are not available at the location or impractical to change. The “LA28” sign plaque will be added to some of the existing traffic signs, a portion of some traffic signs will be overlayed with the “LA28” panel, while some of the traffic signs would be covered during the games. The PERMIT ONLY plaque and panel will be used in conjunction with the LA28 plaque or panel, on some of the traffic signs. The “LA28” pavement markings, in conjunction with, or without, the PERMIT ONLY pavement markings will be installed in some lanes of the GRN roadways. LA28 traffic control devices will be installed from Fall 2027 through July 1, 2028. The LA28 signs will be covered when installed and uncovered starting June 1, 2028. The facilities will be restored beginning September 1, 2028.</p>
<p>04 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and Paralympics (LA28) being an atypical large-scale special event, it necessitates the use of unique identifier, to draw attention to the LA28 traffic signs. Using “LA28” white or black legend on light blue background serves this purpose by aiding recognition and quicker comprehension of the dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games, while keeping the message simple. Using the light blue color for the background was chosen as this color is already included in the National Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD) and has been identified as being appropriate for use in conveying traffic control information. The light blue color in the MUTCD is reserved for future designation and not assigned any specific purpose. The proposed use of the light blue color during the Games does not comply with the National MUTCD, it requires approval to experiment from FHWA.</p>
<p>05 Caltrans prepared a request for permission to experiment for the use of light blue color as the background color on LA28 sign plaques and sign panels. The experiment was approved by Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) on August 13, 2025. The use of LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word messages, whether used on traffic sign plaques, traffic sign panels or pavement markings, do not require experimentation as the use of word messages complies with the National MUTCD. Agencies are allowed to develop special word legend signs based on engineering judgment, and unlike colors that have not been assigned, or symbols that have not been approved for signs, new word legend signs can be used without the need for experimentation.</p>
<p>06 This California (CA) Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD) 2026 LA28 Supplement is issued in support of Caltrans request for experimentation to FHWA for the non-standard use of light blue color as the background color for “LA28” white or black legend word message sign plaques and sign panels planned to be used during this large-scale special event on the network of roads, freeways, highways, arterials and specific managed lanes included in the established GRN. Caltrans request for permission to experiment is specifically for the use of light blue color as the background color for the “LA28” word message. The CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement dictates how the "LA28" word message with the light blue background is to be used to designate the GRN.</p>
<p>07 This CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement incorporates Federal Highway Administration’s Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices 2023 and the current California MUTCD 2026 and standards and specifications for all traffic control devices used in dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games.</p></div>

### Page 21

<div class="dtext"><p>CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement Page 21</p>
<p>Introduction July 15, 2026</p>
<p>08 In general, the CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement contents correlate to the CA MUTCD (and National MUTCD) Parts, Chapters and Sections in the same sequence, heading and numbering and retaining the same titles. In instances where there is no corresponding Section, Figure or Table in the CA MUTCD or National MUTCD for a topic in the CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement, the topic is given a new number and include “LA28” in parentheses to indicate that it is specific only for LA28.</p>
<p>09 The CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement includes only the additional section, figure and table contents that are needed for use on GRN roadways during the Los Angles 2028 Summer Olympics and Paralympics competition as part of the experimentation request. Current CA MUTCD, including its section, figure and table contents, will be used for all traffic control devices used on GRN roadways during Los Angles 2028 Summer Olympics and Paralympics. CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement provides only additional content that are specific to dedicated lanes on GRN roadways and for use only during the Los Angles 2028 Summer Olympics and Paralympics to support the experimentation request.</p></div>
