<p>CHAPTER 2G. PREFERENTIAL AND MANAGED LANE SIGNS</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2G.01/">Section 2G.01</a> Scope The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>11 Specific preferential lanes will be used to designate Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues on roadways that are included in the established GRN during LA28 in locations specified by the supplement. Specific preferential lanes that are used as dedicated lane(s) for Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues and included in the established GRN, are referred to as “GRN lane(s)” in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement. Standard:</p>
<p>12 The provisions of Chapter 2G for regulatory and guide signs shall apply to all roadways included in the established GRN in locations specified by Caltrans experimentation request to FHWA.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2G.03/">Section 2G.03</a> Regulatory Signs for Preferential Lanes - General The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>25 The provisions of Sections 2G.03 through 2G.07 regarding regulatory signs for preferential lanes shall apply to GRN dedicated lanes operated at all times or at certain times based on the needs of established GRN to allow Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues and ensure athletes and accredited persons make their events and venues on time. Such GRN lanes shall use changeable message signs or changeable message elements within static signs, or static signs to display the appropriate regulatory sign messages only when they are in effect. Option:</p>
<p>26 Regulatory signs or regulatory panels within the appropriate guide signs may be used to display the open/closed status of the GRN lane.</p>
<p>27 The LA28 sign plaque or the LA28 sign panel may be used on any regulatory sign included in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement, where engineering judgment determines the need to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games.</p>
<p>28 If used, the LA28 sign plaque or the LA28 sign panel may be combined with the word message PERMIT ONLY in black legend on a white background where engineering judgment determines it is needed. Standard:</p>
<p>29 Regulatory signs applicable only to a dedicated GRN lane shall be distinguished from regulatory signs applicable to general- purpose lanes by the inclusion of the legend “LA28”, or “LA28” and “PERMIT ONLY” (see Figure 2G-1(LA28)). Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.06/">Section 2A.06</a> for "LA28" word legend and legend background colors. If used, the legend “PERMIT ONLY” color shall be black and the background color shall be white. Support:</p>
<p>30 The word message and light blue color background displayed on a particular GRN Lane regulatory sign will vary based on the specific type of allowed traffic and on other related operational constraints that have been established for a particular lane. Option:</p>
<p>31 Changeable message signs may supplement or substitute for static GRN Lane regulatory signs where travel conditions change or where multiple types of operational strategies (such as time of day requirements or vehicle types) are used and varied throughout the day or week, or on a real-time basis, to manage the use of, control of, or access to GRN lanes. Standard:</p>
<p>32 When changeable message signs (see Chapter 2L) are used as regulatory signs for GRN lanes, they shall be the required sign size and shall display the required letter height and legend format that corresponds to the type of roadway facility and design speed. Guidance:</p>
<p>33 When regulatory signs are used at new locations on conventional roads to facilitate GRN travel during LA28 games, the decision regarding whether to use a barrier-mounted, post-mounted, or overhead version of a particular type of sign should be based on an engineering judgment that considers the available space, the existing signs for the adjacent general-purpose traffic lanes, roadway and traffic characteristics, the proximity to existing overhead signs, the ability to install overhead signs, and any other unique local factors.</p>
<p>34 If overhead regulatory signs applicable only to a GRN lane are located in approximately the same longitudinal position along the highway</p></div>
