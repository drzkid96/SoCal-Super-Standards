<h4>PART 3</h4>
<h4>MARKINGS</h4>
<p>CHAPTER 3A. GENERAL</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3A.01/">Section 3A.01</a> Standardization of Application The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>09 The provisions of Part 3 regarding markings in general, material, retroreflectivity (Chapter 3A), word pavement markings (Chapter 3B), preferential lane markings (Chapter 3E) and colored pavements (Chapter 3H) shall apply to all roadways included in the established GRN in locations specified by Caltrans experimentation request to FHWA.</p>
<p>10 When a Games Route Network (GRN) has been established with dedicated lanes for Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues, the LA28 sign plaque, LA28 sign panel, including when combined with PERMIT ONLY message (see Chapters 2A, 2B, 2D, 2E and 2G) and LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings (see Chapters 3A, 3B, 3E and 3H) for these lanes shall be used to advise road users of GRN permit requirements and restrictions. Guidance:</p>
<p>11 The LA28 word pavement marking (see Figures 3B-107(LA28) and 3B-108(LA28)) should be used only where deemed necessary or desirable and justified by engineering judgment, as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1D.03/">Section 1D.03</a>.</p>
<p>12 The LA28 word pavement markings in white color legend, without any background should only be used where the white legend color contrasts significantly with the adjoining paved areas. The LA28 word pavement marking can be used in combination with the black color to enhance contrast with adjoining paved areas. Option:</p>
<p>13 The LA28 word pavement marking may be used on any roadway that is included in the established GRN, where engineering judgment determines the need to identify GR dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games.</p>
<p>14 To enhance the identification of GRN dedicated lanes, the PERMIT ONLY word pavement marking in white legend on a black background maybe used at some locations in conjunction with the LA28 word pavement marking, where engineering judgment determines it is needed. Guidance:</p>
<p>15 If used, the LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings (see Figures 3B-107(LA28) and 3B-108(LA28)) should be in place before the games begin and maintained during the games for the time period June 1, 2028 to September 1, 2028. Standard:</p>
<p>16 Where used on roadways in GRN, the LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings shall be removed after September 1, 2028 or the end of the games and the roadway restored to the condition of previous markings.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3A.02/">Section 3A.02</a> Materials The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>04 The chromaticity coordinates that define the ranges of acceptable colors, including the color white, for traffic control devices are found in the Appendix to Subpart F of 23 CFR 655. Standard:</p>
<p>05 The material used for the white color legend for the LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings shall be retroreflective, as these pavement markings are traffic control devices under this Caltrans experimentation request specific application. Guidance:</p>
<p>06 The materials used for LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings should provide the white color through their temporary use period from June 1, 2028 to September 1, 2028.</p>
<p>07 Consideration should be given to selecting pavement marking materials that will minimize tripping or loss of traction for road users, including pedestrians, bicyclists, and motorcyclists.</p></div>

### Page 70 - 16 If used, the LA28 word pavement marking shall be in a single line.

<div class="pmeta">MUTCD: <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1A.05/">Section 1A.05</a> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3B.21/">Section 3B.21</a> &middot; Topics: parking-curb, signs</div>
<div class="dtext"><p>CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement Page 70</p>
<p>Chapter 3B - Pavement and Curb Markings July 15, 2026 Part 3 Markings</p>
<p>CHAPTER 3B. PAVEMENT AND CURB MARKINGS</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3B.21/">Section 3B.21</a> Word Pavement Markings The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>12 The LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings are used to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games. These LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings can be helpful to road users in some locations by supplementing signs and providing additional emphasis, because the markings do not require diversion of the road user’s attention from the roadway surface. Option:</p>
<p>13 The LA28 word pavement markings may be used as determined by engineering judgment to supplement signs and/or to provide emphasis for regulatory and guide signs that have been modified by using the LA28 sign plaque or sign panel. The LA28 word pavement marking may be used alone, as a single line of information.</p>
<p>14 The PERMIT ONLY word pavement marking may be used in conjunction with the LA28 word pavement markings, as determined by engineering judgment to supplement signs and/or to provide additional emphasis for regulatory and guide signs that have been modified by using the LA28 sign plaque or sign panel. Standard:</p>
<p>15 The PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings shall not be used alone, it shall always be used in conjunction with the LA28 word pavement markings.</p>
<h4>16 If used, the LA28 word pavement marking shall be in a single line.</h4>
<p>17 If PERMIT ONLY word pavement marking is used in conjunction with the LA28 word pavement marking, it shall consist of three lines of information. Each word of the information, LA28, PERMIT and ONLY, shall constitute as a single separate line. These three lines of information shall read as LA28 PERMIT ONLY in the direction of travel, with the first word LA28, nearest to the road user (see Figure 3B-107(LA28)).</p>
<p>18 Detailed drawings of the LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings and alphabet details are shown in Figure 3B- 108(LA28) and the “Standard Highway Signs and Markings” book. CA MUTCD 2026 <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1A.05/">Section 1A.05</a> contains information regarding how to obtain this publication. Standard:</p>
<h4>19 If used, the LA28 word pavement marking shall be white legend.</h4>
<h4>20 If used, the PERMIT ONLY word pavement marking shall be white legend.</h4>
<p>21 If used, the PERMIT ONLY word pavement marking shall not be used alone, it shall be used in conjunction with the LA28 word pavement marking.</p>
<p>22 The LA28 and PERMIT ONLY pavement marking letters and numerals shall be installed in accordance with the design details in the Pavement Markings chapter of the “Standard Highway Signs and Markings” book (see CA MUTCD 2026 <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1A.05/">Section 1A.05</a>). Option:</p>
<p>23 The LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings may be proportionally scaled to fit within the width of the facility upon which they are applied. Guidance:</p>
<p>24 If used, each of the LA28, PERMIT and ONLY word pavement marking used on freeway and arterial streets segments in the GRN, should be spaced 50 feet apart, as shown in Figure 3B-107(LA28) Part B.</p>
<p>25 If used, each of the LA28, PERMIT and ONLY word pavement marking used on continuous lane segments in the GRN, should be spaced 250 feet apart, as shown in Figure 3B-108(LA28) Part C.</p>
<p>26 When used, the LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings letter heights should be as shown in Figure 3B-108(LA28).</p></div>

<p>Chapter 3E - Preferential Lane Markings for Motor Vehicles July 15, 2026 Part 3 Markings</p>
<p>CHAPTER 3E. PREFERENTIAL LANE MARKINGS FOR MOTOR VEHICLES</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3E.02/">Section 3E.02</a> Longitudinal Markings The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>16 Using longitudinal markings that prohibit crossing between a GRN lane and the adjacent general-purpose lane triggers use of multiple overhead signs. Using a single solid white line, which discourages but does not prohibit crossing, between a GRN lane and the adjacent general-purpose lane (see Figure 3E-3) reduces the amount of required overhead signs. Refer to Chapter 2G for sign requirements.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3E.03/">Section 3E.03</a> Preferential Lane Word and Symbol Markings The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>15 Specific preferential lanes will be used to designate Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues on roadways that are included in the established GRN during LA28 in locations specified by the supplement. Specific preferential lanes that are used as dedicated lane(s) for Olympic Games vehicles to travel between venues and included in the established GRN, are referred to as “GRN lane(s)” in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement. Standard:</p>
<p>16 The provisions of Chapter 3E for preferential lane word and symbol markings shall apply to all roadways included in the established GRN in locations specified by Caltrans experimentation request to FHWA.</p>
<p>17 When certain types of vehicles (such as without LA28 permit) are prohibited from using a GRN lane or when a GRN lane is restricted to use by only certain types of vehicles (such as with a LA28 permit) as an operational strategy, the LA28 word pavement marking alone, or in conjunction with the PERMIT ONLY word pavement marking shall be used to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games. Option:</p>
<p>18 The LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings may be omitted at toll plazas where physical conditions preclude the use of the markings. Guidance:</p>
<p>19 The location for the LA28, and if used, PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings should be based on engineering judgment that considers the prevailing speed, block lengths, distance from intersections, and other factors that affect clear communication to the road user.</p>
<p>20 If used, the spacing interval shown in Figure 3B-108(LA28) should be used based on the roadway classification.</p>
<p>21 The LA28, and if used, PERMIT ONLY word pavement markings should be placed on roadways in the established GRN at strategic locations such as major decision points, departures from the GRN lane, and along access to and from adjacent general- purpose lanes.</p></div>
