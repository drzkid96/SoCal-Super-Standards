<h4>PART 2</h4>
<h4>SIGNS</h4>
<p>CHAPTER 2A. GENERAL</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.01/">Section 2A.01</a> Function and Purpose of Signs The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>05 The provisions of Part 2 regarding signs in general (Chapter 2A), regulatory signs (Chapters 2B and 2G), guide signs (Chapters 2D, 2E and 2G) and changeable message signs (Chapter 2L) shall apply to all roadways included in the established GRN in locations specified by Caltrans experimentation request to FHWA.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.02/">Section 2A.02</a> Standardization of Application The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Guidance:</p>
<p>07 The LA28 sign plaque and LA28 sign panel should be used only where deemed necessary or desirable and justified by engineering judgment, as provided in Sections 1D.03 and 1C.02 (Paragraph 3, Definition 76). Option:</p>
<p>08 The LA28 sign plaque or the LA28 sign panel (see Figure 2A-101(LA28)) may be used on any existing or new regulatory or guide sign included in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement, where engineering judgment determines the need to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games.</p>
<p>09 If used, the LA28 sign plaque or the LA28 sign panel may be combined with the word message PERMIT ONLY in black legend on a white background at some locations, where engineering judgment determines it is needed. Standard:</p>
<p>10 If the Transportation Demand Management (TDM) strategies implemented in the established GRN, require regulatory or operational measures different than those existing, the existing permanent regulatory and guide signs that are no longer applicable, and potentially could be confusing or misleading, they shall be covered or removed and superseded by the appropriate temporary regulatory or guide signs during the games.</p>
<p>11 The LA28 sign plaque and the LA28 sign panel shall be rectangular and shall consist of the word message “LA28” (refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.06/">Section 2A.06</a> for legend and background colors). The LA28 legend shall be a single line for horizontal configuration. The LA28 legend for the vertical configuration shall be 2 lines, with LA legend in line 1 and 28 legend in line 2.</p>
<p>12 If used, the LA28 sign plaque shall be added as a header plaque above any existing or new regulatory, warning, or guide sign included in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement, to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games. If using the LA28 sign header plaque in the vertical configuration, it shall be placed on the left edge of the sign.</p>
<p>13 If used, the LA28 sign panel shall be used as a header panel and affixed to the face of a sign to overlay a portion of any existing or new regulatory, warning, or guide sign included in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement, to identify dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games. If using the LA28 sign header panel in the horizontal configuration, it shall be placed in the top portion of the sign. If using the LA28 sign header panel in the vertical configuration, it shall be placed on the left edge of the sign. Option:</p>
<p>14 The LA28 sign panel may be used within an existing or new sign where engineering judgment determines the panel is needed.</p>
<h4>15 The LA28 sign plaque and LA28 sign panel, each shall not be used alone. Support:</h4>
<p>16 If signs using the LA28 sign plaque or LA28 sign panel need to be installed at new locations to identify GRN dedicated lanes during LA28 games, refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.13/">Section 2A.13</a> in current CA MUTCD 2026.</p></div>

<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.04/">Section 2A.04</a> Design of Signs The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>02a In the provisions for the individual regulatory or guide signs included in this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement, the general appearance of the legend, color, and size are shown in the CA MUTCD 2026 tables and illustrations and the sign specifications and are not always detailed in the text.</p>
<p>03a Detailed drawings of these standard regulatory and guide signs, alphabets, symbols, and arrows (see CA MUTCD 2026 Figure 2D-3) are shown in the “Standard Highway Signs and Markings” book. CA MUTCD 2026 <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1A.05/">Section 1A.05</a> contains information regarding how to obtain this publication. Standard:</p>
<p>06a The term legend shall include LA28 and PERMIT ONLY word messages for use on the sign plaque and as part of the sign panel to convey specific meaning.</p>
<p>07a Uniformity in design of the LA28 and PERMIT ONLY sign plaque and sign panel shall include shape, color, dimensions, legends, borders, and illumination or retroreflectivity.</p>
<p>08a Standardization of these sign plaque and sign panel does not preclude further improvement by minor changes in the proportion, width of borders, or layout of word messages, but all shapes and colors shall be as indicated. Support:</p>
<p>09a The daytime color specification limits and luminance factors (percent) for retroreflective material of the light blue color can be determined from Tables 1 and 1a of Appendix to 23 CFR Part 655, Subpart F. Caltrans and 3M conducted testing to establish a range of light blue color that will be used in this experiment. The trial test was initiated by developing samples of light blue color using FHWA daytime chromaticity coordinates. These samples were tested to measure their nighttime chromaticity coordinates. Refer to Table 2A-101(LA28) for daytime and nighttime chromaticity coordinates for light blue color. These coordinates will be verified using field and lab testing to ensure the light blue color is visible at night with comparable characteristics as during the daytime. Luminance is not defined for Type XI sheeting; cap Y values between 10 and 19 was recommended by 3M.</p>
<p>09b Caltrans plans to engage Caltrans Headquarters Division of Research and Information Systems and Headquarters Transportation Laboratory to conduct an off-roadway, closed-course, or laboratory evaluation and study. This evaluation study will test the use of black and white LA28 legend on the Caltrans identified light blue background color. The study will evaluate which color combination provides better contrast, when used for LA28 Sign Plaque and Sign Panel. This evaluation and testing will be the second initial step within this experimentation request, in advance of the actual deployment under this experimentation request of the sign plaques and sign panels during the games.</p>
<p>09c Any field testing, if needed, will be conducted within Caltrans Maintenance Yard or Caltrans facility, to determine the appropriate legend and background color combination, that provides better contrast for the LA28 word message, when used on sign plaques and sign panels. The decision to use legend color, black or white, for LA28 word message on the light blue colored background will be made in advance of the games and not during the games to avoid road user confusion and related safety concerns during the games. Only one color, either black or white, will be used as the legend color in the actual experiment during the games.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.05/">Section 2A.05</a> Shapes The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>10 The standard shapes and standard proportions of existing regulatory and guide sign that are temporarily changed during LA28, shall be retained as much as practical, and comply with Table 2A-1.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.06/">Section 2A.06</a> Colors The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>09 The light blue color to be used as background color on LA28 sign plaque and LA28 sign panel, the white or black "LA28" word legend, and their specific use on these sign plaques and sign panels shall be as provided in the applicable Sections of this CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement. Where required by the CA MUTCD 2026, the LA28 plaque and LA28 sign panel shall use the standard color scheme applicable to the sign category or policy under which they are used. Support:</p>
<p>10 Tolerance limits for light blue color are contained in 23 CFR Part 655, Appendix to Subpart F which is available at the Federal Highway Administration’s MUTCD Web site at http://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov. Nighttime color specification limits for retroreflective material of the light blue color can be determined using ASTM test methods included in 23 CFR Part 655.</p></div>

<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.07/">Section 2A.07</a> Dimensions The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Guidance:</p>
<p>11 When LA28 sign plaque or LA28 sign panel are installed with larger sized signs, a corresponding increase in the size of the plaque or the panel and its legend should also be made. The resulting plaque or panel size should be approximately in the same relative proportion to the larger-sized sign as the conventional sized plaque or panel is to the conventional-sized sign. <p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.08/">Section 2A.08</a> Word Messages The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>20 Except as provided in Paragraph 21, the LA28 sign plaque and LA28 sign panel shall include only the legend “LA28” as a word message. Option:</p>
<p>21 The LA28 sign plaque or the LA28 sign panel may be combined with the word message PERMIT ONLY where engineering judgment determines it is needed. Standard:</p>
<p>22 The LA28 and PERMIT ONLY lettering used on sign plaque and sign panel shall be in upper-case letters and shall be of the Standard Alphabets as provided in the “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/1A.05/">Section 1A.05</a>). The unique letter forms used shall not be stretched, compressed, warped, or otherwise manipulated. Support:</p>
<p>23 <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2D.03/">Section 2D.03</a> contains information regarding the acceptable methods of modifying the length of a word for a given letter height and series for guide signs.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.09/">Section 2A.09</a> Symbols The following is added to this section:</p>
<h4>Standard:</h4>
<p>01a The LA28 Sign Plaque and Sign Panel shall not include any Olympic games, Para-Olympics symbol or any other symbol. The Olympic rings symbol shall not be used on, as part of, or in conjunction with the LA28 Sign Plaque or Sign Panel as its use is prohibited under the copyright laws. Support:</p>
<p>12 The Olympic rings represent only one of the two games that Los Angeles will be hosting during the time period covered under Caltrans experimentation request. Los Angeles will also be hosting the Paralympic Games. The International Paralympic Committee (IPC) is a completely separate organization with a different name, logo, and organizing body.</p>
<p><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2A.11/">Section 2A.11</a> Enhanced Conspicuity for Standard Signs The following is added to this section:</p>
<p>Support:</p>
<p>07 This CA MUTCD 2026 LA28 Supplement is issued in support of Caltrans request for experimentation to FHWA is specifically for the use of light blue color as the background color for the “LA28” word message. The purpose of using light blue color as a background color for LA28 word message, is as a unique identifier to draw attention to the traffic sign plaques and traffic sign panels, and to aid recognition and quicker comprehension of the dedicated lanes on GRN roadways to facilitate travel during LA28 games.</p>

To save costs and expedite implementation, since the change is needed temporarily, opportunities to use the LA28 sign plaque or LA28 sign panel with the existing traffic signs should be considered, if signing is needed.</p>
