<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena layout for a two-way left turn lane marked with a single yellow center line and solid white lane lines. The plan shows lane widths of 3.6 m (12 ft) and 11.0 m (36 ft) segments, a 7.3 m (24 ft) center line dash pattern, and lead-in white lane line lengths of 21.9 m (72 ft) minimum to 29.3 m (96 ft) maximum. If a left turn pocket is included, the lead-in white lane line length equals the pocket length unless otherwise specified. Raised pavement markers, if required, follow S-710 Details 10, 11 and 12.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Two-way left turn lane (yellow). Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>Lane widths 3.6 m (12 ft), 11.0 m (36 ft), 3.6 m (12 ft), 11.0 m (36 ft), 3.6 m (12 ft). Yellow line width 76 mm (3 in).</p>
<p>Single yellow center line: 7.3 m (24 ft) pattern, with 2.1 m (7 ft) and 5.2 m (17 ft) dimensions, 21.9 m (72 ft) minimum to 29.3 m (96 ft) maximum.</p>
<p>White lane line: 14.6 m (48 ft) minimum lead-in with 2.1 m (7 ft), 5.2 m (17 ft) and 7.3 m (24 ft) dimensions, 15.2 m (50 ft).</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. If left turn pocket is included, the length of the lead-in solid white lane line shall equal the length of the left turn pocket unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. If raised pavement markers are required, see Standard Plan S-710, Details 10, 11 and 12.</p></details>
