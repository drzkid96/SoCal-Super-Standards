<p class="dwg-sum">Pasadena layout for a two-way left turn lane marked with a single yellow center line and solid white lane lines. The plan shows lane widths of 3.6 m (12 ft) and 11.0 m (36 ft) segments, a 7.3 m (24 ft) center line dash pattern, and lead-in white lane line lengths of 21.9 m (72 ft) minimum to 29.3 m (96 ft) maximum. If a left turn pocket is included, the lead-in white lane line length equals the pocket length unless otherwise specified. Raised pavement markers, if required, follow S-710 Details 10, 11 and 12.</p>

<p>Single yellow center line: 7.3 m (24 ft) pattern, with 2.1 m (7 ft) and 5.2 m (17 ft) dimensions, 21.9 m (72 ft) minimum to 29.3 m (96 ft) maximum.</p>
<p>White lane line: 14.6 m (48 ft) minimum lead-in with 2.1 m (7 ft), 5.2 m (17 ft) and 7.3 m (24 ft) dimensions, 15.2 m (50 ft).</p>
<p>Notes</p>
