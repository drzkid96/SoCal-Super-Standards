<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena layout for a two-way left turn lane marked with 76 mm (3 in) yellow lines, with 3.6 m (12 ft) dashes and 11.0 m (36 ft) gaps, plus single yellow center line and white lane line patterns. Dashed lines use a 7.3 m (24 ft) cycle with 2.1 m (7 ft) dashes, leading into solid yellow lead-ins of 21.9 m (72 ft) to 29.3 m (96 ft) and solid white lead-ins of 14.6 m (48 ft) to 21.9 m (72 ft). If a left turn pocket is included, the white lead-in equals the pocket length, and raised pavement markers follow S-710 Details 10, 11 and 12.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Two-way left turn lane (yellow), plan view: Two solid yellow edge lines, each 76 mm (3&quot;) wide, run along the top and bottom of the lane. Inside each solid line is a broken yellow line made of 3.6 m (12&#x27;) dashes separated by 11.0 m (36&#x27;) gaps. The dashes on the upper and lower lines line up with each other, and two dashes are shown per line. Dimension labels read 3.6 m (12&#x27;), 11.0 m (36&#x27;) and 3.6 m (12&#x27;).</p>
<p>Single yellow center line, plan view: A two-way approach to an intersection with a curb return and a cross street on the right, with arrows showing both directions of travel. The yellow center line is dashed in a repeating 7.3 m (24&#x27;) cycle made of a 2.1 m (7&#x27;) dash and a 5.2 m (17&#x27;) gap. It becomes a solid yellow lead-in line running 21.9 m (72&#x27;) MIN. to 29.3 m (96&#x27;) MAX. back from the intersection.</p>
<p>White lane line, plan view: A similar approach with a left turn pocket, showing arrows for both directions. The white lane line is dashed with 2.1 m (7&#x27;) dashes, 5.2 m (17&#x27;) gaps and a 7.3 m (24&#x27;) cycle. A solid white lead-in of 14.6 m (48&#x27;) MIN. to 21.9 m (72&#x27;) MAX. runs back from the intersection on the left side. On the right side a solid white lead-in of 15.2 m (50&#x27;) is dimensioned, followed by a 5.2 m (17&#x27;) gap and a 2.1 m (7&#x27;) dash.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Two-Way Left Turn Lane, Single Yellow Centerline, Solid White Lane Line, Standard Plan S-711, Sheet 1 of 1, Revision 1, units DUAL, file S711-1-REV1.DWG 5/24/01, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 6/4/01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. If left turn pocket is included, the length of the lead-in solid white lane line shall equal the length of the left turn pocket unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. If raised pavement markers are required, see Standard Plan S-710, Details 10, 11, and 12.</p>
