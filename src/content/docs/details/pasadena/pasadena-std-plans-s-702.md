<p class="dwg-sum">Layout of a turn bay transition where a single centerline shifts into a double centerline. The transition is 45 m (150 ft) long, with a 27 m (90 ft) straight 30:1 taper from point A to point B, followed by curves that offset the line 1.5 m (5 ft) on one side and 3 m (10 ft) on the other. A six-step layout procedure using points A to J gives the striping geometry.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Dimensions (metric with US customary in parentheses):</h5>
<p>Start of transition to end of transition: 45 m (150&#x27;).</p>
<p>30:1 taper from point A to point B: 27 m (90&#x27;).</p>
<p>Then segments of 3 m (10&#x27;), 3 m (10&#x27;), 6 m (20&#x27;), 3 m (10&#x27;), 3 m (10&#x27;).</p>
<p>Offset from centerline of street: 1.5 m (5&#x27;) on one side and 3 m (10&#x27;) on the other.</p>
<p>Points A to J are labeled along the centerline of street.</p>
<p>Procedure to install transition</p>
<p>1. Layout points A, D, G, &amp; H.</p>
<p>2. Use points A &amp; D to determine a straight line and mark B &amp; C.</p>
<p>3. Use points C &amp; G to determine a straight line and mark E &amp; F.</p>
<p>4. Mark J by measuring 180 mm (7&quot;) in from C. Mark I by measuring 150 mm (6&quot;) in from G.</p>
<p>5. Form curves B-J-E &amp; F-I-H.</p>
<p>6. Stripe.</p></details>
