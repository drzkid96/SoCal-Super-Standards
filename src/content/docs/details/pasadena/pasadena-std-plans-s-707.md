<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard for installing 3.5 inch asphalt speed humps and speed cushions, with installation notes, warning sign and temporary signing requirements. Separate sheets give hump layouts for streets under 50 ft wide and 50 ft or wider, and cushion layouts for streets 28 ft to 38 ft and 38 ft and greater. Humps are 12 ft long in the direction of travel with a 3.5 inch nominal height, built of Type D2 PG 64-10 asphalt, and marked with thermoplastic. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> SPEED HUMP sign and a flashing barricade with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> and W13-1 signs are required until the pavement markings are in place.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Installation details box: A boxed list titled INSTALLATION DETAILS with seven numbered requirements for speed humps (see notes).</p>
<p>Figure A, speed hump warning sign (typical): A drawing of a diamond-shaped warning sign reading SPEED HUMP with a pair of warning flags mounted on its upper corners. Below it on the same post is a small rectangular plaque reading 15 MPH, labeled W13-1(15) SIGN. Callouts: WARNING FLAGS (FIRST 30-DAYS), 30in X 30in, BLACK ON YELLOW OR <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> &quot;SPEED HUMP&quot;, 6 in SERIES E LETTERS, and SIGN LOCATIONS: AS DIRECTED BY ENGINEER.</p>
<p>Temporary signage details box: A boxed list titled TEMPORARY SIGNAGE DETAILS with two numbered requirements for flashing barricades and signs during construction.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Department of Transportation, Speed Hump/Cushion Installation Channelization/Temporary Signing Requirements, Standard Plan S-707, Sheet 1 of 5, Revision 10 (April 2023), units IMPERIAL, approved by the Deputy Director of Transportation on 04/19/2023.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Installation details:</h5>
<p>1. Speed humps shall not be placed over manholes, watergates, junction chambers, etc.</p>
<p>2. Edge of speed hump should be 10 ft minimum from edge and uphill of driveway.</p>
<p>3. Height of speed hump shall nominally be 3.5 in +/- 0.25 in at all cross sections.</p>
<p>4. Whenever possible, speed humps shall be placed at property lines instead of mid-lot.</p>
<p>5. Whenever possible, speed humps shall be placed adjacent to street lights.</p>
<p>6. Speed humps shall be marked using thermoplastic material.</p>
<p>7. Speed hump signs shall be installed per Figure A, where noted on plans.</p>
<p>Figure A: Speed hump warning sign (typical). Warning flags (first 30 days). 30 in x 30 in. Black on yellow or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> SPEED HUMP, 6 in Series E letters. W13-1(15) sign. Sign locations: as directed by Engineer.</p>
<h5>Temporary signage details:</h5>
<p>1. The contractor shall furnish and maintain a flashing barricade, 50 feet from each speed hump location in both directions, until the first application of paint has been applied showing the pavement messages and striping.</p>
<p>2. Mount 30 in x 30 in <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> warning sign (black on yellow, 6 in Series E letters) and W13-1 sign per MUTCD with the California Supplement, on each of the barricades at each speed hump location.</p></details>
