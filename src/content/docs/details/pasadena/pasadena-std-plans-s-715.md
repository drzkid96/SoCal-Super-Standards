<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Drive sign post anchor into ground approximately 1.0 m (3 ft). Leave one hole exposed above ground for rivet connection.</p>
<p>2. Drive in anchor sleeve which is one size larger than post anchor and 455 mm (18 in) long.</p>
<p>3. Insert sign post and fasten in place.</p>
<p>4. 2.1 m (7 ft) minimum clearance to bottom of the lowest sign.</p>
<p>Labels: Drive rivet (typical). 0 mm to 15 mm (0 in to 1/2 in). Clearance from curb face: 455 mm (18 in) desirable, 305 mm (12 in) minimum. 51 mm (2 in) sq. O.D. (12 gage) sign post. 4 holes in sign post only. Two (2) holes at 90 degrees in anchor sleeve. Two (2) drive rivets at 90 degrees. 57 mm (2 1/4 in) sq. O.D. (12 gage) anchor sleeve. 63 mm (2 1/2 in) sq. O.D. (12 gage) anchor sleeve. 150 mm (6 in) dimension at ground line. Ground surface.</p></details>
