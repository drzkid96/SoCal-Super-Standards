<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena detail for extending an existing U-channel post and for strap mounting signs on an existing street light or other pole. Hardware includes a galvanized steel post extension with 8 mm (5/16 in) square head bolts, a stainless steel strap, and a galvanized steel mounting bracket with a neoprene washer. Clearance from the ground to the bottom of the lowest sign must be 2.1 m (7 ft).</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Post extension and sign mounting. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>Strap mounting on existing street light or other pole: sign, 5 1/8 in dimension, 3 mm (1/8 in) fiber washer, 2 each per sign. Galvanized steel bracket with 8 mm (5/16 in) tapped hole, stainless steel button head bolt and square nut, neoprene washer. Stainless steel strap, 16 mm x 0.75 mm (5/8 in x 0.03 in).</p>
<p>Post extension: existing galvanized steel U-channel post, U-channel post extension, 8 mm (5/16 in) square head bolt with square nut and washer.</p>
<p>Note: Clearance from the ground to the bottom of the lowest sign shall be 2.1 m (7 ft).</p></details>
