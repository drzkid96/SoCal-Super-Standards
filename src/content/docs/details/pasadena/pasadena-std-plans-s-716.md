<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard plan S-716 shows how to extend an existing U-channel sign post with a lapped section and how to strap-mount a sign to an existing street light or other pole. Hardware is galvanized steel bolts, a 5 mm spacer with fiber washer, and a stainless steel strap with neoprene-washer bracket. Clearance from the ground to the bottom of the lowest sign must be 2.1 m (7 ft).</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet S-716, City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department standard plan, revision 1, units DUAL (metric with US customary in parentheses), sheet 1 of 1, dated 7-23-01. It has two isometric details, a U-channel post extension on the left and a strap mounting on an existing street light or other pole on the right.</p>
<p>U-channel post extension: An existing U-channel post is shown with a short U-channel post section lapped onto it, with a sign mounted on the face of the post. The added section is 1.0 m (3&#x27;) or 1.2 m (4&#x27;) long, as specified, and its lower end overlaps the existing post by a minimum of 0.3 m (1&#x27;), with overlap as needed for sign height. Bolt holes in the lapped section are spaced at 205 mm (8&quot;) minimum between the bolts that join the two posts. Each connection uses a galvanized steel 8 mm (5/16&quot;) square head bolt with square washer (the label also shows square nut). The sign is held off the post by a 5 mm (1/8&quot;) spacer with fiber washer, 2 each sign, and is attached with galvanized steel 8 mm (5/16&quot;) button head bolts and square nuts. Labels: SIGN, EXISTING U-CHANNEL POST. The detail is titled U-CHANNEL POST EXTENSION.</p>
<p>Strap mounting, existing street light or other pole: An isometric view of a round pole with a sign mounted to it using two strapped-on brackets, one near the top and one near the bottom of the sign. Each bracket is a galvanized steel strapped-on bracket with an 8 mm (5/16&quot;) tapped hole, a stainless steel strap, and a neoprene washer. The strap is a 16 mm x 0.75 mm (5/8&quot; x 0.03&quot;) stainless steel strap with a buckle. A bolt with round washer, shown on a dashed line, passes through the sign into each bracket. Label: SIGN. The detail is titled STRAP MOUNTING, EXISTING STREET LIGHT OR OTHER POLE.</p>
<p>Note at bottom left: clearance from the ground to the bottom of the lowest sign is 2.1 m (7&#x27;).</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Note: Clearance from the ground to the bottom of the lowest sign shall be 2.1 m (7&#x27;).</p>
<p>Callouts: Section as specified 1.0 m (3&#x27;) or 1.2 m (4&#x27;). 205 mm (8&quot;) minimum (MIN.). 0.3 m (1&#x27;) MIN. overlap as needed for sign height. 5 mm (1/8&quot;) spacer with fiber washer, 2 each (EA.) sign. Galvanized steel 8 mm (5/16&quot;) button head bolt and square nut. Galvanized steel 8 mm (5/16&quot;) square head bolt, square washer. Galvanized steel strapped-on bracket with 8 mm (5/16&quot;) tapped hole, stainless steel bracket, and neoprene washer. 16 mm x 0.75 mm (5/8&quot; x 0.03&quot;) stainless steel strap.</p></details>
