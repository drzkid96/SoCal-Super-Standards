<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard plan S-717 shows replacement of a 760 mm (30 in) R1R STOP sign on a 3.0 m (10 ft) galvanized square tube post that slips into a 610 mm (24 in) anchor tube set in a concrete-filled hole. The sign bottom is 2.1 m (7 ft) above grade and the tubes are joined with 9.5 mm (3/8 in) drive rivets. Two notes cover backfill or sidewalk at the surface and concrete requirements.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet S-717, City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department standard plan, revision 1, units DUAL, sheet 1 of 1, dated 7-23-01. One main elevation with an enlarged detail and Section A-A.</p>
<p>Elevation: A 760 mm (2&#x27;-6&quot;) high intensity R1R STOP sign (octagon) is mounted on a galvanized 65 mm (2 1/2&quot;) O.D. square tube (12 gauge), 3.0 m (10&#x27;) long, ASTM-A446. The tube top extends 50 mm (2&quot;) above the top of the sign. Two rivet points are shown on the post centerline, one near the top and one near the bottom of the sign, each located 75 mm (3&quot;) from the sign edge. The bottom of the sign is 2.1 m (7&#x27;) above the ground line. The post slides into a galvanized 75 mm (3&quot;) O.D. square anchor tube [wall thickness 5 mm (3/16&quot;)], 610 mm (24&quot;) long, ASTM-A500 Grade B. A 1.0 m (3&#x27;) dimension runs horizontally from the post to the back of the adjacent curb and sidewalk shown at left. The curb section drawn at left shows a depth of 560 mm (1&#x27;-10&quot;) below grade, with a 50 mm (2&quot;) dimension at the base of the sidewalk. Note 1 marks the surface at the post, with a sloped concrete sidewalk edge.</p>
<p>Ground detail: The anchor tube is set in a concrete-filled hole. The hole is 305 mm (12&quot;) minimum to 455 mm (18&quot;) maximum wide, surrounded by tamped backfill (typical). Concrete (note 2) fills the hole. The anchor tube extends 510 mm (20&quot;) minimum to 610 mm (24&quot;) maximum below grade and its end is plugged to exclude concrete. A 100 mm (4&quot;) dimension shows the top of the anchor tube above grade, with a SLOPE symbol on the sidewalk.</p>
<p>Enlarged detail (circle): The sign post (cut away to show a wavy section) enters the top of the anchor tube. The post projects 25 mm (1&quot;) above the top of the anchor tube in the drawn view. Two holes are on adjacent faces of the anchor tube, 50 mm (2&quot;) below its top, and the anchor tube is shown with a 150 mm (6&quot;) dimension. Label: 11 mm (7/16&quot;) dia. hole in each tube for 9.5 mm (3/8&quot;) drive rivet.</p>
<p>Section A-A: A plan cut through the sign and post showing the square tube wall with a flat sign face against it, joined by an 11 mm (7/16&quot;) hole with a 9.5 mm (3/8&quot;) rivet.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Consolidated backfill or concrete sidewalk, slope to drain.</p>
<p>2. Concrete may be premixed, sacked concrete or field proportioned cement and aggregate with water added and mixing occurring on the job site. 2% calcium chloride (by weight of cement) may be added to produce a quickset concrete. Concrete shall comply with &quot;Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction,&quot; latest edition.</p>
<p>Callouts: 760 mm (30&quot;) high intensity R1R. 11 mm (7/16&quot;) hole, 9.5 mm (3/8&quot;) rivet. Galvanized 65 mm (2 1/2&quot;) O.D. square tube (12 gauge), 3.0 m (10&#x27;) long, ASTM-A446. Galvanized 75 mm (3&quot;) O.D. square anchor tube [wall thickness 5 mm (3/16&quot;)], 610 mm (24&quot;) long, ASTM-A500 Grade B. 11 mm (7/16&quot;) dia. hole in each tube for 9.5 mm (3/8&quot;) drive rivet. Plug end of anchor tube to exclude concrete. Tamped backfill (typical).</p></details>
