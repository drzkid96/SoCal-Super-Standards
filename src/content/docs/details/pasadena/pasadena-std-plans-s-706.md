<p class="dwg-sum">Pavement word message layout for pedestrian crossings. Condition A is a centerline-only street and Condition B is multi-lane. The messages PED and XING are each 2.4 m (8 ft) tall and spaced 8 m (25 ft) minimum apart, working back from the crosswalk. Messages are centered 1.7 m (5.5 ft) off the marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12 ft) or greater, and centered in the lane for narrower lanes.</p>
