<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pavement word message layout for pedestrian crossings. Condition A is a centerline-only street and Condition B is multi-lane. The messages XING and PED are each 2.4 m (8 ft) tall and spaced 8 m (25 ft) minimum apart, working back from the crosswalk. Messages are centered 1.7 m (5.5 ft) off the marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12 ft) or greater, and centered in the lane for narrower lanes.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Condition A, plan view (centerline only, marked or unmarked): A street between two edge lines with a centerline (CL). Two heavy horizontal lines labeled CROSSWALK cross the full width. Working back from the crosswalk, the words XING and PED are stacked along a line labeled CENTERLINE OF MESSAGE (*). The distance from the crosswalk to XING is VARIES. Each word is 2.4 m (8&#x27;) tall, and the clear spacing between XING and PED is 8 m (25&#x27;) (MIN.). The message centerline is 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) from the street centerline.</p>
<p>Condition B, plan view (multi-lane): The same CROSSWALK crosses a multi-lane street with a double centerline (CL) and lane lines that are solid near the crosswalk and dashed farther back. XING and PED are repeated in each of the two lanes on one side of the double centerline. The distance from the crosswalk to XING is VARIES, each word is 2.4 m (8&#x27;) tall, and the spacing between XING and PED is 8 m (25&#x27;) (MIN.). In the lane next to the centerline the callout reads CENTER WORD MESSAGE IN LANE. In the outer lane the message centerline (*) is dimensioned 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) off the lane line.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Pedestrian Crossing Pavement Markings, Standard Plan S-706, Sheet 1 of 1, Revision 2, units DUAL, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 6/4/01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Condition A: centerline only (marked or unmarked).</p>
<p>2. Condition B: multi-lane.</p>
<p>3. Word messages, working back from the crosswalk: XING, PED. The distance from the crosswalk to XING varies.</p>
<p>4. Each word message is 2.4 m (8&#x27;) tall, with 8 m (25&#x27;) minimum (MIN.) between XING and PED.</p>
<p>5. Center word message in lane (multi-lane condition).</p>
<h5>Note:</h5>
<p>* Locate center of word message at a point 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) off marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12&#x27;) or greater. For lanes less than 3.7 m (12&#x27;), center message within lane.</p></details>
