<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Condition A: centerline only (marked or unmarked).</li>
<li value="2">Condition B: multi-lane.</li>
<li value="3">Word messages, working back from the crosswalk: XING, PED. The distance from the crosswalk to XING varies.</li>
<li value="4">Each word message is 2.4 m (8&#x27;) tall, with 8 m (25&#x27;) minimum (MIN.) between XING and PED.</li>
<li value="5">Center word message in lane (multi-lane condition).</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Note</h5>
<p>* Locate center of word message at a point 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) off marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12&#x27;) or greater. For lanes less than 3.7 m (12&#x27;), center message within lane.</p>
</details>
