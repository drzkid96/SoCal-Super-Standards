<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard for turn arrow pavement markings in left and right turn lanes. Arrows are solid white and centered in the lane, with the first arrow 2.4 m (8 ft) from the line at the intersection end. An additional arrow is installed if the pocket is 18.3 m (60 ft) or longer.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: An intersection approach is drawn with a cross street at the top, curb returns at both corners and a heavy double crosswalk or limit line across the approach. The approach has, from left to right, a double centerline that bends to the left to open a left turn pocket, a left turn lane with a solid white line on its right side and left turn arrows, a through lane with no arrows, and a right turn lane between a solid white line and the curb with right turn arrows. Each turn lane has two arrows. The arrow nearest the intersection in each lane is marked with an asterisk, and a dimension of 2.4 m (8&#x27;) is shown at each side from the line at the intersection end to that first arrow. The second arrow is placed farther back in the lane.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Pavement Markings for Left and Right Turn Lanes, Standard Plan S-712, Sheet 1 of 1, Revision 1, units DUAL, file S712-1-REV1.DWG 5/25/01, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 6/4/01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Arrow pavement markings to be solid white.</p>
<p>2. Pavement markings to be centered in lane.</p>
<p>* Install additional arrow if pocket is 18.3 m (60 ft) or longer.</p>
<p>Dimension: 2.4 m (8 ft) at each arrow.</p></details>
