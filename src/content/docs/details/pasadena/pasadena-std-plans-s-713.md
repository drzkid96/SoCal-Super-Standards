<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard for locating limit lines and STOP pavement markings. Where the property line or sidewalk is known, the limit line is a 300 mm (12 in) white stripe with the STOP legend 2.4 m (8 ft) behind it, installed only when authorized by the Traffic Engineer. Where it is unknown, the limit line goes anywhere in a hatched zone 1.2 m (4 ft) minimum to 9.1 m (30 ft) maximum back from the cross street curb line, set by work order, and extends to the street center line unless channelization requires otherwise.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Condition, known property line and/or sidewalk location, plan view: The corner of a street with a curb line, a curb return, a property line and a back line of sidewalk. A heavy 300 mm (12&quot;) white stripe is the limit line, labeled STANDARD LOCATION FOR LIMIT LINE UNLESS OTHERWISE SPECIFIED. It ends at the curb line. The word STOP is shown below (behind) the limit line, with a dimension of 2.4 m (8&#x27;) between the limit line and the top of STOP, and the callout INSTALL ONLY WHEN AUTHORIZED BY TRAFFIC ENGINEER points to the STOP legend. A dashed vertical line at the left is the street center line.</p>
<p>Condition, unknown property line or sidewalk location, plan view: The same corner with a cross-hatched rectangle marking the zone where the limit line may be placed, labeled LIMIT LINE LOCATION TO BE WITHIN THIS AREA (TYP.). The zone starts 1.2 m (4&#x27;) (MIN.) back from the cross street curb line and extends to 9.1 m (30&#x27;) (MAX.) back. Its width runs from the street center line (dashed line at the left) to the curb line at the right. The callout EXTEND LIMIT LINE TO STREET CENTER LINE UNLESS CHANNELIZATION REQUIRES OTHERWISE points to the center line side. The condition text reads UNKNOWN PROPERTY LINE OR SIDEWALK LOCATION. EXACT LOCATION TO BE SPECIFIED ON WORK ORDER.</p>
