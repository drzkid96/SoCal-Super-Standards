<p class="dwg-sum">Pasadena layout for striping a 3 m (10 ft) wide turn bay transition. A five-step procedure establishes points A through H, locating the straight lines and forming the two curves. The diagram is dimensioned for a 27 m (90 ft) transition, using 4.5 m (15 ft) and 9 m (30 ft) segments, with values in brackets for an 18 m (60 ft) transition using 3 m (10 ft) and 6 m (20 ft) segments.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Layout 3 m [10 ft] wide turn bay transition. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>Diagram: start of transition to end of transition, with segments of 4.5 m (15 ft), 4.5 m (15 ft), 9 m (30 ft), 4.5 m (15 ft), 4.5 m (15 ft). For the 18 m (60 ft) transition, use the figures in brackets: 3 m (10 ft), 3 m (10 ft), 6 m (20 ft), 3 m (10 ft), 3 m (10 ft).</p>
<p>Procedure to install transition</p>
<p>1. Lay out points A, B, E and F.</p>
<p>2. Use points B and E to determine a straight line and mark C and D.</p>
<p>3. Mark G and H by measuring in 190 mm (7 1/2 in) perpendicular to lines A-B and E-F (for 27 m (90 ft) and 18 m (60 ft) transitions).</p>
<p>4. Form curves A-G-C and D-H-F.</p>
<p>5. Stripe.</p>
<p>For 18 m (60 ft) transition, use figures in parentheses shown on diagram and proceed with steps 1 through 5.</p></details>
