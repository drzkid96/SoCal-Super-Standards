<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena layout for striping a 3 m (10 ft) wide turn bay transition. A five-step procedure establishes points A through H, locating the straight line through B and E and forming the curves A-G-C and D-H-F. The diagram is dimensioned for a 27 m (90 ft) transition, using 4.5 m (15 ft) and 9 m (30 ft) segments, with bracketed values for an 18 m (60 ft) transition using 3 m (10 ft) and 6 m (20 ft) segments.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Transition diagram, plan view: A turn bay transition is drawn between a lower baseline (the existing lane edge) and an upper line (the centerline or median line shown as a double line), 3 m (10&#x27;) apart at the end of the transition. Stations along the baseline are marked with vertical lines. From the START OF TRANSITION at point A, the dimensions to the right are 4.5 m (15&#x27;) to B, 4.5 m (15&#x27;) to C, 9 m (30&#x27;) to D, 4.5 m (15&#x27;) to E (and H), and 4.5 m (15&#x27;) to F at the END OF TRANSITION. Bracketed values for the 18 m (60&#x27;) transition are [3 m (10&#x27;)], [3 m (10&#x27;)], [6 m (20&#x27;)], [3 m (10&#x27;)] and [3 m (10&#x27;)]. Points B and E are marked with triangles and joined by a dashed straight construction line passing through C and D. The curve A-G-C leaves the baseline at A and passes through G, which is offset from line A-B. A heavy straight stripe runs from C to D. The curve D-H-F passes through H and meets the upper line at F. Points are labeled A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H.</p>
<p>Procedure to install transition: A numbered five-step procedure (see notes).</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Layout - 3 m [10&#x27;] Wide Turn Bay Transition, Standard Plan S-714, Sheet 1 of 1, Revision 1, units DUAL, file S714-1-REV1.DWG 5/24/01, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 6/4/01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Procedure to install transition:</h5>
<p>1. Lay out points A, B, E, and F.</p>
<p>2. Use points (triangle) B and E to determine a straight line and mark C and D.</p>
<p>3. Mark G and H by measuring in 190 mm (7 1/2 in) [for 27 m (90 ft) and 18 m (60 ft) transitions] perpendicular to lines A-B and E-F.</p>
<p>4. Form curves A-G-C and D-H-F.</p>
<p>5. Stripe.</p>
<p>Note: For 18 m (60 ft) transition, use figures in parentheses shown on diagram and proceed with steps 1 through 5. (The figures for the 18 m (60 ft) transition are drawn in square brackets on the diagram.)</p>
<p>Diagram labels: Start of transition. End of transition. 3 m (10 ft) transition width. Segments 4.5 m (15 ft), 4.5 m (15 ft), 9 m (30 ft), 4.5 m (15 ft), 4.5 m (15 ft) [3 m (10 ft), 3 m (10 ft), 6 m (20 ft), 3 m (10 ft), 3 m (10 ft)].</p></details>
