<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard layouts for marked crosswalks, covering the basic crosswalk, the zebra crosswalk and the continental crosswalk. All striping is 12 in white or yellow thermoplastic or similar material. Bar spacing starts from the street center line and avoids the wheel path, and bars are parallel to traffic flow at skewed or offset intersections. Continental bars are 2 ft wide and 14 ft long, and a 12 in white limit line is typically used only in advance of signalized intersections.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, basic crosswalk (sheet 1 of 3): A corner of an intersection is drawn with a curb return running from B.C. (beginning of curve) to E.C. (end of curve). The central angle is labeled phi, split by a bisecting radial line into two angles labeled phi/2. The curb face is shown as a heavy solid line with the edge of gutter as a dashed line outside it. The approach to the intersection runs along the top left, with the DOUBLE YELLOW CENTER LINE (DYCL) drawn as two yellow lines. The leaving side of the intersection runs down the right side, also with a DYCL. The crosswalk is formed by two heavy parallel lines, 12&#x27; apart, across each leg. The two lines turn through a 45 degree chamfer at the corner so that the crosswalk is one continuous L-shaped path around the curb return. A gray limit line (callout 2) runs from the DYCL to the curb face on the approach, 4&#x27; back from the near crosswalk line. A second 4&#x27; dimension is shown at the corner diagonal. The crosswalk lines on the approach leg and on the leaving leg are each marked with callout 1.</p>

### Sheet 2 of 3 (document page 11)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, zebra crosswalk (sheet 2 of 3): The same corner geometry as sheet 1, with a curb return from B.C. to E.C., the curb face as a heavy solid line and the edge of gutter dashed. The approach to the intersection runs along the top left with a double yellow center line (DYCL) and a gray limit line from the DYCL to the curb face. The leaving side of the intersection runs down the right with another DYCL. The crosswalk outline is drawn as thin double lines that follow both legs and turn through a 45 degree chamfer at the corner. Callout 2 points to this outline (crosswalk layout per sheet 1 of 3). Inside the outline, three heavy zebra bars (callout 1) are drawn parallel to the traffic flow on each leg. On the leaving leg the bars are dimensioned 3&#x27; apart. At the leaving side, a 1.5&#x27; MIN dimension is shown from the edge of gutter to the end of the crosswalk outline.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Zebra Crosswalk, Standard Plan S-709, Sheet 2 of 3, Revision 2, file S709-REV2.DWG 12/12/19, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 12/26/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. All striping shall be 12 in white or yellow, in thermoplastic or similar material. Spacing of crosswalk bars shall start from center line of street, avoiding wheel path. For intersections not perpendicular or 90 degrees (skewed or offset), all crosswalk (XWALK) bars shall be parallel to flow of traffic.</p>
<p>2. For crosswalk layout, refer to Sheet 1 of 3.</p>
<p>Labels: Double yellow center line (DYCL). Approach to intersection. Leaving side of intersection. Curb face. Edge of gutter. B.C. E.C. Dimensions: 3 ft (between zebra bars), 1.5 ft minimum (MIN) at the leaving side.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 3 (document page 12)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0012.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0012.webp" alt="S-709 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Pasadena Std. Plans S-709, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/pasadena/standard-plans/01-pages-1-23/#p12">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, continental crosswalk (sheet 3 of 3): A corner with a curb return from B.C. to E.C., the curb face as a heavy solid line and the edge of gutter dashed. The approach to the intersection runs along the top left with a double yellow center line (DYCL), and a gray limit line (callout 3) is drawn from the DYCL to the curb face, 4&#x27; back from the first continental bar. On the approach leg, seven heavy continental bars are drawn parallel to traffic flow. The first bar is placed centered on the DYCL, the bars are callout 1, and the last bar nearest the curb is shorter so that it meets the edge of gutter (callout 2). On the leaving side, eight vertical bars are drawn across the leg, each 14&#x27; long, with a 2&#x27; space between bars and the notation UNLESS OTHERWISE SPECIFIED (MAY BE UP TO 5&#x27; AT THE DIRECTION OF THE ENGINEER). The first leaving-side bar is shortened at the curb return (callout 2). A 1.5&#x27; dimension is shown at the edge of gutter on the leaving side, and a DYCL runs down the right side.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Continental Crosswalk Installation, Standard Plan S-709, Sheet 3 of 3, Revision 1, file S709-REV4.DWG 12/12/19, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 12/26/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. All continental bars shall be 2 ft wide and full length 14 ft, white or yellow, in thermoplastic or similar material.</p>
<p>2. Start of first bar shall be centered to DYCL, avoiding wheel path. Continental crosswalk (XWALK) bars shall meet edge of gutter. For skewed or offset intersections, all crosswalk bars shall be parallel to flow of traffic, in thermoplastic or similar materials.</p>
<p>3. White 12 in limit line in advance of crosswalks typically installed only in advance of signalized intersections, other locations and only when specified. In thermoplastic or similar material.</p>
<p>4. For crosswalk layout, refer to Sheet 1 of 3.</p>
<p>Labels: Unless otherwise specified (may be up to 5 ft at the direction of the Engineer). Double yellow center line (DYCL). Approach to intersection. Leaving side of intersection. Edge of gutter. B.C. E.C. Dimensions: 4 ft (limit line to first bar), 14 ft (bar length), 2 ft (space between bars), 1.5 ft (at edge of gutter).</p></details>
