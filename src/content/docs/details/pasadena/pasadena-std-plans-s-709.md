<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard layouts for marked crosswalks, covering the basic crosswalk, the zebra crosswalk and the continental crosswalk. All striping is 12 in white or yellow thermoplastic or similar material. Bar spacing starts from the street center line and avoids the wheel path, and bars are parallel to traffic flow at skewed or offset intersections. Continental bars are 2 ft wide and 14 ft long, and a 12 in white limit line is typically used only in advance of signalized intersections.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Continental crosswalk. Sheet 3 of 3.</p>
<p>Plan shows the double yellow center line (DYCL), approach to intersection, leaving side of intersection, curb face and edge of gutter. Bar spacing dimensions are shown, unless otherwise specified (may be up to 5 ft at the direction of the Engineer).</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. All continental bars shall be 2 ft wide and full length 14 ft, white or yellow, in thermoplastic or similar material.</p>
<p>2. Start of first bar shall be centered to the DYCL, avoiding wheel path. Continental crosswalk &quot;bars&quot; shall meet edge of gutter. For skewed or offset intersections, all crosswalk &quot;bars&quot; shall be parallel to flow of traffic, in thermoplastic or similar materials.</p>
<p>3. White 12 in limit line in advance of crosswalks typically installed only in advance of signalized intersections. Other locations only when specified. In thermoplastic or similar material.</p>
<p>For crosswalk layout, refer to sheet 1 of 3.</p></details>
