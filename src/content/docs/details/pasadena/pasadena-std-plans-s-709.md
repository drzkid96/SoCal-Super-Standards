<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All striping shall be 12 in white or yellow, in thermoplastic or similar material.</li>
<li value="2">White 12 in limit line in advance of crosswalks typically installed only in advance of signalized intersections, other locations and only when specified. In thermoplastic material or similar material.</li>
</ol>
<p>Labels: Double yellow center line (DYCL). Approach to intersection. Leaving side of intersection. Curb face. Edge of gutter. B.C. E.C. Angle phi and phi/2. Dimensions: 4 ft (limit line to crosswalk line), 12 ft (between the two crosswalk lines), 4 ft (at the corner diagonal).</p>
</details>

### Sheet 2 of 3 (page 11)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0011.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0011.webp" alt="S-709 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Pasadena Std. Plans S-709, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/pasadena/standard-plans/01-pages-1-23/#p11">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All striping shall be 12 in white or yellow, in thermoplastic or similar material. Spacing of crosswalk bars shall start from center line of street, avoiding wheel path. For intersections not perpendicular or 90 degrees (skewed or offset), all crosswalk (XWALK) bars shall be parallel to flow of traffic.</li>
<li value="2">For crosswalk layout, refer to Sheet 1 of 3.</li>
</ol>
<p>Labels: Double yellow center line (DYCL). Approach to intersection. Leaving side of intersection. Curb face. Edge of gutter. B.C. E.C. Dimensions: 3 ft (between zebra bars), 1.5 ft minimum (MIN) at the leaving side.</p>
</details>

### Sheet 3 of 3 (page 12)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0012.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/pasadena/standard-plans/p0012.webp" alt="S-709 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Pasadena Std. Plans S-709, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/pasadena/standard-plans/01-pages-1-23/#p12">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All continental bars shall be 2 ft wide and full length 14 ft, white or yellow, in thermoplastic or similar material.</li>
<li value="2">Start of first bar shall be centered to DYCL, avoiding wheel path. Continental crosswalk (XWALK) bars shall meet edge of gutter. For skewed or offset intersections, all crosswalk bars shall be parallel to flow of traffic, in thermoplastic or similar materials.</li>
<li value="3">White 12 in limit line in advance of crosswalks typically installed only in advance of signalized intersections, other locations and only when specified. In thermoplastic or similar material.</li>
<li value="4">For crosswalk layout, refer to Sheet 1 of 3.</li>
</ol>
<p>Labels: Unless otherwise specified (may be up to 5 ft at the direction of the Engineer). Double yellow center line (DYCL). Approach to intersection. Leaving side of intersection. Edge of gutter. B.C. E.C. Dimensions: 4 ft (limit line to first bar), 14 ft (bar length), 2 ft (space between bars), 1.5 ft (at edge of gutter).</p>
</details>
