<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena standard plan S-718 shows a 57 mm (2 1/4 in) galvanized square steel tube extension, 610 mm (24 in) long, inserted into an existing STOP sign post and fastened with 9.5 mm (3/8 in) drive rivets. Salvaged street name signs and brackets are remounted on it using new stainless steel straps and buckles.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet S-718, City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department standard plan, revision 1, units DUAL, sheet 1 of 1, dated 7-23-01. Two isometric details: the STOP sign post extension at left and the street name signs mounted on it at right.</p>
<p>Stop sign post extension (for street name signs): A 57 mm (2 1/4&quot;) O.D. square steel tube extension, wall thickness of 12 gauge [3 mm (0.118&quot;)], galvanized, is telescoped into the top of the existing STOP sign post. Dimensions: the extension is 610 mm (24&quot;) long overall, with 460 mm (18&quot;) above the post top and 150 mm (6&quot;) inside the post. A 25 mm (1&quot;) dimension is shown at the top of the existing post. Rivet holes are spaced 100 mm (4&quot;) apart vertically on the post, with a further (4&quot;) spacing shown below, and the existing post is drawn with a 610 mm (24&quot;) dimension at its lower end. Holes are 11 mm (7/16&quot;) diameter for 9.5 mm (3/8&quot;) drive rivets (typical): 4 places in the post and 3 places in the extension. The existing mounting holes on the STOP sign post are indicated at the lower end.</p>
<p>Street name signs: An isometric view of the extension tube at the top of the existing post, with two stainless steel straps and buckles wrapped around the post and a street name sign bracket clamped to the face. Salvaged street name signs and brackets (typical) project to the side, with a break line showing the sign blades are longer than drawn. Labels: new stainless steel strap and buckle (15 mm x 0.75 mm) (5/8&quot; x 0.03&quot;), tube extension or street light standard, salvaged street name signs and brackets (typical).</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>No numbered notes on this sheet.</p>
<p>Callouts: 57 mm (2 1/4&quot;) O.D. square steel tube extension, wall thickness of 12 gauge [3 mm (0.118&quot;)], galvanized. 11 mm (7/16&quot;) diam. holes for 9.5 mm (3/8&quot;) drive rivets (typical (TYP.)): 4 places - post, 3 places - extension. Existing mounting holes on STOP sign post. Salvaged street name signs and brackets (typical). New stainless steel strap and buckle (15 mm x 0.75 mm) (5/8&quot; x 0.03&quot;). Tube extension or street light standard.</p></details>
