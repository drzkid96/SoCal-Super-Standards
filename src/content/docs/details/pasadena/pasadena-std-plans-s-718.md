<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena detail for adding a street name sign extension to an existing STOP sign post. A 57 mm (2 1/4 in) O.D. galvanized square steel tube extension with 12 gauge wall (3 mm, 0.118 in) is attached with 9.5 mm (3/8 in) drive rivets through 11 mm (7/16 in) holes, 4 places in the post and 3 places in the extension. Salvaged street name signs and brackets are remounted, and new stainless steel strap and buckle (15 mm x 0.75 mm) secures them to the tube extension or street light standard.</p>
