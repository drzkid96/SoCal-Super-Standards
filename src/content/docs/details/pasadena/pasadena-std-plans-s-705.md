<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pavement word message layout for school crossings. Condition A is a centerline-only street and Condition B is multi-lane, with yellow school crosswalks. The messages XING, SCHOOL and SLOW are spaced 8 m (25 ft) minimum apart, working back from the crosswalk. Messages are centered 1.7 m (5.5 ft) off the marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12 ft) or greater, and centered in the lane for narrower lanes.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Condition A, plan view (centerline only, marked or unmarked): A two-way street is shown between two edge lines with a dashed centerline (CL). Two heavy horizontal lines across the full width, labeled YELLOW SCHOOL CROSSWALKS, mark the crosswalk. Working back from the crosswalk, the pavement words XING, SCHOOL and SLOW are stacked in the lane along one line labeled CENTERLINE OF MESSAGE (*). The distance from the crosswalk to XING is marked VARIES. XING to SCHOOL is 8 m (25&#x27;) MIN and SCHOOL to SLOW is 8 m (25&#x27;) MIN. The message centerline is offset 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) from the street centerline.</p>
<p>Condition B, plan view (multi-lane): The same crosswalk (YELLOW SCHOOL CROSSWALKS) crosses a multi-lane street with a double centerline (CL) and lane lines that are solid near the crosswalk and dashed farther back. The words XING, SCHOOL and SLOW are repeated in each of the two lanes on one side of the double centerline. The distance from the crosswalk to XING is VARIES, and the spacing is 8 m (25&#x27;) MIN between XING and SCHOOL and 8 m (25&#x27;) MIN between SCHOOL and SLOW. In the lane next to the centerline the callout reads CENTER WORD MESSAGE IN LANE. In the outer lane the message centerline (*) is dimensioned 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) off the lane line.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Slow School Xing Pavement Markings, Standard Plan S-705, Sheet 1 of 1, Revision 2, units DUAL, approved by the Transportation Administrator on 6/4/01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Condition A: centerline only (marked or unmarked).</p>
<p>2. Condition B: multi-lane.</p>
<p>3. Yellow school crosswalks.</p>
<p>4. Word messages, working back from the crosswalk: XING, SCHOOL, SLOW. The distance from the crosswalk to XING varies.</p>
<p>5. Spacing: 8 m (25&#x27;) minimum (MIN) between XING and SCHOOL, and 8 m (25&#x27;) minimum between SCHOOL and SLOW.</p>
<p>6. Center word message in lane (multi-lane condition).</p>
<p>* Locate center of word message at a point 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) off marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12&#x27;) or greater. For lanes less than 3.7 m (12&#x27;), center message within lane.</p></details>
