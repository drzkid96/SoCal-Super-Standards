<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pavement word message layout for school crossings. Condition A is a centerline-only street and Condition B is multi-lane, with yellow school crosswalks. The messages XING, SCHOOL and SLOW are spaced 8 m (25 ft) minimum apart, working back from the crosswalk. Word messages are centered 1.7 m (5.5 ft) off the marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12 ft) or greater, and centered in the lane for narrower lanes.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Condition A: centerline only (marked or unmarked).</p>
<p>Condition B: multi-lane.</p>
<p>Yellow school crosswalks.</p>
<p>Centerline of message*.</p>
<p>Word messages from crosswalk back: XING, SCHOOL, SLOW. Distance from crosswalk to XING varies.</p>
<p>Spacing between messages: 8 m (25&#x27;) min between XING and SCHOOL, and 8 m (25&#x27;) min between SCHOOL and SLOW.</p>
<p>1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) offset of message centerline.</p>
<p>Center word message in lane (multi-lane).</p>
<p>* Locate center of word message at a point 1.7 m (5.5&#x27;) off marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12&#x27;) or greater. For lanes less than 3.7 m (12&#x27;), center message within lane.</p></details>
