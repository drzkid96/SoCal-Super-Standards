<p class="dwg-sum">Pavement word message layout for school crossings. Condition A is a centerline-only street and Condition B is multi-lane, with yellow school crosswalks. The messages XING, SCHOOL and SLOW are spaced 8 m (25 ft) minimum apart, working back from the crosswalk. Word messages are centered 1.7 m (5.5 ft) off the marked centerline or lane line for lanes 3.7 m (12 ft) or greater, and centered in the lane for narrower lanes.</p>
