<p class="dwg-sum">Pasadena detail for placing raised pavement markers (RPMs) with different stripe types. Twelve details cover two-way no passing zones, painted medians, left and right turn lane stripes, lane lines, dashed centerlines, reversals into a left turn lane, left edge lines, two-way left turn lanes, and lead-in and lead-out patterns. RPMs are placed at least 150 mm (6 in) from any crosswalk or limit line. Marker types shown are Type D two-way yellow reflective and Type G one-way clear reflective, with lead-in spacing of 6.1 m (20 ft) minimum, 7.3 m (24 ft) standard and 9.1 m (30 ft) maximum.</p>

<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Always place beginning and end R.P.M.</p>
<p>2. Distance between R.P.M. varies with length of single yellow lead-in. Minimum spacing: 6.1 m (20 ft). Standard spacing: 7.3 m (24 ft). Maximum spacing: 9.1 m (30 ft).</p>
<p>3. R.P.M. shall not be placed on painted stripes.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-12" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-12.svg">Detail 12</a>: Lead-in/lead-out solid white lane line. Dimensions 2.1 m (7 ft), 5.2 m (17 ft), 4.6 m (48 ft) minimum as shown on the sheet, 21.9 m (72 ft) maximum, 15.2 m (50 ft), 7.6 m (25 ft).</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Always place beginning and end R.P.M.</p>
<p>2. Distance between R.P.M. varies with length of solid white lead-in/lead-out. Minimum spacing: 6.1 m (20 ft). Standard spacing: 7.3 m (24 ft). Maximum spacing: 9.1 m (30 ft).</p>
<p>3. If left turn pocket is included, the length of lead-in solid white lane line shall equal the length of the left turn pocket unless otherwise specified.</p></details>
