<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pasadena detail for placing raised pavement markers (RPMs) with different stripe types. Twelve details cover two-way no passing zones, painted medians, left and right turn lane stripes, lane lines, dashed centerlines, reversals into a left turn lane, left edge lines, two-way left turn lanes, and lead-in and lead-out patterns. RPMs are placed at least 150 mm (6 in) from any crosswalk or limit line. Marker types shown are Type D two-way yellow reflective and Type G one-way clear reflective, with lead-in spacing of 6.1 m (20 ft) minimum, 7.3 m (24 ft) standard and 9.1 m (30 ft) maximum.</p>
