<p class="dwg-sum">Layout of parking tee markings that delineate parallel parking spaces along a curb. Spaces are 12 m (40 ft) in total, made of two 6 m (20 ft) stalls, with tees set 2.1 m (7 ft) from the curb line. Each tee is formed with 100 mm (4 in) white stripes, and red curb 2.4 m (8 ft) long is used at the ends. Detail A gives the stripe dimensions.</p>
