<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Overall parking layout: 12 m (40&#x27;), made of two 6 m (20&#x27;) spaces.</li>
<li value="2">Parking tees are set 2.1 m (7&#x27;) from the curb line.</li>
<li value="3">Red curb (TYP) is 2.4 m (8&#x27;) long.</li>
<li value="4">See Detail A for the stripe dimensions.</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Detail A</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="5">100 mm (4&quot;) white stripe (TYP.).</li>
<li value="6">Distance between tees (stem to stem): 6 m (20&#x27;).</li>
<li value="7"><strong>Tee stem length: 1 m (3&#x27;).</strong> Tee bar offsets along the stem: 0.6 m (2&#x27;) from one end and 0.4 m (1&#x27;) from the other end.</li>
<li value="8">Bar length: 0.6 m (2&#x27;) on the end tee. 0.6 m (2&#x27;) each side of the stem, 1.2 m (4&#x27;) total, on the middle tee.</li>
<li value="9">Space between the bar ends of adjacent tees: 4.9 m (16&#x27;).</li>
</ol>
</details>
