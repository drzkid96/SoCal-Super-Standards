<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Layout of parking tee markings that delineate parallel parking spaces along a curb. A 12 m (40 ft) run is made of two 6 m (20 ft) stalls, with tees set 2.1 m (7 ft) from the curb line and 2.4 m (8 ft) red curb segments at the ends. Detail A gives the tee dimensions, using 100 mm (4 in) white stripe, a 1 m (3 ft) stem and 0.6 m (2 ft) bars.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A straight horizontal curb line (labeled CURB LINE) runs across the sheet. Above it, parking tee marks are drawn 2.1 m (7&#x27;) out from the curb line. One parking layout is dimensioned as 12 m (40&#x27;) overall, split into two 6 m (20&#x27;) spaces, with a tee mark at each end of the 12 m run and one at the 6 m split point. The marks read, left to right, as end tee, end tee, middle tee, end tee for the first run, and the pattern continues to the right with the next run (end tee, middle tee, end tee, end tee). A dashed rounded outline around one end tee and one middle tee carries the callout SEE DETAIL &quot;A&quot;. Short heavy bars on the curb line, labeled RED CURB (TYP), mark the red curb at the ends of each run, each 2.4 m (8&#x27;) long (dimensioned at the right end of the 12 m run).</p>
<p>Detail A: An enlarged plan of an end tee and the next middle tee, 6 m (20&#x27;) apart stem to stem. Both tees are made of 100 mm (4&quot;) white stripe (TYP.). Each stem is 1 m (3&#x27;) long. End tee: a single bar 0.6 m (2&#x27;) long extends to one side of the stem, set 0.6 m (2&#x27;) from one end of the stem and 0.4 m (1&#x27;) from the other end. Middle tee: the bar is 1.2 m (4&#x27;) long overall, 0.6 m (2&#x27;) to each side of the stem, with the same 0.6 m (2&#x27;) and 0.4 m (1&#x27;) offsets along the stem. The clear space between the end of the first tee&#x27;s bar and the start of the second tee&#x27;s bar is 4.9 m (16&#x27;).</p>
<p>Title block: City of Pasadena Public Works and Transportation Department, Parking Tee Installation, Standard Plan S-704, Sheet 1 of 1, Revision 1, units DUAL (metric with U.S. units in parentheses), approved by the Transportation Administrator on 6/4/01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Overall parking layout: 12 m (40&#x27;), made of two 6 m (20&#x27;) spaces.</p>
<p>2. Parking tees are set 2.1 m (7&#x27;) from the curb line.</p>
<p>3. Red curb (TYP) is 2.4 m (8&#x27;) long.</p>
<p>4. See Detail A for the stripe dimensions.</p>
<h5>Detail A:</h5>
<p>5. 100 mm (4&quot;) white stripe (TYP.).</p>
<p>6. Distance between tees (stem to stem): 6 m (20&#x27;).</p>
<p>7. Tee stem length: 1 m (3&#x27;). Tee bar offsets along the stem: 0.6 m (2&#x27;) from one end and 0.4 m (1&#x27;) from the other end.</p>
<p>8. Bar length: 0.6 m (2&#x27;) on the end tee. 0.6 m (2&#x27;) each side of the stem, 1.2 m (4&#x27;) total, on the middle tee.</p>
<p>9. Space between the bar ends of adjacent tees: 4.9 m (16&#x27;).</p></details>
