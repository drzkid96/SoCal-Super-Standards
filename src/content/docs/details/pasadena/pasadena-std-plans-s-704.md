<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Layout of parking tee markings that delineate parallel parking spaces along a curb. Spaces are 12 m (40 ft) in total, made of two 6 m (20 ft) stalls, with tees set 2.1 m (7 ft) from the curb line. Each tee is formed with 100 mm (4 in) white stripes, and red curb 2.4 m (8 ft) long is used at the ends. Detail A gives the stripe dimensions.</p>

<p>100 mm (4&quot;) white stripe (typ.).</p>
<p>Distance between tees: 6 m (20&#x27;).</p>
<p>Tee stem length: 1 m (3&#x27;). Tee bar lengths: 0.6 m (2&#x27;) on the first tee, and 0.6 m (2&#x27;) each side (1.2 m (4&#x27;) total) on the second tee.</p>
<p>Offset of bar from end of stem: 0.4 m (1&#x27;).</p>
<p>Space between tees: 4.9 m (16&#x27;).</p></details>
