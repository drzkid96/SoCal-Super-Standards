<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT standard drawing S-70.1D for bicycle detector loops. Case I uses two 3 ft loops spaced 3 ft apart for bicycle-only bike lanes and Case II uses two 6 ft loops spaced 8 ft apart for bicycle and vehicle detection, each with a pullbox no more than 3 ft from the curb face. The sheet also gives loop winding details, saw cut sections A-A through E-E, and nine notes.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 69)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Winding detail (not to scale): Four loop sketches across the top, labeled LOOP 1 and LOOP 2 for each of the two cases. In each, the loop wire is wound in concentric turns with direction arrows. The two lead-in wires leave each loop and are TWISTED CLOCKWISE (AT LEAST 2 TURNS PER FT.) INTO A PAIR, then run TO PULLBOX. The four wires are numbered 1, 2, 3 and 4 at the bottom. The left pair of sketches (Case I, bicycle only) shows the same multi-turn alternating winding on both loops. The right pair (Case II) shows Loop 1 wound as simple round turns and Loop 2 wound with the alternating spiral pattern.</p>
<p>Case I (bicycle only), plan view: Two circular loops, LOOP 1 and LOOP 2, are each 3&#x27; in diameter and spaced 3&#x27; apart (3&#x27;, 3&#x27;, 3&#x27; string across the top). Each loop is cut as a circle with a diagonal saw cut for the lead-in. The two diagonal saw cuts join in a Y and run as a single saw cut across the curb face to a PULLBOX. The pullbox is 3&#x27;-0&quot; MAX. from the CURB FACE. The direction of traffic flow arrow points to the right between the two case views. Section cut marks are shown on the plan: A-A and B-B on the loop circles, C-C on the diagonal lead-ins and D-D on the stem leading to the pullbox.</p>
<p>Case II (bicycle and vehicle), plan view: Two circular loops, each 6&#x27; in diameter, spaced 8&#x27; apart (6&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 6&#x27; string). The same Y-shaped lead-in leads to a pullbox 3&#x27;-0&quot; MAX. from the curb face. Section marks: E-E on Loop 1, A-A and B-B on Loop 2, C-C on the diagonal lead-ins and D-D on the stem.</p>
<p>Saw cut sections (all show a hatched pavement with a narrow slot and wires in the slot): Sect. A-A: slot 3/8&quot; wide, 3.5&quot; deep*, four wires stacked in a single column. Sect. B-B: slot 1/2&quot; wide, 5&quot; deep*, eight wires in two columns of four. Sect. C-C: slot 3/8&quot; wide, 5&quot; deep*, two wires at the bottom. Sect. D-D: slot 1/2&quot; MAX wide, 5&quot; deep*, four wires, with the callout 8 WIRES MAX. PER 1/2&quot; SAWCUT. Sect. E-E: slot 1/2&quot; MAX wide, 5&quot; deep*, three wires. The footnote reads * DEPTH OF SLOT NOT TO EXCEED DEPTH OF PAVEMENT.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, BICYCLE DETECTOR, Drawing No. S-70.1D, sheet 1 of 1. Drawn by MT 3-07-17, T.E. JV 3-7-17, Sr. T.E. MA 4-27-17, Pr. T.E. VJ 4-28-17, approved 5-11-17 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. The printed page number is 62.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install four (4) complete alternating turns of loop wires, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. For stub-out and conduit details refer to page 2 of S-70.1A.</p>
<p>3. See LADOT Material Specification No. 92-081-05 or latest revision.</p>
<p>4. Use Case I loops for bike lane installations. Use Case II loops when vehicular loops are intended to detect bicycles.</p>
<p>5. An octagonal shaped loop or other non-round shaped loop may be used instead of the round-loop with prior LADOT approval.</p>
<p>6. Same winding pattern to be used in both loops for Case 1 with 1 pair from each loop leading to the pullbox.</p>
<p>7. Loops installed in bike lanes with parking shall be placed with the near edge of the loop 1&#x27; to the right of the bike lane delineation line, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>8. Loops installed in bike lanes adjacent to the roadside without parking shall be centered in the bike lane, or between the gutter and the bike delineation line, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>9. Any LADOT approved non-round shaped loops must conform to all other specifications shown on this standard drawing.</p>
<p>* Depth of slot not to exceed depth of pavement.</p>
<p>Labels: Winding detail (not to scale); Loop 1; Loop 2; twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) into a pair; to pullbox; curb face; pullbox; 3&#x27;-0&quot; maximum (MAX.); direction of traffic flow; Case I (bicycle only); Case II (bicycle and vehicle).</p></details>
