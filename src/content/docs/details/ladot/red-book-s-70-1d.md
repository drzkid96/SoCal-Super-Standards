<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Install four (4) complete alternating turns of loop wires, unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="2">For stub-out and conduit details refer to page 2 of S-70.1A.</li>
<li value="3"><strong>See LADOT Material Specification No.</strong> 92-081-05 or latest revision.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Use Case I loops for bike lane installations.</strong> Use Case II loops when vehicular loops are intended to detect bicycles.</li>
<li value="5">An octagonal shaped loop or other non-round shaped loop may be used instead of the round-loop with prior LADOT approval.</li>
<li value="6">Same winding pattern to be used in both loops for Case 1 with 1 pair from each loop leading to the pullbox.</li>
<li value="7">Loops installed in bike lanes with parking shall be placed with the near edge of the loop 1&#x27; to the right of the bike lane delineation line, unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="8">Loops installed in bike lanes adjacent to the roadside without parking shall be centered in the bike lane, or between the gutter and the bike delineation line, unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="9">Any LADOT approved non-round shaped loops must conform to all other specifications shown on this standard drawing.</li>
</ol>
<p>* Depth of slot not to exceed depth of pavement.</p>
<p>Labels: Winding detail (not to scale); Loop 1; Loop 2; twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) into a pair; to pullbox; curb face; pullbox; 3&#x27;-0&quot; maximum (MAX.); direction of traffic flow; Case I (bicycle only); Case II (bicycle and vehicle).</p>
</details>
