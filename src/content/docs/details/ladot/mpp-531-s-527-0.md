<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan views of Type II curb extensions at street corners with existing directional or diagonal curb ramps. Extensions use a 25 ft radius with 2 ft radius corners, are 6 ft minimum to 8 ft maximum wide, and are 10 ft minimum long, with 12 in. stop bars and optional 8 in. white edge lines, channelizing bollards, contrasting colored surface and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> signs.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, Type II curb extension with dual uni-directional ramps (upper part of sheet): A street corner with a vertical street on the left and a horizontal street across the top. At the corner, a curb extension bulbs out in a large arc, drawn with a contrasting colored surface (optional, SEE NOTE 3, shown as gray) filled with a row of channelizing bollards (optional, SEE NOTE 2) and bounded on the outside by an 8&quot; SOLID WHITE EDGE LINE (OPTIONAL). The extension follows a RADIUS, SEE NOTE 1. Existing directional curb ramps with detectable warning surfaces are drawn inside the corner (EXISTING DIRECTIONAL CURB RAMP). A crosswalk of transverse bars crosses the vertical street. A 12&quot; STOP BAR crosses the lane next to a narrow Type II curb extension (TYPE II CURB EXTENSION, SEE NOTE 4) on the vertical street, with a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> sign (OPTIONAL). Another 8&quot; SOLID WHITE EDGE LINE (OPTIONAL) curves along the extension. A detail at the upper right shows the Type II curb extension in plan on the horizontal street: it is a rounded island with a 25&#x27;R outer curve and 2&#x27;R corners, 10&#x27; MIN long on the flat part, 2&#x27; MIN from the crosswalk edge, 4&#x27; MIN wide at the sides and 6&#x27; MIN. TO 8&#x27; MAX. wide at the bulb, measured from the dashed parking line.</p>
<p>Plan view, Type II curb extension with diagonal ramp (lower part of sheet): A similar corner on the lower left, with the same Type II curb extension pocket on the vertical street (12&quot; STOP BAR, TYPE II CURB EXTENSION SEE NOTE 4, 8&quot; SOLID WHITE EDGE LINE (OPTIONAL)) and a corner bulb at the top with a contrasting colored surface (SEE NOTE 3, OPTIONAL), channelizing bollards (OPTIONAL, SEE NOTE 2) and an 8&quot; SOLID WHITE EDGE LINE (OPTIONAL). An EXISTING DIAGONAL CURB RAMP with a detectable warning surface is retained at the corner, and a 48&quot; MIN dimension is shown from the crosswalk to the curb ramp. The horizontal street at the right shows a second Type II curb extension pocket with dashed parking line.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, title CURB EXTENSIONS, drawing number S-527.0, sheet 1 of 3, approved 02-20-2020 by Seleta Reynolds, General Manager.</p></details>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, Type II protected intersection with directional ramps (upper half of sheet): A corner where a bike lane approaches from the bottom (marked with a bicycle symbol, an upward arrow and shark-tooth yield triangles) and turns to the right along a horizontal street. A green colored surface (GREEN COLORED SURFACE RECOMMENDED, SEE NOTE 6) fills the bike path through the corner. A corner refuge island at the upper left is bordered by an 8&quot; WIDE WHITE EDGE LINE with 12&quot; WIDE WHITE LINES (SEE NOTE 7) radiating across the colored corner. A 12&quot; WHITE STOP LINE (SEE NOTE 5) is at the head of the vertical street and a 12&quot; STOP BAR is on the lower street next to a Type II curb extension with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a> (OPTIONAL). A separate island at the upper right carries another <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a> (OPTIONAL). Detectable warning surfaces (SEE S-442 FOR DETAIL) are shown at the crosswalk ends, and a 12&quot; WHITE CROSSWALK is drawn between islands, with transverse bars shown for the crossing. The curb is called out and existing directional ramps are shown at the corner. Dimensions on the right-hand island, which has an upper 25&#x27;R curve and a lower 25&#x27;R curve: 9&#x27; MIN. island depth and 5&#x27; MIN. bike lane width, with a BICYCLE LANE SYMBOL and a right-pointing arrow in the bike lane. Other dimensions: 6&#x27; MAX. between the corner island and the next island and 24&quot; MIN. between crosswalk-end islands.</p>
<p>Plan view, Temporary/retrofit curb extension with diagonal ramp (lower half of sheet): A corner with an existing diagonal curb ramp. A bulb at the corner is filled with a CONTRASTING COLORED SURFACE (SEE NOTE 3, OPTIONAL) and channelizing bollards (OPTIONAL, SEE NOTE 2), bounded by an 8&quot; SOLID WHITE EDGE LINE with the radius per Note 1. The side extensions on both streets are striped with 12&quot; SOLID WHITE LINES at 45 degrees SPACED AT 5&#x27; O.C. and an 8&quot; SOLID WHITE EDGE LINE, with chanelizing bollards (OPTIONAL, SEE NOTE 2). The extension lengths are labeled MATCH PARKING RESTRICTION. A 12&quot; STOP BAR is shown on the vertical street, and a 48&quot; MIN dimension is shown from the crosswalk to the diagonal curb ramp.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, title CURB EXTENSIONS, drawing number S-527.0, sheet 2 of 3.</p></details>
