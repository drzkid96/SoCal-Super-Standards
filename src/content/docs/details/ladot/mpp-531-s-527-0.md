<p class="dwg-sum">Plan layouts for Type II curb extensions, including dual uni-directional ramps, a diagonal ramp, a protected intersection with directional ramps, and a temporary or retrofit extension with diagonal ramp. Typical features are a 25' radius, 2' radius corners, 12" stop bars, optional 8" white edge lines, optional channelizing bollards and contrasting colored surface, and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a> signs. Extensions are 6' minimum to 8' maximum wide.</p>
