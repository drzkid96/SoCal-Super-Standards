<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-92.3 for a 22' cantilever sign standard. An 8XX-Strong pipe post carries an 8X-Strong pipe arm, on a PF-22 cast-in-drilled-hole foundation 36" in diameter and 10'-0" deep. Total sign area is limited to 90 sq. ft. The sheet gives the arm connection, end cap, anchor bolt, base and reinforcing details and the AASHO 1968 design basis.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 99)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation (not to scale): 22&#x27; cantilever sign standard. A vertical post of Pipe 8XX-Strong carries a horizontal sign arm of Pipe 8X-Strong at the top. Overall arm reach from the post centerline is 22&#x27;. Dimension F (see note below) runs from the post centerline to the centroid of the sign area. The sign area is a dashed rectangle with break lines, 18&#x27; MAX. long, 5&#x27; MAX. high, and its end extends 2&#x27;-6&quot; MAX. beyond the end of the arm. The post top is 18&quot; above the arm centerline, the arm height above grade is 20&#x27;-1&quot; and the overall post height is 21&#x27;-7&quot;. A 3&#x27;-0&quot; dimension locates a curb from the post centerline. The curb top is flush with grade and a 2&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. dimension is given below it. Below grade is a PF-22 CIDH (cast-in-drilled-hole) foundation 36&quot; dia., 10&#x27;-0&quot; deep, with a 2&quot; conduit to T. S. (traffic signal) pullbox leaving the foundation, see Section A-A for reinforcing. Detail circles A (arm head), B (arm end), C (base at grade) and D (anchor bolt template at the bottom of the bolts) are referenced.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;A&quot; (arm to post connection, not to scale): A plan view shows the post, the arm, 1-1/4&quot; and 1&quot; plate offsets, 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; backing ring, tack weld, 3/8&quot; and 1/8&quot; weld sizes and 5/16&quot; fillet welds, and a note that bolts are not shown on plan. An elevation view shows a Pl. 3/16 x 9-5/8 O.D. (removable top) plate with 2 set screws on the post, a Bar 2 x 3/16 bent to circular shape with 6-3/4&quot; O.D., Pl. 1/2&quot; x 4-7/8&quot; x 1&#x27;-2&quot; long (2 required), the sign arm 8-5/8&quot; O.D., the connection plates 14&quot; tall, a 2&quot; dia. hole finished smooth, and 4 - 1-1/4&quot; dia. x 4&quot; ASTM A-325 bolts with hex nut and washer. The post is 8-5/8&quot; O.D. with a 1/4&quot; weld at the base of the connection and 5/16&quot; welds shown. A face view shows Pl 1-1/4&quot; x 14 x 1&#x27;-5&quot; long, 2 required, with 4 - 1-1/2&quot; dia. holes, 2&quot; edge distances, 1&#x27;-5&quot; overall width with 1&#x27;-1&quot; hole spacing, 1&#x27;-2&quot; overall height with 10&quot; hole spacing, 2&quot;R (TYP.) corners, Bar 5&quot; x 1/2&quot; x 9&quot; long, 2 required, cut to fit flush, and a 3/4&quot; standard half coupling, with 5/16&quot; fillet welds.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;B&quot; (arm end): End plate Pl. 3/16 x 9&quot; O.D. on the sign arm with a 1/8&quot; fillet weld, shown in side and end views, 9&quot; dia. A note says the sign mounting assembly is not shown on the detail.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;D&quot;: Template plate bar with two holes. Dimensions 6-1/2&quot;, 6-1/2&quot; and 3&quot; along the length, 2-1/2&quot; width and 1-7/8&quot; dia. holes.</p>
<p>Section A-A (not to scale): Round foundation cross section 36&quot; dia. with a 12-#7 x 9&#x27;-6&quot; long equally spaced vertical bars, #4 spiral at 6&quot; pitch, 3&quot; clear. The 4 anchor bolt locations form a rectangle with 1&#x27;-6&quot; and 1&#x27;-1&quot; dimensions and 2-1/2&quot; edge distances, on a base plate square 1&#x27;-6&quot; by 1&#x27;-1&quot; with 2&quot;R corners. The center sign line (CL Sign) is marked, with a 3&quot; dia. hole finished smooth at the center and a 2&quot; dia. hole noted.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;C&quot; PF-22 (not to scale): Section through the post base and foundation top. Shows the post, 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover with 1&quot; radius corners, base plate on mortar at top of walk, 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; backing ring, tack weld, 3/8&quot; dia. drain hole, 4 - 1-3/4&quot; dia. anchor bolts (thrd. 8, galv. top 12&quot;, 2 hex nuts and 2 std. washers each, ASTM A-307), banding wire, conduit, and bar 5&quot; x 1&quot; x 1&#x27;-7&quot; long (parallel to axis of sign, 2 required) tack welded at the bolt bottoms. Dimensions include 8&quot; and 3/8&quot;/1/8&quot; welds at the hand hole, 2&quot;, 4-1/2&quot;, 2&quot; and 2&#x27;-8&quot; to the bottom of the anchor bolt projection, 1&quot; and 3&quot; typ. Foundation concrete shall be 560-C-3250 (see reinforcement in Sec. A-A). Section marker A points to the Section A-A cut.</p>
<p>Specification block: Design per A.A.S.H.O. Specifications for the Design and Construction of Structural Supports for Highway Signs, dated 1968. Loading: wind normal to sign face 23.3 psi. Transverse to force 0.2 of normal force. Unit stresses: structural steel fb = 23,000 psi, reinforcing steel fs = 24,000 psi, concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3000 psi, fc = 1200 psi, soil bearing pressure c = 3,000 psi.</p>
<p>Title block: 22&#x27; Cantilever Sign Standard Detail, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager. Approved 11-15-99. Drawing No. S-92.3. Original approved 12-29-70 by Philip H. Skarin. Page number 92.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes (on elevation):</h5>
<p>1. Total sign area shall not exceed 90 square feet (sq. ft.).</p>
<p>2. Length &quot;F&quot; shall not exceed 15.5 feet with maximum sign area.</p>
<p>Specification. Design: American Association of State Highway Officials (A.A.S.H.O.) Specifications for the Design and Construction of Structural Supports for Highway Signs, dated 1968. Loading: wind, normal to sign face: 23.3 psi. Transverse to force: 0.2 of normal force. Unit stresses: structural steel fb = 23,000 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 24,000 psi; concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3000 psi, fc = 1200 psi; soil bearing pressure c = 3,000 psi.</p>
<p>Detail note: Sign mounting assembly not shown on detail (Detail B). Bolts not shown on plan (Detail A plan).</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>2. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B specification.</p>
<p>3. Welding shall be in conformance with AWS-D2.0 specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1969.</p>
<p>4. Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</p>
<p>5. Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>6. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except as shown on plan.</p>
<p>7. See S-45.0 for sign dimensions, positioning and mounting.</p></details>
