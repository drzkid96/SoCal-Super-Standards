<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Notes (on elevation):</h5>
<p>1. Total sign area shall not exceed 90 square feet (sq. ft.).</p>
<p>2. Length &quot;F&quot; shall not exceed 15.5 feet with maximum sign area.</p>
<p>Specification. Design: American Association of State Highway Officials (A.A.S.H.O.) Specifications for the Design and Construction of Structural Supports for Highway Signs, dated 1968. Loading: wind, normal to sign face: 23.3 psi. Transverse to force: 0.2 of normal force. Unit stresses: structural steel fb = 23,000 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 24,000 psi; concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3000 psi, fc = 1200 psi; soil bearing pressure c = 3,000 psi.</p>
<p>Detail note: Sign mounting assembly not shown on detail (Detail B). Bolts not shown on plan (Detail A plan).</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>2. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B specification.</p>
<p>3. Welding shall be in conformance with AWS-D2.0 specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1969.</p>
<p>4. Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</p>
<p>5. Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>6. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except as shown on plan.</p>
<p>7. See S-45.0 for sign dimensions, positioning and mounting.</p></details>
