### Sheet 5 of 8 (page 62)

<figure class="dfig redraw"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/redraw/mpp-521/s-101-0-s5.svg" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/redraw/mpp-521/s-101-0-s5.svg" alt="S-101.0 sheet 5, redrawn"></a><figcaption>Redrawn reference diagram. Original LADOT sheet below.</figcaption></figure><details class="orig"><summary>Original LADOT sheet</summary><figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-521/p0062.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-521/p0062.webp" alt="S-101.0 sheet 5"></a><figcaption>MPP 521 S-101.0, sheet 5. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-521/06-drawings-s-101-to-s-418/#p62">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Where phase 8 traffic is moderately high or where the driveway is offset from the side street then opposed phasing should be considered. (Text on the plan.)</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. This typical design is appropriate where a review of use has determined that the driveway should be signalized. Normally, this design would be used only where the driveway could not easily be reconstructed to be configured with curb returns and a &quot;step down&quot; design along the path of pedestrians. New development driveways with higher traffic volumes at intersections should be required to construct street like driveways with a layout for a &quot;T&quot; or 4-leg intersection, as appropriate.</p>
<p>2. This drawing is not intended to show the required safety lighting design. Final lighting designs may not include the depicted mastarm lighting and likely will include other lighting locations not shown.</p>
<p>3. Detail &quot;B&quot; may be used where visibility is good and a flashing yellow indication provides adequate control of driveway traffic.</p>
<p>Legend: R = red, FL.R. = flashing red, Y = yellow, FL.Y. = flashing yellow.</p></details>
