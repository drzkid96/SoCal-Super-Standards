<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical signal head and mastarm layouts for a T intersection, a 4-leg intersection, a one-way street, left-turn phasing, a signalized driveway, signalized midblock crosswalks on two-way and one-way streets, and an intersection with a signalized frontage road. Each layout shows head numbering and phase numbers 1 through 8. Notes cover mastarm placement within 5 feet of the approach lane center on one-way streets under 46 feet wide, a near side primary head for protected left turns, and the flashing red display during the Phase 4 pedestrian sequence at midblock crossings. Every sheet states that the drawing does not show the required safety lighting design.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Signalized Driveway</p>
<p>Where traffic is moderately high or where the driveway is offset from the side street, then opposed phasing should be considered.</p>
<p>Detail A and Detail B are shown. See Detail &quot;A&quot; and Note 3.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. This typical design is appropriate where a review of use has determined that the driveway should be signalized. Normally, this design would be used only where the driveway could not easily be reconstructed to be configured with curb returns and a &quot;step down&quot; design along the path of pedestrians. New development driveways with higher traffic volumes at intersections should be required to construct street like driveways with a layout for a &quot;T&quot; or 4-leg intersection, as appropriate.</p>
<p>2. This drawing is not intended to show the required safety lighting design. Final lighting designs may not include the depicted mastarm lighting and likely will include other lighting locations not shown.</p>
<p>3. Detail &quot;B&quot; may be used where visibility is good and a flashing yellow indication provides adequate control of driveway traffic.</p></details>
