<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fabrication layout for an LADOT tow-away sign reading NO STOPPING IN THIS AREA with the tow pictograph and the impound recovery line 3-1-1. The drawing dimensions the sign as 18 in. wide by 18 in. high with a 1-1/2 in. corner radius, gives all lettering sizes and series, and carries four fabrication notes. Note 2 states a sign size of 18 in. x 24 in., which differs from the 18 in. height dimensioned on the drawing.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face elevation: A single red-bordered panel dimensioned 18&quot; wide across the top and 18&quot; high at left, with a 1 1/2&quot; radius at the corners (callout at top right). Note 2 on the same sheet states the sign size as 18&quot; X 24&quot;, which differs from the 18&quot; height dimensioned on the drawing. Both values are as printed.</p>
<p>Legend: a red top band with a white tow-truck-towing-car pictograph, 4&quot; tall. Below it a red block at left with the white word NO, next to STOPPING in red on white. The red portion is dimensioned 8&quot; from the top edge to the bottom of the NO block, with the NO block labeled 3 1/2&quot; Series C on the left and STOPPING labeled 3&quot; Series B on the right, as drawn. Next, in red on white, IN THIS on one line and AREA on the next, each 2 1/2&quot; Series C. Then TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1 in 1/2&quot; Series C. A red border runs around the white field.</p>
<p>Vertical dimensions down the left side: 18&quot; overall, 8&quot; red portion, 4&quot; graphic, 1/2&quot; below the NO and STOPPING lettering, 1&quot; from the bottom of the red block to the top of IN THIS, 3/4&quot; between the IN THIS and AREA lines, 1 1/2&quot; from AREA to the TO RECOVER line and 1 3/4&quot; from the TO RECOVER line to the bottom edge. Lettering callouts on the right, top to bottom: 3&quot; Series B, 2 1/2&quot; Series C, 2 1/2&quot; Series C, 1/2&quot; Series C.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved July 21, 2010, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Tow Away No Stopping In This Area Sign. Sheet 1 of 1. T.E. TC, drawn MT 7-20-10. Drawing No. S-521.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by the Los Angeles Department of Transportation (LADOT).</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 18&quot; x 24&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is as noted. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication, e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
