<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Front view of the 2070 controller cabinet with the two 283LA transit sensors, drawing S-70.3A, sheet 1 of 6, approved 2/20/2001. It shows the 2070 controller above input files I and J, with the DB9 serial cable routed from the controller to the sensors. Three notes cover the two sensor configuration, sensor slots and serial cable routing.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 6 (document page 124)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0124.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0124.webp" alt="S-70.3A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p124">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section detail, controller cabinet front view (not to scale): A front elevation of the rack from top to bottom. The Model 2070 unit (2070 controller) has the City of Los Angeles label, an LCD, hex keypad, a function keypad with ESC, arrows, NEXT, YES, NO and ENT keys, an ACTIVE light, an AUX ON/OFF switch, a CONTRAST knob and a C50S serial port. Below are Input File I and Input File J, each a card file with slots. A 283LA sensor module is installed in slot 10 of each input file, shown with its channel lights and J1 serial port. Arrows point from the label 283LA sensor (see note 2) to each of the two modules. A DB9 serial cable (see note 3) runs from the 2070 port down the right side to the J1 ports of the sensors . A callout says See sheet 5/6.</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Detail, sheet 1 of 6, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.3A. Drawn AN 12-01-01, checked AN 12-01-01, T. E. SS 01-01-01, Sr. T. E. SS 01-01-01, Pr. T. E. GO 2-14-01. Approved 2/20/2001, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager. Printed page number 117.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. This drawing depicts a two sensor configuration. Each sensor can accomodate up to two loops.</p>
<p>2. Install 283LA transit sensors in slots 10 of the I &amp; J input files. Connect sensor cable ends (B1/B2, B3/B4) to the sensor J1 serial ports.</p>
<p>3. Route serial communications cable behind cabinet chasis. Connect 2070 cable end to the 2070 C21S port. Secure cable to cabinet chasis with wire ties.</p>
<p>Callouts: 2070 controller, Input File I, Input File J, 283LA sensor (see note 2), DB9 serial cable (see note 3), see sheet 5/6.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 6 (document page 125)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0125.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0125.webp" alt="S-70.3A sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3A, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p125">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section detail, controller cabinet rear view (not to scale): A rear elevation of the cabinet. At the top is the 2070 controller with its rear connectors C2S, C21S, C12S, C11S, C20S, C22S, C1S and a DATA KEY. Below it are the input file backplane terminal blocks. The C20 harness (transit) and C2 harness (ATSAC) are labeled. A DB9 serial cable (see sheet 5/6) runs from the controller down to the C20 panel. The C20 panel (see sheet 6/6, see note 1) sits on the rack with TB0 (see sheets 4/6) just below it, and the C2 panel is below that. At the bottom, cabling labeled DLC to loops leaves the cabinet, which sits on a concrete base over ground shown with hatching.</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Detail, sheet 2 of 6, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.3A. Printed page number 118.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Install C20 harness and panel on rack above C2 panel.</p>
<p>Callouts: DB9 serial cable, see sheet 5/6. C20 harness (transit). C2 harness (ATSAC). 2070 controller. C20 panel, see sheet 6/6 (see note 1). TB0, see sheets 4/6. C2 panel. DLC to loops.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 6 (document page 126)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0126.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0126.webp" alt="S-70.3A sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3A, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p126">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section detail, controller cabinet rear view (not to scale): Rear elevation of the cabinet. The 2070 controller rear panel shows connectors C2S, C21S, C12S, C11S, C20S, C22S, C1S and the data key. A DB9 serial cable (see note 1) runs from C21S down the left side. The C20 harness (see note 3, transit) comes from C20S and the C2 harness (ATSAC) is labeled below it. A power cable comes from the right side of the controller. On the terminal blocks, lugs at positions D, E, J and K are connected on both input file rows. The two input file harnesses (see note 2) run to a terminal panel at lower right with TB0 (see sheet 4/6).</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Detail, sheet 3 of 6, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.3A. Printed page number 119.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Connect 2070 cable end to the 2070 C21S port.</p>
<p>2. Install input file harness from TB0 to input file backplane.</p>
<p>3. Connect C20 harness to the C20S port.</p>
<p>Callouts: DB9 serial cable (see note 1), C20 harness (see note 3, transit), C2 harness (ATSAC), power cable, input file harness (see note 2), TB0 see sheet 4/6.</p></details>

### Sheet 4 of 6 (document page 127)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0127.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0127.webp" alt="S-70.3A sheet 4"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3A, sheet 4. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p127">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>12-position dual barrier strip TB0: A horizontal strip with 12 terminal positions, each with a top and bottom lug. Positions 1 to 3 are labeled NOT USED. Position 4 is GROUND and receives the DRAIN WIRES from the four cables. Positions 5 and 6 go TO PIN D and TO PIN E on the B1 harness (File I slot 10 backplane). Positions 7 and 8 go TO PIN J and TO PIN K on the B2 harness (File I slot 10 backplane). Positions 9 and 10 go TO PIN D and TO PIN E on the B3 harness (File J slot 10 backplane)*. Positions 11 and 12 go TO PIN J and TO PIN K on the B4 harness (File J slot 10 backplane)*.</p>
<p>Below the strip each pair of terminals is connected by a twisted pair to a cable end labeled B1, B2, B3* and B4*. These are the loop detector lead-in cables, labeled DLC (1-pair #12 AWG).</p>
<p>Footnote: * Second harness to input file J is only required for 3 and 4 loop installation.</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Detail, sheet 4 of 6, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.3A. Printed page number 120.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. All connections shall be soldered.</p>
<p>* Second harness to input file J is only required for 3 and 4 loop installation.</p>
<p>Callouts: B1 harness, B2 harness (file I slot 10 backplane); B3 harness, B4 harness (file J slot 10 backplane)*; GROUND; TO PIN D, E, J, K; NOT USED; DRAIN WIRES; DLC (1-pair #12 AWG); TB0.</p></details>

### Sheet 5 of 6 (document page 128)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0128.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0128.webp" alt="S-70.3A sheet 5"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3A, sheet 5. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p128">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Isometric cable assembly: A cable made of #22 AWG stranded 5 conductor cable (length = 3 ft.) leads from a DB9 male connector labeled 2070, which goes to the 2070 controller C21S port, to a split. The cable splits into two legs each with LENGTH = 1 FT., ending in DB9 male connectors labeled B1/B2 and B3/B4. These two connectors go to the 283LA sensor J1 port.</p>
<p>Pin table (PIN, FUNCTION 2070, FUNCTION 283 LA): 1, N/C, N/C. 2, TX, RX. 3, RX, TX. 4, N/C, N/C. 5, GND, GND. 6, N/C, N/C. 7, CTS, RTS. 8, RTS, CTS. 9, GND, GND.</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Detail, sheet 5 of 6, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.3A. Printed page number 121.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Callouts: Length = 1 ft. (each leg). #22 AWG stranded 5 conductor cable (length = 3 ft.). DB9 male to 283LA sensor J1 port. DB9 male to 2070 controller C21S port.</p>
<p>2. Pin table of 9 pins, 2070 function versus 283 LA function (N/C means no connection). TX and RX are crossed and CTS and RTS are crossed between the 2070 and the 283LA. There are no numbered notes on this sheet.</p></details>

### Sheet 6 of 6 (document page 129)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0129.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0129.webp" alt="S-70.3A sheet 6"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3A, sheet 6. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/09-pages-129-130/#p129">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Terminal panel: A 0.125 (1/8&quot;) white aluminum panel, 5.0&quot; wide by 7.0&quot; high with 1/2&quot; radius corners. Two 1/4&quot; mounting holes are at the top and bottom center (see note 1). CTB-4 is a 6-position dual barrier strip with positions N/C, N/C, AUDIO IN +, AUDIO IN -, AUDIO OUT + and AUDIO OUT -. The four audio terminals are labeled TO CTB-1. CTB-5 is an 8-position single barrier strip with positions +5V, -5V, CD, RTS, TX, RX, CTS and GND. Twisted wire pairs connect CTB-4 to the harness cable that leaves the panel at the lower right, and CTB-5 positions fan into the same cable.</p>
<p>C20 connector table (PIN, FUNCTION): A, AUDIO IN+. B, AUDIO IN-. C, AUDIO OUT+. D, +5 VDC. E, AUDIO OUT-. F, N/C. H, CD. J, RTS. K, TX. L, RX. M, CTS. N, DC GND. P, N/C. N, N/C (as drawn).</p>
<p>C20 harness: Isometric view of the connector body at left, with a harness (length = 6 FT) going to the 2070 controller (C20S port).</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Detail, sheet 6 of 6, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.3A. Printed page number 122.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Provide two (2) 1/4&quot; mounting holes centered 0.325&quot; from top and bottom edges.</p>
<p>2. All connections shall be soldered.</p>
<p>Callouts: 1/2&quot; radius corner. 8-position single barrier strip. 6-position dual barrier strip. To CTB-1. 0.125 (1/8&quot;) white aluminum panel. 1/4&quot; mounting hole (see note 1). C20 harness (length = 6 ft). To 2070 controller (C20S port).</p></details>
