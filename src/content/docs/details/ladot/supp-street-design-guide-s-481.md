<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan of a mini-roundabout with yield control, showing optional mountable or painted center and splitter islands, signs and markings. Mountable islands are preferred, and painted islands may be used when islands are under 50 SF. Painted islands require white arrow symbols. Entry uses a 12 in wide dashed white entrance line (2 ft long, 2 ft gap), 20 ft crosswalk markings, and signs <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r6-5p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R6-5P.svg">R6-5P</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>, W16-7PL and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w2-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W2-6.svg">W2-6</a> on each approach, with details in Supplemental Design Guide Detail 710.</p>

<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. This drawing shows options with both mountable and painted islands on the approaches. However, the actual design should use consistent treatments on each approach. Mountable islands are preferred. Painted islands may be used when islands are less than 50 SF in size. If raised center island is used, roundabout directional arrow signs (R6-4) should be included in the center island.</p>
<p>2. All signs and pavement markings shown in CA MUTCD, current edition shall be implemented as specified.</p>
<p>3. Signs shown for only one approach, but should be provided consistently on each approach.</p>
<p>4. For diameter dimensions of center island, see sheet 4 of 4.</p>
<p>5. Install new curb ramps and crosswalks as shown. Diagonal ramps may not be used. Existing ramps within the circulating lane must be removed.</p>
<p>6. Crosswalks may be supplemented with pedestrian activated rectangular rapid flashing beacons (RRFB&#x27;s).</p>
<p>7. See City of Los Angeles Supplemental Street Design Guide Detail 710 for mini-roundabout details.</p></details>
