<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-79.02A showing the precast reinforced concrete Fiber Optic Splice Box I with a plan of its 3/16 in. diamond plate steel parkway cover, a section and isometrics of the cover, frame, riser and base. The base is 18 in. tall and 2'-8" x 3'-8" overall, with a 6 in. riser and a 2'-0" x 3'-0" opening, set on a 12 in. minimum bed of 1 in. crushed rock. Knockouts, lifting inserts and galvanizing requirements are given in the notes.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 84)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0084.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0084.webp" alt="S-79.02A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.02A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p84">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of lid: The precast splice box shown from above with a sloped outer frame (PCC or other material approved by the engineer) around a diamond-plate steel parkway cover. The cover is marked &#x27;LADOT&#x27; and has two 3/4&quot; lifting holes and notches at its edges. Callouts: 3/4&quot; lifting holes. 3/16&quot; diamond plate steel parkway cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. 1&quot; dia. pick holes.</p>
<p>Section: The box top is flush with pavement. Above it is a 6&quot; top, including frame height, with RPM cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;, set in PCC or other material approved by the engineer. Below it is the riser and the base section. The interior width is 44&quot; and the walls are 6&quot; thick. Conduit enters through the walls at holes sealed with approved compound after installing the conduit, and is shown as &#x27;CONDUIT TO TERMINATION PULLBOX&#x27;. The base sits on a 12&quot; MIN. layer of 1&quot; clean crushed rock sump.</p>
<p>Isometric of cover: A 3/16&quot; diamond plate steel parkway cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot; is shown with an L-shaped cutout, an angle frame underneath and 1&quot; dia. pick holes.</p>
<p>Isometric of frame: A 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot; opening frame, 6&quot; tall, called &#x27;6&quot; top incl. frame height w/ RPM cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;&#x27;, sloped outward to the base.</p>
<p>Isometric of riser: Overall 2&#x27;-8&quot; x 3&#x27;-8&quot;, 6&quot; tall, with a 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot; opening, an 8&quot; notch dimension and a 4&quot; wall. Callout: Riser.</p>
<p>Isometric of base section: Overall 18&quot; tall, with 4 lifting inserts (2 each side) and two lifting holes, shown cut away. Walls are precast reinforced concrete. 4&quot; x 8&quot; knockouts, 2 pcs each side wall. 8&quot; x 11&quot; knockouts, 2 pcs each end wall.</p>
<p>Title block: Fiber Optic Splice Box I, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-79.02A. CKD JEM 2-5-08, Sr. T.E. JEM 2-13-08, Pr. T.E. SFS 2-13-08. Approved June 26, 2008, signed for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Printed page number 77.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Lifting rings and bolts shall be provided.</p>
<p>2. Parkway cover shall be 3/16&quot; safety plate, 5/16&quot; safety plate for traffic cover.</p>
<p>3. All metal surfaces for special frame and cover shall have a galvanized finish.</p>
<p>4. All welding, machining and drilling shall be done before galvanizing.</p>
<p>5. Plug threads must be greased thoroughly prior to installation.</p>
<p>Callouts: 3/4&quot; lifting holes. 1&quot; dia. pick holes. 3/16&quot; diamond plate steel parkway cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. Flush with pavement. 6&quot; top incl. frame height w/ RPM cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. PCC or other material approved by the engineer. Riser. Conduit to termination pullbox. Holes sealed with approved compound after installing conduit. 1&quot; clean crushed rock sump, 12&quot; minimum (MIN.). Base section. 4&quot; x 8&quot; knockouts, 2 pcs. each side wall. 8&quot; x 11&quot; knockouts, 2 pcs. each end wall. 4 lifting inserts, 2 each side. Precast reinforced concrete.</p></details>
