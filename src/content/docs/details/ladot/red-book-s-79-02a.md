<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Lifting rings and bolts shall be provided.</li>
<li value="2">Parkway cover shall be 3/16&quot; safety plate, 5/16&quot; safety plate for traffic cover.</li>
<li value="3">All metal surfaces for special frame and cover shall have a galvanized finish.</li>
<li value="4">All welding, machining and drilling shall be done before galvanizing.</li>
<li value="5">Plug threads must be greased thoroughly prior to installation.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> 3/4&quot; lifting holes. 1&quot; dia. pick holes. 3/16&quot; diamond plate steel parkway cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. Flush with pavement. 6&quot; top incl. frame height w/ RPM cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. PCC or other material approved by the engineer. Riser. Conduit to termination pullbox. Holes sealed with approved compound after installing conduit. 1&quot; clean crushed rock sump, 12&quot; minimum (MIN.). Base section. 4&quot; x 8&quot; knockouts, 2 pcs. each side wall. 8&quot; x 11&quot; knockouts, 2 pcs. each end wall. 4 lifting inserts, 2 each side. Precast reinforced concrete.</div>
</details>
