<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fabrication layouts for LADOT Anti-Gridlock Zone tow-away signs with peak-period No Stopping legends. Four versions are shown: 18" x 24" signs reading 4 to 6 PM or 7 to 9 AM and 4 to 6 PM, and 18" x 36" signs that add a 2 Hour or 1 Hour Parking panel below. Each sheet gives lettering sizes and series, the 1/2" corner radius, the 1/4" border, and notes on manufacturer, required bottom markings, and .080 aluminum with SEG Type II graffiti-film sheeting.</p>
<p class="dwg-topics">Topics: <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/curb-parking-loading/">Curb, parking and loading zones</a></p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face elevation (4 TO 6 PM version): An 18&quot; wide by 24&quot; tall sign (18&quot; shown across the top, 24&quot; overall height at far left) with a 1 1/2&quot; radius at the corners. It is built from two red-bordered panels separated by a 1/4&quot; gap.</p>
<p>Header panel: red field, 6&quot; tall, with white legend ANTI-GRIDLOCK on the first line and ZONE on the second, each 2&quot; Series C, and L.A.M.C. 80.70 below in 1/2&quot; Series C.</p>
<p>Body panel (17 3/4&quot; tall, below the 1/4&quot; gap): the top band is red with a white pictograph of a tow truck towing a car, 3 3/4&quot; tall. Below it is a red block at left holding the white word NO, next to the word STOPPING in red on white. The NO block is labeled 3 1/2&quot; Series B on the left and STOPPING is labeled 3&quot; Series B at right, as drawn. The red-panel portion is dimensioned 8&quot; from the top of the body to the bottom of the NO block, with 1/2&quot; below the NO and STOPPING lettering. Below the red panel the legend is red on white: 4 TO 6 PM, with the numerals 4 and 6 in 3&quot; Series C, PM in 2&quot; Series C and the underlined TO in 1&quot; Series C (the right-hand labels list 3&quot;, 2&quot; and 1&quot; Series C). Next is EXCEPT SATURDAY &amp; SUNDAY in 1/2&quot; Series C, then TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1 in 1/2&quot; Series C. A red border runs around the white field.</p>
<p>Vertical dimensions down the left side: 24&quot; overall, 6&quot; header, 1/4&quot; gap, 17 3/4&quot; body, 3 3/4&quot; graphic, 8&quot; red panel, 3 1/2&quot; Series B callout and 1/2&quot; below the lettering, then 1 3/4&quot; from the bottom of the red panel to the top of the 4 TO 6 PM line, 1&quot; from the 4 TO 6 PM line to EXCEPT SATURDAY &amp; SUNDAY, an inner 1 3/4&quot; dimension between the EXCEPT line and the TO RECOVER line, and 1 3/4&quot; from the TO RECOVER line to the bottom edge. Lettering callouts on the right, top to bottom: 2&quot; Series C, 2&quot; Series C, 1/2&quot; Series C, 3&quot; Series B, 3&quot; Series C, 2&quot; Series C, 1&quot; Series C, 1/2&quot; Series C, 1/2&quot; Series C. A 1 1/2&quot; radius leader points to the top right corner.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved July 21, 2010, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Tow Away No Stopping Peak Period Sign. Sheet 1 of 4. T.E. TC, drawn MT 7-20-10. Drawing No. S-515.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by the Los Angeles Department of Transportation (LADOT).</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 18&quot; x 24&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is as noted. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication, e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
