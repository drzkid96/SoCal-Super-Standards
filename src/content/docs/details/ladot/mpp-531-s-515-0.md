<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fabrication layouts for LADOT Anti-Gridlock Zone tow-away signs with peak-period No Stopping legends. Four versions are shown: 18" x 24" signs reading 4 to 6 PM or 7 to 9 AM and 4 to 6 PM, and 18" x 36" signs that add a 2 Hour or 1 Hour Parking panel below. Each sheet gives lettering sizes and series, the 1/2" corner radius, the 1/4" border, and notes on manufacturer, required bottom markings, and .080 aluminum with SEG Type II graffiti-film sheeting.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face elevation (4 TO 6 PM version): A sign 18&quot; wide and 24&quot; tall with a 1 1/2&quot; radius at the corners. The top header is a separate red panel with white lettering, 6&quot; tall, reading &quot;ANTI-GRIDLOCK ZONE&quot; on two lines (each 2&quot; Series C) with &quot;L.A.M.C. 80.70&quot; below in 1/2&quot; Series C. Below the red panel the legend is red on white: &quot;4 TO 6 PM&quot; in 3&quot;, 2&quot; and 1&quot; Series C (numerals, PM, TO), then &quot;EXCEPT SATURDAY &amp; SUNDAY&quot; in 1/2&quot; Series C, then &quot;TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1&quot; in 1/2&quot; Series C. <details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 18&quot; X 24&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is as noted. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p>
<p>Sign labels: 18&quot; wide, 24&quot; tall, 1 1/2&quot; radius, header 6&quot;, gap 1/4&quot;, body 17 3/4&quot;, red panel 8&quot;, 3 3/4&quot; graphic, 2&quot; Series C (ANTI-GRIDLOCK), 2&quot; Series C (ZONE), 1/2&quot; Series C (L.A.M.C. 80.70), 3&quot; Series B (NO STOPPING), 3&quot; / 2&quot; / 1&quot; Series C (4 TO 6 PM), 1/2&quot; Series C (EXCEPT SATURDAY &amp; SUNDAY), 1/2&quot; Series C (TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1).</p></details>
