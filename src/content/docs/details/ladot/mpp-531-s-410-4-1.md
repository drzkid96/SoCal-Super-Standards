<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Elevation of a 2 inch by 42 inch galvanized steel parking meter post set in an 8 inch minimum square or round concrete footing, 18 inches minimum deep, 20 inches back from the curb face. The post top stands 30 inches above the top of curb and has a 1/4 inch hole drilled one side only 26 inches below the top.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Drawing S-410.4.1, Parking Meter Post Installation, Dirt or Asphalt Setting (City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 12/03/2020 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager, sheet 1 of 1).</p>
<p>Elevation: A vertical steel post with a reamed inside at the top (callout Reamed Inside of Top) stands in a concrete post footing set in dirt or asphalt next to a curb. The post is 42&quot; overall. From the top of curb to the top of post is 30&quot; (dimension From Top of Curb), and the post extends 12&quot; below the top of curb. A 1/4&quot; hole is drilled on one side only, 26&quot; below the top of the post (Drill 1/4&quot; Hole (One Side Only)). The post flange is set in cement grout at the top of the footing, with a shaped grout cap around the post at the surface. A 20&quot; dimension runs from the post to the curb face. Curb section at right: Top of Curb, Curb Face (near vertical, slightly sloped) and Gutter are labeled. The concrete post footing is 8&quot; MIN. square or round, extends 18&quot; MIN. below the top of curb, and the post bottom is 6&quot; MIN. above the footing bottom. Callout: Concrete Post Footing.</p>
<p>Specification for post and notes appear at the lower left.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>SPECIFICATION FOR POST</h5>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 42&quot; long, ASTM-A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<h5>NOTES</h5>
<p>1. Concrete to be Class 560-C-3250.</p>
<p>2. Post to be level straight.</p>
<p>3. Area to be left clean.</p>
<p>4. Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</p></details>
