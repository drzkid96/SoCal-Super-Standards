<p class="dwg-sum">Detail for setting a parking meter post in dirt or asphalt with a concrete footing and cement grout. The post is 2 inch standard weight galvanized steel pipe, 42 inches long, with a reamed top, set in a footing at least 8 inches square or round. The notes call for the specified concrete class, a level and straight post, a clean area and a grout mix of 1 part cement to 2 1/2 parts sand.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Parking meter post installation, dirt or asphalt setting</p>
<p>Detail labels: reamed inside of top. Post flange set in cement grout. Drill 1/4&quot; hole (one side only). 20&quot; dimension. Concrete post footing 8&quot; min. square or round. Top of curb, curb face, gutter.</p>
<p>Specification for post</p>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 42&quot; long, ASTM A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Concrete to be Class 560-C-3250.</p>
<p>2. Post to be level, straight.</p>
<p>3. Area to be left clean.</p>
<p>4. Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</p></details>
