<h5 class="dwg-sub">SPECIFICATION FOR POST</h5>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 42&quot; long, ASTM-A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Concrete to be Class 560-C-3250.</li>
<li value="2">Post to be level straight.</li>
<li value="3">Area to be left clean.</li>
<li value="4">Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</li>
</ol>
