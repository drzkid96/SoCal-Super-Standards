<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-79.9 showing an underground service from a Department of Water and Power (DWP) manhole to a PB-3 service pullbox. DWP splices 12 in. #6 copper tails to three #2 aluminum wires, feeding 60 Amp in-line fuses for the traffic signal, a neutral bonded to the ground rod and 3 #6 wires for street lighting. The pullbox is 14 in. minimum deep, with a bonding wire, ground rod and a 2 in. traffic signal service conduit.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 94)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0094.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0094.webp" alt="S-79.9 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.9, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p94">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Wiring diagram: Same wiring arrangement as the overhead service sheet. Three #2 aluminum wires by DWP (the center one is Neutral) end in splices to 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP. Each outer leg splits into two copper wires, one fused through a &#x27;60 Amp fuse in-line Waterproof Fuseholder for Traffic Signals&#x27; leading to #6 traffic signal service wires, and the other leading to 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (Bureau of Street Lighting, see Street Lighting Plan). A #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified) is on the right leg. The neutral is bonded to the ground rod (Bond Neutral to Ground Rod). At upper right is a pullbox cover labeled &#x27;Service T.S. &amp; S.L.&#x27; captioned &#x27;Pullbox Cover for Combination Service&#x27;.</p>
<p>Section: At grade, a Service Pullbox PB-3 set flush, 14&quot; MIN. deep. At the left a conduit and wires by DWP come from the manhole into the pullbox. Inside, a bonding wire joins the conduit bushings and a ground rod is installed below. At the right, one Traffic Signal service conduit (2&quot;) and one street lighting service conduit leave the box, the lighting conduit when specified on the Bureau of Street Lighting plan.</p>
<p>Title block: Under Ground Service, Dept. of Water &amp; Power, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-79.9. Drawn MT 8-17-11, checked RSM 9-14-11. Approved 10-27-11, Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Printed page number 87.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Department of Water and Power (DWP) to install 3 #2 stranded aluminum wires from service pole to pullbox. At the terminus of each #2 aluminum wire the Department of Water and Power to splice 2 #6 stranded copper wires, (12&quot; long) using compression type connectors. The exposed ends of each copper wire shall be taped when not in use.</p>
<p>2. Install continuous and dedicated #6 green service ground conductor to controller cabinet.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction, Sec. 307-2.8.</p>
<p>4. See Standard Drawing S-79.9B for grounding details.</p>
<p>Callouts: 3 #2 alum. wires by DWP. Neutral. 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP. 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals. #6 traffic signal service wires. Bond neutral to ground rod. 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (see Street Lighting Plan). #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified). Pullbox cover for combination service, Service T.S. &amp; S.L. Service pullbox PB-3. Conduit and wires by DWP from manhole. Bonding wire. Install ground rod. One traffic signal service conduit (2&quot;). One street lighting service conduit when specified on Bureau of Street Lighting plan. 14&quot; minimum.</p></details>
