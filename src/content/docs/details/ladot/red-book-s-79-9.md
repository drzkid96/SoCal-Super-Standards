<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Department of Water and Power (DWP) to install 3 #2 stranded aluminum wires from service pole to pullbox. At the terminus of each #2 aluminum wire the Department of Water and Power to splice 2 #6 stranded copper wires, (12&quot; long) using compression type connectors. The exposed ends of each copper wire shall be taped when not in use.</p>
<p>2. Install continuous and dedicated #6 green service ground conductor to controller cabinet.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction, Sec. 307-2.8.</p>
<p>4. See Standard Drawing S-79.9B for grounding details.</p>
<p>Callouts: 3 #2 alum. wires by DWP. Neutral. 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP. 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals. #6 traffic signal service wires. Bond neutral to ground rod. 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (see Street Lighting Plan). #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified). Pullbox cover for combination service, Service T.S. &amp; S.L. Service pullbox PB-3. Conduit and wires by DWP from manhole. Bonding wire. Install ground rod. One traffic signal service conduit (2&quot;). One street lighting service conduit when specified on Bureau of Street Lighting plan. 14&quot; minimum.</p></details>
