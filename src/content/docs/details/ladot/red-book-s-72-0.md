<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows how a pedestrian pushbutton is installed on steel and concrete signal standards, with tapped holes and a typical arrangement at the curb. Pushbutton mounting height is adjusted to 42 in above the surface pedestrians are expected to use to reach the button, with a typical 3 ft 6 in height shown.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 62)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Pushbutton, installation. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>No. 7 drill, 1/4 in-20 N.C. tap, 2 holes. Parallel to crosswalk. Steel standards. Standard inserts for concrete standards, tapped 1/4 in-20 N.C., for which intended. Concrete standards. Curb. Typical arrangement. Pushbutton. 3 ft 6 in typical.</p>
<p>Adjust pushbutton mounting height to 42 in above the surface pedestrians are expected to access pushbutton from.</p></details>
