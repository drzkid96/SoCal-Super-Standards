<p class="dwg-sum">Shows how a pedestrian pushbutton is installed on steel and concrete signal standards, with tapped holes and a typical arrangement at the curb. Pushbutton mounting height is adjusted to 42 in above the surface pedestrians are expected to use to reach the button, with a typical 3 ft 6 in height shown.</p>
