<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-473.0, approved 2/22/89, for a ONE WAY arrow sign hung from a signal mast arm. It gives the 48 inch by 18 inch arrow sign face, the mast arm bracket and banding per S-457.0, a 1-1/4 inch aluminum angle riveted to the back, and .080 inch aluminum sign material. The sign face is diamond grade reflective with 8 inch Series D letters.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Front view: a pentagon-shaped arrow sign pointing right with a black border and the white legend ONE WAY inside an arrow-shaped panel. The overall sign is 48&quot; long measured to the shoulder with a further 12&quot; for the arrow point, and 18&quot; tall. Two mounting posts are located 5&quot; from each end of the rectangular part of the sign with 36&quot; between them (5&quot;, 36&quot;, 5&quot;). The sign face is called out SIGN FACE SEE NOTE 1.</p>
<p>Mast arm installation: an elevation of a signal head with backplate on the left and a signal mast arm sloping down from a pole at the right. The ONE WAY arrow sign hangs from the mast arm by two brackets next to the signal head, with a 30&quot; dimension from the signal backplate to the sign edge. Labels: SIGNAL BACKPLATE and SIGNAL MAST ARM.</p>
<p>Side view: a cross-section through the sign and mast arm showing the round mast arm with BANDING SEE STANDARD PLAN S-457.0, a MAST ARM SIGN BRACKET PER STANDARD PLAN S-457.0 with nut and bolt connection, and a 1-1/4&quot; x 1-1/4&quot; x 1/8&quot; aluminum angle fixed at the sign. The sign is SIGN MATERIAL .080&quot; ALUMINUM 6061 OR 5052-H38 ALLOY.</p>
<p>Rear view: the back of the arrow sign with an angle at the top edge, labeled ANGLE SECURED TO BACK OF SIGN WITH POP RIVETS AT 6&quot; SPACING, with the rivets drawn along the angle.</p>
<p>Title block: approved 2/22/89, signed S. E. Rowe, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, One-Way Arrow Mast Arm Mounted, sheet 1 of 1, drawing number S-473.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign face shall be diamond grade reflective material. Letters shall be 8&quot; Series &quot;D&quot; caps. Sign face may be applied to the front or front and back of sign as specified.</p></details>
