<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows a ONE WAY arrow sign mounted on a signal mast arm, with front, rear, side and mast arm installation views. The sign is 48" long by 18" high, made of .080" aluminum, with a Diamond Grade face and 8" Series D capital letters. It hangs from a mast arm sign bracket per S-457.0 and is secured to a 1-1/4" x 1-1/4" x 1/8" aluminum angle on the back with pop rivets at 6" spacing.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Front view: ONE WAY arrow sign 48&quot; long by 18&quot; high, with 5&quot;, 36&quot; and 5&quot; dimensions across the top and 12&quot; at the arrow point. Sign face, see Note 1.</p>
<p>Mast arm installation: sign hung from the signal mast arm beside the signal head and backplate, 30&quot; dimension shown.</p>
<p>Side view: banding, see Standard Plan S-457.0. Mast arm sign bracket per Standard Plan S-457.0. Sign material .080&quot; aluminum, 6061 or 5052-H38 alloy. 1-1/4&quot; x 1-1/4&quot; x 1/8&quot; aluminum angle.</p>
<p>Rear view: angle secured to back of sign with pop rivets at 6&quot; spacing.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Sign face shall be Diamond Grade reflective material. Letters shall be 8&quot; Series &quot;D&quot; caps. Sign face may be applied to the front or front and back of sign as specified.</p></details>
