<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-78.8 showing the galvanized steel special frame and cover for Type 2 and Type 3 pull boxes in sidewalk. Plan, elevation, an 8 degree sloped-edge section and a lift plug detail are given, with a table of dimensions A to G for each pull box type. The cover is 3/16 in. plate with brass lift plugs and stamped SIGNAL lettering, set flush with sidewalk grade on a 4 in. x 3/16 in. steel plate frame.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 83)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0083.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0083.webp" alt="S-78.8 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-78.8, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p83">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A rectangular special cover seen from above inside its frame, with a wavy break line showing the cover cut away at the right. &#x27;SIGNAL&#x27; is lettered at the center and a centerline passes through two lifting holes marked &#x27;2-3/4&quot; Brass lift plug&#x27;. Callout 2 (revision triangle) points to &#x27;1&quot; Stamped letters&#x27;. Variable dimensions are lettered: &quot;A&quot; is the cover outside width, &quot;B&quot; the frame inside width, &quot;C&quot; the frame inside length, &quot;D&quot; the distance from the top edge of the frame to the centerline. A 4&quot; typical band marks the steel plate frame width.</p>
<p>Cover elevation: The cover is dimensioned &quot;E&quot; overall at the top with 3&quot; typical from the end to each lift plug, and &quot;F&quot; at the bottom, with a beveled (sloped) edge. Cover thickness is 1 13/16&quot;.</p>
<p>Section through frame and cover (8 degree detail): The frame and cover edges are sloped at 8 degrees from vertical on all four sides. A 3/16&quot; plate forms the cover and a 4&quot; x 3/16&quot; steel plate forms the frame, with 1/8&quot; typical clearance between them. The cover rests on a 1/2&quot; x 1/2&quot; continuous steel bar, welded with a 3/16&quot; fillet weld, 3&quot; long, at the end and mid-point (typical). The height shown is 1 13/16&quot;.</p>
<p>Lift plug detail: A steel sleeve with internal thread 3/4&quot;-10 UNC, full length, to match the brass lift plug. The plug has a 3/8&quot; square slot, 3/8&quot; deep. The cover plate is 3/16&quot; steel plate and the plug depth is 5/8&quot; with 3/16&quot; below, within a total thickness of 1 13/16&quot;. A 3/16&quot; weld symbol is shown at the sleeve.</p>
<p>Section in sidewalk: The frame sits flush with sidewalk grade over the pull box. Dimensions: &quot;C&quot; frame inside across the top, &quot;G&quot; frame inside across the base, 1 13/16&quot;, 2 3/16&quot; and 4&quot; depths, a 1/2&quot; x 1/2&quot; continuous steel bar, and 4&quot; x 3/16&quot; steel plate typical on all sides. Concrete surrounds the frame above the pull box.</p>
<h5>Table of dimensions:</h5>
<p>For Type 2 Pullbox: A = 18 3/4&quot;, B = 19&quot;, C = 29&quot;, D = 9 1/2&quot;, E = 28 3/4&quot;, F = 28 1/4&quot;, G = 27 7/8&quot;.</p>
<p>For Type 3 Pullbox: A = 25 3/4&quot;, B = 26&quot;, C = 38&quot;, D = 13&quot;, E = 37 3/4&quot;, F = 37 1/4&quot;, G = 36 7/8&quot;. Revision 1 applies to the table.</p>
<p>Title block: Special Cover for Pull Box. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Donald R. Howery, General Manager, approved August 19, 1983. Drawing No. S-78.8. Drawn BS 7-27-83, checked SB 8-8-83, supervised RO 8-18-83, reviewed JAC 8-19-83. Revisions: 1 Revised RC JK (JAC 8-8-84), 2 Reviewed by (TLJ 6-26-02). Printed page number 76.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. All metal surfaces for special frame and cover shall have a galvanized finish.</p>
<p>2. All welding, machining and drilling shall be done before galvanizing.</p>
<p>3. Plug threads must be greased thoroughly prior to installation.</p>
<p>Callouts: 2-3/4&quot; brass lift plug. 1&quot; stamped letters (revision 2). 1/2&quot; x 1/2&quot; continuous steel bar. Steel sleeve internal thread 3/4&quot;-10 UNC full length to match brass lift plug. Slot 3/8&quot; square and 3/8&quot; deep. 4&quot; x 3/16&quot; steel plate typical all sides. Typical all four (4) sides of frame and cover. End and mid-point (typical). Sidewalk grade. Pull box.</p></details>
