<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-72.1.1 shows the LADOT pedestrian pushbutton station casting, with its sign face and 2 in round button, in post-top and side mounting versions. The back of the casting is shaped to fit a 2 1/2 in diameter standard or the curvature of the standard. Notes cover the sign mounting hardware (four 8-32 x 3/4 in) and the housing and assembly specifications 92-052-02 and 92-053-02.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 63)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Post-top mounting view: a pedestrian pushbutton station casting seen from the front, with a rectangular sign face above a round button housing. The back of the casting is shaped to fit a 2 1/2&quot; dia. standard (callout at top). The sign face has five panels from top to bottom: (1) walking person symbol with START CROSSING, underlined, and Watch For Vehicles; (2) a flashing hand symbol (labeled FLASHING) with DON&#x27;T START, underlined, Finish Crossing, If Started underlined; (3) a timer symbol (labeled TIMER) with TIME REMAINING To Finish Crossing; (4) a steady hand symbol (labeled STEADY) with DON&#x27;T CROSS; (5) a long arrow with TO CROSS above and PUSH BUTTON below. Mounting screw holes sit at the corners of the sign. A round pushbutton with four small screw holes around its ring is below the sign. Caption: POST-TOP MOUNTING (See S-51.7 for installation).</p>
<p>Side mounting view (upper right): the same station seen at an angle. The back of the casting is shaped to fit curvature of standard (callout). Caption: SIDE MOUNTING (See S-72.0A for installation).</p>
<p>Side mounting view, lower right: the same station again, captioned SIDE MOUNTING, TYPE 5 AND 6 STANDARDS ONLY (See S-72.0A for installation).</p>
<p>There are no dimensions on the sheet. The text sections at lower left are SIGN, HOUSING and ASSEMBLY. Title block: Pushbutton, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved December 9, 2005 for Frances T. Banerjee, Interim General Manager, Drawing No. S-72.1.1.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>SIGN</h5>
<p>1. Pedestrian pushbuttons: see Standard Drawings S-73.0 and S-73.1. Bicycle pushbuttons: see Standard Drawings S-72.2.</p>
<p>2. Mounting hardware consists of four (4) 8-32 x 3/4&quot; long.</p>
<h5>HOUSING</h5>
<p>Construction, materials and finish painting shall conform to LADOT material specification 92-052-02 or latest version.</p>
<h5>ASSEMBLY</h5>
<p>Construction, materials and finish painting shall conform to LADOT material specification 92-053-02 or latest version. Pushbuttons shall be 2&quot; in diameter.</p>
<p>View captions: Post-top mounting (see S-51.7 for installation). Side mounting (see S-72.0A for installation). Side mounting, Type 5 and 6 standards only (see S-72.0A for installation). Back of casting shaped to fit 2 1/2&quot; dia. standard (post-top) or to fit curvature of standard (side mounting).</p></details>
