<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows the LADOT pedestrian pushbutton assembly in post-top and side mounting on signal standards, with push button station housing and 2 in diameter pushbuttons. It shows the pedestrian signal instruction messages, such as Walk, Don't Start, Flashing Don't Cross, and Time Remaining. Mounting hardware is four 8-32 x 3/4 in screws, and construction follows LADOT material specifications 92-052-02 and 92-053-02.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 63)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Pushbutton. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>Post-top mounting (see S-51.7 for installation): back of casting shaped to fit 2 1/2 in dia. standard. Sign text: Start Crossing, Watch for Vehicles, Flashing: if started finish crossing, Time remaining to finish crossing, Don&#x27;t Start, Steady: Don&#x27;t cross, Timer, Push button to cross.</p>
<p>Side mounting (see S-72.0A for installation): back of casting shaped to fit curvature of standard. Same sign text.</p>
<p>Type 5 and 6 standards only, side mounting (see S-72.0A for installation).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Pedestrian pushbuttons: see Standard Drawings S-73.0 and S-73.1. Bicycle pushbuttons: see Standard Drawings S-72.2.</p>
<p>2. Mounting hardware consists of four (4) 8-32 x 3/4 in long.</p>
<p>Housing: construction, materials and finish painting shall conform to LADOT material specification 92-052-02 or latest version.</p>
<p>Assembly: construction, materials and finish painting shall conform to LADOT material specification 92-053-02 or latest version. Pushbuttons shall be 2 in in diameter.</p></details>
