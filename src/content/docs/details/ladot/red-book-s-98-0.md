<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-98.0 for a special cantilever sign with a 12' arm mounted at 20'-6" above grade. The post is 8" Schedule 80 Grade B pipe and the arm is 6" Schedule 40 Grade B pipe, on a PF-12 caisson 30" in diameter and 10'-0" deep. Design wind loading is 20 psf, with details for the arm connection, arm end cap and base plate, and sign dimensions on S-45.0.</p>




## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 98)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0098.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0098.webp" alt="S-98.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-98.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p98">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation: A tall single-post cantilever sign standard shown with the sign arm at the top and a caisson foundation below grade. The post is 8&quot; Schedule 80 Grade B pipe. The arm mounting height is dimensioned 20&#x27;-6&quot; from finished grade to the arm centerline, and the post extends 1&#x27;-0&quot; above the arm centerline to a removable top cap (Detail &quot;A&quot;). The arm is 6&quot; Schedule 40 Grade B pipe, shown with break lines, projecting to the right across a dashed sign panel outline (also with break lines). The arm end has a cap (Detail &quot;B&quot;) and the sign panel extends 2&#x27;-6&quot; MAX. beyond the end of the arm. Detail &quot;C&quot; (base) is circled at the foot of the post. A 3&#x27;-0&quot; dimension is shown from the post centerline to the edge of the base area and a 3&quot; MIN. dimension is noted at the base. Below grade is the PF-12 foundation, 10&#x27;-0&quot; foundation depth, with an 18&quot; MIN. dimension near the top of the caisson, anchor bolts hooked at the bottom, and #5 @ 4&quot; pitch spiral reinforcement with 3&quot; clear. Caisson concrete is called out with a revision triangle 1 as 560-C-3250. Section cut A-A is taken through the caisson.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;A&quot; (arm connection at top of post): Shown in side and plan views. The side view shows the 3/16&quot; PL. (removable top) plate, 9-5/8&quot; wide, with 2 set screws and a plate 2&quot; x 3/16&quot; bent to circular shape with 7-1/4&quot; O.D. welded to the post. The arm attaches to the post by 4 - 3/4&quot; dia. x 4&quot; ASTM A-325 bolts, each with hex nut and 2 hardened 9 ga. washers, through 2 plates 4-3/8&quot; x 5/16&quot; x 13&quot; long. Fillet welds shown: 1/4&quot;, 3/8&quot;, 3/16&quot; and 1/8&quot; at various joints. Plan-type views show dimensions 1-1/4&quot;, 1&quot; and 1-1/2&quot; for plate offsets and thicknesses. The lower view shows the 2 plates 13&quot; x 1/4&quot; x 17&quot; long with (4) 7/8&quot; dia. holes, 2&quot; corner radius (2&quot;R), 2&quot; edge distances, a hole spacing of 13&quot; horizontally and 9&quot; vertically (4-1/2&quot; each side of centerline), a 2&quot; dia. hole finished smooth at the center, 2 gusset plates 5&quot; x 5/16&quot; x 8-5/8&quot; long cut to fit flush, a 3/4&quot; std. half coupling, and 1/4&quot; fillet welds. The sign arm is labeled on the side view.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;B&quot; (arm end cap): 1/4&quot; plate with 1/8&quot; fillet weld, 7-1/8&quot; high (round plate, 7-1/8&quot; dia. in the end view) and a 1&quot; NPT pipe plug at its center.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;C&quot; (base): Section through the base plate and anchorage. Shows a 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover (1&quot; radius corners in hand hole) in the post wall, four anchor bolts 1-1/2&quot; dia. x 54&quot; (includes a 6&quot; right angle bend), thrd. 8&quot;, galv. top 12&quot;, with 2 hex. nuts and 2 std. washers each. Vertical dimensions shown include 10&quot;, 5&quot;, 1-1/4&quot;, 1-1/2&quot; and 3-1/2&quot; (base plate to top of concrete). Also shown are a 3/8&quot; dia. drain hole, bonding wire, base plate, and welds of 1/4&quot; and 5/16&quot;.</p>
<p>Base Plate (plan): Square plate 14-1/8&quot; sq. with 2&quot;R corners, (4) 1-3/4&quot; dia. holes on a 13-1/2&quot; dia. bolt circle (B.C.), and the post seat 9-9/16&quot; sq.</p>
<p>Section A-A: Circular caisson cross section, 30&quot; pile dia., with 8 #7 x 9&#x27;-6&quot; long equally spaced vertical bars, 3&quot; cl. to spiral, with the anchor bolts inside the cage on a 9-9/16&quot; sq. pattern.</p>
<p>Specification block (upper right): Design loading, wind loading 20 psf. Design stresses: concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3,000 psi, fc = 1,200 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 20,000 psi; structural steel fb = 18,000 psi; soil bearing pressure 3,000 psi.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager. Approved 11-15-99. Drawing No. S-98.0. Original approved 1-24-1978 by Philip H. Skarin. Page number 91 at bottom.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Specification. Design: Design loading, wind loading 20 pounds per square foot (psf). Design stresses: concrete n = 10, f&#x27;c = 3,000 pounds per square inch (psi), fc = 1,200 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 20,000 psi; structural steel fb = 18,000 psi; soil bearing pressure 3,000 psi.</p>
<p>Callouts: Caisson concrete shall be 560-C-3250 (revision 1). Four anchor bolts 1-1/2&quot; dia. x 54&quot; (includes a 6&quot; right angle bend), threaded (thrd.) 8&quot;, galvanized (galv.) top 12&quot;, 2 hex. nuts and 2 standard (std.) washers each. 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover, 1&quot; radius corners in hand hole.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>2. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B.</p>
<p>3. Welding shall be in conformance with AWS specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1966, revised 1967.</p>
<p>4. Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</p>
<p>5. Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>6. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A7 or A36.</p>
<p>7. See S-45.0 for sign dimensions, positioning and mounting.</p></details>
