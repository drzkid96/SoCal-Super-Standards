<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Specification. Design: Design loading, wind loading 20 pounds per square foot (psf). Design stresses: concrete n = 10, f&#x27;c = 3,000 pounds per square inch (psi), fc = 1,200 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 20,000 psi; structural steel fb = 18,000 psi; soil bearing pressure 3,000 psi.</p>
<p>Callouts: Caisson concrete shall be 560-C-3250 (revision 1). Four anchor bolts 1-1/2&quot; dia. x 54&quot; (includes a 6&quot; right angle bend), threaded (thrd.) 8&quot;, galvanized (galv.) top 12&quot;, 2 hex. nuts and 2 standard (std.) washers each. 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover, 1&quot; radius corners in hand hole.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>2. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B.</p>
<p>3. Welding shall be in conformance with AWS specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1966, revised 1967.</p>
<p>4. Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</p>
<p>5. Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>6. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A7 or A36.</p>
<p>7. See S-45.0 for sign dimensions, positioning and mounting.</p></details>
