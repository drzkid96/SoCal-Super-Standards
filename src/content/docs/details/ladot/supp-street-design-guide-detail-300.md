<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Detail 300 sheet 1 of 6 shows a mountable truck apron at a corner with directional curb ramps located outside the apron, plus Apron Section A for a 1 tier apron. The apron is 5 ft minimum to 15 ft maximum wide, with a 3 inch minimum curb, 8 inch concrete on 8 inch minimum subbase and a 0.25% to 1% slope.</p>

### Sheet 6 of 6 (document page 119)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0119.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0119.webp" alt="Detail 300 sheet 6"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide Detail 300, sheet 6. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/07-pages-109-126/#p119">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Notes sheet: The sheet is a single bordered page headed NOTES with eight numbered notes and no plan, section or detail. The title block reads SUPPLEMENTAL DESIGN GUIDE, DETAIL 300, SHEET 6 OF 6. No detail title is printed in the title block of this sheet (the earlier sheets of Detail 300 carry the Truck Aprons title). A CAD file path stamp at the lower left reads C:\Users\ccueva\Desktop\Carlos\SSD\Supplemental Design\Final\Truck Apron + Pillow_BOE2.dwg, dated 3/31/2020 9:10 PM.</p>
<p>The notes use R1 for the back-of-apron radius and R2 for the apron radius. They set the mountable curb height at 1 to 3 inches along R2, the full height curb at not less than 3 inches along R1 (5 inches preferable), require drainage to follow the apron radius, require the apron to be concrete and visually distinct, and tie the curb ramp transition to Standard Plan S-442. Note 7 refers to Alternative A and note 8 to a raised mountable truck apron at the trailing end near the R1 point of tangency.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. The height of the mountable curb may vary along the apron radius (R2) with a minimum height of 1 inch and a maximum height of 3 inches. A minimum curb height of at least 2 inches should be provided at the point of curvature if grading permits. Curb height must be warped to provide a 0 inch curb reveal approaching curb ramps. Curb height will vary based on the width of the apron, the cross slope of the roadway within the intersection, and the opportunities to make grading changes behind the curb within the sidewalk area.</p>
<p>2. The height of the full height curb varies along the back of the apron (R1) but shall be a minimum of 3 inches (5&quot; preferable). Curb heights less than 5&quot; require approval of the City Engineer, but shall not be less than 3 inches.</p>
<p>3. The stormwater flow line follows the apron radius (R2). When the cross slope of the roadway within the intersection does not allow for positive drainage around the apron radius (R2), a truck pillow is required.</p>
<p>4. The mountable truck apron or pillow shall be distinct from the adjacent roadway and sidewalk. The apron or pillow shall be constructed with concrete and may use integral color. Surface texture may be applied to areas outside of the curb ramp and crosswalk. Pavement markings, raised pavement markers, and/or surface applied color may be applied to achieve conspicuity.</p>
<p>5. The curb ramp transition area towards the bottom of the curb ramp shall meet Standard Plan S-442 for curb ramps. The landing for the curb ramps will be located beyond the street flowline. If the curb ramp is partially located within the truck apron or pillow, the accessible path of travel shall be smooth and the detectable warning surface shall be placed outside of the truck apron or pillow area.</p>
<p>6. The truck apron is shown sloping towards the mountable curb. The truck apron may be designed to instead slope towards the sidewalk with the same minimum and maximum slopes if positive drainage can be maintained and discharged back to the flowline beyond the truck apron.</p>
<p>7. If at least 4 feet of raised mountable truck apron or truck pillow can be provided, then Alternative A showing the raised design should be implemented.</p>
<p>8. The truck apron along the trailing end of the mountable truck apron (near the R1 point of tangency) may be constructed as a raised mountable truck apron instead of flush truck apron. This should be considered if the space between the crosswalks is not sufficient to construct the Alternative A design.</p></details>
