<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows a 30 inch by 30 inch commemorative street sign with brown letters on a tan background, the mission shape outline and the City Seal on top. The sample reads "Duke" Ellington Square with the description EDWARD KENNEDY ELLINGTON, BIG BAND JAZZ LEADER. Notes cover the lettering sizes for the facility name and the description, a ban on naming current elected officials, and the requirement for prior City Council or historical committee approval.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sample sign: A single commemorative sign drawn in the mission shape (a square panel with rounded corners and a double outline, with a raised semicircular notch at the top center that holds the City seal). The overall size is dimensioned 30&quot; wide across the top and 30&quot; tall along the left side. The City of Los Angeles seal sits at the top center. Below it in serif lettering is the name &quot;Duke&quot; Ellington Square on two lines, and below that in all upper case smaller serif lettering EDWARD KENNEDY ELLINGTON, BIG BAND JAZZ LEADER on two lines. Five numbered notes sit below the drawing. The title block reads Approved 8/21/2000, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Commemorative Signs, sheet 1 of 1, drawn by M.T., Drawing No. S-506.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. The sign shall have brown letters on a tan background with the mission shape outline and the City Seal on top.</p>
<p>2. The name of the intersection or facility shall use 4 inch upper case and 3 inch lower case serif letters.</p>
<p>3. Below the name identification shall be a short description of the person&#x27;s significance, using all upper case serif letters. Refer to the Council File for guidance as to the commemorated person&#x27;s significance.</p>
<p>4. The sign shall not include the name of any current elected official.</p>
<p>5. The designation shall have been approved by the City Council or an official state or federal historical committee prior to installation.</p>
<p>Dimension labels: 30&quot; wide, 30&quot; tall.</p></details>
