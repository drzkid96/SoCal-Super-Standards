</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Sample sign: 30 inch wide, &quot;Duke&quot; Ellington Square, with the description &quot;Edward Kennedy Ellington, Big Band Jazz Leader&quot;.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. The sign shall have brown letters on a tan background with the mission shape outline and the City Seal on top.</p>
<p>2. The name of the intersection or facility shall use 4 inch upper case and 3 inch lower case serif letters.</p>
<p>3. Below the name identification shall be a short description of the person&#x27;s significance, using all upper case serif letters. Refer to the Council File for guidance as to the commemorated person&#x27;s significance.</p>
<p>4. The sign shall not include the name of any current elected official.</p>
<p>5. The designation shall have been approved by the City Council or an official state or federal historical committee prior to installation.</p></details>
