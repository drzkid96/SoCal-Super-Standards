<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-62.1 shows typical clamp-mounting fittings for signal heads. They are a pole clamp with a 5/8 in. U-bolt, a Condulet-TB body with a 3-1/2 in. nipple, a 72-tooth lock ring, and a special 90 degree elbow and special tee with 1-1/2 in. pipe threads. One note requires State of California Standard Specifications.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 51)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0051.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0051.webp" alt="S-62.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-62.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p51">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Pole clamp: an isometric view of a pole clamp, a 5/8 in. U-bolt with two slotted ear plates and nuts on the threaded ends, wrapped around a clamp casting with a 1-1/2 in. threaded center boss. The threaded ends are labeled Min. thread length 4 in.</p>
<p>Condulet-TB: an isometric view of a T-shaped conduit body with a removable cover plate fixed by two screws, and a threaded nipple stub. A callout reads 3-1/2 in. length nipple typical for 2-way heads.</p>
<p>Lock ring: an isometric view of a ring with a 72-tooth toothed rim and two keyed notches.</p>
<p>Special 90 degree elbow: an isometric view of an elbow with 1-1/2 in. pipe threads at both ends, one end having a 72-tooth toothed rim.</p>
<p>Special tee: an isometric view of a tee with 1-1/2 in. pipe threads at its ends and a 72-tooth toothed rim on the branch.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Fittings, Typical, Clamp Mounting, drawing number S-62.1, drawn 2-67, revised 3-14-83, approved April 5, 1967 by A. L. Hutchison, Asst. General Manager. Page number 44.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Construction, material and finish painting shall conform to &#x27;Standard Specifications&#x27; (latest edition), Dept. of Transportation, State of California.</p>
<p>Callouts: 5/8 in. U-bolt. Min. thread length 4 in. 3-1/2 in. length nipple typical for 2-way heads. 72 teeth (lock ring, special 90 degree elbow and special tee). 1-1/2 in. pipe threads (special 90 degree elbow and special tee).</p></details>
