<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Group of three LADOT sheets. The first gives structural details for tapered steel pole Types 1, 1A, 8, 8A and 9 (0.1196 in wall, base plate, hand hole, pole top tenon). The second gives the sign arm data and sign sizes for a Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacon (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> and W16-7p). The third gives foundation Types F-1 and F-8 for signal standards and the Type 337 post-top cabinet, with a 36 in by 24 in by 4 in slab at each cabinet door opening in unpaved areas.</p>
<p class="dwg-topics">Topics: <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/crosswalks/">Crosswalks and pedestrian crossings</a> <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/signals/">Traffic signals and pedestrian signals</a></p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 3 (document page 32)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0032.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0032.webp" alt="S-52.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/02-pages-17-32/#p32">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Standards Type 1, 1A, 8, 8A and 9, drawing S-51.1.3A (sheet 1 of 1). The sheet combines several details and five pole elevations.</p>
<p>Base Plate (plan): a 10&quot; square plate with rounded corners and four slotted anchor bolt holes near the corners. Dimensions: 10&quot; sq., 9 1/2&quot; bolt circle and 8 1/2&quot; bolt circle (the slots span between these two circles), each slot 1 1/4&quot; X 1 3/4&quot; for anchor bolt, with 1 3/4&quot; and 1 1/4&quot; slot dimensions shown. The central circle is the pole.</p>
<p>Elevation A: a partial cutaway of the pole welded to a 3/4&quot; base plate. Pole wall thickness T = 0.1196&quot; (shown twice). Weld callouts: a 1/4&quot; fillet at the top of the joint and a 1/8&quot; fillet at the inside bottom. A circle marks the weld area, labeled See Detail A.</p>
<p>Detail A (enlarged weld section): pole wall (hatched) meets the base plate with a built-up weld. Dimensions: 1.83 T Min. (height up the wall), 1.57 T Min. (length along the plate), T (weld throat) and 30 degrees Max. contact angle.</p>
<p>Detail B (Pole Top Tenon): a short tube 4 1/2&quot; O.D., 4 NPS Std, 4.00&quot; tall, passing through a 1/4&quot; plate with 2&quot; central hole welded to the pole shaft with 3/16&quot; welds at both faces (3/16 callouts), 1/4&quot; Min. Typ. weld size noted at the left. Labeled Pole shaft below.</p>
<p>Pole elevations: TYPE 1 (tall left pole in the center group): overall 10&#x27;-6&quot; from the top of the base plate to the top, 4.06&quot; O.D. at top, 5.48&quot; O.D. at base. Holes: a 2 1/4&quot; diameter hole (drill 1 side only) and two 3/4&quot; diameter holes (drill through) located 4.5&quot; above and 4.5&quot; below the center of the large hole, with 2&#x27;-11.5&quot; from the top to the upper hole. A 3&quot; X 5&quot; hand hole reinforced with 1/4&quot; X 2&quot; ring welded to outside of pole and 1/8&quot; cover plate is at the base (see Elevation A). TYPE 1A: overall 14&#x27;-0&quot;, 3.57&quot; O.D. at top, 5.48&quot; O.D. at base, same hole pattern (2 1/4&quot; hole with two 3/4&quot; holes 4.5&quot; above and below) with 6&#x27;-5.5&quot; from the top to the upper hole. A 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding is welded to the interior of the pole shaft at 180 degrees from the hand hole rim. TYPE 8: 1&#x27;-8&quot; tall, 4.5&quot; O.D. at top, 4.73&quot; O.D. at base. TYPE 8A: 2&#x27;-4&quot; tall, 4.5&quot; O.D. at top, 4.83&quot; O.D. at base. TYPE 9: 7&#x27;-2&quot; tall, 4.29&quot; O.D. at top, 5.25&quot; O.D. at base. Each shaft has a Detail B tenon at the top.</p>
<p>Foundation section (Type 1): concrete foundation with anchor bolts, labeled Foundation see S-52.1, 24&quot; X 24&quot; plan (24&quot; diameter optional) and 3&#x27;-6&quot; deep, set beside a stepped curb and sidewalk. Elbowed conduit not dimensioned.</p>
<p>Title block: Bureau of Engineering, Gary Lee Moore, PE, ENV SP, City Engineer. Structural design checked by Vigen Gharibian, SE. Approved by Shailesh Patel, SE, Division Engineer, Structural Engineering Division, 8-9-16. Title: Standards Type 1, 1A, 8, 8A &amp; 9, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-51.1.3A. Drawn MT 8-3-16, TE JV 8-3-16, Sr TE MA 8-3-16, Pr TE VJ 8-3-16, approved 8-4-16 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Booklet page number 25.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. All standards shall be tapered steel posts with 0.1196&quot; wall thickness.</p>
<p>2. Max. taper of posts shall be 0.143&quot; per foot.</p>
<p>Callouts: Pole; 3/4&quot; base plate; T (Wall thickness) = 0.1196&quot;; See Detail A; Detail B; 4.5&quot; O.D. at top; 2 1/4&quot; diameter hole (Drill 1 side only); Two 3/4&quot; diameter holes (Drill through); 3&quot; X 5&quot; Hand hole reinforced with 1/4&quot; X 2&quot; ring welded to outside of pole and 1/8&quot; cover plate; 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding welded to interior of pole shaft at 180 degrees from hand hole rim; 1/4&quot; plate with 2&quot; central hole; Foundation see S-52.1.</p></details>

W16-7p downward diagonal arrow, 24x12, 24x12. Footnote marked with an asterisk below the table.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacon Sign Arm, sheet 2 of 4, drawing number S-52.1.7. Page number 36.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 3 (document page 86)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0086.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0086.webp" alt="S-52.1 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p86">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Top view: A rectangular 4 in. thick slab 24&quot; wide by 96&quot; long, with the long dimension divided 36&quot;, 24&quot;, 36&quot; along the curb face (shown as a double line along the left edge). A 4-bolt pattern lies at the center of the middle 24&quot; square on an 8 1/2&quot; bolt circle, with diagonals at 45 degrees to the centerline. A dashed conduit stub runs from the center to the right side of the slab. Callouts: 4&quot; thick slab (two places), 8 1/2&quot; bolt circle, Curb Face.</p>
<p>Elevation: A pole stands on a 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot; base plate with 1&quot; diameter anchor bolts, set on mortar over a concrete footing. The pole carries a 3&quot; x 5&quot; reinforced hand hole and cover, with the hand hole rim 1&quot; +/- 1/4&quot; above the base plate level and 8&quot; above finished grade. A 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding is welded inside the pole shaft at 180 degrees from the hand hole rim, and a #8 green bonding wire runs from it down to the anchor bolt. The mortar bed between base plate and footing is 2&quot; minimum (MIN.) to 3&quot; maximum (MAX.) thick and is finished with a slope from 45 degrees to 90 degrees including drain holes. A curb face is shown at the left. The footing is 24&quot; x 24&quot; (24&quot; diameter optional) and 42&quot; deep, with a 4&quot; minimum topping (minimum 14&quot; MIN. dimension from grade to the conduit elbow) and a 3&quot; MIN. dimension at the bottom of the footing. Anchor bolts are 1&quot; diameter x 3&#x27;-0&quot; long with the bottom 6&quot; threaded, and an anchor plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot; at the bottom, with 2 nuts and 2 washers each. A conduit rises in the center and bends to exit the side (See notes 1 and 2).</p>
<p>Title block: Foundation Type F-1 &amp; F-8, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-52.1. Drawn MT 9-30-15, T.E. JV 9-30-15, Sr. T.E. MA 10-1-15, Pr. T.E. VJ 10-2-15. Approved 11-20-15, Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Bureau of Engineering, Structural Engineering Division block: City Engineer Gary Lee Moore, PE, Env SP; structural design checked by Peter Chiu, SE; approved by Shailesh Patel, SE, Division Engineer, 1-26-16. Printed page number 79.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Type F-1: 2&quot; nominal size conduit, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Type F-8 and Type F-8A: 3&quot; nominal size conduit, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>3. Unless otherwise specified, in unpaved areas a 36&quot; x 24&quot; x 4&quot; slab shall be provided for each door opening in the case of a model 336 cabinet.</p>
<p>4. On Type F-8 and Type F-8A, ground rod shall be installed in controller pullbox.</p>
<p>Callouts: 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding welded to interior of pole shaft at 180 degrees from hand hole rim. #8 green bonding wire. Anchor bolt: thread top 8&quot; and galvanize 12&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt), total 4. Base plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot;. 2&quot; minimum to 3&quot; maximum mortar. Curb face. After plumbing standard, place mortar all around bolts. Finish with slope ranging from 45 degrees to 90 degrees including drain holes. 1&quot; dia. x 3&#x27;-0&quot; anchor bolts, thread bottom 6&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers, total 4 required per bolt). Anchor plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot;. Pole. 3&quot; x 5&quot; reinforced hand hole and cover. Finished grade. 4&quot; minimum topping. 42&quot;. 14&quot; minimum. 3&quot; minimum. 24&quot; x 24&quot;, 24&quot; dia. optional.</p></details>
