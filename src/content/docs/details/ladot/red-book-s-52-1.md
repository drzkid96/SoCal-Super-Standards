<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Type F-1: 2&quot; nominal size conduit, unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="2">Type F-8 and Type F-8A: 3&quot; nominal size conduit, unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="3">Unless otherwise specified, in unpaved areas a 36&quot; x 24&quot; x 4&quot; slab shall be provided for each door opening in the case of a model 336 cabinet.</li>
<li value="4">On Type F-8 and Type F-8A, ground rod shall be installed in controller pullbox.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding welded to interior of pole shaft at 180 degrees from hand hole rim. #8 green bonding wire. Anchor bolt: thread top 8&quot; and galvanize 12&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt), total 4. Base plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot;. 2&quot; minimum to 3&quot; maximum mortar. Curb face. After plumbing standard, place mortar all around bolts. Finish with slope ranging from 45 degrees to 90 degrees including drain holes. 1&quot; dia. x 3&#x27;-0&quot; anchor bolts, thread bottom 6&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers, total 4 required per bolt). Anchor plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot;. Pole. 3&quot; x 5&quot; reinforced hand hole and cover. Finished grade. 4&quot; minimum topping. 42&quot;. 14&quot; minimum. 3&quot; minimum. 24&quot; x 24&quot;, 24&quot; dia. optional.</div>
</details>
