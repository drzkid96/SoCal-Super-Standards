<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-52.1 showing the LADOT Type F-1 and F-8 signal and lighting pole foundation, a 24 in. x 24 in. (24 in. diameter optional) concrete footing 42 in. deep with four 1 in. anchor bolts on an 8 1/2 in. bolt circle, a 10 in. x 10 in. x 3/4 in. base plate and a mortar bed. A 36 in. x 24 in. x 36 in. long 4 in. slab layout is given in top view. F-1 uses 2 in. conduit and F-8 and F-8A use 3 in. conduit, and a #8 green bonding wire grounds the pole.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 86)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Top view: A rectangular 4 in. thick slab 24&quot; wide by 96&quot; long, with the long dimension divided 36&quot;, 24&quot;, 36&quot; along the curb face (shown as a double line along the left edge). A 4-bolt pattern lies at the center of the middle 24&quot; square on an 8 1/2&quot; bolt circle, with diagonals at 45 degrees to the centerline. A dashed conduit stub runs from the center to the right side of the slab. Callouts: 4&quot; thick slab (two places), 8 1/2&quot; bolt circle, Curb Face.</p>
<p>Elevation: A pole stands on a 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot; base plate with 1&quot; diameter anchor bolts, set on mortar over a concrete footing. The pole carries a 3&quot; x 5&quot; reinforced hand hole and cover, with the hand hole rim 1&quot; +/- 1/4&quot; above the base plate level and 8&quot; above finished grade. A 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding is welded inside the pole shaft at 180 degrees from the hand hole rim, and a #8 green bonding wire runs from it down to the anchor bolt. The mortar bed between base plate and footing is 2&quot; minimum (MIN.) to 3&quot; maximum (MAX.) thick and is finished with a slope from 45 degrees to 90 degrees including drain holes. A curb face is shown at the left. The footing is 24&quot; x 24&quot; (24&quot; diameter optional) and 42&quot; deep, with a 4&quot; minimum topping (minimum 14&quot; MIN. dimension from grade to the conduit elbow) and a 3&quot; MIN. dimension at the bottom of the footing. Anchor bolts are 1&quot; diameter x 3&#x27;-0&quot; long with the bottom 6&quot; threaded, and an anchor plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot; at the bottom, with 2 nuts and 2 washers each. A conduit rises in the center and bends to exit the side (See notes 1 and 2).</p>
<p>Title block: Foundation Type F-1 &amp; F-8, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-52.1. Drawn MT 9-30-15, T.E. JV 9-30-15, Sr. T.E. MA 10-1-15, Pr. T.E. VJ 10-2-15. Approved 11-20-15, Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Bureau of Engineering, Structural Engineering Division block: City Engineer Gary Lee Moore, PE, Env SP; structural design checked by Peter Chiu, SE; approved by Shailesh Patel, SE, Division Engineer, 1-26-16. Printed page number 79.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Type F-1: 2&quot; nominal size conduit, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Type F-8 and Type F-8A: 3&quot; nominal size conduit, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>3. Unless otherwise specified, in unpaved areas a 36&quot; x 24&quot; x 4&quot; slab shall be provided for each door opening in the case of a model 336 cabinet.</p>
<p>4. On Type F-8 and Type F-8A, ground rod shall be installed in controller pullbox.</p>
<p>Callouts: 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding welded to interior of pole shaft at 180 degrees from hand hole rim. #8 green bonding wire. Anchor bolt: thread top 8&quot; and galvanize 12&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt), total 4. Base plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot;. 2&quot; minimum to 3&quot; maximum mortar. Curb face. After plumbing standard, place mortar all around bolts. Finish with slope ranging from 45 degrees to 90 degrees including drain holes. 1&quot; dia. x 3&#x27;-0&quot; anchor bolts, thread bottom 6&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers, total 4 required per bolt). Anchor plate 10&quot; x 10&quot; x 3/4&quot;. Pole. 3&quot; x 5&quot; reinforced hand hole and cover. Finished grade. 4&quot; minimum topping. 42&quot;. 14&quot; minimum. 3&quot; minimum. 24&quot; x 24&quot;, 24&quot; dia. optional.</p></details>
