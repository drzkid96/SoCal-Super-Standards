<p class="dwg-sum">Plan layout of a mountable truck apron at a corner, between the street flowline and a mountable curb. The apron is 5 ft minimum and 15 ft maximum wide and is marked with 12" white lines spaced 12" to 36" on center and an 8" white edge lane line. It refers to Supplemental Street Design Guide Detail 300.</p>
