<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows the layout of the word HUMPS as a pavement marking on a grid, 8'-0" wide by 8'-0" tall overall. Grid cells are 4 inches wide by 8 inches tall. The painted area is 30 sq ft and the material is alkyd thermoplastic per current LADOT specifications.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Layout view: The word HUMPS in five block letters, H, U, M, P and S from left to right, drawn over a grid of narrow columns and rows. The overall width is dimensioned 8&#x27;-0&quot; below the grid and the overall height is dimensioned 8&#x27;-0&quot; at the left. The cell size is dimensioned at the lower left as 4&quot; wide (horizontal) and 8&quot; tall (vertical). The H has two vertical stems joined by a crossbar, the U has a rounded bottom, the M has two stems with tapering V-shaped inner cuts, the P has a stem and a curved bowl, and the S is a curved letter. Below the grid are the painted area and the material statement. The title block reads Approved July 13, 2004, John E. Fisher for Wayne K. Tanda, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Pavement Marking HUMPS, sheet 1 of 1, drawn by MT, Drawing No. S-509.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Painted area = 30 sq ft.</p>
<p>2. Material for pavement markings shall be alkyd thermoplastic in accordance with current LADOT specifications.</p>
<p>Dimension labels: overall 8&#x27;-0&quot; by 8&#x27;-0&quot;. Grid cell 4&quot; wide by 8&quot; tall.</p></details>
