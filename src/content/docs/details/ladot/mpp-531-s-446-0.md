<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows where to post <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w31ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w31ca.webp">W31(CA)</a> END signs (30 by 30 inches) with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a> 18 by 18 inch red object markers, and the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w14-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W14-1.svg">W14-1</a> Dead End sign, at street termini. The signs go at streets without cul-de-sacs and at cul-de-sacs not fully developed. One posting is used for streets 44 ft wide or less, two for 45 to 59 ft, and three for 60 ft or greater, and posts may shift up to 10 ft.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w31ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w31ca.webp">W31(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a> signs shall be posted at street termini as in a. and b. below:</p>
<p>a. Without cul-de-sac designs.</p>
<p>b. With cul-de-sac designs where the frontage of the cul-de-sac is not fully developed.</p>
<p>2. Where installed, the number of signs should be posted as shown below. However, they may deviate as much as 10&#x27; from the standard location in order to utilize existing street light standards or existing sign posts or to avoid driveways or utility installations.</p>
<p>Number of postings: street width. 1: 44&#x27; or less. 2: 45&#x27;~59&#x27;. 3: 60&#x27; or greater.</p></details>
