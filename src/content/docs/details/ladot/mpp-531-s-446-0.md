<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w31ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w31ca.webp">W31(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a> signs shall be posted at street termini as in a. and b. below:</li>
</ol>
<p>a. Without cul-de-sac designs.</p>
<p>b. With cul-de-sac designs where the frontage of the cul-de-sac is not fully developed.</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="2">Where installed, the number of signs should be posted as shown below. However, they may deviate as much as 10&#x27; from the standard location in order to utilize existing street light standards or existing sign posts or to avoid driveways or utility installations.</li>
</ol>
<p>Number of postings: street width. 1: 44&#x27; or less. 2: 45&#x27;~59&#x27;. 3: 60&#x27; or greater.</p>
