<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Guide signs should be considered for installation as per Section 336 of the Department of Transportation&#x27;s Manual of Policy and Procedures.</li>
<li value="2">The minimum lettering size is 8&quot; upper case and 6&quot; lower case for overhead signs and 6&quot; upper case and 4 1/2&quot; lower case for roadside signs. Series E reflective white engineering grade sheeting should be used for the lettering. Total area of sign shall not exceed 18 square feet for roadside installations per Standard Plan S-476.0.</li>
<li value="3">Signs may be fabricated by qualified private parties, with installation by LADOT field forces. Contact LADOT Coordination Section, for a list of approved sign manufacturers. However, after initial installation LADOT is responsible for maintenance and replacement of sign. If the private entity does not furnish a suitable replacement logo to LADOT when the sign need to be replaced, LADOT may elect to install a guide sign without a logo.</li>
<li value="4">Standard Highway Series lettering and arrow style shall be used.</li>
<li value="5">The logo size shall not exceed 25% of the sign area and shall be located in the upper left or right corner for roadside signs.</li>
<li value="6"><strong>Sign colors should be reflectorized.</strong> Background color of sign, except for logo shall be Type 2 (Super-engineering grade) green. Legend except for logo shall be Type 3 (High Intensity) white. Logo colors shall be Type 2.</li>
<li value="7">All lines of legend are to be centered.</li>
<li value="8">The outside edge of the sign shall have a 3/4&quot; reflective white border for roadside signs. There shall be a 1/2&quot; margin between the sign edge and the border.</li>
<li value="9">The entire sign shall be produced on 0.080 inch aluminum sheeting.</li>
<li value="10">Sign stiffener system and hardware shall be secured to the back of the sign as required, per Standard Plan S-476.0.</li>
<li value="11">Use one-line directional arrows as shown in S-418.5.</li>
</ol>
<p>Sign sample labels: D&#x27; (width), D (height), logo &lt;= 1/2 D&#x27; by &lt;= 1/2 D, Name of Facility.</p>
