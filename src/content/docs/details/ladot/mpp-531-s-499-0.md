<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows sample guide signs that carry a facility name and a logo, with design notes on lettering size, colors, sheeting, logo size and placement. The minimum lettering is 8 inch upper case and 6 inch lower case for overhead signs and 6 inch upper case and 4 1/2 inch lower case for roadside signs. Roadside signs may not exceed 16 square feet, and the logo may not exceed 25% of the sign area.</p>

<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Guide signs should be considered for installation as per Section 336 of the Department of Transportation&#x27;s Manual of Policy and Procedures.</p>
<p>2. The minimum lettering size is 8 inch upper case and 6 inch lower case for overhead signs and 6 inch upper case and 4 1/2 inch lower case for roadside signs. Series E reflective white engineering grade sheeting should be used for the lettering. Total area of sign shall not exceed 16 square feet for roadside installations per Standard Plan S-476.0.</p>
<p>3. Signs may be fabricated by qualified private parties, with installation by LADOT field forces. Contact LADOT Coordination Section for a list of approved sign manufacturers. However, after initial installation LADOT is responsible for maintenance and replacement of the sign. If the private entity does not furnish a suitable replacement logo when the sign needs to be replaced, LADOT may elect to install a guide sign without a logo.</p>
<p>4. Standard Highway Series lettering and arrow style shall be used.</p>
<p>5. The logo size shall not exceed 25% of the sign area and shall be located in the upper left or right corner for roadside signs.</p>
<p>6. Sign colors should be reflectorized. Background color and, except for the logo, legend shall be green and white. Sign background shall be Type I (engineering grade) green, and white lettering shall be Type III (high intensity) white. Logo sheeting shall be Type II (Super engineering grade) material.</p>
<p>7. All lines of legend are to be centered.</p>
<p>8. The outside edge of the sign shall have a 3/4 inch reflective white border for roadside signs. There shall be a 1/2 inch margin between the sign and the border.</p>
<p>9. The entire sign shall be produced on 0.080 inch aluminum sheeting.</p>
<p>10. Sign stiffener system and hardware shall be secured to the back of the sign as required, per Standard Plan S-476.0.</p>
<p>11. Use one-line directional arrows as shown in S-418.5.</p></details>
