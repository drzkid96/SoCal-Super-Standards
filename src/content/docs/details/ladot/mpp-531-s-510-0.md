<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</li>
<li value="2">Sign size is 12&quot;x15&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1 1/2&quot; in height and can be Double, Left or Right. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</li>
<li value="3">All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09/05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number 2530.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>The background color is white.</strong> The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, arrow and border are red.</li>
<li value="5">Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</li>
</ol>
<p>Sign labels: 12&quot; wide, 15&quot; tall, 9&quot; outside diameter circle, 5 1/2&quot; tall Series E P, arrow 1 1/2&quot; tall by 8&quot; long, 3/4&quot; dimension at the bottom.</p>
</details>
