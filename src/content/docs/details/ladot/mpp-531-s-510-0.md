<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Details the 12 inch by 15 inch No Parking Any Time sign (LADOT Stock Number 2530) with a 9 inch outside diameter circle and slash, a 5 1/2 inch tall Series E P, and a double, left or right arrow 1 1/2 inch tall. The background is white with a black P and red circle, slash, arrow and border. Signs are .080 aluminum with reflective SEG Type II material and graffiti film.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Sign dimensions: 12 inch wide by 15 inch tall. 9 inch outside diameter circle and slash, 1 inch slash. 5 1/2 inch tall Series E P. Arrow 1 1/2 inch tall, 8 inch long, 3/4 inch from the bottom border.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Signs shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 12 inch x 15 inch, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1 1/2 inch in height and can be Double, Left or Right. The border shall be 1/4 inch wide and spaced 1/4 inch from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09/05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number 2530.</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, arrow and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
