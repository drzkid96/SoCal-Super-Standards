<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT drawing S-51.7 for the Type 7 pushbutton standard, a 4 ft 3 3/4 in shaft of 2 1/2 inch Schedule 40 pipe, hot dip galvanized. It gives the hole pattern for the push button, a 5 inch square base plate with four anchor bolts and a Type F-7 foundation 36 inches from the curb.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 33)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0033.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0033.webp" alt="S-51.7 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-51.7, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p33">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Standard, Type 7 (pushbutton post), drawing S-51.7. The sheet has an elevation, a hole detail, a base detail, a base plan section and a foundation section.</p>
<p>Elevation: a slim vertical shaft with a push button shown in dashed lines at the top of the shaft. The overall height is 4&#x27;-3 3/4&quot; from top of shaft down to the pavement or sidewalk surface at the foundation.</p>
<p>Hole Detail: a side view of the top of the shaft showing three holes on one centerline. Dimensions: 9 3/4&quot; overall from the shaft top to the lowest hole, 1/2&quot; from the top to the first hole, and 6&quot; between the upper holes. The two upper holes are No. 7 Drill, 1/4-20 Tap, 2 Holes. The large lowest hole is a 1 1/8&quot; Drill. The push button is shown on the shaft at that height.</p>
<p>Base Detail: a cutaway elevation of the shaft welded to a base plate, with Section A-A cut marks above. Weld callout 3/16&quot; over 1/8&quot; fillet. Plate thickness 3/8&quot; and the inner shell weld thickness 1/16&quot; are dimensioned.</p>
<p>Section A-A (plan of base plate): a 5&quot; sq. plate with 1/2&quot; R corners, 4 holes of 3/4&quot; Drill on a 4 1/2&quot; Bolt Circle at 45 degree positions, and a center opening Cut hole to fit post.</p>
<p>Foundation section: a concrete foundation, Foundation, Type F-7, set beside the stepped curb and sidewalk, with Anchor Bolts 1/2&quot; X 10 1/2&quot; X 1 1/2&quot; (4 required) and an elbowed conduit leaving the side. A 36&quot; dimension runs from the shaft to the curb edge, and 14&quot; Min. is dimensioned from the top surface to the conduit.</p>
<p>Title block: Standard, Type 7, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-51.7, approved June 26, 2008 by Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Drawn MT 7-09-07, checked RAR 2-05-08, T.E. JV 2-11-08, Sr. T.E. JW 2-13-08, Pr. T.E. SFS 2-13-08. Booklet page number 26.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Shaft material: 2 1/2&quot; Std. Schedule 40 pipe.</p>
<p>2. Finish: Hot dip galvanize after fabrication per ASTM spec. A-120.</p>
<p>Callouts: Push Button; No. 7 Drill, 1/4-20 Tap, 2 Holes; 1 1/8&quot; Drill; Base Detail; Hole Detail; Section A-A; Cut hole to fit post; 3/4&quot; Drill, 4 Holes; 4 1/2&quot; Bolt Circle; 1/2&quot; R; Anchor Bolts 1/2&quot; X 10 1/2&quot; X 1 1/2&quot; (4 required); Foundation, Type F-7.</p></details>
