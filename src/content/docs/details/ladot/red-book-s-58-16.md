<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of standard drawing S-58.16 for the freeway trailblazer sign. A 5' x 5' LED sign with FWY ALT and WEST or EAST legend, optionally paired with a 6' x 5' static Interstate 10 guide sign, is mounted on a cantilever pole with 12' arm per S-98.0. Layout dimensions of the LED legend are given, and the width can be increased by 12 inches for North or South.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Front view of LED legend faces (top of sheet): Two LED trailblazer sign faces are drawn, each a square outline 60&quot; wide by 60&quot; high (the left one is marked *60&quot;, the asterisk referring to the width footnote). The left face shows the legend FWY ALT on the top line and an arrow pointing left, an arrow pointing up, and WEST on the bottom line, made of dot (LED) characters. On each face the vertical layout is 13&quot; top margin, 10&quot; high top line of text, 14&quot; gap, 10&quot; high bottom line of text, 13&quot; bottom margin (total 60&quot;).</p>

<p>Front view (not to scale): Two signs side by side on the arm. The right sign is a Static Guide Sign (optional), 6&#x27; wide by 5&#x27; high, with an Interstate 10 shield at the top, a large left arrow and FWY. The pole is at the right of the front view with a short stub arm. A 1&#x27; Max. dimension is shown at the left side of the front view.</p>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 106)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Detail &quot;A&quot; (not to scale): An isometric view of the Unistrut mounting of the LED trailblazer sign. A horizontal pipe (the cantilever arm) passes through the web area of a vertical aluminum channel and closure strip, held by a pipe clamp with a slotted hex head machine screw and square nut. The channel is shown with a 1-5/8&quot; and 1-5/8&quot; cross section dimension (3-1/4&quot; total width, shown as two 1-5/8&quot; dimensions either side of the centerline). Callouts: Aluminum Channel and Closure Strip, weight 0.66 lbs/ft (0.98 kg/m), thickness 0.105&quot; (2.6 mm). Pipe Clamps, O.D. 6-5/8&quot;, thickness 11 Ga, design load 100 lbs. Slotted hex head machine screw and square nut.</p>
<p>The rest of the sheet is a numbered specification (19 notes) for the LED sign, transcribed below.</p>
<p>Title block: Trailblazer Sign, sheet 2 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Robert R. Yates, General Manager, 8-19-96. Drawing No. S-58.16. Drawn by AH/HM 6-20-96, checked by ID 6-20-96, supervised by VJ 6-20-96. Page number 99.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Trailblazer signs to be used in conjunction with static freeway overhead guide signs.</p>
<p>2. Use one or two arrows on the trailblazer sign as appropriate.</p>
<p>3. Sign shall utilize Light Emitting Diodes (LED) for message illumination. The individual LEDs shall equal or exceed in quality as those manufactured by Hewlett-Packard, high power ALInGAP amber type, catalog #HLMA-DL00.</p>
<p>4. Sign text shall be 10&quot; U.C. (upper case) with 1&quot; spacing between each letter and a minimum of one letter width spacing between words.</p>
<p>5. The overall sign size shall be 60&quot; in height x 60&quot; in width and 8&quot; in depth.</p>
<p>6. The weight of the sign (enclosure + all components) shall not exceed 115 lbs.</p>
<p>7. The sign shall be constructed so that the front face panel shall be contained within an extruded 0.90 aluminum frame having full welded seams and powder coat painted with high gloss black finish meeting the color standards of Fed. Spec. 595b, 17038.</p>
<p>8. The transparent Lexan front face panel shall be hinged at the top allowing front access to the interior of the sign. Support shall be provided to hold the Lexan panel open during maintenance.</p>
<p>9. A honeycomb formed black anodized screen shall be provided between the LED&#x27;s lamp clusters and the front face panel to enhance resistance to &quot;sun phantom&quot; effect.</p>
<p>10. The sign shall use a closed cell neoprene gasket making the sign watertight.</p>
<p>11. Sign enclosure hardware materials shall be stainless steel AISI303, for corrosion resistance.</p>
<p>12. An opening shall be made on the bottom side of the sign so that all conductors, enclosed in flexible conduit, may exit from the sign into the cantilever arm.</p>
<p>13. The sign shall be vented in the bottom portion on the opposite end from the conductor opening.</p>
<p>14. The LED elements shall be comprised of individual removable modules. Each LED module shall be composed of 1&quot; pixels (clusters) having 8 each LED&#x27;s per pixel. One module shall be used for each letter of the message.</p>
<p>15. Each pixel shall be removable or replaceable on the module without the use of any tools other than a screwdriver.</p>
<p>16. The sign shall be equipped with a photoelectric cell mounted on the enclosure to measure ambient light intensity and automatically compensate for ambient light conditions.</p>
<p>17. The LED sign shall be mountable using unistrut mounting system.</p>
<p>18. Sign shall be equipped with a terminal block to provide separate power inputs to each of the text and arrow modules. The sign shall be powered with 120V AC.</p>
<p>19. A full set of proposed sign specifications and drawings shall be supplied to the City prior to manufacturing.</p></details>
