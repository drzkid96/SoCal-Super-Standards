<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-57.2D shows a temporary signal on a Type CD954 standard with a 4 ft luminaire arm and a mast arm up to 15 ft long. The base is a 60 in. x 60 in. x 3/4 in. steel plate with eight precast concrete blocks, cable loops and a coil chain. Plan, elevation, Section A-A and three notes are given, with 17 ft 6 in. min. head clearance.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 48)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0048.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0048.webp" alt="S-57.2D sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-57.2D, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p48">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: a 60 in. x 60 in. square footing assembly seen from above, made of precast concrete blocks laid in a 3 x 3 grid around the pole. Four 23 in. x 23 in. x 30 in. precast concrete blocks (4 req.) are at the corners and four 23 in. x 13 in. x 30 in. precast concrete blocks (4 req.) are at the middle of each side. 5 in. x 3 in. x 1/2 in. steel angles are welded to the plate at the corners and side midpoints (typical). A dashed square marks the 3/8 in. coil chain tie. Called out at the pole center: a 2 in. hole, and 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/2 in. plate washers (4 req.). Four 1-1/2 in. diameter holes are drilled for the 1-1/8 in. bolt size on an 11-1/2 in. bolt circle. Dimensions of 6 in. are shown at the center block and side. Section marks A and A are on both sides.</p>
<p>Elevation: a Type CD954 standard with a 4&#x27;-0&quot; luminaire arm at the top and a mast arm 15&#x27;-0&quot; max. long carrying one all-12 in. section signal head. Temporary overhead (O.H.) signal cable (maximum span 100 feet) is looped at the pole top. A vertical dimension of 17&#x27;-6&quot; min. runs from the roadway to the mast arm head. Signal heads (see Note 3), 12 in. sections, are on the shaft, with a street name sign and 2 pedestrian heads below. A callout reads See Plan and Section A-A. A hatched roadway line is shown at grade.</p>
<p>Section A-A: a vertical cut through the base. A 60 in. x 60 in. x 3/4 in. steel plate sits on A.C. or P.C.C. surface on a mortar leveling bed, with a 1/4 in. weld typical. Blocks stand 30 in. high with 3/8 in. cable embedded 12 in. deep, forming loops 4 in. high (8 req.). The pole is bolted with 1-1/8 in. x 4 in. hex. hd. bolt, hex. nut, flat washer (4 req.) and 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/2 in. plate washers (4 req.). A level indicator is shown on the plate.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Signal, Temporary for Type CD954 Std., drawing number S-57.2D, approved 2-18-97 by Robert R. Yates, General Manager. Structural design checked 8-29-96 by C. W. Robins, Div. Engr. Page number 41.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. The CD954 standard shall conform to Dept. of Transportation, City of Los Angeles, standard drawing # S-51.9.6 (foundation excluded).</p>
<p>2. The mast arm length shall not exceed 15&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>3. There shall be a maximum of 2 - 5 section signal heads and 2 - ped. heads mounted to the shaft and one 3-section signal head mounted at the end of mast arm. All signal heads to be polycarbonate type.</p>
<p>Callouts: Tie with 3/8 in. coil chain. Weld angles to plate 5 in. x 3 in. x 1/2 in. typical. 23 in. x 23 in. x 30 in. precast concrete block (4 req.). 23 in. x 13 in. x 30 in. precast concrete block (4 req.). 2 in. hole. 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/2 in. plate washer (4 req.). Drill 1-1/2 in. diameter holes (4 req.) for 1-1/8 in. bolt size and 11-1/2 in. bolt circle. 3/8 in. cable, embed 12 in. deep (8 req.). Type CD954 std. 1-1/8 in. x 4 in. hex. hd. bolt, hex. nut, flat washer (4 req.). Weld bolts and 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/2 in. plate washers to 3/4 in. plate. Mortar leveling bed, wedge edges until mortar sets. (Mortar is not required on C.A.B., dirt or when slope is less than 1/4 in. per foot.) 60 in. x 60 in. x 3/4 in. steel plate. Coat plate assembly with primer and traffic signal yellow paint. Temporary O.H. signal cable (maximum span 100 feet). 4&#x27;-0&quot; luminaire arm. 15&#x27;-0&quot; max. All 12 in. sections. 17&#x27;-6&quot; min. Signal heads see note 3. Street name sign. 2 pedestrian heads. See plan and Section A-A. Roadway. Type CD 954 Std.</p></details>
