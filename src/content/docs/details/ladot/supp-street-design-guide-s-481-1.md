<p class="dwg-sum">Sheets showing a raised crosswalk at a pedestrian island on a channelized right turn, with and without a bike lane. Approach and departure ramps are at least 6 ft long, or 9 ft minimum where WB-50 trucks or buses are expected. The bike lane version uses a 2 ft green colored bicycle crossing and R1-5L MOD and R1-5R MOD yield signs with W17-1bP, and green pavement is optional and recommended. The raised crosswalk is at least 10 ft wide per S-483.1.</p>

<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Bike lane symbols are recommended to clarify the intended operation of the bike lane space.</p>
<p>2. Optional use of green colored pavement to enhance visibility and conspicuity of bicycle lane. Green colored pavement may be installed for the entire length of the bicycle lane or for only a portion (or portions) of the bicycle lane. All green colored pavement should comply with FHWA Interim Approval for Optional Use of Green Colored Pavement for Bike Lanes (IA-14) and approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>3. See City of Los Angeles Supplemental Street Design Guide, Detail 100 and Detail 600 for pedestrian/bicyclist island and raised crosswalk details.</p>
<p>4. Approach and departure ramps should be a minimum of 6&#x27; in length. Where WB-50&#x27;s or buses are expected the ramp length should be 9&#x27; minimum.</p></details>
