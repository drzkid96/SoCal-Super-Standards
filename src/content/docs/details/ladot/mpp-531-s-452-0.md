<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows installation of a perforated square-tube sign post set in an anchor sleeve in the sidewalk, for signs up to 10 sq ft. The 2" x 2" post is inserted 6" to 8" into the anchor assembly and secured with two blind rivets, with a 2 1/2" x 2 1/2" x 18" sleeve over a 2 1/4" x 2 1/4" x 30" anchor. Mounting height is 7' minimum over pedestrian areas, with a 24" minimum offset noted, and parking signs are set at 45 degrees to the curb while warning and regulatory signs are set at 90 degrees. Larger signs use S-476.1.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Sign post installation</p>
<p>Maximum 10 sq ft sign area. For larger signs see Standard Plan S-476.1.</p>
<p>Blind rivets at the sign corners, with a center blind rivet used on 36&quot; or larger signs.</p>
<p>Sign set 18&quot; from the curb face. 7&#x27; minimum over pedestrian area (see Note 3). 24&quot; minimum (see Note 1) of post above the anchor. Post inserted 6&quot; to 8&quot; into the anchor assembly.</p>
<p>Detail: insert 2&quot; x 2&quot; post 6&quot; to 8&quot; deep into the anchor assembly, 2 blind rivets, 2 holes above surface (see Note 2). Sleeve 2 1/2&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; x 18&quot; over anchor 2 1/4&quot; x 2 1/4&quot; x 30&quot;.</p>
<p>Note</p>
<p>1. 30&quot; typical for parking signs. 36&quot; typical for warning and regulatory signs.</p>
<p>2. Posts for parking signs to be installed at 45 degree angle to curb. Posts for warning and regulatory signs to be installed at 90 degree angle to curb.</p>
<p>3. 9&#x27;6&quot; minimum for street name signs, 7&#x27;6&quot; typical for parking signs, 4&#x27; typical for <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w31ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w31ca.webp">W31(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-6.svg">W1-6</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-7.svg">W1-7</a>, 4&#x27; typical to top of sign for <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-8.svg">W1-8</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a>.</p></details>
