<h5 class="dwg-sub">Note (general)</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">30&quot; typical for parking signs. 36&quot; typical for warning and regulatory signs. (This is the value of the 24&quot; minimum dimension marked &#x27;see Note 1&#x27; on the elevation, measured from the curb face reference line to the post.)</li>
<li value="2">Posts for parking signs to be installed at 45 degree angle to curb. Posts for warning and regulatory signs to be installed at 90 degree angle to curb.</li>
<li value="3">9&#x27;6&quot; minimum for street name signs, 7&#x27;6&quot; typical for parking signs, 4&#x27; typical for <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w31ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w31ca.webp">W31(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-6.svg">W1-6</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-7.svg">W1-7</a>, 4&#x27; typical to top of sign for <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-8.svg">W1-8</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a>. (These are mounting heights that apply in place of the 7&#x27; minimum over the pedestrian area, as referenced on the elevation.)</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Blind rivet (top and bottom). Center blind rivet used on 36&quot; or larger signs. Max. 10 sq ft sign area. For larger signs see Standard Plan <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/ladot/mpp-531-s-476-1/">S-476.1</a>. 18&quot; (sign to curb reference line). 7&#x27; min. over pedestrian area (see Note 3). 24&quot; min. (see Note 1). 6&quot; to 8&quot; insertion depth. See Detail. Insert 2&quot; x 2&quot; post 6&quot; to 8&quot; deep into the anchor assembly. 2 blind rivets. 2 holes above surface. See Note 2. Sleeve 2 1/2&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; x 18&quot;. Anchor 2 1/4&quot; x 2 1/4&quot; x 30&quot;.</div>
