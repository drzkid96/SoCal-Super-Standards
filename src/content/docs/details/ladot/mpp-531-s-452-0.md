<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows installation of a perforated square-tube sign post set in an anchor sleeve in the sidewalk, for signs up to 10 sq ft. The 2 in. x 2 in. post is inserted 6 in. to 8 in. into the anchor assembly and secured with two blind rivets, with a 2 1/2 in. x 2 1/2 in. x 18 in. sleeve over a 2 1/4 in. x 2 1/4 in. x 30 in. anchor. The sign is set 18 in. from the curb face with 7 ft minimum clearance over pedestrian areas, and parking signs are set at 45 degrees to the curb while warning and regulatory signs are set at 90 degrees. Larger signs use S-476.1.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation view (left): A square sign face with a dashed diamond outline inside it (representing a diamond sign shape on a square panel) is mounted on a perforated square-tube post. Blind rivets are shown at the top and bottom corners of the diamond and a center blind rivet at the middle. A callout reads &#x27;Center blind rivet used on 36&quot; or large signs&#x27;. A callout reads &#x27;Max. 10 sq ft sign area. For larger signs see Standard Plan S-476.1&#x27;. The sign&#x27;s left edge is dimensioned 18&quot; from a vertical reference line at the curb face. The bottom of the sign is dimensioned 7&#x27; minimum over the pedestrian area (see Note 3) down to the sidewalk surface. A second horizontal dimension from the curb-face reference line to the post reads 24&quot; min. (see Note 1). The post has a break line, continues down through the sidewalk, and a dimension of 6&quot; to 8&quot; marks the insertion depth of the post into the anchor assembly below the sidewalk surface. A bracket labeled &#x27;See Detail&#x27; covers the post and anchor below grade. The sidewalk and curb are drawn in section at the bottom with the curb on the left.</p>
<p>Detail (right, isometric): A perforated square-tube post 2&quot; x 2&quot; is inserted 6&quot; to 8&quot; deep into the anchor assembly (callout &#x27;Insert 2&quot; X 2&quot; post 6&quot; to 8&quot; deep into the anchor assembly&#x27;). A sleeve labeled 2 1/2&quot; X 2 1/2&quot; X 18&quot; sits at the sidewalk surface with 2 blind rivets through it (&#x27;2 Blind rivets&#x27;) and &#x27;2 holes above surface&#x27; labeled at the sleeve top, with a &#x27;See Note 2&#x27; reference at the sleeve. Below the sleeve the anchor, labeled 2 1/4&quot; X 2 1/4&quot; X 30&quot;, extends down through the concrete sidewalk. The sidewalk slab is shown as a stippled concrete section with the curb at left.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>Note (general):</h5>
<p>1. 30&quot; typical for parking signs. 36&quot; typical for warning and regulatory signs. (This is the value of the 24&quot; minimum dimension marked &#x27;see Note 1&#x27; on the elevation, measured from the curb face reference line to the post.)</p>
<p>2. Posts for parking signs to be installed at 45 degree angle to curb. Posts for warning and regulatory signs to be installed at 90 degree angle to curb.</p>
<p>3. 9&#x27;6&quot; minimum for street name signs, 7&#x27;6&quot; typical for parking signs, 4&#x27; typical for <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w31ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w31ca.webp">W31(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-6.svg">W1-6</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-7.svg">W1-7</a>, 4&#x27; typical to top of sign for <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-8.svg">W1-8</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a>. (These are mounting heights that apply in place of the 7&#x27; minimum over the pedestrian area, as referenced on the elevation.)</p>
<p>Callouts: Blind rivet (top and bottom). Center blind rivet used on 36&quot; or larger signs. Max. 10 sq ft sign area. For larger signs see Standard Plan S-476.1. 18&quot; (sign to curb reference line). 7&#x27; min. over pedestrian area (see Note 3). 24&quot; min. (see Note 1). 6&quot; to 8&quot; insertion depth. See Detail. Insert 2&quot; x 2&quot; post 6&quot; to 8&quot; deep into the anchor assembly. 2 blind rivets. 2 holes above surface. See Note 2. Sleeve 2 1/2&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; x 18&quot;. Anchor 2 1/4&quot; x 2 1/4&quot; x 30&quot;.</p></details>
