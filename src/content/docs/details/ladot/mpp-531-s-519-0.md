<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</li>
<li value="2">Type II barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</li>
<li value="3">Permittee shall obtain <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs and barricades from the Bureau of Street Services and install and maintain barricades as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs and barricades.</li>
<li value="4">The installation of Temporary No Parking signs are optional. If requested by permittee, LADOT shall be reimbursed for the installation cost.</li>
<li value="5">Additional traffic controls may be required as determind by LADOT. (The word is printed &#x27;determind&#x27; on the sheet.)</li>
</ol>
