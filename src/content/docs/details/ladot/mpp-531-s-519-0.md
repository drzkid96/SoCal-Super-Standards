<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical plan for closing one residential block between two cross streets for a neighborhood block party. Type II barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> Road Closed signs close both ends of the block, and optional Temporary No Parking signs (8 AM to 5 PM Sunday) are posted on posts at the four corners. Five notes cover the fire lane, barricade gaps, who supplies signs and barricades, optional sign cost reimbursement and additional controls.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (not to scale): A north-south residential block is closed for the party. The closed block is a hatched area labeled Block Party between a cross street at the top and a cross street at the bottom. Each cross street is drawn with curb returns at both corners of the block and a double solid centerline, and the closed street continues beyond the cross streets (curb lines and an adjoining curb return are drawn above the top cross street and below the bottom cross street).</p>
<p>Barricades: at the top end of the block three Type II barricades span the street side by side, with two <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> callouts (leaders to the first and second barricade from the left). The same arrangement of three Type II barricades with two <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> callouts is drawn at the bottom end of the block.</p>
<p>Parking signs: a sign on a post is shown at each of the four block corners (curb returns at the edge of the block). On each side of the block a pair of leader lines runs from the two corner posts to one enlarged red and white sign, so two enlarged signs are drawn, one left of the block and one right of it. Each enlarged sign reads TEMPORARY, NO PARKING, 8 AM to 5 PM, SUNDAY. A thin red band above the word TEMPORARY has text that is [illegible].</p>
<p>Legend (right): Type II barricade symbol, and the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> ROAD CLOSED sign (white with a black border and black legend).</p>
<p>Title block: Approved December 10, 2009, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Typical Street Closure for Neighborhood Block Party. Sheet 1 of 1. T.E. initials TC, Sr. T.E. initials JW (handwritten), drawn MT 12-10-09. Drawing No. S-519.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</p>
<p>2. Type II barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</p>
<p>3. Permittee shall obtain <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs and barricades from the Bureau of Street Services and install and maintain barricades as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs and barricades.</p>
<p>4. The installation of Temporary No Parking signs are optional. If requested by permittee, LADOT shall be reimbursed for the installation cost.</p>
<p>5. Additional traffic controls may be required as determind by LADOT. (The word is printed &#x27;determind&#x27; on the sheet.)</p></details>
