<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical plan for closing one residential block between two cross streets for a neighborhood block party. Type II barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> Road Closed signs close both ends of the block, and optional Temporary No Parking signs (8 AM to 5 PM Sunday) are posted on posts at the four corners. Five notes cover the fire lane, barricade gaps, who supplies signs and barricades, optional sign cost reimbursement and additional controls.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (not to scale): a vertical block of street shown with a hatched area labeled Block Party between a cross street at the top and one at the bottom, each with curb returns and a double centerline. Across the street at the top end of the block three Type II barricades are drawn with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> callouts, and the same three barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> callouts are drawn at the bottom end.</p>
<p>At each of the four block corners a sign on a post is called out with a leader line to an enlarged red and white Temporary No Parking sign reading TEMPORARY, NO PARKING, 8 AM to 5 PM, SUNDAY. Two enlarged signs are placed to the left of the block and two to the right (the lower right and upper left leaders point to the corner posts).</p>
<p>Legend (right): Type II barricade symbol, and the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> ROAD CLOSED sign (white with black border and legend).</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Typical Street Closure for Neighborhood Block Party, sheet 1 of 1, drawing S-519.0, approved December 10, 2009 for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Drawn MT 12-10-09.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</p>
<p>2. Type II barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</p>
<p>3. Permittee shall obtain <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs and barricades from the Bureau of Street Services and install and maintain barricades as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs and barricades.</p>
<p>4. The installation of Temporary No Parking signs are optional. If requested by permittee, LADOT shall be reimbursed for the installation cost.</p>
<p>5. Additional traffic controls may be required as determined by LADOT.</p></details>
