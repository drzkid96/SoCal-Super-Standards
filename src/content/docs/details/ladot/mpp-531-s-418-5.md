<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0064.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0064.webp" alt="S-418.5 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-418.5, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/05-drawings-s-418-5-to-s-418-6/#p64">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Notes and text on this sheet (OCR)</summary><p>General Notes 1. 10. See S—418.6 regarding the use of overhead versus roadside freeway guide signs. Use roadside freeway guide signing where access to the freeway is provided only by the right lane, where overhead signing may not be feasible or as interim signing until overhead signing can be installed. See S—476.1 regarding mounting of roadside freeway guide signing. 4’X8’, 5’X6’, 4’x6’ and 3’x8’ signs require dual posts, while 3’x6’ signs may be installed on an electrolier or single post. Signs up to 10 sq—ft may be installed on steel electroliers within concrete sidewalks. Lane Assignment freeway guide signs on multilane cross streets that have access to freeways are required per Section 2D.45 of the California MUTCD. Where roadside signs are to be used apply the appropriate formats as shown in signs B, C, F, G, I, L, R, S, W and X. Action freeway guide signs are an essential follow-up to Lane Assignment freeway guide signs for assisting motorists in accessing the desired directions to freeways. Where roadside signs are to be used apply the appropriate formats as shown in Signs D, H, J, K, M, N, O, P, T, U, Y and Z. They should be placed 100’ to 200’ in advance of the turn. See Note 9. . Advance freeway guide signs are necessary in advance of a pair of roadside Lane Assignment freeway guide signs in order to advise motorists early—on that both directions of the freeway are accessible. See the appropriate formats in Signs A, E, Q and V. Freeway names are to be used only on the Advance sign or Lane Assignment sign (if an Advance sign is not appropriate) only where the on—ramps are within one mile of a freeway—to—freeway interchange, as shown in Signs A and E. Where a long freeway name does not fit within the 8’ wide format, as shown in Sign A, then Series C upper and lower case letters may be used. Where the freeway name and the word “Fwy” can fit on the first line then the freeway shield shall be shown alone on the second line. . The first capital letter of the cardinal direction is larger so as to provide improved recognition. . The dual direction Action freeway guide sign format as shown in Signs D and H versus the split directional formats as shown in Signs J, K, M, O, T, U, Y and Z is to be used only when physical contraints preclude effective placement of split signs. The text on each line is to be centered. CITY OF LOS ANGELES DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION Guide Signing S—418.5 Drawing No. Roadside_Freeway </p></details>
