<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT drawing S-51.8 for a Type 337 post-top mounted cabinet on a Type 8 shaft and Type F-8 foundation. The cabinet mounts with doors perpendicular to the curb and swinging toward it, with a slip fitter at the shaft top, a 36 inch offset from the sidewalk edge and a 3 inch conduit at least 14 inches deep.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 34)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0034.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0034.webp" alt="S-51.8 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-51.8, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p34">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Type 337 post-top mounted cabinet, drawing S-51.8 (sheet 1 of 1).</p>
<p>Detail (upper left, plan): a vertical double line labeled Curb Face. Next to it a small square representing the cabinet footprint has two door panels drawn as angled leaves at top and bottom, opening toward the curb, with arrows labeled Door openings on Type 337 PTM cabinet.</p>
<p>Elevation: a rectangular cabinet with a CITY OF LOS ANGELES nameplate near the top, a small rectangular panel with a round lock near the top left, a vertical door handle on the right side, and three columns of louver slots near the bottom. The cabinet is labeled Typical cabinet installation (Type 337 post-top mounted cabinet). Below the cabinet a corrugated Slip Fitter joins the cabinet to the shaft, labeled Shaft-Type 8.</p>
<p>Foundation section: the Type 8 shaft sits on a base plate with anchor bolts and a stepped concrete sidewalk and curb to the left. The foundation is labeled Foundation, Type F-8. A 3&quot; Conduit elbows out of the foundation and exits to the right. A 36&quot; dimension runs from the shaft centerline to the sidewalk edge and a 14&quot; Min. dimension runs vertically from the top surface to the conduit.</p>
<p>Title block: Type 337 Post-Top Mounted Cabinet, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-51.8. Drawn MT 3-1-16, T.E. JV 3-3-16, Sr. T.E. MA 3-8-16, Pr. T.E. VJ 4-11-16, approved 4-11-16 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Booklet page number 27.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Note: Type 337 PTM cabinet to be mounted with doors perpendicular to curb and doors swing towards curb. See detail upper left.</p>
<p>Callouts: Curb Face; Door openings on Type 337 PTM cabinet; Typical cabinet installation (Type 337 post-top mounted cabinet); Slip Fitter; Shaft-Type 8; Foundation, Type F-8; 3&quot; Conduit. Dimensions: 36&quot;, 14&quot; Min.</p></details>
