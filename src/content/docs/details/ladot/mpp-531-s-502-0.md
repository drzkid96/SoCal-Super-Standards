<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2. Shows three LADOT sign types with dimensions: the 66 inch by 24 inch City Limit sign, the Community/District Name sign (up to 96 inches long, 18 inches tall for one line or 24 inches for two lines), and a custom Neighborhood sign limited to 36 inches by 36 inches with clipped corners. The two blue signs carry a 12 inch diameter City seal at top.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>City Limit Sign: A blue rectangular sign with white text and rounded corners, 66&quot; wide by 24&quot; tall. A 12&quot; Dia. City of Los Angeles seal (Founded 1781) sits on the top edge near the center, partly overlapping the top of the sign. The legend is two lines, &quot;City of&quot; and &quot;Los Angeles&quot;. Vertical dimensions on the left, from the top edge down: 6&quot;, 6&quot;, 2&quot;, 6&quot;, 4&quot;, totaling 24&quot;. Width 66&quot; is dimensioned below the sign. Caption: &quot;City Limit Sign&quot;.</p>
<p>Community/District Name Sign: A blue sign with white text, same style, with a 12&quot; Dia. City seal on the top edge. The sample legend is &quot;Atwater Village&quot; on one line. The length is dimensioned &quot;96&quot; max.*&quot; and the height on the right is dimensioned &quot;18&quot; one line&quot; and &quot;24&quot; two lines&quot;. Vertical dimensions on the left: 6&quot;, 6&quot;, 6&quot; (18&quot; total). Caption: &quot;Community/District Name Sign&quot;. An asterisk note below reads that community or district names that would exceed the 96&quot; maximum length shall require two lines of text, with a 24&quot; height.</p>
<p>Custom Neighborhood Sign: A dark green sign with light lettering, clipped (chamfered) corners, and a thin light border. The sample legend is &quot;Castle Heights Neighborhood&quot; in three lines, &quot;Castle&quot;, &quot;Heights&quot; and &quot;Neighborhood&quot;. Dimensioned &quot;36&quot; max.&quot; wide and &quot;36&quot; max.&quot; tall. Caption: &quot;Custom Neighborhood Sign&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved 2002, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Community and Neighborhood Signs, sheet 1 of 2, drawn by M.T., Drawing No. S-502.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>* Community or District names that would exceed the 96&quot; maximum length shall require two lines of text, with a 24&quot; height.</p>
<p>Dimension labels: City Limit Sign 66&quot; wide by 24&quot; tall, vertical stack 6&quot;, 6&quot;, 2&quot;, 6&quot;, 4&quot;. City seal 12&quot; Dia. Community/District Name Sign 96&quot; max.* long, 18&quot; tall for one line, 24&quot; tall for two lines, vertical stack 6&quot;, 6&quot;, 6&quot;. Custom Neighborhood Sign 36&quot; max. by 36&quot; max.</p></details>
