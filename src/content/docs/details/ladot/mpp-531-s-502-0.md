<p class="dwg-sum">Shows three LADOT sign types with dimensions: the 66 inch by 24 inch City Limit sign, the Community/District Name sign, and a custom Neighborhood sign. The community sign is up to 96 inches long and 18 inches tall for one line, and names too long for 96 inches use two lines at 24 inches tall. The custom neighborhood sign is limited to 36 inches by 36 inches.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>City Limit Sign</p>
<p>66 inch wide by 24 inch tall sign with a 12 inch diameter City seal at top. Legend &quot;City of Los Angeles&quot;. Vertical dimensions shown: 6 inch, 6 inch, 2 inch, 6 inch, 4 inch.</p>
<p>Community/District Name Sign</p>
<p>96 inch maximum length,* 18 inch tall for one line, 24 inch tall for two lines. 12 inch diameter City seal at top. Legend example: &quot;Atwater Village&quot;. Vertical dimensions shown: 6 inch, 6 inch, 6 inch.</p>
<p>* Community or District names that would exceed the 96 inch maximum length shall require two lines of text, with a 24 inch height.</p>
<p>Custom Neighborhood Sign</p>
<p>36 inch maximum by 36 inch maximum, with clipped corners. Legend example: &quot;Castle Heights Neighborhood&quot;.</p></details>
