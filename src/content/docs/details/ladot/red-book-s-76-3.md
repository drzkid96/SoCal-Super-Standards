<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-76.3 gives the dimensions of an 8 in. diameter beveled signal visor. It is 18 in. long along the top and 8 in. along the bottom with a 3.5 degree bevel, four ears at 45 degree positions and tolerances of 1/16 in. A note says to bevel the opposite side for a right beveled visor and to see LADOT Purchase Specification 92-061-06.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 57)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Side view of the visor: a cylindrical visor cut at an angle, shown as an elevation, 18 in. +/- 1/16 in. long along the top and 8 in. +/- 1/16 in. long along the bottom, with a dashed hidden line inside and small tabs at the open end. A centerline runs along the axis.</p>
<p>Left beveled view: a plan view of the visor with an elliptical opening, 8 1/8 in. +/- 1/16 in. diameter, and a 3.5 degree bevel angle at the left end. The title below reads Left Beveled.</p>
<p>Ear detail: an enlarged view of an ear with a 3/4 in. +/- 1/32 in. overall width, a 3/8 in. +/- 1/32 in. dimension to the centerline, a 1/8 in. radius +/- 1/32 in. and a 3/8 in. radius +/- 1/32 in.</p>
<p>End view: a circle with four ears at 45 degree positions around the visor, 90 degrees between ears, called out See Ear Detail. Dimensions are 6 7/16 in. +/- 1/16 in. between ears and 8 15/16 in. +/- 1/16 in. diagonal across the ears, with 45 degrees to the vertical centerline.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Visor, 8 in. Dia., Beveled, drawing number S-76.3, drawn 1-04-11, approved January 26, 2011 by Amir Sedadi, Interim General Manager. Page number 50.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Note: Bevel opposite side for Right Beveled Visor. Refer to LADOT Purchase Specification # 92-061-06 for all other details.</p>
<p>Dimensions: 18 in. +/- 1/16 in. 8 in. +/- 1/16 in. 8 1/8 in. +/- 1/16 in. diameter. 3.5 degrees. Ear detail: 3/4 in. +/- 1/32 in., 3/8 in. +/- 1/32 in., 1/8 in. R +/- 1/32 in., 3/8 in. R +/- 1/32 in. End view: 90 degrees, 45 degrees, 6 7/16 in. +/- 1/16 in., 8 15/16 in. +/- 1/16 in.</p></details>
