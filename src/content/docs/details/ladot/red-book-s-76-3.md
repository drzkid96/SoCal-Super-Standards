<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Three sheets showing LADOT signal visor types for 8 in and 12 in heads. The sheets cover the long visor (8 in x 18 in and 12 in x 27 in full circle), the tunnel or pigeon visor (8 in x 8 in and 12 in x 12 in), and the standard full circle visor (8 in and 12 in). Each refers to the ear detail on S-76.3 and to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06.</p>
