<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Three sheets showing LADOT signal visor types for 8 in and 12 in heads. The sheets cover the long visor (8 in x 18 in and 12 in x 27 in full circle), the tunnel or pigeon visor (8 in x 8 in and 12 in x 12 in), and the standard full circle visor (8 in and 12 in). Each refers to the ear detail on S-76.3 and to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 3 (document page 59)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0059.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0059.webp" alt="S-76.3 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-76.3, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p59">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-76.7, Long Visor, 8 in and 12 in diameter full circle. It shows two long visors, each as a side view paired with an end view. All views are marked Not to Scale.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 8&quot; X 18&quot; full circle, side view: a rectangular tube with overall length 18&quot;+/-1/16&quot;. The back edge is tilted 3.5 degrees from vertical, with two small ear tabs on it. Dashed lines near the top and bottom edges, and a dash-dot centerline along the axis.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 8&quot; X 18&quot; full circle, end view: a circular ring dimensioned 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals, spaced with 90 degree callouts between adjacent ears. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 12&quot; X 27&quot; full circle, side view: a tube with overall length 27&quot;+/-1/16&quot; and the same 3.5 degree tilted back edge with two ear tabs. A lens shaped overlap or seam outline is drawn across the lower part of the tube, as it is wrapped from a full circle. Dashed lines at the top and bottom edges and a dash-dot centerline.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 12&quot; X 27&quot; full circle, end view: a circular ring dimensioned 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and four 90 degree callouts. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Title block: Long Visor, 8&quot; &amp; 12&quot; Diameter Full Circle, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved January 26, 2011 for Amir Sedadi, Interim General Manager, Drawing No. S-76.7, page number 52.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. See ear detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3 (shown for both the 8 in x 18 in and the 12 in x 27 in long visors).</p>
<p>2. Note: Refer to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06 for all other details.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 3 (document page 60)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0060.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0060.webp" alt="S-76.3 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-76.3, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p60">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-76.8, Pigeon (Tunnel) Visor, 8 in and 12 in diameter. It shows two tunnel visors, each as a side view paired with an end view. All views are marked Not to Scale.</p>
<p>Visor, 8&quot; X 8&quot; Tunnel, side view: a square looking tube 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; long. The back edge is tilted 3.5 degrees from vertical, with two ear tabs. Dashed lines near the top and bottom edges and a dash-dot centerline. A dash-dot line below the bottom edge marks the open bottom of the tunnel.</p>
<p>Visor, 8&quot; X 8&quot; Tunnel, end view: a ring dimensioned 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and four 90 degree callouts. The lower part of the ring is open, with a gap between the two lower ears dimensioned 4&quot;+/-1/16&quot;. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Visor, 12&quot; X 12&quot; Tunnel, side view: a tube 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; long with the same 3.5 degree tilted back edge, two ear tabs, dashed edges, centerline, and open bottom line.</p>
<p>Visor, 12&quot; X 12&quot; Tunnel, end view: a ring dimensioned 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and 90 degree callouts. The open bottom gap between the two lower ears is 5 3/4&quot;+/-1/16&quot;. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Title block: Pigeon (Tunnel) Visor 8&quot; &amp; 12&quot; Diameter, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved January 26, 2011 for Amir Sedadi, Interim General Manager, Drawing No. S-76.8, page number 53.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. See ear detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3 (shown for both the 8 in x 8 in and the 12 in x 12 in tunnel visors).</p>
<p>2. Note: Refer to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06 for all other details.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 3 (document page 61)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0061.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0061.webp" alt="S-76.3 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-76.3, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p61">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-76.9, Visor 8 in and 12 in diameter full circle (standard short visor). It shows two visors, each as a side view paired with an end view. All views are marked Not to Scale.</p>
<p>Visor, 8&quot; full circle, side view: a short rectangular tube 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; long. The back edge is tilted 3.5 degrees from vertical with two ear tabs. Dashed lines near top and bottom edges and a dash-dot centerline.</p>
<p>Visor, 8&quot; full circle, end view: a closed circular ring dimensioned 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and 90 degree callouts between ears. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Visor, 12&quot; full circle, side view: a tube 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; long with the same 3.5 degree tilted back edge, two ear tabs, dashed edges and centerline.</p>
<p>Visor, 12&quot; full circle, end view: a closed circular ring dimensioned 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and 90 degree callouts. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Title block: Visor 8&quot; &amp; 12&quot; Diameter Full Circle, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved January 26, 2011 for Amir Sedadi, Interim General Manager, Drawing No. S-76.9, page number 54.</p></details>
