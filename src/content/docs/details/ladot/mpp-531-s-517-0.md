<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical layouts for word and symbol pavement markings and for limit and yield lines. Shows WAIT HERE legends, STOP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>) and YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) limit line placement, a BUS ZONE with bike symbol, STOP AHEAD, PED XING and SLOW SCHOOL XING legends spaced 32 ft, and a left-turn arrow pair. A table gives the C distance by posted speed, 100 ft at 25 mph to 325 ft at 50 mph.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet is not to scale. It holds seven small plan views and one table.</p>
<p>Wait here legend (upper left): plan of an intersection approach with a curb return at each side, a stop bar across the lanes, a solid double centerline and dashed lane lines. Two lanes carry the legends HERE above WAIT, set 8&#x27; apart, with 8&#x27; dimensions shown between the stop bar, the first legend and the second.</p>
<p>STOP limit line, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> (upper middle): plan of a corner with an access ramp and the property line. The STOP legend is placed back from the limit line with an 8&#x27; dimension and a 5&#x27; minimum dimension between the curb line extended and the limit line. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> STOP sign is shown beside it.</p>
<p>YIELD line, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> (upper right): a row of four yield triangles (shark teeth), 36&quot; high, with 12&quot; wide triangles spaced 24&quot; apart (12&quot; and 24&quot; dimensions shown). A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> YIELD sign is shown beside it.</p>
<p>Text under these views reads as the first note below.</p>
<p>Bus zone (middle left): a curb-side bus zone with a dashed lane line, the legend BUS ZONE along the curb, a bike symbol and arrow inside a bike lane with a 6&#x27; dimension, and a curb return at the bottom.</p>
<p>Stop ahead (middle): a lane with a stop bar, a crosswalk edge at the top, the legend AHEAD above STOP in the lane, a dimension C from the stop bar to the first legend and 32&#x27; between the legends.</p>
<p>Ped crossing (middle): a marked crosswalk of seven bars at the top, solid and dashed lane lines, the legend XNG above PED with dimension C from the crosswalk and 32&#x27; between legends.</p>
<p>School crossing (middle right): a marked crosswalk at the top, the legends XNG, SCHOOL and SLOW stacked in the lane, with dimension C from the crosswalk and 32&#x27; between each legend.</p>
<p>Turn arrows (lower left): plan of a left-turn lane of length L measured from the limit line. A left-turn arrow is placed at the limit line end of the lane and another L/2 back, with the callout Install if L &gt;= 250&#x27;. A tapered lane line is shown at the lane start.</p>
<p>Table: Posted speed (mph) 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 with C* (ft) 100, 100, 125, 175, 250, 325 respectively.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Typical Pavement Marking Installations, sheet 1 of 1, drawing S-517.0, approved December 11, 2008 for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Drawn M.T. 11-20-08.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Generally, limit and yield lines are located along the extension of the property line. They shall be placed at the near side or upstream of the flat portion (w) of any access ramp. The distance between the curb line extended and the limit line shall be at least 5 feet. The limit line should extend to the middle of the street or the striped centerline.</p>
<p>2. * Distance may exceed the dimensions in the table by up to 100&#x27; in order to adjust to the field conditions.</p>
<p>3. Callout: Install if L &gt;= 250&#x27;. Not to scale.</p></details>
