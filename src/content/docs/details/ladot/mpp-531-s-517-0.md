<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical layouts for word and symbol pavement markings and for limit and yield lines. Shows WAIT HERE legends, STOP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>) and YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) limit line placement, a BUS ZONE with bike symbol, STOP AHEAD, PED XING and SLOW SCHOOL XING legends spaced 32 ft, and a left-turn arrow pair. A table gives the C distance by posted speed, 100 ft at 25 mph to 325 ft at 50 mph.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet layout: seven small plan views and one table, all marked NOT TO SCALE (note at lower left beside the title block).</p>
<p>Wait here legend (upper left): plan of an intersection approach with a curb return on each side, a stop bar across the lanes and a solid double centerline with dashed lane lines. In each of the two lanes the legend HERE is placed above WAIT. Dimensions at right: 8&#x27; from the stop bar to the HERE legend and 8&#x27; from the HERE legend to the WAIT legend.</p>
<p>Stop limit line, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> (upper middle): plan of a corner with an access ramp, the curb line extended and the property line. The 5&#x27; min dimension is between the curb line extended and the limit line. The STOP legend is placed back from the limit line with an 8&#x27; dimension. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> STOP sign is drawn on a post beside the property line, with a larger red octagonal STOP sign labeled <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> below it.</p>
<p>Yield line, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> (upper right): a row of four yield triangles (shark teeth), 36&quot; high, each 12&quot; wide and spaced 24&quot; apart (12&quot; and 24&quot; dimensions shown), placed at the corner with the access ramp. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> YIELD sign is shown on a post, with a larger red and white YIELD sign labeled <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> below it.</p>
<p>Text under these three views: note 1 below.</p>
<p>Bus zone (middle left): a curb-side bus zone. A dashed lane line runs parallel to the curb, the legend BUS ZONE is set along the curb, and a bike symbol with an up arrow is placed inside the bike lane. A 6&#x27; dimension is shown for the bike lane width. A curb return closes the bottom.</p>
<p>Stop ahead (middle): a lane with a stop bar and curb at the top. Two lanes each carry the legend AHEAD above STOP. A dimension C is shown from the stop bar to the first (AHEAD) legend and a 32&#x27; dimension from that legend to the STOP legend.</p>
<p>Pedestrian crossing (middle): a marked crosswalk of vertical bars at the top, solid and dashed lane lines. Each of two lanes carries the legend XNG above PED. Dimension C is from the crosswalk to the first legend and 32&#x27; from the XNG legend to the PED legend.</p>
<p>School crossing (middle right): a marked crosswalk of vertical bars at the top with solid and dashed lane lines. Legends XNG, SCHOOL and SLOW are stacked in each lane. Dimension C is from the crosswalk to the XNG legend, 32&#x27; from XNG to SCHOOL and 32&#x27; from SCHOOL to SLOW.</p>
<p>Turn arrows (lower left): plan of a left-turn lane of length L measured from the limit line at the top. A left-turn arrow is placed at the limit line end of the lane and a second arrow is placed L/2 back (dimension L/2 shown inside dimension L). The callout reads Install if L &gt;= 250&#x27; (on the arrow nearer the limit line). A curving lane line forms the taper at the start of the lane. A dashed lane line and a curb return are drawn to the right.</p>
<p>Table: Posted speed (mph) 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 with C* (ft) 100, 100, 125, 175, 250, 325 respectively.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved December 11, 2008, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Typical Pavement Marking Installations. Sheet 1 of 1. Drawn M.T. 11-20-08, T.E. TC (handwritten initials), Sr. T.E. initials handwritten (appears DW), Pr. T.E. handwritten (appears 88), checked box blank. Drawing No. S-517.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Generally, limit and yield lines are located along the extension of the property line. They shall be placed at the near side or upstream of the flat portion (w) of any access ramp. The distance between the curb line extended and the limit line shall be at least 5 feet. The limit line should extend to the middle of the street or the striped centerline.</p>
<p>Table footnote: * Distance may exceed the dimensions in the table by up to 100&#x27; in order to adjust to the field conditions.</p>
<p>Callout on the turn arrows view: Install if L &gt;= 250&#x27;.</p>
<p>Not to scale.</p></details>
