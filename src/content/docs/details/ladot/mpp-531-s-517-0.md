## Sheet

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0168.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0168.webp" alt="S-517.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-517.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/12-drawings-s-512-to-s-523-0/#p168">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Generally, limit and yield lines are located along the extension of the property line. They shall be placed at the near side or upstream of the flat portion (w) of any access ramp. The distance between the curb line extended and the limit line shall be at least 5 feet. The limit line should extend to the middle of the street or the striped centerline.</li>
</ol>
<p>Table footnote: * Distance may exceed the dimensions in the table by up to 100&#x27; in order to adjust to the field conditions.</p>
<p>Callout on the turn arrows view: Install if L &gt;= 250&#x27;.</p>
<p>Not to scale.</p>
</details>
