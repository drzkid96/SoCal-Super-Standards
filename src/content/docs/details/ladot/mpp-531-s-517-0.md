<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical layouts for word and symbol pavement markings and for limit and yield lines. It shows STOP and YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) line placement with 12" and 24" markings, BUS ZONE, AHEAD, STOP, XING, PED and SCHOOL legends with 32' spacing, and a turn arrow pair for left-turn lanes. Limit and yield lines go along the property line extension, at least 5 feet from the extended curb line, and the C distance varies with posted speed from 100 ft at 25 mph to 325 ft at 50 mph.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Generally, limit and yield lines are located along the extension of the property line. They shall be placed at the near side or upstream of the flat portion (w) of any access ramp. The distance between the curb line extended and the limit line shall be at least 5 feet. The limit line should extend to the middle of the street or the striped centerline.</p>
<p>Not to scale.</p>
<p>Dimensions shown: STOP limit line (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>) with 8&#x27; and 5&#x27; min offsets; YIELD line (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) of 36&quot; triangles, 12&quot; and 24&quot; dimensions; HERE / WAIT legends 8&#x27; apart; BUS ZONE with 6&#x27; bike symbol offset; AHEAD / STOP, XING / PED and XING / SCHOOL / SLOW legends spaced 32&#x27;.</p>
<p>Posted speed (mph) and C distance (ft): 25 = 100, 30 = 100, 35 = 125, 40 = 175, 45 = 250, 50 = 325.</p>
<p>* Distance may exceed the dimensions in the table by up to 100&#x27; in order to adjust to the field conditions.</p>
<p>Install turn arrows if L &gt;= 250&#x27;. A second arrow is placed L/2 back from the limit line.</p></details>
