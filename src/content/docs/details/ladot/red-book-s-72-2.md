<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-72.2 gives the layout of the LADOT bicycle pushbutton sign, a 5 in by 7 3/4 in aluminum sign reading PUSH BUTTON FOR GREEN LIGHT with a bicycle symbol and a directional arrow. Four 1/4 in brass grommets are set 1/2 in from the edges. The sign is .063 aluminum, powder coated, black legend on white, and the arrow may be left, right or double.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 64)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0064.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0064.webp" alt="S-72.2 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-72.2, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p64">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face, full size elevation: a portrait rectangular sign with rounded corners and a double border (outer edge and a thick inner black border line). Symbol and text are black on white. At the top is a bicycle symbol. Below it in large condensed capital letters: PUSH BUTTON, FOR, GREEN LIGHT, on three lines. Below the text is a black arrow marked with an asterisk (use left, right or double arrow as necessary). A 1/4&quot; inside diameter brass grommet is placed in each of the four corners, 4 places typical.</p>
<p>Dimensions: overall sign width is 5&quot; and the horizontal spacing between grommet centers is 4&quot;, with 1/2&quot; from the sign edge to each grommet center. Overall sign height is 7 3/4&quot; and the vertical spacing between grommet centers is 6 3/4&quot;, with 1/2&quot; from the sign edge to each grommet center. The outer corner radius is R = 11/32&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: Bicycle Pushbutton Sign, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved July 11, 2008 for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager, Drawing No. S-72.2.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Note: Sign shall be powder coated aluminum with black legend on white background as shown.</p>
<p>2. Material: .063 aluminum.</p>
<p>3. * Use left, right or double arrow as necessary.</p>
<p>(Callout) 1/4&quot; inside diameter brass grommet, 4 places (typ.).</p></details>
