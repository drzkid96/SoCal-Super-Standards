<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of the City of Los Angeles standard for on-street angle parking stalls, approved 9/18/2020. It shows parking meter locations, a plan of 9 ft by 16 ft angled stalls with a 2 ft maximum overhang and a table of dimensions A, B, C and E for angles from 37.5 to 75 degrees. Notes set thermoplastic material, design basis and the minimum E dimension by street type.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Parking meter locations view: a plan strip with a travel lane between two bands of diagonal hatching, one band along each curb face. The upper curb face carries meters at P=4&#x27; spacing and the hatched band is 20&quot; deep, measured from the curb face. The lower curb face carries meters at P=2&#x27; spacing, also with a 20&quot; dimension. Between the bands, a 4&quot; Y EDGE LINE (yellow) and a 4&quot; W EDGE LINE (white) are called out, and two open arrows point right in the lane. A dimension W is marked across one of the hatched bands.</p>
<p>Stall plan: a plan of angled stalls against a curb face, with a vehicle in two stalls. A 2&#x27; MAX. OVERHANG callout shows the vehicle front extending past the curb face. Stalls are 9&#x27;X16&#x27; with 4&quot; W stall lines drawn as double lines with 10&quot; dimensions at the stall end. Angle phi is measured between the curb face and the stall line. Dimension B is the stall width measured along the curb, C is the spacing along the curb between stall lines at the next stall, A is the perpendicular depth from curb face, and 7&#x27; CLEAR is the clear width between stalls. 16&#x27; is the stall length and 9&#x27; the stall width. E is the distance from the stall ends to the 4&quot; W EDGE LINE (SEE NOTES 3 &amp;4). Below it, the travel lane is 10&#x27; MIN. wide with an open arrow pointing left, bounded by the 4&quot; W EDGE LINE at the stall side and a lane line, double yellow, 4&quot; dashed yellow line, or 4&quot; yellow edge line at the opposite side.</p>
<p>Table of angle dimensions (theta, A, B, C, E): 37.5 deg: A 16.9&#x27;, B 14.8&#x27;, C 22&#x27;, E 11&#x27;. 45 deg: A 17.7&#x27;, B 12.7&#x27;, C 17.7&#x27;, E 11.1&#x27;. 50 deg: A 18.0&#x27;, B 11.75&#x27;, C 15.1&#x27;, E 12&#x27;. 52.5 deg: A 18.2&#x27;, B 11.3&#x27;, C 13.9&#x27;, E 13.1&#x27;. 60 deg: A 18.4&#x27;, B 10.4&#x27;, C 10.6&#x27;, E 15.1&#x27;. 67.5 deg: A 18.2&#x27;, B 9.7&#x27;, C 7.5&#x27;, E 17.3&#x27;. 75 deg: A 17.8&#x27;, B 9.3&#x27;, C 4.8&#x27;, E 19.7&#x27;.</p>
<p>Title block: approved 9/18/2020, signed for Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, On-Street Angle Parking Stalls, sheet 1 of 2, drawing number S-440.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Notes</p>
<p>1. All parking stall lines and edge lines shall be in alkyd-based thermoplastic.</p>
<p>2. Calculations based on, but not limited to Los Angeles Municipal Code (L.A.M.C.), Section 12.21, utilizing 9&#x27;x16&#x27; stall size. Large vehicles requiring the 18-foot dimension are expected to overhang the curb, as shown. Stall design above requires 2-foot overhang for 18-foot vehicle.</p>
<p>3. On local streets, collector streets, and those arterial streets which function as collector streets (see 4, below), the &quot;E&quot; dimension shall be 4&#x27; minimum where the parking angle is greater than 45 degrees. On those local and collector streets with angle parking 45 degrees or less, the &quot;E&quot; dimension may be reduced to a minimum of 2 feet. The 4&#x27; dimension is preferred, however, and should be provided if the roadway width permits.</p>
<p>4. On arterial highways, the &quot;E&quot; dimensions shown in the above table shall be considered required minimums. This is intended to provide for maneuvers to and from the parking stall without obstructing traffic movement in the adjacent traveled lane. Arterial streets are designated in community plans. A designated arterial street may, for the purposes of this standard plan be considered as a collector street if the daily volume is less than 10,000 vehicles, or the striping provides for only one through lane in each direction.</p></details>
