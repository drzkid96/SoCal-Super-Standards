</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Notes</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All parking stall lines and edge lines shall be in alkyd-based thermoplastic.</li>
<li value="2">Calculations based on, but not limited to Los Angeles Municipal Code (L.A.M.C.), Section 12.21, utilizing 9&#x27;x16&#x27; stall size. Large vehicles requiring the 18-foot dimension are expected to overhang the curb, as shown. Stall design above requires 2-foot overhang for 18-foot vehicle.</li>
<li value="3">On local streets, collector streets, and those arterial streets which function as collector streets (see 4, below), the &quot;E&quot; dimension shall be 4&#x27; minimum where the parking angle is greater than 45 degrees. On those local and collector streets with angle parking 45 degrees or less, the &quot;E&quot; dimension may be reduced to a minimum of 2 feet. The 4&#x27; dimension is preferred, however, and should be provided if the roadway width permits.</li>
<li value="4">On arterial highways, the &quot;E&quot; dimensions shown in the above table shall be considered required minimums. This is intended to provide for maneuvers to and from the parking stall without obstructing traffic movement in the adjacent traveled lane. Arterial streets are designated in community plans. A designated arterial street may, for the purposes of this standard plan be considered as a collector street if the daily volume is less than 10,000 vehicles, or the striping provides for only one through lane in each direction.</li>
</ol>
</details>
