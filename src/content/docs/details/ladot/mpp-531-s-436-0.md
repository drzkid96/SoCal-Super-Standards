<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows pavement markings in advance of a railroad crossing: paired RXR symbols, bar lines and railroad limit lines, with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) signs where applicable. Limit lines are set 15 feet from the edge of rail or 8 feet from the gate, and the advance marking distance comes from Table A by posted speed, from 50 feet at 25 and 30 mph up to 400 feet at 55 mph. It illustrates a two-lane road, a multilane road and an angled crossing of 60 degrees or greater.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>The sheet is drawn rotated 90 degrees, with the three plan views stacked left to right and the approach to the railroad crossing running toward the top of the rotated drawing (the text reads sideways).</p>
<p>Two-lane road plan (left view): A roadway with a dashed centerline and a lane on each side, a parking lane on the right shown with a parked car symbol, and an Edge of traveled lane, Parking lane or shoulder label. A pair of railroad tracks crosses the road at an angle at the bottom of the plan. Railroad limit lines (heavy transverse bars) are placed 15&#x27; from edge of rail or 8&#x27; from gate, shown on both approaches with the callout. Advance markings in the traveled lane consist of the paired RXR symbols (an X with R on each side, two symbols per lane) between bar lines. Two bar lines are labeled Bar Line, each placed 90 degrees to the lane (callouts 90 degrees), with 2&#x27; dimensions shown between bar lines and from the limit line. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) (if applicable) sign callout sits at the start of the markings. A dimension of 50&#x27; is shown, with See Table A, for the distance back from the limit line to the sign. The bottom approach shows the same arrangement in the other direction.</p>
<p>Multilane road with angled crossing (center view): Two traffic lanes with dashed lane lines on each side of the centerline and a curb on each side (labeled Curb). The tracks cross at an angle. Railroad limit lines are drawn angled parallel to the tracks. The callout 15&#x27; from edge of rail or 8&#x27; from gate. For angled crossings of 60 degrees or greater, install as shown to left. The angled limit lines meet the lane edge, with the lane line at the curb. A Bar Line is 90 degrees to the lane and the RXR symbols (three per lane) are placed in the lane between bar lines. A dimension See Table A is shown from the limit line. At the right edge, a callout says Striped full time or peak hour curb lane. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) (if applicable) callout is repeated.</p>
<p>Multilane approach (right view): A multilane approach with three paired RXR symbols (R X R X R X in a row, i.e. three RXR symbols) per lane between a Bar Line and a second bar line, with See Table A distance and a Striped full time or peak hour curb lane callout, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) (if applicable) at the start of the markings.</p>
<p>Sign details: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> is the circular railroad crossing advance warning sign with a black X and R R. W48(CA) is a rectangular sign TRACKS with a 2 in a tab above (2 TRACKS), labeled Install if two or more tracks.</p>
<p>Table A (posted speed in mph and distance in feet): 25 mph: 50 ft. 30 mph: 50 ft. 35 mph: 125 ft. 40 mph: 175 ft. 45 mph: 250 ft. 50 mph: 325 ft. 55 mph: 400 ft. The heading is marked with an asterisk.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved May 8, 2008, signed for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. RXR ADVANCE MARKING, sheet 1 of 1. Drawn by M.T./S.A., T.E. TC, Sr. T.E. initials, Pr. T.E. [illegible]. Drawing No. S-436.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>* The distances shown in Table A are approximate only. Dimensions of field installations may exceed the figures in the table by up to 100 feet in order to adjust to field conditions. Distances shown are from Table 2C-4 (MUTCD) using condition B and posted speed plus 5 mph.</p>
<p>Callouts: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) (if applicable). W48(CA): install if two or more tracks.</p>
<p>Railroad limit lines: 15&#x27; from edge of rail or 8&#x27; from gate. For angled crossings of 60 degrees or greater, install as shown to left.</p>
<p>Bar lines are placed at 90 degrees to the lane. See Table A for the distance from the railroad limit line.</p>
<p>Striped full time or peak hour curb lane.</p></details>
