<p class="dwg-sum">Shows pavement markings in advance of a railroad crossing: paired RXR symbols, bar lines and railroad limit lines, with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) signs where applicable. Railroad limit lines are set 15 feet from the edge of rail or 8 feet from the gate, and the advance marking distance comes from Table A by posted speed, from 50 feet at 25 and 30 mph up to 400 feet at 55 mph. It illustrates a two-lane road, a multilane road and an angled crossing of 60 degrees or greater.</p>

<h5>Posted Speed (MPH) and Distance (feet):</h5>
<p>25 mph: 50 ft</p>
<p>30 mph: 50 ft</p>
<p>35 mph: 125 ft</p>
<p>40 mph: 175 ft</p>

<p>The distances shown in Table A are approximate only. Dimensions of field installations may exceed the figures in the table by up to 100 feet in order to adjust to field conditions. Distances shown are from Table 2C-4 (MUTCD) using condition B and posted speed plus 5 mph.</p>
<p>Callouts</p>
<p><a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a> and W48(CA) (if applicable). Install W48(CA) if two or more tracks.</p>
<p>Railroad limit lines: 15&#x27; from edge of rail or 8&#x27; from gate.</p>
<p>For angled crossings of 60 degrees or greater, install as shown to the left.</p>
<p>Bar lines are placed 90 degrees to the lane, with 2&#x27; dimensions shown between the bar lines on the two-lane layout. Two RXR symbols are shown on the two-lane layout and three on the multilane layouts.</p>
<p>Edge of traveled lane, parking lane or shoulder, and curb are labeled.</p>
<p>Striped full time or peak hour curb lane.</p></details>
