<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.1A, sheet 1, shows the layout of a pair of 6 ft round inductive loops spaced 8 ft apart with a twisted lead-in running to a pullbox within 3 ft of the curb face. It gives the winding order (3 clockwise turns per detector) and sawcut slot sections with 3 in or 5 in minimum depth, 1/2 in maximum width, and a limit of 8 wires per slot. Slots are filled with loop sealant to within 1/16 in of the top.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 67)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0067.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0067.webp" alt="S-70.1A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.1A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p67">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: two round loops, LOOP-1 and LOOP-2, in a lane. Each loop is 6&#x27; in diameter and the clear spacing between the two loops is 8&#x27;. Traffic flows left to right, shown by an arrow labeled DIRECTION OF TRAFFIC FLOW. The loop wires leave the loops on the inside edges and join into a single lead-in that runs toward the curb. The lead-in crosses the CURB FACE and ends at a PULLBOX that is 3&#x27;-0&quot; MAX. from the curb face. Section cut markers A-A are at each loop, B-B at the diagonal wire runs, C-C at the joined lead-in, and D-D across the curb face at the pullbox (D-D is detailed on sheet 2 of S-70.1A).</p>
<p>Winding details (not to scale): LOOP-1 and LOOP-2 are each drawn as three concentric clockwise turns. The lead-in wires from each loop are twisted clockwise into a pair, at least 2 turns per foot, and run to the pullbox. The four ends are numbered 1, 2, 3 and 4 at the pullbox end.</p>
<p>Section A-A (through the loop slot): a sawcut slot filled with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of the top (do not overfill). Slot depth is 3&quot; MIN.* and slot width is 1/2&quot; MAX. Three loop wires are stacked at the bottom of the slot (see note 1).</p>
<p>Section B-B (diagonal run between loops): slot depth 5&quot; MIN.*, slot width 1/2&quot; MAX., two wires at the bottom.</p>
<p>Section C-C (joined lead-in): slot depth 5&quot; MIN.*, slot width 1/2&quot; MAX., four wires shown at the bottom, labeled 8 WIRES MAXIMUM PER SAWCUT SLOT.</p>
<p>Title block: Inductive Loop Installation, sheet 1 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved July 9, 2008 for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.1A.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>* Depth of slot not to exceed depth of pavement. For P.C.C. (concrete) surfaces, the minimum cover above loop wire shall be 2.5&quot; minimum.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install 3 clockwise turns of loop wire for each detector, unless otherwise shown on signal plan.</p>
<p>2. See LADOT material specification No. 92-081-05 or latest revision.</p>
<p>3. Any non-round shaped loops must conform to all other specifications shown on this standard drawing.</p>
<p>Callouts: Fill slot with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of top (do not overfill). Twisted clockwise (at least 2 turn per ft.) into a pair. To pullbox. Direction of traffic flow. Curb face. Pullbox 3&#x27;-0&quot; max. from curb face.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 68)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0068.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0068.webp" alt="S-70.1A sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.1A, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p68">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section D-D (not to scale): a cut through the curb, gutter and roadway at the pullbox. At left, the pullbox pit with its lid and the curb and gutter, with sidewalk paving, sits on rock base. A 2&quot; PVC or 2&quot; PVC FLEX conduit leaves the bottom of the pullbox, curves under the curb and gutter and runs to the stub-out in the street. The pavement opening is at least 42&quot; MIN. from the curb face and at least 18&quot; MIN. from the edge of the gutter. A hot patch asphalt concrete patch is placed in the opening, which is 16&quot; MAX. wide. Existing pavement lies on both sides.</p>
<p>Conduit detail (not to scale): a longitudinal section at the stub-out. Existing pavement is at left and right, with HOT PATCH ASPHALT CONCRETE 4&quot; thick over 1&quot; of SAND in the opening. A 2&quot; PVC or 2&quot; PVC FLEX conduit rises at 45 degrees from a minimum 18&quot; below the street surface through sub base and exits at the surface, where the loop wires (marked *) continue horizontally into the sawcut slot filled with LOOP SEALANT. Labels: existing pavement, sub base, sand, loop sealant, loop wires, hot patch asphalt concrete.</p>
<p>Stub-out detail, top view (not to scale): a rectangular hot patch asphalt concrete patch within existing pavement, 16&quot; MAX. long and 12&quot; MAX. wide. A PVC conduit enters from the left as dashed lines and ends in the patch.</p>
<p>Title block: Inductive Loop Installation, sheet 2 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.1A.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes: Perform loop installation in the following order:</h5>
<p>1. Open the pavement at least 42&quot; from curb face and at least 18&quot; from edge of gutter using a star drill or a jack hammer (asphalt concrete only). Open no more than a 12&quot; x 16&quot; area. In cases where there is a concrete bus pad adjacent to the gutter, install the stubout beyond the edge of the bus pad.</p>
<p>2. Install 2&quot; PVC (SC80) or PVC flex (SC40) from the pullbox pit with a 45 degree elbow at the stub-out as shown. Depth of the conduit shall be at least 18&quot; below the street grade.</p>
<p>3. Patch street using hot patch asphalt concrete and sand as shown.</p>
<p>4. Install duct seal where wires enter 2&quot; PVC or 2&quot; PVC flex.</p>
<p>5. Fill sawcut slot with Caltrans approved hot-melt rubberized asphalt sealant.</p>
<p>* No more than 8 loops or 16 wires per stub-out.</p></details>
