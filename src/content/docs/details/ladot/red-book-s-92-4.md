<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of standard drawing S-92.4 for a 14' cantilever standard carrying a changeable message sign up to 5'x12'. It shows the 8XX-Strong pipe post and arm elevation, the arm to post connection, the arm end plate and the 36" diameter, 12'-6" deep cast-in-drilled-hole foundation. Maximum sign weight is 750#, and numbered notes continue on sheet 2.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 103)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0103.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0103.webp" alt="S-92.4 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-92.4, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p103">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation: A 14&#x27; cantilever changeable message sign (CMS) standard. A vertical post of 8XX-Strong pipe carries a horizontal sign arm of 8XX-Strong pipe, with a 5&#x27;X12&#x27; CMS (max.) drawn as a dashed rectangle with break lines on the arm. The arm projects 14&#x27;-0&quot; from the post centerline. The sign arm note reads max. sign weight = 750#. The post is 18&quot; from top of post to arm centerline, 20&#x27;-1&quot; from grade to arm centerline and 21&#x27;-7&quot; from grade to post top (with break lines in the post). A 36&quot; dimension locates the curb from the post centerline. A hand hole is shown low on the post. Detail &quot;A&quot; (arm to post connection, circled at the top), Detail &quot;B&quot; (arm end, circled at the end of the arm), Detail &quot;C&quot; (base, circled at grade) and Detail &quot;D&quot; (anchor bolt bottom plate) are called out. Below grade, a cast in drilled hole foundation 36&quot; O.D. is 12&#x27;-6&quot; deep with a 1&quot; conduit to T. S. (traffic signal) pull box leaving it, 2&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. below grade, see Section A-A for reinforcing.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;A&quot; (arm to post connection): Plan view shows the post, the sign arm, 1-1/4&quot; and 1-1/4&quot; plate dimensions, 1/4&quot; and 1/2&quot; welds, 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; backing ring, tack weld and 1/2&quot; fillet weld. Elevation shows 3/16 pl. (removable top) 9-5/8&quot; O.D. with 2 set screws, a bar 8&quot; dia. x 2&quot; x 3/16&quot; bent to circular shape, a 1/8&quot; weld, 18&quot; dimension, 5/8&quot; pl. x 4-3/8&quot; x 14&quot; long (2 required) with 1/2&quot; weld, 4 - 1-1/2&quot; dia. x 4&quot; ASTM A325 bolts with hex nut and washer, sign arm 8-5/8&quot; O.D. and post 8-5/8&quot; O.D. A face view shows the connection plate with 4 - 1-1/2&quot; dia. holes, 17&quot; overall width with 13&quot; hole spacing and 2&quot; edge distances, 14&quot; overall height with 10&quot; hole spacing and 2&quot; edge distances, R=2&quot; corners, a 2&quot; dia. hole, 3/4&quot; std. half coupling, 5/8&quot; x 5&quot; x 9&quot; long, 2 required, cut to fit flush, a 1/2&quot; weld, a continuous 3/4&quot; bar (see Note 1) with a 13/64&quot; weld.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;B&quot; (arm end): An end plate 3/16&quot; x 9&quot; O.D. with a 1/8&quot; weld on the arm end, a continuous 3/4&quot; bar (see Note 1) with 13/64&quot; weld inside the arm, shown in side and end views.</p>
<p>Title block: 14&#x27; Cantilever Changeable Message Sign, sheet 1 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawn 11-03-05 (MT), Sr. T. E. SS 12-9-05, approved December 9, 2005 by Frances T. Banerjee, Interim General Manager. Drawing No. S-92.4. Approved on 11-21-05, Structural Engineering Division, BOE, by Peter Chiu. Page number 96.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Callouts: Sign arm 8XX-Strong pipe (max. sign weight = 750#). Post 8XX-Strong pipe. 5&#x27;X12&#x27; CMS (max.). 1&quot; conduit to T. S. (traffic signal) pull box. Cast in drilled hole, 36&quot; O.D. See Section A-A for reinforcing. Continuous 3/4&quot; bar, see Note 1 (Details A and B).</p>
<p>The numbered notes referenced (Note 1) are not on this sheet and continue on sheet 2 of 2.</p>
<p>Approval: Approved on 11-21-05, Structural Engineering Division, Bureau of Engineering (BOE), by Peter Chiu.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 104)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0104.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0104.webp" alt="S-92.4 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-92.4, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p104">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section A-A (plan of foundation): Circular cast in drilled hole foundation, 36&quot; Dia., with 3&quot; clear to the reinforcing. Shows 12-#7 x 9&#x27;-6&quot; long bars equally spaced, #4 spiral @ 6&quot; pitch, a base plate with 2&quot; R corners and four anchor bolt holes in a 1&#x27;-1&quot; by 1&#x27;-1&quot; pattern (1&#x27;-6&quot; overall plate width and height, 2-1/2&quot; edge distances), a 3&quot; dia. hole finished smooth at the center and a 2&quot; dia. hole. The center line of the sign (CL Sign) is marked to the right.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;C&quot; (base of post and top of foundation): Section through the post base on the foundation. The post has a 3&quot;x5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover, 1&quot; radius corners in hand hole, 4-1/2&quot; and 8&quot; dimensions above the base plate. The base plate is set at the top of walk on mortar with 2&quot; and 2&quot; dimensions, a 2&#x27;-8&quot; overall dimension from the top of the plate to the bottom plate, and see Weld Detail. Callouts: 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; backing ring, tack weld, 3/8&quot; dia. drain hole, 4 - 1-3/4&quot; dia. anchor bolts, thrd. 8, galv. top 12&quot;, 2 hex nuts and 2 std. washers each, ASTM A-307, banding wire, 2&quot; conduit, foundation concrete 560-C-3250 (see reinf. in Sec. A-A), and a bar 5&quot; x 1&quot; x 1&#x27;-7&quot; long (parallel to axis of sign, 2 required) tack welded at the anchor bolt ends, 1&quot; dimension at the bolt bottom. Section marker A points to Section A-A.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;D&quot; (anchor bar): Plan of the bar 5&quot; wide, with two 1-7/8&quot; dia. holes. Dimensions 3&quot;, 6-1/2&quot;, 6-1/2&quot; and 3&quot; along the length.</p>
<p>Weld Detail (not to scale): The pipe wall on the base plate with a backing ring and a tack weld, fillet weld shown 1/8 by 1/2 and a 1/2&quot; dimension.</p>
<p>Specifications block: Design per 2001 AASHTO Standard Specifications for structural supports for Highway Signs, Luminaires, and Traffic Signals. Wind velocity 85 MPH.</p>
<p>Title block: 14&#x27; Cantilever Changeable Message Sign, sheet 2 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-92.4. Page number 97.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Specifications. Design: 2001 American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials (AASHTO) Standard Specifications for structural supports for Highway Signs, Luminaires, and Traffic Signals. Wind velocity: 85 miles per hour (MPH).</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B Specifications.</p>
<p>2. Bar reinforcements shall conform to ASTM A615, Grade 60.</p>
<p>3. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except as shown on the plan.</p>
<p>4. Welding design and fabrication shall be in accordance with the latest edition of the AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1-Steel.</p>
<p>5. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>6. Sign Arm shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>7. Mounting Brackets shall be per Caltrans Standard Drawing No. S40R.</p>
<p>Detail callouts: Foundation concrete shall be 560-C-3250 (see reinf. in Sec. A-A).</p></details>
