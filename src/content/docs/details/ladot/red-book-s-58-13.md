## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (page 116)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0116.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0116.webp" alt="S-58.13 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-58.13, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p116">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> (No Right Turn symbol), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> (No Left Turn symbol). Message colors: Red - circle and diagonal, width of circle and diagonal - 1.5&quot; stroke - 3 pixel. Lunar white - left arrow or right arrow, width of arrow - 2&quot; stroke - 4 pixel.</li>
<li value="2">The &quot;NO LEFT TURN&quot; and &quot;NO RIGHT TURN&quot; symbols shall be formed by rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</li>
<li value="3">Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernible and readable message.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Refer to LADOT Std.</strong> Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for mounting assembly.</li>
<li value="5"><strong>Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No.</strong> 82-049-03.</li>
<li value="6"><strong>For housing description, see LADOT Std.</strong> Dwg. S-58.21.</li>
<li value="7">24&quot; x 24&quot; electric sign shall include a visor enclosure as shown.</li>
<li value="8">Lamps shall be solid state, 7W maximum.</li>
</ol>
</details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (page 117)

<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>Sign face (SIGN FACE), not to scale.</strong> Standard sign is 24&quot; by 24&quot;. Pedestrian head sign is 16.25&quot; wide by 14.5&quot; high.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Visor enclosure (VISOR ENCLOSURE), not to scale.</strong> 24&quot; wide, 24&quot; tall, 6&quot; deep sides, 2&quot; radius (Rad.) corners.</li>
<li value="3"><strong>No Left &amp; No Right Turn Electric Sign.</strong> City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-58.13, sheet 2 of 2. There are no numbered notes on this sheet.</li>
</ol>
</details>
