Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: R3-1 (No Right Turn symbol), R3—2 (No Left Turn symbol) Message Colors: Red — Circle &amp; Diagonal. Width of Circle and Diagonal — 1.5 ” stroke — 3 pixel Lunar White — Left Arrow or Right Arrow. Width of Arrow — 2” stroke — 4 Pixel 2.
