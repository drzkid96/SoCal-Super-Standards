### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 116)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>14.5&quot; sign face (pedestrian head sign). Visor enclosure. Not to scale.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> (No Right Turn symbol), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> (No Left Turn symbol).</p>
<p>Message colors: Red - circle and diagonal. Width of circle and diagonal - 1.5&quot; stroke - 3 pixel. Lunar white - left arrow or right arrow. Width of arrow - 2&quot; stroke - 4 pixel.</p>
<p>2. The &quot;NO LEFT TURN&quot; and &quot;NO RIGHT TURN&quot; symbols shall be formed by rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</p>
<p>3. Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernible and readable message.</p>
<p>Refer to LADOT Std. Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for mounting assembly. Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No. 82-049-03. For housing description, see LADOT Std. Dwg. S-58.21.</p>
<p>24&quot; x 24&quot; electric sign shall include a visor enclosure as shown. Lamps shall be solid state, 7W maximum.</p></details>
