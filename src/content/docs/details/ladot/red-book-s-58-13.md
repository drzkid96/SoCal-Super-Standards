<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of standard drawing S-58.13 (July 24, 2009) for the fiber optic No Left Turn and No Right Turn electric sign, in a 16.25 x 14.5 inch pedestrian head size and a 24 x 24 inch size with a 6 inch deep visor enclosure. Notes set the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> symbols, red circle and lunar white arrow colors, 0.50 inch fiber optic spacing, 7W solid state lamps and referenced drawings S-63.1.4 and S-58.21.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 116)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0116.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0116.webp" alt="S-58.13 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-58.13, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p116">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face, pedestrian head sign (not to scale): A rounded-corner rectangle 16.25&quot; wide by 14.5&quot; high containing a dotted circle with a diagonal slash from lower left to upper right and a dotted arrow pointing left that turns up from a vertical stem. The dots represent the fiber optic points of the No Left Turn / No Right Turn symbol. The 16.25&quot; x 14.5&quot; size is labeled (Pedestrian Head Sign).</p>
<p>Sign face, 24&quot; x 24&quot; sign: The same symbol on a rounded-corner square 24&quot; wide by 24&quot; high, with a double ring of dots forming the circle and diagonal and a multi-row dotted arrow with a left-pointing head and a vertical stem.</p>
<p>Visor enclosure (isometric, not to scale): A three-sided hood (top and two sides) for the 24&quot; x 24&quot; sign, 24&quot; wide across the top, 24&quot; high on the sides, and 6&quot; deep, with 2&quot; Rad. rounded lower front corners of the side panels.</p>
<p>Title block: No Left &amp; No Right Turn Electric Sign, sheet 1 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Approved July 24, 2009 for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Drawing No. S-58.13. Drawn MT 7-9-09, T. E. JV 7-16-09, Sr. T. E. JW 7-21-09, Pr. T. E. SS 7-22-09. Page number 109.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> (No Right Turn symbol), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> (No Left Turn symbol). Message colors: Red - circle and diagonal, width of circle and diagonal - 1.5&quot; stroke - 3 pixel. Lunar white - left arrow or right arrow, width of arrow - 2&quot; stroke - 4 pixel.</p>
<p>2. The &quot;NO LEFT TURN&quot; and &quot;NO RIGHT TURN&quot; symbols shall be formed by rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</p>
<p>3. Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernible and readable message.</p>
<p>4. Refer to LADOT Std. Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for mounting assembly.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No. 82-049-03.</p>
<p>6. For housing description, see LADOT Std. Dwg. S-58.21.</p>
<p>7. 24&quot; x 24&quot; electric sign shall include a visor enclosure as shown.</p>
<p>8. Lamps shall be solid state, 7W maximum.</p></details>
