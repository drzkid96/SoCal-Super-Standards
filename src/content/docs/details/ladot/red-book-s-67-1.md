<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-67.1 shows mast arm mounting fittings: a plumbizer bracket, a 72-tooth slip fitter, a typical raintight connector with a special 1/2 in.-13NC screw and a lock ring. Bolt and set screw sizes are listed, all hot dip galvanized. One note requires State of California Standard Specifications.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 55)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0055.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0055.webp" alt="S-67.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-67.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p55">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plumbizer: an isometric view of a plumbizer mast arm bracket, a long arm with a threaded round hub at one end, a hollow tubular body with a slot and a clamp collar at the other end. Callouts: 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 3/4 in. sq. hd. cup pt. set screw, hot dip galvanized, and 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 4 in. sq. hd. bolt with 2 nuts and 2 flatwashers, hot dip galvanized.</p>
<p>Slip fitter: an isometric view of a 90 degree elbow-shaped slip fitter with a socket end on the side and a toothed end at the bottom with 72 teeth. Callouts: 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 3/4 in. sq. hd. cup pt. set screw, hot dip galvanized, (2) Req., and the bolt and nut for the clamp.</p>
<p>Typical raintight connector: an exploded isometric view of a pole shaft with a slot, a keyed round plug and a raintight connector body with a threaded hole. A dashed line joins the parts with a special screw, 1/2 in.-13NC x 3/4 in., modified hex. head, hot dip galvanized.</p>
<p>Lock ring: an isometric view of a 72-tooth lock ring with two keyed notches, 5/16 in. high.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Fittings, Mast Arm Mounting, drawing number S-67.1, drawn 2-67, revised 3-14-83, approved April 5, 1967 by A. L. Hutchison, Asst. General Manager. Page number 48.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Construction, material and finish painting shall conform to &#x27;Standard Specifications&#x27; (latest edition), Dept. of Transportation, State of California.</p>
<p>Callouts: 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 3/4 in. sq. hd. cup pt. set screw, hot dip galvanized, (2) Req. 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 4 in. sq. hd. bolt with 2 nuts and 2 flatwashers, hot dip galvanized. 72 teeth. Special screw 1/2 in.-13NC x 3/4 in. modified hex. head, hot dip galvanized. 5/16 in. Views: Plumbizer, Slip Fitter, Typical Raintight Connector, Lock Ring.</p></details>
