<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-52.1.3 showing the LADOT Type F-332 foundation for a 332 controller cabinet, a 30 in. x 24 in. concrete block 18 in. deep with four 3/4 in. anchor bolts, an 8 ft copper weld ground rod and two 3 in. conduits to a #3 pullbox. 36 in. x 24 in. slabs are placed at the front and rear in unpaved areas and no pullbox may be located within them. Grounding details are on S-79.9B.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 92)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0092.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0092.webp" alt="S-52.1.3 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.3, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p92">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Top view: A 30&quot; long by 24&quot; wide foundation seen from above, set 36&quot; back from a curb (curb shown as two parallel lines with break marks at each end). A 36&quot; slab in unpaved area, also 24&quot; wide, is shown at each end of the foundation. Four anchor bolt locations are laid out 25&quot; apart along the length and 15&quot; apart across the width, 4 1/2&quot; typical from the long edges and 2 1/2&quot; typical from the end, with 9&quot; from the rear bolts to the rear edge. Two conduit risers and a ground rod hole are shown, with two conduits running dashed to the rear through the rear slab and labeled &#x27;TO #3 PULLBOX&#x27;. The rear slab is labeled &#x27;MOUNT REARSIDE OF CABINET&#x27;.</p>
<p>Side view: The foundation is 30&quot; long and 18&quot; tall, projecting 5 1/2&quot; above the adjacent unpaved slab and 2&quot; above the bushing plane. 4 anchor bolts 3/4&quot; x 15&quot; x 2&quot; (4 required) project from the top. A 1/2&quot; dia x 8&#x27;-0&quot; copper weld ground rod runs down through the foundation. A bushing sits on the conduit riser. Two 3&quot; conduits (2-3&quot; CONDUIT) bend out of the foundation toward the pullbox at a depth of 14&quot; MIN. below the adjacent slab surface.</p>
<p>Title block: Foundation, Type F-332, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-52.1.3. Drawn MT 11-8-11, Pr. T.E. SS 12-8-11. Approved 12-8-11, signed for Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Printed page number 85.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTE:</h5>
<p>1. No pullbox to be located within the front or rear 24&quot; x 36&quot; concrete slab.</p>
<p>2. See Standard Drawing No. S-79.9B for grounding details.</p>
<p>Callouts: Curb. 36&quot; slab in unpaved area (front and rear). Mount rearside of cabinet. To #3 pullbox. 1/2&quot; dia. x 8&#x27;-0&quot; copper weld ground rod. 3/4&quot; x 15&quot; x 2&quot; anchor bolts (4 required). Bushing. 2-3&quot; conduit. 14&quot; minimum (min.).</p></details>
