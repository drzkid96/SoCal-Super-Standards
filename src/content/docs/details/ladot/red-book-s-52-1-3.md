<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Foundation for a traffic signal controller cabinet, shown in top view and side view. Cabinet is mounted on a 24 by 36 inch concrete slab with four anchor bolts, a 3/4 by 8 foot copper weld ground rod, and 30 inch deep conduit. Pull boxes must not be located within the 24 by 36 inch slab.</p>
