<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Where the lane dropped is a peak period lane, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a> signs shall be modified by the addition of a black-on-yellow sign plate below to indicate the hours during which the curb lane is used by moving traffic.</li>
<li value="2">Where the lane drop is installed in advance of a parking lane which is used as a thru lane for peak period traffic, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a> signs shall be modified by the addition of a black-on-yellow sign plate below to indicate the excepted hours during which the lane is continuous.</li>
</ol>
