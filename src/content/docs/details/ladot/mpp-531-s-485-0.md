<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical signs and pavement markings for a lane reduction (lane drop), based on MUTCD Section 3B.09 and Figure 3B-12(CA). It shows the permanent lane drop and two peak-period cases, a peak-period lane that becomes an all-day parking lane and a full-time lane that becomes a peak-period lane. Table 1 gives the advance placement distance d and taper rate by posted speed, using posted speed plus 5 mph, for example 450 ft and 15:1 for a 25 mph road. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a> signs are used, with a plate when the lane is peak-period only.</p>

<p>Peak period lane to all day parking lane</p>
<p>With a 3-7PM MON-FRI plate (see Note 1) on <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a>. Tow Away No Stopping (peak hours only) before the taper, all day parking permitted beyond it. 8&#x27; parking lane (7&#x27; minimum). 8&quot; white line and 12&quot; W diagonals, taper see Table 1. Lane line and curb line as shown.</p>
<p>Full time lane to peak period lane</p>
<p>With an EXCEPT 3-7PM MON-FRI plate (see Note 2) on <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a>. Tow Away No Stopping Any Time before the taper, Tow Away No Stopping (peak hours only) beyond it. Lane line and curb line as shown.</p>
<p>Table 1</p>
<p>Posted speed limit (mph) / posted speed limit plus 5 mph / d distance (feet) / d/4 (feet) / taper rate (minimum)</p>
<p>25 / 30 / 450 / 113 / 15:1</p>
<p>30 / 35 / 550 / 138 / 20.5:1</p>
<p>35 / 40 / 650 / 163 / 26.7:1</p>
<p>40 / 45 / 750 / 188 / 45:1</p>
<p>45 / 50 / 850 / 213 / 50:1</p>
<p>50 / 55 / 950 / 238 / 55:1</p>
<p>55 / 60 / 1100 / 275 / 60:1</p>
<p>&quot;d&quot; = Advance placement distance per MUTCD, Section 2C.05, Condition A. Taper rates and &quot;d&quot; distances shown are based on posted speed plus 5 mph. Where the 85% speed (critical speed) on the approach is determined to be equal or less than the posted speed, the table numbers for the next lower speed increment may be used.</p>

<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Where the lane dropped is a peak period lane, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a> signs shall be modified by the addition of a black-on-yellow sign plate below to indicate the hours during which the curb lane is used by moving traffic.</p>
<p>2. Where the lane drop is installed in advance of a parking lane which is used as a thru lane for peak period traffic, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-1.svg">W9-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a> signs shall be modified by the addition of a black-on-yellow sign plate below to indicate the excepted hours during which the lane is continuous.</p></details>
