<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.3 is a wiring schematic that connects up to eight actuation or phase loops in series pairs to a 4 pair lead-in cable (blue/white, orange/white, green/white, brown/white). Loops are connected in series, never in parallel, and unused pairs are not connected. Two or three pair cable may be used when shown on the signal plan.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 73)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0073.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0073.webp" alt="S-70.3 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.3, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p73">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Schematic wiring diagram titled Actuation / Phase Loops, with a dashed CURB LINE across the lower part and four arrows labeled DIRECTION OF TRAVEL (pointing right) at upper right.</p>
<p>Loops: eight loops are drawn as coil symbols in two columns. Left column: LOOP 1 (terminals 1 and 2), LOOP 3 (terminals 5 and 6), LOOP 5 (terminals 9 and 10), LOOP 7 (terminals 13 and 14). Right column: LOOP 2 (terminals 3 and 4), LOOP 4 (terminals 7 and 8), LOOP 6 (terminals 11 and 12), LOOP 8 (terminals 15 and 16). Nested lead wires run down from each loop terminal past the curb line. Below the curb line, solid dots mark splices where leads of adjacent loops are joined in series, and four more pairs of dots mark the connections to four twisted pairs.</p>
<p>Cable: the four twisted pairs enter a 4 PAIR CABLE with pairs labeled BLUE/WHITE, ORANGE/WHITE, GREEN/WHITE and BROWN/WHITE at the right. A drain wire is connected to earth ground at the controller cabinet.</p>
<p>Title block: Multi-Pair Loop Lead-In Cable, sheet 1/1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved June 26, 2008, Rita L. Robinson, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.3. Drawn MT 05-07-08.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Connect two to four loops in series to each pair, do not use parallel connections.</p>
<p>2. Do not connect any unused cable pairs.</p>
<p>3. Typical installation shown. Two or three pair cable may be used when shown on signal plan.</p>
<p>Callouts: Actuation / phase loops. Direction of travel. Curb line. 4 pair cable. Blue/white, orange/white, green/white, brown/white. Drain wire connected to earth ground at controller cabinet.</p></details>
