## Sheet

<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">The &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; sign shall be internally illuminated by super LED diodes. The diodes shall emit red colored light. The sign background shall be black.</li>
<li value="2">The sign message &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; shall be 4&quot; in height with brush stroke width of 1/2&quot;. The arrow shall be 6-1/2&quot; long with brush stroke width of 1&quot;.</li>
<li value="3">The sign housing shall consist of two (2) standard* pedestrian signal enclosures connected to one another in a vertical manner.</li>
<li value="4">Minimum space shall be provided to allow free movement of door openings.</li>
<li value="5">The &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; electric sign shall be displayed only with the hand symbol of the pedestrian signal head.</li>
</ol>
<p>* Meeting LADOT specifications.</p>
</details>
