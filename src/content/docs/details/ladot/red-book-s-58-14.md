<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LED electric sign reading USE TUNNEL with an arrow, mounted below a regular pedestrian signal head, drawing S-58.14 (1992). The sign uses red super LED diodes on a black background and is housed in a standard pedestrian signal enclosure stacked vertically under the head. Both left arrow and right arrow versions are shown.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 118)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0118.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0118.webp" alt="S-58.14 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-58.14, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p118">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation, left version and right version side by side, not to scale: Each is a vertical stack of two pedestrian signal enclosures. The upper one is a regular pedestrian signal head showing a hand symbol and a walking person symbol. The lower one is the LED sign with the words USE TUNNEL in two lines and an arrow, a left-pointing arrow before USE on the left version and a right-pointing arrow after USE on the right version. The bracket label above says REGULAR PEDESTRIAN SIGNAL HEADS and the bracket below says LED SIGNS.</p>
<p>Dimensions: each enclosure is 18-1/2&quot; wide and 18-3/4&quot; high (both the regular head and the LED sign). The gap between the two enclosures is labeled SEE NOTE 4.</p>
<p>Title block: title &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; Electric Sign, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, S. E. Rowe, General Manager, approved 3-18-92, Drawing No. S-58.14. Drawn LAR 03-05-92, checked KHC 03-11-92, supervised ERA 03-11-92, reviewed JEF 03-18-92. Printed page number 111. A footnote on the sheet reads * MEETING LADOT SPECIFICATIONS, tied to the asterisk in note 3.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. The &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; sign shall be internally illuminated by super LED diodes. The diodes shall emit red colored light. The sign background shall be black.</p>
<p>2. The sign message &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; shall be 4&quot; in height with brush stroke width of 1/2&quot;. The arrow shall be 6-1/2&quot; long with brush stroke width of 1&quot;.</p>
<p>3. The sign housing shall consist of two (2) standard* pedestrian signal enclosures connected to one another in a vertical manner.</p>
<p>4. Minimum space shall be provided to allow free movement of door openings.</p>
<p>5. The &quot;USE TUNNEL&quot; electric sign shall be displayed only with the hand symbol of the pedestrian signal head.</p>
<p>* Meeting LADOT specifications.</p></details>
