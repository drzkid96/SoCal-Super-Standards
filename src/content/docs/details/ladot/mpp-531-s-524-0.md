## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 4 (page 178)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0178.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0178.webp" alt="S-524.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-524.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/13-drawings-s-524-0-to-s-527-0/#p178">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">This drawing shows options with both mountable and painted islands on the approaches; however, the actual design should use consistent treatments on each approach. Mountable islands are preferred; painted islands may be used when islands are less than 50 square feet (SF) in size. If raised center island is used, roundabout directional arrow signs (R6-4) should be included in the center island.</li>
<li value="2">All signs and pavement markings shown in California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (CA MUTCD), current edition shall be implemented as specified.</li>
<li value="3">Signs shown for only one approach, but should be provided consistently on each approach.</li>
<li value="4">For diameter dimensions of center island, see sheet 4 of 4.</li>
<li value="5"><strong>Install new curb ramps and crosswalks as shown.</strong> Diagonal ramps may not be used. Existing ramps within the circulating lane must be removed.</li>
<li value="6">Crosswalks may be supplemented with pedestrian activated rectangular rapid flashing beacons (RRFBs).</li>
<li value="7">See City of Los Angeles Supplemental Street Design Guide Detail 710 for mini-roundabout details.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Non-walkable area. 4&quot; solid yellow edge line, 1&#x27; off mountable center island. Center island mountable or painted, see Note 1 and see sheet 2. Solid white edge line. Mountable splitter island. Solid white arrow symbols (optional for raised islands, required for painted islands). Dashed white entrance line, 12&quot; wide, 2&#x27; long at 2&#x27; gap. Optional splitter island formed by two sets of double yellow lines, see Note 1 on sheet 2. 4&quot; solid yellow edge line, 1&#x27; off mountable splitter island. Crosswalk markings, see Note 6. 20&#x27; (TYP). Red curb, see DOT Std Plan S-481.</div>
</details>

### Sheet 2 of 4 (page 179)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0179.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0179.webp" alt="S-524.0 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-524.0, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/13-drawings-s-524-0-to-s-527-0/#p179">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Painted splitter islands may be used when site geometry leads to islands less than 50 SF in size.</li>
<li value="2">Use dimension schedule on sheet 4 of 4 as a design guide. Final dimensions to be determined by the engineer.</li>
<li value="3">Where crossing streets are not the same width, consider curb extensions on wider road to create consistent approach widths.</li>
<li value="4">Type D pavement markers placed on the yellow centerline are required for painted center islands and splitter islands and recommended for raised splitter and center islands. Use 12 for less than or equal to 15&#x27; diameter (&lt;= 15&#x27; DIA.). Use 16 for greater than 15&#x27; diameter and less than 20&#x27; diameter (&gt; 15&#x27; DIA. &lt; 20&#x27; DIA.). Use 20 for greater than or equal to 20&#x27; diameter (&gt;= 20&#x27; DIA.).</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Island dimensions when painted. Non-walkable area. Width varies by site conditions, see Note 3. Curb ramp type varies. Splitter island. 11&#x27; - 14&#x27;. 15&#x27; desirable. 10&#x27; (typ). 20&#x27;. Center island. Raised pavement markers, Type D (see Note 4).</div>
</details>

Sign B: 30&quot; x 30&quot; black on yellow, placed 75&#x27; to 100&#x27; back from traffic circle on each approach (optional).</p>
