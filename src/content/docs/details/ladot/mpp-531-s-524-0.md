<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Layouts for mini-roundabouts and neighborhood traffic circles on local streets, with a center island geometry schedule. Mini-roundabouts show mountable or painted splitter and center islands, entrance and edge lines, signs such as <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-7p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-7P.svg">W16-7P</a>, and crosswalks about 20 ft back. Neighborhood traffic circles use stop control on a center island with object markers. Center island diameter ranges from 18 ft to 38 ft by street width (28 ft to 40 ft) and curb return radius (15, 20 or 25 ft), and the opening width is 16 ft to 20 ft depending on offset.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. This drawing shows options with both mountable and painted islands on the approaches; however, the actual design should use consistent treatments on each approach. Mountable islands are preferred; painted islands may be used when islands are less than 50 square feet (SF) in size. If raised center island is used, roundabout directional arrow signs (R6-4) should be included in the center island.</p>
<p>2. All signs and pavement markings shown in California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (CA MUTCD), current edition shall be implemented as specified.</p>
<p>3. Signs shown for only one approach, but should be provided consistently on each approach.</p>
<p>4. For diameter dimensions of center island, see sheet 4 of 4.</p>
<p>5. Install new curb ramps and crosswalks as shown. Diagonal ramps may not be used. Existing ramps within the circulating lane must be removed.</p>
<p>6. Crosswalks may be supplemented with pedestrian activated rectangular rapid flashing beacons (RRFBs).</p>
<p>7. See City of Los Angeles Supplemental Street Design Guide Detail 710 for mini-roundabout details.</p>
<p>Callouts: Non-walkable area. 4&quot; solid yellow edge line, 1&#x27; off mountable center island. Center island mountable or painted, see Note 1 and see sheet 2. Solid white edge line. Mountable splitter island. Solid white arrow symbols (optional for raised islands, required for painted islands). Dashed white entrance line, 12&quot; wide, 2&#x27; long at 2&#x27; gap. Optional splitter island formed by two sets of double yellow lines, see Note 1 on sheet 2. 4&quot; solid yellow edge line, 1&#x27; off mountable splitter island. Crosswalk markings, see Note 6. 20&#x27; (TYP). Red curb, see DOT Std Plan S-481.</p></details>
