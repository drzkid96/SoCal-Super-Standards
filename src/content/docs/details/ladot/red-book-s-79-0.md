<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Details for connecting a traffic signal to a Dept. of Water and Power electrical service, shown for overhead service (S-79.8), underground service (S-79.9) and service grounding with PVC or galvanized conduit (S-79.9B). Shows the service pole, a PB-3 service pullbox 5 to 15 feet from the pole, 2 inch conduits, wire sizes, a 60 amp in-line fuse and the ground rod and bonding. DWP installs the #2 aluminum service wires and the contractor installs a continuous dedicated #6 green ground to the controller cabinet.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 3 (document page 93)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0093.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0093.webp" alt="S-79.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p93">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Wiring diagram: Across the top is a Pullbox Cover labeled &#x27;Service T.S. &amp; S.L.&#x27; (traffic signal and street lighting). Below it, three &#x27;Alum. wires by DWP&#x27; (3 #2) drop from the service pole, the center one marked Neutral. At the end of each, 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP are spliced. Each of the two outer legs splits into two copper wires, each with a 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals, giving #6 traffic signal service wires, and a #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified) on the right leg. The neutral leg is bonded to the ground rod (Bond neutral to ground rod) and continues as the neutral service wire. 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (Bureau of Street Lighting, see Street Lighting Plan) run from the street lighting side of the fuses.</p>
<p>Section: A service pole stands at the left with a 2&quot; conduit running 10&#x27;-0&quot; down its face, then bending at the base and running underground toward the pullbox. The conduit run from pole to pullbox is 5&#x27;-0&quot; to 15&#x27;-0&quot;. The Service Pullbox PB-3 is set flush with the grade, 14&quot; MIN. deep. Inside it a bonding wire joins the conduit bushings, a ground rod is installed below, one Traffic Signal service conduit (2&quot;) enters and one street lighting service conduit enters when specified on the Bureau of Street Lighting plan.</p>
<p>Title block: Over Head Service, Dept. of Water &amp; Power, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-79.8. Drawn MT 9-14-11, checked RSM 9-14-11. Approved 10-27-11, Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Printed page number 86.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Department of Water and Power (DWP) to install 3 #2 stranded aluminum wires from service pole to pullbox. At the terminus of each #2 aluminum wire the Department of Water and Power to splice 2 #6 stranded copper wires, (12&quot; long) using compression type connectors. The exposed ends of each copper wire shall be taped when not in use.</p>
<p>2. Install continuous and dedicated #6 green service ground conductor to controller cabinet.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction, Sec. 307-2.8.</p>
<p>4. See Standard Drawing S-79.9B for grounding details.</p>
<p>Callouts: Pullbox cover Service T.S. &amp; S.L. 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP. 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals. Service pole. #6 traffic signal service wires. 2&quot; conduit. 3 #2 alum. wires by DWP. Neutral. Bond neutral to ground rod. #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified). 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (See Street Lighting Plan). Service pullbox PB-3. Bonding wire. Install ground rod. One street lighting service conduit when specified on Bureau of Street Lighting plan. One traffic signal service conduit (2&quot;).</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 3 (document page 96)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0096.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0096.webp" alt="S-79.0 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.0, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p96">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>PVC Conduit System (top diagram): A schematic of service grounding with a Service Pullbox at left, a Controller Pullbox in the middle and a Traffic Signal Cabinet at right. In the service pullbox, three utility conductors AC+, AC- and AC+ come in &#x27;From Utility&#x27; through bonded galvanized conduit to the service fuse. The service ground rod is 8FT x 1/2&quot;, and a #8 green bond wire connects the conduit bond to the ground rod (marked with an asterisk, with an NC (normally closed) point). Schedule 80 PVC carries a dedicated service of #6 black, #6 green and #6 white (Black, Green, White) plus a #8 green bond wire to the controller pullbox. In the controller pullbox the wires pass through in Schedule 80 PVC, with the #8 green bond wires joined by a compression type fitting between two PVC stubs. The wires continue in Schedule 80 PVC to the traffic signal cabinet, where black, green and white land on the cabinet service block (Ground, AC+, AC-). The #8 green bond wire goes to the cabinet ground rod (8FT x 1/2&quot;) and, through a compression type fitting, to the equipment ground bus.</p>
<p>Galvanized Conduit System (bottom diagram): Same layout, but the conduits between pullboxes are bonded galvanized conduit instead of PVC, with a callout &#x27;Use a bushing on all metal conduit&#x27; and &#x27;Bonded Galvanized conduit&#x27; at the pullbox ends. The #8 green bond wire connects to the conduit bonding at each pullbox. The controller pullbox has a compression type fitting joining the bond wire to the two PVC stubs, and the cabinet ground rod arrangement is the same as in the PVC system.</p>
<p>Title block: Service Grounding Conduit System, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-79.9B. Drawn MT 8-17-11, checked RL 8-17-11, Pr. T.E. SS 9-9-11. Approved September 9, 2011, signed for Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Printed page number 89.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>* Note (both diagrams): Use a ground rod wire clamp for each wire (Acorn).</p>
<p>PVC Conduit System callouts: Service pullbox. Service fuse. Bonded galvanized conduit. NC. From utility AC+, AC-, AC+. #8 green bond wire. Schedule 80 PVC. Service ground rod: 8FT x 1/2&quot;. Dedicated service #6 black, #6 green, #6 white. Black, Green, White. Controller pullbox. Compression type fitting. Traffic signal cabinet. Cabinet service block (Ground, AC+, AC-). Equipment ground bus. Cabinet ground rod: 8FT x 1/2&quot;.</p>
<p>Galvanized Conduit System callouts: Use a bushing on all metal conduit. Bonded galvanized conduit. Other callouts as in the PVC system.</p></details>
