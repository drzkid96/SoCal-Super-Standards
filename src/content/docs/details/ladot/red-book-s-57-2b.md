<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-57.2B shows a temporary signal on a Type 1 standard held by a 36 in. square steel platform and four 5 gallon concrete-filled cans chained around the pole. It gives the platform plan and underside layout, bolt circle, lip, notches and a weight block detail of at least 90 lbs. It requires leveling on slopes steeper than 1/2 in. per foot.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 46)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0046.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0046.webp" alt="S-57.2B sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-57.2B, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p46">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Platform, top view detail (not to scale): a 36 in. x 36 in. square steel platform in plan. Four circles mark the four 5 gallon cans filled with concrete, one near each corner. A Type 1 base sits at the center of the platform with 4 bolts, and a 3/8 in. diameter steel coil chain is run through each can and locked around the Type 1 standard.</p>
<p>Platform, bottom view detail (not to scale): 36 in. x 36 in. in plan, with steel angle brackets crossing the underside as a framed grid. 4 holes at 1-1/4 in. diameter are laid out on an 8-1/2 in. diameter bolt circle at the center. 1 in. x 1 in. notches for cable are cut at the perimeter lip near the lower right corner.</p>
<p>Elevation: a temporary signal on a Type 1 standard. A signal head with three 12 in. sections, a second three-section head on a mast arm, a street name sign, and a pedestrian signal head (hand and walking person) are mounted on the pole. At the bottom, two 5 gal. concrete blocks sit on the platform and are chained around the Type 1 standard. The base assembly is bolted to the platform, which has a 2 in. high lip. Callouts: 1 in. x 1 in. notch for cable, LADOT name on the platform. A note beside the plate says for slopes greater than 1/2 in. rise per foot, the temporary standard is to be set level through the use of grading or wood shims.</p>
<p>Weight block (not to scale): a 5 gal. can filled with concrete, 12 in. wide by 14 in. tall, with a 1/2 in. diameter steel rod loop at the top embedded 9 in. deep, the loop 4 in. wide and projecting 3 in. above the can. Minimum weight is 90 lbs. LADOT or contractor name is embossed or welded on at least two sides.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Temporary Signal for Type 1 Standard, sheet 1 of 1, drawing number S-57.2B, drawn 3-1-16, approved 4-11-16 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Page number 39.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Use 12 in. heads corresponding to permanent heads being replaced.</p>
<p>2. 36 in. square (SQ.) x 1/4 in. thick (THK.) steel platform, hot dip galvanized per ASTM 123.</p>
<p>3. Type 1 base with 4 1 in. diameter x 3-1/2 in. bolts and nuts with 2 washers for each bolt.</p>
<p>4. 3/8 in. diameter steel coil chain tied to each 5 gal. block and locked around the Type 1 standard.</p>
<p>5. 5 gal. can filled with concrete. Four cans are required, spaced as shown.</p>
<p>6. 2 in. high x 1/4 in. thick perimeter lip resting on ground.</p>
<p>7. 1-3/4 in. x 1-3/4 in. x 1/4 in. steel angle brackets welded to plate.</p>
<p>8. 4 holes at 1-1/4 in. diameter. 8-1/2 in. diameter bolt circle.</p>
<p>9. 1 in. x 1 in. notches for cable.</p>
<p>10. For slopes greater than 1/2 in. rise per foot, temporary standard to be set level through the use of grading or wood shims.</p>
<p>11. Weight block: 5 gal. can filled with concrete, minimum weight 90 lbs. 1/2 in. diameter steel rod embedded 9 in. deep. &#x27;LADOT&#x27; or contractor name embossed or welded on at least two sides.</p></details>
