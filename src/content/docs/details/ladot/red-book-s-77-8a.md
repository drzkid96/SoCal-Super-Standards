<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">The width dimension, &quot;A&quot;, should be 5&quot; typical (TYP.) and the corner radius dimension, &quot;R&quot;, should be 2&quot; TYP. The backplate shall have a nominal thickness of 1/8&quot;. All outside and inside edges shall be formed with a 1/2&quot; to 5/8&quot; flange (inside dimension), turned away from the front surface.</li>
<li value="2">For all manufactured signal heads, backplates shall be designed so as to show no background light between the backplate and the head. The fabrication shall be vacuum formed as one piece with a black lusterless, haircell finish on the front side. All surfaces shall be flat and straight without blisters, buckling or warping. Flanges shall be straight, uniform and have a consistent flange dimension throughout.</li>
<li value="3">The backplate material shall be compounded from virgin ABS containing 60% styrene, 20% rubber and 20% acrylic. It shall contain ultra violet inhibitors and stabilizers for protection against ultra violet degradation. The backplate must meet a falling dart impact test of 16 ft/lb.</li>
<li value="4">The internal shapes and dimensions shall match various manufactured signal heads.</li>
<li value="5">Retroreflective sheeting must be Type XI and comply with ASTM D4956. Retroreflective sheeting must have Class 1, 3, or 4 adhesive backing. The adhesive backing must be pressure sensitive and fungus resistant. Retroreflective sheeting must be applied at the fabrication plant under the retroreflective sheeting manufacturer&#x27;s instruction without appreciable stretching, tearing or other damage.</li>
</ol>
<p>Callout: 2-inch yellow retroreflective strip.</p>
</details>
