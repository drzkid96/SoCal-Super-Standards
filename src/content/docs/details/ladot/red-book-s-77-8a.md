<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-77.8A, dated October 20, 2025, shows the outlines of traffic signal backplates for 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 section heads and cluster heads. Each has a 5 inch border (A), 2 inch corner radius (R) and a 2 inch yellow retroreflective strip. Notes set the 1/8 inch ABS material, flange, finish, impact test and Type XI retroreflective sheeting requirements.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 112)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0112.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0112.webp" alt="S-77.8A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-77.8A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p112">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (front view) of the traffic signal backplate outlines, drawn as nine rounded rectangles each with a dashed inner rectangle (the signal head footprint) and dashed circles (the signal lenses). Each backplate has an inner line showing the flange and a 2-inch yellow retroreflective strip along the border. Dimension A TYP. is the border width on all sides and R TYP. is the corner radius.</p>
<p>Left column, top to bottom: a narrow vertical backplate for a 3-section head with small lenses, a vertical backplate for a 2-section head, a near-square backplate for a 1-section head, and a tall backplate for a 5-section head in which three smaller lenses are above two larger lenses (the plate outline steps wider at the larger lenses).</p>
<p>Middle column: a vertical backplate for a 3-section head with larger lenses, and a larger backplate with a 5-light cluster (one lens on top over two rows of two).</p>
<p>Right columns: a vertical backplate for a 4-section head, a vertical backplate for a 5-section head, and a squarer backplate for a 3-section cluster head with two lenses over one.</p>
<p>Dimensions are given only as letters: A is the width of the border (5&quot; typ. per note 1) and R is the corner radius (2&quot; typ. per note 1). The 2-inch yellow retroreflective strip is called out on the border of the backplates.</p>
<p>Title block: Backplates, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-77.8A. Drawn (DWN) SB 10-20-25, T. E. SB 10-20-25, Sr. T. E. JV 10-20-25, Pr. T. E. TF. Approved October 20, 2025 for Laura Rubio-Cornejo, General Manager.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. The width dimension, &quot;A&quot;, should be 5&quot; typical (TYP.) and the corner radius dimension, &quot;R&quot;, should be 2&quot; TYP. The backplate shall have a nominal thickness of 1/8&quot;. All outside and inside edges shall be formed with a 1/2&quot; to 5/8&quot; flange (inside dimension), turned away from the front surface.</p>
<p>2. For all manufactured signal heads, backplates shall be designed so as to show no background light between the backplate and the head. The fabrication shall be vacuum formed as one piece with a black lusterless, haircell finish on the front side. All surfaces shall be flat and straight without blisters, buckling or warping. Flanges shall be straight, uniform and have a consistent flange dimension throughout.</p>
<p>3. The backplate material shall be compounded from virgin ABS containing 60% styrene, 20% rubber and 20% acrylic. It shall contain ultra violet inhibitors and stabilizers for protection against ultra violet degradation. The backplate must meet a falling dart impact test of 16 ft/lb.</p>
<p>4. The internal shapes and dimensions shall match various manufactured signal heads.</p>
<p>5. Retroreflective sheeting must be Type XI and comply with ASTM D4956. Retroreflective sheeting must have Class 1, 3, or 4 adhesive backing. The adhesive backing must be pressure sensitive and fungus resistant. Retroreflective sheeting must be applied at the fabrication plant under the retroreflective sheeting manufacturer&#x27;s instruction without appreciable stretching, tearing or other damage.</p>
<p>Callout: 2-inch yellow retroreflective strip.</p></details>
