<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-52.7 showing the small LADOT Type F-7 foundation, an 18 in. square concrete footing 18 in. deep with a 3 in. minimum topping. It has four 1/2 in. x 10 1/2 in. x 1 1/2 in. anchor bolts on a 4 1/2 in. bolt circle, a bushing, a bonding strap or wire and a 1 in. nominal conduit.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 89)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0089.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0089.webp" alt="S-52.7 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.7, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p89">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Top view: An 18&quot; square footing with a 4 1/2&quot; bolt circle holding four anchor bolts on diagonals at 45 degrees. A dashed conduit stub runs from the center to the right. A 36&quot; dimension runs from the left side of the curb to the centerline.</p>
<p>Section: An 18&quot; wide concrete footing with a minimum 3&quot; topping, a bushing on the center conduit extending 1 3/8&quot; above the topping, a bonding strap or wire, and a curb and sidewalk at the left. Four anchor bolts 1/2&quot; x 10 1/2&quot; x 1 1/2&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers per bolt required) are cast in. Dimensions: 14&quot; MIN. from the top of the footing to the conduit stub and 18&quot; overall depth.</p>
<p>Title block: Foundation, Type F-7, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-52.7. Drawn MT 03-05-04, Pr. T.E. TLJ 02-09-05. Approved February 25, 2005, Wayne K. Tanda, General Manager. Printed page number 82.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Conduit shall be 1&quot; nominal size unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Topping shall be considered a part of the foundation.</p>
<p>Callouts: 4 1/2&quot; bolt circle. 18&quot; square. 36&quot;. Bushing. Bonding strap or wire. Minimum topping 3&quot;. 1 3/8&quot;. 14&quot; minimum. 18&quot;. Anchor bolts (4 required) 1/2&quot; x 10 1/2&quot; x 1 1/2&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers per bolt required).</p></details>
