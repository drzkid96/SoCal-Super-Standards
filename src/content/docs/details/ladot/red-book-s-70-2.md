<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.2 shows how to hook up one to four detector loops to a 2, 3 or 4 pair lead-in cable. Pairs are color coded blue/white, orange/white, green/white and brown/white, each loop uses one pair, and the drain wire is grounded at the controller cabinet. Notes cover cable construction, soldering and waterproofing, and LADOT specification 92-082-03.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 72)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0072.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0072.webp" alt="S-70.2 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.2, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p72">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Four wiring hookup diagrams for loop detector lead-in cable, each showing loops (numbered circles with three turns) at left, twisted lead-in pairs, a cable jacket and the color coded pairs at right. In every diagram a drain wire leaves the cable and is connected to earth ground at the controller cabinet.</p>
<p>One-loop hookup, 2 pair cable: loop 1 is connected by its twisted pair to the cable. At the far end the pairs are BLUE/WHITE (PAIR 1) and ORANGE/WHITE (PAIR 2). The loop uses one pair. Label: DO NOT CONNECT UNUSED PAIR. Drain wire connected to earth ground at controller cabinet.</p>
<p>Two-loop hookup, 2 pair cable: loops 1 and 2 each connect by a twisted pair to the cable. Pairs: BLUE/WHITE (PAIR 1) and ORANGE/WHITE (PAIR 2). Drain wire connected to earth ground at controller cabinet.</p>
<p>Three-loop hookup, 3 pair cable: loops 1, 2 and 3 each connect by a twisted pair. Pairs: BLUE/WHITE (PAIR 1), ORANGE/WHITE (PAIR 2), GREEN/WHITE (PAIR 3). Drain wire connected to earth ground at controller cabinet.</p>
<p>Four-loop hookup, 4 pair cable: loops 1 to 4 each connect by a twisted pair. Pairs: BLUE/WHITE (PAIR 1), ORANGE/WHITE (PAIR 2), GREEN/WHITE (PAIR 3), BROWN/WHITE (PAIR 4). Drain wire connected to earth ground at controller cabinet.</p>
<p>Title block: System Detector Loop Lead-In Cable (2,3,4 Pair), sheet 1/1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved February 25, 2005, Wayne K. Tanda, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.2.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. This standard drawing describes a two, three or four pair loop detector lead-in cable, individually shielded and jacketed and suitable for installation in a pavement sawcut, conduit, or direct burial.</p>
<p>2. The detector lead-in cable can be wired in either a single, double, triple or quadruple channel configuration.</p>
<p>3. Electrical connections shall be carefully soldered and waterproofed.</p>
<p>4. See LADOT material specification No. 92-082-03 or latest revision.</p>
<p>5. Loop numbers shown are typical.</p>
<p>6. System loops use a single pair for each loop.</p>
<p>Callouts: Do not connect unused pair. Drain wire connected to earth ground at controller cabinet.</p></details>
