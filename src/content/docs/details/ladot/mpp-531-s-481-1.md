</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. Implementation. This standard plan applies to installations in accordance with MPP Section 344 for Continental Crosswalks for new and reinstallations of marked crosswalks on signalized and stop-controlled approaches.</p>
<p>2. Crosswalk width. Typical crosswalk widths are 15 feet but may vary according to site conditions. Continental markings shall be installed within existing dimensions unless otherwise indicated on a signed plan.</p>
<p>3. Midblock crosswalks. This standard plan also applies at controlled midblock approaches in accordance with S-493.0 - Location of Limit Lines and Crosswalks.</p>
<p>4. Conflict between signed plan and standard plan. Should conflicts arise between this Standard Plan and a signed signal or geometric plan with their associated markings (e.g., &quot;cat tracks,&quot; extension of the DYCL, etc.), the signed plan shall take precedent.</p>
<p>5. Retrofitting and resurfacing. Refer to MPP Section 344 for retrofitting and resurfacing.</p>
<p>Detail text: Install 2&#x27; wide white or yellow markings along striping. Center the markings on DYCL and lane lines. Add markings according to chart below. Marking should be installed such that wheels straddle the crosswalk markings.</p></details>
