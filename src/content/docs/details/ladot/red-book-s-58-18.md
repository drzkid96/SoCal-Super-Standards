<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fiber optic lane usage control sign, drawing S-58.18, approved 11-03-00. It displays a red X or a green down arrow on a 24 inch by 24 inch face, with a 24 inch wide, 24 inch tall visor 17 to 12 inches deep. Notes give 1.5 inch strokes, 0.50 inch fiber spacing and conformance to specification 82-049-03.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 120)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0120.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0120.webp" alt="S-58.18 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-58.18, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p120">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face: A 24&quot; by 24&quot; square showing a red X formed by two crossing bars, with a dashed outline of the green down arrow behind it as the alternate message. The X is 15-3/8&quot; wide and 18&quot; tall, set 2-5/8&quot; below the top edge and 3-3/8&quot; above the bottom edge. The dashed down arrow is 17&quot; tall (Typ.).</p>
<p>Visor enclosure: An isometric view of a three-sided hood. The top is 24&quot; wide and 17&quot; deep, the sides taper to 12&quot; deep at the bottom, and the height is 24&quot;. Lower front corners are rounded with a 2&quot; Rad. callout.</p>
<p>Title block: Lane Usage Control Electric Sign, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, approved 11-03-00, Drawing No. S-58.18. Drawn LR/HM 8-08-00, checked ERA 8-08-00, reviewed GO 11-02-00. Printed page number 113.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: A: Red &quot;X&quot;, width of &quot;X&quot; 1.5&quot; stroke. B: Green arrow (down), width of arrow 1.5&quot; stroke.</p>
<p>2. The red &quot;X&quot; shall be formed with rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center). The green down arrow shall be formed with rows of fiber optic bundles, spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</p>
<p>3. Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernable and readable message.</p>
<p>4. Sign shall completely blank out when not energized.</p>
<p>5. Door frame, face plate, and interior of housing and visor shall be painted flat black, non-reflective finish.</p>
<p>6. Visor enclosure shall be fastened securely to the front frame of the sign.</p>
<p>7. Refer to LADOT Std. Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for mounting assembly.</p>
<p>8. Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No. 82-049-03.</p>
<p>9. For housing description, see LADOT Std. Dwg. No. S-58.21.</p></details>
