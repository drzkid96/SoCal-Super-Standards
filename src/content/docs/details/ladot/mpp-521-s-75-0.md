<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 5 of 6 of LADOT standard S-75.0 showing plan views of bus priority detection loops at signalized intersections. Three views are given: with an arterial cross street, with a minor cross street and on a divided street. Loops B1 to B4 are set 15 ft upstream of the bus zone or placed 3 ft from the ends of the intersection area, and each is wired to a PB3 pull box. The boxed callouts 1 to 5 refer to notes that are not printed on this sheet.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet: Three plan views stacked on one sheet, each showing a signalized intersection drawn with a rectangle containing a circled S, left turn arrows, crosswalk lines, lane lines and curb returns.</p>
<p>View 1, Bus Priority Detection with An Arterial Cross Street: A multilane street approaches the intersection from the left and also from the right. On the left approach, a long bus loop B2 (labeled BUS LOOP) runs across the through lane next to the bus zone (a thick black line at the curb marked BUS ZONE). The loop sits 15&#x27; upstream of the near end of the bus zone, and has a 3&#x27; dimension at each end to the curb face and the pavement edge. A lead-in runs from the loop to a PB3 pull box (black square) at the south curb, with an existing interconnect conduit (EXIST. I/C, dash-dot line) running along the south side behind the curb. Boxed callouts 1 and 4 point at the pull box. On the right approach, bus loop B1 is 15&#x27; from the bus zone (BUS ZONE shown at the south curb) with a 3&#x27; dimension at the top end. It connects to a PB3 at the north curb. A dash-dot conduit runs to a second PB3 at the curb return and continues to a PB3 at the far corner. Boxed callouts 1 and 3 are shown at these pull boxes.</p>
<p>View 2, Bus Priority Detection with A Minor Cross Street: The minor street crosses the major street at the center. Bus loop B1 is drawn as a long oval inside the intersection rectangle with a 3&#x27; dimension at each end from the edge of the intersection area. The detector lead-in is shown running to a signal pole (small rectangle) with a pair of loop symbols (callouts 1 and 5), with the word OR indicating two possible connection routes, one toward a pole on the left and another through a PB3 pull box on the right marked ALTERNATIVE CONNECTION SEE NOTE 5. SEE NOTE 4 and BUS LOOP are labeled at B1.</p>
<p>View 3, Bus Priority Detection on A Divided Street: A street with a raised median (stippled fill) and a left turn pocket on each side of the intersection. Along the left approach, bus loops B4 (upstream, 15&#x27; from the bus zone) and B3 (near the intersection) flank the BUS ZONE, each at a PB3 pull box, with a dash-dot conduit labeled with callout 3 between them. The loop spans the lane between the curb and the median with dimension 3&#x27; TYP. at each end. On the right approach, the mirror arrangement uses loops B2 (near the intersection) and B1 (upstream, 15&#x27; from the bus zone) with PB3 pull boxes, 3&#x27; TYP. dimensions and callouts 1, 2 and 3. An extra PB3 sits at the corner return with a dashed conduit.</p>
<p>Title block: Special Detector Applications, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, circled sheet number 5 over 6, Drawing No. S-75.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>No numbered notes are printed on this sheet. The boxed callouts 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 on the plan refer to notes shown on another sheet of S-75.0. Labels on the plan: BUS ZONE, BUS LOOP, B1, B2, B3, B4, PB3 (pull box), EXIST. I/C (existing interconnect), S (signalized intersection), OR, ALTERNATIVE CONNECTION SEE NOTE 5, SEE NOTE 4. Dimensions: 15&#x27; from the bus zone to each bus loop, 3&#x27; at the ends of the loops, 3&#x27; TYP. across the lane in the divided street view.</p></details>
