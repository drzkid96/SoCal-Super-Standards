## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (page 147)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0147.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0147.webp" alt="Detail 710 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide Detail 710, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/09-pages-145-162/#p147">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All splitter island corners use minimum 1 ft radius.</li>
<li value="2">Splitter island corner on approach use small radius (MIN 1 ft) to encourage entry deflection.</li>
<li value="3">Splitter island corner on exit use larger radius to minimize damage from impacts.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Mountable splitter islands are preferred as shown.</strong> Painted islands may be used when site conditions lead to splitter islands less than 50 SF in size. Coordinate with DOT.</li>
<li value="5">Install new curb ramps and crosswalks per Standard Plan S-442. Existing ramps within the circulating lane must be removed.</li>
<li value="6">PCC pavement within islands shall be distinct from the roadway and sidewalks. PCC application may use integral color, and surface texture may also be applied.</li>
<li value="7">If the splitter island is at least 6 feet wide, detectable warning shall be used within the refuge area. Detectable warning shall be 36 in deep when the splitter island is 8 ft or more in width. Between 6 ft and 8 ft, the detectable warning shall be 24 in deep.</li>
<li value="8">A non-walkable surface or curb should be installed at the corners to prevent pedestrians from crossing at the intersection.</li>
</ol>
</details>

<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>Center of island typically designed as high point.</strong> In certain locations a constant slope across island may be desirable. Final dimensions to be determined by Engineer.</li>
<li value="2">Mountable splitter islands are preferred, as shown. Painted islands may be used when site conditions lead to splitter islands less than 50 SF in size.</li>
<li value="3">PCC pavement within islands shall be distinct from the roadway and sidewalks. PCC application may use integral color, and surface texture may also be applied.</li>
</ol>
<p>Section A callout: A. For diameter dimensions of the center island, see Sheet 3. Section B callout: A. Width of island varies. See plan view dimensions.</p>
