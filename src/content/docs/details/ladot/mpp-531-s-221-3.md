<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 4 of LADOT standard S-221.3. A schematic plan of a street grid shows where street name signs (for example W Ave., C St., B St., X Ave., A St., Y Ave.) are posted at corners, including a curved street and a U-shaped street. The four notes require signs close to the near-right B.C. or the extension of the near left, ahead of obstructions, on existing posts or electroliers where practical, and treat parallel roadways with different names as separate streets.</p>

Vertical streets are labeled &quot;C&quot; STREET, &quot;B&quot; STREET and &quot;A&quot; STREET. Horizontal streets are labeled &quot;W&quot; AVENUE (across the top), &quot;X&quot; AVENUE (left center) and &quot;Y&quot; AVENUE (a U-shaped street that curves around a rounded end at the right). A curved street at the upper left bends from &quot;C&quot; Street toward B Street and X Avenue. Curbs are drawn as double lines with rounded corner returns.</p>
<p>Sign symbols: Each street name sign is a small rectangle with the street name in it, joined by a curved leader to a post symbol (a short bar with a dot) placed at a corner. Names shown include C St., W Ave., B St., X Ave., A St. and Y Ave. Posts are at the near-right corner of each approach. At some corners two names appear on the same post, for example W Ave. with A St., and B St. with A St. At the bottom a post carries B St. and A St. side by side, with the word OR beside an alternative post location below it.</p>
<p>Title block: Street Name Sign Posting, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, circled sheet 1 over 4, Drawing No. S-221.3. Approved January 21, 2005 for Wayne K. Tonda, General Manager. Drawn by M.T., Pr. T.E. TJ.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>Note:</h5>
<p>1. All signs shall be posted as close to the near-right B.C. or the extension of the near left as is practical.</p>
<p>2. Post sign in advance of obstructions where encountered.</p>
<p>3. Existing sign posts and/or electroliers shall be used to post street name signs wherever practical.</p>
<p>4. Two parallel roadways with different street names (for example, Huntington Drive North and Huntington Drive) shall be treated as two different streets for sign posting purposes.</p></details>
