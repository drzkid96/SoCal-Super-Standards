<h5 class="dwg-sub">Note</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All signs shall be posted as close to the near-right B.C. or the extension of the near left as is practical.</li>
<li value="2">Post sign in advance of obstructions where encountered.</li>
<li value="3">Existing sign posts and/or electroliers shall be used to post street name signs wherever practical.</li>
<li value="4">Two parallel roadways with different street names (for example, Huntington Drive North and Huntington Drive) shall be treated as two different streets for sign posting purposes.</li>
</ol>
</details>
