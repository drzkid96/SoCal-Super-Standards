<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">All standards shall be tapered steel posts with 0.1196&quot; wall thickness.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Max.</strong> taper of posts shall be 0.143&quot; per foot.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Pole; 3/4&quot; base plate; T (Wall thickness) = 0.1196&quot;; See Detail A; Detail B; 4.5&quot; O.D. at top; 2 1/4&quot; diameter hole (Drill 1 side only); Two 3/4&quot; diameter holes (Drill through); 3&quot; X 5&quot; Hand hole reinforced with 1/4&quot; X 2&quot; ring welded to outside of pole and 1/8&quot; cover plate; 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding welded to interior of pole shaft at 180 degrees from hand hole rim; 1/4&quot; plate with 2&quot; central hole; Foundation see S-52.1.</div>
