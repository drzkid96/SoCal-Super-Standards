### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 31)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0031.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0031.webp" alt="S-51.1.2A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-51.1.2A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/02-pages-17-32/#p31">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation: Typical mounting of a Type 1 standard, shown as one vertical elevation with a foundation section at the base. At the top is a signal head drawn as a rectangular case containing three circular lenses stacked vertically, labeled Typical 1-Way Head Mounting, mounted on top of the shaft through a flange collar with dashed hidden lines showing the mounting stub. Dashed outlines to the left of the shaft show a Typical Street Name Sign Installation (a long horizontal sign panel with a shorter bracket panel below it, both at the upper part of the shaft). Below the signal head a pedestrian signal head is mounted on the shaft, showing a raised hand symbol on the left half and a walking person symbol on the right half, labeled Typical Pedestrian Head mounting. The tapered pole is labeled Shaft-Type 1.</p>
<p>Foundation section at the base: the shaft stands on a base plate with anchor bolts set in a concrete foundation labeled Foundation, Type F-1. The foundation sits at the edge of a stepped concrete sidewalk and curb section to the left. A curved conduit elbows out of the foundation and exits to the right. A 36&quot; dimension runs horizontally from the shaft centerline to the sidewalk edge at the left. A 14&quot; Min. dimension runs vertically at the right from the top of the foundation surface to the conduit.</p>
<p>Title block: Typical Mounting of Type 1 Standard, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-51.1.2A. Drawn MT 3-1-16, Traffic Engineer JV 3-3-16, Senior Traffic Engineer MA 3-8-16, Principal Traffic Engineer VJ 4-11-16, approved 4-11-16 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Booklet page number 24.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Callouts on the sheet: Typical Street Name Sign Installation; Typical 1-Way Head Mounting; Typical Pedestrian Head mounting; Shaft-Type 1; Foundation, Type F-1. Dimensions: 36&quot; from shaft to sidewalk edge, 14&quot; Min. foundation or conduit depth. No numbered notes appear on the sheet.</p></details>
