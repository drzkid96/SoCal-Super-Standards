<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>* Adjust pushbutton mounting height to 42&quot; above the surface pedestrians are expected to access pushbutton from.</p>
<p>(Callouts on the drawing) Steel standards: No. 7 drill, 1/4&quot;-20 N.C. tap, 2 holes; 1 1/8&quot; drill. Concrete standards: standard inserts for concrete standards, tapped 1/4&quot;-20 N.C.; 5/8&quot; hole. Parallel to crosswalk for which intended. Pushbutton 3&#x27;-6&quot; typical*.</p>
</details>
