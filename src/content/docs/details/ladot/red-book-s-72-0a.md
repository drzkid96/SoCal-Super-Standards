<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-72.0A shows how a pedestrian pushbutton is installed on a signal standard. It gives the drilling pattern for steel and concrete standards (two tapped holes 6 in apart and a larger wire hole 9 1/4 in below the upper one), the typical corner arrangement with the button parallel to its crosswalk, and the 3 ft 6 in typical mounting height. The mounting height is to be adjusted to 42 in above the surface pedestrians use to reach the button.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 62)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0062.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0062.webp" alt="S-72.0A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-72.0A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p62">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Steel Standards drilling detail: a vertical elevation of a round steel pole (shown with a wavy break line at top and bottom) with a vertical centerline and three horizontal reference lines for holes on the centerline. The upper two holes are labeled No. 7 drill, 1/4&quot;-20 N.C. (national coarse) tap, 2 holes. The lowest hole is labeled 1 1/8&quot; drill. The two tapped holes are 6&quot; apart, and the overall distance from the upper tapped hole to the 1 1/8&quot; hole is 9 1/4&quot;.</p>
<p>Concrete Standards drilling detail: the same hole pattern on a concrete standard drawn as a fluted or ribbed vertical shaft. The upper two holes are labeled Standard inserts for concrete standards, tapped 1/4&quot;-20 N.C. They are 6&quot; apart. The lowest hole is labeled 5/8&quot; hole. The overall distance from the upper insert to the 5/8&quot; hole is 9 1/4&quot;. Long dash-dot lines run from the hole lines to the installation elevation at right.</p>
<p>Typical arrangement (plan): a street corner with a curb return (curved curb) and the crosswalk lines. Two small pushbutton symbols are placed at the corner, one on each crosswalk approach. Leader: Parallel to crosswalk for which intended. A second leader points to the curb (Curb).</p>
<p>Installation elevation: a vertical pole with a rectangular pushbutton unit labeled Pushbutton mounted on it, with the round button at the lower end on the pole centerline. The pushbutton is mounted 3&#x27;-6&quot; Typical, marked with an asterisk, measured from the ground line at the pole base up to the button centerline. The pole sits on a stepped base at the ground line.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>* Adjust pushbutton mounting height to 42&quot; above the surface pedestrians are expected to access pushbutton from.</p>
<p>(Callouts on the drawing) Steel standards: No. 7 drill, 1/4&quot;-20 N.C. tap, 2 holes; 1 1/8&quot; drill. Concrete standards: standard inserts for concrete standards, tapped 1/4&quot;-20 N.C.; 5/8&quot; hole. Parallel to crosswalk for which intended. Pushbutton 3&#x27;-6&quot; typical*.</p></details>
