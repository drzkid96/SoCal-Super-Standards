<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Covers signing and markings for marked crosswalks at uncontrolled approaches, including crosswalk sign assemblies with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> and W16-7p or W16-9p plates, advance warning signs, optional <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> yield-here-to-pedestrians signs with yield lines, PED XING or SCHOOL XING pavement markings, and optional RPMs. Sheets 2 and 3 give the red curb length for pedestrian sight distance as A = 4B/(W-7), or B/(W-10) with a curb extension, with tables by speed and curb lane width and a 50' minimum on arterial approaches. Sheet 4 shows centerline widening at intersections.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Crosswalk sign assembly, advance signing and markings</p>
<p>Crosswalk sign assembly: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> with W16-7p (arrow) or W16-9p (AHEAD) plates, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/s1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/S1-1.svg">S1-1</a> school signs, and School Crosswalk Warning Assembly B and D (CA code).</p>
<p>Advance warning sign (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> or Assembly D (CA)) placed 20&#x27; to 50&#x27; per CA MUTCD, with dimension C from Table 2C-4 and Part 2. ** Field installation of signs or markings may exceed the figures in the table by up to 100 feet per work order or plan to adjust to field conditions.</p>
<p>Optional <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5a.svg">R1-5a</a> MOD (YIELD HERE TO PEDESTRIANS) signs and optional yield line (see Note 7 and Fig 3B-17(CA), CA MUTCD). Optional RPMs (see Note 6). Pavement markings &quot;PED XING&quot; or &quot;SLOW SCHOOL XING&quot; or &quot;SCHOOL XING&quot;. Dimensions shown: 8&#x27;, 32&#x27;, 8&#x27;.</p>
<p>Install markings along striping and space at 4.5&#x27;, 5&#x27;, 5.5&#x27; and 6&#x27; center-to-center for 9&#x27;, 10&#x27;, 11&#x27; and 12&#x27; lanes, respectively. 2&#x27; white or yellow, centered on DYCL and lane lines.</p>
<p>Table, posted speed (mph): C (ft)</p>
<p>25: 100</p>
<p>30: 100</p>
<p>35: 125</p>
<p>40: 175</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Implementation. This standard plan applies to new installations and reinstallations of marked crosswalks and to marked crosswalks determined to be retained as a result of a study on multi-lane facilities. Use of signs shall be in coordination with all other controls shown on this plan.</p>
<p>2. Sign posting at crosswalk. Crosswalk Sign Assembly signs shall be posted and should be positioned within ten feet of the crosswalk. These signs generally shall be posted back-to-back so that one sign is on the right and the other on the left. If a raised median with a width of 6&#x27; or greater exists, the left sign shall be in the median. These signs should be 36&quot; x 36&quot;. The W16-9p or W16-7p plates shall be 36&quot; wide. All signs shall be fluorescent yellow-green.</p>
<p>3. Advance sign posting. Advance Pedestrian Crossing <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> signs, or Assembly D (CA) signs shall be posted as shown and shall be fluorescent yellow-green. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> signs shall be posted and positioned within ten feet of the yield limit line. These locations may be adjusted to accommodate field conditions. All signs should be 36&quot; x 36&quot;.</p>
<p>4. Pavement markings. &quot;PED XING&quot; or &quot;SCHOOL XING&quot; pavement markings shall be installed as shown except the yellow &quot;SLOW SCHOOL XING&quot; pavement markings shall be installed in advance of uncontrolled yellow school crosswalks. If the crosswalk spacing is less than 500 feet, only one set of pavement markings is required in advance of the first crosswalk.</p>
<p>5. Midblock crosswalks. This standard plan also applies at uncontrolled approaches to midblock crosswalks.</p>
<p>6. Raised pavement markings (RPM). Install RPMs one each side of crosswalk bar facing approach lanes. Two-way clear (white) or two-way amber (yellow) per Caltrans standards and specifications may be installed corresponding to the crosswalk color.</p>
<p>7. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> (YIELD HERE TO PEDESTRIANS) signs and yield lines. For uncontrolled locations with more than one lane in each direction of travel, advance yield lines may be used. Yield lines shall be supplemented with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> signs.</p></details>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Red curb for sight distance to pedestrian crossings</p>
<p>A = 4B / (W-7) = curb stopping prohibition necessary for a motorist to see a pedestrian at a point 4&#x27; into the roadway. LADOT practice is to install 50 feet of red curb on arterial approaches to side streets, measured from the side street curb line prolongation (per MPP Sec. 342). Install this minimum dimension whenever the calculated A distance results in a lesser dimension. (See Note 1.)</p>
<p>B = safe stopping distance = 1.47 VT + 1.075 V^2/a</p>
<p>V = posted speed + 5 mph (chart listings calculated on posted speed + 5 mph)</p>
<p>T = perception-reaction time of 1.5 seconds (driver previously alerted with advance sign and pavement markings)</p>
<p>a = deceleration rate = 11.2 ft/s^2 (AASHTO Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets, Stopping Sight Distance)</p>
<p>W = curb lane width (driver is assumed to be 3&#x27; from lane line). Pedestrian is 4&#x27; into the roadway, parked vehicle width 8&#x27;.</p>
<p>Table, posted speed (mph): B (ft)</p>
<p>25: 153</p>
<p>30: 195</p>
<p>35: 242</p>
<p>40: 294</p>
<p>Calculated A distances (ft), by curb lane width W, at posted speed 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 mph</p>
<h5>W = 18&#x27;: 56 / 71 / 88 / 107</h5>
<h5>W = 19&#x27;: 51 / 65 / 81 / 98</h5>
<h5>W = 20&#x27;: 47 / 60 / 74 / 90</h5>
<h5>W = 21&#x27;: 44 / 56 / 69 / 84</h5>

<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. One-way streets. Because one-way streets place drivers closer to the curb on the left side of the roadway, use A = 4B/(W-11) for left side pedestrian approach. This distance is not shown above.</p>
<p>2. Signs. See Sheet 1 for appropriate signs.</p></details>
