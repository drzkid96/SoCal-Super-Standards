<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-456.0, approved March 13, 2006, showing typical placement of pavement turn arrows at an intersection approach. Left and right turn arrows are placed at the beginning of each turn lane, with a second arrow at L/2 only if the turn lane length L1 or L2 is 250 feet or more.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: a typical four-leg intersection with curb returns at all four corners, a central rectangle for the intersection, and lane lines on each approach. The sheet focuses on the lower approach, which has a left-turn lane on the left side and right-turn lane on the right side, separated from through lanes by dashed lane lines, with a solid line at the left edge of the lanes. A double yellow centerline curves into the left-turn pocket. In the left turn pocket, two left-turn arrows are drawn: one at the beginning of the lane at the bottom of the length L1, and one at the middle at L1/2 measured from the stop line. In the right turn lane, two right-turn arrows are drawn: one at the beginning of the lane at length L2 and one at L2/2. Dimension L1 is the full length of the left-turn lane and L1/2 is half of it. Dimension L2 is the full length of the right-turn lane and L2/2 is half of it. Callout in pink highlight with leader lines to both arrows at the half-length position: Install only if L1 or L2 &gt;= 250&#x27;. A second pink callout with leader lines to the arrows at the lane start: Install at beginning of lane.</p>
<p>Title block: approved March 13, 2006, signed for Wayne K. Tanda, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Typical Placement of Pavement Turn Arrows, sheet 1 of 1, drawing number S-456.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Install only if L1 or L2 is 250 feet or greater (greater than or equal to 250&#x27;). Install at beginning of lane.</p></details>
