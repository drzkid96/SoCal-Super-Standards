<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fabrication layout for an 18 in. x 18 in. LADOT tow-away sign reading NO STOPPING ANY TIME with a double arrow and the impound recovery line 3-1-1. Red text, arrow and border on a white field with the tow pictograph in a red band. Lettering sizes, series and vertical spacings are dimensioned, and five notes cover fabrication, arrow options, bottom markings, colors and material.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face elevation: A single red-bordered panel 18&quot; wide across the top and 18&quot; high at left, with a 1 1/2&quot; radius at the corners (callout at top right). Background is white, with red text, arrow and border.</p>
<p>Legend: a red top band with a white tow-truck-towing-car pictograph, 4&quot; tall. Below it a red block at left with the white word NO, next to STOPPING in red on white. The red portion is dimensioned 8 1/4&quot; from the top edge to the bottom of the NO block. The NO block is labeled 3.5&quot; Series B at left and STOPPING is labeled 3&quot; Series B at right, as drawn. Then ANY TIME in red, 3&quot; Series C. Below it a red double-headed arrow, 1 1/2&quot; in height and 8&quot; long (8&quot; dimension below the sign), centered. Then TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1 in 1/2&quot; Series C.</p>
<p>Vertical dimensions down the left side: 18&quot; overall, 8 1/4&quot; red portion, 4&quot; graphic, 1/2&quot; below the NO and STOPPING lettering, 1 1/2&quot; from the bottom of the red block to the top of ANY TIME, then the ANY TIME line height (unlabeled), 1&quot; from ANY TIME to the arrow, 1 1/2&quot; arrow height, 1&quot; from the arrow to the TO RECOVER line and 1 3/4&quot; from the TO RECOVER line to the bottom edge. A 3/4&quot; dimension on the right marks the space between the arrow and the TO RECOVER line. Lettering callouts on the right, top to bottom: 3&quot; Series B, 3&quot; Series C, 1/2&quot; Series C.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved July 21, 2010, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Tow Away No Stopping Any Time Sign. Sheet 1 of 1. T.E. TC, drawn MT 7-15-10. Drawing No. S-522.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by the Los Angeles Department of Transportation (LADOT).</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 18&quot; X 18&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1 1/2&quot; in height and can be Double, Left or Right. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication, e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. All text, arrow and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 Aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
