<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fabrication layout for an 18 in. x 18 in. LADOT tow-away sign reading NO STOPPING ANY TIME with a double arrow and the impound recovery line 3-1-1. Red text, arrow and border on a white field with the tow pictograph in a red band. Lettering sizes, series and vertical spacings are dimensioned, and five notes cover fabrication, arrow options, bottom markings, colors and material.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation of a single-panel sign 18&quot; wide by 18&quot; high, with a 1-1/2&quot; radius at the corners and a red border. The legend is centered.</p>
<p>Top band: red field with the white tow-truck-towing-car pictograph, 4&quot; high. Below it, a red block with the white word NO beside STOPPING in red on white, 3&quot; Series B (callout 3.5&quot; Series B at the NO), within an 8-1/4&quot; dimension from the top edge to the lower edge of that line, followed by a 1/2&quot; dimension.</p>
<p>Next, ANY TIME in red, 3&quot; Series C, with 1-1/2&quot; and 1&quot; spacing dimensions around it. Below is a red double-headed arrow, 1-1/2&quot; in height and 8&quot; long (8&quot; dimension shown below the sign), with a 3/4&quot; spacing dimension below it. Then TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1 in 1/2&quot; Series C, with 1&quot; above and 1-3/4&quot; below it to the bottom edge.</p>
<p>Overall dimensions: 18&quot; across the top, 18&quot; vertical at left, 1-1/2&quot; radius callout at top right.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Tow Away No Stopping Any Time Sign, sheet 1 of 1, drawing S-522.0, approved July 21, 2010 for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Drawn MT 7-15-10.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 18&quot; x 18&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1-1/2&quot; in height and can be Double, Left or Right. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. All text, arrow and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
