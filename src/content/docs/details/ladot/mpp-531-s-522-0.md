## Sheet

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0173.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0173.webp" alt="S-522.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-522.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/12-drawings-s-512-to-s-523-0/#p173">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by the Los Angeles Department of Transportation (LADOT).</li>
<li value="2">Sign size is 18&quot; X 18&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1 1/2&quot; in height and can be Double, Left or Right. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</li>
<li value="3">All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication, e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</li>
<li value="4"><strong>The background color is white.</strong> All text, arrow and border are red.</li>
<li value="5">Sign shall be made of .080 Aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</li>
</ol>
</details>
