<p class="dwg-sum">Layout of on-street parallel parking stalls without a bike lane and with a bike lane, with the direction of travel shown. Stalls are 19 ft long, and 18 ft may be used for the first and last stall in a block. T-shaped and L-shaped 4 inch stall markings are used, the parking meter sits 20 inches from the face of curb and 3 ft from the stall line, and a red curb section 8 ft long ends the stall run. With a bike lane, a 6 inch white bike lane line is shown beside the parking lane.</p>
