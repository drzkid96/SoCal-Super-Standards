<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT standard S-404.1 showing on-street parallel parking stall markings without a bike lane and with a bike lane, drawings not to scale. Stalls are 19 ft long (18 ft may be used for the first and last stall in a block) and are marked with 4 inch T and L markings, a parking meter 20 inches from the face of curb and 3 ft from the stall line, and an 8 ft red curb section. With a bike lane the stall line is 7 ft to 8 ft from the curb and a 6 inch white bike lane line and BIKE LANE legend are shown.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>View 1, WITHOUT BIKE LANE (drawing not to scale): A plan of the curb edge with the direction of vehicular travel arrow pointing right. The face of curb runs horizontally. A line of stall markings sits 7&#x27;-6&quot; out from the face of curb. At the left an L marking begins the run. A T marking sits 19&#x27; from the L marking, with arms 1&#x27;-6&quot; to each side of the stem and a stem length of 1&#x27;-6&quot;. The overall stall length is 19&#x27;*, and a note says 18&#x27; may be used on the first and last stall in a block. At the right, two L markings 8&#x27; apart end the stall run, with a section of RED CURB 8&#x27; long at the curb between them. All marking widths are 4&quot; (TYP.). A parking meter is shown 20&quot; from the face of curb (behind the curb in the sidewalk) and 3&#x27; from the stall line at the T.</p>
<p>View 2, WITH BIKE LANE (drawing not to scale): The same layout with the stall markings drawn as T shapes and L shapes with a 1&#x27;-4&quot; stem. The stall line is 7&#x27; TO 8&#x27; from the face of curb. The 19&#x27;* stall length, 4&quot; (TYP.) markings, 8&#x27; red curb section and the meter at 20&quot; from curb and 3&#x27; from the stall line are repeated. Beyond the stall line, a 6&quot; white bike lane line is drawn with BIKE LANE pavement legend and a direction arrow in the bike lane, and the direction of vehicular travel arrow points right.</p>
