<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Specification for the blue and white sign identifying a Council District field office. The sign is 36" high by 60" wide with a blue reflectorized background, white letters and border, and 2" corner radius. It is mounted on two heavy duty posts, not on signal or street light poles, at a 10 foot clearance height.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Sign legend: councilmember name and district, then FIELD OFFICE (Series C lettering, with 4&quot; and 10&quot; spaces between lines). Sign is 36&quot; high x 60&quot; wide with a 2&quot; radius.</p>
<p>Sign size shall be 36&quot; vertically and 60&quot; horizontally. Background color shall be blue and reflectorized. Letters and border are white. Anti-graffiti film shall be used. Material shall be 0.08 inch minimum thickness aluminum stock. Corners of sign shall be 2 inch radius.</p>
<p>Installation</p>
<p>Signs shall be installed on two heavy duty sign posts. Signs may not be installed on traffic signal poles, or on street lighting poles. Signs should be installed at a clearance height of 10 feet so they will be less prone to vandalism.</p>
<p>Authority</p>
<p>District Office is responsible for determining the exact name and spelling on the sign. Fabrication and installation work orders should be initiated by District Office. Field Operations should install the signs on or before the effective date the new Councilmember assumes office.</p></details>
