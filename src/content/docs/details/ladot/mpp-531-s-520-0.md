<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fabrication layout for a 36 in. x 60 in. blue reflectorized Council District Field Office sign with a 12 in. City Seal, the Councilmember's name, title and district, and the words FIELD OFFICE. Text gives size, material, installation on two heavy duty posts at 10 ft clearance, and who is responsible for name, fabrication and installation.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign elevation: A 60&quot; wide by 36&quot; tall rectangular sign (60&quot; dimensioned below the sign, 36&quot; dimensioned on the right side) with 2&quot; radius corners (callout 2&quot; Radius at the upper right). A border 1/4&quot; wide is set 1/2&quot; from the edge of the sign (callout 1/4&quot; Border 1/2&quot; from edge at upper left). Letters and border are white on a blue reflectorized background.</p>
<p>Legend, stacked and centered: a 12&quot; City Seal (City of Los Angeles seal) centered on the top edge of the border, overlapping it. Below it the example name John Ferraro in 6&quot; Series C. Below that Councilmember, 4th District in 3&quot; Series C, with the superscript th and the &#x27;th&#x27; in 2&quot; Series C (right-hand callouts list 3&quot; Series C and 2&quot; Series C together). Below that FIELD OFFICE in 4&quot; Series C.</p>
<p>Vertical spacing, right side, top to bottom within the 36&quot; height: 10&quot; space, 6&quot; Series C, 4&quot; space, 3&quot; Series C and 2&quot; Series C, 4&quot; space, 4&quot; Series C, 5&quot; space.</p>
<p>Text sections below the drawing are titled Sign, Installation and Authority (transcribed in the notes).</p>
<p>Title block: Approved February 9, 2010, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Council District Field Office Sign. Sheet 1 of 1. Sr. T.E. JW, Pr. T.E. SS, T.E. TC (handwritten initials), drawn MT 2-3-10. Drawing No. S-520.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Sign: Size shall be 36&quot; vertically and 60&quot; horizontally. Background color shall be blue and reflectorized. Letters and border are white. Anti graffiti film shall be used. Material shall be 0.08 inch minimum thickness aluminum stock. Corners of sign shall be 2 inch radius.</p>
<p>Installation: Signs shall be installed on two heavy duty sign posts. Signs may not be installed on traffic signal poles, or on street lighting poles. Signs should be installed at a clearance height of 10 feet so they will be less prone to vandalism.</p>
<p>Authority: District Office is responsible for determining the exact name and spelling on the sign. Fabrication and installation work orders should be initiated by District Office. Field Operations should install the signs on or before the effective date the new Councilmember assumes office.</p>
<p>Drawing callouts: 1/4&quot; border 1/2&quot; from edge. 12&quot; City Seal. 2&quot; radius.</p></details>
