Crossing islands must be at least 6 ft wide to count as a pedestrian refuge. Uncontrolled crossings follow LADOT Standard Drawing S-481.0, and RRFBs or the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-6.svg">R1-6</a> in-street sign may supplement multi-lane crossings. <p>5.5.3 Pavement Markings and Signs</p>
<p>At uncontrolled crossings, the use of pavement markings and signs should follow LADOT Standard Drawing S-481.0. At multi-lane crossings, a Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacon (RRFB) or the In-Street Pedestrian Crossing sign (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-6.svg">R1-6</a>) may be used as supplemental treatments (in a gateway configuration at the crossing) to increase motorist yielding rates. Advanced yield lines and additional traffic control treatments should be considered, per LADOT Marked Uncontrolled Crosswalk Guidelines, where crossing islands are provided.</p>
<p>If Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacons (RRFBs) are used, they shall follow LADOT guidelines for installation and operation and allow actuation from within the refuge island. This may require the installation of 2 additional pedestrian push buttons at locations where only 2 RRFBs are provided.</p>
