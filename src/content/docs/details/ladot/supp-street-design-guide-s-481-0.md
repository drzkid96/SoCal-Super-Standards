## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 4 (page 150)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0150.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0150.webp" alt="S-481.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-481.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/09-pages-145-162/#p150">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>Implementation.</strong> This standard plan applies to new installations and reinstallations of marked crosswalks and to marked crosswalks determined to be retained as a result of a study on multi-lane facilities. Use of signs shall be in coordination with all other controls shown on this plan.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Sign posting at crosswalk.</strong> Crosswalk Sign Assembly signs shall be posted and should be positioned within ten feet of the crosswalk. These signs generally shall be posted back-to-back so that one sign is on the right and the other on the left. If a raised median with a width 6 ft or greater exists, the left sign shall be in the median. These signs should be 36 in x 36 in. The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-9p/">W16-9p</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-7p/">W16-7p</a> plates shall be 36 in wide. All signs shall be fluorescent yellow-green.</li>
<li value="3"><strong>Advance sign posting.</strong> Advance Pedestrian Crossing <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> signs, or Assembly D (CA) signs shall be posted as shown and shall be fluorescent yellow-green. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> signs shall be posted and positioned within ten feet of the yield limit line. These locations may be adjusted to accommodate field conditions. All signs should be 36 in x 36 in.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Pavement markings.</strong> &quot;PED XING&quot; or &quot;SCHOOL XING&quot; pavement markings shall be installed as shown except the yellow &quot;SLOW SCHOOL XING&quot; pavement markings shall be installed in advance of uncontrolled yellow school crosswalks. If the crosswalk spacing is less than 500 feet, only one set of pavement markings is required in advance of the first crosswalk.</li>
<li value="5"><strong>Midblock crosswalks.</strong> This standard plan also applies at uncontrolled approaches to midblock crosswalks.</li>
<li value="6"><strong>Raised pavement markings (RPM).</strong> Install RPMs one each side of crosswalk bar facing approach lanes. Two-way clear (white) or two-way amber (yellow) per CALTRANS standards and specifications may be installed corresponding to the crosswalk color.</li>
<li value="7"><a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> (Yield Here to Pedestrians) signs and yield lines. For uncontrolled locations with more than one lane in each direction of travel, advance yield lines may be used. Yield lines shall be supplemented with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> signs.</li>
</ol>
<p>Figure footnotes: * or &quot;SLOW SCHOOL XING&quot; or &quot;SCHOOL XING&quot;. ** Dimension of field installation of signs or markings may exceed the figures in the table by up to 100 feet per work order or plan in order to adjust to field conditions. C = Advance warning sign placement (CA MUTCD, Table 2C-4 and Part 2).</p>
</details>

### Sheet 2 of 4 (page 151)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0151.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0151.webp" alt="S-481.0 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-481.0, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/09-pages-145-162/#p151">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>One-way streets.</strong> Because one-way streets place drivers closer to the curb on the left side of the roadway, use A = 4B/(W-11) for left side pedestrian approach. This distance is not shown above.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Signs.</strong> See Sheet 1 for appropriate signs.</li>
</ol>
<p>Formula legend: A = 4B/(W-7) = Curb stopping prohibition necessary for motorist to see a pedestrian at a point 4 ft into the roadway. LADOT practice is to install 50 feet of red curb on arterial approaches to side streets, measured from the side street curb line prolongation. Install this minimum dimension whenever the calculated A distance results in a lesser dimension. (See Note 1). B = Safe stopping distance = 1.47 VT + 1.075 V^2/a. V = Posted speed + 5 mph (chart listings calculated on posted speed + 5 mph). T = Perception-reaction time of 1.5 seconds (driver previously alerted with advance sign and pavement markings). a = Deceleration rate = 11.2 ft/s^2 (AASHTO Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets, Stopping Sight Distance, printed ASSHTO). W = Curb lane width (driver is assumed to be 3 ft from lane line). Plan callouts: red curb, 50 ft min. per MPP Sec. 342, pedestrian.</p>
</details>
