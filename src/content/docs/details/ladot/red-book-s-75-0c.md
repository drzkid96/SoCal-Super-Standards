<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-75.0C showing the front, rear, side and plan views and an isometric of the Type II communication cabinet and its foundation. The cabinet is 12 in. wide, 7-1/4 in. deep and 43-7/8 in. tall, set on a foundation 2 in. above grade and 24 in. deep. It has a continuous stainless steel hinge, screened louvers, a padlock staple, a 3/4 in. plywood panel with adjustable studs, two 3 in. PVC conduits, a 1 in. drain pipe and an 8 ft ground rod. Material is .125 in. AL 5052 H32 with anodized finish per MIL-A-8625C, Spec. No. 92-067-02.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Front view: Elevation of the Type II communication cabinet on its concrete foundation. The cabinet is 12&quot; wide and 43-7/8&quot; tall overall, with a cabinet opening 10&quot; wide and 35-1/2&quot; tall and a door panel 38&quot; tall. A continuous stainless steel hinge runs down the left edge. A &#x27;CITY OF LOS ANGELES&#x27; label is on the door. Two 3&quot; PVC conduits (2-3&quot; PVC CONDUITS) enter the foundation below the cabinet. The foundation stands 2&quot; above the ground surface and extends 24&quot; below that, with a break line indicating the full depth is not drawn. Anchor bolts with nuts are shown at each side of the cabinet base.</p>
<p>Rear view: Elevation 12&quot; wide showing venting louvers with protective screens, in two banks of three rows by two columns, one near the top and one near the bottom of the cabinet. A removable staple for a padlock is on the right edge. A 1&quot; drain pipe and a 1/2&quot; DIA. x 8&#x27; ground rod come up through the foundation. The foundation is 2&quot; above grade and 24&quot; deep as in the front view.</p>
<p>Side view: Cabinet depth is 7-1/4&quot;. A 1-1/2&quot; x 1-1/2&quot; x 1/4&quot; thick aluminum mounting flange is welded to the cabinet base. A 3/4&quot; plywood panel inside the cabinet is 33&quot; tall. Adjustable panel mounting studs are shown at the top and bottom of the panel, with 4-15/16&quot; from the top of the cabinet to the upper stud, 6-1/16&quot; from the bottom to the lower stud and 1-5/8&quot; stud offset. A removable staple for padlock is at the door side. Conduits and the ground rod are shown dashed through the foundation, with a 14&quot; MIN. dimension.</p>
<p>Plan view: Dimensions shown are 20&quot; and 12&quot; across the top, 14&quot; across the bottom, 12&quot; and 7-1/4&quot; along the left side, and 8-1/8&quot; and 4&quot; along the right side. The drawing shows the foundation outline, the cabinet outline with mounting flange and slotted bolt holes at each side, the plywood panel with adjustable studs, and dashed circular openings for the conduits, drain pipe and ground rod.</p>
<p>Isometric view: A perspective of the cabinet with its door open, standing on the mounting flange base with slotted bolt holes.</p>

Printed page number 72.</p></details>
