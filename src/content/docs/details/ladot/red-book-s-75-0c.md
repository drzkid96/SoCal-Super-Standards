<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>Material: .125&quot; thick AL 5052 H32.</p>
<p>Finish: anodized per MIL-A-8625C specification.</p>
<p>Spec. No. 92-067-02.</p>
<p>Callouts: Continuous hinge, stainless steel. Venting louvers with protective screens. Removable staple for padlock. Adjustable panel mounting studs. 2-3&quot; PVC conduits. 3/4&quot; plywood panel. 1&quot; drain pipe. 1/2&quot; DIA. x 8&#x27; ground rod. 1-1/2&quot; x 1-1/2&quot; x 1/4&quot; thick aluminum mounting flange welded to cabinet.</p>
<p>Revision note: Supercedes S-75.0B. Show foundation 2&quot; above surface.</p></details>
