### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 111)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet S-5 of 5 of Standard Drawing S-99.0, round pedestal pile foundation for the overhead sign structure (monotube cantilever).</p>
<p>D1, Round pedestal pile foundation (scale 3/4&quot; = 1&#x27;-0&quot;, S-1): Elevation of a cast-in-drilled-hole (CIDH) pile drawn with break lines, with its vertical reinforcement and transverse reinforcement shown as a grid. The pile centerline coincides with the post centerline (CL post = CL shaft). The post stands on a base plate on four anchor rods at the top of the pile, with the pile top up to 3&quot; MAX above final grade. Callouts: final grade slope shall not exceed 1:2.5 within 5&#x27;-0&quot; of support CL, double ties at top, vertical reinforcement (see table), transverse reinforcement (see table), 2&quot; dia conduit curving out of the pile near the top, and a 3&quot; dimension from the bottom of the reinforcing cage to the bottom of the pile. PILE LENGTH is dimensioned from final grade to the bottom of the pile. Reference markers: A6 (S-4, anchor bolt template assembly), J6 (S-4, base plate details), G6 (S-4, anchor plate details, shown at the bottom anchor plate with nuts), and Section A (S-5).</p>
<p>I7, Section A (scale 3/4&quot; = 1&#x27;-0&quot;, S-5): Plan of the round pile, showing pile diameter, a ring of equally spaced vertical bars (about 16 dots shown) inside the circular transverse reinforcement. The transverse hoop is shown with a lap of 1&#x27;-3-3/4&quot;. A 3&quot; CL. MIN. (clear minimum) dimension is given from the bar to the pile edge. Callouts: vertical reinforcement (see table), transverse reinforcement (see table), this centerline parallel to sign face.</p>
<p>A1, Pile foundation table (NTS, S-5): Columns Structure No, CIDH pile (pile dia, pile length), vertical reinforcement (total, bar size), hoops (bar size, spacing).</p>
<p>Structure 1: pile dia 4&#x27;-0&quot;, pile length 13&#x27;-6&quot;, 20 vertical bars, bar size #9, hoops #4 at 6&quot;.</p>
<p>Structure 2: pile dia 4&#x27;-0&quot;, pile length 15&#x27;-0&quot;, 20 vertical bars, bar size #9, hoops #4 at 6&quot;.</p>
<p>Structure 3: pile dia 5&#x27;-0&quot;, pile length 18&#x27;-0&quot;, 28 vertical bars, bar size #9, hoops #4 at 6&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: Overhead Sign Structure Standard Drawings, Round Pedestal Pile Foundation. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Amir H. Sedadi, Interim General Manager. File name S-99.0, project no. 50346, sheet S-5 of 5, status AS BUILT, with the same review and approval signatures (reviewed July 7, 2011, accepted July 15, 2011) and engineer stamp (Civil No. 68001, expiration 6-30-11). Page number 104 in the page margin.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>This sheet has no numbered general notes. Notes and callouts on the sheet:</p>
<p>- Final grade slope shall not exceed 1:2.5 within 5&#x27;-0&quot; of support centerline (CL).</p>
<p>- CL post = CL shaft.</p>
<p>- Double ties at top.</p>
<p>- Vertical reinforcement (see table). Transverse reinforcement (see table).</p>
<p>- 2&quot; dia conduit.</p>
<p>- Lap 1&#x27;-3-3/4&quot; for the transverse reinforcement. 3&quot; CL. MIN. (clear minimum) cover.</p>
<p>- This CL parallel to sign face.</p>
<p>- General notes for the series are on sheet S-1.</p></details>
