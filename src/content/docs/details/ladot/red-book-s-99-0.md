<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Round cast-in-drilled-hole pile foundation for an overhead sign structure, shown in elevation and in Section A. Final grade slope may not exceed 1:2.5 within 5 feet of the support. A table gives pile diameter, length, vertical bars and hoops for three structures.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>D1 Round pedestal pile foundation. Centerline post = centerline shaft. Final grade slope shall not exceed 1:2.5 within 5&#x27;-0&quot; of support centerline. 3&quot; max. above grade. Double ties at top. Vertical reinforcement (see table). Transverse reinforcement (see table). 2&quot; dia. conduit. 3&quot; at pile bottom.</p>
<p>I7 Section A: pile diameter, lap 1&#x27;-3 1/2&quot;, vertical reinforcement (see table), transverse reinforcement (see table), 3&quot; clear min., centerline parallel to sign face.</p>
<h5>A1 Pile foundation table (CIDH pile):</h5>
<p>Structure 1: pile dia. 4&#x27;-0&quot;, length 13&#x27;-6&quot;, 20 vertical bars #9, hoops #4 at 6&quot; spacing.</p>
<p>Structure 2: pile dia. 4&#x27;-0&quot;, length 15&#x27;-0&quot;, 20 vertical bars #9, hoops #4 at 6&quot; spacing.</p>
<p>Structure 3: pile dia. 5&#x27;-0&quot;, length 18&#x27;-0&quot;, 28 vertical bars #9, hoops #4 at 6&quot; spacing.</p></details>
