### Sheet 1 of 5 (page 107)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0107.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0107.webp" alt="S-99.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-99.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p107">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>General notes:</h5>
<h5>Design:</h5>
<p>- Sign support structures designed in accordance with AASHTO Standard Specifications for Structural Support for Highway Signs, Luminaires and Traffic Signals: 5th edition.</p>
<p>- Sign weight used for sign support structure design is 3 psf.</p>
<p>- Basic wind speed (3 second gust) used for sign support structures design is 100 MPH, G = 1.14, I = 1.0 (50 year recurrence interval).</p>
<p>- Fatigue importance factor, I_f = 1.0, used for fatigue design of sign support structures considering galloping, natural wind gust and truck-induced gust.</p>
<p>- Pipe members shall conform to ASTM A53 GR. B.</p>
<p>- HSS tube members shall conform to ASTM A500 GR. B.</p>
<p>- All other structural steel shall conform to ASTM A36, or A992, unless noted otherwise.</p>
<p>- Welding shall be in conformance with the latest edition of AWS - D1.1 Structural Welding Code.</p>
<p>- Notch toughness of all structural steel members and plates greater than 1/2&quot; thick shall conform to Zone 2 requirements of AASHTO M270M/M270 supplementary requirement S5 (ASTM A709/A709M, supplementary requirement S83).</p>
<p>- High strength bolts shall conform to ASTM A325, Type 1.</p>
<p>- Nuts for high strength bolts shall be heavy hex and conform to ASTM A563 Grade DH, with supplementary requirements &quot;S1&quot; and &quot;S2&quot;.</p>
<p>- Hardened steel washers shall conform to ASTM F436, Type 1. Use washers under turning element in tightening unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>- All fasteners shall be hot-dip galvanized.</p>
<p>- All structural steel shall be hot-dip galvanized after fabrication, unless noted otherwise.</p>
<p>- The silicon content of the base metal shall be in the ranges of 0.0% to 0.04% or 0.15% to 0.25% for all hot-dip galvanized steel, unless noted otherwise.</p>
<p>- All H.S. bolts shall be considered slip critical unless noted otherwise. Design slip resistance for bolts shall conform to the bolt specifications for Class C slip coefficient = 0.33.</p>
<p>- Use self locking nuts on non-high strength (H.S.) bolts, unless otherwise shown or specified.</p>
<p>- Foundation design is based on Broms&#x27; method assuming a cohesive soil with an undrained shear strength of 1.50 ksf with a factor of safety of 3.0.</p>
<p>- Pile lengths shall be increased by an amount equal to the thickness of any existing fill soils encountered at the sign location during construction.</p>
<p>- Bureau of Engineering&#x27;s Geotechnical Engineering Group (213-847-0538) shall be contacted for recommendations if weak soils and/or groundwater is encountered during construction of the CIDH (cast-in-drilled-hole) piles.</p>
<p>- All concrete shall be class 3600.</p>
<p>- All reinforcing steel shall conform to ASTM A706 or A615 GR. 60.</p>
<p>- Anchor rods shall conform to ASTM F1554, GR. 55, with supplementary requirement &quot;S4&quot; (AASHTO M314). Top ends of anchor rods shall be ground flat at 90 degrees to rod axis. Final surface roughness shall not exceed 125 micro-inch.</p>
<p>- Anchor rod washers shall conform to ASTM F436.</p>
<p>- Anchor rod nuts shall conform to ASTM A563 GR. DH with supplementary requirements &quot;S1&quot; and &quot;S2&quot;.</p>
<p>- Anchor rods shall be hot-dip galvanized full length.</p>
<p>Plan callouts: 40&#x27;-0&quot; MAX sign length; 6&#x27;-0&quot; MAX sign height and support spacing; 2&#x27;-6&quot; MAX; 20&#x27;-0&quot; CLEAR; 25&#x27;-0&quot; MAX; centroid of sign area; level.</p></details>
