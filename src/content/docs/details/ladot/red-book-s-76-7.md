<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-76.7 shows the long full circle signal visors in two sizes, 8 in diameter by 18 in long and 12 in diameter by 27 in long. Each has a side view with a 3.5 degree tilted back edge and an end view with four ears at 90 degree spacing. Ear details are on S-76.3 and all other details are in LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 59)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-76.7, Long Visor, 8 in and 12 in diameter full circle. It shows two long visors, each as a side view paired with an end view. All views are marked Not to Scale.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 8&quot; X 18&quot; full circle, side view: a rectangular tube with overall length 18&quot;+/-1/16&quot;. The back edge is tilted 3.5 degrees from vertical, with two small ear tabs on it. Dashed lines near the top and bottom edges, and a dash-dot centerline along the axis.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 8&quot; X 18&quot; full circle, end view: a circular ring dimensioned 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals, spaced with 90 degree callouts between adjacent ears. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 12&quot; X 27&quot; full circle, side view: a tube with overall length 27&quot;+/-1/16&quot; and the same 3.5 degree tilted back edge with two ear tabs. A lens shaped overlap or seam outline is drawn across the lower part of the tube, as it is wrapped from a full circle. Dashed lines at the top and bottom edges and a dash-dot centerline.</p>
<p>Long Visor, 12&quot; X 27&quot; full circle, end view: a circular ring dimensioned 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and four 90 degree callouts. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Title block: Long Visor, 8&quot; &amp; 12&quot; Diameter Full Circle, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved January 26, 2011 for Amir Sedadi, Interim General Manager, Drawing No. S-76.7, page number 52.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. See ear detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3 (shown for both the 8 in x 18 in and the 12 in x 27 in long visors).</p>
<p>2. Note: Refer to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06 for all other details.</p></details>
