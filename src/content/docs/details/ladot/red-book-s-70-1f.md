<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.1F shows the elongated transit priority loop, a slot shaped saw cut 6 ft wide with 3 ft end radius and a length set on the plan. Loops shorter than 40 ft get 2 turns of wire and loops of 40 ft or longer get 1 turn. Slot depth is 3 in minimum along the loop and 5 in minimum for the diagonal lead-in cut.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 71)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Table 1, loop turns: two columns, LENGTH OF LOOP and NUMBER OF TURNS. Row 1: less than 40&#x27; = 2 turns. Row 2: 40&#x27; or more (greater than or equal to 40&#x27;) = 1 turn.</p>
<p>Winding detail, 2 turns: an elongated slot shaped loop drawn as two nested long U shaped runs with direction arrows. The wire goes up one side, around the rounded end, and back down, twice, then leaves as a twisted pair (shown as twisted tail at lower left). Caption 2 TURNS.</p>
<p>Winding detail, 1 turn: a single elongated U shaped run with direction arrows, leaving as a twisted pair at lower left. Caption 1 TURN.</p>
<p>Saw slot detail: a long narrow slot, plan view, with semicircular ends. Width is 6&#x27; and the end radius is R=3&#x27;. Length is AS SPECIFIED ON PLAN, shown with a vertical dimension line. Depth callouts: Depth=3&quot; Min. for the slot along the loop sides, and Depth=5&quot; Min. for the diagonal saw cut that leaves the slot at the lower left toward the lead-in.</p>
<p>Symbol: the plan symbol for this loop, a tall narrow rounded rectangle (slot shape) with a leader.</p>
<p>Title block: Transit Priority Loop, sheet 1/1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 4-5-2001, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.1F. Drawn TH 12-04-00, checked MD 12-04-00, T.E. MD 12-04-00, Sr. T.E. SS 12-04-00, Pr. T.E. GO 3-14-01.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Table: Length of loop less than 40&#x27;, number of turns 2. Length of loop 40&#x27; or more, number of turns 1.</p>
<p>Drawing labels: Winding detail. 2 turns. 1 turn. Saw slot detail. Symbol. 6&#x27;. R=3&#x27;. Length as specified on plan. Depth=3&quot; min. Depth=5&quot; min.</p>
<p>(No numbered notes on this sheet.)</p></details>
