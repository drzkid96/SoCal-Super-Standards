<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-63.1 shows typical fittings for signal standards. They are side-mount and post-top terminal compartments, a lock ring, special tee and 90 degree elbow, a curved washer (Detail A) and a pole plate for side mountings, all with 5/8 in. galvanized bolts and 1-1/2 in. pipe threads. One note requires State of California Standard Specifications.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 52)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0052.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0052.webp" alt="S-63.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-63.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p52">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Terminal compartment, side-mount: an isometric view of a terminal compartment attached to the side of a pole, with a cover held by screws. It is fixed to the pole with 5/8 in.-11 N.C. hex. hd. bolts with lock washer and flat washer, hot dip galvanized, with flat washers and curved washers inside the pole. A 1/2 in. (min. thread) hub is called out at the side.</p>
<p>Terminal compartment, post-top mount: an isometric view of a terminal compartment on a slip fitter with two collars and 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 3/4 in. sq. hd. cup pt. set screws, hot dip galvanized (6 Req.).</p>
<p>Lock ring, special tee and special 90 degree elbow: three isometric fitting views, each with a 72-tooth toothed rim. The tee and elbow have 1-1/2 in. pipe threads.</p>
<p>Washer detail A: a plan and edge view of a washer curved to fit the standard, with a 3/4 in. diameter hole and a 1-3/4 in. overall dimension.</p>
<p>Pole plate for side mountings: a section through the signal standard wall showing a pole plate with 1-1/2 in. pipe thread hub, held by two 5/8 in.-11 N.C. hex. hd. bolts, hot dip galvanized, with flat washers and Washers Detail A inside the pole and a 5/8 in. nut, 5/8 in. lockwasher and 5/8 in. flat washer outside. Dimensions are 1/2 in. (min. thds.) bolt thread projection and 5/16 in. plate thickness. A callout reads Cable guide - omit on upper plate.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Fittings, Typical, Terminal Compartment and Pole Plate Mounting, drawing number S-63.1, drawn 11-02-11, approved 12-8-11 by Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Page number 45.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Construction, material and finish painting shall conform to &#x27;Standard Specifications&#x27; (latest edition), Dept. of Transportation, State of California.</p>
<p>Callouts: 5/8 in.-11 N.C. hex. hd. bolt with lock washer and flat washer, hot dip galvanized. Flat washer and curved washer. 1/2 in. (min. thread). Cover. 3/8 in.-16 N.C. x 3/4 in. sq. hd. cup pt. set screw, hot dip galvanized (6 Req.). 72 teeth. 1-1/2 in. pipe threads. Washer curved to fit standard, 3/4 in. diameter, 1-3/4 in. Washer Detail A. Cable guide - omit on upper plate. 1/2 in. (min. thds.). 1-1/2 in. pipe thread. 5/8 in. nut. 5/8 in. lockwasher and 5/8 in. flat washer. Signal standard. 5/16 in.</p></details>
