<p class="dwg-sum">Shows typical fittings for mounting a signal terminal compartment, side mount or post-top mount, on a signal standard, along with the pole plate for side mountings. It lists the bolts, washers, lock ring, slip fitter threads and teeth counts used. Construction, material and finish painting follow the State of California Standard Specifications.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Fittings, typical, terminal compartment and pole plate mounting.</p>
<p>Terminal compartment, side mount: 3/4 in-11 N.C. hex. hd. bolt with lock washer and flat washer, hot dip galvanized. Flat washer and curved washer. Cover. Special tee. 72 teeth. Cable guide, omit on cover. Lock ring. Pipe threads. 5/16-16 N.C. x 7/8 in sq. hd. cup pt. set screw, hot dip galvanized (6 req.). 4 in (min. thread).</p>
<p>Terminal compartment, post-top mount: 72 teeth upper plate. Washer curved to fit standard. Detail &quot;A&quot;: 1 1/2 in pipe thread. Detail A: flat washer, 5/8 in-11 N.C. hex. hd. bolt, hot dip galvanized, nut, washer, 3/4 in lockwasher and 7/8 in flat washer, signal standard.</p>
<p>Pole plate for side mountings.</p>
<p>Note: Construction, material and finish painting shall conform to &quot;Standard Specifications&quot; (latest edition), Dept. of Transportation, State of California.</p></details>
