<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of standard drawing S-486.0 (March 4, 2005) for street name signs hung from traffic signal mast arms. It shows an 18 inch high one-line street name blade and a two-line blade with arrows, plus a 72 x 24 inch freeway ramp sign with an I-405 shield. Notes give legend sizes, colors (white on blue for street names, white on green for ramps), reflectivity and the 96 inch length limit.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 113)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0113.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0113.webp" alt="S-486.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-486.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p113">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section A, Street name signs (front views): Two sign blades with a double border line, each hung from two short hanger posts on the top edge.</p>
<p>Sign 1: &quot;Jefferson Bl&quot; in a single line of black legend (blue background and white legend per notes). The blade is 18&quot; high. Overall length is AS REQUIRED (see note 4), with the hangers located 12&quot; in from each end.</p>
<p>Sign 2: Two lines of legend, &quot;&lt;- Mulholland Dr&quot; on the first line (with a left arrow before the name) and &quot;Valley Circle Bl -&gt;&quot; on the second line (with a right arrow after the name). The blade is 30&#x27; high [printed with a foot mark as shown on the sheet]. Overall length is AS REQUIRED (see note 4), with the hangers located 12&quot; in from each end.</p>
<p>Section B, Freeway ramp signs: One sign blade 72&quot; long by 24&quot; high, with hanger posts 12&quot; in from each end. The legend reads, left to right, a left arrow, an INTERSTATE 405 shield, and the word NORTH (large first letter and smaller capitals). The sign is drawn with a double border line.</p>
<p>Title block: Mast Arm Mounted Street Name Signs, sheet 1 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Approved March 4, 2005, for Wayne K. Tanda, General Manager. Drawing No. S-486.0. Drawn by (DWN) MT, Pr. T.E. TLJ. Page number 106.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>A. Street name signs, notes:</h5>
<p>1. Legend size: 8&quot; upper case, 6&quot; lower case, Series &quot;E&quot; letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow.</p>
<p>2. Color: white legend, blue background.</p>
<p>3. Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</p>
<p>4. Where the length of the sign blade would exceed 96&quot; (generally 16 letters and spaces) the street name titles, &quot;ST&quot;, &quot;AVE&quot;, &quot;BL&quot;, &quot;PL&quot;, &quot;DR&quot;, &quot;RD&quot;, etc. may be deleted.</p>
<h5>B. Freeway ramp signs, notes:</h5>
<p>1. Legend size: 8&quot; and 6&quot; Series D capital letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow; 18&quot; high US, California or Interstate shield, as appropriate with proportional numbers.</p>
<p>2. Color: white legend, Warboys green (L.A. No. 1) background; black on white US shield; white on green California shield; white on blue and red Interstate shield.</p>
<p>3. Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 114)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0114.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0114.webp" alt="S-486.0 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-486.0, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p114">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Typical placement plan (top of sheet): A schematic plan of the Jefferson Blvd street running vertically at the left and again at the right, with a freeway onramp on the left side and offramp on the right, each shown as a rounded curb return at the corners of the intersection with the ramp approaches. The left side is labeled FWY. ONRAMP with a left-pointing arrow. The right side is labeled FWY. OFFRAMP with a left-pointing arrow. JEFFERSON BLVD is labeled vertically on both sides of the center. At each of the four signal pole locations a mast arm is drawn from a pole (filled dot) with a street name sign hung from it. At the left side a &quot;Jefferson Bl&quot; sign hangs from the mast arm running along the street, with a traffic-direction arrow below it. A freeway ramp sign (left arrow, I-405 shield, NORTH) hangs from a mast arm at the lower left, and another ramp sign (left arrow, I-405 shield, NORTH) hangs from a mast arm at the upper right, each with an arrow showing the approach direction. The title for this view is TYPICAL PLACEMENT.</p>
<p>Mast arm detail (side view at top center): A circle labeled MAST ARM, with BANDING straps around it fastening a bracket below with FIBER LOCK NUT.</p>
<p>Side view (right): The mast arm (circle) with banding, a fiber lock nut at the upper bolt, and the mast arm sign bracket per standard plan S-457.0 connecting down to a sign bracket and a second fiber lock nut. The bracket is attached to a Signfix medium section stiffener at the back of the sign. The side of the sign shows FRONT OF SIGN and sign material .080&quot; aluminum.</p>
<p>Rear view: A rectangular sign back with two horizontal Signfix medium section stiffeners, one near the top and one near the bottom, each secured to the back of the sign with pop rivets at 6&quot; spacing (about 13 rivets shown per stiffener). The stiffeners run nearly the full width, with short vertical end strips at each end of the sign.</p>
<p>Title block: Mast Arm Mounted Street Name Signs, sheet 2 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-486.0. Page number 107.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. For installation details see Std. Plan S-457.0.</p>
<p>2. For Signfix aluminum channel framing system details see Standard Plan S-476.0.</p>
<p>Callouts: Signfix medium section stiffener secured to back of sign with pop rivets at 6&quot; spacing. Mast arm sign bracket per Standard Plan S-457.0. Fiber lock nut. Banding. Sign material .080&quot; aluminum. Front of sign. Mast arm. Typical placement: Fwy. onramp, Fwy. offramp, Jefferson Blvd.</p></details>
