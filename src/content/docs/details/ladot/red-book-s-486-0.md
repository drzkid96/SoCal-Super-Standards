<div class="dwg-head"><div><span class="dwg-num">S-486.0</span><span class="dwg-src">LADOT Red Book: Special Provisions and Standard Drawings for Traffic Signals &middot; July 2008 base, revised (2026 file)</span></div>
<div class="dwg-facts"><span><b>2</b> sheets</span><span>Pages 113, 114</span></div></div>

<div class="dwg-summary"><p><strong>Overview:</strong> Governs internally illuminated and retroreflective mast-arm mounted street name signs on signalized intersections, including clamp placement and clearance from signal heads.</p><p class="dwg-use"><strong>Typical Use Cases:</strong> Traffic signal modifications, new signal installations, and arterial wayfinding upgrades.</p></div>





## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (page 113)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0113.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0113.webp" alt="S-486.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-486.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p113">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">A. Street name signs, notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Legend size: 8&quot; upper case, 6&quot; lower case, Series &quot;E&quot; letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow.</li>
<li value="2">Color: white legend, blue background.</li>
<li value="3">Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</li>
<li value="4">Where the length of the sign blade would exceed 96&quot; (generally 16 letters and spaces) the street name titles, &quot;ST&quot;, &quot;AVE&quot;, &quot;BL&quot;, &quot;PL&quot;, &quot;DR&quot;, &quot;RD&quot;, etc. may be deleted.</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">B. Freeway ramp signs, notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Legend size: 8&quot; and 6&quot; Series D capital letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow; 18&quot; high US, California or Interstate shield, as appropriate with proportional numbers.</li>
<li value="2">Color: white legend, Warboys green (L.A. No. 1) background; black on white US shield; white on green California shield; white on blue and red Interstate shield.</li>
<li value="3">Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</li>
</ol>
</details>
