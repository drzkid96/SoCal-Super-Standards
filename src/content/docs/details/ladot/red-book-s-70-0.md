<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Two LADOT detector sheets. The first (S-70.1D) shows bicycle detector loops. Case I is a pair of 3 ft loops for bicycle only (bike lane) and Case II is a pair of 6 ft loops for bicycle and vehicle, with winding details and saw cut sections. The second (S-70.4A, sheet 3 of 3) shows the preformed 30 in LRT track detector loop and the Type A LRT detector handhole.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 69)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0069.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0069.webp" alt="S-70.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p69">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Bicycle detector. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>Winding detail (not to scale): loops 1 and 2. Twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) into a pair. To pullbox. Wire pairs 1, 2, 3, 4.</p>
<p>Case I (bicycle only): loop 1 and loop 2 each 3 ft, spaced 3 ft apart. Pullbox 3 ft 0 in max. from the curb face.</p>
<p>Case II (bicycle and vehicle): loop 1 and loop 2 each 6 ft, spaced 8 ft apart. Pullbox 3 ft 0 in max. from the curb face.</p>
<p>Direction of traffic flow shown.</p>
<h5>Saw cut sections:</h5>
<p>Sect. A-A: 3/8 in wide, 3.5 in deep*.</p>
<p>Sect. B-B: 1/2 in wide, 5 in deep*.</p>
<p>Sect. C-C: 3/8 in wide, 5 in deep*.</p>
<p>Sect. D-D: 1/2 in max. wide, 5 in deep*, 8 wires max. per 1/2 in sawcut.</p>
<p>Sect. E-E: 1/2 in max. wide, 5 in deep*.</p>
<p>* Depth of slot not to exceed depth of pavement.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install four (4) complete alternating turns of loop wires, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. For stub-out and conduit details refer to page 2 of S-70.1A.</p>
<p>3. See LADOT Material Specification No. 92-081-05 or latest revision.</p>
<p>4. Use Case I loops for bike lane installations. Use Case II loops when vehicular loops are intended to detect bicycles.</p>
<p>5. An octagonal shaped loop or other non-round shaped loop may be used instead of the round loop with prior LADOT approval.</p>
<p>6. Same winding pattern to be used in both loops for Case I with 1 pair from each loop leading to the pullbox.</p>
<p>7. Loops installed in bike lanes with parking shall be placed with the near edge of the loop 1 ft to the right of the bike lane delineation line, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>8. Loops installed in bike lanes adjacent to the roadside without parking shall be centered in the bike lane, or between the gutter and the bike delineation line, unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>9. Any LADOT approved non-round shaped loops must conform to all other specifications shown on this standard drawing.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 76)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0076.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0076.webp" alt="S-70.0 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.0, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p76">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Light Rail Train (LRT) track detector loop. Sheet 3 of 3 (S-70.4A).</p>
<p>LRT preformed loop details (cont&#x27;d).</p>
<p>Preformed loop: 30 in diameter. 3/4 in O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. Expansion joint. CVCC tee. 3/8 in O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose to Type A handhole. Twisted tail.</p>
<p>Winding details (not to scale): twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) into a pair, to Type A handhole. Pairs 1 and 2.</p>
<h5>Type &#x27;A&#x27; LRT detector handhole:</h5>
<p>Throat dia. 9 in. 4 in min. 12 in total depth, 11-3/8 in inside depth, 8 in min. 6 in min. below the box. 10-3/8 in box width, 1 in wall. 3 in min. around the handhole. Cast iron frame and cover (cover 11-1/8 in, frame 14-1/4 in, marked LADOT). Loop sealant. Ballast, PCC or hot AC to match adjacent roadbed material. Precast reinforced concrete box. Cables of shielded loop conductor pairs. Portland cement concrete. Conduit to termination pullbox, 2 in Schedule 80 PVC (30 in deep). Clean, crushed rock sump.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Splices shall only be made in the handhole.</p>
<p>2. Splices shall be soldered and water proofed.</p></details>
