<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 7 of standard S-493.0, approved July 24, 2009, showing where to place limit lines and crosswalks at an intersection. Case 1 is a typical crosswalk with a 5 ft minimum setback and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> stop signs, and Case 2 shows left-side stop bar placement with 35 ft minimum for turning vehicles, with an island alternate and a large vehicle set back alternate based on a turning template.</p>

<p>Case 2: a plan of an intersection with a circled S at the center (signal location) and a vehicle making a left turn from the left approach. A dimension of 35&#x27; MIN. is drawn from the left side limit line across to the cross street crosswalk edge, and the turning path of the vehicle is shown as a dashed arc across the intersection. <p>Case 2 Alternate Large Vehicle Set Back: the same layout with a large vehicle drawn turning at an angle, a dimension labeled Base on Turning Template, vertical legends WAIT and HERE painted on the pavement in the approach lane, and NOTE 3.</p>

Below the hatched strip the main street shows dashed white lane lines ending in solid stop lines at the crosswalk and a double yellow curved left-turn lane line with a left-turn arrow. On the right approach a hatched island has a vehicle crossing the stop line at an angle, with an <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> stop sign on a post, a dimension &#x27;20&#x27; MIN.&#x27; measured from the cross street curb line to the sign location and a left pointing arrow labeled &#x27;NOTE 12&#x27; next to a diagonal leader line.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Location of Limit Lines and Crosswalks, Drawing No. S-493.0, sheet 5 of 7.</p></details>

Label &#x27;NOTES 13, 14 &amp; 15&#x27;. The right approach shows a driveway symbol at the curb.</p>
