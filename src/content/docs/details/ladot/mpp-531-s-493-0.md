<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Cases showing where to place limit lines (stop bars) and crosswalks at intersections, with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> stop signs, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3.svg">R9-3</a> and R13A(CA) signs, driveways and keep clear areas. Case 1 covers corners with and without access ramps, with the stop line a 5 ft minimum from the crossing. Case 2 shows a 35 ft minimum setback and alternates with an island or a large vehicle set back based on a turning template. The notes referenced by the cases are not on the sheets in this set, so only the visible dimensions and signs are recorded here.</p>
<p class="dwg-topics">Topics: <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/crosswalks/">Crosswalks and pedestrian crossings</a> <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/striping-intersections/">Lane lines, striping and intersection markings</a></p>
