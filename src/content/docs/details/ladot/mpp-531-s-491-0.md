<p class="dwg-sum">Marking and signing for advance (added) turn lanes and trap lanes, where a through lane ends as a mandatory turn lane. Trap lanes are delineated by elephant tracks, starting about two blocks ahead of the turn but not less than 500 ft and not more than 1,300 ft. They are supplemented with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-7.svg">W4-7</a> and W7-3A(CA) signs. Right-turn trap lanes end with a curved barrier line at least 100 ft long, and left-turn trap lanes need overhead signing.</p>

<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. The second (downstream) pavement marking is not mandatory if advance turn lane is 100&#x27; long or less. At locations where a right turn is prohibited at an intermediate intersection during limited hours, the pavement arrow at that intersection should be deleted.</p>
<p>2. Continuous through lanes that terminate as mandatory turn lanes and that are not otherwise regulated by the Vehicle Code, (commonly known as &quot;Trap Lanes&quot;) shall be delineated by Elephant Tracks. The length of Elephant Tracking should be two blocks in advance of the mandatory turn, but not less than 500 feet and not more than 1,300 feet. This striping shall be supplemented with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-7.svg">W4-7</a> and W7-3A(CA) signing. The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-7.svg">W4-7</a> sign should be placed near the start of the Elephant Tracks.</p>
<p>3. Added turn lanes (i.e. having no continuity upstream) traversing through all intervening intersections shall be delineated by Elephant Tracking (lane drop markings) and pavement arrows as shown.</p>
<p>4. Trap Lanes should be considered where it is not feasible to drop a lane with the standard signing and pavement markings. They should terminate at arterial streets or freeway ramps.</p>
<p>5. Trapped lanes for left turns should have overhead signing.</p>
<p>6. Trapped lanes for right turns shall be terminated by a curved barrier line and separated from the adjacent through lane by a longitudinal line. Barrier lines for right-turn lanes should be 100&#x27; minimum.</p>
<p>7. Placement of arrows should also conform with Standard Plan S-456.0.</p></details>
