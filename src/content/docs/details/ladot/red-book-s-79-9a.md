<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. For cabinet and foundation detail see City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation Standard Drawing No. S-75.0C.</p>
<p>2. Service distribution cabinet shall have installed on its backpanel a 100-amps loadcenter equivalent to Square D Cat. No. Q06-12L100.</p>
<p>3. Circuit breakers shall be provided and installed as follows:</p>
<p>1 each 2 pole 50A</p>
<p>1 each 1 pole 50A</p>
<p>1 each 1 pole 30A</p>
<p>2 each 1 pole 20A</p>
<p>Callouts: 100 amps load center. Circuit breakers. 2-3&quot; conduits. Cabinet opening 10&quot;. Front view (inside cabinet). Side view.</p></details>
