<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-79.9A showing the Type II service distribution cabinet, 12 in. wide, 7-1/4 in. deep and 43-7/8 in. tall on a foundation 2 in. above grade and 24 in. deep, with a 100 amp load center on the back panel. Required breakers are one 2 pole 50A, one 1 pole 50A, one 1 pole 30A and two 1 pole 20A. Cabinet and foundation details are on Standard Drawing S-75.0C and the load center is equivalent to Square D Cat. No. Q06-12L100.</p>




## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 95)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0095.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0095.webp" alt="S-79.9A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.9A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p95">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Isometric: The Type II service distribution cabinet with its door open, showing a load center panel inside and the mounting flange base with slotted bolt holes.</p>
<p>Front view (inside cabinet): The cabinet is 12&quot; wide and 43-7/8&quot; tall overall, with a cabinet opening 10&quot; wide and 35-1/2&quot; tall and a door panel 38&quot; tall. A 100 AMPS load center with circuit breakers is mounted on the back panel at the top of the cabinet, 4&quot; below the top of the opening. Two 3&quot; conduits (2-3&quot; CONDUITS) rise into the cabinet from the foundation. The foundation projects 2&quot; above the surface and is 24&quot; deep, with anchor bolts and a ground rod, and break lines show the depth is not fully drawn.</p>
<p>Side view: The cabinet depth is 7-1/4&quot;. The 100 AMPS load center is shown on the back panel, with mounting studs at top and bottom and a 1-5/8&quot; offset dimension. A conduit is shown dashed bending out of the foundation at 14&quot; MIN. depth.</p>
<p>Circuit breaker schedule (note 3): 1 each 2 pole 50A, 1 each 1 pole 50A, 1 each 1 pole 30A, 2 each 1 pole 20A.</p>
<p>Title block: Type II Service Distribution Cabinet, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, S. E. Rowe, General Manager, approved 9-20-91. Drawing No. S-79.9A. Drawn LAR 09-19-91, checked ID 09-19-91, supervised VJ 09-19-91, reviewed AN 09-19-91. Revision: Show Found. 2&quot; Above Surface &amp; Del. PVC (JEM 02-27-92). Printed page number 88.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. For cabinet and foundation detail see City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation Standard Drawing No. S-75.0C.</p>
<p>2. Service distribution cabinet shall have installed on its backpanel a 100-amps loadcenter equivalent to Square D Cat. No. Q06-12L100.</p>
<p>3. Circuit breakers shall be provided and installed as follows:</p>
<p>1 each 2 pole 50A</p>
<p>1 each 1 pole 50A</p>
<p>1 each 1 pole 30A</p>
<p>2 each 1 pole 20A</p>
<p>Callouts: 100 amps load center. Circuit breakers. 2-3&quot; conduits. Cabinet opening 10&quot;. Front view (inside cabinet). Side view.</p></details>
