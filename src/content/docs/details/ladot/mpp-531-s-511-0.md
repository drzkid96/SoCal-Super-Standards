<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</li>
<li value="2">Sign size is 12&quot;x18&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is Highway Gothic Series C type. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</li>
<li value="3">All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09/05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</li>
<li value="4"><strong>The background color is white.</strong> The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, all text and border are red.</li>
<li value="5">Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</li>
</ol>
<p>Sign labels: 12&quot; by 18&quot;, R=2&quot; at 4 corners, 9&quot; outside diameter, 1&quot; slash, 5.6&quot; Series E P, 2.5&quot; / 1.75&quot; / 0.75&quot; Series C (time line), 1.5&quot; Series C (day), 1&quot; Series C (STREET SWEEPING), 0.25&quot; Series C (bottom line).</p>
</details>
