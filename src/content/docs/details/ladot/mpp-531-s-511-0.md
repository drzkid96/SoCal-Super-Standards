<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Details the 12 inch by 18 inch street sweeping sign (LADOT Stock No. 7000-7199) with a 9 inch outside diameter circle and slash, a 5.6 inch Series E P, and Highway Gothic Series C legend such as "10AM to 12PM Wednesday Street Sweeping". The background is white with a black P and red circle, slash, text and border. Signs are .080 aluminum with reflective SEG Type II material and graffiti film.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Sign dimensions: 12 inch wide by 18 inch tall, 2 inch radius at 4 corners. 9 inch outside diameter circle and slash, 1 inch slash. 5.6 inch Series E P.</p>
<p>Legend sample: &quot;10AM to 12PM / WEDNESDAY / STREET SWEEPING&quot;. Letter heights: 2.5 inch, 1.75 inch and 0.75 inch Series C for the time line, 1.5 inch Series C for the day, 1 inch Series C for STREET SWEEPING, 0.25 inch Series C for the bottom line.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Signs shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 12 inch x 18 inch, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is Highway Gothic Series C type. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4 inch wide and spaced 1/4 inch from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09/05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, all text and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
