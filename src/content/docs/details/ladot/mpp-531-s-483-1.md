<div class="dwg-summary"><p><strong>Overview:</strong> Details raised crosswalk ramp grades, flat-top crossing dimensions, drainage accommodation, and warning markings for vertical deflection at pedestrian crossings.</p><p class="dwg-use"><strong>Typical Use Cases:</strong> Neighborhood slow streets, school zones, and channelized right-turn crossings (paired with <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/ladot/mpp-531-s-494-1/">S-494.1</a>).</p></div>

</ol>
