<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard details for raised crosswalks with bicycle ramps across a bike lane, including approach and departure ramps, Chevron III and Chevron IV markings, and the sign assemblies for advance warning. The flat top is at least 10 ft wide and should match the connecting sidewalk or path. Ramps are at least 6 ft long with a 12.5% maximum slope. Sheet 3 shows a raised side street crosswalk at a standard curb ramp with optional green bicycle crossing.</p>

The bicycle ramp is dimensioned 12.5% MAX. SLOPE and the chevron length 4.5&#x27;. Below the ramp is the BIKE LANE and the FLAT TOP of the raised crosswalk with three black crossing bars.</p>
<p>Sidewalk Culvert Markings: Plan view of a street edge with an 8&quot; minimum white edgeline and 3 flexible delineators on the lane line spaced 20&#x27; on center, shown as two 20&#x27; dimensions (20&#x27; TYP and 20&#x27;). The bike lane approaches the crossing and ends with a Chevron IV bicycle ramp marking (see Sheet 1), a bicycle crossing marking with three bars, and a bicycle yield line with triangle (shark teeth) marks. <details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>NOTES</h5>

<p>2. The placement of advanced warning signs and markings shall follow the guidance for advanced sign posting (C**) shown on S-481.0.</p>
<p>3. See City of Los Angeles Supplemental Street Design Guide, Detail 600 for raised crossing details.</p>
