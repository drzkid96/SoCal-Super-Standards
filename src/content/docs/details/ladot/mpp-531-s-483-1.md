<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard details for raised crosswalks with bicycle ramps across a bike lane, including approach and departure ramps, Chevron III and Chevron IV markings, and the sign assemblies for advance warning. The flat top is at least 10 ft wide and should match the connecting sidewalk or path. Ramps are at least 6 ft long with a 12.5% maximum slope. Sheet 3 shows a raised side street crosswalk at a standard curb ramp with optional green bicycle crossing.</p>

<p>1. The width of the top of raised crosswalks should match the width of the connecting sidewalk, shared use path, or desired crosswalk, but not less than 10&#x27; in width.</p>

Bicycle yield line and limit line (if needed). Bicycle crossing marking with optional solid green colored pavement.</p></details>
