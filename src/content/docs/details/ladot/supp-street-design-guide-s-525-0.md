<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT standard drawing S-525.0 sheet 1 of 5 showing a pedestrian crossing island in the center of a street with a marked crosswalk, a diagonal curb ramp at the corner, channelizing bollards around the island and a yellow mountable rubber speed hump. ## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 4 (document page 167)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0167.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0167.webp" alt="S-525.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-525.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/10-pages-163-175/#p167">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, Crossing Island: A marked crosswalk of horizontal bars crosses an intersection street, with a diagonal curb ramp (stippled detectable surface) at the corner, a dashed curb line prolongation along the left edge, and a heavy stop line and a crosswalk of vertical bars across the cross street at the left. At the left end of the crosswalk, a yellow mountable rubber speed hump is drawn along the curb line prolongation. Detectable warning surface (see note 4) strips (stippled) lie along both edges of the crossing path, with a dimension labeled MATCH CROSSWALK WIDTH WHERE POSSIBLE (SEE NOTE 5) across it. The crossing island at the right is a gray rectangle bounded by an 8&quot; solid yellow line, shown with contrasting colored surface (optional, see note 2) and channelizing bollards (see note 1) set around its perimeter. Dimensions: 8&#x27; MIN island length along the street, 6&#x27; MIN island width (see note 6), and TOTAL CROSSING SEE NOTE 10 measured from the curb line prolongation across the crosswalk to the island end. Beyond the island is a hatched approach area of 12&quot; solid yellow line spaced 20&#x27; C-C running 30&#x27; (dimension 30&#x27;). A left-turn arrow is painted in the adjacent lane.</p>
<p>Plan view, Option When Island Is Less Than 6&#x27; Wide: The same layout at a narrower island, labeled LESS THAN 6&#x27; across the island width, with a 4&#x27; MIN dimension (see note 6) across the hatched area beyond, 8&#x27; MIN island length, TOTAL CROSSING SEE NOTE 10, MATCH CROSSWALK WIDTH WHERE POSSIBLE (SEE NOTE 5), the crossing island label, channelizing bollards and a yellow mountable rubber speed hump at the left end of the crosswalk.</p>
<p>Labels in the main plan: diagonal curb ramp, 6&#x27; MIN see note 6, detectable warning surface see note 4, yellow mountable rubber speed hump, match crosswalk width where possible (see note 5), curb line prolongation, contrasting colored surface (optional, see note 2), 8&quot; solid yellow line, channelizing bollards (see note 1), 12&quot; solid yellow line spaced 20&#x27; C-C.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Crossing Islands, drawing no. S-525.0, sheet 1 of 5, approved 02-20-2020 by Seleta Reynolds, General Manager. Notes 1 to 11 appear on sheet 5 of 5, and note 11 there cites Detail 500.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>No numbered notes are printed on this sheet. Notes 1 through 11 are referenced and appear on sheet 5 of 5.</p>
<p>Callouts: Diagonal curb ramp. 6&#x27; minimum (MIN), see note 6. Detectable warning surface, see note 4. Yellow mountable rubber speed hump. Match crosswalk width where possible (see note 5). Curb line prolongation. Contrasting colored surface (optional, see note 2). 8&quot; solid yellow line. Channelizing bollards (see note 1). 12&quot; solid yellow line spaced 20&#x27; center to center (C-C). Crossing island. Total crossing, see note 10. Dimensions: 8&#x27; MIN, 30&#x27;, less than 6&#x27;, 4&#x27; MIN see note 6. Option when island is less than 6&#x27; wide.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 4 (document page 168)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0168.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0168.webp" alt="S-525.0 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-525.0, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/10-pages-163-175/#p168">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, Hardened Centerline: An intersection plan with a left-turn arrow in a lane on the left street and a marked crosswalk of horizontal bars crossing the street. A yellow hardened centerline, drawn as a long rounded double-line strip, runs along the street centerline and ends at the crosswalk, where a yellow mountable rubber speed hump (a short black rounded bar) continues the line across the crosswalk. A dimension labeled MATCH CROSSWALK WIDTH sets the hump length equal to the crosswalk width. A curb line prolongation (dashed) is shown at right. Diagonal curb ramps with stippled detectable surfaces are at the corners and a second crosswalk of vertical bars crosses the cross street at right. The hardened centerline is labeled YELLOW HARDENED CENTERLINE SEE OPTIONS THIS SHEET.</p>
<p>Options: Two section or elevation sketches are shown. Precast concrete curb is drawn in profile with dimensions 4.5&quot; (height) and 12&quot; (width). Flexible bollard is drawn as a tall tapered post set on a plastic curb base. Labels: precast concrete curb, flexible bollard, plastic curb.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Crossing Islands, drawing no. S-525.0, sheet 2 of 5. Notes 1 to 11 appear on sheet 5 of 5.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>No numbered notes are printed on this sheet. Notes appear on sheet 5 of 5.</p>
<p>Callouts: Yellow hardened centerline, see options this sheet. Match crosswalk width. Curb line prolongation. Yellow mountable rubber speed hump. Precast concrete curb 4.5&quot; tall by 12&quot; wide. Flexible bollard. Plastic curb. Options.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 4 (document page 169)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0169.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0169.webp" alt="S-525.0 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-525.0, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/10-pages-163-175/#p169">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, Case A - Directional Ramps: A marked crosswalk of horizontal bars crosses a roadway with directional curb ramps at the two corners on the left. Along the left, a dashed curb line prolongation runs vertically. The crosswalk passes through two islands. At the left is a small approach nose island, 4&#x27; MIN wide measured along the street, with an approach nose to be designed for site conditions, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-1.svg">OM1-1</a> (TYP) sign, and RPM (TYP) see note 9 shown as three small markers. At right is the center median crossing island, 8&#x27; MIN long with a 6&#x27; MIN width (see note 6). Detectable warning surface strips (see note 4) are stippled on each side of the cut-through, and a 2% MAX cross slope arrow is shown in the cut-through. An 18&quot; MAX. dimension is shown along the lower edge of the island next to the crosswalk. A pedestrian push button (see note 3) is shown. The island carries <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-1.svg">OM1-1</a> (TYP) signs and RPMs. Beside the island runs a 4&quot; solid yellow line 1&#x27; off center median and a hatched area of 12&quot; solid yellow line spaced 20&#x27; C-C. A left-turn arrow is painted in the lane above. Dimensions: TOTAL CROSSING SEE NOTE 10 and MATCH CROSSWALK WIDTH WHERE POSSIBLE (SEE NOTE 5).</p>
<p>Plan view, Option When Island Is Less Than 6&#x27; Wide (see note 3): A narrower crossing island, with a 4&#x27; MIN approach island, TOTAL CROSSING SEE NOTE 10, MATCH CROSSWALK WIDTH WHERE POSSIBLE (SEE NOTE 5), 8&#x27; MIN island length, LESS THAN 6&#x27; island width, 4&#x27; MIN SEE NOTE 6 for the hatched area, and a crossing island label.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Crossing Islands, drawing no. S-525.0, sheet 3 of 5. Notes 1 to 11 appear on sheet 5 of 5.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>No numbered notes are printed on this sheet. Notes 1 through 11 are referenced and appear on sheet 5 of 5.</p>
<p>Callouts: Detectable warning surface, see note 4. 6&#x27; minimum (MIN), see note 6. Approach nose, design for site conditions. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-1.svg">OM1-1</a> (TYP). Raised pavement marker (RPM) (TYP), see note 9. Curb line prolongation. Directional curb ramps. Total crossing, see note 10. Pedestrian push button, see note 3. 4&quot; solid yellow line 1&#x27; off center median. 12&quot; solid yellow line spaced 20&#x27; center to center (C-C). Crossing island. Match crosswalk width where possible (see note 5). 2% maximum (MAX). Dimensions: 4&#x27; MIN, 8&#x27; MIN, 18&quot; MAX. Option when island is less than 6&#x27; wide, see note 3.</p></details>
