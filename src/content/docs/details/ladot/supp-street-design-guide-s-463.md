<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan views of a Type II floating bus bulb with a Class IV bike lane passing behind it and integrated with the street crossing. The bike lane is at least 5 ft wide, the forward boarding and alighting area is 5 ft x 8 ft minimum, and the rear area is 12 ft x 4 ft or 5 ft x 8 ft minimum. Bicycle ramps are 12.5 percent maximum slope, the bus bulb accessible route is 4 ft minimum (5 ft preferred), and the bike crossing uses <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-6.svg">R9-6</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-1.svg">OM1-1</a> signs, yield lines and recommended green colored surface.</p>
