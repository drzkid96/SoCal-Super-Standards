<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of standard drawing S-92.2 for a 34' monotube cantilever sign standard. A 14X-Strong pipe post carries two 10 Std. pipe arms, on a 3'-0" dia. PF-34 cast-in-drilled-hole foundation 15' deep. The sheet gives the arm end plate, post cap and arm connection details and the design loading and stresses, with notes and the foundation on sheet 2.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 100)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0100.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0100.webp" alt="S-92.2 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-92.2, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p100">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation, Monotube Overhead Sign Supports (not to scale): A 34&#x27; cantilever sign standard with a single vertical post of Pipe 14X-Strong and two horizontal arms of Pipe 10 Std. The arms span 34&#x27; from the post centerline. Dimension B (see notes) runs from the post centerline to the centroid of the sign area. The sign (see notes) is drawn as a rectangle with vertical stiffener lines between the two arms, and is 6&#x27; MAX. high. Vertical dimensions on the post: 18&quot; from the post top to the upper arm centerline, 3&#x27; between the two arm centerlines, 21&#x27; from grade to the lower arm centerline, and 25-1/2&#x27; from grade to the post top. A 3&#x27; dimension locates the curb from the post centerline. A 3/4&quot; std. half coupling is on the post. Callouts point to Detail &quot;B&quot; (post top cap), Detail &quot;C&quot; (arm to post connection, at both arms), Detail &quot;A&quot; (arm end plate at both arm ends) and Detail &quot;D&quot; (base, circled at grade). Below grade is a 3&#x27;-0&quot; dia. C.I.D.H. (cast-in-drilled-hole) foundation (PF-34), 15&#x27; deep with a break line, with see Section A-A for reinforcement.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;A&quot; (arm end): An end plate Pl. 3/16 x 11-1/4 dia. with a 1/8&quot; fillet weld on an arm of 10-3/4&quot; O.D.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;B&quot; (post top): Removable top Pl. 3/16 x 15 dia., 2 set screws, 15&quot; dia. plate, 1&quot; plate offset dimension, Bar 2&quot; x 3/16&quot; bent to circular shape with 12-1/2&quot; O.D. inside the post, 1/8&quot; fillet weld.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;C&quot; (arm to post connection): Plan view shows the post, the arm, Bar 7-3/4&quot; x 1/2&quot; x 14-3/8&quot; long, 2 req&#x27;d, cut to fit flush with pipe, 1-1/4&quot; and 1-1/4&quot; plate dimensions, 7&quot; offset, see Weld Detail, Bar 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; backing ring and 5/16&quot; welds. Elevation shows Bar 7&quot; x 1/2&quot; x 14&quot; long, 2 req&#x27;d, 4 - 1-1/4&quot; dia. ASTM A-325 bolts with hex. nut and washer, a 1/2&quot; plate thickness dimension, 1/4&quot; gap, and 5/16&quot; and 3/16&quot; welds. A face view shows 2 - Pl 1-1/4&quot; x 14&quot; x 22&quot; long with 2&quot;R typical corners, 22&quot; overall width with 18&quot; bolt spacing and 2&quot; edge distances, 14&quot; height with 10&quot; bolt spacing (5&quot; each side of center) and 2&quot; edge distances, 9&quot; dimension, a 2&quot; dia. hole in plates and pipe finished smooth, a 3/4&quot; std. half coupling, the 14&quot; O.D. post, and 5/16&quot; welds.</p>
<p>Specification block (upper right): Design per A.A.S.H.O. Specifications for the Design and Construction of Supports for Highway Signs, dated 1968. Loading: wind normal to sign face 23.3 psf, transverse to face 0.2 of normal force. Unit stresses: concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3000 psi, fc = 1200 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 24,000 psi; structural steel fb = 23,000 psi; soil bearing pressure = 23,000 psf.</p>
<p>Title block: 34&#x27; Cantilever Sign Standard and Foundation Details, sheet 1 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Robert R. Yates, General Manager, approved 5-26-96. Drawing No. S-92.2. Page number 93.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Specification. Design: American Association of State Highway Officials (A.A.S.H.O.) Specifications for the Design and Construction of Supports for Highway Signs, dated 1968. Loading: wind, normal to sign face: 23.3 pounds per square foot (psf); transverse to face: 0.2 of normal force. Unit stresses: concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3000 pounds per square inch (psi), fc = 1200 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 24,000 psi; structural steel fb = 23,000 psi; soil bearing pressure = 23,000 psf.</p>
<p>Callouts that refer to notes: Dimension B (See Notes) and Sign (See Notes). The numbered notes are not on this sheet and continue on sheet 2 of 2.</p>
<p>Detail note: See &quot;Weld Detail&quot; (Detail C plan view).</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 101)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0101.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0101.webp" alt="S-92.2 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-92.2, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p101">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Detail &quot;D&quot; (base of post and foundation top, not to scale): Section through the post base and top of the foundation. The post has a 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover (1&quot; radius corners in hand hole), located with dimensions 10&quot; and 5&quot; above the base plate and 2&quot; at the walk. The base plate sits on mortar at the top of walk, with see &quot;Weld Detail&quot;, Bar 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; backing ring and a 3/8&quot; dia. drain hole called out. A 1&quot; +/- dimension is given above the plate for the anchor bolt thread projection. ASTM A-307 anchor bolts 2&quot; dia. x 32&quot; long with nuts and washers, 4 req&#x27;d, run down through the concrete foundation 560-C-3250, with a bonding wire, conduit, and bars 5&quot; x 1&quot; x 22&quot; long at the bolt bottoms, parallel to the axis of sign (see Detail &quot;E&quot;), tack welded, 2-1/2&quot; Typ. edge distance. Overall height from the bottom of the bar to the top of the anchor bolt is 2&#x27;-8&quot;. Section cut markers A point toward Section A-A.</p>
<p>Detail &quot;E&quot; (anchor bar, not to scale): Plan of the bar 5&quot; x 1&quot; x 22&quot; long, 2 req&#x27;d, with chamfered ends and two 2-1/8&quot; dia. holes. Dimensions: 22&quot; overall length, 2&quot; Typ. and 2-1/2&quot; Typ. edge distances, 5&quot; width, 2-1/2&quot; Typ. from the bar edge to hole centerline.</p>
<p>Section A-A (not to scale): Plan of the 3&#x27;-0&quot; cast in drilled hole foundation with 3&quot; clear to the reinforcing. Shown are 12-#8 x 14-1/2&#x27; long bars equally spaced, #4 spiral @ 3-1/4&quot; pitch, a Base Plate PL 2&quot; x 23&quot; x 23&quot; long with 3&quot; dimension to the edge of the 17&quot; bolt pattern, the post centered on the sign centerline (CL Sign), a 3&quot; dia. hole finish smooth and a 2-1/8&quot; dia. hole. Dimensions: 23&quot; plate width, 17&quot; bolt spacing in both directions, 3&quot; edge distances.</p>
<p>Weld Detail (not to scale): The post pipe wall welded to the base plate on a backing ring, with a tack weld on the ring and a fillet weld shown with 1/8 and 3/8 weld legs and a 3/8&quot; dimension.</p>
<p>Title block: 34&#x27; Cantilever Sign Standard and Foundation Details, sheet 2 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Robert R. Yates, General Manager, approved 5-26-96. Drawing No. S-92.2. Page number 94.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Total sign area shall not exceed 180 sq. ft.</p>
<p>2. Length &quot;B&quot; shall not exceed 22&#x27; with maximum sign area.</p>
<p>3. See plan for positioning of panel.</p>
<p>4. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>5. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B specification.</p>
<p>6. Welding shall be in conformance with AWS D2.0 specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1969.</p>
<p>7. Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</p>
<p>8. Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>9. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except where otherwise shown.</p>
<p>Detail callouts: Foundation concrete is 560-C-3250. 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover, 1&quot; radius corners in hand hole.</p></details>
