<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 2 of the 34 foot cantilever sign standard shows the base plate, anchor bolts, drilled hole foundation and weld details, plus general notes. Total sign area may not exceed 180 sq ft, and length B may not exceed 22 feet at maximum sign area. A second sheet in this unit (S-45.0) shows how laminated overhead sign panels mount to mast arms with I-beams and U-bolts, with a table of maximum sign sizes and U-bolt sizes by mast arm length.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Detail D: 3&quot;x5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover, 1&quot; radius corners. Post. Weld detail. 1&quot;x1/4&quot; backing ring. 3/8&quot; dia. drain hole. Top of walk, mortar, base plate, bonding wire, concrete foundation 560-C-3250, conduit. ASTM A-307 anchor bolts 2&quot; dia. x 32&quot; long with nuts and washers, 4 req&#x27;d. Bar 5&quot;x1&quot;x22&quot; long parallel to axis of sign. Overall height 2&#x27;-8&quot;.</p>
<p>Detail E: bar 5&quot;x1&quot;x22&quot; long, 2 req&#x27;d, 2 1/8&quot; dia. holes, 2 1/2&quot; typ.</p>
<p>Section A-A: base plate PL 2&quot;x23&quot;x23&quot; long, 17&quot; bolt spacing, 3&quot; edge distances, 12-#8x14 1/2&quot; long equally spaced, 3&quot; dia. hole finish smooth, 2 1/8&quot; dia. hole, #4 spiral @ 3 1/4&quot; pitch, 3&quot; clear, 3&#x27;-0&quot; cast in drilled hole foundation.</p>
<p>Weld detail: backing ring, tack weld, 3/8&quot; and 1/8&quot; welds.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Total sign area shall not exceed 180 sq. ft.</p>
<p>2. Length &quot;B&quot; shall not exceed 22&#x27; with maximum sign area.</p>
<p>3. See plan for positioning of panel.</p>
<p>4. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>5. All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B specification.</p>
<p>6. Welding shall be in conformance with AWS D2.0 specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1969.</p>
<p>7. Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</p>
<p>8. Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</p>
<p>9. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except where otherwise shown.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 102)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0102.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0102.webp" alt="S-92.2 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-92.2, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p102">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Laminated overhead sign mounting system (S-45.0)</p>
<p>Mast arm length / maximum sign dimensions A x B / positioning dimensions C, D, E / U-bolt inside dia. X, length Y:</p>
<p>12&#x27;: A 14&#x27;, B 5&#x27;, C 3&#x27; max., D 2.5&#x27;, E 3&#x27; max., X 6 5/8&quot;, Y 24&quot;.</p>
<p>22&#x27;: A 18&#x27;, B 5&#x27;, C 3&#x27; max., D 2.5&#x27;, E 3&#x27; max., X 8 5/8&quot;, Y 28&quot;.</p>
<p>34&#x27; (two arms): A 30&#x27;, B 6&#x27;, C 3&#x27; max., D 1.5&#x27;, E 3&#x27; max., X 10 1/4&quot;, Y 34&quot;.</p>
<p>I-beams spaced 6&#x27; max. Sections A-A, B-B, C-C and mounting hardware shown.</p>
<h5>Parts list:</h5>
<p>1. Type &quot;A&quot; laminated sign panel per LADOT specification 82-052-01.</p>
<p>2. I-beam 6&quot; x 3.33&quot;, 4.3 lbs/linear ft, 6061-T6 aluminum alloy. 1 per panel.</p>
<p>3. Mast arm per LADOT Std. Dwg. Nos. S-92.2, S-92.3 or S-98.0.</p>
<p>4. 1/2&quot;-13 UNC-2A stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized U-bolt (4&quot; thread) with hex nut and tapered washer (ASTM A307). 2 per I-beam.</p>
<p>5. 1/4&quot; x 2&quot; x 6&quot; aluminum straps for each panel closure per LADOT specification 82-052-01. 4 per closure.</p>
<p>6. Aluminum mounting clamp. 4 per I-beam.</p>
<p>7. 3/8&quot; x 1.750&quot; (18-8) stainless steel post clip bolt (16 threads) with hex head nylon insert stop nut and flat washer. 4 per I-beam.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Overhead sign panels shall be Type &quot;A&quot; laminated sign panel per LADOT Spec. 82-052-01.</p>
<p>2. See LADOT Std. Dwg. Nos. S-92.2, S-92.3, or S-98.0 for dimensions, spacings, quantities, and other requirements dependent on mast arm specifications.</p>
<p>3. All bolts, nuts and washers shall be stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized in accordance with Section 210-3, Std. Spec. for Public Works Construction (1985 edition).</p>
<p>4. Guide signs shall have a white legend and border made of FP92 Type III (high intensity) reflective material on a green background made of FP92 Type II (super-engineering grade) reflective material.</p>
<p>5. Regulatory signs shall have a black (Scotch-Cal) legend and border, on a white background made of FP92 Type IV (VIP grade) reflective material.</p></details>
