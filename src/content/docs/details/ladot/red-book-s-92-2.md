<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Specification. Design: American Association of State Highway Officials (A.A.S.H.O.) Specifications for the Design and Construction of Supports for Highway Signs, dated 1968. Loading: wind, normal to sign face: 23.3 pounds per square foot (psf); transverse to face: 0.2 of normal force. Unit stresses: concrete n=10, f&#x27;c = 3000 pounds per square inch (psi), fc = 1200 psi; reinforcing steel fs = 24,000 psi; structural steel fb = 23,000 psi; soil bearing pressure = 23,000 psf.</p>
<p>Callouts that refer to notes: Dimension B (See Notes) and Sign (See Notes). The numbered notes are not on this sheet and continue on sheet 2 of 2.</p>
<p>Detail note: See &quot;Weld Detail&quot; (Detail C plan view).</p>
</details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (page 101)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0101.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0101.webp" alt="S-92.2 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-92.2, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p101">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">General notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>Total sign area shall not exceed 180 sq.</strong> ft.</li>
<li value="2">Length &quot;B&quot; shall not exceed 22&#x27; with maximum sign area.</li>
<li value="3">See plan for positioning of panel.</li>
<li value="4">All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</li>
<li value="5">All pipes shall conform to ASTM A53 Grade B specification.</li>
<li value="6">Welding shall be in conformance with AWS D2.0 specifications &quot;Welded Highway &amp; Railway Bridges&quot;, dated 1969.</li>
<li value="7">Tapered tube of equivalent size and capacity may be substituted for pipe post subject to the approval of the Engineer.</li>
<li value="8">Sign arms shall be made level by raking the standard with the leveling nuts or other method approved by the Engineer to compensate for dead load deflection.</li>
<li value="9">All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except where otherwise shown.</li>
</ol>
<p>Detail callouts: Foundation concrete is 560-C-3250. 3&quot; x 5&quot; hand hole with frame and removable cover, 1&quot; radius corners in hand hole.</p>
</details>
