<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.4A, sheet 1, shows detector loops for light rail trains. For existing embedded track a 30 in octagonal saw cut loop with 4 turns is used. For new ballasted track a preformed 30 in circular loop in a 40 in by 80 in concrete slab is used. Both connect through a type A detector handhole and 2 in Schedule 80 PVC conduit 30 in deep to a termination pullbox. Saw cut loops are for existing installations only.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 74)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-70.4A, Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 1 of 3. It has two installations, with sections.</p>
<p>LRT detector for existing track (embedded), plan: two parallel embedded tracks run vertically. Between them is an octagonal saw cut loop 30&quot; across, marked 4-turns of wire, with detector wires installed in saw cut (5&quot; deep). A lead-in runs 36&quot; from the loop down to a detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;, then horizontally to a termination pullbox with two conduits leaving it. Section C-C is cut through the loop, and section D-D is looked at from the handhole.</p>
<p>Section C-C: a saw cut slot filled with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of top (do not overfill). The slot is 5&quot; max. deep with the wires in the lower part at least 3&quot; min. below the surface, and 1/2&quot; max. wide. Four wires are shown stacked at the bottom.</p>
<p>Section D-D, embedded track: layers P.C.C. (concrete), A.B. (aggregate base) and subbase, with subballast bed, P.C.C. or asphalt concrete surface, embedded track rails, the saw cut loop (5&quot; max. below surface) and the detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;. A 2&quot; PVC Sched. 80 conduit, 30&quot; deep, runs from the handhole to the termination pull box, with 30&quot; min. cover.</p>
<p>LRT detector for new track (ballasted), plan: a rectangular concrete slab 40&quot; wide by 80&quot; long between two train tracks, crossing concrete track ties. A circular preformed loop 30&quot; in diameter (4-turns of wire, pre-formed loop) sits in the slab, with a lead 36&quot; long to the detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27; at the slab edge. Another run goes to a termination pullbox with two conduits. Reference: See Detail &#x27;A&#x27;. Section B-B cut.</p>
<p>Section B-B, ballasted track: preformed loop in a slab at the rail, over concrete track tie and ballasted or concrete track bed. 2&quot; PVC Sched. 80 conduit, 30&quot; deep, from the detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27; to the termination pull box (30&quot; min.).</p>
<p>Detail &#x27;A&#x27;, ballasted track: the slab with the loop inside, top of slab even with rail, 2&quot; from the top of slab to the loop, and slab depth varies.</p>
<p>Title block: Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 1/3, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 8-8-2014, Jon Kirk Mukri, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.4A. Drawn MT 8-7-14, T.E. BC 8-7-14, Pr. T.E. SS 8-7-14.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Saw cut loops (octagonal or circular) to be used for existing installations only.</p>
<p>2. See LADOT Std. Dwg. S-70.1.A for inductive loop installation details not shown hereon.</p>
<p>(Notes for the new track (ballasted) installation)</p>
<p>1. Preformed loop to be used for all new construction.</p>
<p>2. See current LADOT material specification for traffic loop.</p>
<p>3. See page 2 and 3 for preformed LRT loop details.</p>
<p>Callouts: 4-turns of wire. Embedded track. Install detector wires in saw cut (5&quot; deep). Fill slot with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of top (do not overfill). Termination pullbox. Conduit. Detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;.</p></details>
