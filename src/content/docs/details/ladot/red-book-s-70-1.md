<p class="dwg-sum">Details for detector loops placed in light rail track, for existing embedded track (saw cut loops) and new ballasted track (preformed loops). Shows sections, 4 turns of wire, 5 inch deep saw cut, 2 inch PVC Schedule 80 conduit 30 inches deep, and the detector handhole and termination pull box. Saw cut loops are for existing embedded track only, and preformed loops are used for all new construction.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 74)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>LRT detector for existing track (embedded)</p>
<p>4 turns of wire. Install detector wires in saw cut (5&quot; deep). Fill slot with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of top (do not overfill). 5&quot; max. 2&quot; PVC Schedule 80 conduit 30&quot; deep to termination pull box. Sections C-C and D-D show embedded track, P.C.C. or asphalt concrete, subbase, subballast bed and detector handhole.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Saw cut loops (octagonal or circular) to be used for existing embedded track installations only. See LADOT Std. Dwg. S-70.1.A for inductive loop installation details not shown hereon.</p>
<p>LRT detector for new track (ballasted)</p>
<p>4 turns of wire in a preformed loop. Top of slab even with rail. Detail A, section B-B: ballasted or concrete track bed, concrete track tie, detector handhole, termination pull box, 2&quot; PVC Schedule 80 conduit 30&quot; deep.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Preformed loop to be used for all new construction.</p>
<p>2. See current LADOT material specification for traffic loop.</p>
<p>3. See page 2 and 8 for preformed LRT loop details.</p></details>
