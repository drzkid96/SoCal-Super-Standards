<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Supplemental Design Guide Detail 100, sheet 1 of 2, shows two plan views of a channelized right turn at a right angle approach: a standard geometry and a constrained geometry with a narrow sidewalk (9 ft or less). Key values are a 55 to 70 degree island approach angle, a 20 ft minimum island length, an 11 to 14 ft turn lane, a 25 to 40 ft corner radius and a 150 to 275 ft turn lane radius. Pedestrian landings are 48 in with 2% maximum slope and curb ramps follow S-442.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 101)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Upper plan view (standard geometry, no view title printed): A triangular raised island with integral curb and gutter and 2 ft radius (R=2&#x27;, TYP) corners separates the right turn lane from the through lanes on the left. A marked crosswalk crosses the through lanes at the left and another crosses the turn lane at the right. A pedestrian cut-through passes through the island, with a 48 in landing (typical, at top of all curb ramps, see Note 2) and 2% MAX slope arrows at the landing and cut-through. Dimensions on the island: 2&#x27; MIN. offset between the crosswalk and the island, 5&#x27; MIN. island width at the top, a 5&#x27; MIN. cut-through dimension, and 4&#x27; MIN on both sides (path width each side). A callout reads: if more than 9 ft wide, install bollard(s) in the detectable warning surface (DWS) at 2 ft maximum from the flow line, allowing a minimum of 4 ft path in between bollards. The island edge along the turn lane is 20&#x27; MIN. long and meets the approach at 55 to 70 degrees. The turn lane is 11&#x27; MIN., 14&#x27; MAX. wide (see Note 1). A 2% TYP, 5% MAX label marks the cross slope on the turn lane side. A unidirectional curb ramp without flares (TYP, see S-442) serves the island and a parallel curb ramp (see S-442) serves the corner. The outside curb has integral curb and gutter and a corner radius R=25&#x27; to 40&#x27; (see corner radius policy). The turn lane curb has integral curb and gutter at R=150&#x27; to 275&#x27;. Pavement arrows show the right turn and through movements.</p>
<p>Lower plan view, titled &#x27;Constrained Geometric Design for Channelized Right Turns&#x27; with the title block line &#x27;Right Angle Approach&#x27;: Same layout at a corner with a narrow sidewalk (9 ft or less, labeled &#x27;Narrow sidewalk (&lt;=9&#x27;)&#x27;). The island is a narrow triangle with integral curb and gutter and R=2&#x27;, TYP, 48 in landing (typical at top of all curb ramps, see Note 2), a curb ramp (TYP, see S-442) at the island, and 2&#x27; MIN. offsets at the crosswalks. The island edge is 20&#x27; MIN. long at 55 to 70 degrees to the approach. The turn lane is 11&#x27; MIN., 14&#x27; MAX. (see Note 1) with 2% TYP and 5% MAX cross slope noted. A parallel curb ramp (see S-442) is shown at the corner on the narrow sidewalk. Curbs are integral curb and gutter at R=150&#x27; to 275&#x27; on the turn lane side. Arrows show a left turn on the through street and a right turn on the slip lane.</p>
<p>Title block: SUPPLEMENTAL DESIGN GUIDE, Detail 100, Sheet 1 of 2. A file stamp along the left margin gives the source drawing name &#x27;Right Turn Slip Lane - BOE2.dwg&#x27; dated 4/2/2020.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Provide mountable truck apron along outside curb line if lane width greater than 14&#x27; is needed to accommodate design vehicle.</p>
<p>2. Provide 48&quot; minimum (MIN.) clear space with 2% maximum (MAX.) slope in all directions for landing area.</p>
<p>Drawing labels: 48&quot; landing, typical (TYP.) at top of all curb ramps, see Note 2. If greater than 9&#x27; wide, install bollard(s) in detectable warning surface (DWS) at 2&#x27; maximum from the flow line, allowing a minimum of 4&#x27; path in between bollards. Integral curb and gutter. R=2&#x27;, TYP. R=25&#x27; to 40&#x27;, see corner radius policy. Parallel curb ramp, see S-442. Unidirectional curb ramp without flares, TYP., see S-442. 11&#x27; MIN., 14&#x27; MAX., see Note 1. R=150&#x27; to 275&#x27;. 55 degrees to 70 degrees. 20&#x27; MIN. 4&#x27; MIN on both sides. 2% MAX. 2% TYP, 5% MAX. Narrow sidewalk (&lt;=9&#x27;).</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 102)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0102.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0102.webp" alt="Detail 100 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide Detail 100, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/06-pages-91-108/#p102">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, titled &#x27;Unconstrained Geometric Design for Channelized Right Turns&#x27;: A bike lane crossing and a pedestrian crossing are shown side by side across the top leg, with a 3&#x27; MIN separation between them. At the left a crosswalk crosses the through lanes. A pedestrian and bicyclist island sits between the through lanes and the turn lane, with detectable warning surface (DWS) at the crossings, 6&#x27; MIN. island width, 4&#x27; MIN on both sides, and section cut A (sheet 2) through the island at the top crossing. A non-walkable surface, raised divisional island or curbing (TYP) edges the island with 5&#x27; MIN and 8&#x27; MIN dimensions at the island ends, R=5&#x27; MIN at a corner and R=10&#x27; MIN at the sidewalk side corner. Unidirectional curb ramps without flares (TYP, see S-442) serve the crossings, with a 4&#x27; MIN dimension at the corner island. Turning geometry: 11&#x27; MIN., 14&#x27; MAX. turn lane (see Note 1), integral curb and gutter R=2&#x27; TYP at the tip of the island, a 55 to 70 degree island approach angle, a 20&#x27; MIN. island length, outer corner radius R=25&#x27; to 40&#x27; (see corner radius policy), and 150&#x27; to 275&#x27; radius on the turn lane curb. Beyond the turn lane the plan shows, from the road outward, a grass buffer, a bike lane and a sidewalk. An optional raised crosswalk and bike crossing is called out as &#x27;see Supplemental Design Guide Detail 600&#x27;.</p>
<p>Section A, &#x27;Typical Section A at Pedestrian/Bicyclist Island&#x27;, subtitle &#x27;Steep Road Cross Slopes&#x27; (cut A on sheet 2): Left to right: repair or reconstruct street paving as necessary, 2&#x27; MIN, TYP, sloping to integral curb and gutter TYP; a 6&#x27; MIN. non-walkable surface (hatched) bounded by a curb; an 8&#x27; MIN. bike lane between a battered curb without gutter (TYP, see Supplemental Design Guide Detail 400) and a second battered curb; a 3&#x27; non-walkable surface (hatched) between battered curbs; then a 3&#x27; warning curb; a sidewalk curb ramp; a variable width island; and an integral curb and gutter beside the right turn lane with a break line.</p>
<p>Title block: SUPPLEMENTAL DESIGN GUIDE, Detail 100, Sheet 2 of 2. File stamp: Right Turn Slip Lane - BOE2.dwg, 4/2/2020.</p></details>
