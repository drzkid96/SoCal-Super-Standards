<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Cast-in-drilled-hole concrete foundation for signal and lighting standards. Shows a 30 inch diameter shaft with a reinforcing cage and four anchor bolts on an 11 1/2 inch bolt circle. Depth is 5 feet where at least 100 sq ft of concrete sidewalk is adjacent to the foundation and 7 feet 3 inches without it.</p>
<p class="dwg-topics">Topics: <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/signals/">Traffic signals and pedestrian signals</a></p>



## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 88)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0088.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0088.webp" alt="S-52.2.2 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.2.2, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p88">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>30&quot; dia. shaft, 11 1/2&quot; bolt circle, 8 1/8&quot; sq. base plate area, 2&quot; clearance.</p>
<p>Reinforcing cage: 26&quot; dia. (O.D.). Total 12 #7 vertical bars, #4 @ 6&quot; or #4 spiral @ 6&quot; pitch with 1&#x27;-6&quot; lap at splice. Overall height 4&#x27;-6&quot; or 6&#x27;-9&quot;.</p>
<p>Concrete 560-C-3250. Bonding strap or wire.</p>
<p>Anchor bolts 18&quot; x 40&quot; x 4&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt).</p>
<p>Depth: 5&#x27; with 100 sq-ft (minimum) concrete sidewalk adjacent to foundation. 7&#x27;-3&quot; without 100 sq-ft (minimum) concrete sidewalk adjacent to foundation.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Use 1&quot; galvanized conduit for street lighting and 2&quot; schedule 80 PVC conduit for traffic signal unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Conduit stubs from foundation shall be in the direction of the nearest corresponding pullbox unless otherwise specified.</p></details>
