<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-52.2.2 showing the cast-in-drilled-hole Type F-2 foundation, a 30 in. diameter shaft of 560-C-3250 concrete with a 26 in. diameter reinforcing cage of twelve #7 bars and #4 spiral at 6 in. pitch. Shaft depth is 5 ft with at least 100 sq-ft of adjacent concrete sidewalk, or 7 ft 3 in. without it. Four 1 1/8 in. x 40 in. anchor bolts sit on an 11 1/2 in. bolt circle with a bonding strap and conduit stubs.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 88)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0088.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0088.webp" alt="S-52.2.2 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.2.2, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p88">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Top view: A circular drilled shaft 30&quot; diameter with a 2&quot; clearance ring to the reinforcing. A bold circle shows the reinforcing cage with 12 longitudinal bars (#7, total 12) and a spiral or hoop of #4 bars. An 8 1/8&quot; square anchor bolt pattern lies on an 11 1/2&quot; bolt circle with diagonals at 45 degrees. Conduit stubs, shown dashed, run to the right and bottom. A 36&quot; dimension runs from the left side of the section to the centerline.</p>
<p>Section: A cast-in-drilled-hole (CIDH) concrete shaft of 560-C-3250 concrete with a curb and sidewalk at the left. A reinforcing cage 26&quot; diameter (O.D.) and overall height 4&#x27;-6&quot; or 6&#x27;-9&quot; has #7 vertical bars and #4 spiral ties. Four anchor bolts 1 1/8&quot; x 40&quot; x 4&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt) are set inside the cage with a bonding strap or wire. A conduit with bushing rises 2 3/4&quot; clear above the topping. Dimensions: 4&quot; topping, 3&quot; clear above the cage, 18&quot; MIN. to the top of the conduit stub, and 3&quot; clear at the bottom. Shaft depth is 5&#x27; with 100 sq-ft (minimum) concrete sidewalk adjacent to the foundation, or 7&#x27;-3&quot; without 100 sq-ft (minimum) concrete sidewalk adjacent to the foundation.</p>
<p>Title block: CIDH Foundation, Type F-2, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-52.2.2. Drawn MT 11-8-04, T.E. JV 6-1-06, Sr. T.E. JW 6-1-06, Pr. T.E. SS 6-2-06. Approved June 2, 2006, Gloria J. Jeff, General Manager. Printed page number 81.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Use 1&quot; galvanized conduit for street lighting and 2&quot; schedule 80 PVC conduit for traffic signal unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Conduit stubs from foundation shall be in the direction of the nearest corresponding pullbox unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>Callouts: 30&quot; dia. 8 1/8&quot; square. 2&quot; clearance. #7 total 12. #4 at 6&quot; or #4 spiral at 6&quot; pitch with 1&#x27;-6&quot; lap at splice. 11 1/2&quot; bolt circle. Bonding strap or wire. Bushing. 560-C-3250 concrete. Anchor bolts 1 1/8&quot; x 40&quot; x 4&quot; (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt). Reinforcing cage 26&quot; dia. (O.D.), overall height 4&#x27;-6&quot; or 6&#x27;-9&quot;. 5&#x27; with 100 sq-ft (minimum) concrete sidewalk adjacent to foundation. 7&#x27;-3&quot; without 100 sq-ft (minimum) concrete sidewalk adjacent to foundation. 3&quot; clear. 2 3/4&quot; clear.</p></details>
