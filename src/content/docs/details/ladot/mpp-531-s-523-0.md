<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan and elevation layouts for Type II bus bulbs where a Class IV bike lane passes behind the bus stop. Sheets show a floating bus bulb with an integrated bike lane, a constrained bulb with a raised bike lane and a narrow boarding area, and a shelter and handrail placement guide. A table of LADOT, DASH and Metro bus series gives door spacing and minimum bulb lengths from 21 ft to 30 ft, and boarding areas of 5' x 8' minimum at the accessible door with a 12' x 4' or 5' x 8' clear zone at the other door.</p>
