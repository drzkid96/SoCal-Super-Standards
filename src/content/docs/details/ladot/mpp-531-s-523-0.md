<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">First of four Bus Bulbs sheets. Two plan views of a Type II floating bus bulb with a Class IV bike lane running behind the bus stop and integrated with the street crossing, one with the bike lane crossing the street at a marked crossing and one with a bicycle ramp marked by Chevron IV. A section shows shelter and handrail placement with an accessible route of 4 ft minimum (5 ft preferred). Bulbs have a forward boarding area of 5' x 8' minimum. Approved 02-20-2020.</p>
