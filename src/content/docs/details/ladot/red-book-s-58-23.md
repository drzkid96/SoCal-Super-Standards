<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Messages shall be formed by rows of fiber optic glass bundles.</li>
<li value="2">Dual rows of fiber optic bundles shall be used to form the word &quot;TRAIN&quot;. Letters in the bus warning sign message shall have a brush stroke of 1-inch. Bus symbol and letters shall also conform to the latest edition of the FHWA Standard Highway Signs book.</li>
<li value="3">The front view of the train or bus shall be solid formed by rows of fiber optic glass bundles.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>The color of the messages shall be yellow.</strong> The sign background shall be black.</li>
<li value="5">Fiber optic glass bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources. In the event of a lamp failure, the sign shall continue to display a readable and discernable message.</li>
<li value="6">Sign housing shall be equipped with four (4) 3/16&quot; drain holes at the bottom.</li>
<li value="7">The visor enclosure shall be fastened securely to the front frame of the sign.</li>
<li value="8">Housing shall contain 4 1/2&quot; x 4 1/2&quot; x 1/4&quot; reinforcement plate located on the top and bottom for use with the Pelco Astro Sign-Brac installation.</li>
<li value="9"><strong>Refer to Std.</strong> Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for the mounting assembly (T=46&quot;, L=29&quot;).</li>
<li value="10"><strong>Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No.</strong> 82-049-03.</li>
<li value="11"><strong>For housing description see LADOT Std.</strong> Dwg. No. S-58.21.</li>
</ol>
<p>Table footnote: * Five 1&quot; spaces to delineate the rail road track.</p>
</details>
