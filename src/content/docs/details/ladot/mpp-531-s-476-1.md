</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. See Detail for square tube sign post installation.</p>
<p>2. Sign stiffener system and hardware per standard plan S-476.0.</p>
<p>3. Signs greater than 32 sq. ft. should be placed on a cantilever standard.</p>
<p>4. Sign may not extend beyond property line.</p>
<p>5. Large roadside mountings on electroliers are permitted only when the poles are steel and embedded in concrete sidewalks.</p>
<p>View callouts: Signs to 10 sq. ft.: center sign on pole, 15&#x27; above the sidewalk. For signs 42&quot; and longer, secure to street light with heavy duty 2-bolt &quot;D&quot; clip in each stiffener channel. Signs 10 to 18 sq. ft. and 18 to 32 sq. ft.: secure to post with square head bolt in each stiffener channel. Detail: 2 1/2&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; x 14&#x27;, 10 gauge post. Insert post 6&quot; to 8&quot; into the anchor. 3&quot; x 3&quot; x 30&quot;, 7 gauge non-perforated anchor base (2 drilled holes at the top).</p></details>
