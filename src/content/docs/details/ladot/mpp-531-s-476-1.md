<p class="dwg-sum">Shows mounting of large roadside signs by area: signs to 10 sq ft on a street light pole centered 15' above the sidewalk, signs 10 to 18 sq ft on one post, and signs 18 to 32 sq ft on two posts 4' minimum apart. Signs are set 18" back from the curb with 7' bottom clearance and 4' minimum from the property line. Posts are 2 1/2" x 2 1/2" x 14', 10 gauge, set in a 3" x 3" x 30" 7 gauge anchor base. Signs over 32 sq ft go on a cantilever standard.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Signs to 10 sq ft: center sign on pole, 15&#x27; above the sidewalk. For signs 42&quot; and longer secure to street light with heavy duty 2-bolt &quot;D&quot; clip in each stiffener channel.</p>
<p>Signs 10 to 18 sq ft: secure to post with square head bolt in each stiffener channel. 18&quot; set back, 2&#x27; to the post, 6&#x27; maximum sign width, 7&#x27; bottom clearance, 4&#x27; minimum to the property line. See Note 1.</p>
<p>Signs 18 to 32 sq ft: two posts, 4&#x27; minimum apart, 18&quot; set back, 7&#x27; bottom clearance, secure with square head bolt in each stiffener channel. See Note 1.</p>
<p>Detail: 2 1/2&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; x 14&#x27;, 10 gauge post. Insert post 6&quot; to 8&quot; into the anchor. 3&quot; x 3&quot; x 30&quot;, 7 gauge non-perforated anchor base (2 drilled holes at the top).</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. See Detail for square tube sign post installation.</p>
<p>2. Sign stiffener system and hardware per standard plan S-476.0.</p>
<p>3. Signs greater than 32 sq ft should be placed on a cantilever standard.</p>
<p>4. Sign may not extend beyond property line.</p>
<p>5. Large roadside mountings on electroliers are permitted only when the poles are steel and embedded in concrete sidewalks.</p></details>
