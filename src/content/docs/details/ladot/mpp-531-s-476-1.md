## Sheet

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0107.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0107.webp" alt="S-476.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-476.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/08-drawings-s-456-to-s-483-1/#p107">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">See Detail for square tube sign post installation.</li>
<li value="2">Sign stiffener system and hardware per standard plan S-476.0.</li>
<li value="3"><strong>Signs greater than 32 sq.</strong> ft. should be placed on a cantilever standard.</li>
<li value="4">Sign may not extend beyond property line.</li>
<li value="5">Large roadside mountings on electroliers are permitted only when the poles are steel and embedded in concrete sidewalks.</li>
</ol>
<p>View callouts: Signs to 10 sq. ft.: center sign on pole, 15&#x27; above the sidewalk. For signs 42&quot; and longer, secure to street light with heavy duty 2-bolt &quot;D&quot; clip in each stiffener channel. Signs 10 to 18 sq. ft. and 18 to 32 sq. ft.: secure to post with square head bolt in each stiffener channel. Detail: 2 1/2&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; x 14&#x27;, 10 gauge post. Insert post 6&quot; to 8&quot; into the anchor. 3&quot; x 3&quot; x 30&quot;, 7 gauge non-perforated anchor base (2 drilled holes at the top).</p>
</details>
