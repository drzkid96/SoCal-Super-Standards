<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-52.7.1 showing the 10 1/2 in. square, 3/8 in. thick steel adapter base plate used to mount a pedestrian push button (PPB) post on an F-1 to F-7 foundation. An exploded isometric shows the post, plate, washers, nuts, conduit and foundation, and a plan shows the slotted corners on a 9 1/2 in. circle. The plate is hot dipped galvanized after machining per ASTM A-123.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 90)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0090.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0090.webp" alt="S-52.7.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.7.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p90">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Exploded isometric: Shows how a pedestrian push button (PPB) post is mounted on an existing F-1 to F-7 foundation using the adapter plate. From top to bottom: the PPB post with its own square base plate bolted by four studs with nuts and washers, the adapter plate with four slotted corner ears fastened to the foundation anchor bolts (two washers each anchor bolt, with a nut), the conduit stub rising from the foundation, and the foundation block below. Callouts: PPB Post. Two washers each anchor bolt. Adapter plate. Conduit. Foundation (spelled &#x27;Fountation&#x27; on the sheet).</p>
<p>Side view of plate: The adapter plate is a flat 3/8&quot; steel plate with two 5/8&quot; x 1 3/4&quot; square or hex head galvanized bolts projecting up from it, with nuts below. A 1/4&quot; fillet weld is shown at each of the two bolts, noted &#x27;Fillet weld at least two opposite flats of each bolt&#x27;.</p>
<p>Plan view of plate: The plate is a 10 1/2&quot; x 10 1/2&quot; square with four slotted corner ears on a 9 1/2&quot; diameter bolt circle, a 4 1/2&quot; diameter circle through the four post bolt holes and a 3&quot; diameter center opening. A 1 3/8&quot; slot width is shown at the upper right ear.</p>
<p>Title block: Adapter Base Plate (For F-1 to F-7 Foundation). City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Donald R. Howery, General Manager. Approved May 27, 1971, B. Fredrickson for General Manager. Drawing No. S-52.7.1. Spec. No. 92-056-01. Printed page number 83.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Note:</h5>
<p>Base plate shall be hot dipped galvanized after machining in accordance with the latest edition of ASTM designation A-123.</p>
<p>Spec. No. 92-056-01.</p>
<p>Callouts: PPB (pedestrian push button) post. Two washers each anchor bolt. Adapter plate. Conduit. Foundation. 5/8&quot; x 1 3/4&quot; square or hex head galvanized bolts. 3/8&quot; steel plate. 1/4&quot; fillet weld at least two opposite flats of each bolt. 10 1/2&quot; x 10 1/2&quot;. 4 1/2&quot; dia. 9 1/2&quot; dia. 3&quot; dia. 1 3/8&quot;.</p></details>
