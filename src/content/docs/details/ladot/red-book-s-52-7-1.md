<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Note:</h5>
<p>Base plate shall be hot dipped galvanized after machining in accordance with the latest edition of ASTM designation A-123.</p>
<p>Spec. No. 92-056-01.</p>
<p>Callouts: PPB (pedestrian push button) post. Two washers each anchor bolt. Adapter plate. Conduit. Foundation. 5/8&quot; x 1 3/4&quot; square or hex head galvanized bolts. 3/8&quot; steel plate. 1/4&quot; fillet weld at least two opposite flats of each bolt. 10 1/2&quot; x 10 1/2&quot;. 4 1/2&quot; dia. 9 1/2&quot; dia. 3&quot; dia. 1 3/8&quot;.</p></details>
