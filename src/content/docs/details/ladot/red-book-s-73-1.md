<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-73.1 gives the layout of the actuated pedestrian pushbutton sign, a 5 in by 7 3/4 in .063 aluminum sign. It matches the non-actuated sign (S-73.0) and adds a bottom PUSH BUTTON TO CROSS panel with a left, right or double arrow. Orange hand and timer symbols sit on black squares, with four 1/4 in holes 1/2 in from the edges.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 66)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0066.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0066.webp" alt="S-73.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-73.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p66">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face, full size elevation: a portrait rectangular sign, 5&quot; wide by 7 3/4&quot; tall, with rounded corners and a double border. Legend is black on white with orange hand and timer symbols on black square backgrounds. The face has five panels from top to bottom: (1) a white walking person symbol on black, with START CROSSING underlined and Watch For Vehicles; (2) a flashing orange hand symbol with radiating rays (labeled FLASHING) with DON&#x27;T START underlined, Finish Crossing, If Started underlined; below it an orange two digit timer symbol 08 (labeled TIMER) with TIME REMAINING To Finish Crossing; (3) a steady orange hand symbol (labeled STEADY) with DON&#x27;T CROSS; (4) a bottom panel with PUSH BUTTON above a heavy horizontal arrow and TO CROSS below, with an asterisk at the arrow head (use left, right or double arrow as necessary). A 1/4&quot; inside diameter hole is shown at each corner, 4 places (typ.).</p>
<p>Dimensions: overall height 7 3/4&quot; with hole centers 6 3/4&quot; apart vertically and 1/2&quot; from the sign edge to each hole center. Overall width 5&quot; with hole centers 4&quot; apart horizontally and 1/2&quot; from the edge to each hole center. Outer corner radius R = 11/32&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: Pedestrian Pushbutton Sign, Actuated, sheet marker 1/1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 8-9-16 for Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager, Drawing No. S-73.1.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Sign shall be .063 aluminum.</p>
<p>2. Powder coated with black legend on white background and orange hand and timer symbols.</p>
<p>* Use left, right or double arrow as necessary.</p>
<p>(Callout) 1/4&quot; inside diameter, 4 places (typ.).</p></details>
