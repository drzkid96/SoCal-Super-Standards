<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-76.9 shows the standard full circle signal visors, 8 in and 12 in diameter, each 8 in or 12 in long with a 3.5 degree tilted back edge. The end views show closed rings with four ears at 90 degree spacing. Ear details are on S-76.3 and other details are in LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 61)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0061.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0061.webp" alt="S-76.9 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-76.9, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p61">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-76.9, Visor 8 in and 12 in diameter full circle (standard short visor). It shows two visors, each as a side view paired with an end view. All views are marked Not to Scale.</p>
<p>Visor, 8&quot; full circle, side view: a short rectangular tube 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; long. The back edge is tilted 3.5 degrees from vertical with two ear tabs. Dashed lines near top and bottom edges and a dash-dot centerline.</p>
<p>Visor, 8&quot; full circle, end view: a closed circular ring dimensioned 8&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and 90 degree callouts between ears. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Visor, 12&quot; full circle, side view: a tube 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; long with the same 3.5 degree tilted back edge, two ear tabs, dashed edges and centerline.</p>
<p>Visor, 12&quot; full circle, end view: a closed circular ring dimensioned 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter with four ears at the diagonals and 90 degree callouts. Leader: See Ear Detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3.</p>
<p>Title block: Visor 8&quot; &amp; 12&quot; Diameter Full Circle, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved January 26, 2011 for Amir Sedadi, Interim General Manager, Drawing No. S-76.9, page number 54.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. See ear detail on Std. Dwg. No. S-76.3 (shown for both the 8 in and the 12 in full circle visors).</p>
<p>2. Note: Refer to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06 for all other details.</p></details>
