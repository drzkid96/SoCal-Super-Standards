<p class="dwg-sum">Continuation of the LADOT MPP Section 531 table that says what each type of striping and pavement marking does and which MUTCD or California figure and detail governs it. Covers reversible lane markings, lane lines, barrier lines, edge and channelization lines, striped islands, lane drop (elephant track) lines, bike lane lines, cross-hatch, chevrons, hook and bar, and marked crosswalks. Key values include a 2 foot minimum internal separation for striped islands, bike lane lines broken at least 96' upstream of the intersection, and 12" crosswalk lines 10 or 15 feet apart with a 10 foot minimum width.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Table 1 - Application of Striping and Pavement Markings (Cont&#x27;d)</p>
<p>Los Angeles DOT Manual of Policies and Procedures, Section 531, Application for Striping, Channelization, and Special Signing. Date 05-20, page 16.</p>
<p>Reversible Lane Marking: Delineates a lane that is reversed in directional flow during various times of the day. Figure 3B-6.</p>
<p>Lane Line: Separates concurrent through lanes. Figure 3A-102 (CA), <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-8" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-8.svg">Detail 8</a>.</p>
<p>Barrier Line: Separates turn lanes from through lanes or other turn lanes. Figure 3A-112 (CA), <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38a.svg">Detail 38A</a>.</p>
<p>Right Edge Line: Delineates the right edge of roadway and is parallel to the edge of roadway. Figure 3A-106 (CA), <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a>.</p>
<p>Right Channelization Line: Delineates right edge of travel path where it is not parallel to the edge of roadway. Figure 3A-112 (CA), <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38a.svg">Detail 38A</a>.</p>
<p>Striped Island: Physically separates converging or diverging traffic flows and prohibits vehicular crossing. Internal separation must be at least 2 feet for enforcement. Figure 3B-8 (CA), 3B-9 (CA), 3B-12 and 3B-15.</p>
<p>Lane Drop Line or &quot;Elephant Track&quot;: Transition striping for an entrapped-turn lane or an advance turn lane. See S-491.0. Figure 3A-111 (CA), <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-37b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-111-ca-detail-37b.webp">Detail 37B</a> and Figure 3B-11 (CA).</p>
<p>Bike Lane Line: Separates the travel path between bicycles and motor vehicles. The Bike Lane Line is broken at least 96&#x27; upstream of the intersection in order to identify where right turning motor vehicles may cross the bicycle lane. Figure 3A-112 (CA) Details 39 and 39A, and Figure 9C-6.</p>
<p>Right Edge Cross-Hatch: Provides emphasis in striped islands or between the right edge of roadway and Right Channelization Line or Right Edge Line, at least 5 feet in width. Also clarifies that the area is not part of the traveled way. Aligned at 45 degrees diagonally forward with respect to the direction of travel. See S-444.0 and 485.0 (not shown).</p>
<p>Chevron: Provides emphasis within a striped island or a gore area at least 5 feet in width where concurrent traffic travels on either side. See S-493.0, Sheets 5 and 6 of 7. Figure 3B-12 and 3B-15.</p>
<p>Hook (Wrap) and Bar: Used at the downstream end of a Barrier Line for a Trap Turn Lane, where the adjacent lane is a through lane. Where the adjacent lane is a concurrent turn lane the Bar is not used. See S-491.0 and S-405.0. Figure 3B-13.</p>
<p>Crosswalk (Marked): Emphasizes the legal crosswalk at signalized intersections, indicates a preferred legal crosswalk at non-signalized intersections, and identifies a legal marked crosswalk where no legal unmarked crosswalk exists. Parallel lines, 12&quot; wide, generally 15 feet or 10 feet apart, with minimum width of 10 feet. See S-490.0 and S-493.0. Figure 3B-17.</p></details>
