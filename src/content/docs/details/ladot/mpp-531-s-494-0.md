<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Five cases of channelized right turn control. Stop sign (Case 1) is used where the curb return radius is under 20 ft, pedestrian traffic is high, or visibility is restricted. Yield (Cases 2 and 3), merge (Case 4) and added lane (Case 5) operation depend on the radius and the available acceleration and merging distance. Tables 1 to 3 give S1, D1 and D2 for the merge case, and the added lane case needs an all-day receiving lane of 1/2 mile or more.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Case 4: Merge operation</p>
<p>D = Minimum distance necessary for accelerating and merging.</p>
<p>D1 = Minimum distance necessary for accelerating from point x at the initial speed, S1, to point y at the speed limit, S2. See Tables 1 and 2. Consideration should be given to using a design speed greater than the speed limit where higher speeds are known to occur.</p>
<p>D2 = Minimum distance necessary for merging maneuver at speed S2. See Table 3.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1) Use merge operation where the available distance for acceleration and merging equals or exceeds &quot;D&quot;.</p>
<p>2) The length of lane line within D is discretionary.</p>
<p>3) Where it is desired to provide traffic signal control for pedestrians to cross the channelized right turn, the following shall apply:</p>
<p>a. Omit the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> sign; and</p>
<p>b. Omit ladder markings in crosswalk.</p>
<p>c. Provide pedestrian heads across the channelized right turn; and</p>
<p>d. Control the channelized right turn with three-arrow signal heads; and</p>
<p>e. Omit reference to Table 1; and</p>
<p>f. Measure D1 from diagonal crosswalk; and</p>
<p>g. S1 = 0 at crosswalk.</p>
<p>4) Install a second <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a> sign on the right side if the median mounted <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a> sign is not visible to merging traffic.</p>
<p>Signs: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> (fluorescent yellow-green), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-7p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-7P.svg">W16-7P</a>. 50&#x27; min. shown on plan.</p></details>
