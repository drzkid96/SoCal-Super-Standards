</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>CASE 1: STOP SIGN CONTROL. Note: Use Stop sign operation where the curb return radius is less than 20 feet (10 miles per hour design speed), pedestrian traffic across the channelized right turn is high, cross-traffic visibility is restricted, or where the use of a Yield sign has been shown to be inadequate.</p>
<p>CASE 2: YIELD SIGN CONTROL. Note: Use Yield sign operation where the curb return radius is 20 feet (10 miles per hour design speed) or greater and pedestrian traffic across the channelized right turn is low.</p>
<p>Callouts: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> (stop sign), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a> (yield sign), 2&#x27; white or yellow, 5&#x27; center to center (C-C), Radius.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 5 (page 135)

<figure class="dfig redraw"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/redraw/mpp-531/s-494-0-s2.svg"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/redraw/mpp-531/s-494-0-s2.svg" alt="S-494.0 sheet 2, redrawn"></a><figcaption>Redrawn reference diagram. Original LADOT sheet below.</figcaption></figure><details class="orig"><summary>Original LADOT sheet</summary><figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0135.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0135.webp" alt="S-494.0 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-494.0, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/10-drawings-s-494-0-to-s-497/#p135">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Use Yield sign operation where the available distance &quot;x&quot; for acceleration and merging is less than &quot;D&quot;, as determined by the tables in Case 4.</p>
<p>2. Installation of the Yield limit line is optional.</p>
<p>3. An optional added location for a crosswalk is where the Yield sign is shown near the end of the island. If added, stop sign control should be considered. If Yield sign control is retained then the crosswalk should have ladder markings as shown on sheet 1.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 5 (page 136)

<figure class="dfig redraw"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/redraw/mpp-531/s-494-0-s3.svg"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/redraw/mpp-531/s-494-0-s3.svg" alt="S-494.0 sheet 3, redrawn"></a><figcaption>Redrawn reference diagram. Original LADOT sheet below.</figcaption></figure><details class="orig"><summary>Original LADOT sheet</summary><figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0136.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0136.webp" alt="S-494.0 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-494.0, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/10-drawings-s-494-0-to-s-497/#p136">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Case 4: Merge Operation</p>
<p>D = Minimum distance necessary for accelerating and merging.</p>
<p>D1 = Minimum distance necessary for accelerating from point x at the initial speed, S1, to point y at the speed limit, S2. See Tables 1 &amp; 2. Consideration should be given to using a design speed greater than the speed limit where higher speeds are known to occur.</p>
<p>D2 = Minimum distance necessary for merging maneuver at speed, S. See Table 3.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1) Use Merge operation where the available distance for acceleration and merging equals or exceeds &quot;D&quot;.</p>
<p>2) The length of lane line within D is discretionary.</p>
<p>3) Where it is desired to provide traffic signal control for pedestrians to cross the channelized right turn, the following shall apply: a. Omit the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> sign; and b. Omit ladder markings in crosswalk. c. Provide pedestrian heads across the channelized right turn; and d. Control the channelized right turn with three-arrow signal heads; and e. Omit reference to Table 1; and f. Measure D, from diagonal crosswalk; and g. S1 = 0 at crosswalk.</p>
<p>4) Install a second <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a> sign on the right side if the median mounted <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a> sign is not visible to merging traffic.</p>
<p><a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> and W16-7p: Fluorescent yellow-green.</p></details>

</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>Case 5: Added Lane Operation</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1) Use Added Lane operation where the receiving lane is available all day and equals or exceeds 1/2 mile in length.</p>
<p>2) Where it is desired to provide traffic signal control for pedestrians to cross the channelized right turn, the following shall apply: a. Omit the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> sign; and b. Omit ladder markings in crosswalk. c. Provide pedestrian heads across the channelized right turn; and d. Control the channelized right turn with three-arrow signal heads.</p>
<p>3) Install a second <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-3.svg">W4-3</a> sign on the right side if the median mounted <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-3.svg">W4-3</a> sign is not visible to merging traffic.</p>
