<p>Display and unit labels: START CROSSING, Watch For Vehicles. DON&#x27;T START, Finish Crossing If Started (flashing), TIME REMAINING To Finish Crossing (timer). DON&#x27;T CROSS (steady). PUSH BUTTON TO CROSS (ped-actuated unit only). Wires to controller pedestrian pushbutton (PPB) input (ped-actuated). No wires connected to controller pedestrian pushbutton (PPB) input (pre-timed and rest-in-walk).</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">APS shall be used for situations where new pedestrian pushbuttons are being installed or where they are being replaced, due to maintenance.</li>
<li value="2">Audio specifications:</li>
</ol>
<p>WALK interval: Rapid &quot;Tick&quot; tone is activated with a duration of 0.15 seconds and repeats at 0.25-second intervals for a maximum of 7 seconds. Then, APS returns to locator tone for the remainder of the WALK interval.</p>
<p>FLASHING DON&#x27;T WALK interval and SOLID DON&#x27;T WALK interval: Locator tone shall have a duration of 0.15 seconds and repeat at 1-second intervals.</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="3">Vibrotactile specifications:</li>
</ol>
<p>Pushbutton vibrates only during the first 7 seconds of the WALK interval.</p>
<p>Tactile arrows shall be aligned parallel to the direction of travel on the associated crosswalk.</p>
<p>WALK tones and vibrotactile require actuation, even at pre-timed crossings.</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="4">See California MUTCD 2014, Figures 4E-3 and 4E-4 for pushbutton locations.</li>
<li value="5">Countdown pedestrian heads shall be installed at all accessible pedestrian signal locations.</li>
<li value="6">See sheet 2/2 for accessible pedestrian signal field wiring for ped-actuated pedestrian crossing.</li>
<li value="7">Low Voltage &quot;Grey Cable&quot; on sheet 2/2 refers to manufacturer supplied cable that is included with the APS unit.</li>
<li value="8">4cc on sheet 2/2 as per LADOT Spec. 92-097-01.</li>
</ol>
</details>

</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Labels, arrangement 1 (pushbutton and pedestrian head on same pole): PED HEAD (4p), APS control unit, low voltage, LED ped module, low voltage &quot;gray cable&quot;, phase 4 APS button (phi 4 APS BUTTON), 3 CC (or 2 #14) PPB wires, 5 CC for ped phase, pull box, A/C.</p>
<p>Labels, arrangement 2 (not on same pole): PED HEAD (2p) and PED HEAD (4p), APS control unit, low voltage &quot;gray cable&quot;, phi 4 APS button and phi 2 APS button, 3 CC (or 2 #14) PPB wires, 5 CC for ped phase, pull box, spares (5 CC if no spares).</p>
<p>Labels, arrangement 3 (Type 7 post): PED HEAD (2p) and PED HEAD (4p), APS control unit, Type 7 post, phi 4 APS button and phi 2 APS button, low voltage &quot;gray cable&quot;, 4 CC, 5 CC for ped phase, 3 CC (or 2 #14) PPB wires, pull box, spares (5 CC if no spares).</p>
<p>Terminal and wire color codes as drawn: BLK (black), BRN (brown), R (red), Y (yellow), W (white), G (green), C (common), D/W (don&#x27;t walk).</p>
<p>Cable abbreviation: conductor cable (CC). Notes 6 to 8 on sheet 1/2 apply to this sheet.</p>
</details>
