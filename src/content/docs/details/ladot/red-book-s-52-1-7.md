### Sheet 2 of 4 (page 43)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0043.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0043.webp" alt="S-52.1.7 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.7, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p43">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Detail B and Detail C are not to scale.</li>
<li value="2">Maximum (max.) sign arm length is 8&#x27;.</li>
<li value="3">Sign arm data: arm span L 8&#x27;-0&quot;, fixed end diameter 3.52 in., free end diameter 2.4 in., gauge 11, degree rise 0.50.</li>
<li value="4">Sign arm material data: sign arm shaft ASTM A595 Grade A, 55 ksi minimum yield. Clamp ASTM A572, 55 ksi. Sign arm connection bolts ASTM A325, 55 ksi. Hardware coating hot dip zinc.</li>
<li value="5"><strong>Finish notes: system galvanized (GV).</strong> Base coat hot dip galvanized to ASTM A123. Prime coat none. Color none. Finish coat none.</li>
<li value="6">Sign dimensions in inches (conventional road, single lane / multi-lane): <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> pedestrian 30x30* / 36x36. W16-7p downward diagonal arrow 24x12 / 24x12.</li>
</ol>
<p>* The minimum size required for diamond-shaped warning signs facing traffic on multi-lane conventional roads shall be 36x36 per MUTCD section 2C.04.</p>
<p>Detail C: (Pelco AB-3004).</p>
</details>

### Sheet 3 of 4 (page 44)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0044.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0044.webp" alt="S-52.1.7 sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.7, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p44">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>Removable cap at the free end.</strong> Arm length 8&#x27;-0&quot; max.</li>
<li value="2">Fixed end diameter 3.52 in. Free end diameter 2.4 in.</li>
<li value="3">(4) 0.50 in. diameter hex bolt with (1) hex head (HH) nut, (2) flat (FL) washers and (1) lock (LK) washer per bolt.</li>
<li value="4">Hole in pole for 0.63 in. x 2 in. anti-rotation pins to be field drilled.</li>
<li value="5">0.13 in. x 1.50 in. cotter pin (holds anti-rotation pin in place).</li>
<li value="6">1.25 in. diameter wiring access hole in pole shaft to be field drilled.</li>
<li value="7">Detail E: 0.313 in. thick minimum. Bolt spacing 4 in. and 3 in., clamp height 6 in.</li>
<li value="8">Dimension A is taken from the sign arm attachment data table for the pole diameter.</li>

### Sheet 4 of 4 (page 45)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0045.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0045.webp" alt="S-52.1.7 sheet 4"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.7, sheet 4. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p45">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1"><strong>Use 1 in.</strong> galvanized conduit for street lighting and 2 in. schedule 80 PVC conduit for traffic signal unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="2">Conduit stubs from foundation shall be in the direction of the nearest corresponding pullbox unless otherwise specified.</li>
<li value="3">Topping shall be considered as a part of the foundation.</li>
<li value="4">Coordinate bolt circle with light pole base plate prior to anchor bolt installation.</li>
<li value="5">Coordinate conduit size and placement with electrical prior to installation.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> 36 in. diameter footing (minimum 3000 psi concrete). 11 1/2 in. bolt circle. 8 1/8 in. square. Bonding strap or wire. Bushing. 4 in. min. topping. (8) #10 long reinforced. 1 in. diameter street light conduit. 2 in. diameter traffic signal conduit. Anchor bolts 1 1/8 in. x 40 in. x 4 in. (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt) (ASTM F1554 Grade 55 bolts). #4 ties at 9 in. OC, typical (provide (2) ties at 2 in. OC at top).</div>
</details>
