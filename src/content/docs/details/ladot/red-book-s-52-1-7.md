<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical installation of Bureau of Street Lighting poles carrying a rectangular rapid flashing beacon sign arm, drawing S-52.1.7. Shows the single luminaire arm poles CD953A, B and C and the twin-arm poles CD953-A2, B2 and C2, each with an 8 ft max. sign arm, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> signs, RRFBs, a control cabinet, solar panel and pushbutton. Foundation details are on sheet 4.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 4 (document page 42)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0042.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0042.webp" alt="S-52.1.7 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.7, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p42">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Typical installation elevations, not to scale, of two BSL (Bureau of Street Lighting) poles with a Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacon (RRFB) sign arm. The left pole is BSL Poles (CD953A, CD953B, CD953C) and the right pole is BSL Poles (CD953-A2, CD953-B2, CD953-C2). Both are drawn identically except the left pole has a single luminaire arm curving to the left and the right pole has two luminaire arms curving left and right.</p>
<p>Upper pole: a pole-top finial sits above a luminaire arm. A solar panel is mounted on a short arm on the pole with the note Tilt solar panel to 60 deg and rotate assembly so collector panel will face due south. Two horizontal arms project to the left, each at most 8&#x27; max. from the pole, attached to the pole by clamps called out as Detail D. The arms carry a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> pedestrian crossing sign, shown as a diamond with walking figure, mounted between them, with RRFB lights below the sign, called out as Detail B (Back to Back). The bottom of the RRFB assembly is at 17&#x27; min. above grade, shown by a vertical dimension with a break symbol.</p>
<p>Lower pole: below a break, a Control Cabinet is mounted on the pole. Below it is a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>(2) pedestrian crossing sign, with RRFB&#x27;s (Back to Back) below it, and a W16-7p(2) supplemental arrow plaque below the RRFBs. A Pedestrian Pushbutton is mounted on the pole, with dimensions of 42 in. from grade to the pushbutton level and 84 in. from grade to the bottom of the W16-7p(2) plaque level. The pole base has a base plate on a concrete foundation with a sidewalk or pad stepped to the left, with a 36 in. dimension from the pole centerline to the edge of the pad. Callout: Foundation Details, See Sheet 4.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, BSL Poles with Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacon Sign Arm, sheet 1 of 4, drawing number S-52.1.7. Drawn by AC 01-22-20, TE SB 09-03-20, Sr. TE JV 09-03-20, approved 9-18-20 for Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Structural design checked by Vigen Gharibian, SE and approved by Shailesh Patel, SE, Division Engineer, Structural Engineering Division, dated 9-04-20. Bureau of Engineering, Gary Lee Moore, PE, ENV SP, City Engineer. Page number 35.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Tilt solar panel to 60 degrees and rotate assembly so collector panel will face due south.</p>
<p>2. Arm projection 8&#x27; max. from the pole.</p>
<p>3. Signs and devices: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>(2), RRFB&#x27;s (back to back), W16-7p(2), pedestrian pushbutton, control cabinet. Detail B (back to back), Detail D.</p>
<p>4. Dimensions: 17&#x27; min. above grade to the bottom of the upper RRFB assembly, 42 in. and 84 in. heights above grade at the pushbutton and W16-7p(2) levels, 36 in. from the pole centerline to the pad edge.</p>
<p>5. Foundation details: see sheet 4.</p>
<p>6. Typical installation. Not to scale.</p></details>

W16-7p downward diagonal arrow, 24x12, 24x12. Footnote marked with an asterisk below the table.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacon Sign Arm, sheet 2 of 4, drawing number S-52.1.7. Page number 36.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Detail B and Detail C are not to scale.</p>
<p>2. Maximum (max.) sign arm length is 8&#x27;.</p>
<p>3. Sign arm data: arm span L 8&#x27;-0&quot;, fixed end diameter 3.52 in., free end diameter 2.4 in., gauge 11, degree rise 0.50.</p>
<p>4. Sign arm material data: sign arm shaft ASTM A595 Grade A, 55 ksi minimum yield. Clamp ASTM A572, 55 ksi. Sign arm connection bolts ASTM A325, 55 ksi. Hardware coating hot dip zinc.</p>

### Sheet 4 of 4 (document page 45)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0045.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0045.webp" alt="S-52.1.7 sheet 4"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.7, sheet 4. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p45">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of the foundation (top): a 36 in. diameter footing (minimum 3000 psi concrete) drawn as a circle, with an 11 1/2 in. bolt circle and four anchor bolts at an 8 1/8 in. square pattern. A 45 degree angle is shown at each of two radial lines on the right side. Conduit stubs project radially from the footing at the right and bottom, with a conduit cluster at the center.</p>
<p>Section view (bottom): a section through the foundation and sidewalk topping. The footing is 36 in. wide and 60 in. deep. A topping at least 4 in. thick sits on top of the footing, with the sidewalk or pad stepped to the left and 36 in. from the pole centerline to the pad edge. A bushing sits 2 3/4 in. above the topping. A bonding strap or wire is shown at the pole. Reinforcement is (8) #10 long reinforced bars with #4 ties at 9 in. on center (OC), typical (TYP.), provide (2) ties at 2 in. OC at top. Clearances of 3 in. and 2 in. are called out at the left side and 3 in. clear at the bottom. Anchor bolts are 1 1/8 in. x 40 in. x 4 in., 2 nuts and 2 washers per bolt, ASTM F1554 Grade 55 bolts. A 1 in. diameter street light conduit and a 2 in. diameter traffic signal conduit enter the footing, the traffic signal conduit stubbing out 6 in. min. beyond the footing face. A 14 in. min. dimension is shown on the right side at the upper part of the footing.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Foundation Details, sheet 4 of 4, drawing number S-52.1.7. Page number 38.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Use 1 in. galvanized conduit for street lighting and 2 in. schedule 80 PVC conduit for traffic signal unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Conduit stubs from foundation shall be in the direction of the nearest corresponding pullbox unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>3. Topping shall be considered as a part of the foundation.</p>
<p>4. Coordinate bolt circle with light pole base plate prior to anchor bolt installation.</p>
<p>5. Coordinate conduit size and placement with electrical prior to installation.</p>
<p>Callouts: 36 in. diameter footing (minimum 3000 psi concrete). 11 1/2 in. bolt circle. 8 1/8 in. square. Bonding strap or wire. Bushing. 4 in. min. topping. (8) #10 long reinforced. 1 in. diameter street light conduit. 2 in. diameter traffic signal conduit. Anchor bolts 1 1/8 in. x 40 in. x 4 in. (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt) (ASTM F1554 Grade 55 bolts). #4 ties at 9 in. OC, typical (provide (2) ties at 2 in. OC at top).</p></details>
