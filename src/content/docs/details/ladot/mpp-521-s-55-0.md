<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT typical layout for a pedestrian-activated flashing yellow beacon at a marked crosswalk, with advance signing, shark-teeth yield markings and PED XING pavement legends. It shows <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a>, R9-3b, R62E(CA) PPB and W16-7p signs, 20 ft mast arms and a 30 ft dimension, plus a Detail A of the 36 inch by 42 inch <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> (mod) sign with W16 (mod) arrows. Timing diagrams show the alternating three-pulse flash pattern of Beacon One and Beacon Two, with 60 flash periods per minute.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (typical layout): A roadway shown vertically with the centerline and lane lines. The plan is drawn with a break line (zigzag across the roadway) at the top and bottom, so the approach lengths are not to scale. A marked crosswalk with high-visibility bar markings crosses the street near the middle of the sheet, with curb returns of side streets on each side. The roadway width between curbs is dimensioned W. On each side of the crosswalk a 30&#x27; dimension is shown from the crosswalk to the line of yield triangles (shark teeth) on the approach, drawn on the right side of the sheet.</p>
<p>On each approach, two white triangle rows (yield line) sit before the crosswalk, the centerline is a double line that shifts (a curved offset) to form a left turn lane with a turn arrow, and PED XING legends are painted in the approach lanes near the break lines. At each end of the crosswalk, curbside signs are called out on posts: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> (advance pedestrian crossing sign, shown at the top and bottom of the layout as a dot and callout), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> (yield here to pedestrians sign) with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a>&#x27;s and R9-3b&#x27;s, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a> (stop sign at the side street), and R62E(CA) PPB (pedestrian push button sign). Each crosswalk end has <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>&#x27;s and W16-7p&#x27;s on a pole assembly with a 20&#x27; Mastarm carrying two flashing yellow beacons (labeled FL.Y) over the roadway, one set on the near side and one set on the far side of the crosswalk. Detail &quot;A&quot; is called out at the beacon pole assemblies. A symbol at the top left of the sheet identifies the beacon symbol as a flashing beacon activated by pedestrian push button.</p>
<p>Detail A: A sign drawing, 36&quot; wide by 42&quot; tall, with a black walking pedestrian symbol and two downward diagonal arrows at the bottom corners on a white field with a black border. Labeled <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> (mod) for the sign and W16 (mod) for the arrows.</p>
<p>Beacon One timing diagram: Pulse intensity against time from 0 to 1.0 sec. Three pulses are shown between 0 and 0.5 sec, each 0.1 sec on and 0.1 sec off (duty cycle dimension On 0.1, Off 0.1), at 0 to 0.1, 0.2 to 0.3 and 0.4 to 0.5 sec. No pulse from 0.5 to 1.0 sec.</p>
<p>Beacon Two timing diagram: Pulse intensity against time from 0 to 1.0 sec. No pulse from 0 to 0.5 sec, then three pulses at about 0.5 to 0.6, 0.7 to 0.8 and 0.9 to 1.0 sec, so the pulses of Beacon Two fill the pause of Beacon One.</p>
<p>Title block: Title Typical Layout for Activated Pedestrian Warning Device, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-55.0, sheet 1 of 1, approved June 24, 2010 by Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Drawn by EC, dated 6-10-10.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Legend: Flashing beacon activated by pedestrian push button (beacon symbol at top left).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. The flash pattern utilizes LED heads and a Model 2070 controller. There are 60 flash periods per minute. The flash period for each beacon includes three pulses within 0.5 seconds and a pause of 0.5 seconds. The pulses of one beacon alternate with the pause of the other beacon, as shown below.</p>
<p>2. Flash length is W/3.5 seconds and retimes with each call. (W is the roadway width dimension shown on the plan.)</p>
<p>3. See S-481.0 for required advance signing, pavement marking, and red curb.</p>
<p>4. At school crossings, school related signs and pavement markings shall be used in place of those shown hereon, as per S-481.0.</p>
<p>Sign and drawing labels on the plan: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>(mod), W16(mod), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a>&#x27;s and W16-7p&#x27;s, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a>&#x27;s, R9-3b&#x27;s, R62E(CA) PPB, 20&#x27; Mastarm, Detail &quot;A&quot;, FL.Y (flashing yellow).</p>
<p>Dimensions: sign 36&quot; by 42&quot;, 30&#x27; from crosswalk to yield line, W roadway width, 20&#x27; mast arm. Timing: On 0.1 sec, Off 0.1 sec.</p></details>
