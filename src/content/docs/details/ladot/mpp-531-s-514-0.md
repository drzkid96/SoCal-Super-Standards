</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 18&quot;x24&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is as noted. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;MS 12/06&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, all text, arrow and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p>
<p>Sign labels: 18&quot; by 24&quot;, R=2&quot; 4 corners, 9.8&quot; outside diameter, 1&quot; slash, 6&quot; Series E P, 2.8&quot; / 2.3&quot; / 1&quot; Series C (time line), 1.2&quot; Series C, 0.8&quot; Series C, 0.3&quot; Series C.</p></details>
