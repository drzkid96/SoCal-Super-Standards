<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan and typical section of a landscaped neighborhood traffic circle with a cast-in-place concrete mountable curb. The circle has a 2 ft wide curb, a topsoil mound up to 2 ft high, depth control joints, steel dowels and yellow raised pavement markers. Five notes cover landscaping sight distance, monument protection, curb concrete, signs and markings, and intersections that are not 4-leg.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 145)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0145.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0145.webp" alt="Detail 700 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide Detail 700, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/09-pages-145-162/#p145">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A circle in plan with a cast-in-place cement concrete mountable curb ring around a backfilled center (backfill with topsoil and mulch, per Section A). An outer dashed octagonal outline marks the asphalt to be removed, with a 2 ft MIN width between the outer curb edge and the removal limit. The circle diameter is dimensioned as Dimension C, see Sheet 2. Section cut marker A on Sheet 1 is drawn at the top of the circle. Callouts: cast-in-place cement concrete mountable curb, per Section A. 2 in depth control joints, fill with sealant, color gray, 4 joints for less than 20 ft diameter and 8 joints for greater than 20 ft diameter. RPM Type 2 yellow (location by DOT): use 12 for 15 ft diameter or less, 16 for greater than 15 ft and less than 20 ft diameter, and 20 for 20 ft diameter or greater. Adjust maintenance holes, valve boxes, and monument cases to new grade, lids do not have to be traffic rated. Use 2 No. 4 steel dowels, 12 in length, at each joint (TYP.). Remove asphalt.</p>
<p>Section A, Typical Section (callout 1): Half section from the edge of the removed asphalt to the center line (CL) of the circle. At left, the existing pavement is 6 in HMA over base. The asphalt is removed back at least 2 ft MIN from the outer face of the new curb. The remaining prepared subgrade carries 6 in MIN untreated base material under the curb, with a 2% slope drawn on the pavement edge at the cut and vertical dimensions of 6 in (existing HMA thickness at left) plus 6 in and 3 in at the curb outer face. The cast-in-place cement concrete mountable curb is 2 ft wide overall. Its top slopes down toward the inside, with a 1 in R rounded outer top edge and a 1 in LIP at the inside edge. Two #4 steel dowels run horizontally through the curb, dimensioned 6 in from the outer face, 12 in between dowels, and 6 in to the inside face. Inside the curb the topsoil mound rises to a crown at the CL, 2 ft MAX. above the curb lip, covered by a thin layer of surface materials to be approved by BSS (hatched). A 4 in dimension is shown at the inside toe of the curb. Callouts: remove asphalt, 6 in HMA, 6 in MIN. untreated base material, #4 steel dowel, cast-in-place cement concrete mountable curb, topsoil, surface materials to be approved by BSS.</p>
<p>Title block: Neighborhood Traffic Circles, Supplemental Design Guide, Detail 700, Sheet 1 of 2.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Landscaping requires Streets-LA review and approval. Plantings and trees must not inhibit intersection sight distances by ensuring a clear zone of visibility between 36 inches high and 80 inches high from the pavement elevation.</p>
<p>2. Monument protection/preservation: notify the BOE Survey Division prior to monument adjustment. Raise monument to grade in appropriate casing.</p>
<p>3. Curb shall be constructed of PCC conforming to Greenbook subsection 201-1 and 303-5.</p>
<p>4. The traffic control devices, pavement markings, and signs associated with the construction of neighborhood traffic circles should follow the guidance in the DOT Neighborhood Traffic Circles Standard Plan S-524.</p>
<p>5. Intersections with more or less than 4 legs require Engineer approval.</p>
<p>Abbreviations used on the drawing: portland cement concrete (PCC), hot mix asphalt (HMA), raised pavement marker (RPM), Bureau of Engineering (BOE), Bureau of Street Services (BSS).</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 146)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0146.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0146.webp" alt="Detail 700 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide Detail 700, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/09-pages-145-162/#p146">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Center Island Geometry (plan view): A plan of an intersection with four curb returns and a circular center island. Dimension A is the street width, shown both vertically (left leg) and horizontally (bottom leg). Dimension B is the curb return radius, shown at the lower right corner. Dimension C is the center island diameter, shown across the circle. The center island is a full circle, with an optional truck apron drawn as a partial outer ring around the island on the left, top and bottom. The offset, 5.5 ft MAX (see Note 3), is the distance at the left of the circle between the curb line extension of the street and the island or apron edge. The opening width, which varies 16 ft to 20 ft (see Notes 3 and 4), is the diagonal clear distance from the island to the corner curb return at the lower right.</p>
<p>Opening Width table: columns Offset and Opening Width. Rows: 5.5 ft (MAX) and 16 ft (MIN). 5.0 and 17 ft. 4.5 and 18 ft. 4.0 and 19 ft. 3.5 or less and 20 ft (MAX).</p>
<p>Dimensions table: columns A Street Width, B Curb Return Radius, C Center Island Diameter. Row 1, A = 28 ft (Hillside Limited Standard): B 15 ft with C 18 ft, B 20 ft with C 20 ft, B 25 ft with C 22 ft. Row 2, A = 30 ft (Local Street Limited): B 15 ft with C 20 ft, B 20 ft with C 22 ft, B 25 ft with C 24 ft. Row 3, A = 36 ft (Local Street Standard): B 15 ft with C 27 ft, B 20 ft with C 29 ft, B 25 ft with C 33 ft. Row 4, A = 40 ft (Collector): B 15 ft with C 32 ft, B 20 ft with C 34 ft, B 25 ft with C 38 ft.</p>
<p>Title block: Supplemental Design Guide, Detail 700, Sheet 2 of 2.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Use dimension schedule as a design guide. Final dimensions to be determined by the Engineer.</p>
<p>2. Where crossing streets are not the same width, consider curb extensions on wider road to create consistent approach widths.</p>
<p>3. For locations with non-standard street widths, the relationship between the offset and the opening width is determined by the opening width table.</p>
<p>4. Where the circulating width is less than 20 feet wide, a mountable truck apron may be required.</p>
<p>Labels: offset (5.5 ft MAX), see Note 3. Center island. Optional truck apron. Opening width varies 16 ft to 20 ft, see Note 3 and 4.</p></details>
