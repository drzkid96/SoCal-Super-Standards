<p class="dwg-sum">Shows a temporary traffic signal on a Type 1 standard held down by a 36 in square steel platform with four 5 gallon cans filled with concrete, locked on with steel coil chain. Heads are 12 in to match the permanent heads being replaced. The standard is set level on slopes greater than 1/2 in rise per foot by grading or wood shims.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 46)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Temporary signal for Type 1 standard. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>Use 12 in heads corresponding to permanent heads being replaced.</p>
<p>Platform (top view detail, not to scale): 36 in x 36 in. 36 in sq. x 1/4 in thick steel platform, hot dip galvanized per ASTM 123. Type 1 base with 4 1 in dia. x 3-1/2 in bolts and nuts with 2 washers for each bolt. 3/8 in dia. steel coil chain tied to each 5 gal. block and locked around the Type 1 standard. 5 gal. can filled with concrete, four cans are required, spaced as shown.</p>
<p>Platform (bottom view detail, not to scale): 36 in x 36 in. 2 in high x 1/4 in thick perimeter lip resting on ground. 1-3/4 in x 1-3/4 in x 1/4 in steel angle brackets welded to plate. 4 holes at 1-1/4 in dia. 8-1/2 in dia. bolt circle. 1 in x 1 in notches for cable.</p>
<p>Weight block (not to scale): 5 gal. can filled with concrete, min. weight 90 lbs. 1/2 in dia. steel rod embedded 9 in deep. Block 12 in wide x 14 in high, 4 in rod width, 3 in rod projection.</p>
<p>Type 1 standard (not to scale): street name sign, pedestrian signal, 3/8 in dia. steel coil chain tied to each 5 gal. block and locked around the Type 1 standard. 5 gal. can filled with concrete. Base assembly. &quot;LADOT&quot; or contractor name embossed or welded on at least two sides. 2 in lip. 1 in x 1 in notch for cable.</p>
<p>For slopes greater than 1/2 in rise per foot, temporary standard to be set level through the use of grading or wood shims.</p></details>
