<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan view of a raised crosswalk showing Chevron III markings, advance warning pavement marking per S-509.0, and crosswalk and advance sign assemblies placed at a distance C** from the crosswalk. The crosswalk assembly is a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> with W17-1bP RAISED CROSSWALK and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-7p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-7P.svg">W16-7P</a> arrow plaque, and the advance assembly is a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a> with W17-1bP and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-9p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-9P.svg">W16-9P</a> AHEAD. Each carries a W13-1 advisory speed plaque of 15, 20, 25 or 30 MPH as appropriate for the design speed.</p>
