<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</li>
<li value="2">Type III barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</li>
<li value="3">Minimum 28&quot; tall traffic cone spaced a maximum 25&#x27; apart on all turn lanes into closure area.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Permanent signs to be installed by LADOT.</strong> Permittee shall reimburse LADOT for the sign fabrication and installation cost.</li>
<li value="5">Permittee shall provide, install and maintain barricades, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs mounted on barricades and traffic cones as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs, cones, and barricades.</li>
<li value="6">Additional traffic controls may be required as determined by LADOT.</li>
</ol>
</details>
