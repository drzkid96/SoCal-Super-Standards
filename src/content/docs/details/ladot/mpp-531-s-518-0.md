<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical traffic control plan for closing one block of street for a Sunday Farmers' Market, 8 AM to 2 PM. Type III barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> Road Closed signs close the block at both ends, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> turn prohibition signs and 8 AM to 2 PM Sunday No Parking signs are posted at the corners, and Modified <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a> Road Closed Ahead signs are placed about 245 ft upstream. Six notes cover the fire lane, barricade gaps, cones, sign costs, permittee duties and additional controls.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (not to scale): A north-south street block is closed for the market. The closed block is a hatched area labeled Farmers&#x27; Market, between a cross street at the top and a cross street at the bottom (each cross street has dashed lane lines, a double solid centerline and curb returns at the four corners). An alley (labeled Alley) enters the block from each side at mid-height, one at the left and one at the right, each closed by a single Type III barricade labeled <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> placed across the alley mouth at the edge of the market block.</p>
<p>Barricades: at the top end and at the bottom end of the market block three Type III barricades span the street side by side with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> callouts (two callouts per end). At each of the four corners of the block a Type III barricade is shown at the curb return (the H symbol) with leader callouts <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a>* and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a>* (no right turn and no left turn signs, each with an asterisk meaning a restriction plate is added).</p>
<p>Parking signs: at each of the four corners of the block, on the sides of the market block, a sign on a post is called out with a curved leader to an enlarged red and white No Parking sign (circled P with a slash) reading 8 AM to 2 PM, SUNDAY, FARMERS&#x27; MARKET with a horizontal arrow (the two left-side signs point away and toward the block as drawn: upper left arrow left, lower left arrow right, upper right arrow right, lower right arrow left), plus a line of small text at the bottom that is [illegible].</p>
<p>Advance warning: above the top cross street and below the bottom cross street, on the closed street&#x27;s centerline extension, a MOD. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a>* sign on a post is shown with a vertical dimension of 245&#x27; +/- measured back from the cross street curb line (drawn at the top-left and the bottom-right of the plan).</p>
<p>Cones: a line of red dots marks traffic cones along the turn-lane tapers on the cross streets (at the top and the left). Callout: See note 3 (at the top right). The cross streets show a lane shift taper with a turn arrow at the approach turn lanes.</p>
<p>Legend (right side): Sign on post symbol, Type III barricade symbol, MOD. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a> (yellow diamond sign reading ROAD CLOSED AHEAD with a yellow plate below reading 8 AM to 2 PM SUNDAY), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> (white ROAD CLOSED sign with a black border), <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> (no right turn symbol sign) and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> (no left turn symbol sign), a white 8 AM to 2 PM SUNDAY restriction plate with a black border, and a Traffic Cone 28&quot; tall with two Retroreflective Bands.</p>
<p>Title block: Approved November 17, 2009, signed John E. Fisher for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. <details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>* Add restriction plate (hours of parking, turn restrictions and road closure to be shown on permit).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</p>
<p>2. Type III barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</p>
<p>3. Minimum 28&quot; tall traffic cone spaced a maximum 25&#x27; apart on all turn lanes into closure area.</p>
<p>4. Permanent signs to be installed by LADOT. Permittee shall reimburse LADOT for the sign fabrication and installation cost.</p>
<p>5. Permittee shall provide, install and maintain barricades, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs mounted on barricades and traffic cones as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs, cones, and barricades.</p>
<p>6. Additional traffic controls may be required as determined by LADOT.</p></details>
