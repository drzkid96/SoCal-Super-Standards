<p class="dwg-sum">Plan view of a one-block street closure for a Farmers' Market at a four-leg intersection. It shows Type III barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> Road Closed signs at both ends, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> turn prohibition signs, Modified <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a> Road Closed Ahead signs about 245 ft back, and No Parking 8 AM to 2 PM Sunday signs along the closure. Notes cover the 20' fire lane, barricade gaps of 4' maximum, cone spacing, and permittee and LADOT responsibilities.</p>

<p>Signs shown: MOD. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a> Road Closed Ahead with 8 AM to 2 PM Sunday plate, placed about 245 ft before the closure on the through street; <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> Road Closed; <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> turn prohibitions; No Parking 8 AM to 2 PM Sunday Farmers&#x27; Market signs; 28&quot; traffic cone with retroreflective bands.</p>
<p>* Add restriction plate (hours of parking, turn restrictions and road closure to be shown on permit).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</p>
<p>2. Type III barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</p>
<p>3. Minimum 28&quot; tall traffic cone spaced a maximum 25&#x27; apart on all turn lanes into closure area.</p>
<p>4. Permanent signs to be installed by LADOT. Permittee shall reimburse LADOT for the sign fabrication and installation cost.</p>
<p>5. Permittee shall provide, install and maintain barricades, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs mounted on barricades and traffic cones as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs, cones, and barricades.</p>
<p>6. Additional traffic controls may be required as determined by LADOT.</p></details>
