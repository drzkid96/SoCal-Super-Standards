<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical traffic control plan for closing one block of street for a Sunday Farmers' Market, 8 AM to 2 PM. Type III barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> Road Closed signs close the block at both ends, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> turn prohibition signs and 8 AM to 2 PM Sunday No Parking signs are posted at the corners, and Modified <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a> Road Closed Ahead signs are placed about 245 ft upstream. Six notes cover the fire lane, barricade gaps, cones, sign costs, permittee duties and additional controls.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (not to scale): a one-block street closure shown as a hatched area labeled Farmers&#x27; Market between two side streets at the left and right and an alley with an <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> barricade on each side of the market block. The cross streets above and below the closure each show dashed lane lines and a double centerline. At each of the four corners of the closed block a Type III barricade is shown at the curb return with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a>* (no right turn) and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a>* (no left turn) sign callouts, and three Type III barricades with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> callouts span the street at the top and bottom edges of the market block. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> is also called out at each alley entrance.</p>
<p>At each corner, on the sides of the market block, a red and white No Parking sign (circled P) reading 8 AM to 2 PM, SUNDAY, FARMERS&#x27; MARKET with an arrow is mounted on a post, with arrows pointing to the sign locations (sign on post). Modified <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a>* advance signs are located on each approach about 245 ft (245&#x27; +/-) upstream of the intersection, drawn at the top and bottom of the plan. A line of red dots with the callout See note 3 marks traffic cones along the approach turn lane tapers at the top and right.</p>
<p>Legend (right): Sign on post symbol, Type III barricade symbol, the MOD. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-3.svg">W20-3</a> yellow diamond sign reading ROAD CLOSED AHEAD with a yellow plate below reading 8 AM to 2 PM SUNDAY, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> ROAD CLOSED sign, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-1.svg">R3-1</a> (no right turn) and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a> (no left turn) signs, an 8 AM to 2 PM SUNDAY restriction plate, and a traffic cone 28&quot; tall with two retroreflective bands.</p>

<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Maintain 20&#x27; fire lane through closure area.</p>
<p>2. Type III barricades shall cover entire street with no more than a 4&#x27; gap between barricades or barricade and curb.</p>
<p>3. Minimum 28&quot; tall traffic cone spaced a maximum 25&#x27; apart on all turn lanes into closure area.</p>
<p>4. Permanent signs to be installed by LADOT. Permittee shall reimburse LADOT for the sign fabrication and installation cost.</p>
<p>5. Permittee shall provide, install and maintain barricades, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> signs mounted on barricades and traffic cones as shown. Permittee must wear safety vest, conforming to ANSI/ISEA 107-2004 when installing and removing signs, cones, and barricades.</p>
<p>6. Additional traffic controls may be required as determined by LADOT.</p></details>
