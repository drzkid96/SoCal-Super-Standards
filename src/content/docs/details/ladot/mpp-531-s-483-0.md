<p>Advance warning sign callouts: 30&quot; x 30&quot; <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w17-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W17-1.svg">W17-1</a> modified with Type II-A retro-reflective sheeting. 24&quot; x 24&quot; <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-1p/">W13-1</a>(15) with Type II-A retro-reflective sheeting. 12&quot; min. from curb face. 7&#x27; min. mounting height. Sign post.</p>
<p>Advance pavement marking callouts: Geometric or striped centerline or solid yellow line of striped median. Curb face or flow line. W. Offset by 2&#x27; if W&gt;10&#x27;, 1&#x27; if W=10&#x27;.</p>
<p>Speed Humps Installation Notes</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Speed humps shall be installed only when authorized by LADOT. Installations shall conform to the latest revision of LADOT Specification No. 51-008-003.</li>
<li value="2">All asphalt concrete pavement shall conform to the most recent edition of the Standard Specification for Public Works Construction Section 302-5.</li>
<li value="3">Speed humps shall not be placed over maintenance covers, watergates, junction chambers, etc.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Edge of speed humps should be 5 ft.</strong> or more from edge of driveway or catch basin.</li>
<li value="5"><strong>Spacing between humps may vary.</strong> Exact locations and type of speed humps and sign locations shall be determined by LADOT.</li>
<li value="6">Asphalt joins shall be accomplished by feather-edging.</li>
<li value="7">The height of the speed hump shall not be less than 2 5/8 inch, and shall not exceed a tolerance of +1/4 inch.</li>
</ol>
</details>
