<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard details for Type I (12 ft long) and Type II (22 ft long) asphalt speed humps, their chevron markings, and the advance warning sign and HUMPS pavement legend. Humps are installed only when authorized by LADOT. Finished height is 2 5/8 inches with a +1/4 inch tolerance, and humps stay 5 ft or more from driveways and catch basins. Sheet 1 shows sign and hump placement on two-lane streets and streets with a striped median.</p>

<p>Speed Humps Installation Notes</p>
<p>1. Speed humps shall be installed only when authorized by LADOT. Installations shall conform to the latest revision of LADOT Specification No. 51-008-003.</p>
<p>2. All asphalt concrete pavement shall conform to the most recent edition of the Standard Specification for Public Works Construction Section 302-5.</p>
<p>3. Speed humps shall not be placed over maintenance covers, watergates, junction chambers, etc.</p>
<p>4. Edge of speed humps should be 5 ft. or more from edge of driveway or catch basin.</p>
<p>5. Spacing between humps may vary. Exact locations and type of speed humps and sign locations shall be determined by LADOT.</p>
<p>6. Asphalt joins shall be accomplished by feather-edging.</p>
<p>7. The height of the speed hump shall not be less than 2 5/8 inch, and shall not exceed a tolerance of +1/4 inch.</p></details>
