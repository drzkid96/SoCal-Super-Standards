<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-76.6 shows the 12 in diameter beveled traffic signal visor, drawn in the left beveled version. It gives the side view lengths (27 in top, 12 in bottom), the 3.5 degree back edge angle, the mounting ear detail and the ear layout on the 11 3/4 in ring. A right beveled visor is made by beveling the opposite side. LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06 governs all other details.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 58)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0058.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0058.webp" alt="S-76.6 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-76.6, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p58">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Side view, left beveled: a long 12 in diameter signal visor drawn as a tube seen from the side, with a diagonal cut across the open (front) end. The top edge is the longer edge and is dimensioned 27&quot;+/-1/16&quot; overall from the back (ear) end. The bottom edge is shorter and is dimensioned 12&quot;+/-1/16&quot; from the back end. A diagonal line joins the end of the bottom edge to the end of the top edge. Dashed lines inside the top and bottom edges show the rolled edge or inner surface, and a dash-dot centerline runs along the axis. Small tabs (ears) are drawn on the back edge near the top and bottom.</p>
<p>Elevation view, left beveled: below the side view, the visor is shown from the front as an elongated oval (the bevel cut opening) with horizontal and vertical centerlines, drawn next to the vertical back edge of the tube with its top and bottom edges dashed. The back edge is tilted from vertical by 3.5 degrees, shown with an angle callout at the bottom left. Caption under it: Left Beveled.</p>
<p>Ear detail: an enlarged profile of one mounting ear, a hook shaped tab on a flat base flange. Overall width is 3/4&quot;+/-1/32&quot;. A dimension from the center of the hook radius to the right side is 3/8&quot;+/-1/32&quot;. The inner hook radius is 1/8&quot; R+/-1/32&quot; and the outer hook radius is 3/8&quot; R+/-1/32&quot;, with the 3/8 in radius shown from a center point on the horizontal centerline.</p>
<p>End (back) view: a circular visor ring with crosshair centerlines and four ears at 45 degree diagonals, labeled See Ear Detail. The ears are spaced 90 degrees apart (90 degree callouts between adjacent ears, with a 45 degree callout from the vertical centerline to the lower ear). The ring is dimensioned 11 3/4&quot;+/-1/16&quot; diameter. The vertical spacing between the upper pair of ears and lower pair of ears is 9&quot;+/-1/16&quot;. The diagonal spacing between opposite ears is 12 1/16&quot;+/-1/16&quot;.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Left beveled visor shown. See ear detail. Bevel opposite side for right beveled visor.</p>
<p>2. Refer to LADOT Purchase Specification #92-061-06 for all other details.</p></details>
