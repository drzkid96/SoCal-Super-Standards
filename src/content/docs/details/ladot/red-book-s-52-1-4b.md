### Sheet 1 of 2 (page 40)

<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Pole specifications</h5>
<p>Base plate and flanges: ASTM A-36.</p>
<p>Shaft: steel of 48,000 PSI minimum yield after fabrication. Design yield of Fy = 48,000 PSI in order that Fu/Fy &gt; 1.5.</p>
<p>Pipe: ASTM A-53 Grade B.</p>
<p>Anchor bolts: ASTM A-307.</p>
<p>Welds: all butt welds to be ground flush with base metal.</p>
<p>Longitudinal: butt weld by the submerged arc process.</p>
<p>Circumferential: butt weld with permanent back-up ring.</p>
<p>Finish pole: hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-123.</p>
<p>Finish hardware: hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-153.</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">General notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Fabrication of the standards, luminaire arms and mast arms must conform to the 2006 Department of Transportation, State of California Standard Plans.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Refer to LADOT Standard Drawing No.</strong> S-52.1.6 for &quot;F&quot; spacing.</li>
<li value="3">Projected luminaire arm length to be determined by Department of Public Works, Bureau of Street Lighting. Refer to Caltrans Standard Drawing No. ES-7C for luminaire arm connection detail.</li>
<li value="4"><strong>Signal arm connection shall be for 45 ft.</strong> arm (Case 4 arm loading), as per Caltrans Standard Drawing No. ES-7F. Shorter arms may be used, as appropriate.</li>
</ol>
</details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (page 41)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0041.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0041.webp" alt="S-52.1.4B sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.4B, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p41">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Pan and tilt mounting flange detail: 4 - 3/8 in. diameter stainless steel studs, equally spaced on 4 3/4 in. diameter B.C. 6 1/2 in. diameter (ASTM A-36). 4 NF threads, stainless nuts and bolts. 1/2 in. plate. 1 1/4 in. 1/4 in. fillet weld.</p>
<p>Upper handhole and coupling detail: 2 in. coupling. 18 in. from pole top. Cable hook* welded to inside of shaft oriented directly opposite coupling and above handhole. 3 in. x 5 in. handhole rim (1/4 in. plate x 2 in. wide) and cover. 1/4 in. welds.</p>
<p>* Each cable shall be supported inside the pole by Kellems grips (or equivalent) one at the top and a second at the mid-point. Two 1/8 in. diameter galvanized steel wires shall secure mid-point grips to the top cable hook.</p>
<p>Clamp and side mounted camera arm detail: 3 in. Schedule 40 pipe (hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-153). 1/2 in. U bolt ASTM A-307. 3/8 in. plate clamp. 6-1/2 in. diameter x 1/2 in. thick plate (ASTM A-36). 12&#x27; max. 4 - 3/8 in. diameter stainless steel studs, equally spaced on 4 3/4 in. diameter B.C. 1/2 in. plate. 1/4 in. gusset plate each side. 1/4 in. welds, 2 places.</p>
<p>Base plate detail: 18 in. x 18 in. Hole diameter = pole inside diameter - 2 in. 4 holes, equally spaced on 18 in. diameter B.C. Diagonal offset dimensions 3 in., 1 1/2 in., 2 3/8 in.</p>
<p>Foundation detail: finished grade. Base plate 18 in. x 18 in. x 1.75 in. 4 in. topping. 4 1/2 in. (4)2 in. x 42 in. x 6 in. anchor bolts with 2 nuts and 2 washers per bolt (one above and one below the plate). Bonding strap or wire. 12 2/5 in. 3 in. conduit (traffic). 1 in. conduit (BSL). 2 in. conduit (CCTV cable). Foundation 36 in. wide, 10&#x27; deep. Reinforcing cage: 32 in. cage diameter, 9&#x27;-6&quot; cage depth, see detail of Section A-A in LADOT Std. Dwg No. S-52.1.6.</p>
<p>Foundation design criteria: wind load design based on 2001 AASHTO Standard Specifications for Structural Support for Highway Signs, Luminaires, and Traffic Signals. (Fatigue requirements of Chapter 11 are not considered.) Wind speed = 100 mph.</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Bar reinforcement shall conform to ASTM A615 Grade 60.</li>
<li value="2">All soils supporting and surrounding the foundation shall be undisturbed natural soil or 90% compacted fill.</li>
<li value="3">Foundation concrete shall be 560-C-3250.</li>
</ol>
</details>
