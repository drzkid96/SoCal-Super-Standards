<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of drawing S-52.1.4B for 45 foot CCTV camera poles, Types C-1-100 and C-2-100, with top-mount or side-mount camera options, optional signal heads, a 15 ft max. luminaire arm and a handhole. Type C-2-100 also carries a 45 ft max. signal arm with a street name sign. Gives pole dimensions, wall thickness, steel and finish specifications and general notes.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 40)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0040.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0040.webp" alt="S-52.1.4B sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.4B, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p40">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation views: two pole elevations side by side, Type C-1-100 Pole at left and Type C-2-100 Pole at right. Both are 45 foot tapered steel poles for CCTV cameras, standing on a CIDH foundation drawn below a hatched grade line, with the foundation labeled See Foundation Detail, Sheet 2.</p>
<p>Type C-1-100 pole: pole height is 45&#x27;-0&quot; with 12.125&quot; base outside diameter (O.D.), 5.938&quot; top O.D. and 0.4184&quot; wall thickness (THK.). At the pole top, a Top-Mount Option (see Pan and Tilt Mounting Flange Detail, sheet 2) is shown, together with a Side-Mount Option (see Clamp and Side Mounted Camera Arm Detail, sheet 2), which is a horizontal camera arm 12&#x27; max. long projecting from the top of the pole. An Upper Handhole and Coupling is called out near the top (see detail, sheet 2). A luminaire arm 15&#x27; max. projects from the pole near 29&#x27;-6&quot; above grade, with the callout See Note 3. Optional heads (signal heads) are shown on the lower pole, with a handhole near the base. Vertical dimensions from grade: 7&#x27;-6&quot;, 11&#x27;-3&quot;, 29&#x27;-6&quot; and 45&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>Type C-2-100 pole: same 45&#x27;-0&quot; pole with 12.125&quot; base O.D. and 5.938&quot; top O.D., with 0.6250&quot; wall thickness. It has the same top-mount and side-mount camera options, upper handhole and coupling, and 15&#x27; max. luminaire arm (See Note 3). In addition it has a signal mast arm 45&#x27; max. long projecting to the left from the pole at about 16&#x27;-0&quot; above grade (See Note 4). Two signal heads are on the arm separated by a sign, with spacing F between the heads (&quot;F&quot; spacing, see Note 2). A street name sign hangs from the arm, called out as Street Name Sign, See S-486.0. Optional heads and a handhole are on the lower pole. Vertical dimensions from grade: 7&#x27;-6&quot;, 11&#x27;-3&quot;, 16&#x27;-0&quot;, 29&#x27;-6&quot; and 45&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>Pole specifications: a list under the C-1 pole, given in the notes field.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Camera Poles (Case 4 Mast Arm Loading - 100 MPH Wind Speed), sheet 1 of 2, drawing number S-52.1.4B. Drawn 11-15-07, approved March 18, 2008 by Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Structural approval signed by Shailesh Patel, S.E., Division Engineer, Structural Engineering Division, Bureau of Engineering, dated 11-29-07. Page number 33.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Pole specifications:</h5>
<p>Base plate and flanges: ASTM A-36.</p>
<p>Shaft: steel of 48,000 PSI minimum yield after fabrication. Design yield of Fy = 48,000 PSI in order that Fu/Fy &gt; 1.5.</p>
<p>Pipe: ASTM A-53 Grade B.</p>
<p>Anchor bolts: ASTM A-307.</p>
<p>Welds: all butt welds to be ground flush with base metal.</p>
<p>Longitudinal: butt weld by the submerged arc process.</p>
<p>Circumferential: butt weld with permanent back-up ring.</p>
<p>Finish pole: hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-123.</p>
<p>Finish hardware: hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-153.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Fabrication of the standards, luminaire arms and mast arms must conform to the 2006 Department of Transportation, State of California Standard Plans.</p>
<p>2. Refer to LADOT Standard Drawing No. S-52.1.6 for &quot;F&quot; spacing.</p>
<p>3. Projected luminaire arm length to be determined by Department of Public Works, Bureau of Street Lighting. Refer to Caltrans Standard Drawing No. ES-7C for luminaire arm connection detail.</p>
<p>4. Signal arm connection shall be for 45 ft. arm (Case 4 arm loading), as per Caltrans Standard Drawing No. ES-7F. Shorter arms may be used, as appropriate.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 41)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0041.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0041.webp" alt="S-52.1.4B sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.4B, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p41">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Pan and tilt mounting flange detail: a plan and an elevation of the top-mount camera flange. The plan shows a 6 1/2 in. diameter (ASTM A-36) round plate with 4 stainless steel studs, 3/8 in. diameter, equally spaced on a 4 3/4 in. diameter bolt circle (B.C.). The elevation shows a 1/2 in. plate welded to the pipe top with a 1/4 in. fillet weld, 4 NF threads with stainless nuts and bolts, and studs projecting 1 1/4 in. above the plate.</p>
<p>Upper handhole and coupling detail: a section through the pole shaft near the top. A 2 in. coupling is welded on one side with a 1/4 in. weld, and a 3 in. x 5 in. handhole rim (1/4 in. plate x 2 in. wide) and cover is welded on the opposite side with 1/4 in. welds. The coupling and handhole are located 18 in. from the pole top. A cable hook, welded to the inside of the shaft, is oriented directly opposite the coupling and above the handhole. A footnote marked with an asterisk explains cable support.</p>
<p>Clamp and side mounted camera arm detail: a plan and elevation of the side-mount arm. A 3 in. Schedule 40 pipe (hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-153) runs up to 12&#x27; max. between a clamp on the pole and a 6 1/2 in. diameter x 1/2 in. thick plate (ASTM A-36) at the camera end. The clamp consists of a 1/2 in. U bolt (ASTM A-307) and a 3/8 in. plate clamp, with 1/4 in. fillet welds. The end plate has 4 stainless steel studs, 3/8 in. diameter, equally spaced on a 4 3/4 in. diameter B.C. In elevation the pipe end plate is a 1/2 in. plate with 1/4 in. gusset plates on each side and 1/4 in. welds, 2 places.</p>
<p>Base plate detail: plan of an 18 in. x 18 in. square base plate with rounded corners, a center hole with diameter equal to the pole inside diameter minus 2 in., and 4 holes equally spaced on an 18 in. diameter bolt circle at the corners. Diagonal offset dimensions of 3 in., 1 1/2 in. and 2 3/8 in. are given for the bolt hole pattern.</p>
<p>Foundation detail: section through the CIDH foundation, 36 in. wide and 10&#x27; deep. A base plate 18 in. x 18 in. x 1.75 in. sits at finished grade on a 4 in. topping, with a 4 1/2 in. dimension at the top. Four anchor bolts, 2 in. x 42 in. x 6 in., each with 2 nuts and 2 washers (one above and one below the plate), are cast in. A bonding strap or wire is shown under the plate. The dimension between conduits is 12 2/5 in. Three conduits enter the foundation: 3 in. conduit (traffic), 1 in. conduit (BSL) and 2 in. conduit (CCTV cable). A reinforcing cage is drawn in the lower part of the foundation, 32 in. cage diameter and 9&#x27;-6&quot; cage depth, see detail of Section A-A in LADOT Std. Dwg No. S-52.1.6.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Camera Poles (Case 4 Mast Arm Loading - 100 MPH Wind Speed), sheet 2 of 2, drawing number S-52.1.4B. Page number 34.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Pan and tilt mounting flange detail: 4 - 3/8 in. diameter stainless steel studs, equally spaced on 4 3/4 in. diameter B.C. 6 1/2 in. diameter (ASTM A-36). 4 NF threads, stainless nuts and bolts. 1/2 in. plate. 1 1/4 in. 1/4 in. fillet weld.</p>
<p>Upper handhole and coupling detail: 2 in. coupling. 18 in. from pole top. Cable hook* welded to inside of shaft oriented directly opposite coupling and above handhole. 3 in. x 5 in. handhole rim (1/4 in. plate x 2 in. wide) and cover. 1/4 in. welds.</p>
<p>* Each cable shall be supported inside the pole by Kellems grips (or equivalent) one at the top and a second at the mid-point. Two 1/8 in. diameter galvanized steel wires shall secure mid-point grips to the top cable hook.</p>
<p>Clamp and side mounted camera arm detail: 3 in. Schedule 40 pipe (hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A-153). 1/2 in. U bolt ASTM A-307. 3/8 in. plate clamp. 6-1/2 in. diameter x 1/2 in. thick plate (ASTM A-36). 12&#x27; max. 4 - 3/8 in. diameter stainless steel studs, equally spaced on 4 3/4 in. diameter B.C. 1/2 in. plate. 1/4 in. gusset plate each side. 1/4 in. welds, 2 places.</p>
<p>Base plate detail: 18 in. x 18 in. Hole diameter = pole inside diameter - 2 in. 4 holes, equally spaced on 18 in. diameter B.C. Diagonal offset dimensions 3 in., 1 1/2 in., 2 3/8 in.</p>
<p>Foundation detail: finished grade. Base plate 18 in. x 18 in. x 1.75 in. 4 in. topping. 4 1/2 in. (4)2 in. x 42 in. x 6 in. anchor bolts with 2 nuts and 2 washers per bolt (one above and one below the plate). Bonding strap or wire. 12 2/5 in. 3 in. conduit (traffic). 1 in. conduit (BSL). 2 in. conduit (CCTV cable). Foundation 36 in. wide, 10&#x27; deep. Reinforcing cage: 32 in. cage diameter, 9&#x27;-6&quot; cage depth, see detail of Section A-A in LADOT Std. Dwg No. S-52.1.6.</p>
<p>Foundation design criteria: wind load design based on 2001 AASHTO Standard Specifications for Structural Support for Highway Signs, Luminaires, and Traffic Signals. (Fatigue requirements of Chapter 11 are not considered.) Wind speed = 100 mph.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Bar reinforcement shall conform to ASTM A615 Grade 60.</p>
<p>2. All soils supporting and surrounding the foundation shall be undisturbed natural soil or 90% compacted fill.</p>
<p>3. Foundation concrete shall be 560-C-3250.</p></details>
