<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-57.2C shows a temporary traffic signal on a Type CD954 standard without a luminaire arm. It sits on a 60 in. steel plate with eight precast concrete blocks, 3/8 in. cable loops and a 3/8 in. coil chain, with a mast arm up to 15 ft long and 17 ft 6 in. min. head clearance. Plan, elevation and Section A-A are given with three notes.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 47)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: a 60 in. x 60 in. square footing assembly seen from above, made of precast concrete blocks laid in a 3 x 3 grid around the pole. Four 23 in. x 23 in. x 30 in. precast concrete blocks (4 req.) sit at the corners and four 23 in. x 13 in. x 30 in. precast concrete blocks (4 req.) sit at the middle of each side, with the pole in the center square. A 6 in. dimension is shown at the edge of the center block and a 6 in. dimension at the side. Steel angles 5 in. x 3 in. x 1/2 in. (typical) are shown at the corners and side midpoints, called out as weld angles to plate (revision 1 mark). A dashed square marks a 3/8 in. coil chain that ties the blocks together. A 2 in. hole is called out at the pole center. Four holes are drilled to accommodate a 1-1/8 in. bolt size on an 11-1/2 in. bolt circle. Section marks A and A are shown on the left and right of the plan.</p>
<p>Elevation: a Type CD954 standard (without luminaire arm) on a temporary base at the roadway. A mast arm up to 15&#x27;-0&quot; max. long reaches left from the pole and carries one all-12 in. section signal head at its end. The signal head has three circles with two arrows in the lower rows. Temporary overhead (O.H.) signal wires with a maximum span of 100 feet are looped at the pole top. A vertical dimension of 17&#x27;-6&quot; min. runs from the roadway to the bottom of the mast arm head. On the pole are 2 signal heads, each with five 12 in. sections (three round, two arrow), a street name sign, and 2 pedestrian heads. A callout reads See Plan and Section A-A, pointing at the base plate.</p>
<p>Section A-A: a vertical cut through the base. A 60 in. x 60 in. x 3/4 in. steel plate sits on an A.C. or P.C.C. surface over a mortar leveling bed. Blocks stand 30 in. high on the plate, each with a 3/8 in. cable embedded 12 in. deep forming a loop 4 in. above the block (8 req.). The pole is bolted to the plate with 1-1/8 in. x 4 in. hex. head bolts, hex. nuts and flat washers (4 req.), and 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/4 in. plate washers (4 req.). A 1/4 in. weld is shown typical. A level indicator is shown on the plate.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Signal, Temporary for Type CD954 Std. (Without Luminaire Arm), drawing number S-57.2C, approved August 8, 1984, Donald R. Howery, General Manager. Structural design checked 8-7-84 by P. H. Skarin, Div. Engr. Revisions list through 12-07-07 (updated mast arm head clearance and reference to standard drawings). Page number 40.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. The CD954 standard (without luminaire arm) shall conform to Dept. of Transportation, City of Los Angeles, standard dwg. # S-51.9.6 (foundation excluded).</p>
<p>2. The mast arm length shall not exceed 15&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>3. There shall be a maximum of 2 - 5 section signal heads and 2 - ped. heads mounted to the shaft and one 5-section signal head mounted at the end of mast arm.</p>
<p>Callouts: Tie with 3/8 in. coil chain. 60 in. x 60 in. Weld angles to plate, 5 in. x 3 in. x 1/2 in. typical (revision 1). 23 in. x 23 in. x 30 in. precast conc. block (4 req.). 23 in. x 13 in. x 30 in. precast conc. block (4 req.). 2 in. hole. Drill holes (4 req.) to accommodate 1-1/8 in. bolt size and 11-1/2 in. bolt circle. 3/8 in. cable, embed 12 in. deep (8 req.). Type CD954 std. (revision 2). 1-1/8 in. x 4 in. hex. hd. bolt, hex. nut, flat washer (4 req.). Weld bolts and 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/4 in. plate washer to 3/4 in. plate (revision 1). Mortar leveling bed, wedge edges until mortar sets. (Mortar is not required on C.A.B., dirt or when slope is less than 1/4 in. per ft.) 60 in. x 60 in. x 3/4 in. steel plate. Coat plate assembly with primer and traffic signal yellow paint (revision 1). Temporary O.H. signal wires (maximum span 100 feet). All 12 in. sections. 15&#x27;-0&quot; max. 17&#x27;-6&quot; min. 2 signal heads. Street name sign. 2 pedestrian heads. See plan and Section A-A. Roadway. Type CD 954 Std. (without luminaire arm) (revision 2).</p></details>
