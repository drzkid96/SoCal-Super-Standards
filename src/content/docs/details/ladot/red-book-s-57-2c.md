## Sheet

<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. The CD954 standard (without luminaire arm) shall conform to Dept. of Transportation, City of Los Angeles, standard dwg. # S-51.9.6 (foundation excluded).</p>
<p>2. The mast arm length shall not exceed 15&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>3. There shall be a maximum of 2 - 5 section signal heads and 2 - ped. heads mounted to the shaft and one 5-section signal head mounted at the end of mast arm.</p>
<p>Callouts: Tie with 3/8 in. coil chain. 60 in. x 60 in. Weld angles to plate, 5 in. x 3 in. x 1/2 in. typical (revision 1). 23 in. x 23 in. x 30 in. precast conc. block (4 req.). 23 in. x 13 in. x 30 in. precast conc. block (4 req.). 2 in. hole. Drill holes (4 req.) to accommodate 1-1/8 in. bolt size and 11-1/2 in. bolt circle. 3/8 in. cable, embed 12 in. deep (8 req.). Type CD954 std. (revision 2). 1-1/8 in. x 4 in. hex. hd. bolt, hex. nut, flat washer (4 req.). Weld bolts and 4 in. x 4 in. x 1/4 in. plate washer to 3/4 in. plate (revision 1). Mortar leveling bed, wedge edges until mortar sets. (Mortar is not required on C.A.B., dirt or when slope is less than 1/4 in. per ft.) 60 in. x 60 in. x 3/4 in. steel plate. Coat plate assembly with primer and traffic signal yellow paint (revision 1). Temporary O.H. signal wires (maximum span 100 feet). All 12 in. sections. 15&#x27;-0&quot; max. 17&#x27;-6&quot; min. 2 signal heads. Street name sign. 2 pedestrian heads. See plan and Section A-A. Roadway. Type CD 954 Std. (without luminaire arm) (revision 2).</p></details>
