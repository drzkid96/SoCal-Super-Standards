<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-79.8 showing an overhead service from a Department of Water and Power (DWP) pole to a PB-3 service pullbox. DWP brings 3 #2 aluminum wires down the pole in a 2 in. conduit and splices 12 in. #6 copper tails, which feed in-line 60 Amp fuses for the traffic signal, bonded neutral and 3 #6 wires for street lighting. The pullbox is 5 to 15 ft from the pole and 14 in. minimum deep, with a ground rod and bonding wire.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 93)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Wiring diagram: Across the top is a Pullbox Cover labeled &#x27;Service T.S. &amp; S.L.&#x27; (traffic signal and street lighting). Below it, three &#x27;Alum. wires by DWP&#x27; (3 #2) drop from the service pole, the center one marked Neutral. At the end of each, 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP are spliced. Each of the two outer legs splits into two copper wires, each with a 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals, giving #6 traffic signal service wires, and a #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified) on the right leg. The neutral leg is bonded to the ground rod (Bond neutral to ground rod) and continues as the neutral service wire. 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (Bureau of Street Lighting, see Street Lighting Plan) run from the street lighting side of the fuses.</p>
<p>Section: A service pole stands at the left with a 2&quot; conduit running 10&#x27;-0&quot; down its face, then bending at the base and running underground toward the pullbox. The conduit run from pole to pullbox is 5&#x27;-0&quot; to 15&#x27;-0&quot;. The Service Pullbox PB-3 is set flush with the grade, 14&quot; MIN. deep. Inside it a bonding wire joins the conduit bushings, a ground rod is installed below, one Traffic Signal service conduit (2&quot;) enters and one street lighting service conduit enters when specified on the Bureau of Street Lighting plan.</p>
<p>Title block: Over Head Service, Dept. of Water &amp; Power, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-79.8. Drawn MT 9-14-11, checked RSM 9-14-11. Approved 10-27-11, Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Printed page number 86.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Department of Water and Power (DWP) to install 3 #2 stranded aluminum wires from service pole to pullbox. At the terminus of each #2 aluminum wire the Department of Water and Power to splice 2 #6 stranded copper wires, (12&quot; long) using compression type connectors. The exposed ends of each copper wire shall be taped when not in use.</p>
<p>2. Install continuous and dedicated #6 green service ground conductor to controller cabinet.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction, Sec. 307-2.8.</p>
<p>4. See Standard Drawing S-79.9B for grounding details.</p>
<p>Callouts: Pullbox cover Service T.S. &amp; S.L. 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP. 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals. Service pole. #6 traffic signal service wires. 2&quot; conduit. 3 #2 alum. wires by DWP. Neutral. Bond neutral to ground rod. #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified). 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (See Street Lighting Plan). Service pullbox PB-3. Bonding wire. Install ground rod. One street lighting service conduit when specified on Bureau of Street Lighting plan. One traffic signal service conduit (2&quot;).</p></details>
