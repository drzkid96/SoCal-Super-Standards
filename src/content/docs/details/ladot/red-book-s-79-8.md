<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Department of Water and Power (DWP) to install 3 #2 stranded aluminum wires from service pole to pullbox. At the terminus of each #2 aluminum wire the Department of Water and Power to splice 2 #6 stranded copper wires, (12&quot; long) using compression type connectors. The exposed ends of each copper wire shall be taped when not in use.</li>
<li value="2">Install continuous and dedicated #6 green service ground conductor to controller cabinet.</li>
<li value="3">See Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction, Sec. 307-2.8.</li>
<li value="4">See Standard Drawing S-79.9B for grounding details.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Pullbox cover Service T.S. &amp; S.L. 6 #6 standard copper wires (12&quot; long) by DWP. 60 Amp fuse in-line waterproof fuseholder for traffic signals. Service pole. #6 traffic signal service wires. 2&quot; conduit. 3 #2 alum. wires by DWP. Neutral. Bond neutral to ground rod. #6 spare for traffic signal (when specified). 3 #6 wires for B.S.L. (See Street Lighting Plan). Service pullbox PB-3. Bonding wire. Install ground rod. One street lighting service conduit when specified on Bureau of Street Lighting plan. One traffic signal service conduit (2&quot;).</div>
</details>
