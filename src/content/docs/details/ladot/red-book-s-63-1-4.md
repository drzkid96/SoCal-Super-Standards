<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Sheet 1 of 2 of drawing S-63.1.4 shows five Pelco mast arm signal mounting items, A through E, as isometric views. Item A is a 6 in. tenon mount, B and C are vertical arm mounts for signal heads, D is for electric signs and E is the standard clamp kit. The numbered notes are on sheet 2.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 53)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0053.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0053.webp" alt="S-63.1.4 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-63.1.4, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p53">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Item A (Pelco AB-3008): an isometric view of a mast arm mounting with a 6 in. long tenon, 2 3/8 in. outside diameter, held by two straps (bands) on a clamp.</p>
<p>Item B (Pelco AB-116): an exploded isometric view of a vertical gusseted tube with vinyl insert (typical), a bottom arm fixed to the tube with set screws (typical) and an upper arm that slides along the tube, with bolts, washers and round plates, and a toothed fitting at the arm end. Item E clamp (typical) is shown fastening the tube to a pole.</p>
<p>Item C (Pelco AB-103): a similar vertical tube with arms, long 14 in. arms, a toothed end, bolts and plates, and Item E clamp.</p>
<p>Item D (Pelco AB-0129 T-L) (modified): a vertical tube with arms with elbows at the top and bottom, aluminum lock nuts, square plates (Detail A, see Note No. 10) and 1-1/2 in. lock nuts (see Note No. 9), clamped to a pole with Item E.</p>
<p>Item E (Pelco AB-3004): an exploded view of a standard clamp kit with two U-bolts, nuts, washers, a clamp plate and two straps.</p>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 54)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0054.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0054.webp" alt="S-63.1.4 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-63.1.4, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p54">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Notes 1 to 10: ten notes describing mounting items A through E. They are listed in the notes field.</p>
<p>Detail A, 4 1/2 in. x 4 1/2 in. x 1/4 in. aluminum plate (not to scale): a square plate in plan, 4 1/2 in. by 4 1/2 in. It has four 3/8 in. diameter holes near the corners, each 3/4 in. typical from the edges, a central 2 in. diameter hole, and a 0.4 in. x 0.2 in. slot at the side of the central hole.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Special Mast Arm Mountings, sheet 2 of 2, drawing number S-63.1.4, drawn 5-7-98, approved May 27, 1998 by Thomas Conner, General Manager. Page number 47.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Item A is the basic mounting design for most mast arm signal installations when standard tenon location on the mast arm cannot be utilized. It uses a 6 in. tenon length with 2 3/8 in. outside diameter for plumbizer mounting.</p>
<p>2. Item B is used for installation of traffic signal heads, except programmed visibility (P.V.) heads, when it is desired to mount them horizontally or to adjust the height vertically by movement of Item E.</p>
<p>3. Item C is used for installation of horizontal or vertical programmed visibility (P.V.) heads. It is equipped with 14 in. arms which allow for replacement of signal lamps located in the rear of the signal head.</p>
<p>4. Item D is used for installation of electric signs.</p>
<p>5. Item E is a standard clamp kit designed to be used for fastening Items B, C and D to mast arms or poles in a vertical or horizontal position.</p>
<p>6. The bottom arms in Items B, C and D are stationary and are held in place with pipe threads and set screws. The upper arms slide along the shaft and are held in place with set screws.</p>
<p>7. The standard 29 in. band for Item A and E fits a 4 in. to 8.6 in. diameter. Band lengths up to 56 in. can be used for larger diameter installations.</p>
<p>8. Items A through E are available from Pelco Products Inc. or equivalent.</p>
<p>9. The 1 1/2 in. x 1 1/2 in. aluminum lock nut shown with Item D is commonly used for signal head and pedestrian head installations.</p>
<p>10. The 4 1/2 in. x 4 1/2 in. x 1/4 in. aluminum plate is used for sign box reinforcement at bracket installation location and must be specially fabricated.</p>
<p>Detail A: 4 1/2 in. x 4 1/2 in. x 1/4 in. aluminum plate, not to scale. 3/4 in. typical. 0.4 in. x 0.2 in. slot. 2 in. diameter hole. 3/8 in. diameter holes.</p></details>
