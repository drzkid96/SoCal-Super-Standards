<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Housing, visor and parts list for the LADOT standard electric sign enclosure, drawing S-58.21, approved June 26, 2008. The housing is 26-3/4 inches wide and 26-3/4 or 32-3/4 inches tall, 8 inches deep, with a 17 inch to 11 inch visor. A 26 item parts table gives materials and quantities, and Detail A gives the 4-1/2 inch reinforcement plate.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Front view: The door frame and housing with the label Message area (See notes) in the center. Overall width is 26-3/4&quot;. Height is 26-3/4&quot; or 32-3/4&quot; depending on the sign. A continuous hinge (item 14) runs down the left side. Three 1/4-turn link locks (item 15) sit on the right side. Balloons also point to item 23 on the right edge, item 18 at the lower right corner, item 19 along the bottom edge and item 24 at the bottom center, where a bracket and hose fitting are drawn at the top and bottom of the housing. Callouts: Heli-arc weld one side of corner post to door frame, both ends. Heli-arc weld seams of door frame as shown, hinge side only, use corner posts other side. Heli-arc weld all seams of housing, 4 corners. Section cut A-A is taken vertically through the center.</p>
<p>Section A-A: A side section of the enclosure. The visor projects 17&quot; from the face at the top and 11&quot; at the bottom, the housing is 8&quot; deep, and the visor has a rounded bottom corner. Numbered balloons show the parts through the depth: door frame (2), housing (1), face plate (3), polycarbonate lens (4), visor (5), back plate (6), optics enclosure (7), transformer shelf (8), lamps (9), transformers (10), common ends (11), end tips (12), fiber optic bundles (13), door gasket (16), terminal block (17), reinforcement plate (20), screw (21), lens stiffener blocks (22), mounting bracket (25) and wire entry bushing (26). The mounting bracket and pipe arms are drawn on the rear of the housing.</p>
<p>Detail A, reinforcement plate (not to scale): A 4-1/2&quot; by 4-1/2&quot; plate. The center has a 2&quot; dia. hole with a 0.4&quot; x 0.2&quot; slot. Four 3/8&quot; dia. holes are in the corners, set 3/4&quot; (Typ.) from each edge.</p>
