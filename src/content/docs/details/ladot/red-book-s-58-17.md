<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: A: Lunar white, R3-5L (left arrow with ONLY). Width of arrow is 1.5&quot; stroke. B: Lunar white, R3-5R (right arrow with ONLY). Letters are series &quot;C&quot;, 1&quot; stroke.</li>
<li value="2">Messages shall be formed with rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</li>
<li value="3">Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernable and readable message.</li>
<li value="4">Sign shall completely blank out when not energized.</li>
<li value="5">Door frame, face plate, and interior of housing and visor shall be painted flat black, non-reflective finish.</li>
<li value="6">Visor enclosure shall be fastened securely to the front frame of the sign.</li>
<li value="7"><strong>Refer to LADOT Std.</strong> Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for mounting assembly.</li>
<li value="8"><strong>Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No.</strong> 82-049-03.</li>
<li value="9"><strong>For housing description, see LADOT Std.</strong> Dwg. No. S-58.21.</li>
</ol>
</details>
