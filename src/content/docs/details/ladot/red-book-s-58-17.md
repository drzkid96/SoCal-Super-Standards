<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Fiber optic electric sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-5.svg">R3-5</a> (lane use arrow with ONLY), drawing S-58.17, approved July 9, 2008. The sign face is 24 inches wide by 30 inches tall with a curved arrow and the word ONLY, and the visor is 24 inches wide, 30 inches tall and 17 to 11 inches deep. Notes cover the lunar white messages, fiber optic spacing of 0.50 inch, black finish and conformance to specification 82-049-03.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 119)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0119.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0119.webp" alt="S-58.17 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-58.17, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p119">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign face: A rectangle 24&quot; wide by 30&quot; high with a dotted curved left arrow above the word ONLY. The solid dotted arrow points left, and a dashed outline of the mirror image right arrow is drawn over it to show the alternate message. Vertical dimensions from top: 3&quot; from the top edge to the top of the arrow, 15-1/2&quot; arrow height, 3&quot; gap, 6&quot; letter height for ONLY, 2-1/2&quot; to the bottom edge, 30&quot; overall. The arrow group is 13-1/2&quot; wide. Lower left shows the notes.</p>
<p>Visor enclosure: An isometric view of a three-sided hood with a top panel and two side panels that taper from 17&quot; deep at the top to 11&quot; deep at the bottom. Overall 24&quot; wide and 30&quot; tall. The lower front corner of each side panel has a 2&quot; Rad. rounded corner.</p>
<p>Title block: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-5.svg">R3-5</a> Electric Sign, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-58.17. Drawn MT 7-08-08, checked RAR 7-09-08, T. E. JV 7-09-08, Sr. T. E. JW 7-09-08, Pr. T. E. SS 7-09-08. Approved July 9, 2008, signed for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Printed page number 112.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages: A: Lunar white, R3-5L (left arrow with ONLY). Width of arrow is 1.5&quot; stroke. B: Lunar white, R3-5R (right arrow with ONLY). Letters are series &quot;C&quot;, 1&quot; stroke.</p>
<p>2. Messages shall be formed with rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</p>
<p>3. Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernable and readable message.</p>
<p>4. Sign shall completely blank out when not energized.</p>
<p>5. Door frame, face plate, and interior of housing and visor shall be painted flat black, non-reflective finish.</p>
<p>6. Visor enclosure shall be fastened securely to the front frame of the sign.</p>
<p>7. Refer to LADOT Std. Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for mounting assembly.</p>
<p>8. Sign shall conform to LADOT Specification No. 82-049-03.</p>
<p>9. For housing description, see LADOT Std. Dwg. No. S-58.21.</p></details>
