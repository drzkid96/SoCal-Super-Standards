<p class="dwg-sum">Shows a 36 in diameter round concrete footing, 60 in deep with 4 in min. topping, for a signal or street light pole with an 11 1/2 in bolt circle. It lists the rebar, anchor bolts, 1 in street light conduit and 2 in traffic signal conduit. Conduit stubs run toward the nearest pullbox, and the bolt circle must be coordinated with the light pole base plate.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 45)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Foundation details. Sheet 4 of 4.</p>
<p>Plan: 36 in dia. footing (min. 3000 psi concrete), 11 1/2 in bolt circle, 8 1/8 in sq. base, anchor bolts at 45 degrees.</p>
<p>Section: 36 in from curb. Bonding strap or wire. Bushing. 4 in min. topping. 2 3/4 in. 14 in min. to conduit. 60 in footing depth. 6 in min. 36 in dia. 3 in clearance. 2 in clearance. (8) #10 long reinforced. #4 ties at 9 in OC typ. (provide (2) ties at 2 in OC at top). 1 in dia. street light conduit. 2 in dia. traffic signal conduit. Anchor bolts 1 1/8 in x 40 in x 4 in (2 nuts and 2 washers required per bolt) (ASTM F1554 Gr 55 bolts).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Use 1 in galvanized conduit for street lighting and 2 in schedule 80 PVC conduit for traffic signal unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Conduit stubs from foundation shall be in the direction of the nearest corresponding pullbox unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>3. Topping shall be considered as a part of the foundation.</p>
<p>4. Coordinate bolt circle with light pole base plate prior to anchor bolt installation.</p>
<p>5. Coordinate conduit size and placement with electrical prior to installation.</p></details>
