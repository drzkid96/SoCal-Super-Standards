<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Raintight box with three knockouts in bottom for 3/4, 1, 1 1/4 or 1 1/2 inch conduit, U.L. approved. 10&quot;W x 12&quot;H x 4&quot;D. Box to be galvanized ASTM Spec. A-525. Similar to circle A-W catalog number 10124 RTSC.</li>
<li value="2">Install one nipple with two 3/4&quot; bushings for each 1&quot; galvanized conduit installed as shown on plans.</li>
<li value="3">Raintight box shall be drilled or otherwise configured to accept a padlock.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Type 332 controller cabinet. Front. Nipple with two 3/4&quot; bushings, see Note 2. 1/2&quot; hole with rubber bushing. 1&quot; grounding bushing, see Note 2. 1&quot; galvanized conduit, see Note 2. Coupling. 18&quot; minimum. 30&quot;. 1&quot;. To telephone Co. manhole or pullbox or to Dept. of Transportation pullbox.</div>
</details>
