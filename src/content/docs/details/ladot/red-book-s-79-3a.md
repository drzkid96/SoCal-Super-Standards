<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-79.3A showing the telephone service connection for an ATSAC Type 332 controller cabinet. A galvanized raintight box 10 in. W x 12 in. H x 4 in. D is mounted on the cabinet side, with a 1 in. galvanized conduit running down to a coupling and 18 in. minimum underground to a telephone company or Department of Transportation pullbox. The box is drilled for a padlock and uses a nipple with two 3/4 in. bushings for each conduit.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 97)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0097.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0097.webp" alt="S-79.3A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.3A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p97">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Front view: The front of a Type 332 controller cabinet on its foundation, with the door closed and handle at the right. A raintight box is mounted on the right side of the cabinet near the top, with two small holes: &#x27;Nipple with two 3/4&quot; bushings, See Note 2&#x27; and &#x27;1/2&quot; hole with rubber bushing&#x27;. A 1&quot; grounding bushing (See Note 2) is at the bottom of the box. A 1&quot; galvanized conduit (See Note 2) runs down the cabinet side, through a coupling at the foundation top, then bends and runs underground at 18&quot; MIN. depth &#x27;To Telephone Co. Manhole or Pullbox or to Dept. of Transportation Pullbox&#x27;.</p>
<p>Side view: The same box and conduit seen from the side of the cabinet. The box top is 30&quot; below the cabinet top and the conduit is offset 1&quot; from the box edge. The 1&quot; galvanized conduit drops to the coupling at the foundation and down into the ground. Labels match the front view: Type 332 Controller Cabinet, nipple with two 3/4&quot; bushings, 1/2&quot; hole with rubber bushing, 1&quot; grounding bushing, 1&quot; galvanized conduit, coupling, 18&quot; MIN.</p>
<p>Title block: Service, Telephone ATSAC 332 Cabinet. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, S. E. Rowe, General Manager, approved 12-19-89. Drawing No. S-79.3A. Drawn RM 12-1-89, checked JEM 12-12-89, supervised AN 12-18-89. Printed page number 90.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Raintight box with three knockouts in bottom for 3/4, 1, 1 1/4 or 1 1/2 inch conduit, U.L. approved. 10&quot;W x 12&quot;H x 4&quot;D. Box to be galvanized ASTM Spec. A-525. Similar to circle A-W catalog number 10124 RTSC.</p>
<p>2. Install one nipple with two 3/4&quot; bushings for each 1&quot; galvanized conduit installed as shown on plans.</p>
<p>3. Raintight box shall be drilled or otherwise configured to accept a padlock.</p>
<p>Callouts: Type 332 controller cabinet. Front. Nipple with two 3/4&quot; bushings, see Note 2. 1/2&quot; hole with rubber bushing. 1&quot; grounding bushing, see Note 2. 1&quot; galvanized conduit, see Note 2. Coupling. 18&quot; minimum. 30&quot;. 1&quot;. To telephone Co. manhole or pullbox or to Dept. of Transportation pullbox.</p></details>
