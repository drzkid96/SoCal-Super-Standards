<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Supplemental Design Guide Detail 100 (2 sheets) showing constrained and unconstrained geometry for channelized right turns, plus the S-527.0 sheet 2 detail for a Type II protected intersection and a temporary/retrofit curb extension. Constrained design uses R = 25 ft to 40 ft corners and a right-angle approach with 48 in landings at curb ramps. A mountable truck apron is provided if a lane wider than 14 ft is needed for the design vehicle. The protected intersection detail shows a 9 ft minimum and 5 ft minimum bikeway, 25 ft radii and green colored surface recommended.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 3 (document page 101)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0101.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0101.webp" alt="S-442 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-442, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/06-pages-91-108/#p101">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Upper plan view, channelized right turn at a right angle approach (no separate heading is printed above it): A triangular island between the right turn slip lane and the through lanes with integral curb and gutter, R=2&#x27; typical at the island tip. The island has 4&#x27; minimum on both sides of the ramp landing, a 5&#x27; minimum width, 2&#x27; minimum and 5&#x27; minimum dimensions at the crosswalk, and a 48&quot; landing typical at the top of all curb ramps, see note 2. Landings are 2% max slope. Crosswalk cross slope along the turn is 2% typical, 5% max. The island edge along the slip lane is 20&#x27; minimum. The island approach angle is 55 degrees to 70 degrees. The turn lane is 11&#x27; minimum, 14&#x27; maximum (see note 1). Turn lane radius is R=150&#x27; to 275&#x27;. Corner radius is R=25&#x27; to 40&#x27;, see corner radius policy. Bollard callout: if &gt;9&#x27; wide, install bollard(s) in DWS at 2&#x27; maximum from the flow line, allowing a minimum of 4&#x27; path in between bollards (shown at two ramps). A unidirectional curb ramp without flares, typical, see S-442, serves the island and a parallel curb ramp, see S-442, serves the corner.</p>
<p>Lower plan view, constrained geometric design for channelized right turns: The same island layout with a tighter plan. It shows the 2&#x27; minimum, a 48&quot; landing typical at top of all curb ramps (see note 2), a curb ramp typical (see S-442), 20&#x27; minimum island length, the 55 degree to 70 degree angle, an 11&#x27; minimum to 14&#x27; maximum turn lane (see note 1), 2% typical and 5% max crosswalk slope, R=2&#x27; typical, integral curb and gutter, turn lane R=150&#x27; to 275&#x27;, narrow sidewalk (&lt;=9&#x27;), and a parallel curb ramp, see S-442. Pavement markings are drawn in gray: crosswalk bars, a right turn arrow and a left turn arrow.</p>
<p>Title block: Right Angle Approach, Supplemental Design Guide, Detail 100, sheet 1 of 2.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Provide mountable truck apron along outside curb line if lane width greater than 14&#x27; is needed to accommodate design vehicle.</p>
<p>2. Provide 48&quot; minimum clear space with 2% max. slope in all directions for landing area.</p>
<p>Drawing labels: Constrained geometric design for channelized right turns. Integral curb and gutter. R=2&#x27;, typ. Narrow sidewalk (&lt;=9&#x27;).</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 3 (document page 102)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0102.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0102.webp" alt="S-442 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-442, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/06-pages-91-108/#p102">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, unconstrained geometric design for channelized right turns: A right turn slip lane with a raised island, bike lane, grass buffer and sidewalk along the curve. Across the top a bike lane crossing and a pedestrian crossing run side by side with a 3&#x27; minimum gap between them. A detectable warning surface is at the ramp, with 6&#x27; minimum and 4&#x27; minimum on both sides dimensions at the crossing, and a section cut A/2 through the island. A non-walkable surface, raised divisional island or curbing (typical) wraps the island with 5&#x27; minimum and 8&#x27; minimum dimensions at the crossing and R=5&#x27; minimum and R=10&#x27; minimum on the island corners. R=2&#x27; typical at the island tip, integral curb and gutter, and an 11&#x27; minimum to 14&#x27; maximum lane (see note 1). Other callouts: unidirectional curb ramp without flares, typical, see S-442. 55 degrees to 70 degrees island angle, 20&#x27; minimum, 4&#x27; minimum at the ramp. R=25&#x27; to 40&#x27;, see corner radius policy. 150&#x27; to 275&#x27; radius at the outside of the turn. Labels along the curve: grass buffer, bike lane and sidewalk. (Optional) raised crosswalk and bike crossing, see Supplemental Design Guide Detail 600.</p>
<p>Section A (A/2), typical section at pedestrian/bicyclist island, steep road cross slopes, from left to right: repair or reconstruct street paving as necessary, 2&#x27; min, typical, then integral curb and gutter typical, a 6&#x27; MIN. non-walkable surface, a battered curb without gutter (TYP), see Supplemental Design Guide Detail 400, an 8&#x27; MIN. bike lane, a 3&#x27; space with non-walkable surface and a warning curb, a sidewalk curb ramp, a variable width island and the right turn lane, ending at an integral curb and gutter.</p>
<p>Title block: Supplemental Design Guide, Detail 100, sheet 2 of 2.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Provide mountable truck apron along outside curb line if lane width greater than 14&#x27; is needed to accommodate design vehicle.</p>
<p>2. Provide 48&quot; minimum clear space with 2% max. slope in all directions for landing area.</p>
<p>Drawing labels: Unconstrained geometric design for channelized right turns. Typical section A, pedestrian/bicyclist island, steep road cross slopes.</p></details>
