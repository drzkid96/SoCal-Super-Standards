<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Supplemental Design Guide Detail 100 (2 sheets) showing constrained and unconstrained geometry for channelized right turns, plus the S-527.0 sheet 2 detail for a Type II protected intersection and a temporary/retrofit curb extension. Constrained design uses R = 25 ft to 40 ft corners and a right-angle approach with 48 in landings at curb ramps. A mountable truck apron is provided if a lane wider than 14 ft is needed for the design vehicle. The protected intersection detail shows a 9 ft minimum and 5 ft minimum bikeway, 25 ft radii and green colored surface recommended.</p>
