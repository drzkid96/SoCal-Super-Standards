<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-75.9A shows the Type 337 post-top mounted traffic signal controller cabinet, 20 in wide, 17 in deep and 35 in high. It calls out lifting plates, doors, police door, vents, 3-point latch with padlock hasp, and gives the bottom bolt pattern for post-top mounting. The cabinet uses foundation F-8 and has 19 in standard rack rails.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 77)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0077.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0077.webp" alt="S-75.9A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-75.9A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p77">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Isometric view of the cabinet: a tall rectangular outdoor cabinet shown with its front door and rear door open slightly. Overall dimensions are 20&quot; wide on the front face, 17&quot; deep and 35&quot; high. Features called out: Removable lifting plates per S-68.1 on top; Identification (required on front door), a CITY OF LOS ANGELES plate on the front door; Police door with shroud to provide access to switches and indicators only (required on front door); Front door; Rear door; Fan Vent on the side; Louvered vents with 20 in2 min. flow area (required in front door) near the bottom of the front door; and a door handle with the note: Cabinet doors shall be secured by a 3-point latch mechanism. Latch handle shall be equipped with hasp for addition of pad lock (required on front and rear doors).</p>
<p>Bottom view (post-top mount): a square plan of the cabinet base, 10&quot; from center to edge each way as shown (10&quot; and 6 1/2&quot; each side of center line). It has a central 3 1/2&quot; dia. opening, a 5 1/4&quot; dia. bolt circle, four 9/16&quot; dia. holes equally spaced at 45 degrees on the bolt circle, and four 1/8&quot; dia. drainage holes for the mounting adapter. Hole positions are dimensioned 6 1/2&quot; and 6 1/2&quot; horizontally from the centerline, 1 3/4&quot; and 8 1/2&quot; vertically, and 3 1/2&quot; between the two upper and lower drainage holes.</p>
<p>Table 1, foundation: Type 337 (post-top mounted), Foundation F-8.</p>
<p>Cabinet details: dimension tolerance +/-0.125 inch.</p>
<p>Title block: 337 Cabinet (Post-top mounted), City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved May 15, 1984 by Donald R. Howery, General Manager, Drawing No. S-75.9A. Revisions: 2-15-85, 9-18-86, 3-4-87, and 8-2-07 (change 170 to 337, SS).</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Cabinet construction, ventilation requirements and other necessary accessories shall conform to the latest specification for &quot;Cabinet Assembly - Type 337 post-top mounted&quot;, Dept. of Transportation, City of Los Angeles.</p>
<p>2. Cabinet shall be equipped with rails for mounting equipment. Rails shall be of standard 19&quot; rack configuration.</p>
<p>Cabinet details: dimension tolerance +/-0.125 inch.</p>
<p>Callouts: Removable lifting plates per S-68.1. Identification (required on front door). Police door with shroud to provide access to switches and indicators only (required on front door). Louvered vents with 20 in2 min. flow area (required in front door). Cabinet doors shall be secured by a 3-point latch mechanism. Latch handle shall be equipped with hasp for addition of pad lock (required on front and rear doors). 1/8&quot; dia. 4 holes required for drainage / M-found. adapter [text partly unclear].</p>
<p>Table: Type 337 (post-top mounted), foundation F-8.</p></details>
