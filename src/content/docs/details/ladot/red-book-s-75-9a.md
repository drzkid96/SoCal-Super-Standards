<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Cabinet construction, ventilation requirements and other necessary accessories shall conform to the latest specification for &quot;Cabinet Assembly - Type 337 post-top mounted&quot;, Dept. of Transportation, City of Los Angeles.</li>
<li value="2">Cabinet shall be equipped with rails for mounting equipment. Rails shall be of standard 19&quot; rack configuration.</li>
</ol>
<p>Cabinet details: dimension tolerance +/-0.125 inch.</p>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Removable lifting plates per S-68.1. Identification (required on front door). Police door with shroud to provide access to switches and indicators only (required on front door). Louvered vents with 20 in2 min. flow area (required in front door). Cabinet doors shall be secured by a 3-point latch mechanism. Latch handle shall be equipped with hasp for addition of pad lock (required on front and rear doors). 1/8&quot; dia. 4 holes required for drainage / M-found. adapter [text partly unclear].</div>
<p>Table: Type 337 (post-top mounted), foundation F-8.</p>
</details>
