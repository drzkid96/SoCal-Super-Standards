<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-78.5.1 showing the lid and a cross section of the LADOT Type PB-2 and PB-3 pull boxes. The lid is cast or stamped with 1 in. letters reading TRAFFIC SIGNAL. The section shows the concrete walls and lid, conduit entries and a 1 in. crushed rock base. Outside dimensions are 15 in. x 25 in. for PB-2 and 22 in. x 34 in. for PB-3, and conduits are bonded with ground strap or spliced #8 green grounds.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 80)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0080.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0080.webp" alt="S-78.5.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-78.5.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p80">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of lid: A rounded-rectangle pull box lid with an inner recessed panel. Text &#x27;TRAFFIC SIGNAL&#x27; is cast or stamped across the lid with a small rectangle symbol at each end of the text. A callout reads &#x27;1&quot; letters cast or stamped in lid&#x27;.</p>
<p>Section: A cross section at the box edge, below a &#x27;Finish grade&#x27; line. A concrete box wall is shown at each side, 12&quot; tall with a stepped inner face. A concrete lid spans the top with a semicircular notch near each end. Dimensions: 2&quot; MIN. lid bearing at each wall, 5&quot; MIN. from the lid underside down to the conduit, 2&quot; MIN. below that, 6&quot; MIN. from the wall to the edge of the base, 12&quot; wall height and 24&quot; MIN. overall depth to the bottom of the rock bed. A conduit enters at each side, bending down and away, with a bell end inside the box, marked &#x27;See Note 3&#x27;. The box sits on a bed of 1&quot; crushed rock.</p>
<p>Title block: Pull Box, Types PB-2 &amp; PB-3, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-78.5.1. Drawn MT 11-8-11, Pr. T. E. SS 12-8-11. Approved 12-8-11, signed for Jaime de la Vega, General Manager. Printed page number 73.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Pullbox details not shown on this plan shall conform to Department (Dept.) of Public Works Standard Drawing # L-201-0.</p>
<p>2. Outside dimension: Type PB2 is 15&quot; x 25&quot;, Type PB3 is 22&quot; x 34&quot;.</p>
<p>3. Galvanized conduits must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>Callouts: 1&quot; letters cast or stamped in lid. Finish grade. 1&quot; crushed rock.</p></details>
