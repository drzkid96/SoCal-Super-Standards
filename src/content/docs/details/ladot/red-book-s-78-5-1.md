<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Section of the standard traffic signal pull box with a lid marked TRAFFIC SIGNAL in 1 inch letters, set flush with finished grade on crushed rock. Outside dimensions are 15 by 25 inches for Type PB2 and 22 by 34 inches for Type PB3. Conduit bonding requirements are given in the notes.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Traffic signal pull box section. 1&quot; letters cast or stamped in lid reading TRAFFIC SIGNAL. Finish grade at top of box (see Note 3). Crushed rock under box, 6&quot; min.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Pullbox details not shown on this plan shall conform to Dept. of Public Works Standard Drawing # L-201-0.</p>
<p>2. Outside dimension: Type PB2 - 15&quot;x25&quot;, Type PB3 - 22&quot;x34&quot;.</p>
<p>3. Galvanized conduits must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p></details>
