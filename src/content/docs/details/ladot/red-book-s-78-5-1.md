<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Pullbox details not shown on this plan shall conform to Department (Dept.) of Public Works Standard Drawing # L-201-0.</li>
<li value="2">Outside dimension: Type PB2 is 15&quot; x 25&quot;, Type PB3 is 22&quot; x 34&quot;.</li>
<li value="3">Galvanized conduits must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> 1&quot; letters cast or stamped in lid. Finish grade. 1&quot; crushed rock.</div>
</details>
