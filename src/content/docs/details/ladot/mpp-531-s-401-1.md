<div class="dwg-summary"><p><strong>Overview:</strong> Provides typical striping configurations for Secondary Highways (Avenues II/III) with narrower curb-to-curb widths, balancing through lanes, two-way left-turn lanes, and on-street parking.</p><p class="dwg-use"><strong>Typical Use Cases:</strong> Secondary corridor slurry/resurfacing striping, buffered bike-lane retrofits, and arterial channelization.</p></div>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (page 42)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0042.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0042.webp" alt="S-401.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>MPP 531 S-401.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/mpp-531/03-drawings-s-401-0-to-s-404-3/#p42">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Selection of length of left turn pocket lane depends on demand, spacing between intersections and location of critical driveways to be served by two-way left turn lanes (2WLTL). The desirable length at signalized intersections is at least 40&#x27; of storage per vehicle per signal cycle averaged during the peak hour. However, this desirable length often may not be feasible. Minimum lengths at signalized intersections on highways with continuous median channelization are: 100&#x27; at major highways, 100&#x27; at secondary highways, 60&#x27; at collector streets, 40&#x27; at local streets. Minimum pocket lengths for secondary highways without continuous median channelization may be less, depending on turning volumes and parking impacts.</li>
<li value="2">Utilize 60&#x27; reversals for posted speed limits of 35 mph and less, and 80&#x27; reversals for posted speed limits of 40 mph and greater. A 40&#x27; minimum (MIN.) reversal may be used in exceptional situations for critical driveways.</li>
<li value="3">Selection of an open-ended (as shown in top drawing) versus closed-ended (as shown in middle drawing) 2WLTL is dependent, in part, on the proximity of a critical driveway to be served by a 2WLTL. Minimum distances to critical driveways are shown.</li>
<li value="4">The painted reversal for a single left turn lane may be eliminated at a signalized intersection on a selective basis if spillover is recurrent and if it is not feasible to lengthen the left-turn pocket.</li>
</ol>
</details>
