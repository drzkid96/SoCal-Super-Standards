<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan layouts for pedestrian crossing islands in a street, with directional curb ramp (Case A) and diagonal curb ramp (Case B) options. Islands are 6' minimum wide, or less than 6' with an optional treatment, with an 8' minimum total island length and 4' minimum cut-through. Yellow mountable rubber speed humps, channelizing bollards, 12" solid yellow lines spaced 20' c-c, 4" solid yellow center line 1' off the island, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-1.svg">OM1-1</a> signs, and detectable warning surfaces are shown. Crossing island is also shown with a hardened centerline and curb options.</p>
