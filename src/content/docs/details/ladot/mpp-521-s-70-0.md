</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Single lane approaches: For single lane approaches, the number of pairs of loop detectors is based on distance A measured between the double yellow center line (DYCL) and the curb face. A single pair shall be used for distance A less than 20 ft. Dual pairs shall be used for distance A greater than or equal to 20 ft. Loop detectors should be installed as specified in the tables below.</p>
<p>Case A: Single pair. Distance between DYCL and curb face (A) / distance between inner loop and curb face (B):</p>
<p>10 ft / 3 ft</p>
<p>11 ft / 3 ft</p>
<p>12 ft / 3 ft</p>
<p>13 ft / 4 ft</p>
<p>14 ft / 5 ft</p>
<p>15 ft / 6 ft</p>
<p>16 ft / 6 ft</p>
<p>17 ft / 7 ft</p>
<p>18 ft / 8 ft</p>
<p>19 ft / 8 ft</p>
<p>Case B: Dual pair. Distance between DYCL and curb face (A) / distance between inner loop and curb face (B) / distance between outer loop and curb face (C):</p>
<p>20 ft / 3 ft / 12 ft</p>
<p>21 ft / 3 ft / 12 ft</p>
<p>22 ft / 3 ft / 13 ft</p>
<p>23 ft / 3 ft / 13 ft</p>
<p>24 ft / 4 ft / 14 ft</p>
<p>25 ft / 5 ft / 15 ft</p>
<p>Dimensions on the diagrams: 5 ft from limit line to crosswalk, 8 ft between front and rear loops. General notes for this drawing are on sheet 2 of 2.</p>
</details>

</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Multiple lane approaches: For multiple lane approaches, loop detectors should be centered in lanes. For curb lane, follow the guidelines for single lane approach. See guidelines on single lane approaches (callout at the curb lane loops). Loops are centered in lanes (callout).</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">This Standard Drawing applies to new installations and re-installations due to maintenance. Loop detectors should be re-installed at existing locations where the loop detectors extend 4 ft or more into marked crosswalks and limit lines.</li>
<li value="2">All loops are 6 ft diameter with 8 ft separation between front and rear loops.</li>
<li value="3">A, B, C, D dimensions shall be shown on plans.</li>
<li value="4">The loop detectors adjacent to the limit line shall be bicycle detectors.</li>
<li value="5"><strong>See Standard Drawing Nos.</strong> S-70.1A and S-70.1D for installation details.</li>
<li value="6">Types and locations of conduits, pullboxes and stub-outs to be installed per plan.</li>
</ol>
</details>
