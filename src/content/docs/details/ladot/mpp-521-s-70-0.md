<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT standard for placing inductive loop detectors at the limit line. On single-lane approaches, one pair of loops is used when the distance A from the double yellow center line to the curb face is less than 20 feet, and dual pairs when A is 20 feet or more, with spacing tables by A. On multiple-lane approaches loops are centered in lanes, and the curb lane follows the single-lane rules. All loops are 6 feet in diameter with 8 feet between front and rear loops, and loops next to the limit line are bicycle detectors.</p>

<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. This Standard Drawing applies to new installations and re-installations due to maintenance. Loop detectors should be re-installed at existing locations where the loop detectors extend 4 ft or more into marked crosswalks and limit lines.</p>
<p>2. All loops are 6 ft diameter with 8 ft separation between front and rear loops.</p>
<p>3. A, B, C, D dimensions shall be shown on plans.</p>
<p>4. The loop detectors adjacent to the limit line shall be bicycle detectors.</p>
<p>5. See Standard Drawing Nos. S-70.1A and S-70.1D for installation details.</p>
<p>6. Types and locations of conduits, pullboxes and stub-outs to be installed per plan.</p></details>
