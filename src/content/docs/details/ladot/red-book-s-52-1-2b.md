<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-52.1.2B showing the LADOT Type F-12A foundation used for the Type M controller cabinet. It is a tapered concrete block 8 in. above and 24 in. below sidewalk grade, 35 in. wide at top and 39 in. at base, with two 3 in. conduits, two 5/8 in. anchor bolts, two bushings, a bonding strap and an 8 ft copper weld ground rod. A slab per Standard Specifications for Public Works Construction section 302-6 is placed beside it.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 91)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A rectangular foundation top 22&quot; wide and about 39&quot; long, with a 36&quot; slab (per Note 2) beyond one end shown dashed with a break line and a 36&quot; dimension from the centerline to the curb face along the bottom. Inside the 22&quot; outline an 18&quot; inner rectangle shows the chamfered top. Two anchor bolts are 12&quot; apart (6&quot; each side of centerline) and three circles on the centerline show the two 3&quot; conduits and the ground rod. Other dimensions: 6&quot; MIN. from the end of the foundation to the nearest circle and 8&quot; MIN. from the centerline to the foundation edge. Callouts: Slab per note 2. Curb face.</p>
<p>Section: A tapered concrete foundation 35&quot; wide at the top and 39&quot; wide at the base, 8&quot; above sidewalk grade and 24&quot; below it, with the top 3&quot; above the plate and bushings. Two bushings and a bonding strap or wire are shown on the top plate. Two 3&quot; conduits rise from the foundation and bend out the side 14&quot; MIN. below sidewalk grade, with a 6&quot; MIN. dimension at the base. Two anchor bolts 5/8&quot; dia. x 16&quot;L (including 2&quot; bend) project 2&quot; above the top. A 1/2&quot; dia. x 8&#x27;-0&quot; copper weld ground rod extends below the foundation.</p>
<p>Title block: Foundation, Type F-12A, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-52.1.2B. Drawn MT 5-07-08, Pr. T.E. SS 6-26-08. Approved June 26, 2008, Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Printed page number 84.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Conduit consists of (2) 3&quot; nominal size unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. Slab shall be constructed as per Standard Specification for Public Works Construction, section 302-6, latest edition.</p>
<p>Callouts: Bolt, anchor 5/8&quot; dia. x 16&quot;L (including 2&quot; bend), 2 required. Bushing. Bonding strap or wire. Sidewalk grade. 3&quot; conduit. 1/2&quot; dia. x 8&#x27;-0&quot; copper weld ground rod. Curb face. Slab per note 2.</p></details>
