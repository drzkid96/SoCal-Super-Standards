<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.4A, sheet 1, shows detector loops for light rail trains. For existing embedded track a 30 in octagonal saw cut loop with 4 turns is used. For new ballasted track a preformed 30 in circular loop in a 40 in by 80 in concrete slab is used. Both connect through a type A detector handhole and 2 in Schedule 80 PVC conduit 30 in deep to a termination pullbox. Saw cut loops are for existing installations only.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 3 (document page 74)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0074.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0074.webp" alt="S-70.4A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.4A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p74">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-70.4A, Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 1 of 3. It has two installations, with sections.</p>
<p>LRT detector for existing track (embedded), plan: two parallel embedded tracks run vertically. Between them is an octagonal saw cut loop 30&quot; across, marked 4-turns of wire, with detector wires installed in saw cut (5&quot; deep). A lead-in runs 36&quot; from the loop down to a detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;, then horizontally to a termination pullbox with two conduits leaving it. Section C-C is cut through the loop, and section D-D is looked at from the handhole.</p>
<p>Section C-C: a saw cut slot filled with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of top (do not overfill). The slot is 5&quot; max. deep with the wires in the lower part at least 3&quot; min. below the surface, and 1/2&quot; max. wide. Four wires are shown stacked at the bottom.</p>
<p>Section D-D, embedded track: layers P.C.C. (concrete), A.B. (aggregate base) and subbase, with subballast bed, P.C.C. or asphalt concrete surface, embedded track rails, the saw cut loop (5&quot; max. below surface) and the detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;. A 2&quot; PVC Sched. 80 conduit, 30&quot; deep, runs from the handhole to the termination pull box, with 30&quot; min. cover.</p>
<p>LRT detector for new track (ballasted), plan: a rectangular concrete slab 40&quot; wide by 80&quot; long between two train tracks, crossing concrete track ties. A circular preformed loop 30&quot; in diameter (4-turns of wire, pre-formed loop) sits in the slab, with a lead 36&quot; long to the detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27; at the slab edge. Another run goes to a termination pullbox with two conduits. Reference: See Detail &#x27;A&#x27;. Section B-B cut.</p>
<p>Section B-B, ballasted track: preformed loop in a slab at the rail, over concrete track tie and ballasted or concrete track bed. 2&quot; PVC Sched. 80 conduit, 30&quot; deep, from the detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27; to the termination pull box (30&quot; min.).</p>
<p>Detail &#x27;A&#x27;, ballasted track: the slab with the loop inside, top of slab even with rail, 2&quot; from the top of slab to the loop, and slab depth varies.</p>
<p>Title block: Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 1/3, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 8-8-2014, Jon Kirk Mukri, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.4A. Drawn MT 8-7-14, T.E. BC 8-7-14, Pr. T.E. SS 8-7-14.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Saw cut loops (octagonal or circular) to be used for existing installations only.</p>
<p>2. See LADOT Std. Dwg. S-70.1.A for inductive loop installation details not shown hereon.</p>
<p>(Notes for the new track (ballasted) installation)</p>
<p>1. Preformed loop to be used for all new construction.</p>
<p>2. See current LADOT material specification for traffic loop.</p>
<p>3. See page 2 and 3 for preformed LRT loop details.</p>
<p>Callouts: 4-turns of wire. Embedded track. Install detector wires in saw cut (5&quot; deep). Fill slot with loop sealant to within 1/16&quot; of top (do not overfill). Termination pullbox. Conduit. Detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 3 (document page 75)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0075.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0075.webp" alt="S-70.4A sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.4A, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p75">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-70.4A, Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 2 of 3.</p>
<p>LRT detector for new track (embedded), plan: two parallel embedded tracks run vertically. Between them is a circular preformed loop 30&quot; in diameter with 4-turns of wire, marked section C-C. A lead 36&quot; long runs from the loop down to a detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27;, then horizontally to a termination pullbox with two conduits leaving it. Section A-A is cut at the handhole.</p>
<p>Section A-A, embedded track: layers of P.C.C., A.B. (aggregate base) and subbase over a subballast bed, with a P.C.C. or asphalt concrete surface and embedded track rails. The preformed loop is set in the surface between the rails, with 2&quot; dimensioned at the top. The detector handhole type &#x27;A&#x27; connects by 2&quot; PVC Sched. 80 conduit, 30&quot; deep, to the termination pull box, with 30&quot; min. cover at the pull box.</p>
<p>LRT preformed loop details: a block of text notes (notes 1 to 6) on materials and installation of the preformed loop. Continues on sheet 3 of 3.</p>
<p>Title block: Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 2/3, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.4A.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes (new track, embedded):</h5>
<p>1. Preformed loop to be used for new installations in embedded track.</p>
<p>2. No rebar or metal of any kind other than the track rails shall be placed within three feet (3&#x27;) horizontally or vertically from LRT loop.</p>
<p>3. See page 2 and 3 for preformed LRT loop details.</p>
<h5>LRT preformed loop details, notes:</h5>
<p>1. Pre-formed loop shall be constructed from 1/2&quot; I.D. polypropolene, with an O.D. of 3/4 inch.</p>
<p>2. Loop is to have one continuous #16 TFFN wire through the loop head and lead-in to prevent loop malfunction due to splicing. Loop shall have four (4) turns of wire.</p>
<p>3. The conduit shall be completely filled with hot, rubberized asphalt which will allow the loop to retain flexibility once cooled, prevent incursion of moisture and set the wire turns firmly in place.</p>
<p>4. Each loop head shall be provided with a 5&quot; contraction/expansion joint to allow for movement of pavement and to prevent breakage of the wire and/or conduit. The joint is to have a 9&quot; long by 3/4&quot; schedule 80 PVC cover slide to be placed over the joint for protection from the elements.</p>
<p>5. Encase lead-in wire in a non-conductive 250 psi flex hose, seamless, with fiber braid reinforcement and a non-conductive seamless extruded urethane non-perforated jacket. Fill lead-in hose completely with hot rubberized asphalt. Twist wires at least two times per foot for entire run. Attach lead-in to loop head with a schedule 80 CVPC tee and a CVPC adapter bushing.</p>
<p>6. Any variation to the installation as described above shall be approved by the design engineer prior to installation.</p></details>

### Sheet 3 of 3 (document page 76)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0076.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0076.webp" alt="S-70.4A sheet 3"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.4A, sheet 3. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p76">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>This sheet is Standard Drawing S-70.4A, Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 3 of 3.</p>
<p>LRT preformed loop details (cont&#x27;d), loop: a circular loop 30&quot; in diameter made of 3/4&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose, with an expansion joint and a CVCC tee on the ring. A 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose runs from the tee to the Type A Handhole and ends in a twisted tail.</p>
<p>Winding details (not to scale): the wire is wound in four clockwise turns around the loop. The ends are twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) into a pair and run to the Type A Handhole, ends labeled 1 and 2.</p>
<p>Type &#x27;A&#x27; LRT detector handhole, section: a precast reinforced concrete box with a 9&quot; throat diameter, set under a cast iron frame and cover at the surface. Overall height is 12&quot; from the surface to the bottom of the box, with a box internal depth of 11-3/8&quot;, 8&quot; min. clear depth below the cover and a 6&quot; min. clean, crushed rock sump below the box. The backfill at the surface is ballast, PCC or hot AC to match adjacent roadbed material, with a loop sealant layer 4&quot; min. at the top. The box sits in Portland cement concrete, 3&quot; min. around the handhole. Box width is 10-3/8&quot; with 1&quot; walls. Cables of shielded loop conductor pairs enter at the side and splices are made inside the handhole. Conduit to termination pullbox is 2&quot; Schedule 80 PVC, 30&quot; deep, entering through the bottom.</p>
<p>Handhole cover, plan: a cast iron frame and cover with a cross hatched top and a rectangular label reading LADOT. Cover diameter is 11-1/8&quot; and the overall frame diameter is 14-1/4&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: Light Rail Train (LRT) Track Detector Loop, sheet 3/3, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-70.4A.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Splices shall only be made in the handhole.</p>
<p>2. Splices shall be soldered and water proofed.</p>
<p>Callouts: 3/4&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose to Type A Handhole. CVCC tee. Expansion joint. Twisted tail. Twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) into a pair. Ballast, PCC or hot AC to match adjacent roadbed material. Precast reinforced concrete box. Conduit to termination pullbox. 2&quot; Schedule 80 PVC (30&quot; deep). Clean, crushed rock sump. Loop sealant. Cables of shielded loop conductor pairs. Portland cement concrete. 3&quot; min. around the handhole. Cast iron frame and cover.</p></details>
