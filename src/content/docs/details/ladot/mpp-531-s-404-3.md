### Sheet 3 of 3 (page 50)

<h5 class="dwg-sub">NOTES</h5>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">SHARED MOBILITY PARKING: ON-STREET</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Parking meter heads, posts and signs shall be removed or adjusted as needed if shared mobility parking (SMP) is installed in a parking meter zone.</li>
<li value="2">Rubberized wheel stops shall be used at the ends of the SMP zones if located next to on-street parking.</li>
<li value="3">Minimum clearance from the top of driveway shall be 5&#x27;.</li>
<li value="4">Minimum clearance from a fire hydrant shall be 15&#x27;.</li>
<li value="5">SMP zones shall remain clear of bus zones.</li>
<li value="6">SMP zones shall not be installed in peak hour lanes, red curb zones, or in parking restricted areas (i.e. tow-away no stopping any time &quot;T.A.N.S.A.T.&quot;, no parking any time &quot;N.P.A.T.&quot;, etc.).</li>
<li value="7">Minimum clearance from two or more consecutive parking spaces shall be 5&#x27;.</li>
<li value="8">SMP zones shall not be installed within 30&#x27; of a limit line at a traffic signal or within 25&#x27; of a &quot;STOP&quot; or &quot;YIELD&quot; sign.</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">SHARED MOBILITY PARKING: SIDEWALK</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="9">A minimum of 5&#x27; of unobstructed sidewalk must be maintained for a pedestrian pathway.</li>
<li value="10">Where there is no parking on the adjacent street, provide:</li>
</ol>
<p>A. Minimum clearance of 1&#x27;-6&quot; from the face of curb to the edge of the SMP zone.</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="11">Where there is parking on the adjacent street, provide:</li>
</ol>
<p>A. Minimum clearance of 3&#x27; from the face of curb to the edge of the SMP zone.</p>
<p>B. Minimum clearance of 4&#x27; from the face of curb to the edge of the SMP zone if the adjacent parking is a loading zone or disabled parking space.</p>
<p>C. Minimum clearance of 4&#x27; of unobstructed pathway at the ends of the SMP zone.</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="12">Minimum clearance of 5&#x27; from the marked crosswalk line or top of curb ramp.</li>
<li value="13">Minimum clearance of 5&#x27; from the top of driveway.</li>
<li value="14">Minimum clearance from a fire hydrant shall be 15&#x27;.</li>
<li value="15">SMP zones shall remain clear of bus loading and unloading zones.</li>
<li value="16">SMP zones shall not:</li>
</ol>
<p>A. Block access to building entrances or exits.</p>
<p>B. Obstruct utility accesses such as manholes, pullboxes, street light poles, power poles, etc.</p>
<p>C. Obstruct the use of sidewalk furniture such as benches, newsstands, mailboxes, bus shelters, etc.</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">MISCELLANEOUS</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="17">All pavement markings shall be skid-resistant.</li>
</ol>
</details>
