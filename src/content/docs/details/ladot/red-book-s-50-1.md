<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Legend of the symbols used on LADOT traffic signal plans. It covers signal heads, signal standards, mast arms, pedestrian signals and pushbuttons, cabinets, pullboxes, detectors, cameras, poles, trees, fire hydrants and other features, each shown as proposed, existing, and existing to be removed. Footnotes A through F set common conditions such as backplates on all heads and a 15 ft default mast arm length.</p>
