<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Legend of the symbols used on LADOT traffic signal plans. It covers signal heads, signal standards, mast arms, pedestrian signals and pushbuttons, cabinets, pullboxes, detectors, cameras, poles, trees, fire hydrants and other features, each shown as proposed, existing, and existing to be removed. Footnotes A through F set common conditions such as backplates on all heads and a 15 ft default mast arm length.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 4 (document page 27)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0027.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0027.webp" alt="S-50.1 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-50.1, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/02-pages-17-32/#p27">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Symbol legend, sheet 1 of 4. The sheet is a three-column legend with the column headings PROPOSED (bold, solid black symbols), EXISTING (outline symbols) and EXISTING TO BE REMOVED (dashed or light outline symbols), followed by a text column naming each symbol. Every row repeats the same signal head symbol in the three line styles. A signal head is drawn as a triangle pointing left (the head face) with a cross tick, joined by a horizontal line to a circle at the right (the signal standard, filled for proposed, open for existing, dashed for to be removed).</p>
<p>Table of signal head symbols, 14 rows from top to bottom: Row 1: triangle head with an 8&quot; label to its left. Signal head, three 8&quot; (200 mm) section heads (Footnote A). Row 2: same symbol with no label. Signal head, three 12&quot; (300 mm) sections (Footnote A). Row 3: head with a trapezoid (bevel) shape on its left. Signal head with beveled visor, left bevel shown (Footnote A). Row 4: the circle alone. Signal standard. Row 5: head with a rectangle and 8&quot; label on its left. Signal head, three 8&quot; (200 mm) sections with long visor (Footnote A). Row 6: head with one upright arrow and 8&quot; label on the left. Signal head, four sections, three 8&quot; sections (R, Y, G) plus 12&quot; green arrow (Footnotes A and B). Row 7: head with one upright arrow, no 8&quot; label. Signal head, four sections, three 12&quot; sections (R, Y, G) plus 12&quot; green arrow (Footnotes A and B). Row 8: 8&quot; label and head with a small flag-shaped arrow on the stem. Signal head, three sections, two 8&quot; sections (R, Y) plus 12&quot; green up arrow (Footnote A). Row 9: head with two arrows on the stem. Signal head, three sections, one 12&quot; section (R) plus 12&quot; yellow and green arrows (Footnotes A and B). Row 10: head with three arrows on the stem. Signal head, three sections, 12&quot; red, yellow and green arrows (Footnotes A and B). Row 11: two upright arrows to the left of an 8&quot; label and head. Signal head, five sections, three 8&quot; sections (R, Y, G) plus 12&quot; yellow and green arrows (Footnotes A and B). Row 12: two upright arrows to the left of the head, no label. Signal head, five sections, three 12&quot; sections (R, Y, G) plus 12&quot; yellow and green arrows, cluster head if on mast arm for left turn phase (Footnotes A and B). Row 13: letters PV to the left of the head. Signal head, three 12&quot; programmed visibility sections (R, Y, G) (Footnote A). Row 14: three horizontal bars to the left of the head. Louvered signal indications, 8&quot; or 12&quot; (Footnotes A, F).</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Traffic Signal Symbols, drawing number S-50.1. Sheet 1 of 4, signed by Jaime de la Vega, General Manager, approved 10-28-11, drawn MT 7-28-11. Booklet page number 20.</p></details>
