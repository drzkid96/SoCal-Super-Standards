</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Layout view: The word PATH in four block letters, P, A, T and H from left to right, drawn over a rectangular grid. The grid is made of narrow vertical columns and rows, with the cell size marked at the bottom left as 2&quot; wide (horizontal dimension) and 4&quot; tall (vertical dimension). The P is at the left, with a straight stem and two curved bowl outlines. The A is a tall tapering shape with a narrow inner triangular opening and a crossbar near the bottom, with two feet at the base. The T has a wide top bar across the upper rows and a stem that extends to the bottom. The H at the right has two vertical stems joined by a crossbar part way up (the stems drawn as narrow strips). Below the grid is the label &quot;A = 5.5 SQ. FT.&quot;, the painted area. No other dimensions or notes are shown. The title block reads Approved, Thomas Conner, General Manager, 1-30-1998, PAVEMENT MARKING PATH, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Thomas K. Conner, General Manager, drawn by M.T., Drawing No. S-498.0.</p></details>
