<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Details the 12 inch by 18 inch Red Flag Days No Parking sign, which has a red tow-truck header panel 4 inches tall, an 8 inch outside diameter circle and slash with a 5 inch Series E P, the legend RED FLAG DAYS in 1 inch Series D, a double, left or right arrow 1 1/2 inch tall, and the line "To recover impounded vehicle call 3-1-1". The background is white with a black P and red circle, slash, text, arrow and border. Signs are .080 aluminum with reflective SEG Type II material and graffiti film.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Sign dimensions: 12 inch wide by 18 inch tall, 1 1/2 inch radius typical. Red header panel 4 inches tall with tow truck graphic. 8 inch outside diameter circle and slash. 5 inch tall Series E P. Legend &quot;RED FLAG DAYS&quot; in 1 inch Series D, double arrow 8 inches long, and bottom line &quot;TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1&quot; in 1/4 inch Series C.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 12 inch x 18 inch, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1 1/2 inch in height and can be Double, Left or Right. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4 inch wide and spaced 1/4 inch from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09/05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, all text, arrow and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p></details>
