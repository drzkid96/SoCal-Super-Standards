<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Details the 12 inch by 18 inch Red Flag Days No Parking sign, which has a red tow-truck header panel 4 inches tall, an 8 inch outside diameter circle and slash with a 5 inch Series E P, the legend RED FLAG DAYS in 1 inch Series D, a double, left or right arrow 1 1/2 inch tall and 8 inches long, and the line TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1 in 1/4 inch Series C. The background is white with a black P and red circle, slash, text, arrow and border. Signs are .080 aluminum with reflective SEG Type II material and graffiti film.</p>

A solid red header panel across the top is 4&quot; tall and carries a white tow truck graphic towing a car. Below the header is a black P in a red circle with a red slash, the circle labeled &quot;8&quot; Outside Diameter&quot; and the P &quot;5&quot; Tall Series E&quot;. Under the circle is &quot;RED FLAG DAYS&quot; in red 1&quot; Series D lettering, then a red double-headed arrow, 8&quot; long, 1 1/2&quot; tall, then the bottom line &quot;TO RECOVER IMPOUNDED VEHICLE CALL 3-1-1&quot; in 1/4&quot; Series C. Vertical dimensions down the right side: 4&quot; (header), 3/4&quot; (gap below the header), then 1/2&quot;, 1/2&quot;, 1 1/2&quot; and 1 3/4&quot; in the lower part of the sign, with the 18&quot; overall. Robinson, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Red Flag Days No Parking Sign, sheet 1 of 1, drawn by MT 7-14-10, Drawing No. S-512.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 12&quot; X 18&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The arrow is 1 1/2&quot; in height and can be Double, Left or Right. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacture &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09 / 05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, all text, arrow and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p>
<p>Sign labels: 1 1/2&quot; radius typical, 8&quot; outside diameter, 5&quot; tall Series E, 1&quot; Series D, 1/4&quot; Series C, 12&quot; by 18&quot;, header 4&quot;.</p></details>
