<p class="dwg-sum">Shows the pedestrian pushbutton sign used for non-actuated crossings. The sign is .063 aluminum, powder coated with black legend on a white background and orange hand and timer symbols. The sign text covers Start Crossing, Watch for Vehicles, Flashing, Time Remaining, Don't Start and Steady Don't Cross, with a Timer symbol. Four 1/4 in inside diameter mounting holes are shown.</p>
