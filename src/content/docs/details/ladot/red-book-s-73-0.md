<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows the pedestrian pushbutton sign used for non-actuated crossings. The sign is .063 aluminum, powder coated with black legend on a white background and orange hand and timer symbols. The sign text covers Start Crossing, Watch for Vehicles, Flashing, Time Remaining, Don't Start and Steady Don't Cross, with a Timer symbol. Four 1/4 in inside diameter mounting holes are shown.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Pedestrian pushbutton sign, non-actuated. Sheet 1 of 1.</p>
<p>1/4 in inside diameter, 4 places (typ.).</p>
<p>Sign text: Start Crossing, Watch for Vehicles, Flashing: if started finish crossing, Time remaining to finish crossing, Don&#x27;t Start, Steady: Don&#x27;t Cross, Timer.</p>
<p>Dimensions as shown: R = 1/2 in corner radius, 4 in and 5 in.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be .063 aluminum.</p>
<p>2. Powder coated with black legend on white background and orange hand and timer symbols.</p></details>
