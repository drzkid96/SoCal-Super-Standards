</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. For Case 1 the 8&quot; channelizing line should be installed where: A) downstream lane width is less than 13&#x27; (lane line to edge line) with parking prohibited; or B) downstream lane width is less than 20&#x27; (lane line to edge of roadway) with parking permitted; or C) the change in roadway width is 6&#x27; or greater.</p>
<p>2. For Case 1 the 8&quot; white edgeline should be a minimum of 100 feet in length where conditions permit, to provide adequate visibility for approaching motorists. Taper rates in Case 1 should be 5:1 or greater.</p>
<p>3. For Case 2 the lane line and 8&quot; channelizing line may be transitioned using the appropriate minimum taper rate from Table 1 or by appropriate curve-tangent-curve design.</p>
<p>4. For narrowed roadway delineation with a lane drop see S-485.0.</p>
<p>5. For Case 1 and Case 2, the addition of 12&quot; white diagonals should be installed to emphasize that large pavement areas are not intended as part of the travelled roadway.</p>
<p>6. Taper rates shown in Table 1 are based on posted speed plus 5 mph. Where the 85% speed (critical speed) on the approach is determined to be equal to the posted speed, the table numbers for the next lower speed increment may be used.</p></details>
