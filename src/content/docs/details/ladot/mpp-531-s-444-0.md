<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-444.0, approved September 20, 2005, for striping a roadway that narrows without dropping a lane. Case 1 and Case 2 show an 8 inch white channelizing line with 12 inch white diagonals, and Table 1 gives preferred taper rates from 15:1 at 25 mph to 60:1 at 55 mph. Six notes give when to install the line and how to set taper rates.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Case 1 (plan): a travel lane heading up the sheet with a dashed lane line on the left. On the right, the roadway edge line runs straight and the roadway narrows by an 8&quot; white channelizing line that tapers diagonally toward the right edge. The line starts from a straight offset line at the top, where the legend reads 2&#x27; OFF RECOMMENDED, with (1&#x27; MINIMUM, 7&#x27; MINIMUM W/PARKING). A SW44(CA) (DOWN LEFT ARROW) sign is placed at the start of the taper. Inside the taper triangle are 12&quot; WHITE DIAGONALS, SPACING PER PLAN (MINIMUM TWO DIAGONALS). Labels: LANE LINE, and 8&quot; WHITE LINE (SEE NOTES 1 &amp; 2). At the bottom, 2&#x27; OR EDGE OF GUTTER IF PARKING IS PROHIBITED, 8&#x27; IF PARKING IS PERMITTED, is the offset from the edge line to the channelizing line. A hollow arrow shows travel direction up the sheet.</p>
<p>Case 2 (plan): the same taper and diagonals, labeled LANE LINE (SEE NOTE 3) and 8&quot; WHITE LINE (SEE NOTE 3). Here the lane width at the start of the taper is dimensioned 10&#x27; TO 12&#x27; from the lane line to the edge line, with the same 2&#x27; OR EDGE OF GUTTER IF PARKING IS PROHIBITED, 8&#x27; IF PARKING IS PERMITTED offset and the same 2&#x27; OFF RECOMMENDED, 1&#x27; MINIMUM, 7&#x27; MINIMUM W/PARKING legend at the top. The SW44(CA) sign is shown as a diamond sign with a down-left arrow.</p>
<p>Table 1 (speed limit in mph, preferred taper rate): 25 mph, 15:1. 30 mph, 20.5:1. 35 mph, 26.7:1. 40 mph, 45:1. 45 mph, 50:1. 50 mph, 55:1. 55 mph, 60:1.</p>
<p>Title block: approved September 20, 2005, signed for Wayne K. Tanda, General Manager, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Narrowed Roadway Delineation (Without Lane Drop), sheet 1 of 1, drawing number S-444.0.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. For Case 1 the 8&quot; channelizing line should be installed where: A) downstream lane width is less than 13&#x27; (lane line to edge line) with parking prohibited; or B) downstream lane width is less than 20&#x27; (lane line to edge of roadway) with parking permitted; or C) the change in roadway width is 6&#x27; or greater.</p>
<p>2. For Case 1 the 8&quot; white edgeline should be a minimum of 100 feet in length where conditions permit, to provide adequate visibility for approaching motorists. Taper rates in Case 1 should be 5:1 or greater.</p>
<p>3. For Case 2 the lane line and 8&quot; channelizing line may be transitioned using the appropriate minimum taper rate from Table 1 or by appropriate curve-tangent-curve design.</p>
<p>4. For narrowed roadway delineation with a lane drop see S-485.0.</p>
<p>5. For Case 1 and Case 2, the addition of 12&quot; white diagonals should be installed to emphasize that large pavement areas are not intended as part of the travelled roadway.</p>
<p>6. Taper rates shown in Table 1 are based on posted speed plus 5 mph. Where the 85% speed (critical speed) on the approach is determined to be equal to the posted speed, the table numbers for the next lower speed increment may be used.</p></details>
