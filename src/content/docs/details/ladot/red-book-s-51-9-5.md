<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes (circled)</h5>
<p>(1) Round tapered steel tube with maximum taper of .15 inches per foot. End section O.D. of 2 3/8&quot; for mounting hardware. Standard 2&quot; pipe extensions of 3&#x27;-0&quot; max. length may be used at the option of the manufacturer.</p>
<p>(2) 4 anchor bolts each base plate. Length indicated does not include required 4&quot;-90 degree bend. Each anchor bolt is threaded 6&quot; at the top and is furnished with 2 nuts &amp; 2 washers. One anchor bolt bonded to conduit.</p>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Pole and signal arm shall be fabricated from sheet steel conforming to the specifications of ASTM Designation A611 Grade C, or ASTM Designation A 570 Grade C.</li>
<li value="2">All welding shall conform to AWS D2.0 &quot;Specifications for welded Highway and Railway Bridges.&quot;</li>
<li value="3">All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except as otherwise shown.</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes (second list)</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Alternative details approved by the Engineer may be subst. for the arm connections and configurations shown.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Fabricators electing to use larger than min.</strong> arm diameters must adjust the details as required to permit solid seating of cap screws.</li>
<li value="3">In lieu of the torque requirements for HS bolts, cap screws shall be tightened by the turn-of-nut method 1/6 turn from snug tight condition. No washer will be required.</li>
<li value="4">Only one signal head may be mounted on each signal arm.</li>
<li value="5">All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</li>
<li value="6">All arms shall be bent to the approximate configuration shown.</li>
</ol>
<p>On the elevation: Note: Top 3&quot; of foundation to be poured level with sidewalk except on structures. Revision flags on the foundation callout: (1) and (2) for the 560-C-3250 Conc. item.</p>
</details>
