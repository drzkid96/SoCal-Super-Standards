It gives the arm connection plate details, 11 1/2 inch square base plate, pole and arm schedules, a Type F-2 foundation 32 by 32 inches, and fabrication notes.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 36)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0036.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0036.webp" alt="S-51.9.5 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-51.9.5, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p36">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Standard, Type 16 (signal mast arm pole) and Foundation, Type F-2, drawing S-51.9.5. The sheet has a pole elevation, Detail A, a pole base plate plan, a pole schedule table, an arm data table and notes.</p>
<p>Elevation: an 18&#x27;-0&quot; tapered steel pole with a curved mast arm extending to the left carrying a three lens signal head at its end. The horizontal dimension to the signal head is 15&#x27;-0&quot; Unless otherwise noted (Max. 20&#x27;). Vertical dimensions on the left: 17&#x27;-6&quot; Min. and Traffic Signal Mounting Height (to the signal head), 17&#x27;-6&quot; and 15&#x27;-6&quot; at the arm and sign levels. At the pole top the mast arm joins at Detail A with a Provide removable rain-tight cap callout and a 6&quot; dimension below the pole top. An 18&quot; X 66&quot; Sign is shown in dashed lines below the arm. Pedestrian heads or pushbutton hardware are shown dashed on the pole at 11&#x27;-3&quot; and 7&#x27;-6&quot; above grade. The pole overall height is 18&#x27;-0&quot;. Next to the pole is a small plan view of the pole shaft with a hole pattern: 2 1/4&quot; Drill, one side, opposite quadrant of mast arm, and 3/4&quot; Drill thru 2 holes (Typ. -2 places), with spacing 4 1/2&quot; and 9&quot;.</p>
<p>Foundation section: Foundation, Type F-2 (S-52.2) 32&quot; X 32&quot; X (48&quot;+4&quot; Topping) with 560-C-3250 Conc. Anchor Bolt 1 1/8&quot;X 40&quot; ASTM A307 (note 2). The foundation sits in Undisturbed earth. A 36&quot; dimension runs from the pole to the sidewalk edge.</p>
<p>Detail A (not to scale): (left) front view of the pole plate: a tapered plate with four holes (two at the top corners, one at the center and one near the bottom). Dimensions 1&quot;, 3&quot;, 3&quot;, 1&quot; across and 3&quot;, 4&quot;, 9&quot; vertical. Tap pole plate for hex. head cap screw ASTM A325, 3/4&quot;-10 X 1 3/4&quot; long, total 3. 1&quot; Dia. chased outlet for electrical conductors. (right) side view of the arm plate welded to the pole: Bar 2&quot; X 1/4&quot; X 0&#x27;-5&quot;, plate thicknesses 10 Ga. with 1/4 weld and 7 Ga. with 5/16 weld, dimensions 3/4&quot;, 5/16&quot; and 1&quot;, two 1/4 fillet weld callouts. Note: Dimensions shown here apply to both the post and signal arm plate. (lower left) pole base cutaway: 4&quot; X 6 1/2&quot; handhole rainforced [sic] with No. 3 gage ring 1 1/2&quot; wide welded to outside of pole, cover of 10 gage plate, locate in opposite quadrant of mast arm. Dimensions 6 1/2&quot; and 8&quot; +/- above the plate, 1&quot; base plate, 3/16 and 1/32 welds, Bar 1&quot; X 1/4&quot; backing ring tack weld to plate.</p>
<p>Pole Base Plate (no scale): 11 1/2&quot; sq. plate with R=1 1/2&quot; corners, 11 1/2&quot; Bolt Circle, four slots 1 3/8&quot; X 1 1/2&quot; for anchor bolt, hole dia.=5 3/4&quot;, axis of mast arm marked.</p>
<p>Pole Schedule table: Round tapered steel pole: HGT. 18.0&quot;, GAGE 10 GA., MIN. O.D. base 7 3/4&quot; top 5 1/4&quot;, Signal arm length 15&#x27; - 20&#x27;. Signal Arm Data (note 1): projected length 15&#x27;-0&quot;, gage 10 GA., minimum O.D. at pole 4.6&quot;, mounting height 22.3+/-&#x27;; 18&#x27;-0&quot;, 10 GA., 5.2&quot;, mounting height 23.3+/-&#x27;; 20&#x27;-0&quot;, 7 GA., 5.0&quot;, mounting height 23.3+/-&#x27;.</p>
<p>Title block: Standard, Type 16 (revision 1), City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, approved Nov. 13, 1998, Drawing No. S-51.9.5. Bridge and Structural Design Division, checked for structural design, Div. Engr. Philip H. Skarin, date August 7, 1973. Revisions: Updated (1) RO 4-3-82, Updated (2) GH 3-27-92. Booklet page number 29.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes (circled):</h5>
<p>(1) Round tapered steel tube with maximum taper of .15 inches per foot. End section O.D. of 2 3/8&quot; for mounting hardware. Standard 2&quot; pipe extensions of 3&#x27;-0&quot; max. length may be used at the option of the manufacturer.</p>
<p>(2) 4 anchor bolts each base plate. Length indicated does not include required 4&quot;-90 degree bend. Each anchor bolt is threaded 6&quot; at the top and is furnished with 2 nuts &amp; 2 washers. One anchor bolt bonded to conduit.</p>
<p>1. Pole and signal arm shall be fabricated from sheet steel conforming to the specifications of ASTM Designation A611 Grade C, or ASTM Designation A 570 Grade C.</p>
<p>2. All welding shall conform to AWS D2.0 &quot;Specifications for welded Highway and Railway Bridges.&quot;</p>
<p>3. All structural steel shall conform to ASTM designation A36, except as otherwise shown.</p>
<h5>Notes (second list):</h5>
<p>1. Alternative details approved by the Engineer may be subst. for the arm connections and configurations shown.</p>
<p>2. Fabricators electing to use larger than min. arm diameters must adjust the details as required to permit solid seating of cap screws.</p>
<p>3. In lieu of the torque requirements for HS bolts, cap screws shall be tightened by the turn-of-nut method 1/6 turn from snug tight condition. No washer will be required.</p>
<p>4. Only one signal head may be mounted on each signal arm.</p>
<p>5. All metal parts shall be galvanized after fabrication.</p>
<p>6. All arms shall be bent to the approximate configuration shown.</p>
<p>On the elevation: Note: Top 3&quot; of foundation to be poured level with sidewalk except on structures. Revision flags on the foundation callout: (1) and (2) for the 560-C-3250 Conc. item.</p></details>
