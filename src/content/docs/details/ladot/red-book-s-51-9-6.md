<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT drawing S-51.9.6 for the CD954 standard, a 30 ft tapered steel pole with a 15 ft mast arm, a luminaire arm and pedestrian equipment. It shows hole details, an 11 1/2 inch square base plate, a Type F-2 foundation with four 1 1/8 inch by 40 inch anchor bolts, and mounting heights of 22.3 or 23.3 ft by arm length.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 37)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>CD954 Standard, drawing S-51.9.6. One elevation with details.</p>
<p>Elevation: a 30&#x27;-0&quot; tapered steel pole (overall height to pole top) with a curved mast arm extending to the left and a luminaire arm with a cobra head luminaire extending to the left near the top. The mast arm is labeled Mast Arm, and its horizontal length is 15&#x27;-0&quot; (Unless otherwise specified), measured from the end of the arm to the pole. A three lens signal head is drawn dashed at the arm end. Left vertical dimensions: Mounting Height (to the arm), 17&#x27;-6&quot; Min. (to the bottom of the signal head). Right of the pole: 30&#x27;-0&quot; overall, 17&#x27;-6&quot; to the mast arm connection, 17&#x27;-6&quot; (to the signal head lower edge), 11&#x27;-3&quot; and 7&#x27;-6&quot; to dashed pedestrian equipment mounted on the pole, all measured from street grade. A dash dotted leader from the pole points to the Detail - Side Opposite Mast Arm.</p>
<p>Detail - Side Opposite Mast Arm: pole cross shaft showing the hole pattern 3/4&quot; Drill Thru, 2 Holes and 2 1/4&quot; Drill, One side, with spacing 9&quot; overall and 4 1/2&quot; between holes.</p>
<p>Pole Base Plate: 11 1/2&quot; Square plate with R=1 1/2&quot; corners, 11 1/2&quot; Bolt Circle, a central cutout with four slots on the diagonals, Axis of Arms marked, slot width dimensions 3/4&quot; and 1 1/2&quot;.</p>
<p>Foundation section: Tapered steel pole on Foundation, Type F-2 with Anchor Bolts 1 1/8&quot; X 40&quot; X 4&quot; (4 required). The foundation is set beside a stepped curb with Street Grade and Top of Curb marked, 36&quot; from the pole to the curb, 14&quot; Min. from the top surface to the conduit.</p>
<p>Table: Mast Arm Length and Mounting Height. 15&#x27;, 22.3&#x27; +/- . 18&#x27;, 23.3&#x27; +/- . (revision 1) 20&#x27;, 23.3&#x27; +/- .</p>
<p>Title block: CD954 Standard, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, approved Nov. 13, 1998, Drawing No. S-51.9.6. Drawn by MT 11-72. Revisions: Updated RO, JF, JAC 11-3-82. Title &amp; Table GH 5-31-90. Booklet page number 30.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Note: Standard must conform to Bureau of Street Lighting Plan L-103-0.</p>
<p>Callouts: Mast Arm; 3/4&quot; Drill Thru, 2 Holes; 2 1/4&quot; Drill, One side; Detail - Side Opposite Mast Arm; Pole Base Plate; Street Grade; Top of Curb; Tapered steel pole; Foundation, Type F-2; Anchor Bolts 1 1/8&quot; X 40&quot; X 4&quot; (4 required).</p>
<p>Table: Mast Arm Length 15&#x27; mounting height 22.3&#x27; +/- ; 18&#x27; mounting height 23.3&#x27; +/- ; 20&#x27; mounting height 23.3&#x27; +/- .</p></details>
