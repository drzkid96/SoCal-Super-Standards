<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Pedestrian barricade, drawing S-454.2, approved June 26, 2008. A galvanized 1-1/2 inch nominal pipe railing 4 feet 6 inches high with 1 foot radius corners carries signs <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> and R9-3b, and posts are 6 feet apart and anchored with a 5 inch square, 1/2 inch base plate and four 1/2 inch bolts.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 130)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0130.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0130.webp" alt="S-454.2 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-454.2, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/09-pages-129-130/#p130">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation of barricade: A pipe railing with two vertical posts and three horizontal 1-1/2&quot; nominal pipe rails, bent with 1&#x27;-0&quot; R corners at the top. The overall height above the top of sidewalk is 4&#x27;-6&quot;. The rails are spaced 1&#x27;-3&quot; below the top rail and another 1&#x27;-3&quot; below that. The posts are 6&#x27;-0&quot; apart (Typical). Signs <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> &amp; R9-3b, 18&quot; x 24&quot; (a no pedestrian symbol in a circle with slash, above the words USE CROSSWALK and an arrow) are mounted between the upper rails. Callouts: 1-1/2&quot; Nominal pipe size galvanized Standard pipe, See Post Anchorage Detail, Top of sidewalk.</p>
<p>Post Anchorage Detail, elevation: A pipe post set in a threaded pipe collar on a 1/2&quot; base plate fastened with 1/2&quot; x 2-1/2&quot; bolts (4) to a PCC sidewalk.</p>
<p>Post Anchorage Detail, plan: A 5&quot; square base plate on a 5&quot; bolt circle with 9/16&quot; dia. holes (4) at the corners and 1/2&quot; dia. expansion anchors (4).</p>
<p>Title block: Pedestrian Barricade, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-454.2. Drawn MT 05-03-06, Pr. T. E. SS 06-26-08, approved June 26, 2008, Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Printed page number 123.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Pipe post to be set 1&#x27;-6&quot; back from face of curb unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. For minimum pipe diameters and wall thickness refer to ASTM A6M.</p>
<p>3. Use left, right or double arrow on sign as needed.</p></details>
