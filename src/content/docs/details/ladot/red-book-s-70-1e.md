<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Pre-formed loop shall be constructed from 1/2&quot; I.D. polypropylene, with an O.D. of 3/4&quot; inch.</li>
<li value="2">Loop is to have one continuous #16 TFFN wire through the loop head and lead-in to prevent loop malfunction due to splicing. Loop shall have four (4) turns of wire.</li>
<li value="3">The conduit shall be completely filled with hot, rubberized asphalt which will allow the loop to retain flexibility once cooled, prevent incursion of moisture and set the wire turns firmly in place.</li>
<li value="4">Each loop head shall be provided with a 5&quot; contraction/expansion joint to allow for movement of pavement and to prevent breakage of the wire and/or conduit. The joint is to have a 9&quot; long by 3/4&quot; Schedule 80 PVC cover slide to be placed over the joint for protection from the elements.</li>
<li value="5">Encase lead-in wires in a non-conductive 250 psi flex hose seamless fiber braid reinforcement and a non-conductive seamless extruded urethane non-perforated jacket. Fill lead-in hose completely with hot rubberized asphalt. Twist wires at least two times per foot for entire run. Attach lead-in to loop head with a Schedule 80 CVPC tee and a CVPC adapter bushing.</li>
<li value="6">Any variation to the installation as described above shall be approved by the design engineer prior to installation.</li>
<li value="7">The 3/8&quot; O.D. lead-in hose shall be installed in 2&quot; PVC conduit between pullbox and entry to pavement slab. Loop shall be attached to top of reinforcing bars when used in the pavement slab.</li>
</ol>
<div class="dwg-callouts"><strong>Callouts:</strong> Expansion joint. CPVC tee. CPVC cross. 3/4&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. Twisted tail. Twisted clockwise (at least 2 turn per ft.) into a pair. To pullbox. Detector loop conductors. Schedule 80 PVC 2&quot; conduit (see note 7). Pavement. 4&quot; min.</div>
</details>
