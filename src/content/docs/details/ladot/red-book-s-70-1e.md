<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-70.1E shows a preformed 6 ft inductive loop built from 3/4 in O.D. hose with expansion joints, CPVC tees and cross, and a 3/8 in O.D. asphalt filled lead-in hose. It gives the winding details, a section from the pullbox through a 2 in Schedule 80 PVC conduit to the loop, and seven notes covering materials, #16 TFFN wire with four turns, sealing with rubberized asphalt, and expansion joints. The pullbox is within 3 ft of the curb face.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 70)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0070.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0070.webp" alt="S-70.1E sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-70.1E, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/05-pages-65-80/#p70">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: a 6&#x27; diameter preformed loop, drawn as a dashed circle with a dashed diagonal bar, connected by a dashed lead-in to a PULLBOX that is 3&#x27;-0&quot; MAX. from the CURB FACE. Section A-A is cut along the lead-in path.</p>
<p>Preformed loop detail: the loop is a circular ring built from 3/4&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose, with four expansion joints and CPVC (chlorinated PVC) tees and a CPVC cross on the ring, a diagonal 3/4&quot; O.D. hose across the ring, and a 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose lead-in leaving at the CPVC cross, ending in a twisted tail.</p>
<p>Winding details (not to scale): the wire is wound in several clockwise turns inside the loop, with the ends twisted clockwise (at least 2 turn per ft.) into a pair and run TO PULLBOX. The two ends are marked 1 and 2.</p>
<p>Section A-A (typical, not to scale): from the pullbox at left, DETECTOR LOOP CONDUCTORS pass from the pullbox through a Schedule 80 PVC 2&quot; conduit (see note 7), under the curb and gutter, then in a 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose to the detector, within the pavement, running PER PLAN to the loop. The loop sits in the pavement with 4&quot; MIN. between the pavement surface and the loop.</p>
<p>Title block: Preformed Loop Installation, sheet 1/1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 2-16-99, signed Frances T. Banerjee, General Manager, Drawing No. S-70.1E. Revision 1: Rev. distance, JV, 5-6-08.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Pre-formed loop shall be constructed from 1/2&quot; I.D. polypropylene, with an O.D. of 3/4&quot; inch.</p>
<p>2. Loop is to have one continuous #16 TFFN wire through the loop head and lead-in to prevent loop malfunction due to splicing. Loop shall have four (4) turns of wire.</p>
<p>3. The conduit shall be completely filled with hot, rubberized asphalt which will allow the loop to retain flexibility once cooled, prevent incursion of moisture and set the wire turns firmly in place.</p>
<p>4. Each loop head shall be provided with a 5&quot; contraction/expansion joint to allow for movement of pavement and to prevent breakage of the wire and/or conduit. The joint is to have a 9&quot; long by 3/4&quot; Schedule 80 PVC cover slide to be placed over the joint for protection from the elements.</p>
<p>5. Encase lead-in wires in a non-conductive 250 psi flex hose seamless fiber braid reinforcement and a non-conductive seamless extruded urethane non-perforated jacket. Fill lead-in hose completely with hot rubberized asphalt. Twist wires at least two times per foot for entire run. Attach lead-in to loop head with a Schedule 80 CVPC tee and a CVPC adapter bushing.</p>
<p>6. Any variation to the installation as described above shall be approved by the design engineer prior to installation.</p>
<p>7. The 3/8&quot; O.D. lead-in hose shall be installed in 2&quot; PVC conduit between pullbox and entry to pavement slab. Loop shall be attached to top of reinforcing bars when used in the pavement slab.</p>
<p>Callouts: Expansion joint. CPVC tee. CPVC cross. 3/4&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. Twisted tail. Twisted clockwise (at least 2 turn per ft.) into a pair. To pullbox. Detector loop conductors. Schedule 80 PVC 2&quot; conduit (see note 7). Pavement. 4&quot; min.</p></details>
