<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-78.5.2 showing the plan and section of the LADOT metal cover traffic pull box, equivalent to Caltrans Pullbox No. 6 (T). It has a reinforced 1/2 in. minimum galvanized steel cover marked TRAFFIC SIGNAL on a Z-bar frame, a concrete footing, a bonding jumper to the conduit grounding bushings and a 1 in. crushed rock base. Metal covered boxes are required in driveways, alleys and where vehicles travel. Remaining details follow Caltrans Standard Plan ES-8.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of cover: A rounded-rectangle steel cover with the text &#x27;TRAFFIC SIGNAL&#x27; embossed across it. A lift hole is at the left of the text and a hold-down bolt (hex head) at each of the left and right edges, the right one noted &#x27;Hold-down bolt See Note 8&#x27;. The outer frame is 2&#x27;-11 1/2&quot; +/- long and the cover is 2&#x27;-9&quot; +/- long. The overall width is 2&#x27;-6&quot; +/- 1&quot; with a 1&#x27;-8&quot; +/- inner width. A dashed inner outline marks the plate, dimensioned 1&#x27;-11 1/2&quot; +/- by 1&#x27;-5&quot; +/- 1&quot;. Callouts: Lift hole. Hold-down bolt. Pull box reinforced with galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Reinforced 1/2&quot; MIN steel plate cover, galvanized after fabrication, See Notes 3 &amp; 6.</p>
<p>Section: A cross section of the pull box set in the ground. A galvanized Z-bar welded frame carries the steel plate cover, which is flush with finished grade (&#x27;Top flush with finished grade (See Note 5)&#x27;). The cover is secured with a 1/2&quot; length sleeve nut with brass bolt. A pull box concrete shell (PCC) surrounds the box, with a stepped lower edge. A bonding jumper (See Note 7) connects from the cover to the conduit grounding bushings, shown as a looped braided strap. Two conduits enter at an angle through the bottom with grounding bushings, and the box rests on a bed of 1&quot; crushed rock. Dimensions: 1&#x27;-0&quot; MIN wall depth below grade, 2&quot; and 6&quot; MIN at the footing step, 6&quot; MIN all around and 3&quot; MIN all around for the special concrete footing outside the box.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Traffic pullboxes in driveways, alleys and locations with vehicular traffic shall be metal covered per this standard.</p>
<p>2. Pullbox details not shown on this plan shall conform to Caltrans Standard Plan ES-8.</p>
<p>3. Traffic pullbox shall be provided with steel cover and special concrete footing. Steel cover shall have embossed non-skid pattern.</p>
<p>4. Galvanized conduits must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>5. Top of pull boxes shall be flush with surrounding grade or top of adjacent curb, except that in unpaved areas where pull box is not immediately adjacent to and protected by a concrete foundation, pole or other protective construction, the box shall be placed with its top 1 1/4&quot; above surrounding grade. Where practicable, pull boxes shown in the vicinity of curbs shall be placed adjacent to the back of curb, and pull boxes shown adjacent to standards shall be placed on side of foundation facing away from traffic, unless otherwise noted. When pull box is installed in sidewalk area, the depth of the pull box shall be adjusted so that the top of the pull box is flush with the sidewalk.</p>
<p>6. Pull box cover shall be marked as &quot;TRAFFIC SIGNAL&quot;.</p>
<p>7. Bonding jumper for metal covers shall be flat braided strap, 2&#x27; long minimum, Panduit Part # BS202446EU or equivalent.</p>
<p>8. Hold-down bolt shall be 5/8&quot; and coarse threaded.</p>
<p>Callouts: Lift hole. Hold-down bolt. Pull box reinforced with galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Reinforced 1/2&quot; minimum (MIN) steel plate cover, galvanized after fabrication, see Notes 3 and 6. Galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Pull box. Bonding jumper, see Note 7. PCC (portland cement concrete). Grounding bushing. 1/2&quot; length sleeve nut with brass bolt. Top flush with finished grade (see Note 5). 6&quot; MIN all around. 3&quot; MIN all around. 1&quot; crushed rock.</p></details>
