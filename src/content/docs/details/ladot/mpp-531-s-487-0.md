<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Typical BUS ONLY pavement markings for exclusive bus lanes, shown at a signalized intersection with legends and arrows on the approaches and at bus turn lanes. Typical spacings of 18 ft for the arrow and 16 ft and 8 ft for the legend words are dimensioned. Markings are installed at the start of bus turn lanes, at the start and end of each block with a bus only curb lane, and mid-block where the block exceeds 700 ft. Concurrent-flow lanes are delineated by 8 in. solid white lines, or 4 in. for part-time lanes, and all markings are alkyd-based thermoplastic.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A signalized four-leg intersection (S symbol at the center) with exclusive bus lanes along the curb on each approach. On each approach the curb lane carries BUS ONLY legends (the words ONLY and BUS in tall letters reading along the lane) and bus lanes are delineated by 8&quot; solid lines labeled &#x27;8&quot; (See Note 5)&#x27;. At the left (west) approach, a left turn pocket with a closed entrance and an arrow is shown with the legend ONLY BUS next to the turn arrow. The bottom approach shows a bus turn lane with a left turn arrow, ONLY, and BUS, with a curving lane line. Opposing lanes show normal lane lines and a double centerline.</p>
<p>Typical Spacings (shown twice, once at the upper left approach and once at the right approach): each detail shows a straight arrow, the legend ONLY and the legend BUS placed in a row along the lane, with dimensions 18&#x27; (arrow), 16&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 8&#x27; and an overall 16&#x27;, as drawn. On the left approach the order from upstream to the stop line is arrow, ONLY, BUS with dimensions 18&#x27;, 16&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 8&#x27; and 16&#x27;. On the right approach the order is mirrored with dimensions 8&#x27;, 18&#x27;, 16&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 8&#x27; and 16&#x27;.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. These pavement markings are supplementary to signing which regulates use of the exclusive bus lanes.</p>
<p>2. Install the &quot;BUS ONLY&quot; pavement markings at the beginning of bus turn lanes and at the beginning and ending of each block where there is a bus only curb lane.</p>
<p>3. Bus only turn lanes shall be supplemented with turn pavement arrows.</p>
<p>4. Contra-flow bus only lanes shall be supplemented with straight ahead direction pavement arrows.</p>
<p>5. Concurrent-flow bus only lanes shall be delineated by 8&quot; solid white lines. For a part-time bus lane installation, which is used as a mixed flow lane during non-restricted hours, a 4&quot; solid white line shall be used.</p>
<p>6. Bus only left turn pocket lanes shall have a closed entrance delineated by the extension of the 8&quot; white barrier line.</p>
<p>7. Mid-block pavement markings for bus only curb lanes shall be installed where the block length exceeds 700 feet.</p>
<p>8. The &quot;BUS ONLY&quot; pavement markings shall conform to the 8&#x27; high Caltrans standard.</p>
<p>9. All markings shall be Alkyd-Based thermoplastic.</p>
<p>Callouts: Typical spacings. 8&quot; (See Note 5).</p></details>
