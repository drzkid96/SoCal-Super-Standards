<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-57.3 shows a 337 controller cabinet mounted on a Type 8 standard for temporary use, held down by a 36 in. square steel platform and four 5 gallon concrete-filled cans chained around the pole. It gives the platform plan and underside layouts, bolt circle, lip and notches and a weight block detail of at least 90 lbs. It requires leveling on slopes steeper than 1/2 in. per foot.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 49)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0049.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0049.webp" alt="S-57.3 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-57.3, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/04-pages-49-64/#p49">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation: a Type 8 standard carrying a 337 cabinet (traffic signal controller cabinet), drawn with the pole cut by break marks. The cabinet is a tall rectangular enclosure with a door handle, ventilation louvers and a City of Los Angeles label plate on the door. It sits on top of the pole and is called out as 337 Cabinet. At the bottom, two 5 gal. concrete blocks sit on a platform with a 3/8 in. diameter steel coil chain tied to each block and locked around the Type 8 standard. The base assembly is bolted to the platform, which has a 2 in. high lip and a LADOT name, a 1 in. x 1 in. notch for cable, and a note for slopes greater than 1/2 in. rise per foot.</p>
<p>Platform, top view detail (not to scale): a 36 in. x 36 in. square steel platform with four 5 gal. cans at the corners, a Type 1 base at the center with 4 bolts, and chain tied through the cans around the standard. Platform, bottom view detail (not to scale): 36 in. x 36 in. with steel angle brackets framing the underside, 4 holes at 1-1/4 in. diameter on an 8-1/2 in. diameter bolt circle, and 1 in. x 1 in. notches for cable at the lip.</p>
<p>Weight block (not to scale): a 5 gal. can filled with concrete, 12 in. wide by 14 in. tall, with a 1/2 in. diameter steel rod loop embedded 9 in. deep at 4 in. spacing and 3 in. above the can. Minimum weight 90 lbs. LADOT or contractor name is embossed or welded on at least two sides.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Temporary Controller for Type 8 Standard, sheet 1 of 1, drawing number S-57.3, drawn 3-1-16, approved 4-11-16 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Page number 42.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. 36 in. square (SQ.) x 1/4 in. thick (THK.) steel platform, hot dip galvanized per ASTM 123.</p>
<p>2. Type 1 base with 4 1 in. diameter x 3-1/2 in. bolts and nuts with 2 washers for each bolt.</p>
<p>3. 3/8 in. diameter steel coil chain tied to each 5 gal. block and locked around the Type 8 standard (and the Type 1 base in the top view detail).</p>
<p>4. 5 gal. can filled with concrete. Four cans are required, spaced as shown.</p>
<p>5. 2 in. high x 1/4 in. thick perimeter lip resting on ground.</p>
<p>6. 1-3/4 in. x 1-3/4 in. x 1/4 in. steel angle brackets welded to plate.</p>
<p>7. 4 holes at 1-1/4 in. diameter. 8-1/2 in. diameter bolt circle.</p>
<p>8. 1 in. x 1 in. notches for cable.</p>
<p>9. For slopes greater than 1/2 in. rise per foot, temporary standard to be set level through the use of grading or wood shims.</p>
<p>10. Weight block: 5 gal. can filled with concrete, minimum weight 90 lbs. 1/2 in. diameter steel rod embedded 9 in. deep. LADOT or contractor name embossed or welded on at least two sides.</p>
<p>Label: 337 cabinet. Base assembly. Lip.</p></details>
