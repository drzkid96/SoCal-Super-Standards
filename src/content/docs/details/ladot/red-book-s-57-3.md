<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">36 in. square (SQ.) x 1/4 in. thick (THK.) steel platform, hot dip galvanized per ASTM 123.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Type 1 base with 4 1 in.</strong> diameter x 3-1/2 in. bolts and nuts with 2 washers for each bolt.</li>
<li value="3">3/8 in. diameter steel coil chain tied to each 5 gal. block and locked around the Type 8 standard (and the Type 1 base in the top view detail).</li>
<li value="4">5 gal. can filled with concrete. Four cans are required, spaced as shown.</li>
<li value="5">2 in. high x 1/4 in. thick perimeter lip resting on ground.</li>
<li value="6">1-3/4 in. x 1-3/4 in. x 1/4 in. steel angle brackets welded to plate.</li>
<li value="7">4 holes at 1-1/4 in. diameter. 8-1/2 in. diameter bolt circle.</li>
<li value="8">1 in. x 1 in. notches for cable.</li>
<li value="9"><strong>For slopes greater than 1/2 in.</strong> rise per foot, temporary standard to be set level through the use of grading or wood shims.</li>
<li value="10"><strong>Weight block: 5 gal.</strong> can filled with concrete, minimum weight 90 lbs. 1/2 in. diameter steel rod embedded 9 in. deep. LADOT or contractor name embossed or welded on at least two sides.</li>
</ol>
<p>Label: 337 cabinet. Base assembly. Lip.</p>
</details>
