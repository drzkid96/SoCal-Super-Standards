<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Drawing S-79.02B showing the larger precast Fiber Optic Splice Box II, with a plan of its diamond plate steel parkway cover, a section and isometrics of the cover, frame, riser and base. The base is 2'-11" tall, 2'-8" x 3'-8" overall, with a 6 in. dia. sump, two 7/8 in. pull irons, two 1 in. ground rod knockouts and 8 in. x 16 in. knockouts on each side. It sits on a 12 in. minimum bed of 1 in. crushed rock.</p>




## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 85)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0085.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0085.webp" alt="S-79.02B sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-79.02B, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p85">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of lid: The precast splice box shown from above with a sloped frame (PCC or other material approved by the engineer) around a diamond-plate steel parkway cover marked &#x27;LADOT&#x27;, with two 3/4&quot; lifting holes and edge notches. Callout: 3/4&quot; lifting holes.</p>
<p>Section: The box top is flush with pavement. The overall width of the top is 44&quot; and the walls are 6&quot; thick, with a stepped inner face and a notched base that holds a small recess at the center of the sloped floor. The base sits on a 12&quot; MIN. bed of 1&quot; clean crushed rock sump. Two small holes show the lifting inserts on the wall.</p>
<p>Isometric of cover: A 3/16&quot; diamond plate steel parkway cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot; with an L-shaped cut, 1&quot; dia. pick holes, 1-1/2&quot; x 1-1/2&quot; x 1/4&quot; angle all around, and a U-slot in plate and angle for bolt down to frame.</p>
<p>Isometric of frame: 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot; opening, 6&quot; tall, called &#x27;6&quot; top incl. frame height w/ RPM cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;&#x27;, placed in PCC or other material approved by the engineer.</p>
<p>Isometric of riser and base: The riser is 2&#x27;-8&quot; x 3&#x27;-8&quot; overall with a 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot; opening and a 4&quot; wall. The base section is labeled 2&#x27;-8&quot; x 3&#x27;-8&quot;, 2&#x27;-11&quot; overall height and 30&quot; inside depth, with a floor 8&quot; thick. It has 4 lifting inserts (2 each side), a 6&quot; dia. sump in the floor, two 1&quot; dia. ground rod knockouts, and 2 pull irons 7/8&quot; dia. 8&quot; x 16&quot; knockouts (2 each side) are shown as a grid of rectangles on the side wall.</p>
<p>Title block: Fiber Optic Splice Box II, sheet 1 of 1. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation. Drawing No. S-79.02B. CKD JEM 2-5-08, Sr. T.E. JEM 2-13-08, Pr. T.E. SFS 2-13-08. Approved June 26, 2008, signed for Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Printed page number 78.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Two 1&quot; dia. grounding rod knockouts shall be provided.</p>
<p>2. Lifting rings and bolts shall be provided.</p>
<p>3. Parkway cover shall be 3/16&quot; safety plate, 5/16&quot; safety plate for traffic cover.</p>
<p>4. All metal surfaces for special frame and cover shall have a galvanized finish.</p>
<p>5. All welding, machining and drilling shall be done before galvanizing.</p>
<p>6. Plug threads must be greased thoroughly prior to installation.</p>
<p>Callouts: 3/4&quot; lifting holes. 1&quot; dia. pick holes. 3/16&quot; diamond plate steel parkway cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. 1-1/2&quot; x 1-1/2&quot; x 1/4&quot; angle all around. U-slot in plate and angle for bolt down to frame. Flush with pavement. 6&quot; top incl. frame height w/ RPM cover 2&#x27;-0&quot; x 3&#x27;-0&quot;. PCC or other material approved by the engineer. 1&quot; clean crushed rock sump, 12&quot; minimum (MIN.). 2&#x27;-8&quot; x 3&#x27;-8&quot;. 8&quot; x 16&quot; knockouts, 2 each side. 4 lifting inserts, 2 each side. Sump 6&quot; dia. (2) 1&quot; dia. ground rod knockouts. 2 pull irons 7/8&quot; dia.</p></details>
