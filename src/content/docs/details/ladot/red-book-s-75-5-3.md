<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows the Type M controller cabinet (Drawing S-75.5.3) with a front elevation, side elevation and anchor bolt layout. The cabinet is 30 in. wide, at least 48 in. high and at least 16 in. deep, with filtered louvers, an optional police door cutout and an optional removable lifting plate per S-68.1. It sits on a Type F-12A foundation. When used as an ATSAC type I communication cabinet, a 1 in. screened drain pipe and a 19 in. x 33 in. plywood panel are required. See Specification No. 92-044-04 or latest revision.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 78)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Front elevation: The Type M controller cabinet is drawn as a rectangular enclosure 30&quot; wide, with a roof overhang at the top and a small lifting-plate lug at each upper corner (arrow callout &#x27;Removable lifting plate per S-68.1 if required&#x27;). The cabinet stands a minimum (MIN.) of 48&quot; high above the foundation. The door has a hinge on the right edge and a handle on the left. A boxed callout in the upper door reads &#x27;Cutout for Police door if required&#x27;. Three groups of three horizontal louver slots at the bottom of the door are called out &#x27;Filtered ventilating louvers&#x27;. The door opening is dimensioned 26&quot; wide.</p>
<p>Side elevation: The side of the cabinet is 48&quot; MIN. high with a sloped roof that rises toward the back and has an overhanging front lip. A removable lifting plate per S-68.1 sits on the roof, shown 2&quot; high with a 1/2&quot; D dimension below it. The depth of the cabinet is dimensioned D = 16&quot; MIN.</p>
<p>Anchor bolt layout (plan): A rectangle with two anchor bolt slots on a horizontal centerline. The slots are 12 3/8&quot; apart and the centerline is 15&quot; from the bottom edge of the rectangle.</p>
<p>Table: Type M cabinet uses Foundation F-12A.</p>
<p>Title block: Cabinet, Controller, Type M. City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Donald R. Howery, General Manager, approved August 2, 1982 (stamped 9/87). Drawing No. S-75.5.3. Revisions: add removable lifting plate (1-22-83), added notes (6-5-90, revision 1), update specification number (2-16-05, revision 2). Printed page number 71.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Revision 1 note:</h5>
<p>1. When this cabinet is used as an ATSAC type I communication cabinet, a one-inch (1&quot;) screened drain pipe shall be provided through the foundation. A 19&quot; x 33&quot; or larger 3/4-inch thick plywood mounting panel shall be installed on the inside of the rear wall. Machine screws and bolts shall not protrude beyond the outside wall of the cabinet.</p>
<p>Callouts: Cutout for police door if required. Filtered ventilating louvers. Removable lifting plate per S-68.1 if required. Anchor bolt layout. Door opening 26&quot;. D = 16&quot; minimum (MIN.).</p>
<p>Revision 2: Specification No. 92-044-04 or latest revision.</p>
<p>Foundation table: Type M, Foundation F-12A.</p></details>
