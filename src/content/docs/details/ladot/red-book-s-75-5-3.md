<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Revision 1 note:</h5>
<p>1. When this cabinet is used as an ATSAC type I communication cabinet, a one-inch (1&quot;) screened drain pipe shall be provided through the foundation. A 19&quot; x 33&quot; or larger 3/4-inch thick plywood mounting panel shall be installed on the inside of the rear wall. Machine screws and bolts shall not protrude beyond the outside wall of the cabinet.</p>
<p>Callouts: Cutout for police door if required. Filtered ventilating louvers. Removable lifting plate per S-68.1 if required. Anchor bolt layout. Door opening 26&quot;. D = 16&quot; minimum (MIN.).</p>
<p>Revision 2: Specification No. 92-044-04 or latest revision.</p>
<p>Foundation table: Type M, Foundation F-12A.</p></details>
