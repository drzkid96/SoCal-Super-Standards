<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-45.0 shows how laminated overhead sign panels in an H-edge aluminum frame are mounted on cantilever mast arms using aluminum I-beams, U-bolts and clamps. A table gives maximum panel size, positioning dimensions and U-bolt size for 12', 22' and 34' arms, with a parts list and sign material notes.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 102)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0102.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0102.webp" alt="S-45.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-45.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/07-pages-97-112/#p102">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A laminated overhead sign panel in a &quot;H&quot; edge aluminum frame (item 1) seen from above, with the mast arm (item 3, shown dashed behind the panel) running horizontally along the panel centerline from the mast arm flange on the left. Overall panel length is dimension A. Along the top, dimension C is the distance from the left end of the panel to the first I-beam, then I-beam spacing is 6&#x27; MAX., and dimension E is the distance from the last I-beam to the right end. Panel height is dimension B, with dimension D locating the arm centerline from the bottom edge, and the arm centerline is shown centered. The I-beams (item 2) run vertically across the panel, attached to the arm by U-bolts (item 4) at two locations. Aluminum straps (item 5) at the panel closures are shown at one vertical joint (4 per closure). Mounting clamps (item 6) and post clip bolts (item 7) are shown at the top and bottom ends of each I-beam. A 2&#x27;-6&quot; MAX. dimension is given on the right end. Cut lines A-A, B-B and C-C are marked.</p>
<p>Elevation: Side view below the plan shows the mast arm (item 3) on the left flange with U-bolts and I-beams (item 2) under the arm, over the panel (item 1) shown edge-on.</p>
<p>End view: The panel seen from the arm end, with the arm (item 3) held by U-bolt (item 4), clamps (item 6) and bolts (item 7) at the top and bottom of the I-beam, and the &quot;H&quot; edge aluminum frame. Dimensions B and D are shown.</p>
<p>Section A-A (not to scale): Cross section through the mast arm (item 3), with a U-bolt (item 4) around it and passing through the I-beam web (item 2) with nuts. X is the inside diameter of the U-bolt, Y is the overall length of U-bolt, 4&quot; dimension at the bottom of the U-bolt. The panel (item 1) with &quot;H&quot; edge aluminum frame is to the right.</p>
<p>Section B-B (not to scale): View through the arm, I-beam and panel showing two U-bolts (item 4) through the arm, the I-beam (item 2) under the arm, mounting clamps (item 6) with bolts (item 7) on the I-beam flanges and the panel (item 1) with &quot;H&quot; edge aluminum frame.</p>
<p>Section C-C (not to scale): View at a panel closure showing the aluminum straps (item 5) with bolts (item 7) on the panel (item 1) with &quot;H&quot; edge aluminum frame.</p>
<p>Mounting Hardware (not to scale): Shows the mounting clamp (item 6), with a bolt, nut and washer, and item 7 post clip bolt in side and end views. Item 7 length is 1.750&quot;, head is .875&quot; by .625&quot;.</p>
<p>Table of dimensions, by mast arm length (A and B are maximum sign dimensions, C, D and E are positioning dimensions, X and Y are U-bolt dimensions):</p>
<p>12&#x27; mast arm: A 14&#x27;, B 5&#x27;, C 3&#x27; MAX., D 2.5&#x27;, E 3&#x27; MAX., X (inside dia.) 6-5/8&quot;, Y (length) 24&quot;.</p>
<p>22&#x27; mast arm: A 18&#x27;, B 5&#x27;, C 3&#x27; MAX., D 2.5&#x27;, E 3&#x27; MAX., X 8-5/8&quot;, Y 28&quot;.</p>
<p>34&#x27; mast arm (two arms): A 30&#x27;, B 6&#x27;, C 3&#x27; MAX., D 1.5&#x27;, E 3&#x27; MAX., X 10-1/4&quot;, Y 34&quot;.</p>
<h5>Parts list (No., description, quantity):</h5>
<p>1. Type &quot;A&quot; laminated sign panel per LADOT Specification 82-052-01. Quantity: none shown.</p>
<p>2. I-beam 6&quot; x 3.33&quot;, 4.3 lbs/linear ft., 6061-T6 aluminum alloy. Quantity: 1 per panel.</p>
<p>3. Mast arm per LADOT Std. Dwg. Nos. S-92.2, S-92.3 or S-98.0. Quantity: none shown.</p>
<p>4. 1/2&quot;-13 UNC-2A, stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized U-bolt (4&quot; thread) with hex nut and tapered washer (ASTM A307). Quantity: 2 per I-beam.</p>
<p>5. 1/4&quot; x 2&quot; x 6&quot; aluminum straps for each panel closure per LADOT Specification 82-052-01. Quantity: 4 per closure.</p>
<p>6. Aluminum mounting clamp. Quantity: 4 per I-beam.</p>
<p>7. 3/8&quot; x 1.750&quot; (18-8) stainless steel post clip bolt (16 threads) with hex head nylon insert stop nut and flat washer. Quantity: 4 per I-beam.</p>
<p>Title block: Laminated Overhead Sign Mounting System, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Robert R. Yates, General Manager. Drawn by RMO 5-12-94, reviewed by KF 5-17-96, approved 5-28-96. Drawing No. S-45.0. Page number 95.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Overhead sign panels shall be Type &quot;A&quot; laminated sign panel per LADOT Spec. 82-052-01.</p>
<p>2. See LADOT Std. Dwg. Nos. S-92.2, S-92.3, or S-98.0 for dimensions, spacings, quantities, and other requirements dependent on mast arm specifications.</p>
<p>3. All bolts, nuts and washers shall be stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized in accordance with Section 210-3, Std. Spec. for Public Works Construction (1985 edition).</p>
<p>4. Guide signs shall have a white legend and border made of FP92 Type III (high intensity) reflective material on a green background made of FP92 Type II (super-engineering grade) reflective material.</p>
<p>5. Regulatory signs shall have a black (Scotch-Cal) legend and border, on a white background made of FP92 Type IV (VIP grade) reflective material.</p>
<p>Drawing labels: Y = overall length of U-bolt. &quot;H&quot; edge aluminum frame. Sections and mounting hardware are not to scale.</p></details>
