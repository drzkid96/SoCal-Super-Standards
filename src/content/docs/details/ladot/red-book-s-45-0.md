<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">General notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Overhead sign panels shall be Type &quot;A&quot; laminated sign panel per LADOT Spec. 82-052-01.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>See LADOT Std.</strong> Dwg. Nos. S-92.2, S-92.3, or S-98.0 for dimensions, spacings, quantities, and other requirements dependent on mast arm specifications.</li>
<li value="3">All bolts, nuts and washers shall be stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized in accordance with Section 210-3, Std. Spec. for Public Works Construction (1985 edition).</li>
<li value="4">Guide signs shall have a white legend and border made of FP92 Type III (high intensity) reflective material on a green background made of FP92 Type II (super-engineering grade) reflective material.</li>
<li value="5">Regulatory signs shall have a black (Scotch-Cal) legend and border, on a white background made of FP92 Type IV (VIP grade) reflective material.</li>
</ol>
</details>
