<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Standard drawing S-457.0 (approved 12-1-88) for the Type 1 mast arm street name sign bracket in two versions: the M10J-OCB250AL adjustable length swing bracket (13-3/8 to 17-7/8 inches) and the M10J-OCB250FL fixed length bracket (5-1/2 inches). It lists the 13 numbered stainless steel and aluminum components and shows typical installation of a Main St sign on a mast arm, banded with 3/4 inch type 201 stainless steel banding.</p>

## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 115)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0115.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0115.webp" alt="S-457.0 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-457.0, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/08-pages-113-128/#p115">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation views of the M10J-OCB250AL adjustable length swing sign bracket (all aluminum 6061T6 alloy and stainless steel components), left side of sheet: Two side elevations. The first is shown fully extended, 17-7/8&quot; overall length, and the second is fully closed, 13-3/8&quot; overall length. At the top is a U-shaped upper bracket 2-1/2&quot; wide with 1/4&quot; leg thickness and 1/2&quot; leg height, held with a bolt and nut. A 2-1/4&quot; dimension is shown from the top of the pivot plate to the bolt. The serrated (toothed) adjustment section allows a length adjustment of 4-1/2&quot;. Dimensions 2&quot; and 3/8&quot; are given at the bottom near the 1&quot; axle housing, with 3/8&quot; from the bottom of the sign mounting hole to the bottom of the bracket. The sign is shown dashed at the bottom. Circled numbers 11 and 12 mark the upper and lower adjustable sections.</p>
<p>Isometric view of the adjustable bracket: shows the pivotal upper bracket (1) with two 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; slots (2) and a 2-1/2&quot; width, bolt (3), upper adjustable section (11), lower adjustable section (12) with a slot and bolt (13), dampener spring (5), lock nut (6), axle housing (7), bolt (8), oilite bushings (9), and two sign mounting bolts (10). Dimensions at the bottom: 1-1/2&quot; bolt hole spacing, 3/8&quot; and 2-1/2&quot; widths.</p>
<p>M10J-OCB250FL fixed length non-adjustable swing sign bracket (all aluminum 6061T6 alloy and stainless steel components), center of sheet: A side elevation, 5-1/2&quot; overall height, with the 2-1/2&quot; wide U-shaped upper bracket (1/4&quot; and 1-1/2&quot; dimensions), bolt (3) with a 2-1/4&quot; dimension, fixed bracket (4), dampener spring (5), axle housing (7), bolt (8) and sign mounting bolt (10) with 2&quot; and 3/8&quot; dimensions, and a note 3/8&quot; from bottom of sign mounting hole to bottom of bracket. An isometric shows the same parts with 2-1/2&quot; and 3/8&quot; widths and 1-1/2&quot; hole spacing. An Extension Straps detail at upper right shows the strap plate with 1/2&quot; dia. holes, 1/2&quot; edge distances, 3-1/2&quot; hole spacing, 1/8&quot; thickness and 2-1/2&quot; width.</p>
<p>Typical installation elevation (lower center): A signal pole with a mast arm and a signal head (centerline marked), with a &quot;Main St&quot; street name sign hanging from the mast arm by two brackets. The signal head is 30&quot; from the sign, and the sign length L has L/4 overhang each side beyond the brackets (brackets spaced L/2 apart). Labels: M10J-OCB250AL Bracket and M10J-OCB250FL Bracket.</p>
<p>Title block: Mast Arm Street Name Sign Bracket Type 1, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, S. E. Rowe, General Manager, approved 12-1-88. Drawing No. S-457.0. Drawn (DWN) HH. Page number 108.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Component legend (circled numbers):</h5>
<p>(1) Pivotal upper bracket.</p>
<p>(2) 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; slot for double strapping to electrolier mast arm. Cat. No. M2G-34S(HD) .032&quot; x 1/4&quot; heavy duty stainless steel strap with Cat. No. M2G-34B(HD) buckle recommended.</p>
<p>(3) 1/2&quot;-13 x 1-1/2&quot; stainless steel hex head bolt with stainless steel hex lock nut and 1/16&quot; stainless steel washer (both sides). Allows upper bracket to pivot and align with electrolier mast arm. Do not tighten past nut locking feature for pivot action to be operational.</p>
<p>(4) 5-1/2&quot; overall length fixed length sign bracket.</p>
<p>(5) Stainless steel dampener spring (removable).</p>
<p>(6) Stainless steel hex lock nut with 1/16&quot; stainless steel washer. Do not tighten, it binds hinges.</p>
<p>(7) 1&quot; O.D. axle housing.</p>
<p>(8) 1/2&quot;-13 x 4&quot; stainless steel hex head bolt with 1/16&quot; stainless steel washer. Do not tighten lock nut past locking feature, it binds hinges.</p>
<p>(9) Oilite bushing.</p>
<p>(10) Sign mounting sets, consisting of two each 5/16&quot;-18 x 1&quot; stainless steel hex head bolt with stainless steel hex lock nut. Two holes on 1-1/2&quot; centers provide positive lock sign mounting to bracket.</p>
<p>(11) 7-3/4&quot; overall length upper adjustable sign bracket section.</p>
<p>(12) 9&quot; overall length lower adjustable sign bracket section, including axle housing (8&quot; overall length to top of axle housing).</p>
<p>(13) 1/2&quot;-13 x 1-1/2&quot; stainless steel hex bolt with stainless steel hex lock nut and 1/16&quot; stainless steel washers (both sides). Loosen lock nut, adjust bracket teeth to level sign.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Brackets available from: Hawkins Traffic Safety Supply, 1255 Eastshore Highway, Berkeley, CA 94710, (415) 525-4040.</p>
<p>2. If another sign is installed on the mast arm next to the signal head, the street name sign shall be installed with 12&quot; clearance to the edge of that sign.</p>
<p>3. Sign bracket shall be attached to mast arm with 3/4&quot; wide type 201 stainless steel sign banding secured with a banding buckle. Banding shall be 0.020&quot; thick double banded or 0.030&quot; thick single banded.</p>
<p>Drawing labels: Allows length adjustment of 4-1/2&quot;. 17-7/8&quot; fully extend overall length. 13-3/8&quot; fully closed overall length.</p></details>
