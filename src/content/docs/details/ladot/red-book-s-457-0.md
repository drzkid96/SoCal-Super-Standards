<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Component legend (circled numbers):</h5>
<p>(1) Pivotal upper bracket.</p>
<p>(2) 1&quot; x 1/4&quot; slot for double strapping to electrolier mast arm. Cat. No. M2G-34S(HD) .032&quot; x 1/4&quot; heavy duty stainless steel strap with Cat. No. M2G-34B(HD) buckle recommended.</p>
<p>(3) 1/2&quot;-13 x 1-1/2&quot; stainless steel hex head bolt with stainless steel hex lock nut and 1/16&quot; stainless steel washer (both sides). Allows upper bracket to pivot and align with electrolier mast arm. Do not tighten past nut locking feature for pivot action to be operational.</p>
<p>(4) 5-1/2&quot; overall length fixed length sign bracket.</p>
<p>(5) Stainless steel dampener spring (removable).</p>
<p>(6) Stainless steel hex lock nut with 1/16&quot; stainless steel washer. Do not tighten, it binds hinges.</p>
<p>(7) 1&quot; O.D. axle housing.</p>
<p>(8) 1/2&quot;-13 x 4&quot; stainless steel hex head bolt with 1/16&quot; stainless steel washer. Do not tighten lock nut past locking feature, it binds hinges.</p>
<p>(9) Oilite bushing.</p>
<p>(10) Sign mounting sets, consisting of two each 5/16&quot;-18 x 1&quot; stainless steel hex head bolt with stainless steel hex lock nut. Two holes on 1-1/2&quot; centers provide positive lock sign mounting to bracket.</p>
<p>(11) 7-3/4&quot; overall length upper adjustable sign bracket section.</p>
<p>(12) 9&quot; overall length lower adjustable sign bracket section, including axle housing (8&quot; overall length to top of axle housing).</p>
<p>(13) 1/2&quot;-13 x 1-1/2&quot; stainless steel hex bolt with stainless steel hex lock nut and 1/16&quot; stainless steel washers (both sides). Loosen lock nut, adjust bracket teeth to level sign.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Brackets available from: Hawkins Traffic Safety Supply, 1255 Eastshore Highway, Berkeley, CA 94710, (415) 525-4040.</p>
<p>2. If another sign is installed on the mast arm next to the signal head, the street name sign shall be installed with 12&quot; clearance to the edge of that sign.</p>
<p>3. Sign bracket shall be attached to mast arm with 3/4&quot; wide type 201 stainless steel sign banding secured with a banding buckle. Banding shall be 0.020&quot; thick double banded or 0.030&quot; thick single banded.</p></details>
