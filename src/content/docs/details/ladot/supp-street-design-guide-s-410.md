<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Supplemental Design Guide Detail 300 (sheets 1 and 2 of 6) for mountable truck aprons at corners, plus the S-526.0 striping layout. The apron is 5 ft minimum to 15 ft maximum wide, with a mountable curb, 3 in minimum curb height at the Type A curb, and an 8 in concrete apron on 8 in minimum subbase. Directional curb ramps are located outside the truck apron. S-526.0 shows 12 in white lines spaced 12 in to 36 in on center and an 8 in white edge lane line over the apron.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 3 (document page 114)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0114.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0114.webp" alt="S-410 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-410, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/07-pages-109-126/#p114">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan, mountable truck apron, directional curb ramps located outside truck apron: A corner with the street flowline and an outer face of curb with radius R2 (15&#x27; typical) and an inner curb with radius R1. The gray apron between the two curves is the mountable truck apron, 5&#x27; MIN. and 15&#x27; MAX. wide. Points of curvature and tangency are labeled for R1 and R2. Mountable curb, see note 1, runs along the outer edge. The inner edge is a curb with 3&quot; min. curb height, see note 2. The mountable truck apron is also tied to note 4. Directional curb ramps are placed outside the apron on each leg. The ramp note reads curb ramp, typ, directional curb ramp shown, but may vary based on available sidewalk space, see S-442. Integral curb and gutter, see S-410. A crosswalk is shown as gray bars. Section cut A/1 is marked at the outer curb.</p>
<p>Section A/1, apron section, 1 tier apron: From left to right, repair or reconstruct street paving as necessary, 2&#x27; min, then the flow line, see note 3, then the mountable curb, see sheet 2. The mountable truck apron width varies (5&#x27; min, 15&#x27; max) and slopes 0.25% MIN to 1% MAX, see note 6, toward the flow line. The apron is 8&quot; concrete, see note 4, on 8&quot; min subbase. A curb, see note 2, stands 3&quot; MIN. above the apron, see note 2, and separates it from the sidewalk.</p>
<p>Title block: Truck Aprons, Supplemental Design Guide, Detail 300, sheet 1 of 6.</p></details>

Referenced items: note 1 mountable curb. Note 2 curb and 3&quot; min. curb height. Note 3 flow line. Note 4 mountable truck apron and 8&quot; concrete. Note 6 apron slope 0.25% min to 1% max.</p>

### Sheet 2 of 3 (document page 115)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0115.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/p0115.webp" alt="S-410 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Supp. Street Design Guide S-410, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/supplemental-street-design-guide/07-pages-109-126/#p115">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan, mountable truck apron, directional curb ramps located within truck apron: A corner with face of curb and gutter radius R2, a mountable curb (see note 1), and a gray truck apron 5&#x27; MIN. 15&#x27; MAX. wide. Callouts: grade break. Warp apron to match bottom landing of curb ramp. 4&#x27; x 4&#x27; curb ramp landing. Street flowline. Point of curvature (R1). Point of tangency (R1). Face of curb radius, R1. Type A curb, 3&quot; min. curb height, see note 2. Mountable truck apron, see note 4. Curb ramp transition area, see note 5. Curb ramp, typ, directional curb ramp shown but may vary based on available sidewalk space, see S-442. Integral curb and gutter, see S-410. A/2 section cut through the ramp.</p>
<p>Section A/2, apron section: Repair or reconstruct street paving as necessary, 2&#x27; min, then mountable curb (see this sheet) and a 48&quot; curb ramp landing with 5% MAX. slope and 0&quot; CH at flowline, see note 3. The curb ramp transition area (length varies) rises at 8.33% MAX. toward the curb ramp, see S-442, over the mountable truck apron whose width varies, 8&quot; concrete (smooth surface) on 8&quot; min subbase. A curb runs along the back of the truck apron, and a detectable warning surface, see note 5, is at the top of the ramp.</p>
<p>Mountable curb and concrete pavement, profile: The curb top is 5&quot; wide with R=3/4&quot; at the nose and a 1/4&quot; rise at the back, a 3&quot; high sloped face over a 2&quot; run, R=1/2&quot; at the toe, a 1&#x27; deep concrete section and 2&#x27; total width, with R=1/2&quot; at the back top corner. The curb line is marked at the nose.</p>
<p>Title block: Supplemental Design Guide, Detail 300, sheet 2 of 6.</p></details>

Referenced items: note 1 mountable curb. Note 2 Type A curb 3&quot; min. height. Note 3 0&quot; curb height at flowline. Note 4 mountable truck apron. Note 5 curb ramp transition area and detectable warning surface.</p>
