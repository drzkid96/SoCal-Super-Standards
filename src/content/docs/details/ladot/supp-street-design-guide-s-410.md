<p class="dwg-sum">Supplemental Design Guide Detail 300 (sheets 1 and 2 of 6) for mountable truck aprons at corners, plus the S-526.0 striping layout. The apron is 5 ft minimum to 15 ft maximum wide, with a mountable curb, 3 in minimum curb height at the Type A curb, and an 8 in concrete apron on 8 in minimum subbase. Directional curb ramps are located outside the truck apron. S-526.0 shows 12 in white lines spaced 12 in to 36 in on center and an 8 in white edge lane line over the apron.</p>
