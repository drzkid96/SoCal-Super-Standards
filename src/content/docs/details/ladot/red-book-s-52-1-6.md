<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows how a 100 mph signal pole is drilled for side-mounted signal indications and tagged. A hole pattern on the side opposite the mast arm sets 9 in spacing, and signal heads mount at 11 ft 3 in and 7 ft 6 in above grade. Metal tags give the pole type, load case, design wind velocity, mast arm length and height.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 39)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>100 MPH Poles, sheet 2 of 2. Side Mount Indications Drill Detail.</p>
<p>Elevation: mast arm length measured from the pole to the outermost signal head. Signal head spacing F1 and F2 along the arm. Standard height shown at the pole. Side-mounted signal indications at 11 ft 3 in and 7 ft 6 in above grade. Hand hole near the base. CIDH foundation, see detail on sheet 1.</p>
<p>Side view opposite of mast arm: two 3/4 in dia. holes (drill through) and one 2 1/4 in dia. hole (drill 1 side only), spaced 4 1/2 in and 9 in as shown.</p>
<p>Metal tag on mast arm: LA-XX-X-XXX-XX, reading Type, Load case, Design wind velocity, Actual mast arm length (ft).</p>
<p>Metal tag at hand hole: LA-XX-X-XXX-XX-XX, reading Type, Load case, Design wind velocity, Max. mast arm length (ft), Height (ft).</p></details>
