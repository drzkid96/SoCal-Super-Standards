## Sheets

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 38)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0038.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0038.webp" alt="S-52.1.6 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.6, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p38">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>100 MPH Poles, sheet 1 of 2, drawing S-52.1.6. The sheet shows a foundation elevation, Section A-A, a base plate plan and a schedule table.</p>
<p>Foundation elevation: a pole shaft sits on a base plate with anchor bolts in a cast-in-drilled-hole concrete pile. Type 560-C-3250 concrete with a Reinforcing cage near the bottom. Dimensions: 36&quot; from the curb to the pole centerline, 18&quot; min. depth to the conduit, Depth (pile depth, from the schedule), 3&quot; clearance below the cage, and Diameter (pile diameter, from the schedule). Anchor bolts (2 nuts &amp; 2 washers required per bolt), total 4, are threaded 8&quot; at the top and galvanized 12&quot;, with 1&quot; +/- 1/4&quot; and 2&quot; Min. to 3&quot; Max. mortar under the plate. After plumbing standard, place mortar all around bolts. Finish with slope ranging from 45 to 90 degrees including drain holes. Finished grade is marked. Callouts: #8 Green bonding wire; 4&quot; X 6 1/2&quot; Hand Hole reinforced with ring welded to outside of pole, see note 2, 1/8&quot; cover plate, 8&quot; dimension to the plate; 0.50&quot; NC square nut holder with fastener for grounding welded to interior of pole shaft at 180 degrees from hand hole rim; Traffic signal conduit (2&quot;); Street lighting conduit (1&quot;). Section cut A-A is marked on the pile.</p>
<p>Section A-A, Cast-in-drilled-hole (CIDH) pile foundation: a circular section with Diameter dimension, 2&quot; clearance to the cage, #8 - total 10 longitudinal bars, and #4 @ 6&quot; or #4 spiral @ 6&quot; pitch with 1&#x27;-6&quot; lap @ splice and 135 degree hook @ terminations. Centerlines marked.</p>
<p>Base plate: a square plate with side C, bolt circle, four anchor bolt slots on the diagonals, 3&quot; and 2 3/8&quot; (2 5/8&quot; for 60 - 5 - 100 &amp; 61 - 5 - 100) dimensions and a 1 1/2&quot; dimension.</p>
<p>Table of 100 MPH poles (12 types). Columns: TYPE, Standard Height, Mast Arm Length, F Min. Spacing, CIDH Pile Foundation (Dia., Depth, Cage Dia., Cage Depth), Anchor Bolts, Bolt Circle, C. Rows: 16 - 3 - 100: 18.5&#x27;, mast arm 15&#x27; or 20&#x27;, F 8&#x27;. 17 - 3 - 100: 30&#x27;, mast arm 15&#x27; or 20&#x27;, F 8&#x27;. 18 - 4 - 100: 17&#x27;, 25&#x27; or 30&#x27;, F 12&#x27;. 19 - 4 - 100: 30&#x27;, 25&#x27; or 30&#x27;, F 12&#x27;. 23 - 4 - 100: 17&#x27;, 35&#x27;, F 14&#x27;. 24 - 4 - 100: 30&#x27;, 35&#x27;, F 14&#x27;. 26 - 4 - 100: 30&#x27;, 40&#x27; or 45&#x27;, F 15&#x27;. 27 - 4 - 100: 17&#x27;, 40&#x27; or 45&#x27;, F 15&#x27;. 28 - 5 - 100: 17&#x27;, 50&#x27; or 55&#x27;, F1 = 15&#x27; and F2 = 12&#x27;. 29 - 5 - 100: 30&#x27;, 50&#x27; or 55&#x27;, same F. 60 - 5 - 100: 17&#x27;, 60&#x27; or 65&#x27;, same F. 61 - 5 - 100: 30&#x27;, 60&#x27; or 65&#x27;, same F. Foundation for all rows: Dia. 36&quot;, cage dia. 32&quot;. Rows 16 through 27: pile depth 9&#x27;, cage depth 8&#x27; - 6&quot;, anchor bolts 2&quot;Ø X 42&quot; X 6&quot;, C 18&quot;; bolt circle * for 16 - 3 - 100 and 17 - 3 - 100 and 18&quot; for 18 - 4 - 100 through 27 - 4 - 100. Rows 28 - 5 - 100 and 29 - 5 - 100: depth 9&#x27; - 2&quot;, cage depth 8&#x27; - 8&quot;, anchor bolts 2&quot;Ø X 42&quot; X 6&quot;, bolt circle 21&quot;, C 21&quot;. Rows 60 - 5 - 100 and 61 - 5 - 100: depth 16&#x27;, cage depth 15&#x27; - 6&quot;, anchor bolts 2 1/4&quot;Ø X 60&quot; X 6&quot;, bolt circle 23&quot;, C 23&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: 100 MPH Poles, sheet 1 of 2, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-52.1.6. Drawn MT 9-30-15, T.E. JV 9-30-15, Sr. T.E. MA 10-1-15, Pr. T.E. VJ 10-2-15, approved 11-20-15 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Booklet page number 31.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Fabrication of the standards shall conform to the 2006 State of California Department of Transportation Standard plans.</p>
<p>2. Hand Hole reinforcement ring shall be 1/4&quot; X 2&quot; for 0.1196&quot; to 0.2391&quot; thick poles, 3/8&quot; X 2&quot; for 0.3125&quot; thick poles.</p>
<p>3. Base plate to be fabricated as shown.</p>
<p>4. Luminaire Arm Length per Street Lighting Sheet.</p>
<p>5. Standard to be pre-drilled as shown.</p>
<p>6. Attach stamped metal tag above hand hole on pole and on mast arm (minimum 1/4&quot; height numbers see sheet 2).</p>
<p>* Fabricate and install with 18&quot; bolt circle.</p>
<p>Sheet 2 of 2 of this drawing continues the pole details (next page).</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 39)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0039.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0039.webp" alt="S-52.1.6 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-52.1.6, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p39">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation of a 100 mph signal pole with a mast arm, drawn with the arm projecting to the left of a vertical pole. A luminaire arm curves up and to the left from the pole top. The mast arm lies below it and carries three vertical three-section signal heads, drawn as rounded rectangles with three circles each. A horizontal dimension labeled Mast Arm Length runs from the outermost signal head to the pole centerline. Along the arm, F1 is the spacing between the first and second heads and F2 is the spacing between the second and third heads, with no numeric values given. A fourth, separate four-section signal head is drawn below the first head on a short stub of arm that is cut off with a break mark at the second head position, showing a four-section head option. A vertical dimension labeled Standard Height runs from grade to the top of the pole.</p>
<p>Metal tag on the mast arm: a rectangular tag reading LA-XX-X-XXX-XX, with a curved leader to the underside of the mast arm near the pole. Its fields are labeled TYPE, LOAD CASE, DESIGN WIND VELOCITY and ACTUAL MAST ARM LENGTH (FT).</p>
<p>Side-mounted indications on the pole: a three-section signal head and a pedestrian-style square indication are mounted on the side of the pole shaft below the mast arm, on the side opposite the mast arm. Two dash-dot reference lines from these heads lead across to the drill detail. Vertical dimensions from grade to the heads read 7&#x27;-6&quot; (to the lower, square indication) and 11&#x27;-3&quot; (to the upper three-section head). The Standard Height dimension line is the tallest one at the right.</p>
<p>Side view opposite of mast arm: an enlarged view of the pole shaft seen from the side opposite the mast arm, showing a vertical column of three holes on the pole centerline. Two small holes are called out as two 3/4 in. diameter holes (drill through) and one center hole is called out as a 2 1/4 in. diameter hole (drill 1 side only). The two small holes sit above and below the center hole. The dimension from the upper small hole to the center hole is 4 1/2 in., and the overall spacing between the two small holes is 9 in. The dash-dot lines from the signal head positions lead to this view.</p>
<p>Hand hole and metal tag at the base: a Hand Hole is shown on the pole shaft low on the pole with a curved leader. A second metal tag reading LA-XX-X-XXX-XX-XX is shown, with fields TYPE, LOAD CASE, DESIGN WIND VELOCITY, MAX. MAST ARM LENGTH (FT) and HEIGHT (FT). A curved leader runs from this tag to the hand hole.</p>
<p>Foundation: the pole stands on a base plate at grade over a rectangular cast-in-drilled-hole foundation drawn below the grade line, labeled CIDH Foundation, See Detail on Sheet 1.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, title 100 MPH Poles, sheet 2 of 2, drawing number S-52.1.6. Page number 32 appears below the sheet.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Title of detail: Side Mount Indications Drill Detail.</p>
<p>2. Mast arm length is measured from the pole to the outermost signal head. Signal head spacings are labeled F1 and F2, values not given on this sheet.</p>
<p>3. Side view opposite of mast arm: two 3/4 in. diameter holes (drill through) and one 2 1/4 in. diameter hole (drill 1 side only), spaced 4 1/2 in. and 9 in. as shown.</p>
<p>4. Metal tag on mast arm: LA-XX-X-XXX-XX, reading type, load case, design wind velocity, actual mast arm length (feet (FT)).</p>
<p>5. Metal tag at hand hole: LA-XX-X-XXX-XX-XX, reading type, load case, design wind velocity, maximum (MAX.) mast arm length (FT), height (FT).</p>
<p>6. Side-mounted indications are at 7&#x27;-6&quot; and 11&#x27;-3&quot; above grade.</p>
<p>7. Cast-in-drilled-hole (CIDH) foundation: see detail on sheet 1.</p></details>
