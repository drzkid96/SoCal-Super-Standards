<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Polymer concrete pull box for non-traffic areas with a non-skid polymer concrete cover marked TRAFFIC SIGNAL. Includes installation section and side view, cover details, captive bolt and threaded insert details, and a nominal dimensions table for Types PB2 and PB3. Boxes may be stacked no more than two deep.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 82)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0082.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0082.webp" alt="S-78.5.3 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-78.5.3, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p82">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Installation details: top flush with finished grade, 1&quot; crushed rock base, conduits enter through mouse hole knockouts, see Note 2.</p>
<p>Cover: polymer concrete cover with non-skid surface, TRAFFIC SIGNAL in 7/8&quot; letters, 4&quot; x 1/2&quot; pull slots with 3/16&quot; center pin, manufacturer&#x27;s logo, load rating, recess in cover for hardware (x2).</p>
<p>Typical cover captive bolt (or similar): 1/2&quot;-13 coarse thread stainless steel penta head bolt, stainless steel flat washer, cast-in bolt gripper, 1/2&quot;-13 unified national coarse thread jam nut with threadlocker.</p>
<p>Typical threaded insert (or similar): insert with drainage hole.</p>
<p>Box: polymer concrete box, open base, 3&quot; x 4-1/4&quot; mouse hole knockouts both end (2X).</p>
<p>Nominal dimensions table</p>
<p>Pull box type PB2: min. depth box (D) 12&quot;, min. depth extension (E) 10&quot;, LI min 1&#x27;-8&quot;, WI min 11&quot;. Cover: TE 2&quot;, B 1 3/4&quot;, L 1&#x27;-11 1/4&quot;, W 1&#x27;-1 3/4&quot;.</p>
<p>Pull box type PB3: min. depth box (D) 12&quot;, min. depth extension (E) 10&quot;, LI min 2&#x27;-4 1/4&quot;, WI min 1&#x27;-3 1/4&quot;. Cover: TE 2&quot;, B 2&quot;, L 2&#x27;-6 1/2&quot;, W 1&#x27;-5 1/2&quot;.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Refer to Dept. of Public Works Standard Drawing # L-201-1 for additional details and requirements.</p>
<p>2. Galvanized conduits in pull box must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>3. Pull box to be used in non-traffic areas, may be stacked no more than two deep.</p>
<p>4. Stencil TRAFFIC SIGNAL in 1&quot; lettering on backside of cover and inside of box.</p></details>
