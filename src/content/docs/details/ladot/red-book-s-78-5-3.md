<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">LADOT standard drawing S-78.5.3 for a polymer concrete pull box for non-traffic areas, with a non-skid cover marked TRAFFIC SIGNAL. It shows installation in section and side view, the cover plan, captive bolt and threaded insert details, an exploded isometric, and a dimensions table for Types PB2 and PB3. Boxes may be stacked no more than two deep.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 82)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0082.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0082.webp" alt="S-78.5.3 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-78.5.3, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/06-pages-81-96/#p82">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Section A-A (installation details, Detail A): A polymer concrete pull box set in the ground with its top flush with finished grade (callout TOP FLUSH WITH FINISHED GRADE). The box is shown in two stacked pieces, the lower box and an extension, with the cover on top. The depth of the upper section is labeled D and the lower extension E. The inside length is labeled LI. Two conduits enter low through the walls from either side, curving up into the box through mouse hole knockouts, bedded in 1&quot; Crushed rock. A &quot;See Note 2&quot; callout points to the conduit ends in the box. Dimensions at the conduit ends: 5&quot; Min and 2&quot; Min vertical, 2&quot; Min horizontal, and a 6&quot; Min dimension outside the box wall.</p>
<p>Side view: The same pull box seen from the side, section marks A at the top and bottom, top flush with finished grade over a crushed rock base. The inside width is labeled WI.</p>
<p>Cover top view: A rounded rectangle, length L and width W, with a 1-3/8&quot; corner dimension. Features: TRAFFIC SIGNAL in 7/8&quot; LETTERS in the center, two 4&quot; x 1/2&quot; PULL SLOT WITH 3/16&quot; CENTER PIN, MANUFACTURER&#x27;S LOGO, LOAD RATING, and RECESS IN COVER FOR HARDWARE (X2) at two diagonal corners. A cutaway shows the non-skid surface texture.</p>
<p>Typical cover captive bolt (or similar): A bolt through the cover with callouts 1/2&quot;-13 COARSE THREAD STAINLESS STEEL PENTA HEAD BOLT, STAINLESS STEEL FLAT WASHER, CAST-IN BOLT GRIPPER, and 1/2&quot;-13 UNIFIED NATIONAL COARSE THREAD JAM NUT WITH THREADLOCKER. Cover thickness is labeled TE, edge distance B, and a 1/8&quot; dimension is shown.</p>
<p>Typical threaded insert (or similar): A section through the cover edge showing an INSERT WITH DRAINAGE HOLE and a DRAINAGE HOLE.</p>
<p>Isometric view: An exploded view of the POLYMER CONCRETE COVER (with NON SKID SURFACE) above the POLYMER CONCRETE BOX with OPEN BASE and 3&quot; X 4-1/4&quot; MOUSE HOLE KNOCKOUTS BOTH END (2X).</p>
<p>Nominal dimensions table: Columns are Pull box type, Minimum depth box (D), Minimum depth extension (E), LI Min, WI Min, then Cover columns TE, B, L, W.</p>
<p>Row PB2: D 12&quot;, E 10&quot;, LI Min 1&#x27;-8&quot;, WI Min 11&quot;, TE 2&quot;, B 1-3/4&quot;, L 1&#x27;-11-1/4&quot;, W 1&#x27;-1-3/4&quot;.</p>
<p>Row PB3: D 12&quot;, E 10&quot;, LI Min 2&#x27;-4-1/4&quot;, WI Min 1&#x27;-3-1/4&quot;, TE 2&quot;, B 2&quot;, L 2&#x27;-6-1/2&quot;, W 1&#x27;-5-1/2&quot;.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, COMPOSITE PULL BOX, TYPES PB-2 &amp; PB-3, Drawing No. S-78.5.3, sheet 1 of 1, approved February 28, 2023 by Connie Llanos, Interim General Manager. Drawn by SB 02-22-23, T.E. SB 02-22-23, Sr. T.E. JV 02-22-23. The printed page number is 75.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Refer to Dept. of Public Works Standard Drawing # L-201-1 for additional details and requirements.</p>
<p>2. Galvanized conduits in pull box must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>3. Pull box to be used in non-traffic areas, may be stacked no more than two deep.</p>
<p>4. Stencil TRAFFIC SIGNAL in 1&quot; lettering on backside of cover and inside of box.</p>
<p>Callouts: Top flush with finished grade; 1&quot; crushed rock; see Note 2; TRAFFIC SIGNAL, 7/8&quot; letters; 4&quot; x 1/2&quot; pull slot with 3/16&quot; center pin; manufacturer&#x27;s logo; load rating; recess in cover for hardware (X2); 1/2&quot;-13 coarse thread stainless steel penta head bolt; stainless steel flat washer; cast-in bolt gripper; 1/2&quot;-13 unified national coarse thread jam nut with threadlocker; insert with drainage hole; drainage hole; polymer concrete cover; non skid surface; polymer concrete box; open base; 3&quot; x 4-1/4&quot; mouse hole knockouts both end (2X).</p></details>
