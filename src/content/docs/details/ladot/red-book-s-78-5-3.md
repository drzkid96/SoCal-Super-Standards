<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Refer to Dept. of Public Works Standard Drawing # L-201-1 for additional details and requirements.</p>
<p>2. Galvanized conduits in pull box must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>3. Pull box to be used in non-traffic areas, may be stacked no more than two deep.</p>
<p>4. Stencil TRAFFIC SIGNAL in 1&quot; lettering on backside of cover and inside of box.</p>
<p>Callouts: Top flush with finished grade; 1&quot; crushed rock; see Note 2; TRAFFIC SIGNAL, 7/8&quot; letters; 4&quot; x 1/2&quot; pull slot with 3/16&quot; center pin; manufacturer&#x27;s logo; load rating; recess in cover for hardware (X2); 1/2&quot;-13 coarse thread stainless steel penta head bolt; stainless steel flat washer; cast-in bolt gripper; 1/2&quot;-13 unified national coarse thread jam nut with threadlocker; insert with drainage hole; drainage hole; polymer concrete cover; non skid surface; polymer concrete box; open base; 3&quot; x 4-1/4&quot; mouse hole knockouts both end (2X).</p></details>
