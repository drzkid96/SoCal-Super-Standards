</details><details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>4 - 1/2&quot; holes to be drilled 1 7/8&quot; deep to receive 4 stud bolt anchors. 1/2&quot; x 3&quot; nuts to be steel hex galvanized. 1/2&quot; washers to be flat steel galvanized. Washers may be used under base as shims for leveling. 30&quot; unthreaded post welded onto the flange.</p>
<h5>SPECIFICATION FOR POST</h5>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 30&quot; long, ASTM-A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<p>Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</p>
<p>Detail labels: 3/4&quot; drill, 4 holes. Continuous weld. 1/2&quot; drill. 1/4&quot; hot roll steel plate.</p></details>
