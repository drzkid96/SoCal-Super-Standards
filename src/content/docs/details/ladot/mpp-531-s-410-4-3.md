<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Elevation and flange detail for a 2 inch by 30 inch galvanized steel parking meter post welded to a 6 inch square, 1/4 inch steel base plate. The plate is anchored to a concrete bridge deck with four 1/2 inch stud bolts in holes drilled 1 7/8 inches deep, and set in cement grout 20 inches from the curb face.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Drawing S-410.4.3, Parking Meter Post Installation on Concrete Bridge (City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 12/03/2020 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager, sheet 1 of 1).</p>
<p>Elevation: A 30&quot; long steel post with reamed inside top stands on a post flange base bolted to a concrete sidewalk (bridge deck) next to a curb. The post rises 30&quot; above the sidewalk surface, with a 1/4&quot; hole drilled on one side only 26&quot; below the post top. A 20&quot; dimension runs from the post to the curb face. The flange base is set in cement grout and fastened by 4 stud bolt anchors. Concrete Sidewalk, Top of Curb, Curb Face and Gutter are labeled. Callout: 4 - 1/2&quot; holes to be drilled 1 7/8&quot; deep to receive 4 stud bolt anchors, 1/2&quot; x 3&quot; nuts to be steel hex galvanized, 1/2&quot; washers to be flat steel galvanized. Washers may be used under base as shims for leveling. 30&quot; unthreaded post welded onto the flange.</p>
<p>Detail: Post Flange Base (plan and side view): The base is a 6&quot; square, 1/4&quot; hot roll steel plate with 4 chamfered corners (7/8&quot; dimension at a chamfer) and four 3/4&quot; drill holes, each 1&quot; from two adjacent plate edges. A 1/2&quot; drill hole is at the center, and the post is attached with a continuous weld around its circumference. The side view shows the 1/4&quot; plate with the post welded to it.</p>
<p>Specification for post and grout notes appear at the lower left.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>4 - 1/2&quot; holes to be drilled 1 7/8&quot; deep to receive 4 stud bolt anchors. 1/2&quot; x 3&quot; nuts to be steel hex galvanized. 1/2&quot; washers to be flat steel galvanized. Washers may be used under base as shims for leveling. 30&quot; unthreaded post welded onto the flange.</p>
<h5>SPECIFICATION FOR POST</h5>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 30&quot; long, ASTM-A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<p>Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</p>
<p>Detail labels: 3/4&quot; drill, 4 holes. Continuous weld. 1/2&quot; drill. 1/4&quot; hot roll steel plate.</p></details>
