### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 35)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0035.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0035.webp" alt="S-51.9A sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Red Book S-51.9A, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/red-book/03-pages-33-48/#p35">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation: Mounting of a Type 9 standard, with a foundation section at the base. A short, slightly tapered shaft carries a pedestrian signal head at the top, drawn as a rectangular case with a raised hand symbol on the left half and a walking person symbol on the right half, mounted through a collar fitting on the shaft top. Callouts: Typical Pedestrian Head Mounting and Shaft-Type 9.</p>
<p>Foundation section: the shaft is bolted to a base plate with anchor bolts set in a concrete foundation labeled Foundation, Type F-1, beside a stepped concrete sidewalk and curb. A conduit elbows out of the foundation to the right. A 36&quot; dimension runs from the shaft centerline to the sidewalk edge at the left and a 14&quot; Min. dimension runs vertically from the top surface to the conduit at the right.</p>
<p>Title block: Mounting of Type 9 Standard, sheet 1 of 1, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-51.9A. Drawn MT 3-1-16, T.E. JV 3-3-16, Sr. T.E. MA 3-8-16, Pr. T.E. VJ 4-11-16, approved 4-11-16 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager. Booklet page number 28.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Callouts: Typical Pedestrian Head Mounting; Shaft-Type 9; Foundation, Type F-1. Dimensions: 36&quot;, 14&quot; Min. No numbered notes appear on the sheet.</p></details>
