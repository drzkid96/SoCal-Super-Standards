<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Details the 12 inch by 18 inch No Parking Unhitched Trailers sign citing L.A.M.C. 80.69.1(C), with a 9 inch outside diameter circle and slash, a 6 inch Series E P, and Highway Gothic Series C legend. The background is white with a black P and red circle, slash, text and border. Signs are .080 aluminum with reflective SEG Type II material and graffiti film.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be fabricated by a qualified, professional sign manufacturer approved by LADOT.</p>
<p>2. Sign size is 12&quot;x18&quot;, and legend lettering size shall be as noted above. The font is Highway Gothic Series C type. All lines of legend are to be centered. The border shall be 1/4&quot; wide and spaced 1/4&quot; from edge of sign.</p>
<p>3. All signs shall include (reading left to right) at the bottom of the sign the following information: a) if fabricated by a private contractor, a manufacturer &quot;code&quot; and date of fabrication e.g. &quot;SM 09/05&quot;, b) &quot;Property of City of Los Angeles&quot;, c) LADOT Stock Number (four numbers).</p>
<p>4. The background color is white. The large &quot;P&quot; is black. The circle and slash, all text and border are red.</p>
<p>5. Sign shall be made of .080 aluminum and have reflective SEG Type II material with graffiti film.</p>
<p>Sign labels: 12&quot; by 18&quot;, 9&quot; outside diameter, 1&quot; slash, 6&quot; Series E P, 1 3/4&quot; Series C (UNHITCHED TRAILERS), 1/2&quot; Series C (L.A.M.C. 80.69.1(C)).</p></details>
