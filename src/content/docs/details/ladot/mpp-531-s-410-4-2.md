<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Detail for setting a parking meter post in an existing concrete sidewalk. A 3 1/4 inch diameter hole is cut in the sidewalk, dirt is cleaned out from under the sidewalk, and the post is held upright with wooden wedges and set with cement grout. The post is 2 inch standard weight galvanized steel pipe, 42 inches long, with a reamed top, and the grout mix is 1 part cement to 2 1/2 parts sand.</p>

</details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Parking meter post installation, concrete setting</p>
<p>Detail labels: reamed inside of top. Finish surface. Wooden wedges to hold post upright. Post flange set on 1/4&quot; dia. hole in cement grout, one side only. 20&quot; dimension. Cut 3 1/4&quot; dia. hole in sidewalk. Clean out dirt under sidewalk approx. as shown. Curb face, gutter.</p>
<p>Specification for post</p>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 42&quot; long, ASTM A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<p>Notes</p>
<p>1. Post to be level, straight.</p>
<p>2. Area to be left clean.</p>
<p>3. Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</p></details>
