<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Elevation of a 2 inch by 42 inch galvanized steel parking meter post set in a 3 1/4 inch diameter hole cut in a concrete sidewalk, grouted with 1:2 1/2 cement grout and held plumb with wooden wedges. The post stands 30 inches above the sidewalk and 20 inches back from the curb face, with 12 inches below the surface.</p>

</details><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Drawing S-410.4.2, Parking Meter Post Installation, Concrete Setting (City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, approved 12/03/2020 by Seleta J. Reynolds, General Manager, sheet 1 of 1).</p>
<p>Elevation: A vertical steel post, top reamed on the inside (Reamed Inside of Top), is set through a hole cut in a concrete sidewalk next to a curb. The post is 42&quot; overall, with 30&quot; above the sidewalk surface and 12&quot; below it. A 1/4&quot; diameter hole is drilled on one side only 26&quot; below the post top. A 20&quot; dimension runs from the post to the curb face. The sidewalk is labeled Concrete Sidewalk, with Finish Surface at its top, and the curb face and gutter at right. The post is placed in a 3 1/4&quot; diameter hole cut in the sidewalk, filled with cement grout. The post flange is set in cement grout with a shaped grout cap at the surface, and wooden wedges 1&quot; x 3/4&quot; x 2&quot; +/- long hold the post upright. Below the sidewalk the dirt is cleaned out (Clean out dirt under sidewalk approx. as shown) so the grout forms a flared plug wider than the hole. A 1&quot; dimension is shown below the post bottom, and the 3 1/4&quot; Dia. dimension is shown at the bottom of the grout.</p>
<p>Specification for post and notes appear at the lower left.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>SPECIFICATION FOR POST</h5>
<p>Steel pipe, standard weight, 2&quot; x 42&quot; long, ASTM-A120-63T, new and unused, hot dipped galvanized, top reamed.</p>
<h5>NOTES</h5>
<p>1. Post to be level straight.</p>
<p>2. Area to be left clean.</p>
<p>3. Cement grout - 1 (cement) : 2 1/2 (sand).</p>
<p>Callouts: Wooden wedges 1&quot; x 3/4&quot; x 2&quot; +/- long to hold post upright. Post flange set in cement grout. Cut 3 1/4&quot; dia. hole in sidewalk. Cement grout. Clean out dirt under sidewalk approx. as shown.</p></details>
