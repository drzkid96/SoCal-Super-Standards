<p>Authority: Installation of these signs is subject to completion of an &quot;Application for Installation of Official Neighborhood Watch Sign&quot; and receipt of Los Angeles Department of Transportation (LADOT) approval. The name of elected officials, donating parties, sponsoring parties, neighborhood groups or neighborhood councils, etc. shall not be shown on the sign.</p>
<p>Sign: Size shall be no greater than 24 inches vertically and 18 inches horizontally. Appearance should be similar to one of the two samples on the right. Colors should be reflectorized. Trademarked logos shall not be used without permission from the owner. Anti-graffiti film should be used. Material shall be 0.08 inch minimum thickness aluminum stock, pre-drilled with two 3/8 inch diameter holes, centered with one near the top of the sign and one near the bottom. Corners of sign shall have a minimum 0.50 inch radius.</p>
<p>Installation: Signs shall be manufactured and installed only by qualified sign companies approved by LADOT. Signs should be installed on non-wooden street lighting poles. They may be installed on street lighting poles with parking restriction signs. However, they may not be installed on traffic signal poles, nor on street lighting poles with other warning, regulatory, or guidance traffic signs facing the same direction. If existing poles or posts are not available, signs may be installed on new posts (see LADOT DWG. No. S-452). They shall be installed at a clearance height greater than 7 feet. LADOT reserves the right to remove Neighborhood Watch Signs not in conformance with these guidelines.</p>
<p>Maintenance: LADOT is not responsible for the maintenance of these signs. If the signs are damaged or stolen, the local group is responsible for sign replacement and reinstallation.</p>
