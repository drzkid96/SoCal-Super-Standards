<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Dimensioned arrow layouts on a 1 ft grid: the Type IX U-turn arrow and the Type FH I through V combination and circular lane-use arrows. Each arrow gives overall length, width, arrowhead size and area in square feet.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Type IX arrow (U-turn arrow): a U shaped arrow with the head pointing down on the left leg. Overall width 7&#x27;-9&quot;, head width 3&#x27;-4&quot;, overall height 10&#x27;-0&quot;, arrowhead 5&#x27;-0&quot; long, right leg stem extends 8&#x27;-6&quot; below the top of the curve, stem width 1&#x27;-0&quot;. Grid is 1&#x27;-0&quot;. A=26 ft2.</p>
<p>Type FH I arrow: a combination arrow with a straight through arrow, a left turn arrowhead and a right turn arrowhead, sharing one stem with an oval void. Overall width 11&#x27;-0&quot;, through arrowhead width 3&#x27;-4&quot;, overall height 19&#x27;-4&quot;, through arrowhead 5&#x27;-0&quot; long, left and right arrowheads each have 3&#x27;-0&quot; and 4&#x27;-0&quot; length dimensions at the tips, stem width 1&#x27;-0&quot;. Grid is 1&#x27;-0&quot;. A=50 ft2.</p>
<p>Type FH II arrow: a through arrow plus a left turn arrowhead. Overall width 7&#x27;-4&quot;, through arrowhead width 3&#x27;-4&quot;, overall height 19&#x27;-4&quot;, through arrowhead 5&#x27;-0&quot; long, left arrowhead 3&#x27;-0&quot; and 4&#x27;-0&quot;, stem width 1&#x27;-0&quot;. Grid is 1&#x27;-0&quot;. A=38 ft2.</p>
<p>Type FH IV arrow: a left turn curved arrow. Overall width 7&#x27;-4&quot;, height 13&#x27;-0&quot;, arrowhead 3&#x27;-0&quot; and 4&#x27;-0&quot;, stem 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide. Grid is 1&#x27;-0&quot;. A=28 ft2.</p>
<p>Type FH III arrow (optional lane use circular arrows): a through arrowhead and a right turn arrowhead sharing a stem with an oval void. Overall width 7&#x27;-6&quot;, through arrowhead width 3&#x27;-4&quot;, height 16&#x27;-8&quot;, through arrowhead 5&#x27;-0&quot; long, right arrowhead 3&#x27;-0&quot; and 4&#x27;-0&quot;, stem width 1&#x27;-0&quot;. Grid is 1&#x27;-0&quot;. A=35 ft2.</p>
<p>Type FH V arrow (mandatory lane use circular arrows): a through arrow whose stem shifts right around an oval void. Overall width 3&#x27;-10&quot;, head width 3&#x27;-4&quot;, height 16&#x27;-8&quot;, arrowhead 5&#x27;-0&quot; long, stem width 1&#x27;-0&quot;. Grid is 1&#x27;-0&quot;. A=23 ft2.</p>
<p>The arrows FH I to FH V are labeled by type: FH III is optional lane use circular arrows and FH V is mandatory lane use circular arrows.</p></details>
