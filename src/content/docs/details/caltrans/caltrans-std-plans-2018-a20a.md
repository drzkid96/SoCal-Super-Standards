<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2018 Standard Plan A20A gives typical dash and gap patterns, marker types and spacing for two-lane centerlines, multilane lanelines and no passing zone lines in one and two directions. Dashes are 6" white or yellow lines in 7'-0" or 12'-0" lengths on 24'-0" or 48'-0" cycles, with Type C, D, G and H retroreflective markers. Details 3, 4, 7, 10, 13, 14, 14A, 17, 20 and 23 are deleted.</p>

<p>Detail 7 is deleted.</p>
<p>Lanelines (multilane highways): <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-8" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-8.svg">Detail 8</a> shows three 7&#x27;-0&quot; white dashes with 17&#x27;-0&quot; gaps. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-9" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-9.svg">Detail 9</a> is a 48&#x27;-0&quot; pattern with 8&#x27;-6&quot; end gaps, two 7&#x27;-0&quot; white dashes, a 17&#x27;-0&quot; gap, and a Type G marker at each end. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-9a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-9a.svg">Detail 9A</a> (see note) is the same pattern with Type C markers at each end. Detail 17 is deleted. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-18" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-18.svg">Detail 18</a> shows a solid yellow line with 12&#x27;-0&quot; yellow dashes on the opposite side separated by 36&#x27;-0&quot;, with 3&quot; and 1 1/2&quot; dimensions. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-19" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-19.svg">Detail 19</a> shows a solid yellow line with 12&#x27;-0&quot; dashes at 18&#x27;-0&quot; gaps with Type D markers and Type H markers at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing, and 3&quot;, 2&quot; and 1 1/2&quot; dimensions. Detail 20 is deleted.</p>
<p>No passing zones, two direction: <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> shows two parallel solid 6&quot; yellow lines on either side of the LOL, with 3&quot; and 1 1/2&quot; dimensions. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-22" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-22.svg">Detail 22</a> shows the same two solid lines with Type D markers at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing along each outer side and a 2&quot; marker offset. Detail 23 is deleted.</p>
<p>Legend: Markers: Type C red-clear retroreflective, Type D two-way yellow retroreflective, Type G one-way clear retroreflective, Type H one-way yellow retroreflective. Lines: 6&quot; white, 6&quot; yellow.</p>
<p>Marker details: Type C and Type D (two-way) and Type G and Type H (one-way) are drawn in side and plan view. Each is 3 7/8&quot; to 4 1/8&quot; wide and 3/8&quot; to 3/4&quot; high, and 1 7/8&quot; to 4 1/8&quot; long in plan. The retroreflective face is hatched.</p>
