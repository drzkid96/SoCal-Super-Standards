<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans typical layouts for centerlines on 2-lane highways and lane lines on multilane highways, with the placement of retroreflective pavement markers and painted line patterns. It also covers no-passing zone layouts for one and two directions. Line and gap dimensions are shown on the details, for example 7'-0" and 17'-0" lane line patterns and 48'-0" or 36'-0" cycles. Several detail numbers are marked deleted.</p>
