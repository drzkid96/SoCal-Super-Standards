<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan layouts of off-street accessible parking: a single stall, a double stall and diagonal double stalls, each with a 5'-0" Min or 8'-0" Min van access aisle, blue bordered aisle with white diagonals, and ISA markings. Table A gives required accessible stall counts, and the sheet shows the ISA marking and the R99, R99C, R99B, R7-8b and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r100bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r100bca.webp">R100B(CA)</a> signs.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Single parking stall: a regular accessible stall with a 9&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide stall beside an access aisle that is 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide for a regular accessible stall or 8&#x27;-0&quot; Min for a van accessible stall (see Notes 2, 11, and 12). The stall is 18&#x27;-0&quot; Min long (Typ) and bounded by 4&quot; white lines. The aisle is bordered by 4&quot; blue line borders with 4&quot; white line diagonals at 3&#x27;-0&quot; Max centers (see Note 8). A curb or bumper (see Note 3) is shown at each stall at the rear, and a 2&#x27;-0&quot; Min unobstructed area is kept at the sidewalk end. An ISA marking is at the rear limit of the stall (see Detail A). A curb ramp (see Note 7) with retaining curb if necessary connects the aisle to the sidewalk, and ISA parking signs (see Notes 2 and 13) stand at the sidewalk. The words NO PARKING (see Note 9) are at the bottom of the aisle. A regular non-accessible parking stall is shown beside the aisle.</p>
<p>Double parking stall: two 9&#x27;-0&quot; Min (Typ) wide stalls on each side of a shared access aisle, with 18&#x27;-0&quot; Min (Typ) stall length. The aisle has 4&quot; blue borders and 4&quot; white diagonals at 3&#x27;-0&quot; Max centers (see Note 8), a 2&#x27;-0&quot; Min unobstructed area, bumpers (see Note 3), ISA markings at the rear limit of each stall, and NO PARKING in the aisle (see Note 9). The aisle is 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min between regular accessible parking stalls and 8&#x27;-0&quot; Min to the right of each van accessible parking stall (see Notes 2 and 12). A curb ramp (see Note 7) and ISA parking signs (see Note 13, and Notes 2 and 13) are at the sidewalk.</p>
<p>Diagonal double parking stalls: two angled stalls, 18&#x27;-0&quot; Min (Typ) long and 9&#x27;-0&quot; Min (Typ) wide, either side of a shared angled access aisle with 4&quot; blue line borders and 4&quot; white line diagonals at 3&#x27;-0&quot; Max centers (Typ, see Note 8). Each stall has a 4&quot; white line, an ISA marking at the rear limit (Typ, see Detail A), a bumper (see Note 3, Typ) with 2&#x27;-0&quot; unobstructed dimension, and NO PARKING in the aisle (see Note 9). The aisle is 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min between regular accessible parking stalls and 8&#x27;-0&quot; Min to the right of each van accessible parking stall (see Notes 2 and 12). A curb ramp (see Note 7), retaining curb if necessary, and ISA parking signs (see Notes 2 and 13) are at the curb.</p>
<p>Detail A, ISA marking: a square blue background with a white border and a white International Symbol of Accessibility. See Standard Plan A24C for the ISA marking.</p>
<p>Table A: minimum number of required accessible parking spaces by total number of parking spaces provided in the parking facility. 1 to 25 spaces: 1. 26 to 50: 2. 51 to 75: 3. 76 to 100: 4. 101 to 150: 5. 151 to 200: 6. 201 to 300: 7. 301 to 400: 8. 401 to 500: 9. 501 to 1000: 2 percent of total. 1001 and over: 20 plus 1 for each 100 or fraction thereof over 1000.</p>
<p>Off-street parking signs (parking lot or garage, see Note 6): Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a>, a vertical sign with the ISA and the words PARKING ONLY. Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a>, the same sign with a MINIMUM FINE $250 panel below (see Note 6). Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>, reading MINIMUM FINE $250, used with Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a> (see Note 6). Sign R7-8b reading VAN ACCESSIBLE (see Notes 2 and 6). Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r100bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r100bca.webp">R100B(CA)</a>, a rectangular sign reading: UNAUTHORIZED VEHICLES PARKED IN DESIGNATED ACCESSIBLE SPACES NOT DISPLAYING DISTINGUISHING PLACARDS OR SPECIAL LICENSE PLATES ISSUED FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES WILL BE TOWED AWAY AT THE OWNER&#x27;S EXPENSE. TOWED VEHICLES MAY BE RECLAIMED AT (Insert Address) OR BY TELEPHONING (Insert Telephone Number) (see Note 10).</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Accessible parking spaces serving a particular building shall be located on the shortest accessible route of travel from adjacent parking to an accessible entrance. In parking facilities that do not serve a particular building, accessible parking shall be located on the shortest accessible route of travel to an accessible pedestrian entrance of the parking facility.</p>
<p>2. One in every six accessible off-street parking stalls, but not less than one, shall be served by an accessible aisle of 8&#x27;-0&quot; minimum width and shall be signed van accessible. The R7-8b sign shall be mounted below the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a> plaque or the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> sign.</p>
<p>3. In each parking stall, a curb or parking bumper shall be provided if required to prevent encroachment of vehicles over the required width of walkways. Parking stalls shall be so located that persons with disabilities are not compelled to wheel or walk behind parked vehicles other than their own. For more parking bumper requirements, see the Standard Specifications.</p>
<p>4. Parking spaces and access aisles shall be level with surface slopes not exceeding 1.5% in all directions.</p>
<p>5. Table A shall be used to determine the required number of accessible parking stalls in each parking lot or garage.</p>
<p>6. Where Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>, Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> or Sign R7-8b are installed, the bottom of the sign or plaque panel shall be a minimum of 7&#x27;-0&quot; above the surrounding surface.</p>
<p>7. Curb ramps shall conform to the details shown on Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>8. Blue paint, instead of white may be used for marking accessibility aisles in areas where snow may cause white markings to not be visible.</p>
<p>9. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;, shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials. See Standard Plan A90B for details of the &quot;NO PARKING&quot; pavement marking.</p>
<p>10. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r100bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r100bca.webp">R100B(CA)</a> sign shall be posted in a conspicuous place at each entrance to off-street parking facilities or immediately adjacent to and visible from each stall. The sign shall include the address where the towed vehicle may be reclaimed and the telephone number of the local traffic law enforcement agency.</p>
<p>11. Where a single (non-van) accessible parking space is provided, the loading and unloading access aisle shall be on the passenger side of the vehicle as the vehicle is going forward into the parking space.</p>
<p>12. Where a van accessible parking space is provided, the loading and unloading access aisle shall be 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide minimum, and shall be on the passenger side of the vehicle as the vehicle is going forward into the parking space.</p>
<p>13. Accessible Parking Only Sign shall be Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> or Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a> with Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>.</p>
<p>Legend: ISA - International Symbol of Accessibility.</p></details>
