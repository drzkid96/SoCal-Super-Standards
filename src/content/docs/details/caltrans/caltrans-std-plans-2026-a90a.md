<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Accessible parking spaces serving a particular building shall be located on the shortest accessible route of travel from adjacent parking to an accessible entrance. In parking facilities that do not serve a particular building, accessible parking shall be located on the shortest accessible route of travel to an accessible pedestrian entrance of the parking facility.</li>
<li value="2">One in every six accessible off-street parking stalls, but not less than one, shall be served by an accessible aisle of 8&#x27;-0&quot; minimum width and shall be signed van accessible. The R7-8b sign shall be mounted below the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a> plaque or the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> sign.</li>
<li value="3">In each parking stall, a curb or parking bumper shall be provided if required to prevent encroachment of vehicles over the required width of walkways. Parking stalls shall be so located that persons with disabilities are not compelled to wheel or walk behind parked vehicles other than their own. For more parking bumper requirements, see the Standard Specifications.</li>
<li value="4">Parking spaces and access aisles shall be level with surface slopes not exceeding 1.5% in all directions.</li>
<li value="5">Table A shall be used to determine the required number of accessible parking stalls in each parking lot or garage.</li>
<li value="6">Where Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>, Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> or Sign R7-8b are installed, the bottom of the sign or plaque panel shall be a minimum of 7&#x27;-0&quot; above the surrounding surface.</li>
<li value="7">Curb ramps shall conform to the details shown on Standard Plan <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/caltrans/caltrans-std-plans-2026-a88a/">A88A</a>.</li>
<li value="8">Blue paint, instead of white may be used for marking accessibility aisles in areas where snow may cause white markings to not be visible.</li>
<li value="9">The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;, shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials. See Standard Plan <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/caltrans/caltrans-std-plans-2026-a90b/">A90B</a> for details of the &quot;NO PARKING&quot; pavement marking.</li>
<li value="10">A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r100bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r100bca.webp">R100B(CA)</a> sign shall be posted in a conspicuous place at each entrance to off-street parking facilities or immediately adjacent to and visible from each stall. The sign shall include the address where the towed vehicle may be reclaimed and the telephone number of the local traffic law enforcement agency.</li>
<li value="11">Where a single (non-van) accessible parking space is provided, the loading and unloading access aisle shall be on the passenger side of the vehicle as the vehicle is going forward into the parking space.</li>
<li value="12">Where a van accessible parking space is provided, the loading and unloading access aisle shall be 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide minimum, and shall be on the passenger side of the vehicle as the vehicle is going forward into the parking space.</li>
<li value="13">Accessible Parking Only Sign shall be Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> or Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a> with Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>.</li>
</ol>
<p>Legend: ISA - International Symbol of Accessibility.</p>
