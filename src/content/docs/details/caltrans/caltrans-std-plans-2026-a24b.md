<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 22 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Each marking is drawn on a 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid with overall dimensions and area A in square feet. Dashed lines inside the turn arrows show where the curved shafts merge into the stem.</p>
<p>Railroad crossing symbol: An X with two R letters, centered on the lane. The X is 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 20&#x27;-0&quot; tall, with legs 1&#x27;-4&quot; wide. Each R is 6&#x27;-0&quot; tall and sits to the left and right of the X crossing point. A transverse line 2&#x27;-0&quot; wide by the width of the lane is placed above and below the symbol, each 14&#x27;-0&quot; from the X (14&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions with break symbols). Area A=70 sq ft. Footnote: 70 SQFT does not include the 2&#x27;-0&quot; x variable width transverse lines.</p>
<p>Type II (B) arrow: Straight-through arrow with a left and a right turn branch, 10&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 24&#x27;-0&quot; long, 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide stem, A=59 sq ft.</p>
<p>Type II (L) arrow: Straight-through arrow with a left turn branch, 7&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 24&#x27;-0&quot; long, 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide stem, A=45 sq ft. For Type II (R) use mirror image.</p>
<p>Type III (B) arrow: Left and right turn arrow (no through head) with both curved branches, 11&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 24&#x27;-0&quot; long, 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide stem, A=73 sq ft.</p>
<p>Type III (L) arrow: Left turn arrow with a curved shaft, 6&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 24&#x27;-0&quot; long, 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide stem, A=42 sq ft. For Type III (R) use mirror image.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>* 70 SQFT does not include the 2&#x27;-0&quot; x variable width transverse lines.</p>
<p>For Type II (R) use mirror image. For Type III (R) use mirror image. Return to Table of Contents link at the bottom of the page.</p></details>
