<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. L=WS, where W is the offset in feet and S is bicycle approach speed in mph.</p>
<p>2. Provide an additional foot of offset for a raised obstruction and use the formula L=(W+1)S for the taper length.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Plan A20D for <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39.svg">Detail 39</a>.</p>
<p>4. Use markings only when there is 5&#x27;-0&quot; of clear space.</p></details>
