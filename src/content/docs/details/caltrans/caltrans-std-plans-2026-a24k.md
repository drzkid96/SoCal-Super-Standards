<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan views of the flying wedge stripe, a tapered shoulder stripe that guides cyclists around a rumble strip or an obstruction such as a pier, abutment or grate. The taper length is L=WS (W offset in feet, S bicycle approach speed in mph), or L=(W+1)S for a raised obstruction. Use only with 5'-0" of clear space.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Flying wedge stripe, placed at beginning of rumble strip at right shoulder only: a plan of the shoulder between the edge of traveled way (ETW) and the edge of shoulder (ES), with an edgeline stripe along the ETW. The rumble strip appears as a row of small rectangular elements at the upstream end next to the ETW. The flying wedge stripe is a long thin black wedge drawn from the rumble strip start, offset W from the ETW (W marked at the start of the wedge), tapering back to the edgeline over a taper length L (see Note 1). <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39.svg">Detail 39</a> is referenced (see Note 3). The direction of bicycle travel arrow points toward the wedge start end. Break lines indicate the layout continues.</p>
<p>Flying wedge stripe, placed at obstruction: a plan of the shoulder between ETW and ES with an obstruction (pier, abutment, grate, or other obstruction that is inappropriate for bicycle travel) shown as a rectangle against the ES line, offset width W. The flying wedge stripe starts beside the obstruction at offset W and tapers to the edgeline stripe over length L (see Notes 1 and 2). See Note 3 for the stripe detail. The direction of bicycle travel arrow is shown, with break lines at the end.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. L=WS, where W is the offset in feet and S is bicycle approach speed in mph.</p>
<p>2. Provide an additional foot of offset for a raised obstruction and use the formula L=(W+1)S for the taper length.</p>
<p>3. See Standard Plan A20D for <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39.svg">Detail 39</a>.</p>
<p>4. Use markings only when there is 5&#x27;-0&quot; of clear space.</p></details>
