<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A24A gives dimensioned grid drawings of nine pavement arrows: Type I in 10, 18 and 24 ft lengths, the bike lane arrow, and Types IV (L), V, VI, VII (L) and VIII. Each is drawn on a grid with its overall width and length and its area in square feet.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 21 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Each arrow is drawn on a grid (1&#x27;-0&quot; grid for road arrows and 6 in. grid for the bike lane arrow) with overall dimensions and the area A in square feet shown under the arrow name.</p>
<p>Type I 10&#x27;-0&quot; arrow: 3&#x27;-6&quot; wide by 10&#x27;-0&quot; long, shaft 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide (1&#x27;-0&quot; dimension at the base), 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=14 sq ft.</p>
<p>Type I 18&#x27;-0&quot; arrow: 3&#x27;-6&quot; wide by 18&#x27;-0&quot; long, shaft 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide, 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=25 sq ft.</p>
<p>Type I 24&#x27;-0&quot; arrow: 3&#x27;-6&quot; wide by 24&#x27;-0&quot; long, shaft 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide, 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=31 sq ft.</p>
<p>Bike lane arrow: 1&#x27;-9&quot; wide by 5&#x27;-0&quot; long, shaft 6 in. wide, 6 in. grid, A=3.5 sq ft.</p>
<p>Type IV (L) arrow: left turn arrow with a curved shaft turning left, 6&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 8&#x27;-0&quot; long, shaft 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide, 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=15 sq ft. For a Type IV (R) arrow, use mirror image.</p>
<p>Type VI arrow: right lane drop arrow, 5&#x27;-6&quot; wide by 18&#x27;-0&quot; long (arrowhead length 8&#x27;-6&quot;, with a 2&#x27;-0&quot; dimension at the barb), shaft slanted at 20 degrees to the edge of pavement (labeled EDGE OF PAVEMENT and DIRECTION OF VIEW), 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=42 sq ft. For a left lane, use mirror image.</p>
<p>Type V arrow: 24&#x27;-0&quot; overall length with a 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide stem of 17&#x27;-6&quot; length, and two diverging 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide legs each 9&#x27;-0&quot; long forming a V, with a 2&#x27;-0&quot; dimension across the stem and legs, A=33 sq ft.</p>
<p>Type VII (L) arrow: combined through and left turn arrow, 7&#x27;-3&quot; wide by 13&#x27;-0&quot; long, shaft 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide, 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=27 sq ft. For a Type VII (R) arrow, use mirror image.</p>
<p>Type VIII arrow: combined left, through and right arrow, 9&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 15&#x27;-0&quot; long, stem 1&#x27;-0&quot; wide with a dashed center line, 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=36 sq ft.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>Arrow notes on the sheet: For Type IV (R) arrow, use mirror image. For Type VI arrow (right lane drop arrow), for left lane use mirror image. For Type VII (R) arrow, use mirror image. A = area in square feet. Return to Table of Contents link at the bottom of the page.</p></details>
