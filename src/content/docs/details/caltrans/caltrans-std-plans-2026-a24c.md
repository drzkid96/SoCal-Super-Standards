<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 23 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Each symbol is drawn on a labeled grid with overall dimensions and area A in square feet.</p>
<p>Bike lane symbol without person: Bicycle only, 3&#x27;-4&quot; wide by 6&#x27;-0&quot; tall, 4 in. grid, A=7 sq ft.</p>
<p>Bike lane symbol with person: Bicycle with a rider and helmet, 3&#x27;-4&quot; wide by 6&#x27;-6&quot; tall, 4 in. grid, A=7 sq ft.</p>
<p>International Symbol of Accessibility (ISA) marking: A blue square 4&#x27;-5&quot; by 4&#x27;-5&quot; containing a white outline square 3&#x27;-9&quot; by 3&#x27;-9&quot; and the white wheelchair symbol 2&#x27;-8 1/2&quot; wide by 3&#x27;-0&quot; tall, 6 in. grid. A=9 sq ft (white) and A=14 sq ft (blue). Labels show the white symbol and border and the blue field.</p>
<p>Diamond symbol: A diamond outline 3&#x27;-3&quot; wide by 12&#x27;-0&quot; long with a 6 in. wide band, 1&#x27;-0&quot; grid, A=11 sq ft.</p>
<p>Numerals: Numeral 25 and numeral 35, each 4&#x27;-8&quot; wide by 8&#x27;-0&quot; tall on a 4 in. grid (A=17.5 sq ft for 25, A=16.5 sq ft for 35). Numeral 40, 5&#x27;-0&quot; wide by 8&#x27;-0&quot; tall, 4 in. grid (A=19.5 sq ft). A 1 in. dimension is shown at the left of the numeral 4.</p>
<p>Bicycle loop detector symbol: A bicycle with rider centered between two rectangular bars, 10 in. wide overall with the bars 2 in. wide (2 in. dimension), 1 in. grid. Vertical dimensions top to bottom: 1&#x27;-8&quot; bar, 3&quot;, 1&#x27;-8&quot; bicycle zone, 3&quot;, 1&#x27;-8&quot; bar, with 5&#x27;-6&quot; overall. The bars have zigzag break symbols. A=2 sq ft.</p>
<p>Shared roadway bicycle marking: Three chevrons (3&#x27;-3&quot; wide, 6 in. spacing between bands, 2&#x27;-0&quot; vertical dimension for the chevron group) above a bicycle without rider. The chevron group is 3&#x27;-6&quot; tall, the bicycle 5&#x27;-9&quot; tall, overall height 9&#x27;-3&quot;, with a 1 in. dimension at the base. 6 in. grid, A=11.5 sq ft.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>Symbol labels and colors: ISA marking shown in white and blue. Return to Table of Contents link at the bottom of the page.</p></details>
