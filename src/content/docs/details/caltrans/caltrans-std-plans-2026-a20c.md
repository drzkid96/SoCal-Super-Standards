<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20C shows ramp neutral area and gore striping for exit ramps, entrance ramp merges and acceleration lanes (Details 36, 36A, 36B), 8 in. white lane drop patterns at exit ramps and intersections (Details 37 and 37B), and 45 degree chevron markings for exit and entrance gores. Details 37A and 37C are deleted.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 3)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 16 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Plan views of ramp gores read left to right with arrows showing the direction of travel. Marker symbols: Type C red-clear (square with cross lines), Type G one-way clear (half black) and Type RY red-yellow (hatched square).</p>
<p>Exit ramp neutral area (gore) treatment, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-36" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-110-ca-detail-36.webp">Detail 36</a>: Mainline edge of traveled way (top) is a broken white line with Type C markers every 24&#x27;-0&quot; and a 2 in. offset. Below it, a 12 in. white line runs along the mainline side of the gore, 1&#x27;-0&quot; below the edge line, to the theoretical gore point, then a 12 in. white line diverges along the ramp edge. The ramp edge of traveled way carries a broken line with Type C markers every 24&#x27;-0&quot; and a 2 in. offset. Past a break symbol the 6 in. white line reaches the physical gore (see <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a>, Standard Plan A20B) and a 6 in. yellow line runs along the ramp side of the physical gore (see <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25a.svg">Detail 25A</a>, Standard Plan A20B). Chevron marking is called out (see chevron marking detail).</p>
<p>Entrance ramp neutral area (merge) treatment, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-36a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-110-ca-detail-36a.webp">Detail 36A</a>: Mainline edge line with a 6 in. white line (see <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a>, Standard Plan A20B) at the physical gore, a 12 in. white line along the mainline and ramp sides of the gore, 6&#x27;-0&quot; dimension between the ramp edge and mainline edge at the merge, 24&#x27;-0&quot; marker spacing and 2 in. offset on the ramp edge with Type C markers, and a 6 in. yellow line at the physical gore (see <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25a.svg">Detail 25A</a>, Standard Plan A20B). Downstream segments of the ramp and mainline edge lines use <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-8" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-8.svg">Detail 8</a> or 9A, Standard Plan A20A.</p>
<p>Entrance ramp neutral area (acceleration lane) treatment, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-36b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-110-ca-detail-36b.webp">Detail 36B</a>: Similar to <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-36a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-110-ca-detail-36a.webp">Detail 36A</a> with the 12 in. white line continuing along the acceleration lane to the theoretical gore point, a 6 in. white line (<a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a>, Standard Plan A20B), a 6 in. yellow line at the physical gore (<a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25a.svg">Detail 25A</a>, Standard Plan A20B), Type C markers at 24&#x27;-0&quot; with a 2 in. offset on the ramp edge, and mainline edge of traveled way as a broken line.</p>
<p>Lane drop at exit ramps, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-37" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-111-ca-detail-37.webp">Detail 37</a>: Broken 8 in. white line with Type C markers placed every 30&#x27;-0&quot; (two 30&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions shown). Dimensions locate each marker and segment: 6&#x27;-0&quot;, 12&#x27;-0&quot;, 6&#x27;-0&quot; and 3&#x27;-0&quot; in each cycle. At the right end, a 300&#x27;-0&quot; dimension (note *) leads to the exit ramp where the pattern joins <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-36" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-110-ca-detail-36.webp">Detail 36</a> and the solid channelizing line of <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38b.svg">Detail 38B</a>, Standard Plan A20D, with 3&#x27;-0&quot; and 6&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions at the start of the line.</p>
<p>Detail 37A: Deleted.</p>
<p>Lane drop at intersections, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-37b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-111-ca-detail-37b.webp">Detail 37B</a>: Same 8 in. white broken line with Type G markers every 30&#x27;-0&quot;, 6&#x27;-0&quot;, 12&#x27;-0&quot;, 6&#x27;-0&quot; and 3&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions, ending in the solid 8 in. white channelizing line (<a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38b.svg">Detail 38B</a>, Standard Plan A20D) at the intersection. Direction of through traffic is shown.</p>
<p>Detail 37C: Deleted.</p>
<p>Chevron pavement markings at exit ramp gore area: 12 in. white chevron lines at 45 degrees to the gore edge lines, 18 in. between chevron legs, chevrons 50&#x27;-0&quot; apart, first chevron at 6&#x27;-0&quot; dimension from the mainline side. The gore is bounded by 12 in. white lines on the ramp side (see <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-36" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/ca-3a-110-ca-detail-36.webp">Detail 36</a>).</p>
<p>Chevron pavement markings at entrance ramp gore area: The same 45 degree chevrons, 18 in. leg spacing and 50&#x27;-0&quot; spacing, bounded by 12 in. white lines (see Details 36A or 36B).</p>
<p>Marker details: Type C and Type RY marker plan about 3 7/8 in. to 4 1/2 in. wide and 1 7/8 in. to 4 1/8 in. tall, side profile 3/8 in. to 3/4 in. high, retroreflective face on both ends. Type G marker same dimensions with a black body on one end.</p>
<p>Legend: Type RY red-yellow retroreflective marker, Type G one-way clear retroreflective marker, Type C red-clear retroreflective marker.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Install a minimum of 1 chevron in the gore area. If at least 1 chevron will not fit into the gore area, do not install chevrons.</p>
<p>2. Terminate chevron markings at physical gore.</p>
<p>3. Gore area chevron pavement markings shown. For exit and entrance ramp channelizing lines details, see Details 36, 36A, and 36B.</p>
<p>* The solid channelizing line shown may be omitted on short auxiliary lanes where weaving length is critical.</p>
<h5>Legend:</h5>
<p>Type RY red-yellow retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Type G one-way clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Type C red-clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Details 37A and 37C are marked deleted. Return to Table of Contents link at the bottom of the page.</p></details>
