<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. Install a minimum of 1 chevron in the gore area. If at least 1 chevron will not fit into the gore area, do not install chevrons.</p>
<p>2. Terminate chevron markings at physical gore.</p>
<p>3. Gore area chevron pavement markings shown. For exit and entrance ramp channelizing lines details, see Details 36, 36A, and 36B.</p>
<p>* The solid channelizing line shown may be omitted on short auxiliary lanes where weaving length is critical.</p>
<h5>Legend:</h5>
<p>Type RY red-yellow retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Type G one-way clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Type C red-clear retroreflective marker.</p>
