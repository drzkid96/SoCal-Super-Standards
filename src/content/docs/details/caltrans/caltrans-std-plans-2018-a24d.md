<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2018 Standard Plan A24D gives the letter shapes and areas for 18 pavement words such as STOP, SCHOOL, BIKE LANE, XING and RUNAWAY VEHICLES. Letters are drawn on 4" by 8" grids (2" by 4" for COMPACT, BIKE and LANE). Notes require words to read UP and set word spacing at 4 to 10 times the character height.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet 20 of the 2018 Caltrans Standard Plans, A24D, Pavement Markings, Words. Each word is drawn as tall condensed letters on a grid, labeled with its area A in square feet. For most words the grid is 4&quot; wide by 8&quot; tall. For COMPACT, BIKE and LANE the grid is 2&quot; wide by 4&quot; tall.</p>
<p>The words drawn, with areas: RUNAWAY A=43 ft2, VEHICLES A=42 ft2, YIELD A=24 ft2, SLOW A=23 ft2, WAIT A=19 ft2, SCHOOL A=35 ft2, AHEAD A=31 ft2, TURN A=24 ft2, HERE A=26 ft2, XING A=21 ft2, SIGNAL A=32 ft2, LEFT A=19 ft2, RIGHT A=26 ft2, STOP A=22 ft2, PED A=18 ft2, COMPACT A=10 ft2, BIKE A=5 ft2 and LANE A=6 ft2.</p>

<p>Title block: State of California, Department of Transportation, Pavement Markings Words, no scale, A24D. Plans approval date May 31, 2018, registered civil engineer Atifa Ferouz, C80402. Date at foot 1-26-18.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. If a message consists of more than one word, it must read &quot;UP&quot;, i.e., the first word must be nearest the driver.</p>
<p>2. The space between words must be at least four times the height of the characters for low speed roads, but not more than ten times the height of the characters. The space may be reduced appropriately where there is limited space because of local conditions.</p>
<p>3. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>4. Portions of a letter, number or symbol may be separated by connecting segments not to exceed 2&quot; in width.</p>
