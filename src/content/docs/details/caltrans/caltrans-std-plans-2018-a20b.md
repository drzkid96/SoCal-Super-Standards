<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2018 Standard Plan A20B gives typical patterns for left and right edgelines, right edgeline extensions through intersections, median island lines, two-way left turn lanes and intersection treatments. It also gives the Type D, H and RY marker dimensions. Details 27A, 30 and 33 are deleted.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sheet 13 of the 2018 Caltrans Standard Plans, A20B, Pavement Markers and Traffic Lines, Typical Details. Details are drawn in plan with direction of travel arrows. LOL is the line of the lane or center line.</p>
<p>Left edgelines (divided highways): <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-24" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-24.svg">Detail 24</a> is a 6&quot; yellow line 2&quot; from the edge of traveled way. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25.svg">Detail 25</a> adds Type RY markers on the edge line side at 48&#x27;-0&quot; spacing with 2&quot; offset. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25a.svg">Detail 25A</a> is the same with Type RY markers at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-26" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-26.svg">Detail 26</a> shows only the edge of traveled way with Type RY markers at 48&#x27;-0&quot; spacing and a 2&quot; offset. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27.svg">Detail 27</a> shows two yellow lines, 3&quot; and 2&quot; dimensions and Type RY markers at 48&#x27;-0&quot; spacing.</p>
<p>Right edgelines: Detail 27A is deleted. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a> is a 6&quot; white line 2&quot; from the edge of traveled way.</p>
<p>Right edgeline extension through intersections: <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27c" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27c.svg">Detail 27C</a> shows 6&quot; white dashes 12&#x27;-0&quot; long, spaced by 3&#x27;-0&quot; gaps, with 3&#x27;-0&quot; end gaps.</p>
<p>Median islands: <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-28" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-28.svg">Detail 28</a> shows two pairs of yellow lines on either side of the LOL with 3&quot; spacing within each pair and 2&#x27;-0&quot; minimum between the pairs. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-29" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-29.svg">Detail 29</a> shows the same with Type D markers at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing and 2&quot; offset.</p>
<p>Detail 30 is deleted.</p>
<p>Two-way left turn lanes: <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-31" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-31.svg">Detail 31</a> shows an outer solid yellow line on each side with a 12&#x27;-0&quot; typical turn lane width, and a center pair of 12&#x27;-0&quot; yellow dashes with 36&#x27;-0&quot; gaps, with 3&quot; and 1 1/2&quot; dimensions. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-32" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-32.svg">Detail 32</a> is a 96&#x27;-0&quot; pattern with 24&#x27;-0&quot; marker spacing (Type D), 18&#x27;-0&quot; and 12&#x27;-0&quot; dashes, 36&#x27;-0&quot; gaps and 12&#x27;-0&quot; typical lane width, with 2&quot; marker offset. Detail 33 is deleted.</p>
<p>Intersection treatments: Details 34, 34A, 35 and 35A are drawn on both sides of an intersecting street centerline. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-34" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-34.svg">Detail 34</a> shows 100&#x27;-0&quot; minimum of solid yellow line with 24&#x27;-0&quot; marker spacing, 12&#x27;-0&quot; and 18&#x27;-0&quot; dashes. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-34a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-34a.svg">Detail 34A</a> shows 100&#x27;-0&quot; minimum of solid yellow line and 12&#x27;-0&quot; dashes with 36&#x27;-0&quot; gaps. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-35" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-35.svg">Detail 35</a> shows 100&#x27;-0&quot; minimum of solid line, 24&#x27;-0&quot; marker spacing, and 8&#x27;-6&quot; and 7&#x27;-0&quot; dashes. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-35a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-35a.svg">Detail 35A</a> shows 100&#x27;-0&quot; minimum of solid line with 7&#x27;-0&quot; dashes and 17&#x27;-0&quot; gaps. Dimensions of 3&quot;, 2&quot; and 1 1/2&quot; are shown at each.</p>
<p>Legend: Markers: Type D two-way yellow retroreflective, Type H one-way yellow retroreflective, Type RY red-yellow retroreflective. Lines: 6&quot; white, 6&quot; yellow.</p>
<p>Marker details: Type RY and Type D, and Type H, are shown in side and plan view. Each is 3 7/8&quot; to 4 1/8&quot; wide, 3/8&quot; to 3/4&quot; high and 1 7/8&quot; to 4 1/8&quot; long. Retroreflective face is hatched.</p>
<p>Title block: State of California, Department of Transportation, Pavement Markers and Traffic Lines Typical Details, no scale, A20B. Plans approval date May 31, 2018, registered civil engineer Atifa Ferouz, C80402.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Legend: Markers: Type D two-way yellow retroreflective. Type H one-way yellow retroreflective. Type RY red-yellow retroreflective. Lines: 6&quot; white. 6&quot; yellow.</p>
<p>Deleted details: 27A, 30 and 33.</p>
<p>Dimensions: edge of traveled way, minimum (MIN.) 2&#x27;-0&quot; between yellow line pairs in median islands, 12&#x27;-0&quot; typical for the two-way left turn lane width, and 100&#x27;-0&quot; minimum for intersection treatments.</p>
