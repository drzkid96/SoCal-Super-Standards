<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans typical layouts for left edgelines on divided highways, right edgelines, edgeline extensions through intersections, and median island markings. It also shows the marker types used, including Type D, Type H, and Type RY retroreflective markers. Dimensions on the sheet include 24'-0" and 48'-0" cycles, and a 100'-0" minimum along the centerline of an intersecting street.</p>
