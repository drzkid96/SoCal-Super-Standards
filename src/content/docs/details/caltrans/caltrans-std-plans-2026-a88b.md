<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Curb ramp Cases CM and CH for ramps at raised islands, Section A-A and B-B, and Type A, B and C island passageways with detectable warning surfaces. Passageways slope 1.5% Max to drain, are 4'-2" Min wide, and need detectable warnings 2'-0" or 3'-0" deep depending on passageway length.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Case CM curb ramp: plan of a curb ramp with a curved (BCR, beginning of curb return) front and detectable warning surface (see Note 2) wrapping the curved corner, with crosswalk if provided. The ramp is 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide if crosswalk provided with 1.5% Max cross slope and 7.5% Max slope, with 3&#x27;-0&quot; Typ dimension at the ramp. A 6&quot; Min high retaining curb is at the edge of the sidewalk, 6&quot; Typ, and if necessary, retaining curb is constructed at the edge of sidewalk. The curb matches the ramp slope. Gutter not shown. Section A-A is cut through the ramp.</p>
<p>Case CH curb ramp: plan of a straight ramp 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide with 1.5% Max cross slope and 7.5% Max slope for the sidewalk side and 1.5% Max, with required curb on one side and a 6&quot; Min high retaining curb. A transition from full height to zero height curb occurs over a 1&#x27;-0&quot; dimension at the ramp. The detectable warning surface (see Note 2) is at the face of curb. If necessary, construct retaining curb at edge of sidewalk, 6&quot; Typ. Curb to match ramp slope. Crosswalk if provided. Section A-A is cut through the ramp.</p>
<p>Section A-A: retaining curb if necessary at the high end, a 1.5% Max ramp slope sloping to the gutter flowline, thickness T, and gutter (see Note 7).</p>
<p>Section B-B: a cut through an island passageway: clear passageway between two retaining curbs, each 6&quot; Typ, with the clear width 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min, surface of raised island at a variable (Var) height above the passageway and thickness T.</p>
<p>Type A passageway: plan of a straight island passageway through a raised island between two traveled ways, with a curb ramp (see Note 7) on the sidewalk at each side. The raised island is 6&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide. A detectable warning surface (see Notes 2 and 8) is placed at each side of the passageway, with the passageway 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min wide, sloped to drain 1.5% Max. Dimension See Note 3 marks the detectable warning depth.</p>
<p>Type B passageway: plan of a raised island with an angled (bent) passageway: the passageway enters from one side and exits diagonally, with a detectable warning surface (see Notes 2 and 8) at each end, each 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min wide, and sloped to drain 1.5% Max. A curb ramp (see Note 7) is at the adjacent corner. See Note 3 for detectable warning depth.</p>
<p>Type C passageway: plan of a triangular raised island with a passageway that has detectable warning surfaces (see Notes 2 and 8) on three sides: two at the entry and exit ends and one on the diagonal side, each 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min wide, with passageway sloped to drain 1.5% Max. See Note 3.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Sidewalk, ramp and passageway thickness &quot;T&quot;, shall be 3 1/2&quot; minimum.</p>
<p>2. For details of detectable warning surfaces, see Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>3. Where an island passageway length is greater than or equal to 6&#x27;-0&quot;, but less than 8&#x27;-0&quot;, each detectable warning surface shall extend the full width and 2&#x27;-0&quot; depth of the passageway length. Where an island passageway length is greater than or equal to 8&#x27;-0&quot;, each detectable warning surface shall extend the full width and 3&#x27;-0&quot; depth of the passageway length. Detectable warning surfaces shall extend the full width of the island passageway except a maximum gap of 1&quot; is allowed on each side of the passageway.</p>
<p>4. The adjacent surfaces at transitions at curb ramps to walks, gutters, and streets shall be at the same level.</p>
<p>5. Utility pull boxes, manholes, vaults and all other utility facilities within the boundaries of the curb ramp will be relocated or adjusted to grade by the owner prior to, or in conjunction with, curb ramp construction.</p>
<p>6. Detectable warning surface may have to be cut to allow removal of utility covers while maintaining detectable warning width and depth.</p>
<p>7. For additional curb ramp details, see Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>8. The detectable warning surface will be a rectangle as shown at the face of curb, unless modified in the Project Plans.</p></details>
