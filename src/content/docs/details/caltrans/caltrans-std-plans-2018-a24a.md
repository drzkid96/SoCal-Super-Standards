<p class="dwg-sum">Caltrans dimensioned layouts for pavement arrows, drawn on a 1-foot grid, with the area of each in square feet. It covers Type I arrows at 10, 18 and 24 feet long, Type II (L) and (R) arrows, the Type III lane drop arrow, Type IV, Type V and Type VI arrows, and the bike lane arrow. Mirror images are used for opposite-hand versions.</p>
