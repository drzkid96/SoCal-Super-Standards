<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20G shows a freeway exit ramp approaching a cross street with enhanced retroreflective pavement markers to discourage wrong-way entry. Type RY markers line the left edge, Type R one-way red markers line the right edge facing wrong-way drivers, Type C markers sit on the lane line, and Type R markers are placed on the outermost limit line or crosswalk. Marker spacing changes in zones of 120 ft, 240 ft and 120 ft ahead of the cross street.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 20 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. One plan view of an exit ramp running left to right toward a cross street at the right. Traffic arrows show the ramp direction. Break symbols interrupt the ramp. Dimension strings above and below the ramp measure distances back from the limit line at the cross street. The left edge of the ramp carries a hatched 6 in. yellow edge line and the right edge an edge line, both marked as edge of traveled way. A broken 6 in. white lane line runs down the middle.</p>
<p>Marker details: Type C and Type RY marker plan about 3 7/8 in. to 4 1/2 in. wide by 1 7/8 in. to 4 1/8 in. tall, side profile 3/8 in. to 3/4 in. high, with retroreflective face at both ends. Type R marker same size with a white body (Note 7), one end retroreflective red and the other end non-reflective (black symbol).</p>
<p>Left (top) edge of ramp: Type RY two-way red-yellow markers inboard of the yellow line. Right (bottom) edge: Type R one-way red markers (half black) about 2 in. from the edge of traveled way (2 in. dimension shown near the cross street). Center lane line: white broken segments between Type C red-clear markers spaced 24&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>Marker spacing zones (measured upstream from the cross street): The dimensioned zones are 120&#x27;-0&quot;, 240&#x27;-0&quot; and 120&#x27;-0&quot; with break symbols. Near the cross street, markers are at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing (two 24&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions at the limit line). Upstream of that the spacing is 12&#x27;-0&quot; (two 12&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions). In the other zones, clusters of markers at 6&#x27;-0&quot; spacing are shown on both edges (6&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions) and markers continue at wider spacing between clusters.</p>
<p>Start of ramp (left): Two Type V arrows with Type R one-way red markers beside them, called out See Note 6.</p>
<p>Cross street (right): A limit line or crosswalk (see Note 1) crosses the ramp at the cross street. Three Type R marker groups, drawn as angled V symbols, are placed at the limit line facing the intersection with red facing the intersection (see Notes 1, 2, 3 and 5). The cross street runs vertically, labeled CROSS STREET, with two-way arrows on each approach.</p>
<p>Legend: Type C two-way red-clear retroreflective marker, Type RY two-way red-yellow retroreflective marker, Type R one-way red retroreflective marker, 6 in. white line, 6 in. yellow line.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. May be a limit line or crosswalk.</p>
<p>2. See Standard Plans A24F and A24G for crosswalk and pavement marker details.</p>
<p>3. For raised markers, place Type R one-way red retroreflective markers on outermost limit line or crosswalk line with red facing the intersection. For recessed markers, place Type R one-way retroreflective markers with red facing the intersection and as shown on Standard Plan A24G.</p>
<p>4. The distances and marker spacings may be adjusted based on site specific conditions, exit ramp geometry, or loop detectors.</p>
<p>5. See Standard Plan A20D for recess details.</p>
<p>6. See Standard Plan A24G for Type V arrow detail with Type R one-way red retroreflective markers.</p>
<p>7. The Type R marker body must be white in color.</p>
