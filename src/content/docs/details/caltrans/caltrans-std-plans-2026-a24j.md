<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan: a roadway shown with a dashed lane line or centerline, the edge of traveled way (ETW) line and the edge of shoulder (ES) line, with an arrow showing the direction of travel. In the shoulder, between the ETW and ES lines, white transverse bars are placed that span the shoulder and are paired with a white X pavement marking just ahead of each bar. Successive bars are spaced 1 MILE apart. A dimension reads IF LESS THAN 1 MILE, SEE NOTE 1 for the section length between markings. A circle calls out Detail A at one X and bar. A sign symbol on the right shoulder at the start of the section is Sign R48-2 (CA), a vertical sign reading SPEED ENFORCED BY AIRCRAFT.</p>
<p>Detail A: the shoulder bar is a 12 in. white line (marked 12&quot; WHITE) running transversely across the shoulder 6&#x27;-0&quot; long from the ETW to the ES line side (dimension 6&#x27;-0&quot;), with an X marking next to it. The X is 3&#x27;-0&quot; wide and 2&#x27;-0&quot; high, made of 4 in. white lines (marked 4&quot; WHITE). The X is placed 1&#x27;-0&quot; away from the bar, with a 3&#x27;-0&quot; dimension for the X width, 1&#x27;-0&quot; gap, and the 12 in. bar. Also shown is the ETW line (4 in. white, labeled 4&quot; WHITE at the ETW) and the ES line.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Install white &quot;X&quot; pavement marking at each end of the section if the markings are less than 1 mile apart.</p></details>
