<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Letter layouts: all fifteen word markings are drawn as elongated outline letters on a 4 in. grid. Every word is 8&#x27;-0&quot; tall. The width and area (A) of each word are: LANE 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide, A=24 ft2. CLEAR 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=27 ft2. BIKE 5&#x27;-4&quot; wide, A=21 ft2. ONLY 6&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=22 ft2. NO 3&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=14 ft2 (letters stacked side by side in a narrow block). KEEP 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide, A=24 ft2. BUS 4&#x27;-8&quot; wide, A=20 ft2. FWY 4&#x27;-8&quot; wide, A=16 ft2. EXPRS 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=30 ft2. TRAIL 7&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=23 ft2. HOV 4&#x27;-8&quot; wide, A=18 ft2. NORTH 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=31 ft2. SOUTH 8&#x27;-0&quot; wide, A=29 ft2. EAST 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide, A=22 ft2. WEST 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide, A=24 ft2. The sheet is arranged in three rows: LANE, CLEAR, BIKE, ONLY, NO in the first row, KEEP, BUS, FWY, EXPRS, TRAIL in the second, and HOV, NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST in the third.</p>
<p>Word Markings table: a three-column table of item and square footage (SQFT). LANE 24, CLEAR 27, BIKE 21. ONLY 22, NO 14, KEEP 24. BUS 20, FWY 16, EXPRS 30. TRAIL 23, HOV 18, NORTH 31. SOUTH 29, EAST 22, WEST 24. Bottom row: NO PARKING 2.</p>
<p>NO PARKING detail: the words NO and PARKING are drawn as stencil style letters on a 1 in. grid style layout, NO on a first line and PARKING on a second line below it. NO is 1&#x27;-1 1/2&quot; wide and 1&#x27;-0&quot; high. PARKING is 3&#x27;-9&quot; wide and 1&#x27;-0&quot; high. A 1 1/2&quot; dimension marks the letter stroke or bridge width and a 2&quot; dimension marks the gap between the two lines. A=2 ft2. The detail refers to Notes 5 and 6.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. If a message consists of more than one word, it must read &quot;UP&quot;, i.e., the first word must be nearest the driver.</p>
<p>2. The space between words must be at least four times the height of the characters for low speed roads, but not more than ten times the height of the characters. The space may be reduced appropriately where there is limited space because of local conditions.</p>
<p>3. Minor variations in dimensions may be accepted by the Engineer.</p>
<p>4. Portions of a letter, number, or symbol may be separated by connecting segments not to exceed 2 in. in width.</p>
<p>5. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot; pavement marking is to be used for parking facilities. For typical locations of markings, see Standard Plans A90A and A90B.</p>
<p>6. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot; shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high on a contrasting background and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials.</p></details>
