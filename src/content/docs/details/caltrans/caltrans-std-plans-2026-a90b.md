<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Two plan layouts of accessible on-street parallel parking: the Conventional detail with a curb ramp and the Restricted Right of Way Width detail with blue painted curb and no ramp. It also shows the NO PARKING pavement marking and the R99, R99B and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> signs. Spaces must be at least 20'-0" long and 8'-0" wide.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Conventional (see Note 9): plan of an on-street parallel parking space along the curb, with the sidewalk behind and regular parking spaces on both sides. At the upstream end of the accessible space is an access aisle 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min long along the curb and 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min deep from the curb, bordered by a 4&quot; blue line with 4&quot; white line diagonals at 3&#x27;-0&quot; Max centers (see Note 6) and marked NO PARKING (see Note 7). The accessible space beyond the aisle has its length and width per Note 5. The curb along the aisle is blue painted curb. A curb ramp (see Note 4) with detectable warning surface is built from the aisle into the sidewalk, with a 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min unobstructed area beside it, and the curb returns to the sidewalk line at a 1:1 Typ transition. An ISA parking sign (see Note 11) stands at the sidewalk.</p>
<p>Restricted Right of Way Width: plan of an on-street parallel parking space at a street corner with a sidewalk too narrow for a ramp (parallel parking, see Note 10). No curb ramp is used. The curb is painted blue along the accessible space (blue painted curb), with a NO PARKING AS REQUIRED zone along the curb nearest the corner, a regular parking space beyond, and an ISA parking sign (see Note 11) at the sidewalk. The length of the space is per Note 5.</p>
<p>Pavement marking: the words NO PARKING stenciled in two lines, NO above PARKING (see Note 7).</p>
<p>Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a>: a vertical sign with the ISA and the words PARKING ONLY. Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>, Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a> with Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>: the plaque reads MINIMUM FINE $250 (see Note 3). Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a>: a vertical sign combining ISA, PARKING ONLY and MINIMUM FINE $250 panels (see Note 3).</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Parking spaces shall be so located that persons with disabilities are not compelled to wheel or walk behind parked vehicles other than their own.</p>
<p>2. Surface slopes of accessible on-street parking spaces shall be the minimum feasible.</p>
<p>3. Where Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a> or Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> are installed, the bottom of the sign or plaque panel shall be a minimum of 7&#x27;-0&quot; above the surrounding surface.</p>
<p>4. Curb ramps shall conform to the details shown on Standard Plan A88A.</p>
<p>5. Accessible on-street parking spaces shall not be smaller in length or width than that specified by the local jurisdiction for other parking spaces, but not less than 20&#x27;-0&quot; in length and not less than 8&#x27;-0&quot; in width.</p>
<p>6. Blue paint, instead of white may be used for marking accessibility aisles in areas where snow may cause white markings to not be visible.</p>
<p>7. The words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;, shall be painted in white letters no less than 1&#x27;-0&quot; high on a contrasting background and located so that it is visible to traffic enforcement officials. See Standard Plan A24E for square foot area for painting the words &quot;NO PARKING&quot;.</p>
<p>8. There shall be no obstructions on the sidewalk adjacent to and for the full length of the parking space, except for the ISA parking sign shown.</p>
<p>9. The Conventional detail should be the primary choice of accessible on-street parking. However, if the sidewalk lacks adequate space to construct a standard curb ramp, the restricted right of way detail should be used.</p>
<p>10. If the Restricted Right of Way Width detail is selected and it conflicts with a bus stop or other uses, this detail may apply to the other end of the block.</p>
<p>11. Accessible Parking Only Sign shall be Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99cca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99cca.webp">R99C(CA)</a> or Sign <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99ca.webp">R99(CA)</a> with Plaque <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r99bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r99bca.webp">R99B(CA)</a>.</p>
<p>Legend: ISA - International Symbol of Accessibility.</p></details>
