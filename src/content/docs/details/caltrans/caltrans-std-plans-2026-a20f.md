<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20F gives 8 in. white line patterns for preferential lane lines such as HOV and managed lanes. Detail 42 is a broken line with Type C markers, Details 44A, 44B and 45 are double lines with Type C markers and varying buffer space, and Details 43 and 43A are deleted.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 19 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. All lines shown are 8 in. white and all markers are Type C red-clear retroreflective markers.</p>
<p>Preferential lane lines, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-42" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-42.svg">Detail 42</a>: Broken 8 in. white line with 12&#x27;-0&quot; segments, an 18&#x27;-0&quot; gap, a Type C marker, another 18&#x27;-0&quot; gap, and the next 12&#x27;-0&quot; segment (48&#x27;-0&quot; cycle with the marker at the center of the 36&#x27;-0&quot; gap).</p>
<p>Detail 43: Deleted. Detail 43A: Deleted.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-44a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-44a.svg">Detail 44A</a>: Double solid 8 in. white lines on either side of the LOL line, with a 4 in. space between them (2 in. each side of LOL). Type C markers outside each line 2 in. from the line at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. Dimensions at the right end: 2 in. marker offsets and 4 in. space between lines.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-44b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-44b.svg">Detail 44B</a>: Double solid 8 in. white lines with an 8 in. space between them (4 in. each side of LOL). Type C markers outside each line, 2 in. from it, at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. Right end: 8 in. between lines and 2&#x27;-0&quot; overall buffer space.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-45" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-45.svg">Detail 45</a>: Two pairs of solid 8 in. white lines, one pair on each side of the LOL line. Within each pair the lines are 3 in. apart. Between the two pairs the space is 1 in. to 2 in. on each side of LOL. Type C markers outside each pair, 2 in. from the outer line, at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing, and a Type C marker on the LOL line (shown as a larger two-cell marker) at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. Right end: 3 in. spaces and the overall BUFFER SPACE marked with an asterisk.</p>
<p>Marker details (bottom right): Type C marker plan about 3 7/8 in. to 4 1/2 in. wide by 1 7/8 in. to 4 1/8 in. tall, side profile 3/8 in. to 3/4 in. high, retroreflective face hatched at both ends.</p>
<p>Legend: Type C red-clear retroreflective marker, 8 in. white line.</p></details>
