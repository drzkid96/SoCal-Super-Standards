<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20E shows contrast striping, where black stripes are placed beside or between yellow or white traffic lines to improve visibility on light pavement. It gives a double stripe detail, a lane or edge line detail with 2 in. black borders (Option 1) and a lane line detail with black segments alternating with white segments and a retroreflective marker (Option 2).</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 18 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Three plan views of stripes drawn with solid fills: black stripes are filled black, yellow or white lines are drawn as open boxes.</p>
<p>Typical double stripe contrast detail: Three stacked stripes. A yellow or white stripe on top, a black stripe in the middle and a yellow or white stripe on the bottom. The black stripe separates the two colored stripes. No dimensions are given on this view.</p>
<p>Option 1, typical lane line or edge line contrast detail: A yellow or white line (6 in. to 12 in. wide, as dimensioned) between two black stripes. Each black stripe is 2 in. wide and sits directly against the colored line. Dimensions at the right end: 2 in. black, 6 in. to 12 in. colored line, 2 in. black.</p>
<p>Option 2, typical lane line contrast detail (see Note 2): A longitudinal sequence along the lane line. Black segment, then 1&#x27;-0&quot; minimum clear space, then a retroreflective marker (half white, half black symbol), then 1&#x27;-0&quot; minimum clear space, then a black segment, then a gap of 0&#x27; to 2&#x27;-0&quot;, then a white 6 in. wide segment, then a gap of 0&#x27; to 2&#x27;-0&quot;, then a black segment, and so on. The 6 in. dimension marks the width of the white line. An additional gap of 0&#x27; to 2&#x27;-0&quot; is shown between the white segment and the next black segment at the left end (white followed by black).</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. See Standard Plans A20A, A20B, A20C, A20D, and A20F for traffic lines typical details.</p>
