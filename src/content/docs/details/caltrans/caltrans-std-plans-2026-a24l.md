<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Contrast treatment details: three small plan details, each showing a black rectangle with 2 in. contrast treatment around it. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25.svg">Detail 25</a> at the edge of traveled way (ETW): the pavement marking is 12 in. wide by 18 in. long (12&quot; and 18&quot; dimensions), set against the ETW line, with a 2&quot; contrast treatment on the sides and ends. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-12a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-12a.svg">Detail 12A</a> at a lane line (LL): the pavement marking is 12 in. wide and 36 in. long, centered on the lane line, with 18&quot; each side of the line and a 2&quot; contrast treatment Typ around the mark. <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a> at the ETW: the pavement marking is 12 in. wide by 18 in. long set against the ETW, with the 2&quot; contrast treatment. Arrows show direction of travel.</p>
<p>Plan: a four lane roadway (two lanes each direction) with edge lines (ETW) and a double center line. In the upper pair of lanes, short transverse bars (pavement markings) are placed across the lanes at the ETW edges and at the lane line, with a spacing that gets progressively closer in the direction of travel (arrows pointing left). The lane line dashes continue between the bars. The lower pair of lanes shows the opposite direction (arrows pointing right) with only the dashed lane line and no bars. The bars at the lane line are drawn longer than those at the ETW edges, and where a bar meets a lane line dash a cross appears.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Exact location for speed reduction pavement markings will be determined by the Engineer.</p>
<p>2. Do not cover pavement markings.</p>
<p>3. Speed reduction markings are white. Contrast treatment is black.</p>
<p>4. Engineer to determine length and spacing based on approach and target speeds in the field.</p>
<p>Legend: arrow symbol means direction of travel.</p></details>
