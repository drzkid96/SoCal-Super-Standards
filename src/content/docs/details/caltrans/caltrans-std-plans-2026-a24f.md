<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Plan views of the higher visibility crosswalk patterns: continental, triple four, ladder and diagonal, plus the basic crosswalk of two parallel lines. Dimensions give bar widths, gaps, minimum crosswalk width and offsets from the limit line. Notes require spaces in wheel tracks, limit spacing, and white marking except yellow near schools.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Continental: a row of long narrow rectangular bars set perpendicular to the direction of the crosswalk, with the bars&#x27; long dimension in the direction of pedestrian travel. Bar length is 6&#x27;-0&quot; minimum. Bar width is dimensioned 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 2&#x27;-0&quot;. The space between bars is dimensioned 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 5&#x27;-0&quot; (see Note 2). Labeled see Note 1 (spaces between markings placed in wheel tracks).</p>
<p>Triple Four: a pattern of rectangular blocks, each 2&#x27;-0&quot; wide and 4&#x27;-0&quot; long, laid out in two rows along the crosswalk, with the 4&#x27;-0&quot; long block of the first row, a 4&#x27;-0&quot; gap, and the 4&#x27;-0&quot; long block of the second row (three 4&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions stacked in the direction of travel, 12&#x27;-0&quot; overall). Blocks within a row are 2&#x27;-0&quot; wide with a 2&#x27;-0&quot; minimum space between them. Block spacing across the crosswalk is 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 5&#x27;-0&quot; (see Note 2). Labeled see Note 1.</p>
<p>Ladder: two parallel transverse lines bounding the crosswalk with bars set between them parallel to the direction of travel. The crosswalk is 6&#x27;-0&quot; minimum wide between the transverse lines, and each transverse line is 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 2&#x27;-0&quot; wide. The bars are spaced 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 5&#x27;-0&quot; apart (see Note 2) and the bar width is 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 2&#x27;-0&quot;. Break lines show that the pattern continues across the roadway.</p>
<p>Diagonal: two parallel transverse lines with diagonal bars at 45 degrees between them. Crosswalk width is 6&#x27;-0&quot; minimum, transverse lines are 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 2&#x27;-0&quot; wide, the diagonal bar width is 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 2&#x27;-0&quot;, and the space between diagonal bars is 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 5&#x27;-0&quot; (see Note 2). The angle shown is 45 degrees.</p>
<p>Basic: two parallel transverse lines, each 1&#x27;-0&quot; to 2&#x27;-0&quot; wide, spaced 6&#x27;-0&quot; minimum apart (clear distance), with break lines showing it continues across the roadway.</p>
<p>The group of Continental, Triple Four, Ladder and Diagonal is titled Higher Visibility Crosswalks.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. Spaces between markings must be placed in wheel tracks of each lane.</p>
<p>2. Spacings not to exceed 2.5 times width of longitudinal line.</p>
<p>3. All crosswalk markings must be white except those near schools must be yellow.</p></details>
