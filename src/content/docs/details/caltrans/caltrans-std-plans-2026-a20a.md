<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20A gives typical plan-view patterns for 6 in. yellow and white traffic lines with Type C, D, G and H retroreflective pavement markers. It covers two-lane highway centerlines (Details 1 to 7), multilane lane lines (Details 8 to 14A) and no passing zone lines in one and two directions (Details 15 to 23). Details 3, 4, 7, 10, 13, 14, 14A, 17, 20 and 23 are deleted.</p>
<p class="dwg-topics">Topics: <a class="chip" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/striping-intersections/">Lane lines, striping and intersection markings</a></p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 14 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Every pattern is drawn in plan with arrows showing the direction of travel and dimension strings giving the repeating cycle. Line patterns use a hatched box for a 6 in. yellow line and an open box for a 6 in. white line. Markers are small squares: Type C red-clear (square with vertical and horizontal split), Type D two-way yellow (square with X), Type G one-way clear (half filled black) and Type H one-way yellow (half black with X hatch). Right side of the sheet also shows the marker detail drawings and a note.</p>
