<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20D covers 8 in. white channelizing lines (Details 38 to 38D), 6 in. bike lane lines (39, 39A), lane line and centerline extensions through intersections (40, 41), and recess details for traffic stripes and retroreflective pavement markers. Details 38C, 40A and 41A are deleted.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 17 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. The left side shows line patterns in plan, the middle shows marker details and the recessed traffic stripe, and the right shows the recess detail for retroreflective pavement markers. Markers: Type C red-clear (cross-line square) and Type G one-way clear (half black).</p>
<p>Channelizing line, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38.svg">Detail 38</a>: A continuous 8 in. white line with a break symbol at the left end and Type G markers 2 in. below the line at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. Through traffic arrow shown.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38a.svg">Detail 38A</a>: Continuous 8 in. white line, no markers.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38b.svg">Detail 38B</a>: Continuous 8 in. white line with Type C markers on both sides at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing, each 2 in. from the line.</p>
<p>Detail 38C: Deleted.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-38d" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-38d.svg">Detail 38D</a> (exit ramps only): Continuous 8 in. white line with Type C markers on one side at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing, 2 in. from the line.</p>
<p>Bike lane line, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39.svg">Detail 39</a>: Continuous 6 in. white line.</p>
<p>Intersection line, bike lane, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39a.svg">Detail 39A</a>: Broken 6 in. white line of 8&#x27;-0&quot; segments with 4&#x27;-0&quot; gaps over a 200&#x27;-0&quot; length toward the intersection.</p>
<p>Lane line extensions through intersections, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-40" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-40.svg">Detail 40</a>: Short 6 in. white segments 1&#x27;-0&quot; long with 6&#x27;-0&quot; gaps.</p>
<p>Detail 40A: Deleted.</p>
<p>Centerline extensions through intersections, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-41" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-41.svg">Detail 41</a>: Short 6 in. yellow segments 1&#x27;-0&quot; long with 6&#x27;-0&quot; gaps.</p>
<p>Detail 41A: Deleted.</p>
<p>Marker details: Type C marker plan about 3 7/8 in. to 4 1/2 in. wide and 1 7/8 in. to 4 1/8 in. tall, side profile 3/8 in. to 3/4 in. high, with retroreflective face top and bottom. Type G marker same dimensions with a black body at one end.</p>
<p>Detail for recessed traffic stripe: Section through finished roadway surface showing a recess 6 in. to 8 in. wide filled with striping material flush with the surface (see Recessed Notes A and B).</p>
<p>Recess detail for retroreflective pavement marker, Plan Type 1 and Section A-A: Recess 5 3/16 in. plus or minus 1/8 in. wide, length at least 2&#x27;-0&quot; ramp plus 1&#x27;-0&quot; min, with the retroreflective pavement marker at the downstream end of the recess. The marker top sits 0 in. to 3/16 in. below the pavement surface (see Note 3). Recess depth varies (Var). See Note 5. Arrow shows direction of traffic.</p>
<p>Plan Type 2 and Section B-B: Same 5 3/16 in. plus or minus 1/8 in. wide recess, 2&#x27;-0&quot; Min ramp each side of a 1&#x27;-0&quot; Min center section that is 6 in. wide, with the marker at the center of the recess, 0 in. to 3/16 in. below pavement surface, depth Var.</p>
<p>Retroreflective pavement marker for recessed installation, Type C, Type RY and Type D: plan 4 9/16 in. to 4 13/16 in. wide by 2 in. to 2 1/2 in. tall, height 3/8 in. to 7/16 in., two-way retroreflective face. Type G, Type R and Type H: same dimensions with a black end and one-way face. See Notes 1 and 2.</p>
<p>Legend: Type C red-clear retroreflective marker, Type G one-way clear retroreflective marker, 6 in. yellow line.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>Note: Pavement markers shown off the solid line may be placed on the line.</p>
<h5>Recessed notes:</h5>
<p>A. See typical traffic line details for pavement marking patterns.</p>
<p>B. See Standard Specifications for recess depth and recess striping material thickness.</p>
<h5>Recessed marker notes:</h5>
<p>1. See typical traffic line details for marker patterns to be used with recessed pavement markers.</p>
<p>2. The retroreflective pavement markers shown for recessed installations are not to be used for non-recessed installations.</p>
<p>3. The top of pavement markers installed in recesses shall be 0 in. to 3/16 in. below the pavement surface.</p>
<p>4. Use Type 1 recess for pavement markers with one-way retroreflective face. Use Type 2 recess for pavement markers with two-way retroreflective face.</p>
<p>5. For exit ramps, use Type 1 recess for Type R markers with one-way retroreflective face. Reverse the orientation of the recess and place the marker at the upstream end of the recess.</p>
<h5>Legend:</h5>
<p>Type C red-clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>Type G one-way clear retroreflective marker.</p>
<p>6 in. yellow line.</p>
<p>Details 38C, 40A and 41A are marked deleted. Return to Table of Contents link at the bottom of the page.</p></details>
