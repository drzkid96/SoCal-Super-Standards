<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Caltrans 2026 Standard Plan A20B gives plan-view patterns for left and right edge lines, median island double yellow lines, two-way left turn lane lines and the approach to an intersecting street centerline, with Type D, H and RY markers. Details 24 to 27C, 28, 29, 31, 32, 34, 34A, 35 and 35A are active. Details 27A, 30 and 33 are deleted.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Overall layout: Sheet 15 of the 2026 Standard Plan set, no scale. Patterns are drawn in plan with arrows for the direction of travel. ETW labels a dashed reference line that is the edge of traveled way, and LOL labels a dash-dot reference line between the paired lines in median and left turn lane details. Hatched boxes are 6 in. yellow lines and open boxes are 6 in. white lines. Marker symbols are Type D two-way yellow (square with X), Type H one-way yellow (half black) and Type RY red-yellow (square with cross lines).</p>
<p>Left edge lines (divided highways), <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-24" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-24.svg">Detail 24</a>: A continuous 6 in. yellow line on the left edge, placed 2 in. from the ETW reference line (2 in. dimension at right end). No markers.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25.svg">Detail 25</a>: Same continuous yellow line with Type H markers on the line side 2 in. from the line (2 in. dimensions at both ends), spaced 48&#x27;-0&quot; apart.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-25a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-25a.svg">Detail 25A</a>: Same yellow line with Type RY markers at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing (two 24&#x27;-0&quot; dimensions shown between three markers, with a marker at each end of the 48&#x27;-0&quot; length).</p>
<p>Detail 25B: Same yellow line with Type RY markers at 48&#x27;-0&quot; spacing.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-26" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-26.svg">Detail 26</a>: No solid line. A broken reference at the ETW with Type H markers 48&#x27;-0&quot; apart and a 2 in. offset dimension at the right end.</p>
<p>Detail 26A: As <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-26" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-26.svg">Detail 26</a> with Type RY markers 48&#x27;-0&quot; apart.</p>
<p>Detail 26B: Two parallel continuous yellow lines (3 in. between them, 2 in. offset from the ETW at the left and right ends) with Type RY markers 48&#x27;-0&quot; apart.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27.svg">Detail 27</a>: As Detail 26B (two continuous yellow lines, 3 in. spacing, 2 in. offset) with Type H markers 48&#x27;-0&quot; apart.</p>
<p>Left edge lines note (bottom left): On freeways use traffic stripe details with Type RY markers.</p>
<p>Right edge lines, Detail 27A: Deleted.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a>: A continuous 6 in. white right edge line with a 2 in. offset dimension from the ETW reference line. No markers.</p>
<p>Right edge line extension through intersections, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27c" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27c.svg">Detail 27C</a>: Short 6 in. white line segments, each 3&#x27;-0&quot; long, separated by 12&#x27;-0&quot; gaps (dimensioned 12&#x27;-0&quot; between segments and 3&#x27;-0&quot; along each segment).</p>
<p>Median islands, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-28" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-28.svg">Detail 28</a>: Two pairs of continuous yellow lines on either side of the LOL line. The nearest line of each pair is 2&#x27;-0&quot; minimum from the LOL line. Each pair has 3 in. spacing between the lines (3 in. dimension at right end). No markers.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-29" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-29.svg">Detail 29</a>: Same as <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-28" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-28.svg">Detail 28</a> (2&#x27;-0&quot; Min from LOL, 3 in. between lines) with Type D markers outside each pair at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing, with a 2 in. offset dimension at the right end.</p>
<p>Detail 30: Deleted.</p>
<p>Two-way left turn lanes, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-31" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-31.svg">Detail 31</a>: Two outer continuous yellow lines with a broken yellow line inside each, arranged symmetrically about the LOL line. Broken segments are 12&#x27;-0&quot; long with 36&#x27;-0&quot; gaps. The broken line is 3 in. and 1 1/2 in. from the solid line, and the two broken lines are 12&#x27;-0&quot; Typ apart (as drawn at the right end). No markers.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-32" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-32.svg">Detail 32</a>: Same arrangement with Type D markers. Markers are spaced 24&#x27;-0&quot; along the outside of each solid line, dimensioned over a 96&#x27;-0&quot; length. The broken lines, from the 96&#x27;-0&quot; dimension start, show 18&#x27;-0&quot; gap, 12&#x27;-0&quot; segment, 36&#x27;-0&quot; gap, 12&#x27;-0&quot; segment, 36&#x27;-0&quot; gap. Type D markers also appear inside the lane between broken segments. 2 in. offset dimensions on the outer markers and 3 in. and 1 1/2 in. dimensions at the right end.</p>
<p>Detail 33: Deleted.</p>
<p>Intersection treatments, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-34" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-34.svg">Detail 34</a>: Continuous yellow line along the approach and a broken yellow line, each ending at the centerline of the intersecting street with a break symbol and &quot;100&#x27;-0&quot; Min&quot; dimension from the intersection. Broken segments are 12&#x27;-0&quot; with gaps of 18&#x27;-0&quot;, 18&#x27;-0&quot;, 12&#x27;-0&quot; and 18&#x27;-0&quot; (as dimensioned above the line). Type D markers sit on the broken line side and Type H markers on the solid line side at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. Dimensions at left end: 1 1/2 in., 3 in. and 2 in. Pattern repeats mirrored on the far side of the intersecting street.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-34a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-34a.svg">Detail 34A</a>: Continuous yellow line with a broken yellow line of 12&#x27;-0&quot; segment, 36&#x27;-0&quot; gap, 12&#x27;-0&quot; segment, over a 100&#x27;-0&quot; Min length to the intersecting street centerline. 3 in. and 1 1/2 in. dimensions. No markers.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-35" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-35.svg">Detail 35</a>: Continuous yellow line with a broken line of 7&#x27;-0&quot; segments and gaps dimensioned 8&#x27;-6&quot;, 8&#x27;-6&quot;, 7&#x27;-0&quot;, 8&#x27;-6&quot;, over a 100&#x27;-0&quot; Min length. Type H markers on the solid line side and Type D markers on the broken side at 24&#x27;-0&quot; spacing. 3 in., 1 1/2 in. and 2 in. dimensions.</p>
<p><a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-35a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-35a.svg">Detail 35A</a>: Continuous yellow line with a broken line of 7&#x27;-0&quot; segments and 17&#x27;-0&quot; gaps (shown 17&#x27;-0&quot; and 7&#x27;-0&quot; at the intersection end) over a 100&#x27;-0&quot; Min length. 1 1/2 in. and 3 in. dimensions. No markers.</p>
<p>The right half of the intersection treatment panel (beyond the line labeled CENTERLINE INTERSECTING STREET) repeats Details 34, 34A, 35 and 35A for the opposite approach with the same dimensions: 24&#x27;-0&quot; marker spacing, 18&#x27;-0&quot; and 12&#x27;-0&quot; gaps, 12&#x27;-0&quot; and 36&#x27;-0&quot; cycle, 8&#x27;-6&quot; and 7&#x27;-0&quot; segments, 17&#x27;-0&quot; and 7&#x27;-0&quot; cycle, 100&#x27;-0&quot; Min, and 2 in., 3 in. and 1 1/2 in. offsets, with an LOL line.</p>
<p>Marker details (right): Type RY and Type D marker: plan about 3 7/8 in. to 4 1/2 in. wide by 1 7/8 in. to 4 1/8 in. tall, side profile 3/8 in. to 3/4 in., retroreflective face hatched on both ends. Type H marker: same dimensions with a black body on one end and a one-way retroreflective face.</p>
<p>Legend: Type D two-way yellow retroreflective marker, Type H one-way yellow retroreflective marker, Type RY red-yellow retroreflective marker, 6 in. white line, 6 in. yellow line.</p></details>
