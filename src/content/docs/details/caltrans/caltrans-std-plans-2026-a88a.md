<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Case A: plan of a perpendicular curb ramp with a flare on each side and a landing behind the ramp. The sidewalk landing is 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min long with 1.5% Max slope in both directions. The ramp width is 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min with 1.5% Max cross slope. The ramp slope is 7.5% Max. Flares slope at 9.0% Max at the curb. The detectable warning surface (see Note 10) lies at the bottom of the ramp at the back of curb. Gutter not shown. Section A-A is cut through the ramp.</p>
<p>Case B: ramp with a retaining curb at the back of sidewalk. The ramp is 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide, with 1.5% Max cross slope, the sidewalk beside the ramp sloping 7.5% Max toward the ramp and 1.5% Max along the sidewalk, flares at 9.0% Max at curb, and a 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min landing. A 6&quot; Min high retaining curb is at the edge of the sidewalk, with 6&quot; Typ offset. Section B-B is cut through the ramp.</p>
<p>Case C: sidewalk depressed longitudinally. The ramp is 5&#x27;-0&quot; Min wide with 1.5% Max cross slope, sidewalks each side at 7.5% Max sloping toward the ramp and 1.5% Max longitudinal, with no flares. The curb matches ramp slope (curb to match ramp slope). A 6&quot; Min high retaining curb is shown at the edge of the sidewalk, 6&quot; Typ. Section C-C is cut through the ramp.</p>
<p>Case D: Case A widened where distance from curb to back of sidewalk is short: the ramp widens with 1:1 Typ side transitions up to the 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min landing, with 9.0% Max flares at the curb, 1.5% Max slopes and 7.5% Max ramp slope. A 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min ramp width.</p>
<p>Case E: ramp with a flare on one side and a retaining curb on the other side, with a planting area behind. The flare is 9.0% Max at curb and the front edge of sidewalk is noted. A 6&quot; Typ and a 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min ramp width are shown. Retaining curb on both sides of ramp is called out where the planting area is.</p>
<p>Case F: ramp between two planting areas with retaining curbs on both sides of the ramp, no flares. 1.5% Max ramp cross slope, 7.5% Max ramp slope, 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min width and 6&quot; Typ curb.</p>
<p>Case G: ramp with a flare on one side and a retaining curb on the other, with planting area behind (see Note 4). Flare slope 9.0% Max at curb, retaining curb at the ramp side, 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min width, 6&quot; Typ curb.</p>
<p>Detail A, typical two-ramp corner installation (see Note 1): plan of a corner with two perpendicular ramps, one on each leg of the curb return. Each ramp has a RAMP area with detectable warning surface, a flare on each side, and a landing behind. A crosswalk if provided is shown on each leg.</p>
<p>Detail B, typical one-ramp corner installation (see Notes 1 and 3): plan of a corner with one diagonal ramp at the center of the curb return with flares on both sides and a landing behind it. The ramp is 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min along its length. Crosswalk if provided. Where a flared side occurs, provide 2&#x27;-0&quot; Min of curb.</p>
<p>Section A-A: gutter flowline (see Note 8), a ramp sloping 7.5% Max from the top of the ramp (rounded) down to the gutter, a landing 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min at 1.5% Max, and thickness T.</p>
<p>Section B-B: gutter flowline, rounded top of ramp, ramp 7.5% Max, landing 4&#x27;-2&quot; Min at 1.5% Max, retaining curb if necessary, thickness T. Depress entire sidewalk as required.</p>
<p>Section C-C: gutter flowline, a continuous 1.5% Max slope path from the gutter to the retaining curb if necessary, with thickness T.</p>
<p>Raised truncated dome: a side view and plan of one dome. Top diameter 0.45&quot; Min and 0.47&quot; Max. Base diameter 0.9&quot; Min and 0.92&quot; Max. Height 0.2&quot;.</p>
<p>Raised truncated dome pattern (in-line): domes placed in a square grid with 2.3&quot; Min and 2.4&quot; Max center to center spacing, Typ.</p>
<p>Detectable warning surface: see Note 10.</p>
<p>Gutter pan transition: a plan strip showing curb at the top, typical gutter pan applies to all cases at each end, a 3&#x27;-0&quot; Typ transition (see Note 9) on each side, and between them the gutter in front of the detectable warning surface (see Note 8), with pavement below.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the PDF text)</summary><p>1. As site conditions dictate, Case A through Case G curb ramps may be used for corner installations similar to those shown in Detail A and Detail B. The case of curb ramps used in Detail A do not have to be the same. Case A through Case G curb ramps also may be used at mid block locations, as site conditions dictate. For specific site condition configuration, including the conform to existing sidewalk, see Project Plans.</p>
<p>2. If distance from curb to back of sidewalk is too short to accommodate ramp and 4&#x27;-2&quot; platform (landing) as shown in Case A, the sidewalk may be depressed longitudinally as in Case B or C or may be widened as in Case D.</p>
<p>3. When ramp is located in center of curb return, crosswalk configuration must be similar to that shown for Detail B.</p>
<p>4. As site conditions dictate, the retaining curb side and the flared side of the Case G ramp shall be constructed in reversed position.</p>
<p>5. The ramp portion of the curb ramp is a typical rectangle, unless modified in the Project Plans.</p>
<p>6. Side slope of ramp flares vary uniformly from a maximum of 9.0% at curb to conform with longitudinal sidewalk slope adjacent to top of the ramp, except in Case C and Case F.</p>
<p>7. The adjacent surfaces at transitions at curb ramps to walks, gutters, and streets shall be at the same level.</p>
<p>8. Counter slopes of adjoining gutters and road surfaces immediately adjacent to and within 24 inches of the curb ramp shall not be steeper than 1V:20H (5.0%). Gutter pan slope shall not exceed 1&quot; of depth for each 2&#x27;-0&quot; of width.</p>
<p>9. Transition gutter pan slope from 1&quot; of depth for each 2&#x27;-0&quot; of width to match typical gutter pan slope per Standard Plan A87A.</p>
<p>10. The detectable warning surface will be a rectangle as shown at back of curb, unless modified in the Project Plans. Curb ramps shall have a detectable warning surface that extends the full width and 3&#x27;-0&quot; depth of the ramp. Detectable warning surfaces shall extend the full width of the ramp except a maximum gap of 1 inch is allowed on each side of the ramp.</p>
<p>11. Sidewalk and ramp thickness &quot;T&quot;, shall be 3 1/2&quot; minimum.</p>
<p>12. Utility pull boxes, manholes, vaults and all other utility facilities within the boundaries of the curb ramp will be relocated or adjusted to grade by the owner prior to, or in conjunction with, curb ramp construction.</p>
<p>13. Detectable warning surface may have to be cut to allow removal of utility covers while maintaining detectable warning width and depth.</p></details>
