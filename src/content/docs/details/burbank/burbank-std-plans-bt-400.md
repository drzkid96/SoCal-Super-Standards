<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-400 shows how to lay out circulation driveways and aisle geometry for angled parking in a private lot. Minimum driveway width is 13' for one-way traffic with parking angles of 45 to 90 degrees and 16' for 30 to 44 degrees, with 10' added for two-way traffic. It also gives an R=20' turning radius access driveway detail and rules for posts or obstructions beside stalls.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Circulation driveways for various parking angles (plan view): A large boxed plan shows a one-way circulation driveway between rows of angled parking stalls, with a dash-dot driveway centerline running left to right. A PROPERTY LINE is labeled along the top edge. Large outline arrows at the top (pointing left) and bottom (pointing right) and a down arrow at the left entry show traffic direction through the aisle. A rotated stall bay is drawn on both sides of the centerline with the PARKING ANGLE measured from the centerline to the stall edge. An R=20&#x27; dimension line is drawn across the stall bay at the turning arc at the left end. On the left side, a leader marked with an asterisk reads DRIVEWAY with arrows to the edges of the driveway. A vertical dimension line on the right side, running from the property line to the bottom edge of the box, is labeled NOT LESS THAN BAY WIDTHS SHOWN ON ATTACHED TABLES. Three asterisk notes at left give the driveway widths (see notes).</p>
<p>Obstructions adjoining stalls (plan detail): A single stall is drawn on its side with the aisle labeled AISLE at the right and the BASIC STALL WIDTH dimension on the left. A triangle inside the stall shows the stall outline. Cross-hatched obstruction areas are drawn along the long sides of the stall, set back from the aisle end. The dimensions along the stall are 4&#x27;, 8&#x27;, 4&#x27; and 2&#x27; in sequence: the first hatched block spans 8&#x27; between 4&#x27; offsets, and a second smaller hatched block (post or obstruction near the aisle end) follows a 2&#x27; dimension. The same hatched blocks appear on both sides of the stall.</p>
<p>Circulation driveways (plan detail): A boxed plan of an ACCESS DRIVEWAY entering from the top. The driveway is 10&#x27;-0&quot; wide at the property line with a 10&#x27; MIN dimension at left. The driveway edge on the right flares into a curve of R=20&#x27; made of two 90 degree arcs (a 90 degree angle is marked at each end of the radial lines). Vertical dimensions on the left are 31&#x27;-7&quot; and 37&#x27;-8&quot; measured from the top. On the right, a 12&#x27;-0&quot; dimension along the top, a 12&#x27;-0&quot; dimension to the right of the curve, a 10&#x27;-0&quot; vertical dimension, and a 10&#x27; MIN horizontal dimension at the lower right. Horizontal dimensions along the bottom are 31&#x27;-7&quot; and 37&#x27;-8&quot;. A small note at the lower left states to add 9&#x27; to the drive for two-way traffic or more than 20 cars in &quot;C&quot; zone or 40 cars in &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Driveway and Turning Areas. Standard Plan BT-400, Sheet 1 of 1. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 6-1-92.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Circulation driveways for various parking angles:</h5>
<p>1. Driveway = 13&#x27; minimum (MIN.) for one-way traffic and parking angles of from 90 degrees to 45 degrees.</p>
<p>2. Driveway = 16&#x27; MIN. for one-way traffic and parking angles of from 30 degrees to 44 degrees.</p>
<p>3. The driveway dimension shall be increased by 10&#x27;-0&quot; for two-way traffic.</p>
<p>4. Not less than bay widths shown on attached tables.</p>
<h5>Notes (obstructions adjoining stalls):</h5>
<p>1. Standard space shall be a minimum of 10&#x27; wide if post or obstruction is in cross-hatched area on either one or both sides.</p>
<p>2. End stalls shall be a minimum of 11&#x27; wide or the access aisle must extend 3&#x27; beyond the bay.</p>
<p>3. For compact spaces, the minimums shall be 1&#x27; less than the above requirements.</p>
<p>4. (See sheet 10 for angles of spaces less than 75 degrees.)</p>
<h5>Circulation driveways note:</h5>
<p>Add 9&#x27; to drive for two-way traffic or more than 20 cars in &quot;C&quot; zone or 40 cars in &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone.</p></details>
