<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>Circulation driveways for various parking angles:</h5>
<p>1. Driveway = 13&#x27; minimum (MIN.) for one-way traffic and parking angles of from 90 degrees to 45 degrees.</p>
<p>2. Driveway = 16&#x27; MIN. for one-way traffic and parking angles of from 30 degrees to 44 degrees.</p>
<p>3. The driveway dimension shall be increased by 10&#x27;-0&quot; for two-way traffic.</p>
<p>4. Not less than bay widths shown on attached tables.</p>
<h5>Notes (obstructions adjoining stalls):</h5>
<p>1. Standard space shall be a minimum of 10&#x27; wide if post or obstruction is in cross-hatched area on either one or both sides.</p>
<p>2. End stalls shall be a minimum of 11&#x27; wide or the access aisle must extend 3&#x27; beyond the bay.</p>
<p>3. For compact spaces, the minimums shall be 1&#x27; less than the above requirements.</p>
<p>4. (See sheet 10 for angles of spaces less than 75 degrees.)</p>
<h5>Circulation driveways note:</h5>
<p>Add 9&#x27; to drive for two-way traffic or more than 20 cars in &quot;C&quot; zone or 40 cars in &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone.</p></details>
