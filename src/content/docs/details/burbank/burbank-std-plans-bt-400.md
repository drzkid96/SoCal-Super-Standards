<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Circulation driveways for various parking angles</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Driveway = 13&#x27; minimum (MIN.) for one-way traffic and parking angles of from 90 degrees to 45 degrees.</li>
<li value="2"><strong>Driveway = 16&#x27; MIN.</strong> for one-way traffic and parking angles of from 30 degrees to 44 degrees.</li>
<li value="3">The driveway dimension shall be increased by 10&#x27;-0&quot; for two-way traffic.</li>
<li value="4">Not less than bay widths shown on attached tables.</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes (obstructions adjoining stalls)</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Standard space shall be a minimum of 10&#x27; wide if post or obstruction is in cross-hatched area on either one or both sides.</li>
<li value="2">End stalls shall be a minimum of 11&#x27; wide or the access aisle must extend 3&#x27; beyond the bay.</li>
<li value="3">For compact spaces, the minimums shall be 1&#x27; less than the above requirements.</li>
<li value="4">(See sheet 10 for angles of spaces less than 75 degrees.)</li>
</ol>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Circulation driveways note</h5>
<p>Add 9&#x27; to drive for two-way traffic or more than 20 cars in &quot;C&quot; zone or 40 cars in &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone.</p>
</details>
