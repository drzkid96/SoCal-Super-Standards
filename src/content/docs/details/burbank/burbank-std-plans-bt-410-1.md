<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows where to place left and right turn arrows on approaches to uncontrolled, signalized and stop-controlled intersections. Two arrows go 25' from the end of stripe at a two-way left turn lane, and one arrow goes 8' from the start of a turn lane, with an added arrow if the turn lane is longer than 60'. Arrows use Caltrans Standard Plan A24A Type IV(L) and IV(R).</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>PAVEMENT TURN ARROWS</h5>
<p>Shows arrow placement for uncontrolled intersection, signalized intersection and stop controlled intersection, with stop line and &quot;STOP&quot; marking.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Intersection with two-way left turn lane, install two arrows 25 feet from end of stripe.</p>
<p>2. Intersection with turn lane, install one arrow 8 feet from beginning of turn lane.</p>
<p>3. If a turn lane is longer than 60 feet, install one additional arrow 8 feet from:</p>
<p>- End of stripe (uncontrolled intersection)</p>
<p>- Stop line (signalized intersection)</p>
<p>- &quot;STOP&quot; marking (stop controlled intersection)</p>
<p>Left turn lane shall use Caltrans Standard Plan A24A, Type IV(L) arrow.</p>
<p>Right turn lane shall use Caltrans Standard Plan A24A, Type IV(R) arrow.</p>
<p>Crosswalks shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-409A-1.</p>
<p>Stop lines and stop markings shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-411-1.</p></details>
