<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-410-1 shows where to place pavement turn arrows at uncontrolled, signalized and stop controlled intersections. Typical placement is one arrow 8 feet from the start of a turn lane, two arrows 25 feet from the end of stripe for a two-way left turn lane, and an additional arrow 8 feet from the end of stripe, stop line or STOP marking when the turn lane is longer than 60 feet. Arrows are Caltrans A24A Type IV(L) and IV(R).</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Five plan views of an intersection approach with curb returns, each titled and underlined, show where pavement turn arrows are placed. Numbered boxes tie the arrows to the notes.</p>
<p>Uncontrolled intersection, two-way left turn lane (upper left): A street with curbs, a double-line median stripe pair and a two-way left turn lane between them. Two left-turn arrows are drawn in the turn lane, pointing opposite ways. Box 1 points to the upper arrow. A 25&#x27; dimension runs from the end of the stripe at the top down to the upper arrow, and an 8&#x27; dimension is shown between the two arrows.</p>
<p>Uncontrolled intersection, turn lanes (upper middle): A street with a dashed centerline, a left-turn lane at left with a left-turn arrow, and a right-turn lane at the right with a right-turn arrow. Box 2 points to both arrows. An 8&#x27; dimension at the right is measured from the beginning of the right turn lane to the arrow.</p>
<p>Uncontrolled intersection, one turn lane (lower left): A street with a dashed centerline and a single left-turn lane at the left with one left-turn arrow. Box 3 points to the arrow. An 8&#x27; dimension is measured from the end of the stripe (top of the lane) to the arrow.</p>
<p>Signalized intersection (lower middle): The approach ends at a crosswalk of parallel bars and a STOP LINE across the approach lanes. A left-turn arrow in the left turn lane is dimensioned 8&#x27; from the stop line, with box 3 pointing to it. A small circle at the right of the stop line marks a signal or push button.</p>
<p>Stop controlled intersection (lower right): The approach has a crosswalk of parallel bars, a stop line, and the word STOP marked in the right lane. A left-turn arrow in the left turn lane is dimensioned 8&#x27; from the STOP marking, with box 3 pointing to it.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Intersection with two-way left turn lane, install two arrows 25 feet from end of stripe.</p>
<p>2. Intersection with turn lane, install one arrow 8 feet from beginning of turn lane.</p>
<p>3. If a turn lane is longer than 60 feet, install one additional arrow 8 feet from:</p>
<p>- End of stripe (uncontrolled intersection)</p>
<p>- Stop line (signalized intersection)</p>
<p>- &quot;STOP&quot; marking (stop controlled intersection)</p>
<p>Left turn lane shall use Caltrans Standard Plan A24A, Type IV(L) arrow.</p>
<p>Right turn lane shall use Caltrans Standard Plan A24A, Type IV(R) arrow.</p>
<p>Crosswalks shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-409A-1.</p>
<p>Stop lines and stop markings shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-411-1.</p></details>
