<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows plan, signing, markings and construction notes for a 3.0 inch speed cushion that leaves a gap at the centerline. Cushions are 12' long and spaced 250' to 300' apart, with BUMP legends and a 6' chevron in 12" solid white thermoplastic. The advance sign is a 30"x30" <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> SPEED BUMPS AHEAD with an 18"x18" W13-1 (15) plaque, placed about 100' to 125' ahead.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 22)

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>STANDARD 3.0&quot; SPEED CUSHION</h5>
<p>Plan: install <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20A at the centerline. Cushion length 12&#x27;, 50&#x27; to BUMP legend, 20&#x27; typ. each side of centerline gap. Cushions spaced 250&#x27; to 300&#x27;. Advance sign +/-100&#x27;-125&#x27; ahead of first cushion. Roadway width varies. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> and W13-1 signs at each end.</p>
<p>Advance sign to be placed ahead of first speed cushion from each direction: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> 30&quot;x30&quot;, W13-1(15) 18&quot;x18&quot; (15 MPH). Not to scale.</p>
<p>Marking <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-1" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-1.svg">Detail 1</a>: 12&quot; solid white chevron, 6&#x27; wide by 6&#x27; high, with 16&quot; and 12&quot; dimensions.</p>
<p>Marking <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-2" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-2.svg">Detail 2</a>: BUMP legend, 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide by 8&#x27; high, 4&quot; and 8&quot; dimensions.</p>
<h5>Construction notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install marking per Marking <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-1" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-1.svg">Detail 1</a> and sheet 2.</p>
<p>2. Install &quot;BUMP&quot; per Marking <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-2" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-2.svg">Detail 2</a>.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Speed cushions shall not be placed over utility manholes, water valves, or other subsurface utilities access features.</p>
<p>2. Edge of speed cushion shall be minimum 5 feet from driveway apron.</p>
<p>3. Whenever possible, speed cushions shall be installed near street lights.</p>
<p>4. Finish grade of asphalt may vary by 1/4&quot; (higher or lower).</p>
<p>5. All markings shall be thermoplastic.</p>
<p>6. All signs shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-412-1.</p></details>
