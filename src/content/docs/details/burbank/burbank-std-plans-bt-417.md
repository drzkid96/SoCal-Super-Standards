<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows plan, signing, markings and construction notes for a 3.0 inch speed cushion that leaves a gap at the centerline. Cushions are 12' long and spaced 250' to 300' apart, with BUMP legends and a 6' chevron in 12" solid white thermoplastic. The advance sign is a 30"x30" <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> SPEED BUMPS AHEAD with an 18"x18" W13-1 (15) plaque, placed about 100' to 125' ahead.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 22)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0022.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0022.webp" alt="BT-417 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans BT-417, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/burbank/standard-plans/01-pages-1-27/#p22">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: a two-lane street shown with a speed cushion set at two locations along the street, 250&#x27; to 300&#x27; apart. At each location, one speed cushion is placed in each travel lane, with a narrower hatched center hump straddling the centerline (CL) between them, so each set reads as three pieces across the street. Each cushion is 12&#x27; wide along the street and has a white chevron (marking detail 1, callout 1) and the word BUMP (marking detail 2, callout 2) painted ahead of it in each lane. Dimensions: 12&#x27; cushion width, 50&#x27; from the cushion to the BUMP legend, 250&#x27; to 300&#x27; between sets, 20&#x27; (TYP.) of double yellow centerline extending beyond each side of the cushion set, and road width marked VARIES. The double yellow centerline uses <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20A. A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> with W13-1 sign assembly is shown at the curb about 100&#x27;-125&#x27; (+/-) before the first cushion. Break lines show the street continues.</p>
<p>Advance signs (not to scale): a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> SPEED BUMPS AHEAD diamond warning sign, 30&quot;x30&quot;, mounted above a W13-1(15) 15 MPH advisory speed plaque, 18&quot;x18&quot;, on a post. Note beside the sign: advance sign to be placed ahead of the first speed cushion from each direction.</p>
<p>Marking detail 1: a white chevron 6&#x27; wide and 6&#x27; tall, with a 12&quot; solid white stripe, an inner chevron triangle, a 16&quot; dimension at the base and a 12&quot; leg width noted.</p>
<p>Marking detail 2: the legend BUMP drawn on a grid, 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide and 8&#x27; tall, with 4&quot; grid spacing across and 8&quot; grid spacing vertically.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department, Standard 3.0&quot; Speed Cushion, Standard Plan BT-417, sheet 1 of 2. Approved by the City Engineer, dated 7/23/25.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Construction notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install marking per marking detail 1 and sheet 2.</p>
<p>2. Install &quot;BUMP&quot; per marking detail 2.</p>
<h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Speed cushions shall not be placed over utility manholes, water valves, or other subsurface utilities access features.</p>
<p>2. Edge of speed cushion shall be minimum 5 feet from driveway apron.</p>
<p>3. Whenever possible, speed cushions shall be installed near street lights.</p>
<p>4. Finish grade of asphalt may vary by 1/4&quot; (higher or lower).</p>
<p>5. All markings shall be thermoplastic.</p>
<p>6. All signs shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-412-1.</p>
<p>Sign callouts: Install <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20A. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> and W13-1 sign assembly. Advance sign to be placed ahead of first speed cushion from each direction. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w84ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/w84ca.webp">W84(CA)</a> 30&quot;x30&quot;. W13-1(15) 18&quot;x18&quot;. Marking detail 1: 12&quot; solid white.</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 23)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0023.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0023.webp" alt="BT-417 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans BT-417, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/burbank/standard-plans/01-pages-1-27/#p23">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: one speed cushion shown across the full roadway width from sidewalk and gutter on the left to gutter and sidewalk on the right, with the street centerline (CL STREET) at the middle. The cushion is made of a left lane piece, a center piece on the centerline, and a right lane piece, each 12&#x27; long along the street (the 12&#x27; dimension at left). The lane pieces are separated from the 6&#x27; wide center piece by a 1.5&#x27; gap on each side. A 1&#x27; taper is shown at the curb-side end of each lane piece, with the edge of taper dashed. The cushion centerline is marked, and Section A-A cut runs along the cushion centerline from the left curb across the street. Section B-B is cut across the cushion at its left end. Each cushion has a white chevron marking (callout 1, per sheet 1 marking detail 1). A double yellow centerline pair, 6&#x27; overall with a 20&#x27; (TYP.) extension, is shown at the center with <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20A. Overall dimension is the width of roadway.</p>
<p>Speed cushion cross-section A-A (not to scale): a section across the street from existing curb and gutter on the left to the sidewalk on the right. Existing AC pavement is overlaid with A.C. standard 3/8&quot; mix D2-AR-4000 over a tack coat. The cross-section shows 1&#x27; taper at the curb, a 1.5&#x27; gap, a 6&#x27; wide center piece with a 4&#x27; dimension at its crown and a 1.5&#x27; gap on the other side, with the centerline of street at the middle.</p>
<p>Section B-B (not to scale): the longitudinal profile of one cushion, 12&#x27; long overall and 6&#x27; from the end to the cushion centerline. The profile is a parabolic hump with a height of 3.0&quot; at the cushion centerline. Heights above pavement from the end at 0&quot; are 0.50&quot;, 0.92&quot;, 1.31&quot;, 1.67&quot;, 1.98&quot;, 2.25&quot;, 2.48&quot;, 2.67&quot;, 2.81&quot;, 2.92&quot; and 2.98&quot;, reaching 3.0&quot; high at the center, and symmetrical on the far side.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department, Standard 3.0&quot; Speed Cushion, Standard Plan BT-417, sheet 2 of 2. Approved by the City Engineer, dated 7/23/25.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Install marking per marking detail 1 (callout 1, edge of taper shown).</p>
<p>Install <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20A.</p>
<p>Existing curb and gutter. Existing AC pavement. A.C. standard 3/8&quot; mix D2-AR-4000. Apply tack coat.</p>
<p>Section B-B and Section A-A are not to scale.</p>
<p>See sheet 1 for construction notes and general notes.</p></details>
