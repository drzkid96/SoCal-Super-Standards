<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows saw-cut inductive loop layouts for bicycle detection, with Case 1 octagonal loops for bicycle lanes and Case 2 circular loops for vehicle lanes. Includes winding detail (twisted clockwise, at least 2 turns per foot), layout dimensions, pullbox connection and saw-cut sections. Saw-cut depth is 3.5 to 4 inches.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>BICYCLE DETECTOR</h5>
<p>Case 1 and Case 2</p>
<p>Winding detail: twisted clockwise, at least 2 turns per foot (typ.). To pullbox (typ.), conductors 1 2 3 4.</p>
<p>Layout detail: loops placed with 3&#x27; and 1&#x27; dimensions from the stopline and curb face for Case 1, and 6&#x27;, 10&#x27;, 6&#x27;, 1&#x27; and 2&#x27; from the lane line for Case 2. Pullbox at curb face, gutter, stopline, lane line.</p>
<p>Sections A-A through E-E show saw cut slots with 1/8&quot; max (typ.).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Curb termination shall be Type A per Caltrans Standard Plan ES-5D.</p>
<p>2. Sawcut depth shall be 3.5 inches to 4 inches.</p>
<p>3. Sawcut width shall be 1/4 inch to 3/8 inch.</p>
<p>4. Sawcut width shall be 1/2 inch to 5/8 inch.</p>
<p>Use Case 1 for bicycle lanes. Use Case 2 for vehicle lanes.</p>
<p>Loops shall be centered in lane, unless otherwise noted.</p></details>
