<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows saw-cut inductive loop layouts for bicycle detection. Case 1 uses octagonal loops for bicycle lanes and Case 2 uses circular loops for vehicle lanes. Each case gives the winding detail (twisted clockwise, at least 2 turns per foot), loop spacing from the stopline, lane line and curb face, and saw-cut sections A-A through E-E. Saw-cut depth is 3.5 to 4 inches.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 20)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Case 1 winding detail: two octagonal inductive loops side by side, each wound with four turns of wire. Arrows on the wire show the winding direction. The lead-in from each loop is twisted clockwise, at least 2 turns per foot (TYP.), and runs down to the pullbox with four conductor ends labeled 1, 2, 3 and 4 at the bottom of each twisted pair.</p>
<p>Case 1 layout detail (bicycle lane): the two octagonal loops are drawn between the curb face and the stopline. Each loop is 3&#x27; wide, made up of three 1&#x27; segments, and the two loops are 3&#x27; apart. A 1&#x27; dimension is shown between the right loop and the stopline. Each loop is also dimensioned as three 1&#x27; segments vertically along its right side. The loop lead-in cuts (marked D-D) angle down from the loops and join at a single slot (marked E-E) that runs to the pullbox at the curb face. The lead-in start point is 3&#x27; from the stopline measured along the gutter. Callout 1 points at the curb termination where the slot crosses the curb face to the pullbox. Section cut markers A-A are shown at each loop, D-D on each angled lead-in and E-E on the common lead-in. Labels: stopline, gutter, curb face, pullbox.</p>
<p>Case 2 winding detail: two circular loops with three turns each. The left loop is a simple three-turn loop. The right loop has a three-turn circular winding with a diagonal slot cut across it carrying additional wire runs. Lead-ins are twisted as for Case 1 and end at conductors 1, 2, 3 and 4.</p>
<p>Case 2 layout detail (vehicle lane): two circular loops placed from the lane line. The dimensions along the lane line are 6&#x27; (left loop), 10&#x27; (gap between the loops), 6&#x27; (right loop) and 1&#x27; (to the stopline). The loops sit 2&#x27; below the lane line. Section cut C-C is at the left loop, A-A and B-B at the right loop, D-D on the angled lead-ins and E-E on the common lead-in that runs to the pullbox at the curb face. Callout 1 points at the curb termination. Labels: lane line, stopline, gutter, curb face, pullbox.</p>
<p>Saw-cut sections (all drawn as hatched slots with circular wire cross-sections and a maximum 1/8 inch (1/8&quot; MAX, TYP.) fill below the pavement surface): Section A-A shows one column of 4 wires and uses note 3 for width and note 2 for depth (TYP.). Section B-B shows two columns of 4 wires (8 wires) in a wider slot and uses note 4 for width. Section C-C shows 3 wires and uses note 3. Section D-D shows 2 wires and uses note 3. Section E-E shows 4 wires in a column in a wider slot and uses note 4.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department, Bicycle Detector, Standard Plan BT-415-1, sheet 1 of 1. Signed by the Public Works Director and stamped by a registered professional engineer (electrical), dated 11/14/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Curb termination shall be Type A per Caltrans Standard Plan ES-5D.</p>
<p>2. Sawcut depth shall be 3.5 inches to 4 inches.</p>
<p>3. Sawcut width shall be 1/4 inch to 3/8 inch.</p>
<p>4. Sawcut width shall be 1/2 inch to 5/8 inch.</p>
<p>Use Case 1 for bicycle lanes. Use Case 2 for vehicle lanes.</p>
<p>Loops shall be centered in lane, unless otherwise noted.</p>
<p>Winding detail callouts: twisted clockwise, at least 2 turns per foot (TYP.). To pullbox (TYP.).</p>
<p>Section callout: 1/8 inch maximum (MAX.) (TYP.).</p></details>
