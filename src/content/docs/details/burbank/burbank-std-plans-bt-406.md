<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<p>Case 1: garage above street level (no scale). Garage setback minimum (MIN.) 16.5&#x27; from curb face. Top of curb (TC) level, garage floor level, 20% slope, sidewalk slope 1/4&quot; per 1&#x27;. Maximum (MAX.) rise 1.26&#x27; + 0.24&#x27; (see table).</p>
<p>Case 2: garage below street level (no scale). Garage setback MIN. 20.5&#x27; from curb face. Maximum fall 0.38&#x27; + 0.28&#x27; (see table).</p>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">The distances and vertical curves shown hereon are the minimum which can be used with a minimum tangent slope of 20% without causing cars to drag on the paved surfaces.</li>
<li value="2">The sidewalk slope of 1/4&quot; per 1&#x27; shall be carried from the curb to the beginning of the vertical curve for Case 1 and Case 2.</li>
<li value="3">If the back of the existing sidewalk is more than 5&#x27; from the curb face in Case 1, or more than 11.5&#x27; from the curb face in Case 2, the vertical curve shall begin at the back of the existing sidewalk, and the minimum garage setback increased accordingly to provide desired rise or fall to garage level.</li>
<li value="4">Where existing conditions make this profile inapplicable, the applicant shall file a plan and profile of the proposed driveway at a scale of 1&quot; = 2&#x27; with the Traffic Engineer for approval.</li>
<li value="5">All driveways which do not conform to City Standard must be processed by special permit. Request for said permit is made with the Traffic Engineer for checking and approval.</li>
</ol>
</details>
