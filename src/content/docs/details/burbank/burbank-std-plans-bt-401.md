<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-401 gives two charts relating basic stall width to the minimum access aisle width, one for standard cars (8'-6" to 9'-4" stalls with 27'-4" to 24'-0" aisles) and one for compact cars (7'-6" to 8'-4" stalls with 25'-4" to 22'-0" aisles). It also lists special requirements for driveway widths, driveway slopes and ramps, and parking stall location.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Chart No. 1: A boxed table titled STANDARD SIZE AUTOMOBILE SPACES ADJOINING ACCESS AISLE with columns BASIC STALL WIDTH and ACCESS AISLE WIDTH. Rows: 8&#x27;-6&quot; with 27&#x27;-4&quot;; 8&#x27;-8&quot; with 26&#x27;-8&quot;; 9&#x27;-0&quot; with 25&#x27;-4&quot;; 9&#x27;-4&quot; with 24&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>Chart No. 2: A boxed table titled COMPACT AUTOMOBILE SPACES ADJOINING ACCESS AISLE with columns BASIC STALL WIDTH and ACCESS AISLE WIDTH. Rows: 7&#x27;-6&quot; with 25&#x27;-4&quot;; 7&#x27;-10&quot; with 24&#x27;-0&quot;; 8&#x27;-2&quot; with 22&#x27;-8&quot;; 8&#x27;-4&quot; with 22&#x27;-0&quot;.</p>
<p>A sentence below the charts says that where parking adjoining the access aisle is a mix of standard and compact spaces, Chart No. 1 governs the minimum access aisle width.</p>
<p>Special requirements: The rest of the sheet is text in three groups, Driveway widths and locations, Driveway slopes and ramps (see sheet 11), and Parking stall location, with lettered items as transcribed in the notes. There are no drawn views.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Allowable Standard and Compact Parking Width Dimension. Standard Plan BT-401, Sheet 1 of 1. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 6-1-92.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Under the charts: Where parking adjoining the access aisle is a combination of standard size and compact size automobile spaces, Chart No. 1 shall be used for determination of minimum access aisle width.</p>
<p>Special requirements.</p>
<h5>Driveway widths and locations:</h5>
<p>A. 10&#x27; minimum in all zones.</p>
<p>B. 19&#x27; minimum when serving more than 40 cars in an &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone, or more than 20 cars in a &quot;C&quot; zone, or in lieu thereof there shall be two 10&#x27; minimum access driveways. Traffic Division approval for the location and size of driveways shall be obtained for every parking area or garage.</p>
<h5>Driveway slopes and ramps (see sheet 11):</h5>
<p>A. 20% maximum slope on driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>B. 10% maximum cross slope of a driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>C. 5% maximum slope in any direction in a parking stall.</p>
<p>D. Transition slopes are required when the slope of the driveway or ramp exceeds 10%. There shall be a maximum change in gradient of 10% in any 10 foot section of the driveway including the driveway approach within the street right of way, except that on a driveway sloping up from the property line the gradient within the first 5 feet from the property line shall not exceed 10%.</p>
<h5>Parking stall location:</h5>
<p>A. No parking stall is to be located so that an automobile is required to back onto a major or secondary highway.</p>
<p>B. No automobile is allowed to back from any lot onto a street unless the parking area or garage and the driveway leading to this parking serves no more than two dwelling units.</p></details>
