<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Gives minimum access aisle widths for standard and compact parking stalls and lists special requirements for parking facilities. Also covers driveway widths, maximum driveway slopes (20 percent) and cross slopes (10 percent), and where parking stalls may not back onto streets.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>ALLOWABLE STANDARD AND COMPACT PARKING WIDTH DIMENSION</h5>
<p>Chart No. 1, standard size automobile spaces adjoining access aisle (basic stall width, access aisle width):</p>
<p>8&#x27;-6&quot; | 27&#x27;-4&quot;</p>
<p>8&#x27;-8&quot; | 26&#x27;-8&quot;</p>
<p>9&#x27;-0&quot; | 25&#x27;-4&quot;</p>
<p>9&#x27;-4&quot; | 24&#x27;-0&quot;</p>
<p>Chart No. 2, compact automobile spaces adjoining access aisle (basic stall width, access aisle width):</p>
<p>7&#x27;-6&quot; | 25&#x27;-4&quot;</p>
<p>7&#x27;-10&quot; | 24&#x27;-0&quot;</p>
<p>8&#x27;-2&quot; | 22&#x27;-8&quot;</p>
<p>8&#x27;-4&quot; | 22&#x27;-0&quot;</p>
<p>Where parking adjoining the access aisle is a combination of standard size and compact size automobile spaces, Chart No. 1 shall be used for determination of minimum access aisle width.</p>
<h5>SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS</h5>
<p>Driveway widths and locations</p>
<p>A. 10&#x27; minimum in all zones.</p>
<p>B. 19&#x27; minimum when serving more than 40 cars in an &quot;R&quot; or &quot;M&quot; zone, or more than 20 cars in a &quot;C&quot; zone, or in lieu thereof there shall be two 10&#x27; minimum access driveways. Traffic Division approval for the location and size of driveways shall be obtained for every parking area or garage.</p>
<p>Driveway slopes and ramps (see sheet 11)</p>
<p>A. 20% maximum slope on driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>B. 10% maximum cross slope of a driveway or ramp.</p>
<p>C. 5% maximum slope in any direction in a parking stall.</p>
<p>D. Transition slopes are required when the slope of the driveway or ramp exceeds 10%.</p>
<p>There shall be a maximum change in gradient of 10% in any 10 foot section of the driveway including the driveway approach within the street right of way, except that on a driveway sloping up from the property line the gradient within the first 5 feet from the property line shall not exceed 10%.</p>
<p>Parking stall location</p>
<p>A. No parking stall is to be located so that an automobile is required to back onto a major or secondary highway.</p>
<p>B. No automobile is allowed to back from any lot onto a street unless the parking area or garage and the driveway leading to this parking serves no more than two dwelling units.</p></details>
