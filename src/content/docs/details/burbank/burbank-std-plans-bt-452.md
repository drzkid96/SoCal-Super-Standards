<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">A guide for deciding whether a proposed dwelling addition leaves adequate access to a garage. The driveway must be at least 10 feet wide, and construction cannot extend past a line that starts 10 feet minimum back from the garage door and angles at a 7:10 ratio toward the street. That angled line preserves auto back-up space in front of the 16-foot garage door.</p>
