<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows a guide for checking whether a proposed dwelling addition leaves adequate access to an existing garage. The garage is 19' by 19' with a 16' door, and a clear auto back up space and a 10' minimum wide driveway must be kept. Construction must not extend past the 7:10 diagonal line.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (not to scale): guide for determining adequate access to a garage for a proposed dwelling addition. The street is at the bottom, with its centerline-style dashed line and a property line (PL) running along the street frontage, and a side property line on the left and right. The existing dwelling is drawn in hatching above the street, with a proposed addition (labeled PROPOSED ADDITION) attached to its upper right. A garage sits at the upper right of the lot, drawn as a 19&#x27; by 19&#x27; interior space with a 16&#x27; garage door opening facing the driveway. The garage wall is 3&#x27; from the right side property line. A driveway runs from the street up along the right side of the lot, with 10&#x27; MIN. width dimensioned. The auto back up space is a triangular area in front of the garage door that is bounded by the garage, the proposed addition and the driveway, with the diagonal edge labeled at an angle of 7:10 ratio (7&#x27; horizontally by 10&#x27; vertically). A 10&#x27; MIN. dimension is shown from the front of the garage to the proposed addition along the left side, and 10&#x27; is shown vertically on the diagonal. A circled note says: Construction shall not extend beyond this line, pointing at the diagonal line.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department, Residential Driveway Access Guidelines, Standard Plan BT-452, one sheet 1 of 1. Revision 1 by EF dated 08/14/06. Approved by the Public Works Director (unsigned).</p></details>
