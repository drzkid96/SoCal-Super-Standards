<p class="dwg-sum">Shows pavement marking layouts for Class II bike lanes with and without parking and with and without a buffer, plus Class III shared roadway markings. Bike lane symbols and arrows are placed 6' from the end of curb return, with 100' dashed lines at intersections. Class III markings use a table of dimensions A and B based on lane width.</p>
