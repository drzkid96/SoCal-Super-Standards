<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows pavement marking layouts for Class II bike lanes with and without parking and with and without a buffer, plus Class III shared roadway markings. Bike lane symbols and arrows are placed 6' from the end of curb return, with 100' dashed lines at intersections. Class III markings use a table of dimensions A and B based on lane width.</p>

<p>Class II lane with buffer and parking.</p>

<p>Each layout shows ECR and BCR, markings 6&#x27; from ECR, 100&#x27; dashed lines and 50&#x27; lengths at intersections, 1:1 buffer tapers, and shared roadway marking installed if greater than 250&#x27; from ECR.</p>
