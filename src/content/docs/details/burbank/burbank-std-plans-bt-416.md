<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows pavement marking layouts for Class II bike lanes with and without parking and with and without a buffer, plus Class III shared roadway markings. Symbols and arrows start 6' from the end of curb return, with 100' of dashed intersection line at the beginning of curb return. A table gives dimensions A and B for Class III markings by lane width.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 1 (document page 21)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, Class II lane with parking: a bike lane drawn between the curb-side parking line and the travel lane line, starting at the end of curb return (ECR) on the left and ending at the beginning of curb return (BCR) on the right. The bicycle lane symbol with person (callout 1) is placed 6&#x27; from the ECR. The bike lane arrow (callout 2) is placed a further 6&#x27; beyond the symbol. The bike lane line (callout 3) runs along the lane. Near the BCR a 100&#x27; length of bike lane intersection line (callout 4, dashed) is shown, and the right edgeline (callout 5) ends 50&#x27; before the BCR.</p>
<p>Plan view, Class II lane without parking: same arrangement with symbol 6&#x27; from the ECR, arrow 6&#x27; after the symbol, bike lane line (3), 100&#x27; of intersection line (4) at the BCR end. No separate right edgeline stub is shown.</p>
<p>Plan view, Class II lane with buffer and parking: the bike lane has a dashed edge on the buffer side. Symbol 6&#x27; from the ECR, arrow 6&#x27; after the symbol. The buffer edge line tapers to the lane line at the intersection at a 1:1 taper, with 50&#x27; dimensioned and 100&#x27; of intersection line (4) at the BCR. Callouts 4 and 5 are shown on the buffer lines.</p>
<p>Plan view, Class II lane with buffer and without parking: same as the buffer layout above, with symbol and arrow at 6&#x27; and 6&#x27; from the ECR, the 1:1 taper of the buffer lines, 100&#x27; of intersection line (4) and callout 5 on the buffer and edge lines.</p>
<p>Plan view, Class III lane (shared roadway): bicycle symbol with chevrons (callout 6) placed 6&#x27; from the ECR. Distance A is the lane width between the lane line and the curb-side line, and B is the offset of the marking from the curb-side line. A second symbol with chevrons is drawn toward the BCR end, labeled with callout 6 and the text install if greater than 250&#x27; from ECR. A 100&#x27; length of intersection line is dimensioned at the BCR end.</p>
<p>Table of dimensions A and B for Class III: A (lane width) 10&#x27; to 13&#x27; gives B 4&#x27;. A 14&#x27; to 16&#x27; gives B 1.5&#x27;. A 17&#x27; to 18&#x27; gives B 12&#x27;. A 19&#x27; to 20&#x27; gives B 13&#x27;. A 21&#x27; to 23&#x27; gives B 14&#x27;. A 24&#x27;+ gives B 11.5&#x27;.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department, Bicycle Markings, Standard Plan BT-416, sheet 1 of 1. Signed by the Public Works Director, stamped by a registered professional engineer (traffic), dated 11/14/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Bicycle lane symbol with person per Caltrans Std Plan A24C.</p>
<p>2. Bike lane arrow per Caltrans Std Plan A24A.</p>
<p>3. Bike lane line, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39.svg">Detail 39</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20D.</p>
<p>4. Bike lane intersection line, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-39a" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-39a.svg">Detail 39A</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20D.</p>
<p>5. Right edgeline, <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-27b" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-27b.svg">Detail 27B</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20B.</p>
<p>6. Shared roadway bicycle marking per Caltrans Std Plan A24C.</p>
<p>Install if greater than 250&#x27; from the end of curb return (ECR) (Class III symbol, callout 6).</p>
<p>Abbreviations on sheet: ECR is end of curb return and BCR is beginning of curb return.</p>
<p>Table: see dimensions A and B for Class III lane in the description.</p></details>
