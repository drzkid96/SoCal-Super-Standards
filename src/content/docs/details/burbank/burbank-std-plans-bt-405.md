<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-405 shows where columns may and may not be placed next to angled parking stalls. Cross-hatched zones along the shared stall line, based on a 4' offset and an 8' length on 18' by 9' stalls, are kept free of columns, and no column may sit behind any part of a space. Angles below 65 degrees and from 65 to 75 degrees follow the layouts shown, and steeper angles use the 90 degree standard.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view, angle below 65 degrees (upper left): A vertical wall line at the left with two angled stalls leaning down to the right from it, one above the other. A callout reading ANGLE BELOW 65 degrees TO BE AS SHOWN points to the upper stall. The shared stall line between the two stalls carries cross-hatched no-column zones. Dimensions along that shared line are 4&#x27; from the wall end to the first hatched zone, then 8&#x27; for the hatched length. A second hatched zone lies at the far (open) end of the shared line. The lower stall is dimensioned 18&#x27; along its length and 9&#x27; across its width.</p>
<p>Plan view, angle below 65 degrees (upper right): A mirror-image arrangement with the wall line at the right and the stalls leaning down to the left. A callout points to the upper stall with the same text. The shared stall line has a cross-hatched zone running along most of its length, starting 4&#x27; from the wall end.</p>
<p>Plan view, angle 65 degrees to 75 degrees (lower right): A third arrangement with the wall at the right and two flatter stalls leaning to the left. Callout at left reads ANGLE 65 degrees TO 75 degrees TO BE AS SHOWN, then ANY ANGLE GREATER TO USE 90 degrees STANDARD, then (SEE SHEET NO. 1). The shared stall line has two cross-hatched zones, one at the open end and one near the wall. Dimensions are 4&#x27; from the wall end, 8&#x27; for the hatched zone next to it, 18&#x27; stall length and 9&#x27; stall width.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Column Placement. Standard Plan BT-405, Sheet 1 of 1. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 6-1-92.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Column placement with angle parking:</h5>
<p>Angle below 65 degrees to be as shown.</p>
<p>Angle 65 degrees to 75 degrees to be as shown.</p>
<p>Any angle greater to use 90 degree standard. (See sheet no. 1.)</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. No columns to be placed in cross-hatched areas.</p>
<p>2. No columns to be placed to the rear of any portion of any space.</p></details>
