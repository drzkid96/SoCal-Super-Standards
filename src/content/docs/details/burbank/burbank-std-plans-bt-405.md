<p class="dwg-sum">Shows where structural columns may be placed next to angled parking stalls in garages. Cross-hatched zones along the stall sides are kept clear, with 4' and 8' dimensions shown on 18' stalls. Angles below 65 degrees and from 65 to 75 degrees follow the layouts shown, and any greater angle uses the 90 degree standard.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>COLUMN PLACEMENT WITH ANGLE PARKING</h5>
<p>Angle below 65 degrees to be as shown.</p>
<p>Angle 65 degrees to 75 degrees to be as shown.</p>
<p>Any angle greater to use 90 degree standard. (See sheet no. 1.)</p>
<p>Dimensions shown: 4&#x27;, 8&#x27; cross-hatched zones, 18&#x27; stall length, 9&#x27; stall width.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. No columns to be placed in cross-hatched areas.</p>
<p>2. No columns to be placed to the rear of any portion of any space.</p></details>
