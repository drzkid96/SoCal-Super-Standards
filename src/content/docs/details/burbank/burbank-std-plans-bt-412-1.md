<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-412-1 is a not-to-scale elevation of a standard sign post. A 2 inch square 12 gage post slips into a 2-1/4 inch square galvanized anchor sleeve, is fastened with rivets through 3/8 inch holes, and carries the sign at least 7 feet above the sidewalk, 6 inches minimum from the curb face and 36 inches minimum from the sidewalk edge. Signs use 0.063 inch aluminum with 3M prismatic sheeting.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign post elevation (not to scale): A vertical elevation of a street sign mounted on a post next to a sidewalk and curb. A rectangular sign with rounded corners and a heavy border is mounted at the top of a 2&quot;X2&quot; SQUARE POST (12 GAGE). Two small circles on the post at the top and bottom of the sign face are called out SECURE SIGN WITH DRIVE RIVET. A dimension labeled 7&#x27; MINIMUM runs from the sidewalk surface up to the bottom of the sign. A horizontal dimension 6&quot; MIN. runs from the edge of the sign to the face of the curb on the right.</p>
<p>The post stands 36&quot; MIN. from the EX. EDGE OF SIDEWALK (horizontal dimension to the left). At the base, the post passes into a galvanized anchor sleeve set in the ground through the existing sidewalk, with a hole near the top of the sleeve called out 3/8&quot; HOLES. INSERT SIGN POST AND FASTEN WITH RIVETS. A 4&quot; dimension is shown from the sidewalk surface to the hole, and a 2&#x27; dimension is shown from that same level to the bottom of the sleeve. The sidewalk is drawn in section as concrete with dots and a thickness shown by a vertical arrow. To the right is the EX. CONCRETE CURB AND GUTTER, and dashed lines with a break symbol show the EX. ASPHALT PAVING. The sleeve is labeled 2-1/4&quot; X 2-1/4&quot; SQUARE HEAVY DUTY GALVANIZED ANCHOR SLEEVE (TELESPAR OR EQUAL). A (NOT TO SCALE) label is below the detail.</p>
<p>Bottom text: A note on sign sheeting (transcribed in the notes).</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Sign Post. Standard Plan BT-412-1, Sheet 1 of 1. Review initials dated 11/8/19. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 11/14/19. A California registered professional engineer (traffic) stamp is shown, signed 11/8/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Callouts: 2&quot;x2&quot; square post (12 gage). 3/8&quot; holes, insert sign post and fasten with rivets. Secure sign with drive rivet. 7&#x27; minimum from sidewalk to bottom of sign. 6&quot; minimum (MIN.) from sign to curb face. 36&quot; MIN. from existing (EX.) edge of sidewalk. 4&quot; and 2&#x27; dimensions at the anchor sleeve. EX. concrete curb and gutter. EX. asphalt paving. 2-1/4&quot; x 2-1/4&quot; square heavy duty galvanized anchor sleeve (Telespar or equal). (Not to scale.)</p>
<p>All regulatory and warning signs shall be minimum 0.063 inch with 3M high intensity prismatic sheeting (Series #3930) with 3M premium protective overlay film (Series #1160).</p></details>
