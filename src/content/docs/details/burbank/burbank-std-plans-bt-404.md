<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-404 shows stripe layout and dimensions for standard and compact parking stalls, both angled and 90 degree. Standard stalls are 18' long with 7'-0" clear between stripes and compact stalls are 15' long with 6'-0" clear. Under B.M.C. 31-1417 stalls are double-striped with 4 inch stripes, white for standard cars and yellow (or white if marked COMPACT or SMALL CAR) for small cars.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Standard parking stalls, angled stall (plan view): Two angled stall-line pairs are drawn leaning to the right from a baseline. Each stall line is double-striped, drawn as two parallel strips joined by a closed end, to show the double stripe. The overall stall length along the stripe is dimensioned 18&#x27; Stall. Stall Width is dimensioned along the open end perpendicular to the stripes, and an X-shaped diagonal pair of lines marks the stall corners. The clear distance between the inner stripes is dimensioned 7&#x27;-0&quot; Clear. A vertical dimension at the right gives the stall depth perpendicular to the baseline, labeled 16&#x27;-0&quot; Min for 45 degrees and above and 15&#x27;-0&quot; Min for less than 45 degrees. The Parking Angle is shown as an arc between the baseline and the stripe.</p>
<p>Standard parking stalls, 90 degree stall (plan view): Two vertical double-striped stall lines with an X of diagonals between them. Stall Width is dimensioned across the top. 7&#x27;-0&quot; Clear is dimensioned between the inner stripes. Two vertical dimensions to the right read 16&#x27;-0&quot; Min (stripe length) and 18&#x27; Stall (overall stall length from the baseline). The view is captioned STANDARD PARKING STALLS, underlined.</p>
<p>Compact parking stalls, angled stall (plan view): The same layout at smaller size. The stall length is dimensioned 15&#x27; Stall, Stall Width is labeled, and the clear distance is 6&#x27;-0&quot; Clear. The vertical dimension reads 13&#x27;-0&quot; Min for 45 degrees and above and 12&#x27;-0&quot; Min for less than 45 degrees. A Parking Angle arc is shown.</p>
<p>Compact parking stalls, 90 degree stall (plan view): Two vertical double-striped stall lines with diagonals. Stall Width is labeled across the top, 6&#x27;-0&quot; Clear is dimensioned between inner stripes, and vertical dimensions read 13&#x27;-0&quot; Min and 15&#x27; Stall. The view is captioned COMPACT PARKING STALLS, underlined.</p>
<p>B.M.C. 31-1417 text block: A paragraph of code text on double striping, stripe colors and 4 inch width, transcribed in the notes.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Striping. Standard Plan BT-404, Sheet 1 of 1. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 6-1-92.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Standard parking stalls: 18&#x27; stall length. 7&#x27;-0&quot; clear between stripes. Stall length 16&#x27;-0&quot; minimum (MIN.) for 45 degrees and above, 15&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. for less than 45 degrees (angled stalls). 16&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. for 90 degree stalls.</p>
<p>Compact parking stalls: 15&#x27; stall length. 6&#x27;-0&quot; clear between stripes. Stall length 13&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. for 45 degrees and above, 12&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. for less than 45 degrees (angled stalls). 13&#x27;-0&quot; MIN. for 90 degree stalls.</p>
<p>Burbank Municipal Code (B.M.C.) 31-1417: Parking spaces shall be double-striped and access lanes clearly defined including directional arrows to guide internal movements. Striping for standard car spaces shall be white in color, whereas striping for small car spaces shall be yellow in color or may be painted white if marked &quot;COMPACT&quot; or &quot;SMALL CAR&quot;. All painted stripes shall be 4&quot; in width.</p></details>
