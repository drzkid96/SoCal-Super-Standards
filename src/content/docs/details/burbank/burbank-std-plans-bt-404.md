<p class="dwg-sum">Shows stripe layout and dimensions for standard and compact angled and 90 degree parking stalls under B.M.C. 31-1417. Standard stalls are 18' long with 7'-0" clear, and compact stalls are 15' long with 6'-0" clear. Spaces are double-striped with 4" stripes, white for standard cars and yellow for small cars, or white if marked COMPACT or SMALL CAR.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>STRIPING FOR PARKING STALLS</h5>
<p>Standard parking stalls</p>
<p>18&#x27; stall length. 7&#x27;-0&quot; clear between stripes. Stall length 16&#x27;-0&quot; min for 45 degrees and above, 15&#x27;-0&quot; min for less than 45 degrees (angled stalls). 16&#x27;-0&quot; min for 90 degree stalls.</p>
<p>Compact parking stalls</p>
<p>15&#x27; stall length. 6&#x27;-0&quot; clear between stripes. Stall length 13&#x27;-0&quot; min for 45 degrees and above, 12&#x27;-0&quot; min for less than 45 degrees (angled stalls). 13&#x27;-0&quot; min for 90 degree stalls.</p>
<h5>B.M.C. 31-1417</h5>
<p>Parking spaces shall be double-striped and access lanes clearly defined including directional arrows to guide internal movements. Striping for standard car spaces shall be white in color, whereas striping for small car spaces shall be yellow in color or may be painted white if marked &quot;COMPACT&quot; or &quot;SMALL CAR&quot;. All painted stripes shall be 4&quot; in width.</p></details>
