<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-409A-1 shows the layout of a controlled high visibility crosswalk made of longitudinal bars 2 feet wide, set between two alignment lines at least 10 feet apart. Alignment line #1 is set back distance 'B' (1' to 6') from the curb face depending on distance 'A' (15' to 39'). At signalized intersections alignment line #2 must be within 5 feet of the push button, and stop lines follow BT-411-1.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: A controlled high visibility (continental style) crosswalk at an intersection corner. At the top left a DOUBLE YELLOW centerline stripe leads to the crosswalk bar, with a CENTERLINE dashed line at the far right of the sheet. A curved arrow shows a turning movement. A STOP LINE is drawn as a wide double-line bar across the approach lane at the left of the crosswalk. Lane lines are dashed. EDGE OF GUTTER and CURB FACE are dashed lines along the bottom of the approach, ending at the BCR (beginning of curb return) where the curb turns on a radius. A small circle near the BCR marks the pedestrian push button.</p>
<p>The crosswalk is drawn as six solid dark longitudinal bars running left to right (parallel to the street centerline direction), each spaced with gaps between them, set between two vertical dashed lines labeled ALIGNMENT LINE #2 (left) and ALIGNMENT LINE #1 (right). The dimension between the alignment lines is 10&#x27; MINIMUM. A 5&#x27; MAX. dimension is drawn from alignment line #2 to the push button. The lowest bar near the curb return is shorter and is called out by box 3. Boxes on the bars: 4 points to two bars at the top, 5 points to a bar near the bottom. Box 2 points to the 5&#x27; MAX. dimension and box 1 points to alignment line #1. A dimension labeled &#x27;A&#x27; runs horizontally at the bottom from the BCR line over to the curb face, and a dimension &#x27;B&#x27; is shown from alignment line #1 to the curb face.</p>
<p>Table: A small table at left titled &#x27;A&#x27; and &#x27;B&#x27;. Rows: &#x27;A&#x27; = 15&#x27; to 19&#x27; with &#x27;B&#x27; = 1&#x27;; &#x27;A&#x27; = 20&#x27; to 24&#x27; with &#x27;B&#x27; = 2&#x27;; &#x27;A&#x27; = 25&#x27; to 29&#x27; with &#x27;B&#x27; = 3.5&#x27;; &#x27;A&#x27; = 30&#x27; to 34&#x27; with &#x27;B&#x27; = 5&#x27;; &#x27;A&#x27; = 35&#x27; to 39&#x27; with &#x27;B&#x27; = 6&#x27;.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Alignment line #1 shall be distance &#x27;B&#x27; behind curb face based on distance &#x27;A&#x27;.</p>
<p>2. Alignment line #2 must be a minimum (MIN.) of 10 feet from alignment line #1. At signalized intersections, alignment line #2 must be within 5 feet of a crosswalk push button.</p>
<p>3. Longitudinal lines near gutter may have length reduced.</p>
<p>4. Longitudinal lines shall be centered at lane lines and at midpoint of travel lanes.</p>
<p>5. Curb lanes or approaches with one travel lane shall have longitudinal lines separated by 3 feet, edge to edge (5 feet, center to center).</p>
<p>Alignment lines shall be parallel to street centerline.</p>
<p>Longitudinal lines shall be 2 feet wide. Longitudinal lines shall have length increased to ensure curb ramps are within alignment lines.</p>
<p>Stop lines shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-411-1.</p></details>
