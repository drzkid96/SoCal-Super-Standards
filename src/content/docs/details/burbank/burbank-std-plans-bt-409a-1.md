<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary>
<h5 class="dwg-sub">Notes</h5>
<ol class="dwg-notes">
<li value="1">Alignment line #1 shall be distance &#x27;B&#x27; behind curb face based on distance &#x27;A&#x27;.</li>
<li value="2">Alignment line #2 must be a minimum (MIN.) of 10 feet from alignment line #1. At signalized intersections, alignment line #2 must be within 5 feet of a crosswalk push button.</li>
<li value="3">Longitudinal lines near gutter may have length reduced.</li>
<li value="4">Longitudinal lines shall be centered at lane lines and at midpoint of travel lanes.</li>
<li value="5">Curb lanes or approaches with one travel lane shall have longitudinal lines separated by 3 feet, edge to edge (5 feet, center to center).</li>
</ol>
<p>Alignment lines shall be parallel to street centerline.</p>
<p>Longitudinal lines shall be 2 feet wide. Longitudinal lines shall have length increased to ensure curb ramps are within alignment lines.</p>
<p>Stop lines shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-411-1.</p>
</details>
