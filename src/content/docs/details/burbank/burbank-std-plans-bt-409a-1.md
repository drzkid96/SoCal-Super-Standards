<p class="dwg-sum">Shows layout of a high visibility (continental style) crosswalk at controlled locations with a stop line, curb ramp and alignment lines. Alignment line 1 sits distance B behind the curb face based on curb return distance A, and alignment line 2 is at least 10' from line 1. Longitudinal bars are 2' wide and centered on lane lines and lane midpoints.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>CONTROLLED HIGH VISIBILITY CROSSWALK</h5>
<p>Callouts: double yellow to crosswalk bar, stop line, edge of gutter, curb face, BCR, alignment line #1, alignment line #2, 10&#x27; minimum between alignment lines, 5&#x27; max from alignment line #2 to push button, centerline.</p>
<h5>Table, distance &#x27;A&#x27; | distance &#x27;B&#x27;:</h5>
<p>15&#x27; to 19&#x27; | 1&#x27;</p>
<p>20&#x27; to 24&#x27; | 2&#x27;</p>
<p>25&#x27; to 29&#x27; | 3.5&#x27;</p>
<p>30&#x27; to 34&#x27; | 5&#x27;</p>
<p>35&#x27; to 39&#x27; | 6&#x27;</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Alignment line #1 shall be distance &#x27;B&#x27; behind curb face based on distance &#x27;A&#x27;.</p>
<p>2. Alignment line #2 must be a minimum of 10 feet from alignment line #1. At signalized intersections, alignment line #2 must be within 5 feet of a crosswalk push button.</p>
<p>3. Longitudinal lines near gutter may have length reduced.</p>
<p>4. Longitudinal lines shall be centered at lane lines and at midpoint of travel lanes.</p>
<p>5. Curb lanes or approaches with one travel lane shall have longitudinal lines separated by 3 feet, edge to edge (5 feet, center to center).</p>
<p>Alignment lines shall be parallel to street centerline.</p>
<p>Longitudinal lines shall be 2 feet wide. Longitudinal lines shall have length increased to ensure curb ramps are within alignment lines.</p>
<p>Stop lines shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-411-1.</p></details>
