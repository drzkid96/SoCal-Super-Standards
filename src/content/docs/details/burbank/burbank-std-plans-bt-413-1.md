<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-413-1 is a not-to-scale elevation of a sign and object marker on a 2 inch square post in a median. The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a> sign (or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a>) is at least 7 feet above the paving and 6 inches minimum from the curb face, with a type <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a> yellow object marker below it 4 feet above the paving. The post sits in a 2-1/4 inch galvanized anchor sleeve and narrow medians under 3 feet use an 18 by 24 inch <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> and OM3-L marker.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Sign and post in median elevation (not to scale): An elevation of a 2&quot;X2&quot; SQUARE POST (12 GAGE) standing in a median paving slab with the existing concrete curb and gutter at the left edge of the median and EX. ASPHALT PAVING beyond it at the left (dashed with a break line). On the post, a diamond <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a> sign (double arrow, callout INSTALL <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a> SIGN (24&quot; X 24&quot;) OR <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> SIGN (24&quot; X 30&quot;)*) is mounted at the top and a plain yellow diamond INSTALL TYPE <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a> YELLOW OBJECT MARKER (24&quot; X 24&quot;)* is mounted below it. Both are secured with drive rivets (SECURE SIGN WITH DRIVE RIVET).</p>
<p>Dimensions: 7&#x27; MINIMUM from the median paving surface up to the bottom of the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a> sign. 4&#x27; from the paving surface up to the bottom of the object marker. 6&quot; MIN. horizontal clearance from the curb face to the side of the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a> sign, and another 6&quot; MIN. from the curb face to the side of the object marker. At the post base, a 4&quot; dimension is shown from the paving surface to the 3/8&quot; HOLES (INSERT SIGN POST AND FASTEN WITH RIVETS) at the top of the anchor sleeve. A 2&#x27; dimension is shown from that level to the bottom of the sleeve. The sleeve is labeled 2-1/4&quot; X 2-1/4&quot; SQUARE HEAVY DUTY GALVANIZED ANCHOR SLEEVE (TELESPAR OR EQUAL). The paving at the post is labeled EXIST. CONCRETE OR ASPHALT PAVING, with the curb labeled EX. CONCRETE CURB AND GUTTER.</p>
<p>Alternative sign: At the right side of the sheet an <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> sign (keep right, a vertical rectangle with a curved arrow bending right past an island) is drawn with two rivet holes at top and bottom.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Sign and Post in Median. Standard Plan BT-413-1, Sheet 1 of 1. Review initials dated 11/8/19. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 11/14/19. A California registered professional engineer (traffic) stamp is shown, signed 11/8/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>* If median width is less than 3 feet, use <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> (18&quot; x 24&quot;) sign and OM3-L object marker.</p>
<p>All regulatory and warning signs shall be minimum 0.063 inch thick and have 3M high intensity prismatic sheeting (Series #3930) with 3M premium protective overlay film (Series #1160).</p>
<p>Callouts: Secure sign with drive rivet. Install <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-1.svg">W12-1</a> sign (24&quot; x 24&quot;) or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-7.svg">R4-7</a> sign (24&quot; x 30&quot;)*. Install type <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a> yellow object marker (24&quot; x 24&quot;)*. 2&quot;x2&quot; square post (12 gage). 3/8&quot; holes, insert sign post and fasten with rivets. Existing (EXIST.) concrete or asphalt paving. Existing (EX.) concrete curb and gutter. EX. asphalt paving. 2-1/4&quot; x 2-1/4&quot; square heavy duty galvanized anchor sleeve (Telespar or equal). (Not to scale.)</p></details>
