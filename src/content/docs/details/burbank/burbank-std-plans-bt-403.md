<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Five lookup tables of required parking bay widths for angled parking from 30 to 90 degrees. Tables 1 to 4 cover standard cars with 8'-6" to 9'-4" stalls for one-way and two-way traffic with double or single loaded aisles. Table 5 covers compact cars with 7'-6" by 15'-0" stalls.</p>
