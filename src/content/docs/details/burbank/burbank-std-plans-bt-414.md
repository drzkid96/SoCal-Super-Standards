<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows plan, section and signing for a standard 3 inch asphalt speed hump, 12' long in the travel direction with a 12" taper and a 15 mph advance sign. Humps are spaced about 250' to 300' apart, with BUMP legends and thermoplastic chevron markings. Advance signs are a 30"x30" <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> modified BUMPS sign over an 18"x18" W13-1 (15) plaque.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 18)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0018.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0018.webp" alt="BT-414 sheet 1"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans BT-414, sheet 1. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/burbank/standard-plans/01-pages-1-27/#p18">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view (upper): A straight roadway drawn with break lines, curb lines at top and bottom and a dash-dot centerline (CL), roadway width labeled VARIES. A series of speed humps, each shown as a dotted rectangle spanning the full width of the road, is marked ASPHALT SPEED HUMP. Each hump is 12&#x27; long, with a 12&#x27; dimension at left. Each hump carries a white chevron marking (nested double-V pointing toward the oncoming travel direction) in each travel lane. The word BUMP is painted in each lane on the pavement before the hump, rotated to read along the lane, with a callout THERMOPLASTIC OR 2 COATS OF TRAFFIC PAINT pointing to the BUMP legend and chevrons. A 50&#x27; dimension runs from the hump to the next BUMP legend, an 8&#x27; TYPICAL dimension sets the BUMP legend offset, and spacing between humps is dimensioned 250&#x27; - 300&#x27; +/-. The distance from the last hump to the intersection at the right is dimensioned 200&#x27; - 250&#x27; +/-. Section A-A is cut across the hump at the right side. A small sign symbol at the lower left reads <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> and W13-1.</p>
<p>Advance signs, not to scale (lower left): A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> diamond sign reading BUMPS, with a W13-1 advisory speed plaque reading 15 MPH below it, both on one post. Text beside it: SIGN LOCATIONS AS DIRECTED BY ENGINEER. Dimensions are 12&#x27; MIN. lateral clearance from the roadway edge and 6.5&#x27; MIN. mounting height to the bottom of the plaque. Callouts are 30&quot;X30&quot; <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> MODIFIED COPY &quot;BUMPS&quot; with the sheeting requirement in the notes, and 18&quot;X18&quot; W13-1 (15).</p>
<p>Section A-A, not to scale (lower right): A cross-section from the curb face across the gutter and hump profile. The concrete curb and gutter is drawn with a CURB FACE callout. Dimensions along the top are 6&quot; from the curb face, 18&quot; TYP., and a 12&quot; TAPER. The asphalt hump profile tapers up from the gutter over the existing asphalt pavement, with a dashed TACK COAT line beneath the new asphalt. Callouts read EXISTING ASPHALT PAVEMENT, TACK COAT and A.C. STANDARD 3/8&quot; MIX D2-AR-4000, 5.6% ASPHALT BINDER FOR USE PERFORMANCE GRADING PER CALTRANS STANDARDS.</p>
<p>Installation details: Four numbered notes at the bottom of the sheet.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Standard 3&quot; Speed Hump. Standard Plan BT-414, Sheet 1 of 2. Review initials dated 12/20/16. Approved by the Acting Public Works Director, dated 12/21/16.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Plan callouts: Asphalt speed hump. Thermoplastic or 2 coats of traffic paint (BUMP legend and chevrons). Roadway width varies. Humps are 12&#x27; long, 50&#x27; to next BUMP legend, 8&#x27; typical, humps spaced 250&#x27; - 300&#x27; +/-, and 200&#x27; - 250&#x27; +/- from the intersection.</p>
<p>Advance signs: Sign locations as directed by Engineer. 12&#x27; minimum (MIN.). 6.5&#x27; MIN. mounting height. 30&quot;x30&quot; <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a> modified copy &quot;BUMPS&quot;. Signs shall have 3M high intensity prismatic sheeting (Series #3930) with 3M premium protective overlay film (Series #1160). 18&quot;x18&quot; W13-1 (15). Not to scale.</p>
<p>Section A-A callouts: 6&quot;, 18&quot; typical (TYP.), 12&quot; taper, curb face, A.C. standard 3/8&quot; mix D2-AR-4000, 5.6% asphalt binder for use performance grading per Caltrans standards, tack coat, existing asphalt pavement. Not to scale.</p>
<h5>Installation details:</h5>
<p>1. Speed humps should not be placed over manholes, water valves, etc.</p>
<p>2. Edge of speed hump shall be 5 feet minimum from edge of driveway.</p>
<p>3. Whenever possible speed humps shall be placed adjacent to street lights.</p>
<p>4. Finish grade of asphalt may vary by 1/4&quot; (higher or lower).</p></details>

### Sheet 2 of 2 (document page 19)

<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0019.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/burbank/standard-plans/p0019.webp" alt="BT-414 sheet 2"></a><figcaption>Burbank Std. Plans BT-414, sheet 2. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/burbank/standard-plans/01-pages-1-27/#p19">Open in the document</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Speed hump marking, not to scale (plan view): A rectangular plan of one hump across half the roadway, with the roadway centerline (CL OF ROADWAY) as a dash-dot line at the left-center and the curb and gutter at the far right. The hump is stippled. Its length across the road is 12&#x27; TYPICAL, split as 6&#x27; and 6&#x27; about a dash-dot line labeled CENTER OF SPEED HUMP. A 1&#x27; TAPER is dimensioned at each end of the hump along the road. Section B-B is cut across the hump (arrow marks B at the top and bottom). Across the roadway, a white chevron marking in each travel lane is drawn: in the left lane a V pointing down with a 6&#x27; overall width dimension, and in the right lane a nested three-line V pointing up (the lane is entered from the bottom, shown by arrows at the bottom of the sheet). The chevrons are 12&quot; wide stripes, called out 12&quot; WHITE MARKINGS THERMOPLASTIC OR 2 COATS OF TRAFFIC PAINT. Lane dimensions measured from the CL OF ROADWAY are 5&#x27; to the center of the travel lane (CENTER OF TRAVEL LANE) and 10&#x27; TYPICAL to the edge of the travel lane. Beyond that is an 8&#x27; PARKING LANE with an EDGE OF GUTTER line and a FLOW LINE shown at the curb, with a 1.5&#x27; TYPICAL dimension for the gutter. A dashed dimension string at the top of the sheet shows the same measurements from the centerline.</p>
<p>Section B-B, not to scale: A longitudinal profile of the 3 inch hump over a baseline. The hump rises from 0&quot; at the left end along a smooth parabolic curve to a crest of 3.00&quot; at the middle and returns to 0&quot; at the right end. The overall length is 12&#x27;, with a 6&#x27; dimension from the left end to the crest. Ordinates are shown at 6&quot; TYP. spacing and read, from the left end to the crest: 0&quot;, .90&quot;, .92&quot;, 1.31&quot;, 1.67&quot;, 1.98&quot;, 2.25&quot;, 2.48&quot;, 2.67&quot;, 2.81&quot;, 2.92&quot;, 2.98&quot;, 3.00&quot;. The profile is symmetric about the crest.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Standard 3&quot; Speed Hump. Standard Plan BT-414, Sheet 2 of 2. Review initials dated 12/20/16. Approved by the Acting Public Works Director, dated 12/21/16.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Speed hump marking (not to scale): 12&quot; white markings, thermoplastic or 2 coats of traffic paint. Dimensions: 12&#x27; typical (TYP.) hump length with 6&#x27; on each side of the center of speed hump, 1&#x27; taper, 6&#x27; chevron width, 5&#x27; to center of travel lane, 10&#x27; TYP. travel lane, 8&#x27; parking lane, 1.5&#x27; TYP. gutter, centerline (CL) of roadway, edge of gutter, flow line.</p>
<p>Section B-B (not to scale): 6&quot; TYP. ordinate spacing. Heights above the base at each station: 0&quot;, .90&quot;, .92&quot;, 1.31&quot;, 1.67&quot;, 1.98&quot;, 2.25&quot;, 2.48&quot;, 2.67&quot;, 2.81&quot;, 2.92&quot;, 2.98&quot;, 3.00&quot;. Overall length 12&#x27;, with 6&#x27; from the end to the crest.</p>
<p>No numbered notes appear on this sheet. See sheet 1 of 2 for installation details.</p></details>
