<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows the layout, cross-section, signing and pavement markings for a 22-foot-long, 3-inch-high asphalt speed table on a City of Burbank street. Tables are spaced about 250 to 300 feet apart, with a W84 (CA) / W13-1 (15) advance sign placed 100 to 125 feet ahead of the first table from each direction. Markings are 12-inch solid white thermoplastic chevrons and BUMP legends, and the table must be at least 5 feet from the edge of a driveway.</p>

### Sheet 1 of 2 (document page 24)

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view: a two-lane street shown with an asphalt speed table crossing both lanes, centered about the street centerline (CL), with break lines showing the street continues. The speed table is 22&#x27; long along the street. Chevron markings (marking detail 1) are placed at both ends of the table in each lane, pointing toward the table&#x27;s approach in each direction. The double yellow centerline passes through the table with 20&#x27; (TYP.) dimensions beyond the table on each side. A BUMP legend (marking detail 2) is placed ahead of the table in each lane, 50&#x27; (TYP.) from the table and 8&#x27; (TYP.) long. The BUMP legend and chevrons are labeled thermoplastic or 2 coats of traffic paint. Overall: 250&#x27; - 300&#x27; +/- between the two speed tables shown, with a W84 (CA) and W13-1 sign assembly placed 100&#x27;-125&#x27; +/- before the first table, and road width marked VARIES. Section A-A is cut across the street near the lower curb.</p>
<p>Advance sign (not to scale): W84 (CA) 30&quot;x30&quot; SPEED BUMPS AHEAD diamond sign above a W13-1 (15) 18&quot;x18&quot; 15 MPH plaque on a post. Text: advance sign to be placed ahead of first speed table from each direction.</p>
<p>Section A-A (not to scale): at the curb, from the curb face, 6&quot; is the curb reveal region and 18&quot; (TYP.) is the gutter pan width, followed by a 12&quot; taper onto the speed table. The asphalt is A.C. standard 3/8&quot; mix D2-AR-4000 5.6% asphalt binder for use performance grading per Caltrans standards, placed over a tack coat on existing asphalt pavement.</p>
<p>Marking detail 1: a white chevron 6&#x27; tall and 6&#x27; wide, 12&quot; solid white stripe, with a 16&quot; dimension at the inner triangle height and 12&quot; stripe width.</p>
<p>Marking detail 2: the legend BUMP on a grid, 6&#x27;-4&quot; wide by 8&#x27; tall, with 4&quot; and 8&quot; grid spacing.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department, Standard 3&quot; Speed Table, Standard Plan BT-418, sheet 1 of 2. Approved by the City Engineer, dated 7/23/25.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>General notes:</h5>
<p>1. Speed tables shall not be placed over manholes, water valves, etc.</p>
<p>2. Edge of speed table shall be 5 feet minimum from edge of driveway.</p>
<p>3. Whenever possible speed tables shall be placed adjacent to street lights.</p>
<p>4. Finish grade of asphalt may vary by 1/4&quot; (higher or lower).</p>
<p>6. All markings shall be thermoplastic.</p>
<p>7. All signs shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-412-1.</p>
<p>Note numbered 5 is not shown on the sheet.</p>
<p>Callouts: Asphalt speed table. Thermoplastic or 2 coats of traffic paint. Marking detail 2. W84 (CA), W13-1. A.C. standard 3/8&quot; mix D2-AR-4000 5.6% asphalt binder for use performance grading per Caltrans standards. Tack coat. Existing asphalt pavement. Curb face.</p></details>

Width of roadway is dimensioned along the top, with the street centerline (CL STREET) near the middle, flanked by a double yellow centerline using <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> per Caltrans Std Plan A20A. The table length along the direction of travel is 6&#x27; ramp, 10&#x27; flat top and 6&#x27; ramp (22&#x27; overall), with dashed lines at the ramp breaks and the table centerline (CL speed table) in the middle of the flat top. A white chevron (marking detail 1) is placed on each ramp, 5&#x27; from the street centerline, and the dashed edge of the table runs 18&#x27; typical from the street centerline. Near the curb, the table has a 1&#x27; taper, a dimension of 1&#x27;-2&#x27; typical at the gutter, and labels for edge of gutter, flowline, gutter and sidewalk. Section B-B is cut along the table length. Direction-of-travel arrows are shown below.</p>
<p>Section B-B (not to scale): longitudinal profile of the table. A 6&quot; (TYP.) dimension is shown at the ramp&#x27;s low end. Label: CL of roadway.</p>
