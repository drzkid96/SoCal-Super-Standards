<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Burbank Standard Plan BT-411-1 shows stop line and STOP marking placement for single lane approaches with unmarked and marked crosswalks and for multiple lane approaches. Stop lines are 12 inch solid white, placed at distance 'B' from the beginning of curb return (based on distance 'A') or 4 feet from a marked crosswalk. STOP markings sit 8 feet from the stop line.</p>

<details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Single lane approach, unmarked crosswalk (plan view, upper left): A one-lane approach at an intersection, with a centerline (C/L) at left and curb returns on both sides. A solid white stop line (box 1) is drawn across the lane at the curb return. Dimension &#x27;A&#x27; is measured at the right from the far end of the curb return to the beginning of the curb return (BCR), and dimension &#x27;B&#x27; is measured from the BCR to the stop line. The word STOP is marked in the lane (box 3), 2&#x27; from the double yellow stripe at left and 8&#x27; from the stop line. A double yellow stripe runs along the centerline up to the stop line, called out by box 4: UP TO STOP LINE.</p>
<p>Single lane approach, marked crosswalk (plan view, upper middle): The same approach with a marked crosswalk of parallel bars across the intersection. The stop line (box 2) is placed 4&#x27; from the crosswalk, shown by a 4&#x27; dimension. STOP marking (box 3) is 2&#x27; from the double yellow stripe and 8&#x27; from the stop line. The double yellow stripe runs UP TO CROSSWALK (box 4).</p>
<p>Multiple lane approach (plan view, upper right): A two-lane approach with a crosswalk, one stop line across both lanes, and a STOP marking in each lane (box 5), centered in each lane and dimensioned 8&#x27; from the stop line. A left-turn arrow is shown in the left lane below the left STOP marking.</p>
<p>Table: Titled &#x27;A&#x27; and &#x27;B&#x27; (lower left). Rows: &#x27;A&#x27; = 15&#x27; to 19&#x27; with &#x27;B&#x27; = -3&#x27;; 20&#x27; to 24&#x27; with 1&#x27;; 25&#x27; to 29&#x27; with 4.5&#x27;; 30&#x27; to 34&#x27; with 8&#x27;; 35&#x27; to 39&#x27; with 12&#x27;.</p>
<p>Title block: City of Burbank, Public Works Department. Stop Bar Detail. Standard Plan BT-411-1, Sheet 1 of 1. Review initials dated 11/8/19. Approved by the Public Works Director, dated 11/14/19. A California registered professional engineer (traffic) stamp is shown, signed 11/8/19.</p></details>
<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install solid white 12 inch stop line at distance &#x27;B&#x27; from beginning of curb return (BCR) based on distance &#x27;A&#x27;.</p>
<p>2. Install solid white 12 inch stop line 4 feet from a marked crosswalk.</p>
<p>3. Install &quot;STOP&quot; marking 2 feet from double yellow stripe and 8 feet from stop line.</p>
<p>4. Install 50 feet of <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> stripe per Caltrans Standard Plan A20A.</p>
<p>5. Install &quot;STOP&quot; marking centered in each lane and 8 feet from stop line.</p>
<p>Marked crosswalks shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-409A-1.</p>
<p>Pavement arrows shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-410-1.</p>
<p>Callouts on the plan: Box 4 reads UP TO STOP LINE for an unmarked crosswalk and UP TO CROSSWALK for a marked crosswalk.</p></details>
