<p class="dwg-sum" data-pagefind-weight="6">Shows stop line and STOP word marking placement for single lane and multiple lane approaches, with unmarked and marked crosswalks. Stop lines are solid white 12 inch lines placed distance B from the BCR based on curb return distance A, or 4' from a marked crosswalk. STOP markings go 8' behind the stop line.</p>

<details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>STOP BAR DETAIL</h5>
<p>Single lane approach: unmarked crosswalk and marked crosswalk. Multiple lane approach. Dimensions shown: 2&#x27; from double yellow stripe to STOP marking, 8&#x27; from stop line to STOP marking, 4&#x27; from marked crosswalk to stop line.</p>
<h5>Table, distance &#x27;A&#x27; | distance &#x27;B&#x27;:</h5>
<p>15&#x27; to 19&#x27; | -3&#x27;</p>
<p>20&#x27; to 24&#x27; | 1&#x27;</p>
<p>25&#x27; to 29&#x27; | 4.5&#x27;</p>
<p>30&#x27; to 34&#x27; | 8&#x27;</p>
<p>35&#x27; to 39&#x27; | 12&#x27;</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Install solid white 12 inch stop line at distance &#x27;B&#x27; from BCR based on distance &#x27;A&#x27;.</p>
<p>2. Install solid white 12 inch stop line 4 feet from a marked crosswalk.</p>
<p>3. Install &quot;STOP&quot; marking 2 feet from double yellow stripe and 8 feet from stop line.</p>
<p>4. Install 50 feet of <a class="sref dref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/markings/line-types/#detail-21" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/linetypes/detail-21.svg">Detail 21</a> stripe per Caltrans Standard Plan A20A.</p>
<p>5. Install &quot;STOP&quot; marking centered in each lane and 8 feet from stop line.</p>
<p>Marked crosswalks shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-409A-1.</p>
<p>Pavement arrows shall be installed per Burbank Standard Plan BT-410-1.</p></details>
