## Complete Streets Design Standards (June 2025) — Key Dimensions

| Element | Culver City Standard | Notes / Cross-Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard vehicle travel lane** | **10 ft** standard; **+1 ft (11 ft)** on curb-adjacent lane | Truck routes (e.g., Culver Blvd Sawtelle–Sepulveda) may require wider lanes for &gt; 6,000 lb vehicles; see [Lane Width Comparison](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/lane-widths/) |
| **Dedicated bus / transit priority lane** | **11 ft** wide per lane | With boarding islands linked to sidewalk via accessible pedestrian crossing; **8 ft × 5 ft** ADA clear space at front door |
| **Sidewalk clear through-zone** | **72 in. (6 ft)** local; **72–96 in.** arterials/connectors; **144 in. (12 ft)** High-Volume Pedestrian Areas | Exceeds 48 in. PROWAG minimum; 4-zone PROWAG layout (frontage 18–120 in., through, furnishing/buffer, curb) |
| **Continental crosswalks** | **12 ft wide** per **LADOT S-481.1** (unless plans show otherwise) | Thermoplastic per Caltrans Std. Specs § 84-2; see [LADOT S-481.0 / S-481.1](/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/ladot/mpp-531-s-481-0/) |
| **Bike boxes** | **10 to 16 ft deep**, green pavement + white stop lines + **NTOR** sign where right-turns conflict | Includes embedded bicycle loop detectors; green bike boxes/symbols use Ennis-Flint PreMark "Emerald Green" with Vizigrip |
| **Striping &amp; pavement markers** | Alkyd thermoplastic + RPMs per Caltrans **A20A–A20D** &amp; **A24A–A24E** | Caltrans Std. Specs §§ 84-2 &amp; 85; [Caltrans 2026 Standard Plans](/SoCal-Super-Standards/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/) |
| **Parklets (outdoor dining)** | **8 ft wide** × **12 ft** (1 stall), **32 ft** (2 stalls), or **52 ft** (3 stalls) | May not encroach into adjacent parking spaces; Parklet Street Use Permit required |

## Traffic Control Plan (TCP) Requirements

From Culver City's [Traffic Control Plan Requirements and Procedures](https://www.culvercity.gov/Services/Parking-Streets-Transportation/Traffic-Control-Plan-Requirements-and-Procedures) (Mobility &amp; Traffic Engineering: `(310) 253-5634`, `traffic.engineering@culvercity.org`):

### Major Streets Requiring Site-Specific Engineered TCPs
Work impacting travel lanes on these **15 Major Street corridors** requires a site-specific engineered TCP (work confined strictly to parking lane, shoulder, sidewalk, or parkway may use a CA MUTCD / TTCH / WATCH Standard Plan with City approval):
- Washington Blvd (Walnut Ave–Fairfax Ave), Washington Pl (Zanja St–Tilden Ave), Culver Blvd (Sawtelle Blvd–East City Limit)
- Jefferson Blvd (I-405–Sepulveda Blvd &amp; Sawtelle Blvd–East City Limit), Playa St (Overland Ave–Sepulveda Blvd)
- Sepulveda Blvd (Venice Blvd–Centinela Ave, excl. City of LA segment), Sawtelle Blvd (Venice Blvd–Braddock Dr), Overland Ave (Venice Blvd–Playa St)
- Centinela Ave (Mesmer Ave–Green Valley Cir &amp; Washington Pl–Washington Blvd), Slauson Ave (Jefferson Blvd–East City Limit)
- National Blvd (Venice Blvd–Jefferson Blvd), La Cienega Blvd (Ballona Creek Bridge–Fairfax Ave), Fairfax Ave (Washington Blvd–La Cienega Blvd)

### PE Stamp Triggers &amp; Field Rules
- **CA PE Stamp (Civil or Traffic) required when:** continuous traffic control exceeds **72 hours**, uses crash cushions / K-rail / temporary striping beyond Type II–III barricades &amp; channelizers (any duration), involves **contra-flow**, or when directed by Mobility &amp; Traffic Engineering.
- **Restriping trigger:** continuous traffic control planned **&gt; 14 days** requires striping and subsequent restriping plans.
- **Key Culver City TCP specs:** **Type I barricades prohibited** on Major Streets; **17 ft max** cone spacing near work/conflict zones; **28 in. min** cone height with retroreflective bands at night; solar-powered flashing arrow signs; **2 ft min** clearance from open excavation to live lane; AC ramp for **≥ 1 in.** vertical pavement offsets.
- **Temporary No Parking Signs (TNPS):** post **≥ 48 hours** in advance, angled 90° facing oncoming traffic, **BEGIN / END** marked, **30 ft max** spacing (or **50 ft** full-block), ≥ 5 ft bottom height on trees (twine/zip ties/clear tape only — no nails/staples/wire).
