## Complete Streets Design Standards (June 2025) — Key Dimensions

| Element | Culver City Standard | Technical Spec / Cross-Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard vehicle travel lane** | **10 ft** standard; **+1 ft (11 ft)** on curb-adjacent lane | Truck routes (e.g., Culver Blvd Sawtelle–Sepulveda) sized for &gt; 6,000 lb vehicles; see [Lane Width Comparison](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/lane-widths/) |
| **Dedicated bus / transit priority lane** | **11 ft** wide per lane | Boarding islands linked to sidewalk via accessible crossing; **8 ft × 5 ft** ADA clear space at front door |
| **Sidewalk clear through-zone** | **72 in. (6 ft)** local; **72–96 in.** arterials/connectors; **144 in. (12 ft)** High-Volume Pedestrian Areas | 4-zone PROWAG layout: frontage (18–120 in.), through, furnishing/buffer, curb |
| **Continental crosswalks** | **12 ft wide** per **LADOT S-481.1** (unless plans show otherwise) | Alkyd thermoplastic per Caltrans Std. Specs § 84-2; [LADOT S-481.0](/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/ladot/mpp-531-s-481-0/) |
| **Bike boxes &amp; green bike markings** | **10 to 16 ft deep**, Emerald Green thermoplastic + white stop lines + **NTOR** sign + embedded bike loop detectors | Pre-formed thermoplastic: Ennis-Flint **PreMark Bike Lane Green** ("Emerald Green") with Vizigrip |
| **Curb ramps (Technical Specs)** | **SPPWC 111-5**; **7.5% target** running slope (8.33% max); 4" PCC over 4" CMB; Class 520-C-3250 | Truncated domes: TekWay StrongCo **Terracotta** (or approved equal) set in wet cement |
| **Medians &amp; cross gutters** | Medians per **APWA 120-1** (520-C-3250 over 6" CMB + 24" AC/PCC slot pave); cross gutters 4,500 psi over 8" CMB | Catch basin local depressions per SPPWC 313-3; 1-1/2" steel traffic plates prior to opening |
| **Striping &amp; pavement markers** | Alkyd thermoplastic + RPMs per Caltrans **A20A–A20D** &amp; **A24A–A24E** | Caltrans Std. Specs §§ 84-2 &amp; 85; paint water valve covers blue after resurfacing |
| **Parklets (outdoor dining)** | **8 ft wide** × **12 ft** (1 stall), **32 ft** (2 stalls), or **52 ft** (3 stalls) | Max street running slope per guidelines; wheel stops required; continuous gutter drainage cutout |

### Major Streets Requiring Site-Specific Engineered TCPs
Work impacting travel lanes on these **15 Major Street corridors** requires a site-specific engineered TCP (work confined strictly to parking lane, shoulder, sidewalk, or parkway may submit a CA MUTCD / TTCH / WATCH Standard Plan with City approval):
- Washington Blvd (Walnut Ave–Fairfax Ave), Washington Pl (Zanja St–Tilden Ave), Culver Blvd (Sawtelle Blvd–East City Limit)
- Jefferson Blvd (I-405–Sepulveda Blvd &amp; Sawtelle Blvd–East City Limit), Playa St (Overland Ave–Sepulveda Blvd)
- Sepulveda Blvd (Venice Blvd–Centinela Ave, excl. City of LA segment), Sawtelle Blvd (Venice Blvd–Braddock Dr), Overland Ave (Venice Blvd–Playa St)
- Centinela Ave (Mesmer Ave–Green Valley Cir &amp; Washington Pl–Washington Blvd), Slauson Ave (Jefferson Blvd–East City Limit)
- National Blvd (Venice Blvd–Jefferson Blvd), La Cienega Blvd (Ballona Creek Bridge–Fairfax Ave), Fairfax Ave (Washington Blvd–La Cienega Blvd)

### PE Stamp Triggers &amp; Field Rules
- **CA PE Stamp (Civil or Traffic) required when:** continuous traffic control exceeds **72 hours**, uses crash cushions / K-rail / temporary striping beyond Type II–III barricades &amp; channelizers (any duration), involves **contra-flow**, or when directed by Mobility &amp; Traffic Engineering.
- **Restriping trigger:** continuous traffic control planned **&gt; 14 days** requires striping and subsequent restriping plans.
- **Key TCP rules:** **Type I barricades prohibited** on Major Streets; **17 ft max** cone spacing near work/conflict zones; **28 in. min** cone height with retroreflective bands at night; solar-powered flashing arrow signs; **2 ft min** clearance from open excavation to live lane; AC ramp for **≥ 1 in.** vertical pavement offsets.
- **Temporary No Parking Signs (TNPS):** post **≥ 48 hours** in advance, angled 90° facing oncoming traffic, **BEGIN / END** marked, **30 ft max** spacing (or **50 ft** full-block), ≥ 5 ft bottom height on trees (twine/zip ties/clear tape only — no nails/staples/wire).
