## 3K.01 Longitudinal Rumble Strip Markings

<div class="secmeta">Section 3K.01 &middot; manual page 1034 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3K.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Longitudinal rumble strips consist of a series of rough-textured or slightly raised or depressed road surfaces intended to alert inattentive drivers through vibration and sound that their vehicle has left the travel lane. Shoulder rumble strips are typically installed along the shoulder near the travel lane. On divided highways, rumble strips are sometimes installed on the median side (left-hand side) shoulder as well as on the outside (right-hand side) shoulder. On two-way roadways, rumble strips are sometimes installed along the center line.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> This Manual contains no provisions regarding the design and placement of longitudinal rumble strips. The provisions in this Manual address the use of markings in combination with a longitudinal rumble strip. <a href="#fig-3k-1">Figure 3K-1</a> illustrates markings used with or near longitudinal rumble strips.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6m/#6m06">Section 6M.06</a> contains information related to longitudinal rumble strips.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> An edge line or center line may be located over a longitudinal rumble strip to create a rumble stripe.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The color of an edge line or center line associated with a longitudinal rumble stripe shall be in accordance with <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3a/#3a03">Section 3A.03</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> An edge line shall not be used in addition to a rumble stripe that is located along a shoulder.</p>
</div>

## 3K.02 Transverse Rumble Strip Markings

<div class="secmeta">Section 3K.02 &middot; manual page 1034 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3K.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Transverse rumble strips consist of intermittent narrow, transverse areas of rough-textured or slightly raised or depressed road surface that extend across the travel lanes to alert drivers to unusual vehicular traffic conditions. Through noise and vibration, they attract the attention of road users to features such as unexpected changes in alignment and conditions requiring a reduction in speed or a stop.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> This Manual contains no provisions regarding the design and placement of transverse rumble strips that approximate the color of the pavement. The provisions in this Manual address the use of markings in combination with a transverse rumble strip.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6m/#6m06">Section 6M.06</a> contains information related to transverse rumble strips.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as otherwise provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6m/#6m06">Section 6M.06</a> for TTC zones, if the color of a transverse rumble strip used within a travel lane is not the color of the pavement, the color of the transverse rumble strip shall be either black or white.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> White transverse rumble strip markings used in a travel lane should not be placed in locations where they could be confused with other transverse markings such as stop lines or crosswalks.</p>
</div>
