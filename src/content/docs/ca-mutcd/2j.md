## 2J.01 Eligibility

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.01 &middot; manual page 773 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Specific Service signs shall be defined as guide signs that provide road users with business identification and directional information for eligible services. Eligible service categories shall be limited to gas <span class="ca">fuel</span>, food, lodging, camping, attractions, and electric vehicle (EV) charging.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01a</span> <span class="ca">In California, the generic term FUEL is used for GAS. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The use of Specific Service signs should<span class="ca"> shall</span>be limited to areas primarily rural in character with adequate space for all signs to be properly accommodated. <span class="ca">Refer SHC, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 3, § 101.7.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> When services at an interchange are abundant, this is an indication that the character of the area is no longer primarily rural and General Service signs would be more appropriate.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where an engineering study determines a need, Specific Service signs may be used on any class of highway, including freeways, expressways, and conventional roads.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04a</span> <span class="ca">SHC, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 3, § 101.7 includes the use of specific service signs for freeways only. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Specific Service signs should<span class="ca">shall</span> not be installed at an interchange where the road user cannot conveniently reenter the freeway or expressway and continue in the same direction of travel.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2108(d).</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Eligible service facilities shall comply with laws concerning the provisions of public accommodations without regard to race, religion, color, age, sex, or national origin, and laws concerning the licensing and approval of service facilities.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The attraction services shall include only facilities that have the primary purpose of providing amusement, historical, cultural, or leisure activities to the public.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 9 of this Section, distances to eligible services should not exceed 3 miles in any direction.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2111 for distances to eligible services.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> If, within the 3-mile limit, facilities for the services being considered are not available or choose not to participate in the program, the limit of eligibility may be extended in 3-mile increments until one or more facilities for the services being considered chooses to participate, or until 15 miles is reached, whichever comes first.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> If State or local agencies elect to provide Specific Service signing, there shall be a statewide policy for such signing and criteria for the eligibility and availability of the various types of services.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the SHC, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 3, </span><span class="ca">§</span><span class="ca"> 101.7 and CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, §§ 2100 through </span> <span class="ca">2120 for detailed policies on specific service signs. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a> for information regarding these publications. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> The criteria for the statewide policy should consider the following: A.To qualify for a GAS <span class="ca">FUEL</span> business identification sign panel, a business should have: 1.Vehicle services including gasoline, oil, and water; 2.Continuous operation at least 16 hours per day, 7 days per week for freeways and expressways, and continuous operation at least 12 hours per day, 7 days per week for conventional roads; and 3.Modern sanitary facilities and drinking water. B.To qualify for a FOOD business identification sign panel, a business should have: 1.Licensing or approval, where required; 2.Continuous operations to serve at least 2 meals per day, at least 6 days per week; and 3.Modern sanitary facilities. C.To qualify for a LODGING business identification sign panel, a business should have: 1.Licensing or approval, where required; 2.Adequate sleeping accommodations; and 3.Modern sanitary facilities. D.To qualify for a CAMPING business identification sign panel, a business should have: 1.Licensing or approval, where required; 2.Adequate parking accommodations; and 3.Modern sanitary facilities and drinking water. E.To qualify for an ATTRACTION business identification sign panel, a facility should have: 1.Regional significance, in compliance with the provisions of Paragraph 7 of this Section; and 2.Adequate parking accommodations.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> To be eligible for an Electric Vehicle (EV) CHARGING business identification sign panel, the EV chargers provided shall meet the criteria for Direct Current Fast Chargers provided in 23 CFR 680.106 and be in continuous operation at least 16 hours per day, 7 days per week.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Business identification sign panels for a proprietary electric vehicle charging service may be included on an EV Charging Specific Service sign if it meets the eligibility criteria in Paragraph 12 of this Section <span class="ca">and the criteria found in the </span> <span class="ca">CCR.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j12">Section 2J.12</a> contains additional information on criteria for the statewide policy regarding signing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2i/#2i04">Section 2I.04</a> contains information regarding the Interstate Oasis program.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, §§ 2100 through 2120 for administration, standards, eligibility, and </span> <span class="ca">fees concerning the Logo Sign Program. See <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a> for information regarding these publications</span><span class="ca">. </span></p>
</div>

## 2J.02 Application

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.02 &middot; manual page 774 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Examples of Specific Service signs are shown in <a href="#fig-2j-1">Figure 2J-1</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Examples of sign locations are shown in <a href="#fig-2j-2">Figure 2J-2</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The number of Specific Service signs along an approach to an interchange or intersection, regardless of the number of service types displayed, shall be limited to a maximum of four. Except as provided in Paragraph 4 of this Section, in the direction of traffic flow, successive Specific Service signs shall be for attraction, camping, lodging, food, EV charging, and gas <span class="ca">fuel</span> services, in that order.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> When spacing does not allow EV Charging Specific Service signs to be located as described in Paragraph 3 of this Section, then the EV Charging Specific Service signs may be located anywhere within the successive Specific Service sign order where adequate spacing between signs allows.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The Specific Service signs should be located to take advantage of natural terrain, to have the least impact on the scenic environment, and to avoid visual conflict with other signs within the highway right-of- way.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Where a service type is displayed on two signs, the signs for that service should follow one another in succession.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A Specific Service sign shall display the word message GAS<span class="ca"> FUEL</span>, EV CHARGING, FOOD, LODGING, CAMPING, or ATTRACTION, an appropriate directional legend such as the word message EXIT XX, NEXT RIGHT, SECOND RIGHT, or directional arrows, and the related business identification sign panels. Distances to eligible facilities shall not be displayed on the Specific Service signs on the approach to an interchange.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> A business that does not offer gasoline, but offers alternative fuels, shall not be signed using GAS Specific Service signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> A business that does not offer gasoline but offers alternative fuels may be signed using General Service signs for the alternative fuel provided.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> General Service signs for facilities providing alternative fuels, including EV charging, <span class="ca">methanol,</span> compressed natural gas, liquefied natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, and hydrogen, are provided in Chapter 2I.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Due to the unique and widely varying characteristics of the services that qualify as attractions, and lesser recognition of their business identification sign panels (see Paragraph 12 of this Section), ATTRACTION Specific Service signs should have no more than four business identification sign panels.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The types of services that meet the definition of attraction, such as those providing amusement, historical, cultural, or leisure activities to the public, vary considerably. In most cases, attractions do not include well-known services or easily recognizable logos, making it more difficult and requiring more time to distinguish between types of attractions shown on an ATTRACTION sign than for other categories of Specific Service signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> No more than three <span class="ca">two</span> types of services shall be represented on any sign or sign assembly and no more than six business identification sign panels shall be displayed on any one sign. If three <span class="ca">two</span> types of services are displayed on one sign, then the business identification sign panels shall be limited to two <span class="ca">three</span> for each service type (for a total of six business identification sign panels)<span class="ca"> Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2110(f)</span>. If two types of services are displayed on one sign, then the business identification sign panels shall be limited to either three for each service type, or four for one service type and two for the other service type (for a total of six business identification sign panels in either case). The legend and business identification sign panels applicable to a service type shall be displayed such that the road user will not associate them with another service type on the same sign. Other configurations or arrangements of business identification sign panels shall not be allowed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> No service type shall appear on more than two <span class="ca">one</span> signs (see Paragraph 6 of this Section).<span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, </span> <span class="ca">Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2108(c). </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> The Specific Service signs shall have a blue background, a white border, and white legends of upper-case letters, numerals, and arrows.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> If a service type is no longer available from an interchange or intersection, the Specific Service sign should be removed when the business identification sign panels are removed. If a sign is to remain, but the service type is no longer available, then the service type legend should be covered <span class="ca">by a blue panel overlay or by another method</span>so that road users do not misinterpret the sign as a General Service sign implying that the service is available.</p>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> A Specific Service sign should not be installed unless a service type is currently available from an interchange or intersection.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> If there is indication that a service type will again be available in the near future, the sign may be covered, in accordance with Paragraph 16 of this Section, rather than removed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">19</span> Separate installations of General Service signs (see <a href="#fig-2j-3">Figure 2J-3</a>and Sections 2I.02 and 2I.03) may be used in conjunction with Specific Service signs for eligible types of services that are not represented by a Specific Service sign.</p>
</div>

## 2J.03 Logos and Business Identification Sign Panels

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.03 &middot; manual page 776 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A business identification sign panel legend shall be either an identification trademark or a word message of the business’s name. Each logo or word message shall be placed on a separate business identification sign panel that shall be attached to the Specific Service sign. Logos or trademarks used alone for a business identification sign panel shall be reproduced in the colors and general shape consistent with customary use, and any integral legend shall be in proportionate size. A logo that resembles an official traffic control device shall not be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Scanning graphics that are visible to the road user from the roadway for the purpose of obtaining information shall not be displayed on business identification sign panels, including on any logo displayed thereupon.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The logo or trademark used on a business identification sign panel should be consistent with the on- premise business identification signs at the location of the business that are visible from the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A word message business identification sign panel that does not use a logo or trademark should have a blue background with a white legend and border.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j05">Section 2J.05</a> contains information regarding the minimum letter heights for business identification sign panels.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> A portion of a business identification sign panel may be used to display a supplemental message horizontally along the bottom of the business identification sign panel, provided that the message displays essential motorist information consistent with the service category type and related to the operation of the business (see <a href="#fig-2j-4">Figure 2J-4</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> All supplemental messages shall be displayed within the business identification sign panel and shall have letters and numerals that comply with the minimum height requirements shown in <a href="#fig-2j-1">Table 2J-1</a>. Supplemental messages promoting the availability of products, amenities, or services that are not directly related to the service category and/or those not available to non-patrons of the primary service provided for the service category, such as car wash, automated teller machines, Internet, lottery, or swimming pool, shall not be displayed on business identification sign panels.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Messages related to the promotion or availability of business identification sign panel space shall not be displayed on Specific Service signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> To be eligible for an EV CHARGING supplemental message on a business identification sign panel, the business shall: A.Offer electric vehicle charging to the general public without purchasing the primary service (gas, food, lodging, camping, or attraction, as appropriate); and B.For the service categories of gas, food, and attraction, provide EV chargers meeting the criteria for Direct Current Fast Chargers (DCFC) provided in 23 CFR 680.106; or C.For the service categories of camping and lodging, provide EV chargers meeting the criteria for DCFCs provided in 23 CFR 680.106 and/or AC Level 2 Charging.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> A Supplemental message identifying an alternative fuel available may be added only to the business identification sign panels on the GAS <span class="ca">FUEL</span>Specific Services sign for gasoline facilities that provide the specified alternative fuel in addition to gasoline.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> The Supplemental message EV CHARGING may be added to a business identification sign panel for the service categories of gas, food, lodging, or camping in accordance with the criteria in Paragraph 9 of this Section.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">11a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in Paragraph 11. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> A business identification sign panel should <span class="ca">shall</span> not display more than one supplemental message.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The supplemental message should <span class="ca">shall</span> be displayed in a black legend on a yellow background for that portion of the business identification sign panel.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> State or local agencies that elect to allow supplemental messages on business identification sign panels should develop a statewide policy for such messages.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> Typical supplemental messages might include DIESEL, LP-GAS, EV CHARGING, 24 HOURS, CLOSED SUNDAY, and RV ACCESS.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> If a State or local agency elects to display the designation of businesses as providing on-premise accommodations for recreational vehicles with the RV ACCESS supplemental message, there should be a statewide policy for such designation and criteria for qualifying businesses. The criteria should include such site conditions as access between the public roadway and the site, on-premise geometry, and parking.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> If a business designated as an Interstate Oasis (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2i/#2i04">Section 2I.04</a>) has a business identification sign panel on the Food and/or Gas <span class="ca">Fuel </span>Specific Service signs, the word OASIS may be displayed on the bottom portion of the business identification sign panel for that business.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> A business identification sign panel shall not display the identification logo/trademark or name of more than one business. A business identification sign panel shall not display more than one name or identification logo/trademark for the same business. Slogans, such as marketing slogans associated with the business, shall not be displayed on business identification sign panels or the Specific Service sign itself.</p>
</div>

## 2J.04 Number and Size of Signs and Business Identification Sign Panels

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.04 &middot; manual page 777 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Sign sizes should be determined by the amount and height of legend and the number and size of business identification sign panels attached to the sign. All business identification sign panels on a sign should be the same size.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Each Specific Service sign or sign assembly shall be limited to no more than six business identification sign panels.<span class="ca"> There shall be no more than three logo panels for one of the two service types on the same sign or sign assembly. </span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2110(f).</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Where more than six businesses of a specific service type are eligible for business identification sign panels at the same interchange, additional business identification sign panels of that same specific service type may also be displayed in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 4 of this Section. The additional business identification sign panels may be displayed either by placing more than one specific service type on the same sign (see Paragraph 13 of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j02">Section 2J.02</a>) or by using a second Specific Service sign of that specific service type if the additional sign can be added without exceeding the limit of four Specific Service signs at an interchange or intersection approach (see Paragraph 3 of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j02">Section 2J.02</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where business identification sign panels for more than six businesses of a specific service type are displayed at the same interchange or intersection approach, the following provisions shall apply: A.No more than 12 business identification sign panels of a specific service type shall be displayed on no more than two Specific Service signs or sign assemblies; B.No more than six business identification sign panels shall be displayed on a single Specific Service sign; and C.No more than four Specific Service signs shall be displayed on the approach.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2108(c). </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j08">Section 2J.08</a> contains information regarding Specific Service signs for double-exit interchanges.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j09">Section 2J.09</a> contains information regarding Specific Service signs for multiple interchanges that are accessed from collector-distributor roadways rather than from the highway mainline.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Each business identification sign panel attached to a Specific Service sign shall be a horizontally oriented rectangle with a width longer than the height. A business identification sign panel on signs for freeways and expressways shall not exceed 60 inches in width and 36 inches in height (see <a href="#fig-2j-2">Table 2J-2</a>). A business identification sign panel on signs for conventional roads and freeway and expressway ramps shall not exceed 30 inches in width and 18 inches in height (see <a href="#fig-2j-2">Table 2J-2</a>). <span class="ca">SHC, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 3, § 101.7 includes the use of specific </span> <span class="ca">service signs for freeways only. </span>The vertical and horizontal spacing between business identification sign panels shall not exceed 8 inches and 12 inches, respectively.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07a</span> <span class="ca">A logo panel on signs for the mainline shall be 48 inch in width and 36 inch in height.</span></p>
<p><span class="pn">07b</span> <span class="ca">A logo panel on signs for the ramps shall be 18 inch in width and 12 inch in height. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Sections 2A.10, 2E.13, and 2E.14 contain information regarding borders, interline spacing, and edge spacing.</p>
</div>

## 2J.06 Signs at Interchanges

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.06 &middot; manual page 778 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.06/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Specific Service signs shall be installed between the preceding interchange and at least 800 feet in advance of the Exit Direction sign at the interchange from which the services are available (see <a href="#fig-2j-2">Figure 2J-2</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01a</span> <span class="ca">At the discretion of Caltrans, the location of the Specific Service signs with respect to their distances from the gore may be </span> <span class="ca">increased to avoid conflict with existing guide signs. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Specific Service signs shall not be used at freeway-to-freeway interchanges (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2e/#2e37">Section 2E.37</a>), except where the exit ramp also provides direct access to a conventional road within that interchange (see <a href="#fig-2j-5">Figure 2J-5</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> There should be at least an 800-foot spacing between the Specific Service signs, except for Specific Service ramp signs. Excessive spacing should not be used between Specific Service signs, as this is not desirable either.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Specific Service ramp signs should be spaced at least 100 feet longitudinally beyond the Exit Gore sign, from each other, and from the ramp terminal. Specific Service ramp signs should be spaced at least 200 feet longitudinally from any Destination guide signs along the ramp. Longer longitudinal spacing should be provided between Specific Service ramp signs and any warning or regulatory signs along the ramp, and any intersection traffic control devices at the ramp terminal.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When the distance to the next exit providing access to EV charging service is 50 miles or greater, the Next EV Charging (D9-17a) sign should be used (see <a href="#fig-2h-9">Figure 2H-9</a>). When used, the Next EV Charging sign should be located directly after the General Service sign for the fuel type displayed in the signing sequence for the exit (see <a href="#fig-2h-10">Figure 2H-10</a>).</p>
</div>

## 2J.07 Single-Exit Interchanges

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.07 &middot; manual page 778 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.07/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> At numbered single-exit interchanges, the name of the service type followed by the exit number shall be displayed on one line above the business identification sign panels. At unnumbered interchanges, the directional legend NEXT RIGHT (LEFT) shall be used in place of the exit number.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> At single-exit interchanges where traffic is allowed to turn onto the crossroad in either direction from the ramp, Specific Service ramp signs shall be installed along the ramp or opposite the ramp terminal for facilities that have business identification sign panels displayed along the main roadway if the facilities are not readily visible from the ramp terminal. Directions to the service facilities shall be indicated by arrows on the ramp signs. Business identification sign panels on Specific Service ramp signs shall be duplicates of those displayed on the Specific Service signs located in advance of the interchange, but shall be reduced in size (see Paragraph 7 of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j04">Section 2J.04</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Specific Service ramp signs may display distances (see Paragraphs 14 and 15 of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a08">Section 2A.08</a>) to a service facility when the facility is not visible from ramp intersection with the crossroad.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Distances of less than ¼ mile, when displayed, should be displayed to the nearest 1/10 mile.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <span class="ca">The Single-Exit Interchange (One Service) Mainline sign (SG42-1(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing </span> <span class="ca">Program (Logo Program) where there are at least four qualified facilities available with the possibility of more. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <span class="ca">The Single-Exit Interchange (One Service) Mainline sign (SG42-2(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing </span> <span class="ca">Program (Logo Program) where there are one or two qualified facilities available and it is not likely that there will be more </span> <span class="ca">than three. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">The Single-Exit Interchange (Two Services) Mainline sign (SG42-6(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing </span> <span class="ca">Program (Logo Program) where there are a limited number of services, three or four, in remote rural areas. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">The Single-Exit Interchange (Two Services) Mainline sign (SG42-7(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing </span> <span class="ca">Program (Logo Program) where there are a limited number of services, one or two, in remote rural areas. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <span class="ca">The Single-Exit Interchange (One Service) Mainline sign (SG42-9(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing </span> <span class="ca">Program (Logo Program) where there is only one service, in remote rural areas. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> <span class="ca">The Single-Exit Interchange (One Service) Mainline sign (SG42-10(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing </span> <span class="ca">Program (Logo Program) where there are at least two qualified facilities and it is not likely that there will be more than four. </span></p>
</div>

## 2J.08 Double-Exit Interchanges

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.08 &middot; manual page 779 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.08/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> At double-exit interchanges, the Specific Service signs should consist of two sections, one for each exit (see <a href="#fig-2j-1">Figure 2J-1</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> At a double-exit interchange, the top section shall display the business identification sign panels for the first exit and the bottom section shall display the business identification sign panels for the second exit. At numbered interchanges, the name of the service type and the exit number shall be displayed above the business identification sign panels in each section. At unnumbered interchanges, the word message NEXT RIGHT (LEFT) and SECOND RIGHT (LEFT) shall be used in place of the exit number. The number of business identification sign panels on the sign (total of both sections) or the sign assembly shall be limited to six.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> At a double-exit interchange, where a service type is displayed on two Specific Service signs in accordance with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j04">Section 2J.04</a>, one of the signs should display the business identification sign panels for that service type for the businesses that are accessible from one of the two exits and the other sign should display the business identification sign panels for that service type for the businesses that are accessible from the other exit.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2108(c). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> At a double-exit interchange where there are four business identification sign panels to be displayed for one of the exits and one or two business identification sign panels to be displayed for the other exit, the business identification sign panels may be arranged in three rows with two business identification sign panels per row.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to the CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, § 2110(f). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> At a double-exit interchange, where a service is to be signed for only one exit, one section of the Specific Service sign may be omitted, or a single exit interchange sign may be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Signs on ramps and crossroads as described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j07">Section 2J.07</a> <span class="ca">and <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j101ca">Section 2J.101(CA)</a> </span>may be used at a double-exit interchange.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">The Double-Exit Interchange Mainline sign (SG42-3(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing Program (Logo </span> <span class="ca">Program) where there are one or two qualified facilities available from each exit and it is not likely that there will be more </span> <span class="ca">than three from each exit. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">The Double-Exit Interchange Mainline sign (SG42-11(CA)) shall be used for the Specific Service Signing Program (Logo </span> <span class="ca">Program) where there is at least one qualified facility available from each exit and it is not likely that there will be more that </span> <span class="ca">two from each exit. </span></p>
</div>

## 2J.09 Collector-Distributor Roadways for Successive Interchanges

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.09 &middot; manual page 780 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.09/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Examples of Specific Service signs used in advance of interchanges for collector-distributor roadways that provide access to multiple interchanges are shown in <a href="#fig-2j-6">Figure 2J-6</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If services are available from more than one of the interchanges along the collector-distributor roadway and those services are signed with Specific Service signs as described in Paragraph 4 of this Section, then Specific Service signs may be used on the mainline in conformance with the provisions of this Chapter.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> No more than four Specific Service signs shall be displayed on a highway mainline approach to a collector- distributor roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If Specific Service signs are located on the highway mainline for services accessed from the collector- distributor roadway, then the business identification sign panels displayed on the collector- distributor roadway shall be only duplicates of those displayed on the highway mainline.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If more than four Specific Services signs would be required on the mainline in advance of the collector- distributor roadway in order to display all the business identification sign panels used on Specific Service signs in advance of the collector-distributor roadway exits, then General Service signs shall be used on the mainline to identify the types of services displayed on Specific Service signs on the collector-distributor roadway.</p>

## 2J.10 Specific Service Trailblazer Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.10 &middot; manual page 780 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.10/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Specific Service trailblazer signs (see <a href="#fig-2j-7">Figure 2J-7</a>) are guide signs with one to four business identification sign panels that display business identification and directional information for services and eligible attractions. Specific Service trailblazer signs are for facilities that have installed along crossroads business identification sign panels displayed along the main roadway and ramp, and that require additional vehicle maneuvers or are a long distance from the ramp along the crossroad.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Specific Service trailblazer signs shall be installed along crossroads where the route to the business requires a direction change, where it is questionable as to which roadway to follow, or where additional guidance is needed. Where it is not feasible or practical to install Specific Service trailblazer signs to such businesses, those businesses shall not be considered eligible for signing from the ramp and main roadway. A Specific Service trailblazer sign shall not be required at the point where the business is visible from the roadway and its access is readily apparent.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If used, a Specific Service trailblazer sign should be located a maximum of 500 feet in advance of any required turn.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The location of other traffic control devices shall take precedence over the location of a Specific Service trailblazer sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When used, each Specific Service trailblazer sign or sign assembly shall be limited to no more than four business identification sign panels. The business identification sign panels on Specific Service trailblazer signs shall be duplicates of those displayed on the Specific Service ramp signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Appropriate legends, such as directional arrows or the action message NEXT RIGHT or SECOND RIGHT, shall be displayed with the business identification sign panel to provide proper guidance. The directional legend and border shall be white and shall be displayed on a blue background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Specific Service trailblazer signs may contain various types of services on a single sign or on a sign assembly.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Specific Service trailblazer signs may be placed farther from the edge of the road than other traffic control signs.</p>
</div>

## 2J.11 Signs at Intersections

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.11 &middot; manual page 781 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.11/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> If both tourist-oriented information (see Chapter 2K) and specific service information are proposed to be used at the same intersection, the tourist-oriented directional and Specific Service signs should be spaced sufficiently apart from one another, as well as apart from other guide, warning, and regulatory signs, to avoid confusion and allow sufficient time for road users to read and react to the information.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If sufficient space to provide appropriate reading and reaction to all proposed signs is not available, higher priority shall be given to guide, warning, and regulatory signs and either the tourist- oriented directional signs or the Specific Service signs, or both, shall not be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If Specific Service signs are used on conventional roads or at intersections on expressways, they should be installed between the previous interchange or intersection and at least 300 feet in advance of the intersection from which the services are available.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Business identification sign panels should not be displayed for a type of service for which a qualified facility is readily visible.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If Specific Service signs are used on conventional roads or at intersections on expressways, the name of each type of service shall be displayed above its business identification sign panel(s), together with an appropriate legend, such as NEXT RIGHT (LEFT) or a directional arrow, either displayed on the same line as the name of the type of service or displayed below the business identification sign panel(s).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Signs similar to Specific Service ramp signs as described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j07">Section 2J.07</a> may be provided on the crossroad.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">Per SHC, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 3, § 101.7 includes the use of specific service signs for freeways only. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">The tourist-oriented information and specific service information signs shall be separate installations. Refer SHC, </span> <span class="ca">Division 1, Chapter 1.5, Article 3, § 229.285. </span></p>
</div>

## 2J.12 Signing Policy

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.12 &middot; manual page 781 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.12/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In addition to a statewide policy for eligibility of service providers (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j01">Section 2J.01</a>), each highway agency that elects to use Specific Service signs shall establish a signing policy.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The signing policy should include, at a minimum, the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j01">Section 2J.01</a> and at least the following criteria: A.Selection of eligible businesses; B.Distances to eligible services; C.The use of business identification sign panels, legends, and signs complying with the provisions of this Manual and State design requirements; D.Removal or covering of business identification sign panels during off seasons for businesses that operate on a seasonal basis; E.The circumstances, if any, under which Specific Service signs are permitted to be used in non-rural areas; and F.Determination of the costs to businesses for initial permits, installations, annual maintenance, and removal of business identification sign panels.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <span class="ca">SHC, Division 1, Chapter 1, Article 3, § 101.7 provides for placement of Specific Service Signs (Logo Sign Program) on all rural </span> <span class="ca">freeways in California. The term "rural" for this purpose means any area outside of an "urban" area. An urban area is an area </span> <span class="ca">encompassing a population of 5,000 or more. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <span class="ca">CCR, Title 21, Division 2, Chapter 19, §§ 2100 through 2120 contain standards for the Specific Service Signs (Logo Sign Program).</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <span class="ca">No new Specific Service (SG42 Series(CA)) signs shall be installed in a geographic area with a population over 5,000 as </span> <span class="ca">identified on maps prepared by Caltrans based on the most recent United States Bureau of Census data. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <span class="ca">Upon the district’s determination to terminate the logo sign program within a specified geographic area, the district </span> <span class="ca">shall cease the acceptance of new applications as well as the renewal of existing applications with immediate effect. </span> <span class="ca">Subsequently, the district must issue formal notification to all businesses currently participating in the program within the </span> <span class="ca">affected area. Such notification shall provide a minimum notice period of three months and shall stipulate that the removal </span> <span class="ca">of the specific service signs will become effective at the commencement of the following calendar year. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">General Service signs may be installed following the removal of specific service signs. </span></p>
</div>

## 2J.101(CA) Signs at Ramps (SG42-4(CA), SG42-5(CA), SG42-8(CA) and SG42-12(CA))

<div class="secmeta">Section 2J.101(CA) &middot; manual page 782 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/2J.101(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> <span class="ca">Specific Service (Ramp) Signs shall be located on, opposite of, or at the terminus of an off-ramp, in the same direction </span> <span class="ca">of travel as the Specific Service (Mainline) Signs (Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j07">Section 2J.07</a> and 2J.08). As viewed in the direction of travel, the </span> <span class="ca">successive signs shall be those for</span> “<span class="ca">ATTRACTION”, “CAMPING”, “LODGING”, “FOOD”, “EV CHARGING” and "FUEL" </span> <span class="ca">services in that order. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> <span class="ca">When spacing does not allow EV Charging Specific Service Ramp Signs to be located as described in Paragraph 01 of this </span> <span class="ca">Section, then the EV Charging Specific Service Ramp Signs may be located anywhere within the successive Specific Service Ramp </span> <span class="ca">Sign order where adequate spacing between signs allows. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <span class="ca">If either the business premises or an On-Site Sign of a Qualified Specific Service Business is not visible from any point </span> <span class="ca">on the off-ramp or from the terminus of the off-ramp, the Owner or Responsible Operator shall be required to make </span> <span class="ca">application to have a Logo Panel placed on a Specific Service (Ramp) Sign. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <span class="ca">If either the business premises or an on-site sign of a Qualified Specific Service Business is visible from any point on the off-</span> <span class="ca">ramp or from the terminus of the off-ramp, the Owner or Responsible Operator may apply for placement of a Logo Panel on the </span> <span class="ca">Specific Service (Ramp) Sign. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <span class="ca">Caltrans may require that a Logo panel be placed on a Specific Service (Ramp) Sign when either the business premises or an </span> <span class="ca">On-Site Sign is visible from the off-ramp or from the terminus of the off-ramp, if a sign is necessary to avoid misdirection of the </span> <span class="ca">motorist because of the complexity of the interchange. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <span class="ca">Appropriate trailblazers may be required by Caltrans along other public highways as necessary to adequately direct road users </span> <span class="ca">to the business referred to on any Logo Panel. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2j/#2j10">Section 2J.10</a>. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">The Logo Panels fastened to a Specific Service (Ramp) Sign or a trailblazer sign shall be the same in shape, color, and </span> <span class="ca">message as those shown on the Specific Service (Mainline) Signs, but shall be of smaller size. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">The Specific Service Ramp sign (SG42-4(CA)) may be used for the Specific Service Signing Program (Logo Program) at an exit </span> <span class="ca">ramp where there are one or two qualified facilities available and it is not likely that there will be more than three in each direction. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <span class="ca">The Specific Service Ramp sign (SG42-5(CA)) may be used for the Specific Service Signing Program (Logo Program) at an exit </span> <span class="ca">ramp where there are only one or two qualified facilities in only one direction. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> <span class="ca">The Specific Service Ramp sign (SG42-12(CA)) may be used for the Specific Service Signing Program (Logo Program) where </span> <span class="ca">there is only one qualified facility available and it is not likely that there will ever be more. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> <span class="ca">Ramp signs shall be installed along the ramp or at the ramp terminal for facilities that have logo panels displayed along </span> <span class="ca">the main roadway if the facilities are not readily visible from the ramp terminal. Directions to the service facilities shall be </span> <span class="ca">indicated by arrows on the ramp signs. Logo panels on Specific Service ramp signs shall be duplicates of those displayed </span> <span class="ca">on the mainline signs located in advance of the interchange, but shall be reduced in size. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> <span class="ca">The Specific Service Ramp sign (SG42-8(CA)) may be used for the Specific Service Signing Program (Logo Program) in </span> <span class="ca">combination with a Directional Arrow Auxiliary (M6 Series) signs, at an exit ramp terminus, as a follow-up sign to freeway signs. </span> <span class="ca"> A Mileage Plate may be applied to the sign panel, under the business logo where a business is not visible from the sign’s location. </span></p>
</div>
