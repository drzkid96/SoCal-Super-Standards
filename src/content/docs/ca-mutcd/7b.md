## 7B.01 Design of School Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.01 &middot; manual page 1470 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Except as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a07">Section 2A.07</a>, the sizes of signs and plaques to be used on conventional roadways in school areas shall be as shown in <a href="#fig-7b-1">Table 7B-1</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The sizes in the Oversized column in <a href="#fig-7b-1">Table 7B-1</a> shall be used on expressways in school areas.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The sizes in the Oversized column should be used on roadways that have four or more lanes with posted speed limits of 40 mph or higher.</p>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>

</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> School warning signs, including the “SCHOOL” portion of the School Speed Limit (S5-1) sign and including any supplemental plaques used in association with these warning signs, shall have a fluorescent yellow-green background with a black legend and border unless otherwise provided in this Manual for a specific sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The signs used for school area traffic control shall be retroreflective or illuminated.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>

<p><span class="pn">08</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a15">Section 2A.15</a> contains provisions regarding the mounting heights of signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a16">Section 2A.16</a> contains provisions regarding the lateral offsets of signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>) contains information regarding sign lettering.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> In-roadway signs for school traffic control areas may be used consistent with the requirements of Sections 2B.20 and 7B.03.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>

</div>

## 7B.02 School Area Signs and Plaques

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.02 &middot; manual page 1470 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Many state and local jurisdictions find it beneficial to advise road users that they are approaching a school that is adjacent to a highway, where additional care is needed, even though no school crossing is involved and the speed limit remains unchanged. Additionally, some jurisdictions designate school zones that have a unique legal standing in that fines for speeding or other traffic violations within designated school zones are increased or special enforcement techniques such as photo radar systems are used. It is important and sometimes legally necessary to mark the beginning and end points of these designated school zones so that the road user is given proper notice. The School (S1-1) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> <span class="ca">or 7B-1(CA)</span>) has the following four applications: A.School Area – the S1-1 sign can be used to warn road users that they are approaching a school area that might include school buildings or grounds, a school crossing, or school related activity adjacent to the highway. B.School Zone – the S1-1 sign can be used to identify the location of the beginning of a designated school zone. C.School Advance Crossing – if combined with an AHEAD (W16-9P) plaque or an XX FEET (W16-2P or W16- 2aP) plaque to comprise the School Advance Crossing assembly (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>), the S1-1 sign can be used to warn road users that they are approaching a crossing where schoolchildren cross the roadway (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7b/#7b03">Section 7B.03</a>). D.School Crossing – if combined with a diagonal downward-pointing arrow (W16-7P) plaque to comprise the School Crossing assembly (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>), the S1-1 sign can be used to warn approaching road users of the location of a crossing where schoolchildren cross the roadway (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7b/#7b03">Section 7B.03</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">02a</span> <span class="ca">The School Assemblies A(CA) through E(CA) are shown in <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> and <a href="#fig-7b-1ca">Table 7B-1(CA)</a>. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If a school area or school zone is located on a cross street in close proximity to the intersection, a School (S1-1) sign with a supplemental arrow (W16-5P or W16-6P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be installed on each approach of the street or highway to warn road users making a turn onto the cross street that they will encounter a school area soon after making the turn.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If a school zone has been designated under State or local statute, a School (S1-1) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> <span class="ca">or 7B-</span> <span class="ca">1(CA)</span>) shall be installed to identify the beginning point(s) of the designated school zone (see <a href="#fig-7b-2">Figure 7B-2</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p>A School Zone (S1-1) sign may be supplemented with a SCHOOL (S4-3P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> <span class="ca">or 7B-1(CA)</span>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> A School Zone (S1-1) sign may be supplemented with an ALL YEAR (S4-7P) plaque (see Figure 7B- 1) if the school operates on a 12-month schedule.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The downstream end of a designated school zone may be identified with an END SCHOOL ZONE (S5-2) sign (see Figures 7B-1 and 7B-2).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">The School Warning Assembly A(CA) does not need to be posted if there are no school pedestrians using the highway and the </span> <span class="ca">school grounds are separated from the highway by a fence, gate or other physical barrier. Refer to CVC § 22352. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <span class="ca">The School Warning Assembly A(CA) shall be used on streets with prima facie 25 mph speed limits that are contiguous </span> <span class="ca">to a school building or school grounds. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> <span class="ca">The SCHOOL (S4-3P) plaque shall not be used alone. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> <span class="ca">If used, the School Warning Assembly A(CA) should be posted at the school boundary. Refer to CVC § 22352. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> <span class="ca">If used, the School Warning Assembly A(CA) may be posted up to 500 feet in advance of the school boundary. Refer to CVC § </span> <span class="ca">22352. </span></p>
</div>

## 7B.03 School Crossing Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.03 &middot; manual page 1471 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The School Advance Crossing assembly (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> <span class="ca">or 7B-1(CA)</span>) shall consist of a <span class="ca">School Advance warning </span> <span class="ca">Assembly D(CA), or a</span>School (S1-1) sign supplemented with an AHEAD (W16-9P) plaque or an XX FEET (W16-2P or W16-2aP) plaque.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 3 of this Section, a School Advance Crossing assembly <span class="ca">or Assembly D(CA)</span> shall be used in advance (see <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a> for advance placement guidelines) of the first School Crossing assembly that is encountered in each direction as traffic approaches a school crosswalk (see Figure 7B- 3).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p>The School Advance Crossing assembly <span class="ca">or Assembly D(CA)</span>may be omitted (see <a href="#fig-7b-2">Figure 7B-2</a>) where a School Zone (S1- 1) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7b/#7b02">Section 7B.02</a>) is installed to identify the beginning of a school zone in advance of the School Crossing assembly.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If a school crosswalk is located on a cross street in close proximity to an intersection, a School Advance Crossing assembly with a supplemental arrow (W16-5P or W16-6P) plaque may be installed on each approach of the street or highway to warn road users making a turn onto the cross street that they will encounter a school crosswalk soon after making the turn (see <a href="#fig-7b-3">Figure 7B-3</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A 12-inch reduced size in-street School (S1-1) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>), installed in compliance with the mounting height and special mounting support requirements for an In-Street Pedestrian Crossing (R1-6 or R1-6a) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b20">Section 2B.20</a>), may be used in advance of a school crossing to supplement the post-mounted school warning signs. A 12 x 6-inch reduced size AHEAD (W16-9P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be mounted below the reduced size in-street School (S1-1) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05a</span> <span class="ca">The School Advance Warning Assembly D(CA) shall be used in advance of any School Crosswalk Warning Assembly </span> <span class="ca">B(CA), School Crosswalk Warning Assembly E(CA) or the School Speed Limit Assembly C(CA). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If used, the School Crossing assembly <span class="ca">Assembly B(CA)</span>(see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> <span class="ca">or 7B-1(CA)</span>) shall be installed at the school crossing (see Figures 7B-2 and 7B-3), or as close to it as possible, and shall consist of a School (S1-1) sign supplemented with a diagonal downward-pointing arrow (W16-7P) plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c63">Section 2C.63</a>) to show the location of the crossing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The School Crossing assembly<span class="ca">Assembly B(CA) or E(CA)</span>shall not be used at crossings other than those adjacent to schools and those on established school pedestrian routes.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The School Crossing assembly <span class="ca">Assembly B(CA) or E(CA)</span> shall not be installed on an approach controlled by a STOP<span class="ca">(R1-1) sign</span>or a YIELD <span class="ca">(R1-2)</span>sign <span class="ca">or a traffic signal,</span>except as provided in Paragraphs 9 and 10 of this Section.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08a</span> <span class="ca">The School Crosswalk Warning Assembly B(CA) or E(CA) shall be posted at all yellow school crosswalks that are not </span> <span class="ca">controlled by a STOP (R1-1) sign, a YIELD (R1-2) sign or a traffic signal. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08b</span> <span class="ca">The School Crosswalk Warning Assembly B(CA) or E(CA) should be posted at all white school crosswalks that are not </span> <span class="ca">controlled by a STOP (R1-1) sign, a YIELD (R1-2) sign or a traffic signal. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08c</span> <span class="ca">The School Crosswalk Warning Assemblies B(CA) and E(CA) are shown in <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a> and 7B-101(CA) through 7B-104(CA). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> The School Crossing assembly may be installed on an approach to a circular intersection controlled by a YIELD sign where the crosswalk is at least 20 feet in advance of the yield point at the entrance to a circulatory roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> At a signalized or stop-controlled intersection the School Crossing assembly may be installed on an approach to a channelized right turn lane controlled by a YIELD sign where the crosswalk is at least 20 feet in advance of the yield point.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> A Yield Here To (Stop Here For) School Crossing (R1-5a or R1-5c) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-4">Figure 7B-4</a>) may be used, in accordance with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b19">Section 2B.19</a>, in advance of a marked crosswalk that crosses an uncontrolled multi-lane approach within school zones.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">11a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in Paragraph 11 text. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The In-Street Pedestrian Crossing (R1-6 or R1-6a) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b20">Section 2B.20</a> and <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) or the In- Street School Crossing (R1-6b or R1-6c) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be used at school crossings on approaches that are not controlled by a traffic control signal, a pedestrian hybrid beacon, or emergency- vehicle hybrid beacon. If used at a school crossing, a 12 x 4-inch SCHOOL (S4-3P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be mounted above the sign. The STATE LAW legend on the R1-6 series signs may be omitted.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The In-Street Pedestrian Crossing (R1-6 or R1-6a) sign or In-Street School Crossing (R1-6b or R1-6c) sign may be used at intersections or midblock crossings with flashing beacons.</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> The Overhead School Crossing (R1-9b or R1-9c) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be used at school crossings on approaches that are not controlled by a traffic control signal, pedestrian hybrid beacon, or an emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon. The STATE LAW legend on the R1-9 series signs may be omitted.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">14a</span> <span class="ca">If used, the School Crosswalk Warning Assembly E(CA) (see Figures 7B-1 and 7B-101(CA) through 7B-104(CA)) shall </span> <span class="ca">be installed in an overhead location at the marked crosswalk, or as close to it as possible, and shall consist of a modified </span> <span class="ca">R1-9 sign to show the location of the crossing. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">14b</span> <span class="ca">For uncontrolled locations with more than one lane in each direction of travel, advance yield lines (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3b/#3b19">Section 3B.19</a>) may be </span> <span class="ca">used with the ‘Yield Here to Pedestrians’ signs (R1-5 or R1-5a). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> When used at an uncontrolled crossing, the In-Street or Overhead Pedestrian Crossing sign shall be used only as a supplement to a School Crossing assembly with a diagonal downward-pointing arrow (W16-7P) plaque at the crosswalk location.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> A 12-inch reduced size in-street School (S1-1) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be used instead of the In- Street Pedestrian Crossing (R1-6 or R1-6a) or the In-Street School Crossing (R1-6b or R1-6c) sign at a school crossing on approaches that are not controlled by a traffic control signal, pedestrian hybrid beacon, or an emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon. A 12 x 6-inch reduced size diagonal downward-pointing arrow (W16-7P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be mounted below the reduced size in-street School (S1-1) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">16a</span> <span class="ca">The In-Street Pedestrian Crossing and the In-Street Schoolchildren Crossing (R1-6a and R1-6c) signs are deleted as a stop is </span> <span class="ca">not required in California per CVC § 21950. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> If an In-Street Pedestrian Crossing sign, an In-Street School Crossing sign, or a reduced size in- street School (S1-1) sign is placed in the roadway, the sign support shall comply with the mounting height and special mounting support requirements for an In-Street Pedestrian Crossing (R1-6 or R1-6a) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b20">Section 2B.20</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> The In-Street Pedestrian Crossing sign, the In-Street School Crossing sign, the Overhead Pedestrian Crossing sign, and the reduced size in-street School (S1-1) sign shall not be used on approaches that are controlled <span class="ca">by stop control,</span> a traffic control signal, pedestrian hybrid beacon, or an emergency- vehicle hybrid beacon.</p>
</div>

## 7B.04 School Bus Stop Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.04 &middot; manual page 1473 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The School Bus Stop Ahead (S3-1) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) should be installed in advance of locations where a school bus, when stopped to pick up or discharge passengers, is not visible to road users for an adequate distance and where there is no opportunity to relocate the school bus stop to provide adequate sight distance.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01a</span> <span class="ca">The School Bus Stop Ahead (S3-1) sign shall be installed in advance of an approved school bus stop where there is not </span> <span class="ca">a clear view in advance of the stop from a distance of 200 feet. Refer to CVC § 22504(c). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The SCHOOL BUS TURN AHEAD (S3-2) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) may be installed in advance of locations where a school bus turns around on a roadway at a location not visible to approaching road users for a distance as determined by the “0” column under Condition B of <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a>, and where there is no opportunity to relocate the school bus turn around to provide the distance provided in <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a>.</p>
</div>

## 7B.05 School Speed Limit Signs and Plaques

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.05 &middot; manual page 1473 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.05/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A School Speed Limit assembly <span class="ca">Assembly C(CA)</span> (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) or a School Speed Limit When Flashing (S5- 1) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) shall be used to indicate the speed limit where a reduced school speed limit zone has been established based upon an engineering study or where a reduced school speed limit is specified for such areas by statute. The School Speed Limit assembly <span class="ca">Assembly C(CA)</span> or School Speed Limit When Flashing sign shall be placed at or as near as practicable to the point where the reduced school speed limit zone begins (see Figures 7B-2 and 7B-4).</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If a reduced school speed limit zone has been established, a School (S1-1) sign shall be installed in advance (see <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a> for advance placement guidelines) of the first School Speed Limit sign assembly or S5-1 sign that is encountered in each direction as traffic approaches the reduced school speed limit zone (see Figures 7B-2 and 7B-4).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 4 of this Section, the <span class="ca">The </span>downstream end of an authorized and posted reduced school speed limit zone shall be identified with an END SCHOOL SPEED LIMIT (S5-3) <span class="ca">and/or Speed </span> <span class="ca">Limit (R2-1)</span> sign (see Figures 7B-1, <span class="ca">7B-1(CA)</span>, 7B-2, and <span class="ca">7B-2(CA)</span>7B-4).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If a reduced school speed limit zone ends at the same point as a designated school zone (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7b/#7b02">Section 7B.02</a>), an END SCHOOL ZONE (S5-2) sign may be used instead of an END SCHOOL SPEED LIMIT (S5- 3) sign. A standard Speed Limit sign showing the speed limit for the section of highway that is downstream from the authorized and posted reduced school speed limit zone may be mounted on the same post above the END SCHOOL SPEED LIMIT (S5-3) sign or the END SCHOOL ZONE (S5-2) sign.<span class="ca">or the Speed Limit (R2-1) sign may be posted by itself (see Figures 7B-2(CA) </span> <span class="ca">and 7B-102(CA)).</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The beginning point of a reduced school speed limit zone should be at least 200 feet in advance of the school grounds or a school crossing; however, this 200-foot distance should be increased if the reduced school speed limit is 30 mph or higher. The maximum beginning point of a reduced school speed limit zone should not be greater than 500 feet in advance of the school grounds or a school crossing.<span class="ca">Refer Figures 7B-1(CA), 7B-2, 7B-2(CA), and 7B-101(CA) through </span> <span class="ca">7B-103(CA).</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The School Speed Limit assembly <span class="ca">Assembly C(CA)</span> shall be either a static sign assembly, a blank-out sign, or a changeable message sign (see Chapter 2L).</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The static School Speed Limit assembly <span class="ca">Assembly C(CA)</span>shall consist of a top plaque (S4-3P) with the legend SCHOOL, a Speed Limit (R2-1) sign, and a bottom plaque <span class="ca">WHEN CHILDREN ARE PRESENT </span>(S4-1P, S4-2P, S4-4P, or S4-6P) indicating the specific periods of the day and/or days of the week that the special school speed limit is in effect (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> When a School Speed Limit When Flashing (S5-1) sign or a Speed Limit (R2-1) sign with a supplemental WHEN FLASHING (S4-4P) plaque is used, a Speed Limit Sign Beacon (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4s/#4s04">Section 4S.04</a>) shall be used to identify the periods that the school speed limit is in effect.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Fluorescent yellow-green pixels shall be used when the “SCHOOL” message is displayed on a changeable message sign for a school speed limit.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Changeable message signs may use blank-out messages or other methods in order to display the school speed limit only during the periods it applies.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> A Vehicle Speed Feedback (W13-20aP) plaque that displays the speed of approaching drivers (see Sections 2B.21 and 2C.13), that is part of a School Speed Limit assembly or a School Speed Limit When Flashing (S5-1) sign, may be used in a school speed limit zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> If used, the Vehicle Speed Feedback (W13-20aP) plaque should only be used during the time period when the school speed limit is in effect.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> A Reduced School Speed Limit Ahead (S4-5or S4-5a) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a><span class="ca">)</span>) should be used to inform road users of a reduced speed zone where the speed limit is being reduced by more than 10 mph, or where engineering judgment indicates that advance notice would be appropriate<span class="ca">for the School Advance Warning Assembly D(CA).</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> If used, the Reduced School Speed Limit Ahead sign shall be followed by a School Speed Limit sign or a School Speed Limit assembly <span class="ca">Assembly C(CA).</span></p>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> The speed limit displayed on the Reduced School Speed Limit Ahead sign shall be identical to the speed limit displayed on the subsequent School Speed Limit sign or School Speed Limit assembly <span class="ca">Assembly C(CA)</span>. <span class="ca">EXTENDED 25 MPH AND/OR REDUCED SPEEDS IN SCHOOL ZONES (CVC § 22352) </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> <span class="ca">For school area traffic control with a reduced school zone speed limit of 15 mph and/or an extended school zone speed limit of </span> <span class="ca">25 mph in a residential district, the Reduced Speed School Zone Ahead (S4-5, S4-5a) sign should be used to give advance notice of </span> <span class="ca">a reduced 15 mph school zone speed limit and/or an extended school zone speed limit of 25 mph.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> <span class="ca">For school area traffic control with a reduced school zone speed limit of 20 mph and/or an extended school zone speed limit of </span> <span class="ca">25 mph in a residential district, the Reduced Speed School Zone Ahead (S4-5, S4-5a) sign may be used to give advance notice of a </span> <span class="ca">reduced 15 mph school zone speed limit and/or an extended school zone speed limit of 25 mph. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> <span class="ca">The School Speed Limit Assembly C(CA) shall be used on streets with speed limits greater than 25 mph that are </span> <span class="ca">contiguous to a school building or school grounds. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">19</span> <span class="ca">The School Speed Limit Assembly C(CA) is shown in <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">20</span> <span class="ca">If used, the School Speed Limit Assembly C(CA) may be posted up to 500 feet in advance of the school boundary.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">21</span> <span class="ca">The “WHEN FLASHING” and specific time period messages shall not be used in school areas in California as they are </span> <span class="ca">not supported by CVC § 22352. Hence, the Specific Time Period Plaque (S4-1P), WHEN FLASHING (S4-4P) and SCHOOL </span> <span class="ca">SPEED LIMIT 20 WHEN FLASHING (S5-1) signs shall not be used in California. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">22</span> <span class="ca">The “WHEN FLASHING” message is misleading because it suggests that the speed limit is in force only when the flashing </span> <span class="ca">beacons are in operation. The prima facie speed limit of 25 mph is in effect based on the presence of children per CVC § 22352, not </span> <span class="ca">on the operation of the flashing beacons. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">23</span> <span class="ca">Not using the “WHEN FLASHING” message also addresses the situation when children are present but the flashing beacons are </span> <span class="ca">inoperative for any reason. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">24</span> <span class="ca">Not using the “WHEN FLASHING” message does not alter the warrants or the use of a flashing yellow beacon or its </span> <span class="ca">effectiveness as an attention-getting device. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">25</span> <span class="ca">The specific time period message is misleading because it suggests that the speed limit is in force only during the time period </span> <span class="ca">specified. The prima facie speed limit of 25 mph is in effect based on the presence of children per CVC § 22352, not on the time </span> <span class="ca">period specified. </span> <span class="ca">EXTENDED 25 MPH AND/OR REDUCED SPEEDS IN SCHOOL ZONES (CVC § 22358.4) </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">26</span> <span class="ca">A local authority may declare a 20 or 15 mph prima facie speed limit within 500 feet of a school building or school grounds and </span> <span class="ca">an extended 25 mph prima facie speed limit within 500 to 1000 feet from a school or school grounds. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">27</span> <span class="ca">The extended 25 mph school speed zone can provide a progressive speed reduction. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">28</span> <span class="ca">If the local authority declares by ordinance or resolution the above prima facie speed limits, all of the following criteria </span> <span class="ca">shall be met: </span> <span class="ca">A.</span><span class="ca">Street or highway is in a residential district. </span> <span class="ca">B.</span><span class="ca">Street or highway outside of a school zone has a posted speed limit no greater than 30 mph. </span> <span class="ca">C.</span><span class="ca">Street or highway has no more than a total of two through traffic lanes (one in each direction or two in one </span> <span class="ca">direction). </span> <span class="ca">D.</span><span class="ca">The reduced school zone speed limit of 20 or 15 mph is within 500 feet of school grounds. </span> <span class="ca">E.</span><span class="ca">The extended school zone speed limit of 25 mph is within 500 to 1000 feet of school grounds. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">29</span> <span class="ca">When used, a local ordinance or resolution adopted to establish a 20 or 15 mph reduced school zone speed limit and/or </span> <span class="ca">an extended 25 mph school zone speed limit shall not be effective until School Speed Limit Assembly C (CA) giving notice </span> <span class="ca">of the speed limit(s) is erected upon the highway. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">30</span> <span class="ca">On a State highway, the ordinance or resolution shall not be effective until the ordinance or resolution has been </span> <span class="ca">approved by Caltrans and appropriate school zone speed signs are erected upon the State highway. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">31</span> <span class="ca">For purposes of a 20 or 15 mph reduced prima facie speed limit, School Speed Limit Assembly C (CA) indicating a </span> <span class="ca">speed limit of 20 or 15 mph shall be placed at a distance up to 500 feet away from school grounds. For purposes of an </span> <span class="ca">extended 25 mph prima facie speed limit, School Speed Limit Assembly C (CA) indicating a speed limit of 25 mph shall be </span> <span class="ca">placed at any distance between 500 to 1,000 feet away from school grounds. Refer to <a href="#fig-7b-103ca">Figure 7B-103(CA)</a>. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">32</span> <span class="ca">The established school speed limits shall be effective when children are going to or leaving the school, either during </span> <span class="ca">school hours or during the noon recess hour. The school speed limits shall also apply when the school grounds are not </span> <span class="ca">separated from the highway by a fence, gate, or other physical barrier while the grounds are in use by children (this </span> <span class="ca">condition can apply at any time of day or any day of the week). </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">32</span> <span class="ca">The determination to reduce a prima facie speed limit to 20 or 15 mph and/or extend a 25 mph school zone speed limit, </span> <span class="ca">as described above, shall be documented in writing, in an engineering study. The engineering study shall identify the </span> <span class="ca">provisions of Section 627 of the California Vehicle Code (CVC) that support the reduced and/or extended school zone </span> <span class="ca">speed limit(s). </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">33</span> <span class="ca">When preparing an engineering study pursuant to the Standard above, the local authority should cite all elements of an </span> <span class="ca">Engineering and Traffic Survey, as discussed in CVC § 627, that support the need for a reduced speed limit of 20 or 15 mph and/or </span> <span class="ca">an extended 25 mph school zone speed limit. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">34</span> <span class="ca">The documentation of prevailing speeds found in CVC § 627 can be used to establish an existing speed profile for the school </span> <span class="ca">zone, but the 85</span><span class="ca">th</span><span class="ca"> percentile speed is not used to set the reduced or extended school speed limit. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">35</span> <span class="ca">The local authority shall reimburse Caltrans for all costs incurred by Caltrans under this section. </span></p>
</div>

## 7B.06 Higher Fines Zone Signs and Plaques in School Areas

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.06 &middot; manual page 1476 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.06/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">00</span> <span class="ca">The section is deleted as there is no law on higher fines in school areas in California. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The signs and plaques used to inform road users of higher fines zones and their locations depend on whether the fines apply to all traffic violations or only to speeding violations. Their locations also depend on whether the higher fines zone begins and/or ends at the same point as the school zone or school speed limit zone. Figures 7B-4 and 7B-5 show examples of higher fines zones signing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Where increased fines are imposed for traffic violations within a designated school zone: A.A BEGIN HIGHER FINES ZONE (R2-10) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) or a FINES HIGHER (R2-6P), FINES DOUBLE (R2-6aP), or $XX FINE (R2-6bP) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) shall be installed as a supplement to the School Zone (S1-1) sign to identify the beginning point of the higher fines zone (see Figures 7B-4 and 7B-5); and B.An END HIGHER FINES ZONE (R2-11) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) or an END SCHOOL ZONE (S5-2) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) shall be installed at the downstream end of the zone to notify road users of the termination of the increased fines zone (see <a href="#fig-7b-5">Figure 7B-5</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If exceeding the speed limit is the only traffic violation that is subject to higher fines, a FINES HIGHER (R2- 6P), FINES DOUBLE (R2-6aP), or $XX FINE (R2-6bP) plaque shall be posted with the School Speed Limit (S5- 1) sign and shall not be posted beneath the School Zone (S1-1) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7b/#7b05">Section 7B.05</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If the portion of the roadway that is subject to higher fines does not begin at the location of the School Zone (S1-1) sign, a BEGIN HIGHER FINES ZONE (R2-10) sign shall be placed at the point where the higher fines begin (see Sheet 2 of <a href="#fig-7b-5">Figure 7B-5</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If a BEGIN HIGHER FINES ZONE (R2-10) sign is used downstream of the School Zone (S1-1) sign, a FINES HIGHER (R2-6P), FINES DOUBLE (R2-6aP), or $XX FINE (R2-6bP) plaque may also be placed beneath the School Zone (S1-1) sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Where appropriate, one of the following plaques may be mounted below the sign that identifies the beginning point of the higher fines zone: A.A S4-1P plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) specifying the times that the higher fines are in effect, B.A WHEN CHILDREN ARE PRESENT (S4-2P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>), or C.A WHEN FLASHING (S4-4P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-7b-1">Figure 7B-1</a>) if used in conjunction with a yellow flashing beacon.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> If other traffic violations in addition to exceeding the speed limit are subject to higher fines, then the duplicate FINES HIGHER (R2-6P), FINES DOUBLE (R2-6aP), or $XX FINE (R2-6bP) plaque should be omitted from the School Speed Limit When Flashing (S5-1) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7b/#7b05">Section 7B.05</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If a higher fines zone ends at the same point as a reduced school speed limit zone, an END SCHOOL ZONE (S5-2) sign may be used instead of a combination of an END HIGHER FINES ZONE (R2-11) sign and an END SCHOOL SPEED LIMIT (S5-3) sign (see <a href="#fig-7b-5">Figure 7B-5</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Where the higher fines zone is established by statute, the BEGIN HIGHER FINES ZONE (R2-10) sign, FINES HIGHER (R2-6P), FINES DOUBLE (R2-6aP), and $XX FINE (R2-6bP) plaques may be omitted.</p>
</div>

## 7B.07 Parking and Stopping (R7 and R8 Series) Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 7B.07 &middot; manual page 1476 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/7B.07/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Parking and stopping regulatory signs may be used to prevent parked or waiting vehicles from blocking pedestrians’ views, and drivers’ views of pedestrians, and to control vehicles as a part of the school traffic plan.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Parking signs and other signs governing the stopping and standing of vehicles in school areas cover a wide variety of regulations. Typical examples of regulations are as follows: A.NO PARKING X:XX AM to X:XX PM SCHOOL DAYS ONLY B.NO STOPPING X:XX AM to X:XX PM SCHOOL DAYS ONLY, C.XX MIN LOADING X:XX AM to X:XX PM SCHOOL DAYS ONLY, and D.NO STANDING X:XX AM to X:XX PM SCHOOL DAYS ONLY.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Sections 2B.53 through 2B.55 contain information regarding the signing of parking regulations in school zone areas.</p>
</div>
