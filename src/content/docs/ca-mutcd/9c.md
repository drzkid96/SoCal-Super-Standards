## 9C.02 Intersection Warning Signs (W2 Series)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.02 &middot; manual page 1609 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Intersection Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W2-1.svg">W2-1</a> through <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w2-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W2-5.svg">W2-5</a>) signs (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) may be used on a roadway, street, or shared-use path in advance of an intersection to indicate the presence of an intersection and the possibility of turning or entering traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> When engineering judgment determines that the visibility of the intersection is limited on the shared- use path approach, Intersection Warning signs should be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Intersection Warning signs should not be used where the shared-use path approach to the intersection is controlled by a STOP sign, a YIELD sign, or a traffic control signal.</p>
</div>

## 9C.03 Bicycle Surface Condition Warning Sign (W8-10)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.03 &middot; manual page 1609 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Bicycle Surface Condition Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-10.svg">W8-10</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) may be installed where roadway <span class="ca">bikeway, </span>or shared-use path conditions could cause a bicyclist to lose control of the bicycle.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Signs warning of other conditions that might be of concern to bicyclists, including BUMP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-1.svg">W8-1</a>), DIP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-2.svg">W8-2</a>), PAVEMENT ENDS (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-3.svg">W8-3</a>), and any other word message that describes conditions that are of concern to bicyclists, may also be used (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A supplemental plaque may be used to clarify the specific type of surface condition.</p>
</div>

## 9C.04 Bicycle Warning and Trail Crossing Signs (W11-1 and W11-15)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.04 &middot; manual page 1609 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Bicycle Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-1.svg">W11-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) alerts the road user to unexpected entries into the roadway by bicyclists, and other crossing activities that might cause conflicts. These conflicts might be relatively confined, or might occur randomly over a segment of roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9c/#9c06">Section 9C.06</a> contains information for Bicycle Cross Traffic Warning plaques that can be used below STOP signs on crossroads or driveways that intersect with bicycle facilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Trail Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) may be used where both bicyclists and pedestrians might be crossing the roadway, such as at an intersection with a shared-use path. A TRAIL X-ING (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15P.svg">W11-15P</a>) supplemental plaque may be mounted below the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a> sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in labeling Paragraph 4. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details.</span></p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If used in advance of a trail crossing, a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15a.svg">W11-15a</a> sign should be supplemented with an AHEAD (W16- 9P) or XX FEET (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-2p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-2P.svg">W16-2P</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-2ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-2aP.svg">W16-2aP</a>) plaque to inform road users that they are approaching a point where crossing activity might occur.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for errors in Paragraph 4 and in between Paragraphs 4 and 5. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for  more details.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If used in advance of a specific crossing point, the Bicycle Warning or Trail Crossing sign should be placed at a distance in advance of the crossing location that complies with <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Bicycle Warning and Trail Crossing signs, when used at the location of the crossing, shall be supplemented with a diagonal downward-pointing arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-7p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-7P.svg">W16-7P</a>) plaque to show the location of the crossing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A fluorescent yellow-green background color with a black legend and border may be used for Bicycle Warning and Trail Crossing signs and supplemental plaques.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> When the fluorescent yellow-green background color is used, a systematic approach featuring one background color within a zone or area should be used. The mixing of standard yellow and fluorescent yellow-green backgrounds within a zone or area should be avoided.</p>
</div>

## 9C.05 EXCEPT BICYCLES Warning Plaque (W16-20P)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.05 &middot; manual page 1610 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.05/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Where it might be advantageous to notify bicyclists that the conditions or hazards depicted by a warning sign are not applicable to bicycles, the EXCEPT BICYCLES (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-20p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-20P.svg">W16-20P</a>) warning plaque (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) may be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Examples of warning signs where an EXCEPT BICYCLES warning plaque can be mounted include DEAD END (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w14-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W14-1.svg">W14-1</a>) and NO OUTLET (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w14-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W14-2.svg">W14-2</a>) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c24">Section 2C.24</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Sections 2C.57 and 2C.58 contain information on the design of supplemental warning plaques.</p>
</div>

## 9C.06 Two-Way Bicycle Cross Traffic Warning Plaque (W16-21P)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.06 &middot; manual page 1610 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.06/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> When used, the Two-Way Bicycle Cross Traffic (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-21p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-21P.svg">W16-21P</a>) warning plaque (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) shall be installed below a STOP or YIELD sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The Two-Way Bicycle Cross Traffic warning plaque may be used below STOP or YIELD signs on crossroads and driveways to alert road users of an unexpected bicycle movement.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Two-Way Bicycle Cross Traffic warning plaque can help minimize overuse or misapplication of other warning signs such as the Bicycle Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-1.svg">W11-1</a>) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The Two-Way Bicycle Cross Traffic warning plaque should be used in combination with a STOP or YIELD sign when a counter-flow or two-way bicycle facility has an approach that is counter to the customary scanning behavior of a motorist at that location.</p>
</div>

## 9C.07 Bicycle Lane Ends Warning Sign (W9-5) and Bicycles Merging Sign (W9-5a)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.07 &middot; manual page 1610 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.07/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Where a warning sign is appropriate, the Bicycle Lane Ends (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-5.svg">W9-5</a>) warning sign (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) is intended to alert road users that a bicycle lane is ending and that bicycles will share or occupy the travel lane after merging.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The Bicycle Lane Ends warning sign may be used in advance of the end of a bicycle lane to warn that a bicycle lane will be ending.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Bicycles Merging (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-5a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-5a.svg">W9-5a</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) may be used where a bicycle merging maneuver might occur. The Bicycles Merging sign may be used in addition to the Bicycle Lane Ends (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-5.svg">W9-5</a>) warning sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> To avoid excessive use of signs, the Bicycle Lane Ends warning sign should not be used where a bicycle lane is dropped on the approach to an intersection and resumes immediately after the intersection.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A Bicycles Allowed Use of Full Lane (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-20/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-20.svg">R9-20</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9b/#9b14">Section 9B.14</a>) and/or shared-lane markings (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9e/#9e09">Section 9E.09</a>) may be installed downstream of the merge area.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> A <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-2ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-2aP.svg">W16-2aP</a> supplemental warning plaque may be used to inform road users of the distance to the end of the bicycle lane and/or to the bicycle merge.</p>
</div>

## 9C.08 Other Bicycle Warning Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.08 &middot; manual page 1611 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.08/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Other bicycle warning signs (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>) such as PATH NARROWS (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w5-4a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W5-4a.svg">W5-4a</a>) and Hill (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w7-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W7-5.svg">W7-5</a>) may be installed on shared-use paths<span class="ca">,</span> to warn bicyclists of conditions not readily apparent.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In situations where there is a need to warn road users to watch for bicycles traveling along the highway, the Bicycle Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-1.svg">W11-1</a>) sign may be used with the IN ROAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-1p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-1P.svg">W16-1P</a>) plaque or the IN STREET (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-1ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-1aP.svg">W16-1aP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-9c-1">Figure 9C-1</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">02a</span> <span class="ca">In situations where there is a need to warn motorists to watch for bicyclists traveling along the freeway, the NEXT XX MILES  (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w7-3ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W7-3aP.svg">W7-3aP</a>) plaque (refer to <a href="#fig-2c-5">Figure 2C-5</a>) may be used in conjunction with the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-1.svg">W11-1</a> sign.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If used, other advance bicycle warning signs should be installed at least 50 feet in advance of the beginning of the condition.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where temporary traffic control zones are present on bikeways, appropriate signs from Part 6 should be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Other warning signs described in Chapters 2C and 8C may be installed on bicycle facilities as appropriate.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/8b/#8b22">Section 8B.22</a> for Skewed Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-12/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-12.svg">W10-12</a>) Sign.</p>
</div>

## 9C.09 Object Markers

<div class="secmeta">Section 9C.09 &middot; manual page 1611 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9C.09/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Obstructions in a shared-use path shall be marked with retroreflective material or appropriate object markers as described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c70">Section 2C.70</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Fixed objects adjacent to shared-use paths may be marked with Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3 object markers. If the object marker is not also intended to be seen by motorists, a smaller version of the Type 3 object marker may be used (see <a href="#fig-9a-1">Table 9A-1</a>).</p>
</div>
