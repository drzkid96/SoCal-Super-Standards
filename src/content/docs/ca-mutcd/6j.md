## 6J.01 Pavement Markings in TTC Zones

<div class="secmeta">Section 6J.01 &middot; manual page 1290 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6J.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Pavement markings are installed or existing markings are maintained or enhanced in TTC zones to provide road users with a clearly defined path for travel through the TTC zone in day, night, and twilight periods under both wet and dry pavement conditions.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The work should be planned and staged to provide for the placement and removal of the pavement markings in a way that minimizes the disruption to traffic flow approaching and through the TTC zone during the placement and removal process.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Existing pavement markings shall be maintained in all long-term stationary (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6n/#6n01">Section 6N.01</a>) TTC zones in accordance with Chapters 3A and 3B, except as otherwise provided for temporary pavement markings in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6j/#6j02">Section 6J.02</a>. Pavement markings shall match the alignment of the markings in place at both ends of the TTC zone. Pavement markings shall be placed along the entire length of any paved detour or temporary roadway prior to the detour or roadway being opened to road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> For long-term stationary operations, pavement markings in the temporary traveled way that are no longer applicable should be removed or obliterated as soon as practical. Pavement marking obliteration should remove the non-applicable pavement marking material, and the obliteration method should minimize pavement scarring.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Painting over existing pavement markings with black paint or spraying with asphalt shall not be accepted as a substitute for removal or obliteration.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Removable, non-reflective, preformed tape that is approximately the same color as the pavement surface may be used where markings need to be covered temporarily.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">Centerlines and lane lines should be placed, replaced, or delineated where appropriate before the roadway is opened to traffic. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">On State highways, whenever construction or maintenance work causes obliteration of center stripe, temporary or </span> <span class="ca">permanent center stripe shall be in place prior to opening the State highway to public traffic. </span></p>
</div>

## 6J.02 Temporary Markings

<div class="secmeta">Section 6J.02 &middot; manual page 1290 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6J.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Temporary markings are those pavement markings or devices that are placed within TTC zones to provide road users with a clearly defined path of travel through the TTC zone when the permanent markings are either removed or obliterated during the work activities. Temporary markings are typically needed during the reconstruction of a road while it is open to traffic, such as overlays or surface treatments or where lanes are temporarily shifted on pavement that is to remain in place.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Unless justified based on engineering judgment, temporary pavement markings should not remain in place for more than 14 days after the application of the pavement surface treatment or the construction of the final pavement surface on new roadways or over existing pavements.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The temporary use of edge lines, channelizing lines, lane-reduction transitions, gore markings, and other longitudinal markings, and the various non-longitudinal markings (such as stop lines, railroad crossings, crosswalks, words, symbols, or arrows) should be in accordance with the State’s or highway agency’s policy.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Warning signs, channelizing devices, and delineation shall be used to indicate required road user paths in TTC zones where it is not possible to provide a clear path by pavement markings.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Except as otherwise provided in this Section, all temporary pavement markings for no-passing zones shall comply with the requirements of Chapters 3A and 3B. All temporary broken line pavement markings shall use the same cycle length as permanent markings and shall have line segments that are at least 2 feet long.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> All pavement markings and devices used to delineate road user paths should be reviewed during daytime and nighttime periods.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Half-cycle lengths with a minimum of 2-foot stripes may be used on roadways with severe curvature (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3a/#3a04">Section 3A.04</a>) for broken line center lines in passing zones and for lane lines.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> For temporary situations of 14 days or less, for a two- or three-lane road, no-passing zones may be identified by using DO NOT PASS (R4-1), PASS WITH CARE (R4-2), and NO PASSING ZONE (W14-3) signs (see Sections 2B.36, 2B.37, and 2C.53) rather than pavement markings. Also, DO NOT PASS, PASS WITH CARE, and NO PASSING ZONE signs may be used instead of pavement markings on roads with low volumes for longer periods in accordance with the State’s or highway agency’s policy.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> If used, the DO NOT PASS, PASS WITH CARE, and NO PASSING ZONE signs should be placed in accordance with Sections 2B.36, 2B.37, and 2C.53.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> If used, the NO CENTER LINE sign should be placed in accordance with <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h29">Section 6H.29</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> <span class="ca">Temporary lane lines and/or centerlines shall consist of retroreflectorized lines approximately 24 inches long, 4 inches </span> <span class="ca">wide, spaced approximately 24 feet apart. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> <span class="ca">Day/night raised retroreflectorized pavement markers, approved by Caltrans, may be used in lieu of 24-inch lines. See Section </span> <span class="ca">6J.03 for spacing requirements. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> <span class="ca">Right edge lines should not be simulated with dashed lines or raised pavement markers because they could confuse motorists. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> <span class="ca">Portable delineators, permanent type delineators, etc., may be used where it is considered desirable to enhance the edge of </span> <span class="ca">traveled way due to curvilinear alignment, narrowing pavement, etc. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> <span class="ca">Locations on two-lane conventional highways where no-passing zone centerline delineation has been obliterated shall </span> <span class="ca">be posted with a sign package consisting of a ROAD (STREET) WORK (W20-1) sign and WORK ZONE (G20-5aP) plaque in </span> <span class="ca">combination with a DO NOT PASS (R4-1) sign. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> <span class="ca">The R4-1 sign should be posted at approximate 2000 feet intervals throughout the extended no-pass zone. A PASS WITH CARE </span> <span class="ca">(R4-2) sign should also be placed at the end of the zone. </span></p>
</div>

## 6J.03 Temporary Raised Pavement Markers

<div class="secmeta">Section 6J.03 &middot; manual page 1291 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6J.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Retroreflective or internally illuminated raised pavement markers, or non-retroreflective raised pavement markers supplemented by retroreflective or internally illuminated markers, may be substituted for markings of other types in TTC zones.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If used, the color and pattern of the raised pavement markers shall simulate the color and pattern of the markings for which they substitute.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If temporary raised pavement markers are used to substitute for broken line segments, a group of at least three retroreflective markers equally spaced at no greater than 5 feet shall be installed every 40 feet.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If temporary raised pavement markers are used to substitute for solid lines, the markers shall be equally spaced at no greater than 10 feet, with retroreflective or internally illuminated units at a spacing no greater than 20 feet.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Temporary raised pavement markers may be used to substitute for broken line segments by using at least two retroreflective markers placed at each end of a segment of 2 to 5 feet in length, using the same cycle length as permanent markings.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Raised pavement markers should be considered for use along surfaced detours or temporary roadways, and other changed or new travel-lane alignments.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Retroreflective or internally illuminated raised pavement markers, or non-retroreflective raised pavement markers supplemented by retroreflective or internally illuminated markers, may also be used in TTC zones to supplement markings as prescribed in Chapters 3A and 3B.</p>
</div>

## 6J.04 Delineators

<div class="secmeta">Section 6J.04 &middot; manual page 1292 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6J.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Delineators may be used in TTC zones to indicate the alignment of the roadway and to outline the required vehicle path through the TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> When used, delineators shall combine with or supplement other TTC devices. They shall be mounted on crashworthy supports and shall be in accordance with Chapter 3G.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Spacing along roadway curves should be as set forth in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3g/#3g04">Section 3G.04</a> and should be such that several delineators are visible to an approaching driver.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <span class="ca">The delineators shall be placed 2 feet to 6 feet outside the outer edge of the shoulder. Retroreflection of delineators </span> <span class="ca">shall be 3 x 12 inch minimum size. </span></p>
</div>
