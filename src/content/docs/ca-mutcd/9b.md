## 9B.01 STOP and YIELD Signs (R1-1 and R1-2)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.01 &middot; manual page 1593 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> STOP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>) signs (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) shall be installed on bicycle facilities at points where bicyclists are required to stop.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) signs (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) shall be installed on bicycle facilities at points where bicyclists have an adequate view of conflicting traffic as they approach the sign, and where bicyclists are required to yield the right- of-way to that conflicting traffic.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A STOP sign or a YIELD sign shall not be installed in conjunction with a bicycle signal face (see Chapter 4H).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Larger signs may be used on shared-use paths and separated bikeways for added emphasis.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Where conditions require shared-use path users or bicyclists on separated bikeways, but not roadway users, to stop or yield, the STOP or YIELD sign should be placed or shielded so that it is not readily visible to roadway users.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> When the placement of STOP or YIELD signs is being considered, the priority at a shared-use path/<span class="ca">bikeway with</span> roadway intersection should be assigned with consideration of the following: A.Relative speeds of shared-use path, <span class="ca">bikeway</span> and roadway users, B.Relative volumes of shared-use path, <span class="ca">bikeway</span>and roadway traffic, and C.Relative importance of shared-use path, <span class="ca">bikeway</span> and roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Speed should not be the sole factor used to determine priority, as it is sometimes appropriate to give priority to a high-volume shared-use path that crosses a low-volume street, or to a regional shared-use path that crosses a minor collector street <span class="ca">or where a shared-use path begins, ends, or it merges with other types of roadway</span>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> When priority is assigned (see Sections 2B.06 and 2B.08), the least-restrictive control that is appropriate should be placed on the lower-priority approaches. STOP signs should not be used where YIELD signs would provided adequate control.</p>
</div>

## 9B.02 EXCEPT BICYCLES Regulatory Plaque (R3-7bP)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.02 &middot; manual page 1593 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> There are circumstances where it might be appropriate to exempt bicyclists from regulatory restrictions applied to other traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Where an engineering study or engineering judgment demonstrates that it is appropriate to exempt bicyclists from the provisions of a regulatory sign, the EXCEPT BICYCLES (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-7bp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-7bP.svg">R3-7bP</a>) regulatory plaque (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) should be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02a</span> <span class="ca">Where signs are provided to prohibit or regulate turns from streets or driveways that intersect with a roadway and those signs </span> <span class="ca">are not intended for bicycle traffic, the supplemental EXCEPT BICYCLES (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-7bp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-7bP.svg">R3-7bP</a>) regulatory plaque can be used. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <a href="#fig-9b-2">Figure 9B-2</a> shows examples of how the EXCEPT BICYCLES (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-20p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-20P.svg">W16-20P</a>) regulatory plaque can be applied.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in Paragraph 3 text. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9c/#9c05">Section 9C.05</a> contains information regarding the EXCEPT BICYCLES warning plaque when applicable to a warning sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The EXCEPT BICYCLES regulatory plaque shall not be used to exempt bicyclists from the legal requirement of a STOP or YIELD sign, Yield Here to Pedestrians Signs, Stop Here for Pedestrians Signs, or a traffic signal indication.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Where a regulatory sign, such as the No Left Turn (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-2.svg">R3-2</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b26">Section 2B.26</a>), is installed on the same post or mounting as a STOP sign or YIELD sign, the EXCEPT BICYCLES regulatory plaque shall not be installed in conjunction with the regulatory sign on that post or mounting that includes the STOP sign or YIELD sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The EXCEPT BICYCLES regulatory plaque shall be placed below the regulatory sign that it supplements.</p>
</div>

## 9B.03 Advance Intersection Lane Control Signs (R3-8 Series) for Bicycle Lanes

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.03 &middot; manual page 1594 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Advance Intersection Lane Control (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-8.svg">R3-8</a> series) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b30">Section 2B.30</a>) may display the arrangement of a conventional or buffer-separated bicycle lane in relation to other lanes in the same direction that are present on a roadway approach to an intersection.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The number and combination of permissible movements by both the motor vehicle and the bicycle on the same approach to an intersection might be practically limited by the amount of information that can be legibly displayed on signs or in signing sequences and still be readily comprehended by road users. The excessive display of all movements by more than one mode can result in unwieldy signs that are difficult to locate and install.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> On an approach to an intersection with complex geometry that can include multiple through lanes and multiple turn lanes and also includes a bicycle lane, consideration should be given to displaying all allowable movements on separate signs, such as using Mandatory Movement Lane Control (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-5.svg">R3-5</a>) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b28">Section 2B.28</a>) for the through lanes and Mandatory Movement Lane Control (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-7/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-7__Sheet_1_of_2.svg">R3-7</a>) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b28">Section 2B.28</a>) for the turn lanes, and guide signs for bicycle routes (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9d/#9d02">Section 9D.02</a> through 9D.07) and Bicycle Route Sign auxiliary plaques (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9d/#9d08">Section 9D.08</a>) for the bicycle movement.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The portion of the sign face for the bicycle lane shall be limited to the relationship of the bicycle lane to the other lanes on the roadway approach to the intersection. The portion of the sign face for the bicycle lane shall not be modified to display specific, supplementary information about the bicycle lane such as bicycle lane extensions, contiguous buffer spaces, or other ancillary bicycle operations such as two-stage turn boxes or bicycle boxes.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Counter-flow bicycle lanes shall not be displayed on Advance Intersection Lane Control signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The shared-lane marking symbol shall not be displayed on Advance Intersection Lane Control signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Shared-use paths shall not be displayed on Advance Intersection Lane Control signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Advance Intersection Lane Control signs that display the bicycle lane shall use a contrasting white legend on a black background for the bicycle lane (see <a href="#fig-2b-4">Figure 2B-4</a>). The portion of the display for the bicycle lane shall not use the color green on the sign face in an attempt to be consistent with the green-colored pavement that might be present on the intersection approach.</p>
</div>

## 9B.04 Bicycle Lane Signs and Plaques (R3-17, R3-5hP, R3-17aP, and R3-17bP)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.04 &middot; manual page 1594 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The BIKE LANE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-17/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-17.svg">R3-17</a>) sign and the BIKE LANE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-5hp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-5hP.svg">R3-5hP</a>), AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-17ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-17aP.svg">R3-17aP</a>), and ENDS (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-17bp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-17bP.svg">R3-17bP</a>) plaques (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) shall be used only in conjunction with marked bicycle lanes as described in Sections 9E.01, 9E.06, and 9E.07.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If used, Bicycle Lane signs and plaques should <span class="ca">shall</span>be located at the beginning of the bicycle lane<span class="ca">,</span> and in advance of the downstream end of the bicycle lane<span class="ca">, and at all major changes in direction</span>. <span class="ca">The BIKE LANE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-17/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-17.svg">R3-17</a>) sign </span> <span class="ca">shall be used to regulate bicycle and motor vehicle traffic, in accordance with CVC §§ 21207, 21207.5, 21208, 21209 and </span> <span class="ca">21717.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02a</span> <span class="ca">The BIKE LANE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-17/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-17.svg">R3-17</a>) sign should be placed at every arterial street and at 1/2 mile intervals of each designated Bike lane. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Additional Bicycle Lane signs and plaques may be used at periodic intervals along the bicycle lane as determined by engineering judgment based on the operating speed of bicycle and other traffic, block length, distances from adjacent intersections, and other considerations.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b33">Section 2B.33</a> contains information for the application of BEGIN and END plaques.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9b/#9b03">Section 9B.03</a> contains information on displaying the bicycle lane on Advance Intersection Lane Control signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Where two or more movements from a bicycle lane are allowed, or where the emphasis of allowed bicycle movements is needed, an Optional Movement Lane Control sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b29">Section 2B.29</a>) may be supplemented with a BIKE LANE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-5hp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-5hP.svg">R3-5hP</a>) plaque above the Optional Movement Lane Control sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Where bicycle lanes are located between travel lanes on intersection approaches or where only a single bicycle movement is allowed from a certain bicycle lane, a Mandatory Movement Lane Control sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b28">Section 2B.28</a>) may be supplemented with a BIKE LANE plaque to require a bicyclist in a particular bicycle lane at an intersection to stay in the same lane and proceed straight through the intersection, or to indicate a required turn from a particular bicycle lane.</p>
</div>

## 9B.18 Two-Stage Bicycle Turn Box Regulatory Signing (R9-23 Series)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.18 &middot; manual page 1598 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.18/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Where two-stage bicycle turn boxes are provided in an intersection, the design of an approach to that intersection will determine whether the use of a two-stage bicycle turn box is required by bicycles to facilitate a turn.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Situations in which a two-stage bicycle turn box might be necessary to facilitate turns include, but are not limited to, those in which: A.A separated bicycle facility is provided where upstream access to a lane used to facilitate turns by motor vehicle traffic is physically inaccessible to bicycles; B.Left turns are prohibited from the left-most lane, or right turns are prohibited from the right-most lane, at an intersection; or C.Locations where physical or operational conditions make it impracticable or unsafe for a bicyclist to merge and make the appropriate turn as would any other vehicle.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Where bicycles are required to use a two-stage bicycle turn box (see <a href="#fig-9b-5">Figure 9B-5</a>), the Two- Stage Bicycle Turn Box regulatory sign series (see <a href="#fig-9b-5">Figure 9B-5</a>) shall be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where bicycles are required to use a two-stage bicycle turn box, the Bicycles All Turns from Bike Lane (R9- 23) or Bicycle Left Turn from Bike Lane (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-23a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-23a.svg">R9-23a</a>) advance regulatory sign shall be mounted in advance of the intersection, and at least one Bicycle Turn Must Use Turn Box (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-23b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-23b.svg">R9-23b</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-23c/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-23c.svg">R9-23c</a>) sign shall be used at the intersection.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Where used, the Bicycle Turn Must Use Turn Box (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-23b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-23b.svg">R9-23b</a>) sign shall be mounted at the near side of the intersection.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Where used, the Bicycle Turn Must Use Turn Box location (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-23c/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-23c.svg">R9-23c</a>) sign shall be mounted at the far side of the intersection.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Where use of a two-stage bicycle turn box is optional, the Two-Stage Bicycle Turn Box guide sign series (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9d/#9d13">Section 9D.13</a>) may be used to provide directional information.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If used, an appropriately sized Street Name (D3-1) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2d/#2d45">Section 2D.45</a>) may be installed below the All Turns from Bike Lane sign or Left Turn from Bike Lane sign to identify the crossroad where the turn box will be available.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9e/#9e11">Section 9E.11</a> contains information regarding pavement markings and turning restrictions for two- stage turn boxes.</p>

## 9B.20 Bicycle Actuation Signs (R10-4, R10-22, R10-24, R10-25, and R10-26)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.20 &middot; manual page 1599 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.20/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Where bicycles are not controlled by pedestrian signal indications, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-4.svg">R10-4</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-24/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-24.svg">R10-24</a>, or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-26/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-26.svg">R10-26</a> sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b58">Section 2B.58</a>) may be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If used, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-4.svg">R10-4</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-24/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-24.svg">R10-24</a>, or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-26/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-26.svg">R10-26</a> signs (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) should be installed in the vicinity of where bicycles will be crossing the street.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If bicycles are crossing a roadway where In-Roadway Warning Lights (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4u/#4u02">Section 4U.02</a>) or other warning lights or beacons have been provided, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-25/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-25.svg">R10-25</a> <span class="ca">or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-3kca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r10-3kca.webp">R10-3k(CA)</a> sign (refer to <a href="#fig-9b-1ca">Figure 9B-1 (CA)</a>)</span> sign may be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The Bicycle Detector (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-22/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-22.svg">R10-22</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) may be installed at signalized intersections where pavement markings are used to indicate the location where a bicycle is to be positioned to actuate the signal (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9e/#9e15">Section 9E.15</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If the Bicycle Detector sign is installed, it should be placed at the roadside adjacent to the marking to emphasize the location of the marking.</p>
</div>

## 9B.22 Bicycle Signal Signs (R10-40, R10-40a, R10-41, R10-41a, R10-41b, and R10-41c)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.22 &middot; manual page 1600 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.22/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The purposes of the Bicycle Signal signs (see <a href="#fig-9b-1">Figure 9B-1</a>) are to inform road users that the signal indications in the bicycle signal face are intended only for bicyclists, and to inform bicyclists which specific bicycle movements are controlled by the bicycle signal face.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4h/#4h03">Section 4H.03</a> contains information on signs that are used in conjunction with bicycle signal faces.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Bicycle Signal – Mandatory Movement (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-40/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-40.svg">R10-40</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-40a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-40a.svg">R10-40a</a>) sign or the Bicycle Signal – Optional Movement (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-41/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-41.svg">R10-41</a>. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-41a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-41a.svg">R10-41a</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-41b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-41b.svg">R10-41b</a>, or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-41c/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-41c.svg">R10-41c</a>) sign shall require bicycles to turn, shall permit turns where such turns would otherwise not be allowed, shall require a bicycle to stay in the same lane and proceed straight through an intersection, or shall indicate allowed movements when a GREEN BICYCLE signal indication is displayed on a bicycle signal face.</p>
</div>

## 9B.101(CA) Bike Turn-out Signs SR64A(CA) and SR64B(CA)

<div class="secmeta">Section 9B.101(CA) &middot; manual page 1600 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/9B.101(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> <span class="ca"> On two-lane highways in areas where bicyclists share the road with motorists and roadway width, traffic volumes and/or </span> <span class="ca">vertical or horizontal curvature make passing bicyclists difficult for motorists, turn-out areas for bicyclists are sometimes provided for </span> <span class="ca">the purpose of allowing vehicles an opportunity to pass bicyclists. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> <span class="ca"> Where an area has been provided for bicyclists to use as a turn-out to allow vehicles an opportunity to pass bicyclists, a BIKE </span> <span class="ca">TURN-OUT XX FEET (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sr64aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sr64aca.webp">SR64A(CA)</a>) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-9b-1ca">Figure 9B-1(CA)</a>) should be installed in advance of the turn-out area. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <span class="ca"> A BIKE TURN-OUT (with arrow) (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sr64bca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sr64bca.webp">SR64B(CA)</a>) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-9b-1ca">Figure 9B-1(CA)</a>) should be installed at the entrance of the turn-</span> <span class="ca">out area provided for bicyclists.</span></p>
</div>
