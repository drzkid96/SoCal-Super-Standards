## 2M.01 Scope


<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Recreational or cultural interest areas are attractions or traffic generators that are open to the general public for the purpose of play, amusement, or relaxation. Recreational attractions include such facilities as parks, campgrounds, game- hunting facilities, and ski areas, while examples of cultural attractions include museums, art galleries, and historical buildings or sites.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The purpose of recreation and cultural interest area signs is to guide road users to a general area and then to specific facilities or activities within the area.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Recreational and cultural interest area guide signs directing road users to significant traffic generators may be used on freeways and expressways where there is direct access to these areas as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2m/#2m09">Section 2M.09</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Recreational and cultural interest area signs may be used off the road network, as appropriate.</p>
</div>

## 2M.02 Application of Recreational and Cultural Interest Area Signs


<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Provisions for signing recreational or cultural interest areas are subdivided into two different types of signs: (1) symbol signs and (2) destination guide signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Highway agencies providing recreational and cultural interest area signing should establish a policy with signing criteria for the eligibility of the various types of services, accommodations, and facilities. These signs should not be used where they might be confused with other traffic control signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Recreational and cultural interest area guide signs may be used in recreational or cultural interest areas for signing non-vehicular events and amenities such as trails, structures, and facilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Symbols for use only within recreational and cultural interest area facilities are noted in <a href="#fig-2m-1">Table 2M-1</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a09">Section 2A.09</a> contains information regarding the use of recreational and cultural interest area symbols on other types of signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The recreational and cultural interest area signs are supplemental signs and are subject to the same spacing and number of  messages limitations set forth in Chapters 2A, 2D, and 2E. Under these limitations, the supplemental destination, recreational and  cultural interest area signs compete for signing on the basis of traffic service.</p>
</div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Recreational area signs to National Heritage Areas, National Parks, and State Parks should normally include the name of the  area. County and City Park signs should not normally include the name.</p>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Recreational area signs may be placed for the following facilities:  A. National Parks or Monuments. B. State Parks, when located within 5 miles of the highway. C. County Parks, when located within 3 miles of the highway. D. In urban areas, City Parks within 1 mile may be signed from conventional highways. Normally, City Parks will not be signed to or from metropolitan freeways. E. Campgrounds in National Forests or State Parks may be signed from conventional highways when the entrances are located on the highway. An advance sign reading "Campground 1/4 mile" may be placed. Signs at the immediate entrance will be placed by the agency having jurisdiction over the campground. F. Major rural recreational areas may be signed by name. When a recreational area is served by more than a single exit, the appropriate colored NEXT X EXITS (E9) sign may be used. Normally, the sign will include the name of the area and the text "RECREATIONAL AREA". G.<span class="ca">In rural recreational areas, guide signs may be supplemented with white on brown symbol signs mounted below indicating recreational facilities available to the road users. H. National Heritage Areas, at or near the boundary or within the National Heritage Areas.</span></p>

</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>

## 2M.05 Symbol Sign Sizes


<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Recreational and cultural interest area symbol signs should be 24 x 24 inches. Where greater visibility or emphasis is needed, larger sizes should be used. Symbol sign enlargements should be in 6-inch increments.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Recreational and cultural interest area symbol signs should be 30 x 30 inches when used on guide signs on freeways or expressways.</p>

## 2M.07 Use of Prohibitive Circle and Diagonal for Non-Road Applications


<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>

## 2M.09 Destination Guide Signs


<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Whenever the trapezoidal shape is used, the color combination shall be a white legend and border on a brown background. When the trapezoidal shape is used for a sign with a directional arrow, a right-angled trapezoid with the wider dimension of the bases (parallel sides) at the top of the sign shall be used. The diagonal leg of the trapezoid shall be oriented in the same direction as the directional arrow. When the trapezoidal shape is used for an advance sign legend, such as with a distance or action message, an isosceles trapezoid with the wider dimension of the bases at the top of the sign shall be used.</p>

## 2M.10 Memorial or Dedication Signing


<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Legislative bodies will occasionally adopt an act or resolution memorializing or dedicating a highway, bridge, or other component of the highway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01a</span> <span class="ca">The Legislature, by legislative action, may designate names for State highways and bridges. The Legislature may request  memorial named highway facilities to be designated with signs instead of a plaque and specify that the signs are to be furnished and  installed "at no cost to the State".</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Named highways (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2d/#2d56">Section 2D.56</a>) are officially designated and shown on official maps and serve the purpose of providing route guidance, primarily on unnumbered highways, and property addresses. A highway designated as a memorial or dedication is not considered to be a named highway for the purposes of highway signing or road user navigation and orientation.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a20">Section 2A.20</a> contains information regarding excessive use of signs. Because memorial or dedication names are not official highway names, memorial and dedication signing is not essential to providing navigational guidance.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Such memorial or dedication names should not appear on or along a highway or be placed on bridges or other highway components. If a route, bridge, or highway component is officially designated as a memorial or dedication, and if notification of the memorial or dedication is to be made on the highway right- of-way, such notification should consist of installing a memorial or dedication marker in a rest area, scenic overlook, recreational area, or other appropriate location where parking is provided with the signing inconspicuously located relative to vehicle operations along the highway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Memorial or dedication signs should have a white legend and border on a brown background. On all such signs, the design should be simple and dignified, devoid of any appearance of advertising, and in general compliance with other signing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The lettering for the name of the person or entity being recognized should be composed of a combination of lower- case letters with initial upper-case letters.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Where such memorial or dedication signs are installed on the highway mainline because the provisions of Paragraph 4 of this Section cannot be met, (1) memorial or dedication names shall not appear on directional guide signs, (2) memorial or dedication signs shall not interfere with the placement of any other traffic control devices, and (3) memorial or dedication signs shall not compromise the safety or efficiency of traffic flow. The memorial or dedication signing shall be limited to one sign at an appropriate location in each route direction <span class="ca">except as noted in  Paragraph 07a</span>,each as an independent post-mounted sign installation.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07a</span> <span class="ca">When highway facilities are named by the Legislature, the following guidelines shall apply according to the type of  facility:  1.</span><span class="ca">Bridges. </span><span class="ca">One sign (G11(CA) series) shall be placed at each approach end of the bridge, underpass, tunnel or other  structure with the name of the memorialized individual.  2.</span><span class="ca">Freeways and Highways. </span><span class="ca">One sign (G12(CA) series) shall be placed at each terminal of the highway segment  memorialized by the Legislature.  3.</span><span class="ca">Rest Areas. </span><span class="ca">One bronze plaque shall be installed at each legislatively named rest area. Memorial name signs shall  not be installed in advance of rest areas.  4.</span><span class="ca">Interchanges. </span><span class="ca">One sign (G12(CA) series) shall be placed at a minimum of two approaches to the legislatively  named interchange.  5.</span><span class="ca">Vista Points. </span><span class="ca">One bronze plaque shall be installed at each legislatively named vista point. Memorial name signs  shall not be installed in advance of vista points.  6.</span><span class="ca">Roundabout. </span><span class="ca">One sign (G12(CA) series) shall be placed at a minimum of two approaches to the roundabout.  7.</span><span class="ca">Bikeways. </span><span class="ca">Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9d/">Chapter 9D</a>. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">07b</span> <span class="ca">The principal legend on post-mounted memorial or dedication signs shall be in letters and numerals 6 inches in height  for upper-case letters and 4.5 inches in nominal loop height. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2d/#2d05">Section 2D.05</a>.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07c</span> <span class="ca">Bronze plaques normally should bear the name in 1 inch letters. However, the plaque should be no larger than 30 x 30 inches.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">07d</span> <span class="ca">Caltrans is authorized to expend reasonable sums for bronze plaques.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Memorial or dedication signs shall be rectangular in shape. The legend displayed on memorial or dedication signs shall be limited to the name of the person <span class="ca">with an optional nickname in quotation marks if stated in the  concurrent resolution,</span> or entity being recognized and a simple message preceding the name, such as “DEDICATED TO.” Additional legend, such as biographical information, shall not be displayed on memorial or dedication signs. Decorative or graphical elements, pictographs, logos, or symbols shall not be displayed on memorial or dedication signs. All letters and numerals displayed on memorial or dedication signs shall be as provided in the “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>). The route number or officially mapped name of the highway shall not be displayed on the memorial or dedication sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Memorial or dedication signs shall not imply that a highway has been officially renamed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Memorial or dedication names shall not appear on supplemental signs<span class="ca">, street name signs</span> or on any other information sign on or along the highway or its intersecting routes.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10a</span> <span class="ca">Memorial or dedication signs shall not be installed as overhead signs.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Freeways and expressways should not be signed as memorial or dedicated highways.</p>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> When used, memorial or dedication signs should be located in accordance with the provisions for excessive use of signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a20">Section 2A.20</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Paragraph 36 of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2d/#2d45">Section 2D.45</a> contains provisions regarding the use of memorial or dedication signing in conjunction with Street Name signs.</p>
</div>

<p><span class="pn">21</span> The following conditions shall be satisfied to qualify for a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/s35ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/s35ca.webp">S35(CA)</a> sign on a state highway:  1.<span class="ca">At least one of the deceased victim's immediate family members requests a memorial sign. An immediate family  member is a spouse, child, stepchild, brother, stepbrother, sister, stepsister, mother, stepmother, father or  stepfather.  2.</span><span class="ca">The accident occurred on or after January 1, 1991.  3.</span><span class="ca">Either (a) or (b) is true:  a.</span><span class="ca">The intoxicated driver was convicted of second-degree murder, or gross vehicular manslaughter, or vehicular  manslaughter.  b.</span><span class="ca">The intoxicated driver died or could not be prosecuted because of mental incompetence.  4.</span><span class="ca">Note: An intoxicated driver who died does NOT qualify as a victim. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">22</span> The placement of the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/s35ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/s35ca.webp">S35(CA)</a> sign on state highways shall be per the following requirements:  1.<span class="ca">Signs will be installed in accordance with applicable Caltrans policies and standards for signs. This includes  posts, hardware, materials, vertical, longitudinal, and lateral positioning.  2.</span><span class="ca">Caltrans will NOT install or maintain a memorial sign if there is written opposition from any immediate family  member.  3.</span><span class="ca">Only one sign will be installed in one direction of travel on the right side of the state highway in close proximity to  where the accident occurred at a location where it is safe and practical to do so.  4.</span><span class="ca">Caltrans will maintain the sign for 7 years or until the condition of the sign has deteriorated to a point where it is no  longer serviceable, whichever occurs first.  5.</span><span class="ca">Only one sign will be installed per accident. Multiple victim names may appear on the sign.  6.</span><span class="ca">A sign will NOT be installed in the median of any state highway. </span></p>
</div>

## 2M.101(CA) Historical Landmark Signs (G13-1A(CA), G13-2A(CA) and G14A(CA))


<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Historical Landmark (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-1aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-1aca.webp">G13-1A(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a>) signs and the Advance Historical Landmark (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g14aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g14aca.webp">G14A(CA)</a>) sign  shall have a white legend and border on a brown colored background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-1aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-1aca.webp">G13-1A(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g14aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g14aca.webp">G14A(CA)</a> signs may be in addition to the normal compliment of signs, but minimum spacing  will be maintained.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-1aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-1aca.webp">G13-1A(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g14aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g14aca.webp">G14A(CA)</a> signs may be placed directing to Historical Landmarks that are registered with the  Department of Parks and Recreation.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> On freeways, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-1aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-1aca.webp">G13-1A(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g14aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g14aca.webp">G14A(CA)</a> signs shall be limited to those more important and better  known landmarks where some physical evidence remains, such as missions, forts, state monuments, etc., rather than mere  sites of former buildings or happenings.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The Office of Historic Preservation within the Department of Parks and Recreation (or the Resource Protection Division  in the case of State Historic Park sites) shall be notified prior to the removal of existing <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-1aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-1aca.webp">G13-1A(CA)</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a> and  <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g14aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g14aca.webp">G14A(CA)</a> signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The Historical Landmark (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-1aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-1aca.webp">G13-1A(CA)</a>) sign should be used on conventional highways to guide road users by the most direct  route to registered historical landmarks which are located within 5 miles of the highway. The sign should be placed not more than  150 feet in advance of the intersection on the right.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The Historical Landmark (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a>) sign should be used on freeways to guide road users to the original 21 California  Missions and other important well-known historical landmarks. Refer to § 123.5 of the SHC for signing to Missions. The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g13-2aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g13-2aca.webp">G13-2A(CA)</a>  sign should also be used on freeways to guide road users to historical landmarks that have a profound impact on the history of  California as a whole.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Supplemental Destination (G86(CA) Series) signs (white text on green background) may be used on freeways where the  landmark generates considerable traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> These G86(CA) Series signs shall be followed up by standard Historical Landmark signs on the next exit ramps.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The Advance Historical Landmark (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g14aca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/g14aca.webp">G14A(CA)</a>) sign should be used in advance of a registered historical landmark monument or  plaque within or adjacent to the right of way. The sign should be placed 500 to 1500 feet in advance of the landmark or monument on  the right, depending on the approach speed of traffic.</p>
</div>
