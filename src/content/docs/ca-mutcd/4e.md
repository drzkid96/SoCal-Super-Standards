## 4E.01 Signal Indications – Design, Illumination, Color, and Shape

<div class="secmeta">Section 4E.01 &middot; manual page 1093 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4E.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The illuminated part of each signal indication shall be circular or arrow, except those used for bicycle symbol signal indications, pedestrian signal heads, light rail transit signal indications, and lane-use control signals.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Letters or numbers (including those associated with countdown displays) shall not be displayed as part of a vehicular signal indication.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Strobes shall not be used within or adjacent to any signal indication.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except for the flashing vehicular and pedestrian signal indications and the distinctive indications for emergency-vehicle preemption (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4f/#4f19">Section 4F.19</a>) that are expressly allowed by the provisions of this Part, flashing displays shall not be used within or adjacent to any signal indications.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Each circular signal indication shall emit a single color: red, yellow, or green.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 7 of this Section, each arrow signal indication shall emit a single color: red, yellow, or green.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A bimodal signal section that is capable of alternating between the display of a GREEN ARROW signal indication and the display of a YELLOW ARROW signal indication, both pointing in the same direction, may be used provided that both colors are never displayed simultaneously.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The arrow, which shall show only one direction, shall be the only illuminated part of an arrow signal indication.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Arrows shall be pointed: A.Vertically upward to indicate a straight-through movement, B.Horizontally in the direction of the turn to indicate a turn at approximately or greater than a right angle, C.Upward with a slope at an angle approximately equal to that of the turn if the angle of the turn is substantially less than a right angle, or D.In a manner that directs the driver through the turn if a U-turn arrow is used (see <a href="#fig-4e-1">Figure 4E-1</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 11 of this Section, the requirements of Chapters 1 and 2 of the publication entitled “Equipment and Materials Standards of the Institute of Transportation Engineers” that pertain to the aspects of the signal head design that affect the display of the signal indications shall be met for signal optical units that use incandescent lamps within optical assemblies that include lenses. Except as provided in Paragraph 11 of this Section, the requirements of the Institute of Transportation Engineers’ publications entitled “Vehicle Traffic Control Signal Heads: Light Emitting Diode (LED) Circular Signal Supplement,” 2005, ITE, and “Vehicle Traffic Control Signal Heads: Light Emitting Diode (LED) Vehicle Arrow Traffic Signal Supplement,” 2008, ITE, that pertain to the aspects of the signal head design that affect the display of the signal indications shall be met for light-emitting diode (LED) traffic signal modules.</p>
</div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> The intensity and distribution of light from each illuminated signal lens or LED signal module should comply with the publications specified in Paragraph 10 of this Section, as appropriate.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> References to signal lenses in this section are not intended to limit signal optical units to incandescent lamps within optical assemblies that include lenses. Research has resulted in signal optical units that are not lenses, such as, but not limited to, light-emitting diode (LED) traffic signal modules. Some units are practical for all signal indications, and some are practical for specific types such as visibility-limited signal indications.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> If a signal indication is so bright that it causes excessive glare during nighttime conditions, some form of automatic dimming should be used to reduce the brilliance of the signal indication.</p>
</div>

## 4E.02 Size of Vehicular Signal Indications

<div class="secmeta">Section 4E.02 &middot; manual page 1093 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4E.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> There shall be three nominal diameter sizes for vehicular signal indications: 4 inches, 8 inches, and 12 inches.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Four-inch signal indications shall only be used for bicycle signal faces per <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4h/#4h07">Section 4H.07</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Twelve-inch signal indications shall be used for all arrow signal indications.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 5 of this Section, 12-inch signal indications shall be used for all circular signal indications in all new signal faces.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Eight-inch circular signal indications may be used in new signal faces only for: A.The green or flashing yellow signal indications in an emergency-vehicle traffic control signal (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4m/#4m02">Section 4M.02</a>); B.The circular indications in signal faces controlling the approach to the downstream location where two adjacent signalized locations are close to each other and it is impractical because of factors such as high approach speeds, horizontal or vertical curves, or other geometric factors to install visibility- limited signal faces for the downstream approach; C.The circular indications in a signal face that is located less than 120 feet from the stop line on a roadway with a posted or statutory speed limit or operating speed of 30 mph or less; D.The circular indications in a supplemental near-side signal face; E.The circular indications in a supplemental signal face installed for the sole purpose of controlling pedestrian movements rather than vehicular movements; and F.The circular indications in a flashing beacon (see Chapter 4S).</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Different sizes of signal indications may be used in the same signal face or signal head, provided that the signal face or signal head complies with the requirements contained in Paragraphs 3 through 5 of this Section.</p>
</div>

## 4E.03 Positions of Signal Indications Within a Signal Face – General

<div class="secmeta">Section 4E.03 &middot; manual page 1094 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4E.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Standardization of the number and arrangements of signal sections in vehicular traffic control signal faces enables road users who are color vision deficient to identify the illuminated color by its position relative to other signal sections.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Unless otherwise provided in this Manual for a particular application, each signal face at a signalized location shall have three, four, or five signal sections. Unless otherwise provided in this Manual for a particular application, if a vertical signal face includes a cluster (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4e/#4e04">Section 4E.04</a>), the signal face shall have at least three vertical positions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A single-section signal face shall be permitted at a traffic control signal if it consists of a continuously- displayed GREEN ARROW signal indication that is being used to indicate a continuous movement.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The signal sections in a signal face shall be arranged in a vertical or horizontal straight line, except as otherwise provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4e/#4e04">Section 4E.04</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The arrangement of adjacent signal sections in a signal face shall follow the relative positions listed in Sections 4E.04 or 4E.05, as applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If a signal section that displays a CIRCULAR YELLOW signal indication is used, it shall be located between the signal section that displays the red signal indication and all other signal sections.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> If a U-turn arrow signal section is used in a signal face for a U-turn to the left, its position in the signal face shall be the same as stated in Sections 4E.04 and 4E.05 for a left-turn arrow signal section of the same color. If a U-turn arrow signal section is used in a signal face for a U-turn to the right, its position in the signal face shall be the same as stated in Sections 4E.04 and 4E.05 for a right-turn arrow signal section of the same color.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> A U-turn arrow signal indication pointing to the left shall not be used in a signal face that also contains a left- turn arrow signal indication. A U-turn arrow signal indication pointing to the right shall not be used in a signal face that also contains a right-turn arrow signal indication.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Within a signal face, two identical CIRCULAR RED or RED ARROW signal indications may be displayed immediately horizontally adjacent or immediately vertically adjacent to each other in a vertical signal face (see Drawing A in <a href="#fig-4e-2">Figure 4E-2</a>) or immediately horizontally adjacent to each other in a horizontal signal face (see Drawing B in <a href="#fig-4e-2">Figure 4E-2</a>) for emphasis.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Horizontally-arranged and vertically-arranged signal faces may be used on the same approach provided they are separated to meet the lateral separation spacing required in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4d/#4d07">Section 4D.07</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> <a href="#fig-4e-2">Figure 4E-2</a> illustrates some of the typical arrangements of signal sections in signal faces that do not control separate turning movements. Figures 4F-1 through 4F-7 illustrate the typical arrangements of signal sections in left-turn signal faces. Figures 4F-8 through 4F-14 illustrate the typical arrangements of signal sections in right-turn signal faces.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> There shall be at least two signal faces for each movement on each signal-controlled approach.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Supplemental signal faces should be considered if any of the following conditions exist:  A.<span class="ca">The area is rural.  B.</span><span class="ca">The area is urban and the signal is the first one on a particular highway.  C.</span><span class="ca">The roadway is striped for two or more approach lanes.  D.</span><span class="ca">Where visibility of the signal is affected by alignmen</span><span class="ca">t or obstructions. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> On an undivided roadway, the signal faces for each through approach of an intersection are usually placed at the far right and  far left corners.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> The signal faces for two or more approaches may be combined on a single standard.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> It is generally desirable to locate the signal faces on separate standards at curb returns. This practice will tend to maximize the  visibility of the signal faces for the controlled approach while minimizing the visibility of the signal faces intended for the cross-street  approach.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> Separate standards should be considered whenever the curb return radius is greater than 10 feet.</p>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> The preferred locations for new installations of signal faces for fully-protected left turn movements at a typical intersection are on  a mast arm of sufficient length to place one signal face as nearly as practical in line with the left turn lane and to place the second  face on a standard at the far left corner.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">19</span> Unusual roadway geometrics, wide medians, wide roadways, more than one left turn lane in the same direction or other factors  may require the left turn signal face(s) to be mounted on standard(s) located in a median to satisfy visibility requirements.</p>
<p><span class="pn">20</span> A signal face, containing a circular green indication, may be located in a far median only when:  A.<span class="ca">The signal phasing provides a protected left turn movement; or  B.</span><span class="ca">The signal face is provided with some type of visibility control so that the indications are not visible to traffic in the left turn  storage lane; or  C.</span><span class="ca">It is not facing a left turn storage lane; or  D.</span><span class="ca">LEFT TURN YIELD ON GREEN (symbolic circular green) (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-12/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-12.svg">R10-12</a>) sign is installed below the said signal face. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">21</span> A signal face containing a circular green indication may be located in the near median where there is a left turn storage lane and  there is no associated left turn phase.</p>
<p><span class="pn">22</span> Supplemental signal faces may be placed at a near side location or suspended from a mast arm.</p>
</div>

## 4E.04 Positions of Signal Indications Within a Vertical Signal Face

<div class="secmeta">Section 4E.04 &middot; manual page 1095 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4E.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In each vertically-arranged signal face, all signal sections that display red signal indications shall be located above all signal sections that display yellow and green signal indications.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In vertically-arranged signal faces, each signal section that displays a YELLOW ARROW signal indication shall be located above the signal section that displays the GREEN ARROW signal indication to which it applies.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The relative positions of signal sections in a vertically-arranged signal face, from top to bottom, shall be as follows: CIRCULAR RED Steady and/or flashing left-turn RED ARROW Steady and/or flashing right-turn RED ARROW CIRCULAR YELLOW CIRCULAR GREEN Straight-through GREEN ARROW Steady left-turn YELLOW ARROW Flashing left-turn YELLOW ARROW Left-turn GREEN ARROW Steady right-turn YELLOW ARROW Flashing right-turn YELLOW ARROW Right-turn GREEN ARROW</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If a bimodal signal section (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4e/#4e01">Section 4E.01</a>) is used in a vertically-arranged signal face, the bimodal signal section shall occupy the same position relative to the other sections as the signal section that displays the GREEN ARROW signal indication in a vertically-arranged signal face would occupy.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> In a vertically-arranged signal face, signal sections that display signal indications of the same color may be arranged horizontally adjacent to each other at right angles to the basic straight line arrangement to form a clustered signal face (see Figures 4E-2, 4F-4, 4F-6, 4F-10, 4F-11, 4F-13, and 4F-15).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in Paragraph 5 text. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Such clusters shall be limited to the following: A.Two identical signal sections, B.Two or three different signal sections that display signal indications of the same color, or C.For only the specific case described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4f/#4f16">Section 4F.16</a> (see Drawing B in <a href="#fig-4f-15">Figure 4F-15</a>), two signal sections, one of which displays a GREEN ARROW signal indication and the other of which displays a flashing YELLOW ARROW signal indication.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Except as otherwise provided in Sections 4F.04, 4F.08, 4F.11, and 4F.15 for a three-section separate turn signal face with a bimodal signal section that displays a flashing YELLOW ARROW signal indication, the signal section that displays a flashing yellow signal indication during steady mode operation: A.Shall not be placed in the same vertical position as the signal section that displays a steady yellow signal indication, and B.Shall be placed below the signal section that displays a steady yellow signal indication.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Sections 4J.02 and 4N.02 contain exceptions to the provisions of this Section that are applicable to hybrid beacons.</p>
</div>

## 4E.05 Positions of Signal Indications Within a Horizontal Signal Face

<div class="secmeta">Section 4E.05 &middot; manual page 1096 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4E.05/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In each horizontally-arranged signal face, all signal sections that display red signal indications shall be located to the left of all signal sections that display yellow and green signal indications.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In horizontally-arranged signal faces, each signal section that displays a YELLOW ARROW signal indication shall be located to the left of the signal section that displays the GREEN ARROW signal indication to which it applies.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The relative positions of signal sections in a horizontally-arranged signal face, from left to right, shall be as follows: CIRCULAR RED Steady and/or flashing left-turn RED ARROW Steady and/or flashing right-turn RED ARROW CIRCULAR YELLOW Steady left-turn YELLOW ARROW Flashing left-turn YELLOW ARROW Left-turn GREEN ARROW CIRCULAR GREEN Straight-through GREEN ARROW Steady right-turn YELLOW ARROW Flashing right-turn YELLOW ARROW Right-turn GREEN ARROW</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If a bimodal signal section (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4e/#4e01">Section 4E.01</a>) is used in a horizontally-arranged signal face, the signal section that displays the dual left-turn arrow signal indication shall be located immediately to the right of the signal section that displays the CIRCULAR YELLOW signal indication, the signal section that displays the straight- through GREEN ARROW signal indication shall be located immediately to the right of the signal section that displays the CIRCULAR GREEN signal indication, and the signal section that displays the dual right-turn arrow signal indication shall be located to the right of all other signal sections.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Except as otherwise provided in Sections 4F.04, 4F.08, 4F.11, and 4F.15 for a three-section separate turn signal face with a flashing YELLOW ARROW signal indication, the signal section that displays a flashing yellow signal indication during steady mode operation: A.Shall not be placed in the same horizontal position as the signal section that displays a steady yellow signal indication, and B.Shall be placed to the right of the signal section that displays a steady yellow signal indication.</p>
</div>
