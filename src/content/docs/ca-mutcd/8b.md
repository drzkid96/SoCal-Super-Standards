## 8B.01 Purpose and Application

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.01 &middot; manual page 1507 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Passive traffic control systems, consisting of signs and pavement markings only, identify and direct attention to the location of a grade crossing and advise road users to reduce their speed or stop at the grade crossing as necessary in order to yield to any rail traffic occupying, or approaching and in proximity to, the grade crossing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Signs and markings regulate, warn, and guide the road users so that they, as well as LRT vehicle operators on mixed-use alignments, can take appropriate action when approaching a grade crossing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Unless otherwise provided in this Chapter, the provisions of Part 2 are applicable to the design and location of signs at grade crossings.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Signs placed in advance of a grade crossing should be positioned to not obstruct the motorists’ line of sight of the flashing-light  signals or other traffic control devices at the grade crossing.</p>
</div>

## 8B.03 Grade Crossing (Crossbuck) Sign (R15-1) and Number of Tracks Plaque (R15-2P) at Active and Passive Grade Crossings

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.03 &middot; manual page 1507 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Grade Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r15-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R15-1.svg">R15-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-8b-1">Figure 8B-1</a>), commonly identified as the Crossbuck sign, shall be retroreflective white with the words RAILROAD CROSSING in black lettering, mounted as shown in <a href="#fig-8b-2">Figure 8B-2</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In most States, the Crossbuck sign requires road users to yield the right-of-way to rail traffic at a grade crossing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> As a minimum, one Crossbuck sign shall be used on each highway approach to every highway- rail grade crossing, alone or in combination with other traffic control devices.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> As a minimum, one Crossbuck sign shall be used on each highway approach to every highway- LRT grade crossing where flashing-light signals or automatic gates are used, alone or in combination with other traffic control devices.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A Crossbuck sign may be used on a highway approach to a highway-LRT grade crossing where flashing-light signals or automatic gates are not used, alone or in combination with other traffic control devices.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If there are two or more tracks at a grade crossing, the number of tracks shall be indicated on a supplemental Number of Tracks (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r15-2p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R15-2P.svg">R15-2P</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-8b-1">Figure 8B-1</a>) of inverted T shape mounted below the Crossbuck sign in the manner shown in <a href="#fig-8b-2">Figure 8B-2</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> On each approach to a highway-rail grade crossing and, if used, on each approach to a highway-LRT grade crossing, the Crossbuck sign shall be installed on the right-hand side of the highway on each approach to the grade crossing. Where restricted sight distance or unfavorable highway geometry exists on an approach to a grade crossing, or where there is a one-way multi-lane approach, an additional Crossbuck sign shall be installed on the left-hand side of the highway, possibly placed back-to-back with the Crossbuck sign for the opposite approach, or otherwise located so that two Crossbuck signs are displayed for that approach.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> A strip of retroreflective white material not less than 2 inches in width shall be used on the back of each blade of each Crossbuck sign for the length of each blade at all passive grade crossings, except those where Crossbuck signs have been installed back-to-back or where double-faced Crossbuck signs have been installed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> A strip of retroreflective white material not less than 2 inches in width may be used on the back of each blade of each Crossbuck sign for the length of each blade at active grade crossings.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Minimum clearance dimensions for crossbuck signs relative to the proximity to the nearest rail should conform to the requirements of the railroad company and/or transit agency, and the regulatory agency with statutory authority (if applicable) <span class="ca">CPUC</span>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 12 of this Section, the mounting height of Crossbuck signs, measured vertically from the center of the sign to the elevation of the near edge of the pavement, should be approximately 9 feet (see <a href="#fig-8b-2">Figure 8B-2</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The 9-foot mounting height for the Crossbuck sign may be varied as required by local conditions and may be increased to accommodate signs mounted below the Crossbuck sign.</p>
</div>

## 8B.04 Crossbuck Assemblies with YIELD or STOP Signs at Passive Grade Crossings

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.04 &middot; manual page 1508 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Crossbuck Assembly shall consist of a Crossbuck (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r15-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R15-1.svg">R15-1</a>) sign, and a Number of Tracks (R15- 2P) plaque if two or more tracks are present, that complies with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/8b/#8b03">Section 8B.03</a>, and either a YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) or STOP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>) sign installed on the same support, except as provided in Paragraph 10 of this Section. YIELD or STOP signs used at passive grade crossings shall be installed in compliance with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b18">Section 2B.18</a>, and Figures 8B-2 and 8B-3.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> At all public highway-rail grade crossings that are not equipped with the active traffic control systems that are described in Chapter 8D, except crossings where road users are directed by an authorized person on the ground to not enter the crossing at all times that an approaching train is about to occupy the crossing, a Crossbuck Assembly shall be installed on the right-hand side of the highway on each approach to the highway- rail grade crossing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If a Crossbuck sign is used on a highway approach to a public highway-LRT grade crossing that is not equipped with the active traffic control systems that are described in Chapter 8D, a Crossbuck Assembly shall be installed on the right-hand side of the highway on each approach to the highway-LRT grade crossing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where restricted sight distance or unfavorable highway geometry exists on an approach to a grade crossing that has a Crossbuck Assembly, or where there is a one-way multi-lane approach, an additional Crossbuck Assembly shall be installed on the left-hand side of the highway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A YIELD sign shall be the default traffic control device for Crossbuck Assemblies on all highway approaches to passive grade crossings unless an engineering study performed by the regulatory agency or highway authority having jurisdiction over the roadway approach determines <span class="ca">recommends</span>that a STOP sign is appropriate and <span class="ca">is  consistent with determination by CPUC</span>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05a</span> <span class="ca">STOP signs shall not be installed at any highway-rail grade crossing which is controlled by automatic traffic control  devices except as provided in CVC § 21355 and in the Options in this section of this Manual.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The use of STOP signs at passive grade crossings should be limited to unusual conditions where requiring all motor vehicles to make a full stop is determined to be necessary <span class="ca">recommended</span>by a Diagnostic Team <span class="ca">and consistent with  determination by CPUC</span>. Among the factors that should be considered by the Diagnostic Team are the line of sight to approaching rail traffic (giving due consideration to seasonal crops or vegetation beyond both the highway and railroad or LRT rights-of-ways), the number of tracks, the speeds of trains or LRT equipment and motor vehicles, and the crash history at the cross grading.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Where a passive grade crossing is located on a stop-controlled approach and the clear storage distance is less than the length of the design vehicle, and where adequate sight distance to oncoming traffic on the parallel roadway is available to road users stopped on the approach to the grade crossing, consideration should be given to installing a STOP sign at the Crossbuck Assembly instead of at the highway-highway intersection. If the STOP sign is installed at the Crossbuck Assembly instead of at the highway-highway intersection, the Diagnostic Team should consider installing some other intersection traffic control device at the highway-highway intersection.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If a Crossbuck Assembly is installed on the approach to a passive grade crossing located at a highway- highway intersection controlled by a traffic control signal that is not interconnected with the grade crossing and not preempted by the approach of rail traffic, a Diagnostic Team shall be convened to determine <span class="ca">evaluate and  recommend</span>the appropriate traffic control devices. A STOP sign shall not be installed on a Crossbuck Assembly in this situation.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Sections 8A.01 through 8A.05 contain information regarding the responsibilities of the Diagnostic Team, highway agency, regulatory agency with statutory authority (if applicable) <span class="ca">CPUC</span>, and the railroad company or transit agency regarding the selection, design, and operation of traffic control devices placed at grade crossings.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> If a YIELD or STOP sign is installed for a Crossbuck Assembly at a grade crossing, it may be installed on the same support as the Crossbuck sign or it may be installed on a separate support at a point where the motor vehicle is to stop, or as near to that point as practicable, but in either case, the YIELD or STOP sign is considered to be a part of the Crossbuck Assembly.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> If a YIELD or STOP sign is installed on an existing Crossbuck sign support, the mounting height, measured vertically from the bottom of the YIELD or STOP sign to the top of the curb, or in the absence of curb, measured vertically from the bottom of the YIELD or STOP sign to the elevation of the near edge of the traveled way, shall be at least 4 feet (see <a href="#fig-8b-2">Figure 8B-2</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> If a Crossbuck Assembly is installed on a new sign support (see <a href="#fig-8b-2">Figure 8B-2</a>) or if the YIELD or STOP sign is installed on a separate support (see <a href="#fig-8b-3">Figure 8B-3</a>), the mounting height, measured vertically from the bottom of the YIELD or STOP sign to the top of the curb, or in the absence of curb, measured vertically from the bottom of the YIELD or STOP sign to the elevation of the near edge of the traveled way, shall be at least 5 feet in rural areas and shall be at least 7 feet in areas where parking or pedestrian movements are likely to occur.</p>
</div>
<div class="mfig-inline single"><figure class="mfig"><span id="fig-8b-3"></span><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ca-mutcd/p1523.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ca-mutcd/p1523.webp" alt="Figure 8B-3. Crossbuck Assembly with a YIELD or STOP Sign on a Separate Sign Support"></a><figcaption>Figure 8B-3. Crossbuck Assembly with a YIELD or STOP Sign on a Separate Sign Support <span class="src-page">p 1523</span></figcaption></figure></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> If a YIELD or STOP sign is installed for a Crossbuck Assembly at a grade crossing on a separate support than the Crossbuck sign (see <a href="#fig-8b-3">Figure 8B-3</a>), the YIELD or STOP sign <span class="ca">face of the sign</span>should be placed in the same plane as the Crossbuck sign and closer to the traveled way than the Crossbuck sign. The minimum separation between the nearest point of the YIELD or STOP sign and the nearest point of the Crossbuck sign should be 2 inches as shown in Figure 8B- 3.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> The meaning of a Crossbuck Assembly that includes a YIELD sign is that a road user approaching the grade crossing needs to be prepared to decelerate, and when necessary, yield the right-of-way to any rail traffic that might be occupying the crossing or might be approaching and in such close proximity to the crossing that it would be unsafe for the road user to cross.</p>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> Certain commercial motor vehicles and school buses are required to stop at all grade crossings in accordance with 49 CFR 392.10 even if a YIELD sign (or just a Crossbuck sign) is posted.</p>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> The meaning of a Crossbuck Assembly that includes a STOP sign is that a road user approaching the grade crossing must come to a full stop not less than 15 feet short of the nearest rail, and remain stopped while the road user determines if there is rail traffic either occupying the crossing or approaching and in such close proximity to the crossing that the road user must yield the right-of-way to rail traffic. The road user is permitted to proceed when it is safe to cross.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> A vertical strip of retroreflective white material, not less than 2 inches in width, shall be used on each Crossbuck support at passive grade crossings for the full length of the back of the support from the Crossbuck sign or Number of Tracks plaque to within 2 feet above the near edge of the roadway, except as provided in Paragraph 18 of this Section. A white retroreflective strip wrapped around a round support for the full length of the support from the Crossbuck Sign or Number of Tracks plaque to within 2 feet above the near edge of the roadway shall satisfy this requirement as long as the round support has an outside diameter of at least 2 inches.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> The vertical strip of retroreflective material may be omitted from the back sides of Crossbuck sign supports installed on one-way streets and at pathway or sidewalk grade crossings (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/8e/#8e05">Section 8E.05</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">19</span> If a YIELD or STOP sign is installed on the same support as the Crossbuck sign, a vertical strip of red (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a11">Section 2A.11</a>) or white retroreflective material that is at least 2 inches wide may be used on the front of the support from the YIELD or STOP sign to within 2 feet above the near edge of the roadway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">20</span> If a Crossbuck sign support at a passive grade crossing does not include a YIELD or STOP sign (either because the YIELD or STOP sign is placed on a separate support or because a YIELD or STOP sign is not present on the approach), a vertical strip of retroreflective white material, not less than 2 inches in width, shall be used for the full length of the front of the support from the Crossbuck sign or Number of Tracks plaque to within 2 feet above the near edge of the roadway. A white retroreflective strip wrapped around a round support for the full length of the support from the Crossbuck Sign or Number of Tracks plaque to within 2 feet above the near edge of the roadway shall satisfy this requirement as long as the round support has an outside diameter of at least 2 inches.</p>
<p><span class="pn">21</span> At all grade crossings where YIELD or STOP signs are installed, Yield Ahead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-2.svg">W3-2</a>) or Stop Ahead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-1.svg">W3-1</a>) signs shall also be installed if the criteria for their installation in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c35">Section 2C.35</a> is met.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">22</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/8c/#8c03">Section 8C.03</a> contains provisions regarding the use of stop lines or yield lines at grade crossings.</p>
</div>

## 8B.05 Use of STOP (R1-1) or YIELD (R1-2) Signs without Crossbuck Signs at Highway-LRT Grade Crossings

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.05 &middot; manual page 1510 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.05/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The use of only STOP or YIELD signs for road users at highway-LRT grade crossings should be limited to those crossings where the need and feasibility is determined <span class="ca">evaluated</span>by the Diagnostic Team <span class="ca">and consistent with determination  by CPUC</span>. Such crossings should have all of the following characteristics: A.The crossing roadways are secondary in character (such as a minor street with one lane in each direction, an alley, or a driveway) with low traffic volumes and low speed limits. The specific thresholds of traffic volumes and speed limits should be determined by the local agencies. B.The line of sight for an approaching LRT operator is adequate from a sufficient distance such that the operator can sound an audible signal and bring the LRT equipment to a stop before arriving at the crossing. C.The road user has sufficient sight distance at the stop line to permit the vehicle to cross the tracks before the arrival of the LRT equipment. D.If at an intersection of two roadways, the intersection does not meet the warrants for a traffic control signal as provided in Chapter 4C. E.The LRT tracks are located such that motor vehicles are not likely to stop on the tracks while waiting to enter a crossroad or highway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> For all highway-LRT grade crossings where only STOP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-1.svg">R1-1</a>) or YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) signs are installed, the placement shall comply with the requirements of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b18">Section 2B.18</a>. Stop Ahead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-1.svg">W3-1</a>) or Yield Ahead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-2.svg">W3-2</a>) Advance Warning signs shall also be installed if the criteria for their installation given in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c35">Section 2C.35</a> is met.</p>
</div>

## 8B.06 Grade Crossing Advance Warning Signs (W10-1 through W10-4)

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.06 &middot; manual page 1510 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.06/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Grade Crossing Advance Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-8b-4">Figure 8B-4</a>) shall be used on each highway in advance of every grade crossing, except in the following circumstances: A.On an approach to a grade crossing from an intersection with a parallel highway if the distance from the nearest rail of the tracks <span class="ca">stop line or yield line</span>to the edge of the parallel roadway is less than 100 feet and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-2.svg">W10-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-3.svg">W10-3</a>, or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-4.svg">W10-4</a> signs are used on the approaches of the parallel highway (see Paragraph 5 of this Section); B.On low-volume, low-speed highways crossing minor spurs or other tracks that are infrequently used and road users are directed by an authorized person on the ground to not enter the crossing at all times that approaching rail traffic is about to occupy the crossing; C.In business or commercial areas where active grade crossing traffic control systems are in use; D.Where physical conditions do not permit even a partially effective display of the sign; or E.At highway-LRT grade crossings where Crossbuck signs are not used (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/8b/#8b03">Section 8B.03</a>).</p>
</div>

## 8B.07 DO NOT STOP ON TRACKS Sign (R8-8)

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.07 &middot; manual page 1512 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.07/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> If motor vehicle queues are likely to extend onto the tracks, a DO NOT STOP ON TRACKS (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r8-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R8-8.svg">R8-8</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-8b-1">Figure 8B-1</a>) should be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Locations where motor vehicles could queue onto the grade crossing include intersections where a STOP or YIELD sign is installed downstream of the grade crossing, where there is a downstream circular intersection, or where there is a pre-signal installed at the grade crossing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r8-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R8-8.svg">R8-8</a> sign, if used, should be located on the right-hand side of the highway on either the near or far side of the grade crossing, depending upon which position provides better visibility to approaching drivers<span class="ca"> or motorists at the grade  crossing stop or yield line</span>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> DO NOT STOP ON TRACKS signs may be placed on both sides of the track.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> On divided highways and one-way streets, a second DO NOT STOP ON TRACKS sign may be placed on the near or far left-hand side of the highway at the grade crossing to further improve the visibility of the sign.</p>
</div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Where tracks are out of service, except as provided in Paragraphs 3 and 4 of this Section, traffic control devices and gate arms shall be removed and the signal heads shall be removed or hooded or turned from view to clearly indicate that they are not in operation.</p>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Warning signs, such as the Low Ground Clearance Grade Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5.svg">W10-5</a>) sign and the Skewed Crossing (W10- 12) sign, that warn road users about physical roadway conditions at the grade crossing should be left in place after the tracks are taken out of service, until the physical condition is no longer present.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in Paragraph 4 text. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r8-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R8-9.svg">R8-9</a> sign shall be removed when the tracks have been removed or paved over or when the grade crossing is returned to service. The Emergency Notification System (I13-1) sign shall be removed when the tracks have been removed or paved over.</p>
</div>

## 8B.16 Low Ground Clearance Grade Crossing Sign (W10-5)

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.16 &middot; manual page 1515 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.16/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> If the highway profile conditions are sufficiently abrupt to create a hang-up situation for long wheelbase vehicles or for trailers with low ground clearance, the Low Ground Clearance Grade Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5.svg">W10-5</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-8b-4">Figure 8B-4</a>) should be installed in advance of the grade crossing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Because this symbol might not be readily recognizable by the public, the Low Ground Clearance Grade Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5.svg">W10-5</a>) warning sign shall be accompanied by a LOW GROUND CLEARANCE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5P.svg">W10-5P</a>) educational plaque. The LOW GROUND CLEARANCE educational plaque shall remain in place for at least 3 years after the initial installation of the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5.svg">W10-5</a> sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a09">Section 2A.09</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Because other vehicle types and combinations also face the potential risk of hanging up at a grade crossing, word message warning signs and selective exclusion regulatory signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b45">Section 2B.45</a>) for specific vehicle types and combinations should be used in addition to, or in place of, the Low Ground Clearance Grade Crossing (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5.svg">W10-5</a>) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> While not all inclusive, some potential low ground clearance vehicles and combinations include single-unit trucks, buses, motor coaches, low-boy trailers, car carriers, and recreational vehicles.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Auxiliary plaques such as AHEAD, NEXT CROSSING, or USE NEXT CROSSING (with appropriate arrows), or a supplemental distance plaque should be placed below the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-5.svg">W10-5</a> sign at the nearest intersecting highway where a vehicle can detour or at a point on the highway wide enough to permit a U-turn.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If engineering judgment of roadway geometric and operating conditions confirms that motor vehicle speeds across the tracks should be below the posted speed limit, a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-1p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-1P.svg">W13-1P</a> advisory speed plaque should be posted.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A signed detour should be installed to guide potential hang-up vehicles to alternate nearby crossings to avoid the potential hang-up condition.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Information on ground clearance requirements <span class="ca">design considerations</span>at grade crossings is available in the 2019 edition of the “American Railway Engineering and Maintenance-of-Way Association’s Engineering Manual,” or in “A Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets,” 2018 Edition, AASHTO.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> An inventory of crossings with low ground clearance concerns, including a list of potential vehicle types that could hang up on the crossing, can be useful in tracking locations of low ground clearance crossings. Specific geometric conditions, known incidents, or anecdotal evidence of vehicle hang- ups can also be used to identify crossings with low ground clearance concerns.</p>
</div>

## 8B.27 Emergency Notification System Sign (I13-1)

<div class="secmeta">Section 8B.27 &middot; manual page 1517 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/8B.27/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">00</span> CPUC General Order 75, as amended, includes requirements on posting grade crossing identification.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Emergency Notification System (I13-1) sign (see <a href="#fig-8b-5">Figure 8B-5</a>) shall be installed on each approach at all highway-rail grade crossings, and at all highway-LRT grade crossings with automatic gates or flashinglight- signals <span class="ca">flashing-light signals</span>, to provide information to road users so that they can notify the railroad company or transit agency about emergencies or malfunctioning traffic control devices.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> At a highway-rail grade crossing, the Emergency Notification System sign shall, at a minimum, include the USDOT grade crossing inventory number and the emergency contact telephone number.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Where Emergency Notification System signs are used at a highway-LRT grade crossing, they shall, at a minimum, include a unique crossing identifier <span class="ca">either the CPUC or USDOT grade crossing inventory number</span> and the emergency contact telephone number.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The minimum width of the Emergency Notification System sign shall be 12 inches and the minimum height shall be 9 inches. The lettering on Emergency Notification System signs for the telephone number, the grade crossing inventory number, and the explanation of the purpose of the sign shall be composed of numerals and upper-case letters that are at least 1 inch in height.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Emergency Notification System signs shall be retroreflective.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 7 of this Section, Emergency Notification System signs shall have a white legend and border on a blue background.</p>
</div>
<div class="mfig-inline single"><figure class="mfig"><span id="fig-8b-5"></span><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ca-mutcd/p1527.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ca-mutcd/p1527.webp" alt="Figure 8B-5. Example of an Emergency Notification System Sign"></a><figcaption>Figure 8B-5. Example of an Emergency Notification System Sign <span class="src-page">p 1527</span></figcaption></figure></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The seven-character grade crossing inventory number may be shown on the sign as a black legend on a white rectangular background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 12 of this Section, Emergency Notification System signs should be attached to the Crossbuck Assemblies or grade crossing signal masts on the right-hand side of each roadway approach to the grade crossing rather than on the railroad or LRT signal control equipment housings. Emergency Notification System signs should be oriented so the face of the sign is approximately parallel or approximately perpendicular to the edge of the roadway or pathway and is visible to road users or pathway users. The visibility of the Emergency Notification System sign should not be obstructed by automatic gates in either the vertical or horizontal position.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> The Emergency Notification System signs should be positioned so as to not obstruct any traffic control devices or limit the view of rail traffic approaching the grade crossing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Emergency Notification System signs mounted on Crossbuck Assemblies or signal masts should only be large enough to provide the necessary contact information. Use of larger signs on Crossbuck Assemblies or signal masts that might obstruct the view of rail traffic or other motor vehicles should be avoided.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> At station crossings, Emergency Notification System signs or information should be posted in a conspicuous location.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> Emergency Notification System signs may be located on a separate post. Where located on a separate post, the size of the Emergency Notification System sign may be increased for improved visibility.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Where the improvement of the conspicuity of an Emergency Notification System sign is desired, a solid yellow rectangular header panel with a legend of “NOTICE” in black letters may be used (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a11">Section 2A.11</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> Additional Emergency Notification System signs may be installed at a grade crossing.</p>
</div>
