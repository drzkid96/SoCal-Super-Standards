## 4S.01 General Design and Operation of Flashing Beacons

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.01 &middot; manual page 1194 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A flashing beacon is a highway traffic signal with one or more signal sections that operates in a flashing mode. It can provide traffic control when used as an Intersection Control Beacon (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4s/#4s02">Section 4S.02</a>) or it can provide warning when used in other applications (see Sections 4S.03, 4S.04, and 4S.05).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Flashing beacon units, their mountings, signal visors, and backplates shall comply with the provisions of Chapters 4D and 4E, except as otherwise provided in this Chapter.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Beacons shall be flashed at a rate of not less than 50 or more than 60 times per minute. The illuminated period of each flash shall be a minimum of ½ and a maximum of ⅔ of the total cycle.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A beacon shall not be included within the border of a sign except for Interchange Exit Direction signs with advisory speed panels (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2e/#2e25">Section 2E.25</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> There shall be two nominal diameter sizes for flashing beacon signal indications: 8 inches and 12 inches.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If used to supplement a warning or regulatory sign, the edge of the beacon signal housing should normally be located no closer than 12 inches outside of the nearest edge of the sign or from the nearest edge of any of the signs and plaques in a sign assembly.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> An automatic dimming device may be used to reduce the brilliance of flashing yellow signal indications during night operation.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Backplates (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4d/#4d06">Section 4D.06</a>) may be used with flashing beacons.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Typical uses include:  A. Obstructions in or immediately adjacent to the roadway. B. Supplemental to advance warning signs. C. At mid-block crosswalks. D. At intersections where a warning is appropriate.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Only warning, regulatory or construction signs may be supplemented by flashing beacons.</p>
</div>

## 4S.02 Intersection Control Beacon

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.02 &middot; manual page 1194 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> An Intersection Control Beacon shall consist of one or more signal faces directed toward each approach to an intersection. Each signal face shall consist of one or more signal sections of a standard traffic signal face, with flashing CIRCULAR YELLOW or CIRCULAR RED signal indications in each signal face. They shall be installed and used only at an intersection to control two or more directions of travel.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Application of Intersection Control Beacon signal indications shall be limited to the following: A.Yellow on one route (normally the major street) and red for the remaining approaches that are controlled by STOP signs, or B.Red for all approaches (if all of the intersection approaches are controlled by STOP signs).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Flashing yellow signal indications shall not face conflicting vehicular approaches.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A STOP sign (see <a href="#">Section 2B.04</a>) shall be used on approaches to which a flashing red signal indication is displayed on an Intersection Control Beacon.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If two horizontally-aligned red signal indications are used on an approach for an Intersection Control Beacon, they shall be flashed simultaneously to avoid being confused with grade crossing flashing-light signals. If two vertically-aligned red signal indications that have a physical separation between them are used on an approach for an Intersection Control Beacon, they shall be flashed alternately.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Twelve-inch signal indications shall be used for Intersection Control Beacons facing approaches where: A.Road users view both Intersection Control Beacon and lane-use control signal indications simultaneously; or B.The nearest Intersection Control Beacon signal face is more than 120 feet beyond the stop line, unless a supplemental near-side Intersection Control Beacon signal face is provided.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Twelve-inch signal indications should be used for Intersection Control Beacons facing approaches where: A.The posted or statutory speed limit or the 85th-percentile approach speed is higher than 40 mph, or B.Where only post-mounted flashing beacon signal faces are used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> An Intersection Control Beacon should not be mounted on a pedestal in the roadway unless the pedestal is within the confines of a traffic or pedestrian island.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Supplemental signal indications may be used on one or more approaches in order to provide adequate visibility to approaching road users.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Intersection Control Beacons may be used at intersections where traffic or physical conditions do not justify conventional traffic control signals but crash rates indicate the possibility of a special need.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> An Intersection Control Beacon is generally located over the center of an intersection; however, it may be used at other suitable locations.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The cost of installing an Intersection Control Beacon and intersection lighting is shared with the local agency in the same  manner as a traffic signal.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> On undivided highways or on highways where the median is less than 8 feet wide, the installation may consist of a single  standard located off of the right shoulder or Type 9 cantilever flashing beacon installation as described for use on divided highways,  or it may be a Type 15-FBS flashing beacon installation.</p>
</div>

## 4S.03 Warning Beacon

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.03 &middot; manual page 1195 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Typical applications of Warning Beacons include the following: A.As supplemental emphasis to signs or object markers on or in front of obstructions that are in or immediately adjacent to the roadway; B.As supplemental emphasis to warning signs; C.As emphasis for midblock crosswalks; D.As supplemental emphasis to regulatory signs, except STOP, DO NOT ENTER, WRONG WAY, and SPEED LIMIT signs; and E.In conjunction with a regulatory or warning sign that includes the phrase WHEN FLASHING in its legend or on a supplemental plaque to indicate that the regulation is in effect or that the condition is present only at certain times. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2a/#2a12">Section 2A.12</a> prohibits the use flashing light-emitting diode (LED) units within the legend or border of the sign in conjunction with the phrase WHEN FLASHING in its legend or on a supplemental plaque.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> A Warning Beacon shall consist of one or more signal sections of a standard traffic signal face with a flashing CIRCULAR YELLOW signal indication in each signal section.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A Warning Beacon shall be used only to supplement an appropriate warning or regulatory sign or marker.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Warning Beacons, if used at intersections, shall not face conflicting vehicular approaches.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The condition or regulation justifying Warning Beacons should largely govern their location with respect to the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If an obstruction is in or adjacent to the roadway, illumination of the lower portion or the beginning of the obstruction or illumination of the sign on or in front of the obstruction, in addition to the beacon, should be considered.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Warning Beacons should be operated only during those periods or times when the condition or regulation exists.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If Warning Beacons have more than one signal section, they may be flashed either alternately or simultaneously.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> A Warning Beacon interconnected with a traffic signal controller assembly may be used with a BE PREPARED TO STOP (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-4.svg">W3-4</a>) sign and a WHEN FLASHING (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-13p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-13P.svg">W16-13P</a>) plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c35">Section 2C.35</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Warning Beacons that are actuated by pedestrians, bicyclists, or other road users may be used as appropriate to provide additional warning to vehicles approaching a crossing or other location.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> An audible information device should be used with pedestrian-actuated Warning Beacons to assist pedestrians with vision disabilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> If an audible information device is used in conjunction with a pedestrian-actuated Warning Beacon at a pedestrian crossing, the audible information device shall not use vibrotactile indications or percussive indications.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> If an audible information device is used in conjunction with a pedestrian-actuated Warning Beacon at a pedestrian crossing, the audible message should be a speech message that says, “Warning lights are flashing.” The audible message should be spoken twice.</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> Where a warning beacon is located 200 feet or less from a grade crossing, an engineering study should be made to evaluate the  placement and determine if queuing could impact the grade crossing.</p>
</div>

## 4S.04 Speed Limit Sign Beacon

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.04 &middot; manual page 1196 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Speed Limit Sign Beacon shall be used only to supplement a Speed Limit sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> A Speed Limit Sign Beacon shall consist of one or more signal sections of a standard traffic control signal face, with a flashing CIRCULAR YELLOW signal indication in each signal section. If two or more signal indications are used, they shall be alternately flashed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A Speed Limit Sign Beacon may be used with a fixed or variable Speed Limit sign. If applicable, a flashing Speed Limit Sign Beacon (with an appropriate accompanying sign) may be used to indicate that the displayed speed limit is in effect.</p>
</div>

## 4S.05 Stop Beacon

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.05 &middot; manual page 1196 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.05/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Stop Beacon shall be used only to supplement a STOP sign, a DO NOT ENTER sign, or a WRONG WAY sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> A Stop Beacon shall consist of one or more signal sections of a standard traffic signal face with a flashing CIRCULAR RED signal indication in each signal section. If two horizontally-aligned signal indications are used for a Stop Beacon, they shall be flashed simultaneously to avoid being confused with grade crossing flashing-light signals. If two vertically-aligned signal indications are used for a Stop Beacon, they shall be flashed alternately.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The edge of the signal housing of a Stop Beacon should be not less than 12 inches or more than 24 inches from the nearest edge of the STOP sign, DO NOT ENTER sign, or WRONG WAY sign that it supplements.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A Stop Sign Flashing Beacon consists of one or two signal sections with a flashing circular red indication in each section.</p>
</div>

## 4S.101(CA) Flashing Beacons at School Crosswalks

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.101(CA) &middot; manual page 1196 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.101(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Flashing yellow beacons may be installed to supplement standard school signing and markings for the purpose of providing  advanced warning during specified times of operation when justified.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Flashing beacons at school crosswalks may be installed on State highways in accordance with CVC §§ 21372 and 21373.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A flashing yellow beacon can be considered under the following conditions:  A.<span class="ca">The uncontrolled school crossing is on the “Suggested Route to School”; and  B.</span><span class="ca">At least 40 school pedestrians use the crossing during each of any two hours (not necessarily consecutive) of a normal  school day; and  C.</span><span class="ca">The crossing is at least 600 feet from the nearest alternate crossing controlled by traffic signals, stop signs or crossing  guards; and  D.</span><span class="ca">The vehicular volume through the crossing exceeds 200 vehicles per hour in urban areas or 140 vehicles per hour in rural  areas during the same hour the students are going to and from school during normal school hours; and  E.</span><span class="ca">The critical approach speeds exceeds 35 mph or the approach visibility is less than the stopping sight distance. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If school authorities are to operate flashing yellow beacon, an inter-agency agreement needs to be executed to assure  designations of a responsible adult to operate the beacon controls and to provide accessibility for necessary equipment  maintenance.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="#fig-4s-101ca">Figure 4S-101(CA)</a> shows the worksheet for flashing beacon at school crossings.</p>
</div>

## 4S.102(CA) Flashing Beacons for Fire Stations

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.102(CA) &middot; manual page 1197 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.102(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Flashing beacons at fire station driveways or at intersections immediately adjacent to a fire station may be installed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The flashing beacon shall be used only to supplement an appropriate warning or regulatory sign. The flashing beacon  shall be actuated from an non-illuminated condition by a switch at the fire station.</p>
</div>

## 4S.103(CA) Flashing Beacons at Bus Stops on Freeway Interchanges

<div class="secmeta">Section 4S.103(CA) &middot; manual page 1197 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/4S.103(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> At locations of approved bus stops within interchange areas, a flashing beacon may be provided near the top of a lighting  standard to provide a flag stop.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The following design and operational requirements shall be met:  A.<span class="ca">A push button shall be provided on the lighting standard with a sign explaining the purpose and operation. The  sign shall state that if no bus has arrived within 15 minutes (or other time) after the button has been actuated it will  be necessary to actuate it again.  B.</span><span class="ca">The flashing beacon shall consist of a 8-inch, signal section with an uncolored or white lens mounted on the  lighting standard in such a position that an approaching bus driver can see it on the freeway.  C.</span><span class="ca">The operation of the control shall be such that the flashing beacon will operate for 15 minutes after the button has  been actuated and then go out. </span></p>
</div>
