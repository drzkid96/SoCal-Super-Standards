## 6G.03 Regulatory Sign Applications

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.03 &middot; manual page 1251 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> If a TTC zone requires regulatory measures different from those existing, the existing permanent regulatory devices shall be removed or covered and superseded by the appropriate temporary regulatory signs. ## 6G.04 Road Closed Signs (R11-2 Series)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.04 &middot; manual page 1251 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.04/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The ROAD CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used when the roadway is closed to all road users except contractors’ equipment or officially authorized vehicles. The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> sign should be accompanied by appropriate warning and detour signing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> STREET CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2a.svg">R11-2a</a>), BRIDGE OUT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2b.svg">R11-2b</a>), or PATH CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2c/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2c.svg">R11-2c</a>) signs may be substituted for Road Closed signs where applicable.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Road Closed signs should be installed at or near the center of the roadway on or above a Type 3 Barricade that closes the roadway (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6k/#6k07">Section 6K.07</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Road Closed signs shall not be used where road user flow is maintained through the TTC zone with a reduced number of lanes on the existing roadway or where the actual closure is some distance beyond the sign.</p>
</div>

## 6G.05 Local Traffic Only Signs (R11-3 Series and R11-4)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.05 &middot; manual page 1251 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.05/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Local Traffic Only signs (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used where road user flow detours to avoid a closure some distance beyond the sign, but where local road users can use the roadway to the point of closure. These signs should be accompanied by appropriate warning and detour signing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for errors in Paragraph 1. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details.</span></p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In rural applications, the Local Traffic Only sign should have the legend ROAD CLOSED XX MILES AHEAD, LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3.svg">R11-3</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> In urban areas, a ROAD (STREET) CLOSED TO THRU TRAFFIC (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-4.svg">R11-4</a>) sign or the legend ROAD CLOSED, LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY may be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> In urban areas, a word message that includes the name of an intersecting street name or well-known destination may be substituted for the words XX MILES AHEAD on the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3.svg">R11-3</a> sign where applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A STREET CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3a.svg">R11-3a</a>) or BRIDGE OUT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3b.svg">R11-3b</a>) sign may be substituted for a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3.svg">R11-3</a> sign, where applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The words BRIDGE OUT, BRIDGE CLOSED, or STREET CLOSED may be substituted for the words ROAD CLOSED on the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-4.svg">R11-4</a> sign where applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The word RAMP may be substituted for ROAD or STREET where applicable.</p>
</div>

## 6G.06 Weight Limit Signs (R12-1, R12-2, and R12-5)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.06 &middot; manual page 1252 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.06/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Weight Limit sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>), which shows the gross weight or axle weight that is permitted on the roadway or bridge, shall be consistent with State or local regulations and shall not be installed without the approval of the authority having jurisdiction over the highway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> When weight restrictions are imposed because of the activity in a TTC zone, a marked detour shall be provided for vehicles weighing more than the posted limit.</p>
</div>

## 6G.07 STAY IN LANE Signs (R4-9 and R4-9a)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.07 &middot; manual page 1252 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.07/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A STAY IN LANE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-9.svg">R4-9</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be used where a multi-lane shift has been incorporated as part of the TTC on a highway to direct road users around road work that occupies part of the roadway on a multi-lane highway.</p>
</div>

<p><span class="pn">02</span> A STAY IN LANE TO MERGE POINT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r4-9a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R4-9a.svg">R4-9a</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used during late merge operations (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6n/#6n19">Section 6N.19</a>) to direct traffic to use all available lanes until the merge point is reached.</p>
</div>

## 6G.08 Work Zone and Higher Fines Signs and Plaques

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.08 &middot; manual page 1252 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.08/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A WORK ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be mounted above a Speed Limit sign to emphasize that a reduced speed limit is in effect within a TTC zone. An END WORK ZONE SPEED LIMIT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-12/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-12.svg">R2-12</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be installed at the downstream end of the reduced speed limit zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> A BEGIN HIGHER <span class="ca">DOUBLE</span> FINES ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-10.svg">R2-10</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be installed at or near the beginning of a TTC zone where increased fines are imposed for traffic violations, and an END HIGHER <span class="ca">DOUBLE </span> FINES ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-11.svg">R2-11</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be installed at or near the downstream end of the TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Alternate legends such as BEGIN (or END) DOUBLE FINES ZONE may also be used for the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-10.svg">R2-10</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-11.svg">R2-11</a> signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A FINES HIGHER, FINES DOUBLE, or $XX FINE plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/#2b25">Section 2B.25</a> and <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be mounted below the Speed Limit sign if increased fines are imposed for traffic violations within the TTC zone.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Individual signs and plaques for work zone speed limits and higher fines may be combined into a single sign or may be displayed as an assembly of signs and plaques.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The TRAFFIC FINES DOUBLED IN CONSTRUCTION ZONES (C40(CA)) and TRAFFIC FINES DOUBLED IN WORK ZONES  (C40A(CA)) (see <a href="#fig-6g-1ca">Figure 6G-1(CA)</a>) signs may be placed approximately 500 feet in advance of the first required TTC sign(s). The  placement of the C40(CA) and C40A(CA) signs is at the discretion of the responsible person(s) in charge of the work zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Refer to CVC § 42009 for fines for offenses committed in highway construction or maintenance area. In California, as per CVC  only doubling of the fines is allowed, not higher fines of other denominations.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The C40A(CA) sign is intended to be manufactured as a fabric sign and should be used on a short term (daily) basis only.  Longer term situations should use the C40(CA) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> CVC § 22362 applies to "When Workers are Present" condition and signs need to be covered or removed when no work is in  progress. However, per CVC 21367, agency can "...regulate the movement of traffic...whenever the traffic would endanger the  safety of workers or the work would interfere with or endanger the movement of traffic through the area." If obstructions would be  present throughout the project duration the signs would not need to be covered or removed. This would also apply to situations  where the construction work changes the highway configuration, curvature or elevation, making it necessary to post reduced speed  limits.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The Speed Limit (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a>) sign with a WORK ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a>) plaque mounted above it may be used for the protection of workers  during working hours to reduce speed limit within a TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> sign with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> plaque mounted above it shall only be used in conjunction with appropriate advance  warning signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> signs with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> plaques mounted above them shall be removed or covered promptly when no longer  applicable.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> sign with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> mounted above it is authorized for use by CVC § 22362. This section provides authority to post a  speed limit of not less than 25 mph at locations where employees of any contractor, or of the agency in charge of the job, are  engaged in work upon the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> Posting unrealistically low speed limits will result in loss of sign credibility and a high violation rate.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> Before using a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> sign with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> plaque mounted above it, work zone conditions should be analyzed to determine what  maximum speed limit would be appropriate for that particular location.</p>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> sign with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> plaque mounted above it should be placed within 400 feet of the zone where workers are on the  roadway or so nearly adjacent as to be endangered by traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> sign with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> plaque mounted above it may be provided by the agency having jurisdiction over the street or road.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-1.svg">R2-1</a> sign with <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a> plaque mounted above it should be posted a maximum distance of 400 feet in advance of where,  and when workers are present; and the Speed Reduction (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-5.svg">W3-5</a>) sign or Speed Zone Ahead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-4ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r2-4ca.webp">R2-4(CA)</a>) sign informs road users of  the reduced speed limit TTC zone.</p>
<p><span class="pn">19</span> As the TTC zone activities change, signs should be moved as appropriate.</p>
</div>

## 6G.10 SIDEWALK CLOSED Signs (R9-9, R9-10, R9-11, and R9-11a)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.10 &middot; manual page 1253 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.10/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> SIDEWALK CLOSED signs (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used where pedestrian flow is restricted. Bicyclist/Pedestrian Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9a.svg">M4-9a</a>) signs or Pedestrian Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9b.svg">M4-9b</a>) signs should be used where pedestrian flow is rerouted (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6i/#6i02">Section 6I.02</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-9.svg">R9-9</a>) sign should be installed at the beginning of the closed sidewalk, at the intersections preceding the closed sidewalk, and elsewhere along the closed sidewalk as needed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED, (ARROW) USE OTHER SIDE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-10.svg">R9-10</a>) sign should be installed at the beginning of the restricted sidewalk when a parallel sidewalk exists on the other side of the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED AHEAD, (ARROW) CROSS HERE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-11.svg">R9-11</a>) sign should be used to indicate to pedestrians that sidewalks beyond the sign are closed and to direct them to open crosswalks, sidewalks, or other travel paths.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED, (ARROW) CROSS HERE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-11a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-11a.svg">R9-11a</a>) sign should be installed just beyond the point to which pedestrians are being redirected.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> These signs are typically mounted on a detectable barricade to encourage compliance and to communicate with pedestrians that the sidewalk is closed. Printed signs are not useful to many pedestrians with vision disabilities. A barrier or barricade detectable by a person with a vision disability is sufficient to indicate that a sidewalk is closed. If the barrier is continuous with detectable channelizing devices for an alternate route, accessible signing might not be necessary.</p>
</div>

## 6G.101(CA) RAMP CLOSED (C2(CA)) and RAMP CLOSED, USE RAMP AT \_\_ (SC7(CA)) Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.101(CA) &middot; manual page 1254 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6G.101(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The RAMP CLOSED (C2(CA)) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-6g-1ca">Figure 6G-1(CA)</a>) shall be installed at the entrance of an on-ramp or off-ramp  that is closed to all road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The RAMP CLOSED, USE RAMP AT __ (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc7ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc7ca.webp">SC7(CA)</a>) sign may be used in lieu of the RAMP CLOSED (C2(CA)) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The RAMP CLOSED AHEAD (C19(CA)) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a>) should be used in advance of the point where an on- ramp or off-ramp is closed to all road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The USE NEXT EXIT (C38(CA)) sign may be used with the RAMP CLOSED (C2(CA)) sign on freeways if the next exit provides  access to destinations from the closed ramp.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The Day – Specific hours (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc6-3pca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc6-3pca.webp">SC6-3P(CA)</a>) plaque should be installed below the RAMP CLOSED (C2(CA)) sign to notify motorists  when a freeway or expressway on-ramp or off-ramp is temporarily closed for no more than one day.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The Multiple Days – Specific Hours (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc6-4pca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc6-4pca.webp">SC6-4P(CA)</a>) plaque should be installed below the RAMP CLOSED (C2(CA)) sign to notify  motorists when a freeway or expressway on-ramp or off-ramp is temporarily closed for multiple consecutive days.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The RAMP CLOSED assembly should be removed when the ramp is reopened to traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc6-3pca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc6-3pca.webp">SC6-3P(CA)</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc6-4pca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc6-4pca.webp">SC6-4P(CA)</a> plaques (refer to <a href="#fig-6i-1ca">Figure 6I-1(CA)</a>) shall display the correct day of the week, month,  calendar date, and times during which the ramp is closed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> For extended ramp closures, a PCMS may be used in advance of the ramp to supplement the RAMP CLOSED assembly for the  duration of the closure.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The RAMP CLOSED, USE RAMP AT ___ (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc7ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc7ca.webp">SC7(CA)</a>) sign may be used in lieu of the combination of the RAMP CLOSED  (C2(CA)) sign and USE NEXT EXIT (C38(CA)) sign as shown on Caltrans’ Standard Plan T-14 to inform motorists of a closed  entrance or exit ramp and to provide an alternate route. See <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a> for information regarding this publication.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> The ___ EXIT – RAMP CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc8ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc8ca.webp">SC8(CA)</a>) sign should be used to inform motorists of a closed exit ramp.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> If used, the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc8ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc8ca.webp">SC8(CA)</a> sign shall be placed on the right shoulder, upstream of the preceding exit ramp.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The EXIT with Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc18ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc18ca.webp">SC18(CA)</a>) sign should be used to inform motorists of the direction to follow for a freeway exit within a  work zone.</p>
</div>
