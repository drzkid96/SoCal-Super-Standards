## 6H.01 Warning Sign Function, Design, and Application

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.01 &middot; manual page 1261 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> TTC zone warning signs (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) notify road users of specific situations or conditions on or adjacent to a roadway that might not otherwise be apparent.</p>
</div>
<div class="mfig-inline single"><figure class="mfig"><span id="fig-6h-1"></span><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ca-mutcd/p1273.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ca-mutcd/p1273.webp" alt="Figure 6H-1. Warning Signs and Plaques in Temporary Traffic Control Zones"></a><figcaption>Figure 6H-1. Warning Signs and Plaques in Temporary Traffic Control Zones <span class="src-page">p 1273</span></figcaption></figure></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> TTC warning signs shall comply with the Standards for warning signs presented in Part 2 and in the FHWA’s “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The sizes for TTC warning signs shall be as shown in <a href="#fig-6h-1">Table 6H-1</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 5 of this Section, TTC warning signs shall be diamond-shaped with a black legend and border on an orange <span class="ca">(or fluorescent orange) </span>background, except for the Grade Crossing Advance Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a>) sign, which shall have a black legend and border on a yellow <span class="ca">(or fluorescent yellow) </span>background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Warning signs that are required or recommended in Parts 2 or 7 to have a fluorescent yellow-green background may have that color background in TTC zones.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Existing warning signs with a yellow background that are still applicable may remain in place.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Warning signs used for TTC incident management situations may have a black legend and border on a fluorescent pink background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Mounting or space considerations may justify a change from the standard diamond shape to a rectangular shape.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> In emergencies, available warning signs having yellow backgrounds may be used if signs with orange or fluorescent pink backgrounds are not at hand.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Where roadway or road user conditions require greater emphasis, larger than standard size warning signs should be used, with the symbol or legend enlarged approximately in proportion to the outside dimensions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Where any part of the roadway is obstructed or closed by work activities or incidents, advance warning signs should be installed to alert road users well in advance of these obstructions or restrictions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> Where road users include pedestrians, the provision of supplemental audible information or detectable barriers or barricades should be provided for people with vision disabilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Detectable barriers or barricades communicate very clearly to pedestrians who have vision disabilities that they can no longer proceed in the direction that they are traveling.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> Advance warning signs may be used singly or in combination.</p>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> Where distances are not displayed on warning signs as part of the message, a supplemental plaque with the distance legend may be mounted immediately below the sign on the same support.</p>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> The NO SHOULDER (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-23/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-23.svg">W8-23</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) may be used where no earth, gravel or paved shoulders are available for  vehicles to pull off the roadway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> Some of the California warning signs used in TTC zones are shown in <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a> and <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Table 6H-1(CA)</a>.</p>
</div>

## 6H.02 Position of Advance Warning Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.02 &middot; manual page 1261 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.02/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Where highway conditions permit, warning signs should be placed in advance of the transition and activity areas at varying distances depending on roadway type, condition, and posted speed. <a href="#fig-6b-1">Table 6B-1</a> contains information regarding the spacing of advance warning signs. Where a series of two or more advance warning signs is used, the closest sign to the transition and activity areas should be placed approximately 100 feet for low-speed urban streets to 1,000 feet or more for freeways and expressways.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Where multiple advance warning signs are needed on the approach to a transition and activity area, the ROAD WORK AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a>) sign should be the first advance warning sign encountered by road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Various conditions, such as limited sight distance or obstructions that might require a driver to reduce speed or stop, might require additional advance warning signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> As an alternative to a specific distance on advance warning signs, the word AHEAD may be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> At TTC zones on lightly-traveled roads, all of the advance warning signs prescribed for major construction might not be needed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Utility work, maintenance, or minor construction can occur within the TTC zone limits of a major construction project, and additional warning signs may be needed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Utility, maintenance, and minor construction signing and TTC should be coordinated with appropriate authorities so that road users are not confused or misled by the additional TTC devices.</p>
</div>

## 6H.03 ROAD (STREET) WORK Sign (W20-1)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.03 &middot; manual page 1262 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The ROAD (STREET) WORK (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>), which serves as a general warning of obstructions or restrictions, should be located in advance of the work space or any detour, on the road where the work is taking place.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Where traffic can enter a TTC zone from a crossroad or a major (high-volume) driveway, an advance warning sign should be used on the crossroad or major driveway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The legend STREET may be substituted for ROAD and the distance legend may be either XX FEET, XX MILES, or AHEAD.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The RAMP WORK AHEAD (C23(CA)) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a>) may be substituted for the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a> sign where applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The ROAD (STREET) WORK Informational plaque (C23B(CA)) (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a>) may be used with the ROAD  (STREET) WORK (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a>) sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The message displayed on the ROAD (STREET) WORK Informational plaque (C23B(CA)) shall be worded in terms  common to motorists, as shown in examples below. The height and width of the plate will vary according to the lettering  size and message. The width of the plate shall not exceed the overall width of the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a> sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Following are some example messages:  A. BRIDGE REPLACEMENT  B. BRIDGE WIDENING  C. BRIDGE REPAIR  D. CURVE IMPROVEMENT  E. HIGHWAY REALIGNMENT  F. HIGHWAY WIDENING  G. HIGHWAY WIDENING AND PAVING  H. HIGHWAY REHABILITATION  I. STORM REPAIR  J. PAVING  K. SIGNING IMPROVEMENT  L. PAVEMENT MAINTENANCE  M. SAFETY IMPROVEMENT</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The SPECIAL EVENT AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc5ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc5ca.webp">SC5(CA)</a>) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a>) should be used in lieu of the ROAD (STREET) WORK  (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a>) sign for special events, such as bike races, movie filming, etc., where the event is on the travel way or close enough or of  such a nature as to have a potential effect on motorists, bicyclists and pedestrians.</p>
</div>

## 6H.07 Lane(s) Closed Signs (W20-5, W20-5a, and W9-3)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.07 &middot; manual page 1263 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.07/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Lane(s) Closed sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) shall be used in advance of that point where one or more through lanes of a multi-lane roadway are closed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> For a single lane closure, the Lane Closed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-5.svg">W20-5</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) shall use the legend RIGHT (LEFT) LANE CLOSED. </div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02a</span> <span class="ca">The Lane Closed or LANE(S) CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-5.svg">W20-5</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-5a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-5a.svg">W20-5a</a> or C20(CA)) sign by itself, or in combination with LEFT (C20A(CA))  sign overlay and/or Numeral (C20B(CA)) sign overlay may be used. (Refer to Figures 6H-1, 6H-1(CA) and 6I-1(CA)).</span></p>
<p><span class="pn">02b</span> <span class="ca">The LANE CLOSED (C30(CA)) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a>) may be used within a closed lane of a multilane highway as  follow-up information to the appropriate advance warning signs. The C30(CA) sign may be repeated at intervals, throughout long  lane closures, as a reminder to motorists.</span></p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The distance legend may be either XX FEET, XX MILES, or AHEAD.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The Interior Lane Shift (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-3.svg">W9-3</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) should be used in advance of that point where work occupies an interior lane(s) and approaching motor vehicle traffic is directed to the right or left of the work zone in the lane(s) by using a shifting taper to route traffic around the closed interior lane(s).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> For moving lane closures on State highways, see Caltrans’ Standard Plan T-16. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a> for information regarding  this publication.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Do not use the CENTER LANE CLOSED AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w9-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W9-3.svg">W9-3</a>) sign (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) for moving lane closures on State highways.</p>

## 6H.10 RAMP NARROWS Sign (W5-4)

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.10 &middot; manual page 1264 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.10/">short link</a></div>

## 6H.25 Signs for Blasting Areas

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.25 &middot; manual page 1266 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.25/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Road users shall be warned where blasting operations occur. A sequence of signs shall be prominently displayed to warn all road users of blasting operations and to direct operators of mobile radio equipment, including cellular telephones, to turn off transmitters in a blasting area. These signs shall be covered or removed when there are no explosives in the area or the area is otherwise secured.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The BLASTING ZONE AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-1.svg">W22-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) shall be used in advance of any TTC zone where explosives are being used. The TURN OFF 2-WAY RADIO AND CELL PHONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r22-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R22-2.svg">R22-2</a>) and END BLASTING ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-3.svg">W22-3</a>) signs shall be used in sequence with this sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The TURN OFF 2-WAY RADIO AND CELL PHONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r22-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R22-2.svg">R22-2</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g11">Section 6G.11</a> and <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) shall follow the BLASTING ZONE AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-1.svg">W22-1</a>) sign and shall be placed at least 1,000 feet before the beginning of the blasting zone.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The END BLASTING ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-3.svg">W22-3</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) shall be placed a minimum of 1,000 feet past the blasting zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The END BLASTING ZONE sign may be placed either with or preceding the END ROAD WORK sign.</p>

## 6H.103(CA) OPEN TRENCH Sign (C27(CA))

<div class="secmeta">Section 6H.103(CA) &middot; manual page 1271 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6H.103(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<p><span class="pn">02</span> The edge of the traveled way shall be defined by edge line delineation consisting of appropriate markers or striping.  Edge line delineation shall be white when located on the right of traffic and yellow when located on the left of traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Trenches in excess of 0.15 feet in depth but not exceeding 0.25 feet in depth that are less than 8 feet from the edge of traveled  way should be identified by LOW SHOULDER (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-9.svg">W8-9</a>) signs (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) set in the trench adjacent to the edge of pavement  at intervals not to exceed every 2,000 feet.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Portable delineators may be placed at intervals not to exceed 100 feet in lieu of edge line delineation.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Trenches in excess of 0.25 feet but less than 2.5 feet in depth that are less than 8 feet from the edge of traveled way  shall be identified by alternating C27(CA) and NO SHOULDER (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-23/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-23.svg">W8-23</a>) signs (refer to <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a> and <a href="#fig-6h-1ca">Figure 6H-1(CA)</a>) set  in the trench at intervals not to exceed 2,000 feet.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Channelizers (CA) or portable delineators should be placed 2 feet to 6 feet outside of the edge line at 100 feet intervals for the  conditions described above.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Trenches in excess of 0.25 feet in depth but not exceeding 2.5 feet in depth that are 8 feet to 15 feet from the edge of traveled  way should be identified by C27(CA) signs set in the trench at intervals not to exceed every 2,000 feet. Portable delineators should  be placed at 200 feet intervals within 2 feet from the edge of the trench and at 100 feet intervals for edge conditions exceeding 0.5  feet in depth.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Trenches in excess of 0.5 feet in depth but not exceeding 2.5 feet in depth that are more than 15 feet from the edge of traveled  way at locations where a recovery area was available prior to construction should be identified by placing delineators at 200 feet  intervals within 2 feet from the edge of the trench and by placing C27(CA) signs in the trench at intervals not to exceed 2,000 feet.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Signing for trenches in excess of 2.5 feet in depth shall be based upon engineering judgment or studies (as noted in  <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1d/#1d03">Section 1D.03</a>) to ensure proper visibility of barricades and signing.</p>
</div>
