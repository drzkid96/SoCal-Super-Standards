## 6I.01 Guide Signs – General

<div class="secmeta">Section 6I.01 &middot; manual page 1283 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6I.01/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Guide signs along highways provide road users with information to help them along their way through the TTC zone. The design of guide signs is presented in Part 2.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The following guide signs should be used in TTC zones as needed: A.Standard route markings where temporary route changes are necessary, B.Directional signs and street name signs, and C.Special guide signs relating to the condition or work being done.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If additional temporary guide signs are used in TTC zones, they shall have a black legend and border on an orange background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Guide signs used in TTC incident management situations may have a black legend and border on a fluorescent pink background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When temporary directional signs and temporary street name signs are used in conjunction with detour routing, these signs may have a black legend and border on an orange background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> When permanent directional signs or permanent street name signs are used in conjunction with detour signing, they may have a white legend on a green background (see Sections 2D.35 and 2D.45).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The sizes for TTC guide signs shall be as shown in <a href="#fig-6i-1">Table 6I-1</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">Refer to FHWA’s List of Known Errors for error in labeling Paragraph 7. Refer to <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/1a/#1a04">Section 1A.04</a> for more details. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <span class="ca">Some of the California guide signs used in TTC zones are shown in <a href="#fig-6i-1ca">Figure 6I-1(CA)</a> and <a href="#fig-6i-1ca">Table 6I-1(CA)</a>. </span></p>
</div>

## 6I.02 Detour Signs and Plaques (M4-8P, M4-8a, M4-8bP, M4-9, M4-9a, M4-9b, M4-9c, and M4-

<div class="secmeta">Section 6I.02 &middot; manual page 1283 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6I.02/">short link</a></div>

<p>10)</p>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Each detour shall be adequately marked with standard temporary route signs and destination signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01a</span> <span class="ca">Refer to CVC § 21363 for detour signs. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Detour signs in TTC incident management situations may have a black legend and border on a fluorescent pink background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Detour Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-10.svg">M4-10</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be used where a detour route has been established.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The DETOUR (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-8p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-8P.svg">M4-8P</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be mounted at the top of a route sign assembly to mark a temporary route that detours from a highway, bypasses a section closed by a TTC zone, and rejoins the highway beyond the TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The Detour Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-10.svg">M4-10</a>) sign should normally be mounted just below the ROAD CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3a.svg">R11-3a</a>, or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-4.svg">R11-4</a>) sign. The Detour Arrow sign should include a horizontal arrow pointed to the right or left as required.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The DETOUR (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9.svg">M4-9</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) should be used for unnumbered highways, for emergency situations, for periods of short durations, or where, over relatively short distances, road users are guided along the detour and back to the desired highway without route signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A Street Name sign should be placed above, or the street name should be incorporated into, a DETOUR (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9.svg">M4-9</a>) sign to indicate the name of the street being detoured.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The END DETOUR (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-8a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-8a.svg">M4-8a</a>) sign or the END (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-8bp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-8bP.svg">M4-8bP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be used to indicate that the detour has ended.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> When the END DETOUR sign is used on a numbered highway, the sign should be mounted above a route sign after the downstream end of the detour.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The Pedestrian/Bicyclist Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9a.svg">M4-9a</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) should be used where a pedestrian/bicyclist detour route has been established because of the closing of a pedestrian/bicycle facility to through traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> If used, the Pedestrian/Bicyclist Detour sign shall have an arrow pointing in the appropriate direction.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The arrow on a Pedestrian/Bicyclist Detour sign may be on the sign face or on a supplemental plaque.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The Pedestrian Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9b.svg">M4-9b</a>) sign or Bicyclist Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9c/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9c.svg">M4-9c</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be used where a pedestrian or a bicyclist detour route (not both) has been established because of the closing of the pedestrian or bicycle facility to through traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> <span class="ca">The DETOUR (M4-8) sign should be placed on tangent sections at intervals not to exceed 0.25 miles and at major intersections. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> <span class="ca">In urban areas, the M4-8 signs may be placed at every intersection. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> <span class="ca">The DETOUR with Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc3ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc3ca.webp">SC3(CA)</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1ca">Figure 6I-1(CA)</a>) should be used for unnumbered highways, for emergency </span> <span class="ca">situations, for periods of short duration, or where, over relatively short distances, road users are guided along the detour and back to </span> <span class="ca">the desired highway without route markers. </span></p>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> <span class="ca">The (FWY) DETOUR with Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/sc9ca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/sc9ca.webp">SC9(CA)</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1ca">Figure 6I-1(CA)</a>) should be used to inform motorists of the direction to </span> <span class="ca">follow for a detour. The words FWY DETOUR may be substituted for DETOUR, where applicable. </span></p>
</div>

## 6I.03 EXIT CLOSED Panel

<div class="secmeta">Section 6I.03 &middot; manual page 1284 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6I.03/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> When an exit ramp is closed, an EXIT CLOSED sign panel with a black legend and border on an orange background should be placed diagonally across the interchange/intersection guide signs.</p>
</div>

## 6I.101(CA) Construction Project Funding Identification Signs

<div class="secmeta">Section 6I.101(CA) &middot; manual page 1284 &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/6I.101(CA)/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> <span class="ca">For use on projects with estimated contract costs of $1,000,000 or more and 60 working days or more, or 70 working days </span> <span class="ca">minimum when Saturdays or holidays are counted as working days, the Construction Funding Identification signs may be used to </span> <span class="ca">identify funding sources for a highway project. Formats of the sign series are flexible to include federal, state and/or local agency </span> <span class="ca">funding sources. See <a href="#fig-6i-101ca">Figure 6I-101(CA)</a>.</span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> <span class="ca">If used, header panel shall include type of project and anticipated year of completion, according to established contract </span> <span class="ca">completion schedule, limited to one line each with a minimum letter size based on sign panel size and the “Standard </span> <span class="ca">Highway Signs and Markings” book (refer to <a href="#">Section 1A.11</a>), designed to fit within fluorescent orange portion for Building </span> <span class="ca">California (C48B(CA)), Federal-Aid Highway Funds - Building CA (C50(CA) Series), and Local Funds Only (C51(CA)) signs, </span> <span class="ca">or shall include legend “Your Tax Dollars AT WORK” for Clean California (C49(CA) Series) signs. Installation shall be </span> <span class="ca">placed in advance of temporary traffic control zone signs and limited to one sign installed in each direction on up to two </span> <span class="ca">approaches. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <span class="ca">Information on the sign should include type of project, such as Highway Construction, Highway Repair, Highway Improvement, </span> <span class="ca">Bridge Construction, Bridge Repair, or Roadside Work for C48B(CA) and C51(CA) signs. </span> <span class="ca">Building California (C48B(CA)) Sign </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <span class="ca">The C48B(CA) sign may be used on projects funded by State Highway Funds (Building California Funds), formerly known as </span> <span class="ca">Senate Bill 1. Format of this sign is flexible to include up to four funding agency pictographs. See <a href="#fig-6i-101ca">Figure 6I-101(CA)</a>. </span> <span class="ca">Clean California (C49A(CA) or C49D(CA)) Signs </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <span class="ca">The C49(CA) Series signs may be used on projects funded by State General Funds (Clean California Funds), projects to </span> <span class="ca">beautify and revitalize public spaces across the State. Format of these signs is flexible to include up to four funding agency </span> <span class="ca">pictographs. See <a href="#fig-6i-101ca">Figure 6I-101(CA)</a>. </span> <span class="ca">Federal-Aid Highway Funds - Building CA (C50C(CA) or C50D(CA)) Signs </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <span class="ca">The C50(CA) Series signs may be used to identify funding source for a project using Federal-Aid Highway Program funds. </span> <span class="ca">Format of these signs is flexible to include up to three funding agency pictographs. See <a href="#fig-6i-101ca">Figure 6I-101(CA)</a>. </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <span class="ca">The project description used on the C50(CA) Series signs shall be limited to the following project categories: </span> <span class="ca">1.</span><span class="ca">New Pavement </span> <span class="ca">2.</span><span class="ca">New Carpool Lane </span> <span class="ca">3.</span><span class="ca">Bridge Maintenance </span> <span class="ca">4.</span><span class="ca">Bridge Restoration </span> <span class="ca">5.</span><span class="ca">New Biking/Walking Paths </span> <span class="ca">6.</span><span class="ca">Broadband installation </span> <span class="ca">7.</span><span class="ca">Improved Drainage System </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <span class="ca">If the project scope does not fit any of the project categories, an alternated project category should be considered, such as </span> <span class="ca">Highway Construction, Highway Repair, Highway Improvement, Bridge Construction, or Roadside Work. </span> <span class="ca">Local Funds Only (C51(CA)) Sign </span></p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> <span class="ca">The C51(CA) sign may be used on projects funded by local funds only (city, county, or other funding agencies). Format of this </span> <span class="ca">sign is flexible to include up to four funding agency pictographs. See <a href="#fig-6i-101ca">Figure 6I-101(CA)</a>. </span></p>
</div>
