The City of Gardena Public Works Engineering Division maintains the **ST-Series Street & Traffic Standard Plans (`ST-00` through `ST-19`)**. Notable traffic engineering sheets include **inductive detector loop details (`ST-16`)**, a dedicated **Traffic Loop No-Excavation Zone (`ST-17`)**, **Traffic Control Device placement at curb returns (`ST-13`)**, and the updated **2026 Curb Ramp standard (`ST-14`)**.

:::tip[Governing Hierarchy & Local Practice]
**Governing hierarchy & key local rules:**
- **Signalized intersections (`ST-13`, `ST-16`, `ST-17`):** Utility excavations are strictly restricted inside the `ST-17` loop no-excavation zone; damaged detector loops must be replaced per `ST-16` and tested prior to final acceptance.
- **Trench plating (`ST-18`):** All steel plates in active travel lanes must be pinned/recessed with non-skid surfacing and cold-mix ramping.
- **Supplement:** For details not listed in the `ST` series, Gardena directs engineers to **SPPWC (Greenbook)** and **Caltrans / CA MUTCD**.
:::
