| Street Element | NACTO Urban Street Guidance | Governing California / SoCal Standard on This Site |
|---|---|---|
| **Urban travel lane width** | **10 ft** standard; **11 ft** on designated transit / heavy freight routes | **10.5–12 ft** on State highways ([Caltrans DIB 94 Table 5.3](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-94/)); **10–11 ft** local ([Lane Width Comparison](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/lane-widths/)) |
| **Corner curb radius** | **10–15 ft** actual radius in urban settings (effective radius larger with parking/bike lane) to slow turning speeds to ≤ 15 mph | [LADOT Complete Streets](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/complete-streets/) &middot; [Culver City CSDS](/SoCal-Super-Standards/culver-city/standard-plans/) |
| **Curb extensions / bulb-outs** | Extend **6 ft** into an 8 ft parking lane (1–2 ft shy from travel lane); shorten crossings &amp; enforce daylighting | [FHWA STEP Guide](/SoCal-Super-Standards/fhwa/step-uncontrolled-crossings/) &middot; [LADOT S-523.0](/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/ladot/mpp-531-s-523-0/) |
| **Sidewalk zones** | Frontage + **5–7 ft** clear pedestrian through-zone (8–12 ft downtown) + furnishing/buffer + curb | **48 in.** PROWAG min; **6–12 ft** local urban ([PROWAG 2023](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/prowag-2023/) &middot; [DIB 82-06](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-82-06/)) |
| **Leading Pedestrian Interval (LPI)** | **3–7 sec** head start for pedestrians before concurrent green | [LADOT LPI Policy (2021)](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/ladot-lpi-2021/) &middot; [CA MUTCD 4I.06](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4i/) |
| **Speed humps / tables / cushions** | 3–3.5 in. height; 12–14 ft hump or 22 ft flat-top speed table (10 ft plateau) for 15–25 mph target speeds | [Burbank BT-414 / BT-417 / BT-418](/SoCal-Super-Standards/burbank/standard-plans/) &middot; [Speed &amp; Calming Topic](/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/speed-and-calming/) |
