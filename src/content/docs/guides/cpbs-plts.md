## PLTS 1–4 Framework at a Glance

| PLTS Level | Pedestrian Experience | Primary Segment &amp; Crossing Drivers | Governing Standards to Improve Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PLTS 1** | Comfortable for virtually all pedestrians (including children &amp; older adults) | Wide, good-condition sidewalk (≥ 6 ft) with wide buffer/trees; low speed (≤ 25 mph); narrow/signalized or refuge-protected crossings | [PROWAG 2023](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/prowag-2023/) &middot; [Caltrans DIB 94](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-94/) |
| **PLTS 2** | Comfortable for most adults; modest traffic exposure | Continuous ≥ 5 ft sidewalk with modest buffer (or on-street parking); 25–30 mph; marked crossings with visibility/curb extensions | [FHWA STEP Guide](/SoCal-Super-Standards/fhwa/step-uncontrolled-crossings/) &middot; [Crosswalks Topic](/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/crosswalks/) |
| **PLTS 3** | Uncomfortable for many; tolerable for confident adult walkers | Narrow/poor sidewalk or minimal buffer on 35 mph multi-lane street; long uncontrolled crossings without refuge or RRFB | [Caltrans DIB 82-06](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-82-06/) &middot; [CA MUTCD Ch. 3C](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3c/) |
| **PLTS 4** | High stress; missing sidewalk or high-speed multi-lane unprotected crossing | Missing/impassable sidewalk, ≥ 40–45 mph adjacent traffic with no buffer, or wide multi-lane uncontrolled crossings | [Caltrans 2026 A88A/B](/SoCal-Super-Standards/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/) &middot; [LADOT Marked Crosswalks](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/marked-crosswalks/) |
