<div class="srcbar">July 2025, Center for Pedestrian and Bicyclist Safety (Schneider et al.) &middot; 66 pages &middot; outline only</div>

A research report that applies and validates a pedestrian level of traffic stress (PLTS) method for rating how stressful streets and crossings feel to walk along and across. Appendix A has the PLTS criteria. Use it for network analysis and prioritization, not as a design standard.
