<div class="srcbar">Third edition (Island Press) &middot; 427 pages &middot; outline only</div>

NACTO's national guide to designing bikeways and bike intersections for all ages and abilities. It covers network planning, bike lane and protected bikeway types, intersection treatments and maintenance. It is guidance, not a standard: in California, CA MUTCD 2026 and Caltrans and local standards govern devices and markings.

Each heading links to the topic pages where the matching CA MUTCD and local standards are.</div>
