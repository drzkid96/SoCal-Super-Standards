|---|---|---|
| **All Ages &amp; Abilities (AAA) selection** | Protected bike lane when speed &gt; 25 mph, volume &gt; 3,000 ADT, or &gt; 1 lane/direction; bike boulevard on ≤ 20–25 mph / ≤ 1,500–3,000 ADT local streets | [Caltrans DIB 94 Fig. 5-A](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-94/) &middot; [Bikeway Design Comparison](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/bikeway-design-comparison/) |
| **One-way protected bike lane width** | **6.5 ft** desired (5 ft min constrained; wider for high volume / passing) | **7 ft** preferred / **5 ft** min ([Caltrans DIB 89-02 § 3.4](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-89-02/)) |
| **Two-way protected bike lane width** | **10–12 ft** desired (8 ft min constrained) | **10–12 ft** ([Caltrans HDM 1003.1](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-hdm-ch1000/) / [DIB 94 § 5.1](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-94/)) |
| **Horizontal separation / buffer** | **3 ft** min next to parking (door zone); physical vertical separation (curb, planter, precast curb, bollards) | **3 ft** preferred / **2 ft** min; **3 ft** min w/ parking; **5 ft** at accessible parking ([DIB 89-02 § 3.3](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-89-02/)) |
| **Protected intersection geometry** | Corner refuge island (10–15 ft radius), 6–20 ft bikeway setback for 1-car queuing &amp; clear sight angle, forward bike queue box | [DIB 89-02 Fig. 1](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-89-02/) &middot; [CA MUTCD Ch. 9E](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9e/) |
| **Transit boarding island** | Route bikeway behind 6–8 ft wide boarding island (min 5×8 ft ADA landing) with pedestrian yield crossing | [DIB 89-02 Fig. 2](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-89-02/) &middot; [LADOT S-523.0](/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/ladot/mpp-531-s-523-0/) |
