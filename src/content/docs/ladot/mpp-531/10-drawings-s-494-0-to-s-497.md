<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Restricted Sight Distance Criteria for Protected Left-Turn Phasing.</b> Sets the sight distance criteria that justify protected left-turn phasing at signalized intersections, supplementing Table 1 of Section 521. An acceptable gap is (5.0 + 0.5n) seconds, where n is the number of opposing through lanes, multiplied by a design speed of 5 mi/hr above the posted or advisory speed to get the minimum sight distance in Table A. Figures 1 to 6 show restrictions from horizontal curves, vertical alignment, physical obstructions, and negatively offset left-turn lanes with light or moderate opposing left-turn demand, with or without a right horizontal curve.</p><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Figure 5: Example of Frequently Restricted Sight Distance Due To Negatively Offset Left Turn Lanes with Moderate Opposing Left Turn Demand</p>
<p>This figure is for general use on arterial highways or high volume collector streets with negatively offset left turn lanes at higher volume cross streets (generally an arterial or high volume collector street over 10,000 ADT). In these situations, the negative offset, in combination with moderate opposing left turn demand, may result in frequently restricted sight distance for permissive left turns.</p>
<p>The determination of frequently restricted sight distance can be made by 1) assessing the frequency of restricted sight distance and 2) measuring the sight distance. Restricted sight distance will be assumed to occur frequently with moderate opposing left-turn demand if the cross street has more than 10,000 ADT. However, a left turn study may be conducted in place of this guideline to document the actual frequency of restricted sight distance. If the study shows that more than 15% of queues do not clear during their cycles of arrival during the peak hour, then restricted visibility is judged to be frequent.</p>
<p>The sight distance can be plotted on a striping plan as shown in this figure or as measured in the field. If the sight distance is frequently restricted and is less than the values shown in Table A, then remedial action should be taken.</p>
<p>Remedial action should first consider removal of the raised median island and restriping to eliminate the negative offset. If this is not feasible, then protected left turn phasing is required.</p>

<p>X, ft: 1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5 / 3.0</p></details></div>
