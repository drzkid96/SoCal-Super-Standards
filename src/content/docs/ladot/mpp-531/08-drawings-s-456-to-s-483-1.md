<p>1. IMPLEMENTATION This standard plan applies to new installations and reinstallations of marked crosswalks and to marked crosswalks determined to be retained as a result of a study on multi-lane facilities. Use of signs shall be in coordination with all other controls shown on this plan. 2. SIGN POSTING AT CROSSWALK Crosswalk Sign Assembly signs shall be posted and should be positioned within ten feet of the crosswalk. These signs generally shall be posted back-to-back so that one sign is on the right and the other on the left. If a raised median with a width 6' or greater exists, the left sign shall be in the median. These signs should be 36" X 36". The W16-9p or W16-7p plates shall be 36" wide. All signs shall be fluorescent yellow-green. 3. ADVANCE SIGN POSTING Advance Pedestrian Crossing <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-15.svg">W11-15</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W11-2.svg">W11-2</a> signs, or Assembly D (CA) signs shall be posted as shown and shall be fluorescent yellow-green. <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-5.svg">R1-5</a> signs shall be posted and positioned within ten feet of the yield limit line. These locations may be adjusted to accommodate field conditions. All signs should be 36" X 36". 4. PAVEMENT MARKINGS "PED XING" or "SCHOOL XING" pavement markings shall be installed as shown except the yellow "SLOW SCHOOL XING" pavement markings shall be installed in advance of uncontrolled yellow school crosswalks. If the crosswalk spacing is less than 500 feet, only one set of pavement markings is required in advance of the first crosswalk. 5. MIDBLOCK CROSSWALKS This standard plan also applies at uncontrolled approaches to midblock crosswalks.</p>
<p>6. RAISED PAVEMENT MARKINGS (RPM) Install RPMs one each side of crosswalk bar facing approach lanes.
