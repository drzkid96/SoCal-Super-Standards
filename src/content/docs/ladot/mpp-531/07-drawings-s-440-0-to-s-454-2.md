### Page 92 - Street Name Signs

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-438.0</strong> &middot; Topics: signals, parking-curb, signs</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0092.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0092.webp" alt="MPP 531 page 92 S-438.0 Street Name Signs"></a><figcaption>MPP 531, page 92.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>1. Signs shall have Diamond Grade or Type VIII retro-reflective white lettering and numbers on a standard blue Type IV retro-reflective background with match component UV/anti-graffiti film. For Type A signs, all colors shall be provided in the official 4&quot; diameter City Seal. Signs shall be provided with a 10-year replacement warranty against wear, delamination or fading. An LADOT-approved vendor shall be used for fabrication.</p>
<p>2. The sign blade material shall be 1/8 inch thick aluminum stock.</p>
<p>3. Letters and numbers shall be Highway Series B, with at least one stroke width between letters (45% spacing). For Type A signs, the initial upper case letters shall be 6&quot; and the lower case letters shall be 4.5&quot;, except as discussed in Note 9. For Type B signs, the initial upper case letters shall be 4&quot; and the lower case letters shall be 3&quot;. Intermediate (non-first) words such as &quot;of&quot;, &quot;the&quot;, &quot;el&quot;, &quot;la&quot;, &quot;los&quot;, &quot;las&quot;, &quot;de&quot; and &quot;del&quot; shall have all lower case letters. The block number and upper case letter representing the cardinal address direction, if any, shall be 3&quot;. The space between words shall be same width as an upper case letter &quot;O&quot;. The legend shall appear on both sides of the aluminum blade. The street name text shall be left justified starting a minimum of 2&quot; from left edge of sign. The legend may be centered.</p>
<p>4. Numbered streets shall be indicated by Arabic numerals and not letters. Where the number appears before the title (such as 3 rd Street or 4 th Avenue) the number shall be followed by an ordinal abbreviation, &quot;st&quot;, &quot;nd&quot;, &quot;rd&quot; or &quot;th&quot;, at the same level, with a space between them. However, where the number appears after the title (such as Avenue 19), an ordinal abbreviation shall not be used. The space shall be the same width as an uppercase letter &quot;O&quot;.</p>
<p>5. The block number shall be followed by an upper case letter representing the cardinal direction, only where the named street has address numbers in another direction (such as for Sepulveda Boulevard, Vermont Avenue, Olympic Boulevard, 3rd Street, etc.) within the City of Los Angeles or an adjacent city. The space between the block number and the upper case letter representing the cardinal direction shall be the same width as upper case &quot;O&quot;.</p>
<p>6. Where the named street is a private street or driveway, the block number shall be replaced by &quot;PVT&quot; in 3&quot; upper case letters.</p>
<p>7. Apostrophes shall be used, where appropriate. Accent and tilde markings shall be provided for non-English words and names, where appropriate. A lower case &quot;i&quot; or &quot;j&quot; shall be dotted.</p>
<p>8. The Bureau of Engineering is the source for street names. If a private street or driveway requires a name, due to signalization or some other reason, the Bureau of Engineering must process the naming proposal.</p>
<p>9. The following street name abbreviations shall be used, except where using the unabbreviated word would not require an increase in sign length (such as &quot;B Street&quot;). Periods shall not be used after abbreviations.</p>

### Page 93 - Street Name Signs

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-438.0</strong>, <strong>S-221.3</strong> &middot; Topics: parking-curb, signs, intersections</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0093.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0093.webp" alt="MPP 531 page 93 S-438.0 Street Name Signs"></a><figcaption>MPP 531, page 93.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>9. (continued) In addition, cardinal direction names may be abbreviated to &quot;N&quot;, &quot;S&quot;, &quot;E&quot; or &quot;W&quot;, only where the unabbreviated word would require the sign length to exceed 60&quot;. For example, use &quot;Huntington Dr S&quot; (abbreviated) and &quot;Cahuenga Bl East&quot; (unabbreviated).</p>
<p>10. The blade length should be determined using the table below. (Table as listed in the description.)</p>
<p>* Longer street names for Type A signs shall use two-line blades. The width of the two-line blade shall be based on the text of the longer line. The maximum length of a two-line blade shall be 48 inches. Where the text of the longer line of a two-line blade exceeds 14 characters, 4&quot; upper case and 3&quot; lower case letters may be used.</p>
<p>11. Type A and Type B signs that are 30&quot; or 36&quot; long shall be side-mounted only, as shown on sheets 6 and 7. Type A and Type B signs that are 42&quot; and longer shall be side-mounted with vertical support, as shown on sheets 8 and 9. Minor deviations in the sign brackets shown are permissible only with the prior written approval of LADOT.</p>
<p>12. When provided for initial installation, replacement or maintenance, Type A signs shall be used at intersections when at least one approach is a designated Major, Secondary or Collector Street. When provided for initial installation, replacement or maintenance, Type B signs shall be used at intersections where all approaches are designated Local Streets.</p>
<p>13. Street name signs shall be provided on the near-right side of each leg of a primary street at the named crossing street, flagged in the direction of the sidewalk or parkway, unless it is physically infeasible. If so, an alternate location shall be provided. If the named cross street does not intersect the primary street, due to a separation island, an additional near-right sign shall be provided on the island. For unusual situations, see S-221.3.</p></details>

### Page 98 - On-Street Angle Parking Stalls (Sheet 1 of 2)

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-440.0</strong> &middot; Topics: parking-curb, striping</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0098.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0098.webp" alt="MPP 531 page 98 S-440.0 On-Street Angle Parking Stalls"></a><figcaption>MPP 531, page 98.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>Notes</p>
<p>1. All parking stall lines and edge lines shall be in alkyd-based thermoplastic.</p>
<p>2. Calculations based on, but not limited to Los Angeles Municipal Code (L.A.M.C.), Section 12.21, utilizing 9&#x27;x16&#x27; stall size. Large vehicles requiring the 18-foot dimension are expected to overhang the curb, as shown. Stall design above requires 2-foot overhang for 18-foot vehicle.</p>
<p>3. On local streets, collector streets, and those arterial streets which function as collector streets (see 4, below), the &quot;E&quot; dimension shall be 4&#x27; minimum where the parking angle is greater than 45 degrees. On those local and collector streets with angle parking 45 degrees or less, the &quot;E&quot; dimension may be reduced to a minimum of 2 feet. The 4&#x27; dimension is preferred, however, and should be provided if the roadway width permits.</p>
<p>4. On arterial highways, the &quot;E&quot; dimensions shown in the above table shall be considered required minimums. This is intended to provide for maneuvers to and from the parking stall without obstructing traffic movement in the adjacent traveled lane. Arterial streets are designated in community plans. A designated arterial street may, for the purposes of this standard plan be considered as a collector street if the daily volume is less than 10,000 vehicles, or the striping provides for only one through lane in each direction.</p></details>
