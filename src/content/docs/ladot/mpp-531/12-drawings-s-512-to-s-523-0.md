### Page 171 - Council District Field Office Sign

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-520.0</strong> &middot; Topics: signals, parking-curb, signs</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0171.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0171.webp" alt="MPP 531 page 171 S-520.0 Council District Field Office Signs"></a><figcaption>MPP 531, page 171.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>Sign: Size shall be 36&quot; vertically and 60&quot; horizontally. Background color shall be blue and reflectorized. Letters and border are white. Anti graffiti film shall be used. Material shall be 0.08 inch minimum thickness aluminum stock. Corners of sign shall be 2 inch radius.</p>
<p>Installation: Signs shall be installed on two heavy duty sign posts. Signs may not be installed on traffic signal poles, or on street lighting poles. Signs should be installed at a clearance height of 10 feet so they will be less prone to vandalism.</p>
<p>Authority: District Office is responsible for determining the exact name and spelling on the sign. Fabrication and installation work orders should be initiated by District Office. Field Operations should install the signs on or before the effective date the new Councilmember assumes office.</p>
<p>Drawing callouts: 1/4&quot; border 1/2&quot; from edge. 12&quot; City Seal. 2&quot; radius.</p></details>
