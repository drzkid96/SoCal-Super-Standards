### Page 37 - Typical Major Highway Striping Treatments: Major Highways (Prior to 11-10-99), 100&#x27; R/W, 80&#x27; Roadway

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-401.0</strong> &middot; Topics: bikeways, signals, parking-curb, striping, intersections</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0037.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0037.webp" alt="MPP 531 page 37 S-401.0 Typical Major Highway Striping Treatments"></a><figcaption>MPP 531, page 37.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Selection of length of left turn pocket lane depends on demand, spacing between intersections and location of critical driveways to be served by two-way left turn lanes (2WLTL). The desirable length at signalized intersections is at least 40&#x27; of storage per vehicle per signal cycle averaged during the peak hour. However, this desirable length often may not be feasible. Minimum lengths at signalized intersections on highways with continuous median channelization are: 100&#x27; at major highways. 100&#x27; at secondary highways. 60&#x27; at collector streets. 40&#x27; at local streets. Minimum pocket lengths for major highways without continuous median channelization may be less than the above, depending on turning volumes and parking impacts.</p>
<p>2. In general, 10&#x27; wide left turn channelization should be used. However a 12-foot median should be used if large vehicles are a significant portion of turning vehicles.</p>
<p>3. Utilize 60&#x27; reversals for posted speed limits of 35 MPH and less, and 80&#x27; reversals for posted speed limits of 40 MPH and greater. A 40&#x27; minimum reversal may be used in exceptional situations for critical driveways.</p>
<p>4. Selection of an open-ended (as shown in first drawing) versus closed ended (as shown in second drawing) 2WLTL is dependent, in part, on the proximity of a critical driveway to be served by a 2WLTL. Minimum distances to critical driveways are shown.</p>
<p>5. The painted reversal for a single left turn lane may be eliminated at a signalized intersection on a selective basis if spillover is recurrent and if it is not feasible to lengthen the left-turn pocket.</p>
<p>6. For new raised median installations, the width of the island should be limited to 8&#x27; to allow wider adjacent through lanes.</p>
<p>7. The 4&#x27; wide island adjacent to the left turn lane should be removed, where feasible, so as to avoid left turn lane offsets which reduce left turn driver visibility of approaching through vehicles. Where an island adjacent to the left-turn lane is proposed, or is to be retained, protected left-turn phasing shall be considered. See Standard Plan S-497.0.</p>
<p>8. Third through lanes should be installed when conditions so justify. Right turn only lanes may be installed instead of the third through lane, as appropriate.</p>
<p>9. Bike lanes with all day parking may be installed in place of a curb lane or right turn lane where the width is 13&#x27; or greater (or 12&#x27; in selected cases).</p></details>

### Page 47 - On-Street Motorcycle Parking Stalls

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-404.2</strong> &middot; Topics: parking-curb</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0047.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/mpp-531/p0047.webp" alt="MPP 531 page 47 S-404.2 On-Street Motorcycle Parking Stalls"></a><figcaption>MPP 531, page 47.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>A. Installation notes:</h5>
<p>1. Parking meters may be replaced with a Pay Station Kiosk, and space numbering at the curb of each stall.</p>
<p>2. All parking stall lines and edge lines shall be in Alkyd-based Thermoplastic in accordance with the current LADOT specifications.</p>
<p>3. &quot;MOTORCYCLE&quot; pavement messages shall be in paint in accordance with the current LADOT specifications.</p>
<p>4. Motorcycle stalls should be installed at the beginning and/or at the end of the block, adjacent to the required intersection red curb.</p>
<p>5. Motorcycle stalls shall not be considered on severe slope. Angled stalls are preferred for ease of access, unless noted otherwise per engineering recommendation. Existing road condition and other factors will determine which style (angled vs. perpendicular) stall will be installed.</p>
<p>6. (K) = K71 bollards or candlestick delineators (optional) may be installed at locations not affected by street cleaning operations. Space at equal distance on the center within the box from the curb to the 4&quot; white lines.</p>
<p>7. There shall be at least one set of motorcycle stalls and no more than three sets connected in parallel at each installation. Each set consists of two motorcycle stalls.</p>
<p>8. Motorcycle stall marking may also be used in non-metered spaces. Notes #2 through #6 above apply.</p>
<p>9. Two meter heads shall be installed on a yoke on one single meter post for each set of metered motorcycle stalls.</p>
<p>B. Motorcycle parking stalls (MPS): (See Note A-5)</p>
<p>1. Parking meter heads, posts and signs shall be removed or adjusted as needed if MPS are installed in a parking meter zone.</p>
<p>2. Rubberized wheel-stops may be used in between the MPS zones and an on-street parking space to supplement bollards and/or delineators.</p>
<p>3. Minimum clearance from the top of a driveway to a MPS zone shall be 6&#x27;.</p>
<p>4. Minimum clearance from a fire hydrant to a MPS zone shall be 15&#x27;.</p>
<p>5. Minimum clearance from a parallel or perpendicular vehicle parking space shall be at least 5&#x27; as measured at the curb and at least 10&#x27; minimum clearance from an angle vehicle parking space.</p>
<p>6. MPS zones shall not be considered adjacent to any commercial loading zone or any bus zones.</p>
<p>7. MPS zones shall not be installed within any minimum required intersection red curb.</p>
<p>8. Signs similar to <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r32fca/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/ca/r32fca.webp">R32F(CA)</a> with arrow, in the MUTCD (CA) can be installed in lieu at the beginning and at the end of the MPS zone.</p>
<p>C. Miscellaneous</p>
<p>1. All pavement markings shall be skid-resistant.</p></details>
