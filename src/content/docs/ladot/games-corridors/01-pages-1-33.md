<p>LADOT 2028 Games Design Guide | 2</p>
<h4>1. Introduction</h4>
<p>The purpose of this document is to establish consensus on the design of some of the more common elements to the LADOT 2028 Games Active Transportation Projects before design begins to ensure a more efficient design development and review process. The Design Guide has been organized into sections and includes sources for each design decision. It includes:</p>
<p> Corridor design elements, including the design speed, design vehicles, turn template approach, and minimum lane widths. It also covers bikeway buffer design and standard roadway striping elements such as crosswalks and limit lines, shift tapers and turn pockets, and parking design.  Bikeway Intersection Treatments such as sight distance treatments, traffic calming at intersections and driveways, and intersection safety treatments including bike signals, turn prohibitions, protected phasing, etc.  Bus-Bike Interface Treatments, such as bus boarding islands, shared bus/bike boarding, and bus pull outs.  Neighborhood Enhanced Network Elements, including gateway treatments, mini roundabouts, diverters, sharrows, and speed humps. This document is not intended to be comprehensive nor is it intended to replace engineering judgement in unique situations. The Design Guide will be updated as new common design treatments are identified.</p></div>
