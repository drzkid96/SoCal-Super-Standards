<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Operation and Design of Signalized Jogged Intersections.</b> Guidance for signalizing jogged (offset) intersections, with a selection procedure and plan examples for left and right jogs. The designer determines the jog direction, the internal storage distance D (interior limit lines are required if D is more than 40 ft, otherwise the jog operates as a single intersection), the angles theta 1 and theta 2 (opposed phasing for the minor street if either exceeds 20 degrees with D under 40 ft), and the distances X1 and X2 (internal mastarms if both exceed 50 ft, external if under 50 ft). Plan sheets show signal head, APS, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> and phasing layouts for each case, including KEEP CLEAR markings where D is 20 ft or more.</p></div>
