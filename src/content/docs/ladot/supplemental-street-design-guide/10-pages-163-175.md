<p>5. If the pedestrian median opening is greater than 20 feet wide, then a bollard may be considered to prevent vehicles from encroaching into the pedestrian refuge space. This can be accomplished by marking the corner radius with a white edge line and locating the bollards six inches behind the edge line.
