<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Transit Priority Unit Cabinet Details.</b> Terminal panel and C20 connector of the transit priority unit, drawing S-70.3A, sheet 6 of 6. A 5.0 inch by 7.0 inch white aluminum panel carries barrier strips CTB-4 and CTB-5 and a 6 foot C20 harness runs to the C20S port of the 2070 controller. Tables list C20 connector pins A through N, and two notes cover mounting holes and soldering.</p><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Terminal panel: A 0.125 (1/8&quot;) white aluminum panel, 5.0&quot; wide by 7.0&quot; high with 1/2&quot; radius corners. The four audio terminals are labeled TO CTB-1. CTB-5 is an 8-position single barrier strip with positions +5V, -5V, CD, RTS, TX, RX, CTS and GND. Twisted wire pairs connect CTB-4 to the harness cable that leaves the panel at the lower right, and CTB-5 positions fan into the same cable.</p>

### Page 130 - Pedestrian Barricade

<div class="pmeta">Signs: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> &middot; Topics: crosswalks, parking-curb, signs</div>

<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Pedestrian Barricade.</b> Pedestrian barricade, drawing S-454.2, approved June 26, 2008. A galvanized 1-1/2 inch nominal pipe railing 4 feet 6 inches high with 1 foot radius corners carries signs <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> and R9-3b, and posts are 6 feet apart and anchored with a 5 inch square, 1/2 inch base plate and four 1/2 inch bolts.</p><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Elevation of barricade: A pipe railing with two vertical posts and three horizontal 1-1/2&quot; nominal pipe rails, bent with 1&#x27;-0&quot; R corners at the top. The overall height above the top of sidewalk is 4&#x27;-6&quot;. The rails are spaced 1&#x27;-3&quot; below the top rail and another 1&#x27;-3&quot; below that. The posts are 6&#x27;-0&quot; apart (Typical). Signs <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> &amp; R9-3b, 18&quot; x 24&quot; (a no pedestrian symbol in a circle with slash, above the words USE CROSSWALK and an arrow) are mounted between the upper rails. Callouts: 1-1/2&quot; Nominal pipe size galvanized Standard pipe, See Post Anchorage Detail, Top of sidewalk.</p>
<p>Post Anchorage Detail, elevation: A pipe post set in a threaded pipe collar on a 1/2&quot; base plate fastened with 1/2&quot; x 2-1/2&quot; bolts (4) to a PCC sidewalk.</p>
<p>Post Anchorage Detail, plan: A 5&quot; square base plate on a 5&quot; bolt circle with 9/16&quot; dia. holes (4) at the corners and 1/2&quot; dia. expansion anchors (4).</p>
<p>Title block: Pedestrian Barricade, City of Los Angeles Department of Transportation, Drawing No. S-454.2. Drawn MT 05-03-06, Pr. T. E. SS 06-26-08, approved June 26, 2008, Rita L. Robinson, General Manager. Printed page number 123.</p></details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>1. Pipe post to be set 1&#x27;-6&quot; back from face of curb unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. For minimum pipe diameters and wall thickness refer to ASTM A6M.</p>
<p>3. Use left, right or double arrow on sign as needed.</p></details></div>
