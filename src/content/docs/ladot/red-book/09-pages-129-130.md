### Page 130 - Pedestrian Barricade

<div class="pmeta">Signs: <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> &middot; Topics: crosswalks, parking-curb, signs</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0130.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0130.webp" alt="Red Book page 130 "></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 130 (scanned, cleaned and sharpened). Click to enlarge.</figcaption></figure>
<div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Pedestrian Barricade.</b> Elevation and post anchorage detail for a pedestrian barricade at a crosswalk, using a 1 1/2 inch nominal pipe size galvanized standard pipe post with an <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> or R9-3b sign (18 in x 24 in). The post is set in PCC sidewalk on a base plate with four 5/8 inch expansion anchors, and is set 1 ft 6 in back from the face of the curb unless otherwise specified. Left, right or double arrow signs are used as needed.</p><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Elevation</p>
<p><a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-3a.svg">R9-3a</a> and R9-3b, 18&quot; x 24&quot; sign, with left, right or double arrow.</p>
<p>1 1/2&quot; nominal pipe size galvanized standard pipe post.</p>
<p>Threaded pipe collar on base plate.</p>
<p>Top of sidewalk, PCC sidewalk.</p>
<p>4 bolts, 5/8&quot; x 2 1/2&quot; long, with 5/8&quot; dia. expansion anchors (4).</p>
<p>5&quot; bolt circle, 11/16&quot; dia. holes in base plate.</p>
<p>Post anchorage detail (see post anchorage detail for base plate and anchors).</p>
<p>Crosswalk shown beside the barricade.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Pipe post to be set 1&#x27;-6&quot; back from face of curb unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>2. For minimum pipe diameters and wall thickness refer to ASTM A6M.</p>
<p>3. Use left, right or double arrow on sign as needed.</p>
<p>Drawn 05-03-06. Approved June 26, 2008. Drawing title: Pedestrian Barricade, S-454.2 (printed sheet 123).</p></details></div>
