### Page 106 - Trailblazer Sign (Specifications, Sheet 2 of 2, S-58.16)

<div class="pmeta">Topics: signs</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0106.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0106.webp" alt="Red Book page 106 "></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 106.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. Trailblazer signs to be used in conjunction with static freeway overhead guide signs.</p>
<p>2. Use one or two arrows on the trailblazer sign as appropriate.</p>
<p>3. Sign shall utilize Light Emitting Diodes (LED) for message illumination. The individual LEDs shall equal or exceed in quality as those manufactured by Hewlett-Packard, high power ALInGAP amber type, catalog #HLMA-DL00.</p>
<p>4. Sign text shall be 10&quot; U.C. (upper case) with 1&quot; spacing between each letter and a minimum of one letter width spacing between words.</p>
<p>5. The overall sign size shall be 60&quot; in height x 60&quot; in width and 8&quot; in depth.</p>
<p>6. The weight of the sign (enclosure + all components) shall not exceed 115 lbs.</p>
<p>7. The sign shall be constructed so that the front face panel shall be contained within an extruded 0.90 aluminum frame having full welded seams and powder coat painted with high gloss black finish meeting the color standards of Fed. Spec. 595b, 17038.</p>
<p>8. The transparent Lexan front face panel shall be hinged at the top allowing front access to the interior of the sign. Support shall be provided to hold the Lexan panel open during maintenance.</p>
<p>9. A honeycomb formed black anodized screen shall be provided between the LED&#x27;s lamp clusters and the front face panel to enhance resistance to &quot;sun phantom&quot; effect.</p>
<p>10. The sign shall use a closed cell neoprene gasket making the sign watertight.</p>
<p>11. Sign enclosure hardware materials shall be stainless steel AISI303, for corrosion resistance.</p>
<p>12. An opening shall be made on the bottom side of the sign so that all conductors, enclosed in flexible conduit, may exit from the sign into the cantilever arm.</p>
<p>13. The sign shall be vented in the bottom portion on the opposite end from the conductor opening.</p>
<p>14. The LED elements shall be comprised of individual removable modules. Each LED module shall be composed of 1&quot; pixels (clusters) having 8 each LED&#x27;s per pixel. One module shall be used for each letter of the message.</p>
<p>15. Each pixel shall be removable or replaceable on the module without the use of any tools other than a screwdriver.</p>
<p>16. The sign shall be equipped with a photoelectric cell mounted on the enclosure to measure ambient light intensity and automatically compensate for ambient light conditions.</p>
<p>17. The LED sign shall be mountable using unistrut mounting system.</p>
<p>18. Sign shall be equipped with a terminal block to provide separate power inputs to each of the text and arrow modules. The sign shall be powered with 120V AC.</p>
<p>19. A full set of proposed sign specifications and drawings shall be supplied to the City prior to manufacturing.</p></details>

<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><h5>General notes:</h5>
<h5>Design:</h5>
<p>- Sign support structures designed in accordance with AASHTO Standard Specifications for Structural Support for Highway Signs, Luminaires and Traffic Signals: 5th edition.</p>
<p>- Sign weight used for sign support structure design is 3 psf.</p>
<p>- Basic wind speed (3 second gust) used for sign support structures design is 100 MPH, G = 1.14, I = 1.0 (50 year recurrence interval).</p>
<p>- Fatigue importance factor, I_f = 1.0, used for fatigue design of sign support structures considering galloping, natural wind gust and truck-induced gust.</p>
<p>- Pipe members shall conform to ASTM A53 GR. B.</p>
<p>- HSS tube members shall conform to ASTM A500 GR. B.</p>
<p>- All other structural steel shall conform to ASTM A36, or A992, unless noted otherwise.</p>
<p>- Welding shall be in conformance with the latest edition of AWS - D1.1 Structural Welding Code.</p>
<p>- Notch toughness of all structural steel members and plates greater than 1/2&quot; thick shall conform to Zone 2 requirements of AASHTO M270M/M270 supplementary requirement S5 (ASTM A709/A709M, supplementary requirement S83).</p>
<p>- High strength bolts shall conform to ASTM A325, Type 1.</p>
<p>- Nuts for high strength bolts shall be heavy hex and conform to ASTM A563 Grade DH, with supplementary requirements &quot;S1&quot; and &quot;S2&quot;.</p>
<p>- Hardened steel washers shall conform to ASTM F436, Type 1. Use washers under turning element in tightening unless otherwise specified.</p>
<p>- All fasteners shall be hot-dip galvanized.</p>
<p>- All structural steel shall be hot-dip galvanized after fabrication, unless noted otherwise.</p>
<p>- The silicon content of the base metal shall be in the ranges of 0.0% to 0.04% or 0.15% to 0.25% for all hot-dip galvanized steel, unless noted otherwise.</p>
<p>- All H.S. bolts shall be considered slip critical unless noted otherwise. Design slip resistance for bolts shall conform to the bolt specifications for Class C slip coefficient = 0.33.</p>
<p>- Use self locking nuts on non-high strength (H.S.) bolts, unless otherwise shown or specified.</p>
<p>- Foundation design is based on Broms&#x27; method assuming a cohesive soil with an undrained shear strength of 1.50 ksf with a factor of safety of 3.0.</p>
<p>- Pile lengths shall be increased by an amount equal to the thickness of any existing fill soils encountered at the sign location during construction.</p>
<p>- Bureau of Engineering&#x27;s Geotechnical Engineering Group (213-847-0538) shall be contacted for recommendations if weak soils and/or groundwater is encountered during construction of the CIDH (cast-in-drilled-hole) piles.</p>
<p>- All concrete shall be class 3600.</p>
<p>- All reinforcing steel shall conform to ASTM A706 or A615 GR. 60.</p>
<p>- Anchor rods shall conform to ASTM F1554, GR. 55, with supplementary requirement &quot;S4&quot; (AASHTO M314). Top ends of anchor rods shall be ground flat at 90 degrees to rod axis. Final surface roughness shall not exceed 125 micro-inch.</p>
<p>- Anchor rod washers shall conform to ASTM F436.</p>
<p>- Anchor rod nuts shall conform to ASTM A563 GR. DH with supplementary requirements &quot;S1&quot; and &quot;S2&quot;.</p>
<p>- Anchor rods shall be hot-dip galvanized full length.</p>

### Page 112 - Backplates (Standard Drawing S-77.8A)

<div class="pmeta">Topics: signals, accessibility</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0112.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0112.webp" alt="Red Book page 112 "></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 112.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Sheet notes</summary><p>1. The width dimension, &quot;A&quot;, should be 5&quot; typical (TYP.) and the corner radius dimension, &quot;R&quot;, should be 2&quot; TYP. The backplate shall have a nominal thickness of 1/8&quot;. All outside and inside edges shall be formed with a 1/2&quot; to 5/8&quot; flange (inside dimension), turned away from the front surface.</p>
<p>2. For all manufactured signal heads, backplates shall be designed so as to show no background light between the backplate and the head. The fabrication shall be vacuum formed as one piece with a black lusterless, haircell finish on the front side. All surfaces shall be flat and straight without blisters, buckling or warping. Flanges shall be straight, uniform and have a consistent flange dimension throughout.</p>
<p>3. The backplate material shall be compounded from virgin ABS containing 60% styrene, 20% rubber and 20% acrylic. It shall contain ultra violet inhibitors and stabilizers for protection against ultra violet degradation. The backplate must meet a falling dart impact test of 16 ft/lb.</p>
<p>4. The internal shapes and dimensions shall match various manufactured signal heads.</p>
<p>5. Retroreflective sheeting must be Type XI and comply with ASTM D4956. Retroreflective sheeting must have Class 1, 3, or 4 adhesive backing. The adhesive backing must be pressure sensitive and fungus resistant. Retroreflective sheeting must be applied at the fabrication plant under the retroreflective sheeting manufacturer&#x27;s instruction without appreciable stretching, tearing or other damage.</p>
<p>Callout: 2-inch yellow retroreflective strip.</p></details>
