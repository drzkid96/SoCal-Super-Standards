ITEM "A” IS THE BASIC MOUNTING DESIGN FOR MOST MAST ARM SIGNAL INSTALLATIONS WHEN STANDARD TENON LOCATION ON THE MAST ARM CANNOT BE UTILIZED. IT USES A 6” TENON LENGTH WITH 2 %” OUTSIDE DIAMETER FOR PLUMBIZER MOUNTING. ITEM "B” IS USED FOR INSTALLATION OF TRAFFIC SIGNAL HEADS, EXCEPT PROGRAMMED VISIBILITY (P.V.) HEADS, WHEN IT IS DESIRED TO MOUNT THEM HORIZONTALLY OR TO ADJUST THE HEIGHT VERTICALLY BY MOVEMENT OF ITEM "E”. ITEM "C” IS USED FOR INSTALLATION OF HORIZONTAL OR VERTICAL PROGRAMMED VISIBILITY (P.V.) HEADS. IT IS EQUIPPED WITH 14” ARMS WHICH ALLOW FOR REPLACEMENT OF SIGNAL LAMPS LOCATED IN THE REAR OF THE SIGNAL HEAD. ITEM "D” IS USED FOR INSTALLATION OF ELECTRIC SIGNS. ITEM "E” IS A STANDARD CLAMP KIT DESIGNED TO BE USED FOR FASTENING ITEMS ”B”, ”C”, AND ”D” TO MAST ARMS OR POLES IN A VERTICAL OR HORIZONTAL POSITION. THE BOTTOM ARMS IN ITEMS ”B”, °C”, AND ”D” ARE STATIONARY AND ARE HELD IN PLACE WITH PIPE THREADS AND SET SCREWS. THE UPPER ARMS SLIDE ALONG THE SHAFT AND ARE HELD IN PLACE WITH SET SCREWS. THE STANDARD 29” BAND FOR ITEM A” AND "E” FITS A 4” TO 8.6” DIAMETER. BAND LENGTHS UP TO 56” CAN BE USED FOR LARGER DIAMETER INSTALLATIONS. ITEMS ”A” THROUGH "E” ARE AVAILABLE FROM PELCO PRODUCTS INC. OR EQUIVALENT. THE 1 1/2°X 1 1/2” ALUMINUM LOCK NUT SHOWN WITH ITEM ”D” IS COMMONLY USED FOR SIGNAL HEAD &amp; PEDESTRIAN HEAD INSTALLATIONS. .
