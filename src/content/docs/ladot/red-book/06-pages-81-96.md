### Page 81 - Metal Cover Traffic Pull Box (Caltrans Pullbox No. 6 (T))

<div class="pmeta">Drawings: <strong>S-78.5.2</strong> &middot; Topics: signals, parking-curb</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0081.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0081.webp" alt="Red Book page 81 (S-78.5.2)"></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 81.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Traffic pullboxes in driveways, alleys and locations with vehicular traffic shall be metal covered per this standard.</p>
<p>2. Pullbox details not shown on this plan shall conform to Caltrans Standard Plan ES-8.</p>
<p>3. Traffic pullbox shall be provided with steel cover and special concrete footing. Steel cover shall have embossed non-skid pattern.</p>
<p>4. Galvanized conduits must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>5. Top of pull boxes shall be flush with surrounding grade or top of adjacent curb, except that in unpaved areas where pull box is not immediately adjacent to and protected by a concrete foundation, pole or other protective construction, the box shall be placed with its top 1 1/4&quot; above surrounding grade. Where practicable, pull boxes shown in the vicinity of curbs shall be placed adjacent to the back of curb, and pull boxes shown adjacent to standards shall be placed on side of foundation facing away from traffic, unless otherwise noted. When pull box is installed in sidewalk area, the depth of the pull box shall be adjusted so that the top of the pull box is flush with the sidewalk.</p>
<p>6. Pull box cover shall be marked as &quot;TRAFFIC SIGNAL&quot;.</p>
<p>7. Bonding jumper for metal covers shall be flat braided strap, 2&#x27; long minimum, Panduit Part # BS202446EU or equivalent.</p>
<p>8. Hold-down bolt shall be 5/8&quot; and coarse threaded.</p>
<p>Callouts: Lift hole. Hold-down bolt. Pull box reinforced with galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Reinforced 1/2&quot; minimum (MIN) steel plate cover, galvanized after fabrication, see Notes 3 and 6. Galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Pull box. Bonding jumper, see Note 7. PCC (portland cement concrete). Grounding bushing. 1/2&quot; length sleeve nut with brass bolt. Top flush with finished grade (see Note 5). 6&quot; MIN all around. 3&quot; MIN all around. 1&quot; crushed rock.</p></details>
