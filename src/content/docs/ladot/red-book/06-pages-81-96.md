It has a reinforced 1/2 in. minimum galvanized steel cover marked TRAFFIC SIGNAL on a Z-bar frame, a concrete footing, a bonding jumper to the conduit grounding bushings and a 1 in. crushed rock base. Metal covered boxes are required in driveways, alleys and where vehicles travel. Remaining details follow Caltrans Standard Plan ES-8.</p><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>Plan view of cover: A rounded-rectangle steel cover with the text &#x27;TRAFFIC SIGNAL&#x27; embossed across it. Pull box reinforced with galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Reinforced 1/2&quot; MIN steel plate cover, galvanized after fabrication, See Notes 3 &amp; 6.</p>
<p>Section: A cross section of the pull box set in the ground. A bonding jumper (See Note 7) connects from the cover to the conduit grounding bushings, shown as a looped braided strap. Two conduits enter at an angle through the bottom with grounding bushings, and the box rests on a bed of 1&quot; crushed rock. Printed page number 74.</p></details><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><h5>NOTES:</h5>
<p>1. Traffic pullboxes in driveways, alleys and locations with vehicular traffic shall be metal covered per this standard.</p>
<p>2. Pullbox details not shown on this plan shall conform to Caltrans Standard Plan ES-8.</p>
<p>3. Traffic pullbox shall be provided with steel cover and special concrete footing. Steel cover shall have embossed non-skid pattern.</p>
<p>4. Galvanized conduits must be bonded with copper ground strap around the neck of each conduit. PVC conduits must have their #8 green ground wires spliced together.</p>
<p>5. Top of pull boxes shall be flush with surrounding grade or top of adjacent curb, except that in unpaved areas where pull box is not immediately adjacent to and protected by a concrete foundation, pole or other protective construction, the box shall be placed with its top 1 1/4&quot; above surrounding grade. Where practicable, pull boxes shown in the vicinity of curbs shall be placed adjacent to the back of curb, and pull boxes shown adjacent to standards shall be placed on side of foundation facing away from traffic, unless otherwise noted. When pull box is installed in sidewalk area, the depth of the pull box shall be adjusted so that the top of the pull box is flush with the sidewalk.</p>
<p>6. Pull box cover shall be marked as &quot;TRAFFIC SIGNAL&quot;.</p>
<p>7. Bonding jumper for metal covers shall be flat braided strap, 2&#x27; long minimum, Panduit Part # BS202446EU or equivalent.</p>
<p>8. Hold-down bolt shall be 5/8&quot; and coarse threaded.</p>
<p>Callouts: Lift hole. Hold-down bolt. Pull box reinforced with galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Reinforced 1/2&quot; minimum (MIN) steel plate cover, galvanized after fabrication, see Notes 3 and 6. Galvanized Z-bar welded frame. Pull box. Bonding jumper, see Note 7. PCC (portland cement concrete). Grounding bushing. 1/2&quot; length sleeve nut with brass bolt. Top flush with finished grade (see Note 5). 6&quot; MIN all around. 3&quot; MIN all around. 1&quot; crushed rock.</p></details></div>

A ground rod wire clamp (Acorn) is used for each wire, and bushings are required on all metal conduit.</p><details class="sdesc" open><summary>What this sheet shows</summary><p>PVC Conduit System (top diagram): A schematic of service grounding with a Service Pullbox at left, a Controller Pullbox in the middle and a Traffic Signal Cabinet at right. In the service pullbox, three utility conductors AC+, AC- and AC+ come in &#x27;From Utility&#x27; through bonded galvanized conduit to the service fuse. The service ground rod is 8FT x 1/2&quot;, and a #8 green bond wire connects the conduit bond to the ground rod (marked with an asterisk, with an NC (normally closed) point). Schedule 80 PVC carries a dedicated service of #6 black, #6 green and #6 white (Black, Green, White) plus a #8 green bond wire to the controller pullbox. In the controller pullbox the wires pass through in Schedule 80 PVC, with the #8 green bond wires joined by a compression type fitting between two PVC stubs. The wires continue in Schedule 80 PVC to the traffic signal cabinet, where black, green and white land on the cabinet service block (Ground, AC+, AC-). The #8 green bond wire goes to the cabinet ground rod (8FT x 1/2&quot;) and, through a compression type fitting, to the equipment ground bus.</p>
<p>Galvanized Conduit System (bottom diagram): Same layout, but the conduits between pullboxes are bonded galvanized conduit instead of PVC, with a callout &#x27;Use a bushing on all metal conduit&#x27; and &#x27;Bonded Galvanized conduit&#x27; at the pullbox ends. The #8 green bond wire connects to the conduit bonding at each pullbox. The controller pullbox has a compression type fitting joining the bond wire to the two PVC stubs, and the cabinet ground rod arrangement is the same as in the PVC system.</p>
