### Page 113 - Mast Arm Mounted Street Name Signs (Sheet 1 of 2, S-486.0)

<div class="pmeta">Topics: signals, signs</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0113.webp"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0113.webp" alt="Red Book page 113 "></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 113.</figcaption></figure>
<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><h5>A. Street name signs, notes:</h5>
<p>1. Legend size: 8&quot; upper case, 6&quot; lower case, Series &quot;E&quot; letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow.</p>
<p>2. Color: white legend, blue background.</p>
<p>3. Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</p>
<p>4. Where the length of the sign blade would exceed 96&quot; (generally 16 letters and spaces) the street name titles, &quot;ST&quot;, &quot;AVE&quot;, &quot;BL&quot;, &quot;PL&quot;, &quot;DR&quot;, &quot;RD&quot;, etc. may be deleted.</p>
<h5>B. Freeway ramp signs, notes:</h5>
<p>1. Legend size: 8&quot; and 6&quot; Series D capital letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow; 18&quot; high US, California or Interstate shield, as appropriate with proportional numbers.</p>
<p>2. Color: white legend, Warboys green (L.A. No. 1) background; black on white US shield; white on green California shield; white on blue and red Interstate shield.</p>
<p>3. Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</p></details>

<details class="ocr"><summary>Drawing Notes &amp; Specifications</summary><p>1. Messages shall be formed by rows of fiber optic glass bundles.</p>
<p>2. Dual rows of fiber optic bundles shall be used to form the word &quot;TRAIN&quot;. Letters in the bus warning sign message shall have a brush stroke of 1-inch. Bus symbol and letters shall also conform to the latest edition of the FHWA Standard Highway Signs book.</p>

<p>7. The visor enclosure shall be fastened securely to the front frame of the sign.</p>

<p>9. Refer to Std. Dwg. No. S-63.1.4 for the mounting assembly (T=46&quot;, L=29&quot;).</p>
