<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0119.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0119.webp" alt="Red Book page 119 "></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 119 (scanned, cleaned and sharpened). <div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b><a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r3-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R3-5.svg">R3-5</a> Electric Sign.</b> Fiber optic electric sign that displays the R3-5L (left arrow with ONLY) and R3-5R (right arrow with ONLY) lane use messages in lunar white. Messages are formed by fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch center to center, and the sign blanks out completely when not energized. Housing is described on S-58.21 and mounting on S-63.1.4.</p><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Dimensions shown: 30&quot; and 13-1/2&quot;. Visor enclosure with 2&quot; Rad.</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Sign shall be capable of displaying the following messages:</p>
<p>A: Lunar white - R3-5L (left arrow w/ONLY). Width of arrow - 1.5&quot; stroke.</p>
<p>B: Lunar white - R3-5R (right arrow w/ONLY). Letters are Series &quot;C&quot;, 1&quot; stroke.</p>
<p>2. Messages shall be formed with rows of fiber optic bundles spaced 0.50 inch (center to center).</p>
<p>3. Fiber optic bundles shall alternate between two or more lamp sources so that in the event of a lamp failure the sign shall continue to display a discernable and readable message.</p>
<p>4. Sign shall completely blank out when not energized.</p>
<p>5. Door frame, face plate, and interior of housing and visor shall be painted flat black non-reflective finish.</p>
<p>6. Visor enclosure shall be fastened securely to the front frame of the sign.</p>
