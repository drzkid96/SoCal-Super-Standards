<p>Section B, Freeway ramp signs: One sign blade 72&quot; long by 24&quot; high, with hanger posts 12&quot; in from each end. <p>1. Legend size: 8&quot; upper case, 6&quot; lower case, Series &quot;E&quot; letters; 11-1/4&quot; x 12-3/4&quot; one-line arrow.</p>

<p>3. Reflectivity: high intensity letters on super engineer grade background or reverse screening on high intensity sheeting.</p></details></div>
