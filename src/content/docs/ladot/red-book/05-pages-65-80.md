### Page 70 - Preformed Loop Installation

<div class="pmeta">Topics: signals, parking-curb, accessibility</div>
<figure class="dfig"><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0070.webp" target="_blank"><img loading="lazy" src="/SoCal-Super-Standards/figs/ladot/red-book/p0070.webp" alt="Red Book page 70 "></a><figcaption>Red Book, page 70 (scanned, cleaned and sharpened). <div class="pclean"><p class="psum"><b>Preformed Loop Installation.</b> Shows a preformed detector loop built from 1/2&quot; I.D. polypropylene with a 3/4&quot; O.D., with 4 turns of continuous #16 TFFN wire, and filled with hot rubberized asphalt. Each loop head has a 5&quot; contraction/expansion joint with a 9&quot; long by 3/4&quot; Schedule 80 PVC cover slide, and the 3/8&quot; O.D. lead-in hose runs in 2&quot; PVC conduit from the pullbox to the pavement slab. Lead-in wires are twisted at least 2 times per foot, and any variation needs design engineer approval.</p><details class="ocr" open><summary>Notes on this sheet (cleaned from the scan)</summary><p>Winding details (not to scale)</p>
<p>6&#x27; expansion joint. CPVC tee. 3/4&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. CVPC cross. Expansion joint. 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. Twisted clockwise (at least 2 turns per ft.) to pullbox. 3&#x27;-0&quot; max. Twisted tail.</p>
<p>Section A-A (not to scale)</p>
<p>Detector loop conductors. 3/8&quot; O.D. asphalt rubber filled hydraulic hose. Pavement. To detector per plan. Schedule 80 PVC 2&quot; conduit (see Note 7) (typical).</p>
<h5>Notes:</h5>
<p>1. Pre-formed loop shall be constructed from 1/2&quot; I.D. polypropylene, with an O.D. of 3/4&quot; inch.</p>
<p>2. Loop is to have one continuous #16 TFFN wire through the loop head and lead-in to prevent loop malfunction due to splicing. Loop shall have four (4) turns of wire.</p>
<p>3. The conduit shall be completely filled with hot, rubberized asphalt which will allow the loop to retain flexibility once cooled, prevent incursion of moisture and set the wire turns firmly in place.</p>
<p>4. Each loop head shall be provided with a 5&quot; contraction/expansion joint to allow for movement of pavement and to prevent breakage of the wire and/or conduit. The joint is to have a 9&quot; long by 3/4&quot; Schedule 80 PVC cover slide to be placed over the joint for protection from the elements.</p>
<p>5. Encase lead-in wires in a non-conductive 250 psi flex hose with seamless fiber braid reinforcement and a non-conductive seamless extruded urethane non-perforated jacket. Fill lead-in hose completely with hot rubberized asphalt. Twist wires at least two times per foot for entire run. Attach lead-in to loop head with a Schedule 80 CVPC tee and a CVPC adapter bushing.</p>
<p>6. Any variation to the installation as described above shall be approved by the design engineer prior to installation.</p>
<p>7. The 3/8&quot; O.D. lead-in hose shall be installed in 2&quot; PVC conduit between pullbox and entry to pavement slab. Loop shall be attached to top of reinforcing bars when used in the pavement slab.</p></details></div>

The joint is to have a 9&quot; long by 3/4&quot; schedule 80 PVC cover slide to be placed over the joint for protection from the elements.</p>
<p>5. Encase lead-in wire in a non-conductive 250 psi flex hose, seamless, with fiber braid reinforcement and a non-conductive seamless extruded urethane non-perforated jacket. Fill lead-in hose completely with hot rubberized asphalt. Twist wires at least two times per foot for entire run. Attach lead-in to loop head with a schedule 80 CVPC tee and a CVPC adapter bushing.</p>
<p>6. Any variation to the installation as described above shall be approved by the design engineer prior to installation.</p></details></div>
