## 7C.01 Crosswalk Markings


<div class="secmeta">Section 7C.01 &middot; manual page 984 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7c/#7c01">CA amended: 7C.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/7C.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Crosswalks should be marked at all intersections on established routes to a school where there is substantial conflict between motorists, bicyclists, and student movements; where students would not otherwise recognize the proper place to cross; or where motorists or bicyclists might not expect students to cross (see Figure 7A‑1).</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> An engineering study considering the factors described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3c/#3c02">Section 3C.02</a> should be performed before a marked crosswalk is installed at a location away from a traffic control signal or an approach controlled by a STOP or YIELD sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Because non‑intersection school crossings are generally unexpected by the road user, warning signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/7b/#7b03">Section 7B.03</a>) should be installed for all marked school crosswalks at non‑intersection locations. Adequate visibility of students by approaching motorists and of approaching motorists by students should be provided by parking prohibitions or other appropriate measures.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3c/#3c02">Section 3C.02</a> contains provisions regarding crosswalk markings at non‑intersection locations. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3c/#3c03">Section 3C.03</a> contains provisions regarding the placement and design of crosswalks, and <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b19">Section 3B.19</a> contains provisions regarding the placement and design of the stop lines and yield lines that are associated with them. Provisions regarding the curb markings that can be used to establish parking regulations on the approaches to crosswalks are contained in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b18">Section 3B.18</a>.</p>
</div>

## 7C.02 Pavement Word, Symbol, and Arrow Markings


<div class="secmeta">Section 7C.02 &middot; manual page 984 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7c/#7c02">CA amended: 7C.02</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/7C.02/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> If used, the SCHOOL word marking may extend to the width of two approach lanes (see Figure 7C‑1).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If the two‑lane SCHOOL word marking is used, the letters should be 10 feet or more in height.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b20">Section 3B.20</a> contains provisions regarding other word, symbol, and arrow pavement markings that can be used to guide, warn, or regulate traffic.</p>
</div>
