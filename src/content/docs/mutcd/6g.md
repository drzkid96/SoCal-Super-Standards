## 6G.01 Regulatory Sign Authority


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.01 &middot; manual page 793 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g01">CA amended: 6G.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Regulatory signs such as those shown in <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a> inform road users of traffic laws or regulations and indicate the applicability of legal requirements that would not otherwise be apparent.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Regulatory signs shall be authorized by the public agency or official having jurisdiction and shall conform with Chapter 2B.</p>
</div>

## 6G.03 Regulatory Sign Applications


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.03 &middot; manual page 793 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g03">CA MUTCD 2026: 6G.03</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.03/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> If a TTC zone requires regulatory measures different from those existing, the existing permanent regulatory devices shall be removed or covered and superseded by the appropriate temporary regulatory signs. This change shall be made in compliance with applicable ordinances or statutes of the jurisdiction.</p>
</div>

## 6G.04 Road Closed Signs (R11-2 Series)


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.04 &middot; manual page 793 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g04">CA MUTCD 2026: 6G.04</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.04/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The ROAD CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used when the roadway is closed to all road users except contractors’ equipment or officially authorized vehicles. The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2.svg">R11-2</a> sign should be accompanied by appropriate warning and detour signing.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> STREET CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2a.svg">R11-2a</a>), BRIDGE OUT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2b.svg">R11-2b</a>), or PATH CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-2c/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-2c.svg">R11-2c</a>) signs may be substituted for Road Closed signs where applicable.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Road Closed signs should be installed at or near the center of the roadway on or above a Type 3 Barricade that closes the roadway (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6k/#6k07">Section 6K.07</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Road Closed signs shall not be used where road user flow is maintained through the TTC zone with a reduced number of lanes on the existing roadway or where the actual closure is some distance beyond the sign.</p>
</div>

## 6G.05 Local Traffic Only Signs (R11-3 Series and R11-4)


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.05 &middot; manual page 793 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g05">CA amended: 6G.05</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.05/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Local Traffic Only signs (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used where road users follow detours to avoid a closure some distance beyond the sign, but where local road users can use the roadway to the point of closure. These signs should be accompanied by appropriate warning and detour signing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In rural applications, the Local Traffic Only sign should have the legend ROAD CLOSED XX MILES AHEAD, LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3.svg">R11-3</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> In urban areas, a ROAD (STREET) CLOSED TO THRU TRAFFIC (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-4.svg">R11-4</a>) sign or the legend ROAD CLOSED, LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY may be used.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> In urban areas, a word message that includes the name of an intersecting street name or well-known destination may be substituted for the words XX MILES AHEAD on the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3.svg">R11-3</a> sign where applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A STREET CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3a.svg">R11-3a</a>) or BRIDGE OUT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3b.svg">R11-3b</a>) sign may be substituted for an <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-3.svg">R11-3</a> sign, where applicable.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The words BRIDGE OUT, BRIDGE CLOSED, or STREET CLOSED may be substituted for the words ROAD CLOSED on the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r11-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R11-4.svg">R11-4</a> sign where applicable.</p>
</div>

## 6G.06 Weight Limit Signs (R12-1, R12-2, and R12-5)


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.06 &middot; manual page 795 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g06">CA MUTCD 2026: 6G.06</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.06/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Weight Limit sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>), which shows the gross weight or axle weight that is permitted on the roadway or bridge, shall be consistent with State or local regulations and shall not be installed without the approval of the authority having jurisdiction over the highway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> When weight restrictions are imposed because of the activity in a TTC zone, a marked detour shall be provided for vehicles weighing more than the posted limit.</p>
</div>

## 6G.08 Work Zone and Higher Fines Signs and Plaques


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.08 &middot; manual page 795 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g08">CA amended: 6G.08</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.08/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A WORK ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/g20-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/G20-5aP.svg">G20-5aP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be mounted above a Speed Limit sign to emphasize that a reduced speed limit is in effect within a TTC zone. An END WORK ZONE SPEED LIMIT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-12/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-12.svg">R2-12</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be installed at the downstream end of the reduced speed limit zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> A BEGIN HIGHER FINES ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-10.svg">R2-10</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be installed at or near the beginning of a TTC zone where increased fines are imposed for traffic violations, and an END HIGHER FINES ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-11.svg">R2-11</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be installed at or near the downstream end of the TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Alternate legends such as BEGIN (or END) DOUBLE FINES ZONE may also be used for the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-10.svg">R2-10</a> and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r2-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R2-11.svg">R2-11</a> signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A FINES HIGHER, FINES DOUBLE, or $XX FINE plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b25">Section 2B.25</a> and <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) may be mounted below the Speed Limit sign if increased fines are imposed for traffic violations within the TTC zone.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Individual signs and plaques for work zone speed limits and higher fines may be combined into a single sign or may be displayed as an assembly of signs and plaques.</p>
</div>

## 6G.10 SIDEWALK CLOSED Signs (R9-9, R9-10, R9-11, and R9-11a)


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.10 &middot; manual page 797 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g10">CA MUTCD 2026: 6G.10</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.10/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> SIDEWALK CLOSED signs (see <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) should be used where pedestrian flow is restricted. Bicyclist/ Pedestrian Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9a.svg">M4-9a</a>) signs or Pedestrian Detour (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/m4-9b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/M4-9b.svg">M4-9b</a>) signs should be used where pedestrian flow is rerouted (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6i/#6i02">Section 6I.02</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-9/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-9.svg">R9-9</a>) sign should be installed at the beginning of the closed sidewalk, at the intersections preceding the closed sidewalk, and elsewhere along the closed sidewalk as needed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED, (ARROW) USE OTHER SIDE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-10.svg">R9-10</a>) sign should be installed at the beginning of the restricted sidewalk when a parallel sidewalk exists on the other side of the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED AHEAD, (ARROW) CROSS HERE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-11.svg">R9-11</a>) sign should be used to indicate to pedestrians that sidewalks beyond the sign are closed and to direct them to open crosswalks, sidewalks, or other travel paths.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The SIDEWALK CLOSED, (ARROW) CROSS HERE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r9-11a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R9-11a.svg">R9-11a</a>) sign should be installed just beyond the point to which pedestrians are being redirected.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> These signs are typically mounted on a detectable barricade to encourage compliance and to communicate with pedestrians that the sidewalk is closed. Printed signs are not useful to many pedestrians with vision disabilities. A barrier or barricade detectable by a person with a vision disability is sufficient to indicate that a sidewalk is closed. If the barrier is continuous with detectable channelizing devices for an alternate route, accessible signing might not be necessary.</p>
</div>

## 6G.12 Other Regulatory Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 6G.12 &middot; manual page 797 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6g/#6g12">CA MUTCD 2026: 6G.12</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6G.12/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Regulatory word message signs other than those classified and specified in this Manual and the “Standard Highways Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>) may be developed and used based on engineering judgment to aid the enforcement of other laws or regulations in TTC zones.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Special regulatory signs should comply with the general requirements of color, shape, and alphabet size and series. The sign message should be brief, legible, and clear.</p>
</div>
