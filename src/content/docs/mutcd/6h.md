## 6H.01 Warning Sign Function, Design, and Application


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h01">CA amended: 6H.01</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> TTC zone warning signs (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) notify road users of specific situations or conditions on or adjacent to a roadway that might not otherwise be apparent.</p>
</div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> TTC warning signs shall comply with the Standards for warning signs presented in Part 2 and in the FHWA’s “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The sizes for TTC warning signs shall be as shown in <a href="#fig-6h-1">Table 6H-1</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as provided in Paragraphs 5-9 of this Section, TTC warning signs shall be diamond-shaped with a black legend and border on an orange background, except for the Grade Crossing Advance Warning (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w10-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W10-1.svg">W10-1</a>) sign, which shall have a black legend and border on a yellow background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Warning signs that are required or recommended in Parts 2 or 7 to have a fluorescent yellow-green background may have that color background in TTC zones.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Existing warning signs with a yellow background that are still applicable may remain in place.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Warning signs used for TTC incident management situations may have a black legend and border on a fluorescent pink background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Mounting or space considerations may justify a change from the standard diamond shape to a rectangular shape.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> In emergencies, available warning signs having yellow backgrounds may be used if signs with orange or fluorescent pink backgrounds are not at hand.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Where roadway or road user conditions require greater emphasis, larger than standard size warning signs should be used, with the symbol or legend enlarged approximately in proportion to the outside dimensions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Where any part of the roadway is obstructed or closed by work activities or incidents, advance warning signs should be installed to alert road users well in advance of these obstructions or restrictions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> Where road users include pedestrians, the provision of supplemental audible information or detectable barriers or barricades should be provided for people with vision disabilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Detectable barriers or barricades communicate very clearly to pedestrians who have vision disabilities that they can no longer proceed in the direction that they are traveling.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> Advance warning signs may be used singly or in combination.</p>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> Where distances are not displayed on warning signs as part of the message, a supplemental plaque with the distance legend may be mounted immediately below the sign on the same support.</p>
</div>

## 6H.02 Position of Advance Warning Signs


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h02">CA MUTCD 2026: 6H.02</a></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Where highway conditions permit, warning signs should be placed in advance of the transition and activity areas at varying distances depending on roadway type, condition, and posted speed. <a href="#fig-6b-1">Table 6B-1</a> contains information regarding the spacing of advance warning signs. Where a series of two or more advance warning signs is used, the closest sign to the transition and activity areas should be placed approximately 100 feet for low-speed urban streets to 1,000 feet or more for freeways and expressways.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Where multiple advance warning signs are needed on the approach to a transition and activity area, the ROAD WORK AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w20-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W20-1.svg">W20-1</a>) sign should be the first advance warning sign encountered by road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Various conditions, such as limited sight distance or obstructions that might require a driver to reduce speed or stop, might require additional advance warning signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> As an alternative to a specific distance on advance warning signs, the word AHEAD may be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> At TTC zones on lightly-traveled roads, all of the advance warning signs prescribed for major construction might not be needed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Utility work, maintenance, or minor construction can occur within the TTC zone limits of a major construction project, and additional warning signs may be needed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Utility, maintenance, and minor construction signing and TTC should be coordinated with appropriate authorities so that road users are not confused or misled by the additional TTC devices.</p>
</div>

## 6H.25 Signs for Blasting Areas


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h25">CA MUTCD 2026: 6H.25</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Radio-Frequency (RF) energy can cause the premature firing of electric detonators (blasting caps) used in TTC zones.</p>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Road users shall be warned where blasting operations occur. A sequence of signs shall be prominently displayed to warn all road users of blasting operations and to direct operators of mobile radio equipment, including cellular telephones, to turn off transmitters in a blasting area. These signs shall be covered or removed when there are no explosives in the area or the area is otherwise secured.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The BLASTING ZONE AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-1.svg">W22-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) shall be used in advance of any TTC zone where explosives are being used. The TURN OFF 2-WAY RADIO AND CELL PHONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r22-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R22-2.svg">R22-2</a>) and END BLASTING ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-3.svg">W22-3</a>) signs shall be used in sequence with this sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The TURN OFF 2-WAY RADIO AND CELL PHONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r22-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R22-2.svg">R22-2</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6g/#6g11">Section 6G.11</a> and <a href="#fig-6g-1">Figure 6G-1</a>) shall follow the BLASTING ZONE AHEAD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-1.svg">W22-1</a>) sign and shall be placed at least 1,000 feet before the beginning of the blasting zone.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The END BLASTING ZONE (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w22-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W22-3.svg">W22-3</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) shall be placed a minimum of 1,000 feet past the blasting zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The END BLASTING ZONE sign may be placed either with or preceding the END ROAD WORK sign.</p>
</div>

<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Double Reverse Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w24-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W24-1.svg">W24-1</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w24-1a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W24-1a.svg">W24-1a</a>, or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w24-1b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W24-1b.svg">W24-1b</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) may be used where the tangent distance between two reverse curves is less than 600 feet, thus making it difficult for a second Reverse Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-4.svg">W1-4</a> series) sign to be placed between the curves. If the design speed of the curves is 30 mph or less, Double Reverse Turn signs may be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If a Double Reverse Curve (or Turn) sign is used, the direction of the double reverse curve (or turn) shall be appropriately illustrated. Except as provided in Paragraph 3 of this Section, the number of lanes illustrated on the sign shall be the same as the number of through lanes available to road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Where two or more lanes are being shifted, a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w24-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W24-1.svg">W24-1</a> (or Double Reverse Turn sign showing one lane) sign with an ALL LANES (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w24-1cp/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W24-1cP.svg">W24-1cP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) may be used instead of a sign that illustrates the number of lanes.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where more than three lanes are being shifted, the Double Reverse Curve (or Turn) sign may be rectangular.</p>
</div>

## 6H.32 Advisory Speed Plaque (W13-1P)


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h32">CA amended: 6H.32</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In combination with a warning sign, an Advisory Speed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-1p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-1P.svg">W13-1P</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) may be used to indicate a recommended speed through the TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The Advisory Speed plaque shall not be used in conjunction with any sign other than a warning sign, nor shall it be used alone. When used with orange TTC zone signs, this plaque shall have a black legend and border on an orange background. Except in emergencies, an Advisory Speed plaque shall not be mounted until the recommended speed is determined by the highway agency.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Warning signs with advisory speed plaques (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c59">Section 2C.59</a>) inform drivers of the recommended operating speed based on temporary conditions within a TTC zone. Examples include narrow lanes, temporary diversion (reverse curves), lane shifts, sight distance restrictions, rough road surface, bumps, low/no shoulder, workers on foot, work vehicles or equipment close to the open travel lane, or other conditions that indicate the need for reduced speed.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> AASHTO and ITE design documents contain established engineering practices for the determination of the recommended advisory speeds for horizontal curves or locations with limited sight distance.</p>
</div>

## 6H.33 Supplementary Distance Plaque (W7-3aP)


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h33">CA MUTCD 2026: 6H.33</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In combination with a warning sign, a Supplementary Distance (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w7-3ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W7-3aP.svg">W7-3aP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6h-1">Figure 6H-1</a>) with the legend NEXT XX MILES may be used to indicate the length of highway over which a work activity is being conducted, or over which a condition exists in the TTC zone.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In long TTC zones, Supplementary Distance plaques with the legend NEXT XX MILES may be placed in combination with warning signs at regular intervals within the zone to indicate the remaining length of highway over which the TTC work activity or condition exists.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Supplementary Distance plaque with the legend NEXT XX MILES shall not be used in conjunction with any sign other than a warning sign, nor shall it be used alone. When used with orange TTC zone signs, this plaque shall have a black legend and border on an orange background. The plaque shall be at least 30 x 24 inches in size when used with a sign that is 36 x 36 inches or larger.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> When used in TTC zones, the Supplementary Distance plaque with the legend NEXT XX MILES should be placed below the initial warning sign designating that, within the approaching zone, a temporary work activity or condition exists.</p>
</div>

## 6H.38 Other Warning Signs


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6h/#6h38">CA MUTCD 2026: 6H.38</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Advance warning signs may be used by themselves or with other advance warning signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Besides the warning signs specifically related to TTC zones, several other warning signs in Part 2 may apply in TTC zones.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Word message warning signs other than those classified and specified in this Manual and the “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>) may be developed and used based on engineering judgment to warn of special conditions in TTC zones.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6f/#6f01">Sections 6F.01</a> and 6H.01, other warning signs that are used in TTC zones shall have black legends and borders on an orange background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Other warning signs should comply with the general requirements of color, shape, and alphabet size and series. The sign message should be brief, legible, and clear.</p>
</div>
