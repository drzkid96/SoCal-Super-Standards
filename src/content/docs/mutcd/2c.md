## 2C.01 Application of Warning Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.01 &middot; manual page 147 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c01">CA MUTCD 2026: 2C.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The use of warning signs shall be based on an engineering study or on engineering judgment.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Warning signs shall be retroreflective or illuminated (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2a/#2a21">Section 2A.21</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The use of warning signs should be kept to a minimum as the unnecessary use of warning signs tends to breed disrespect for all signs. In situations where the condition or activity is seasonal or temporary, the warning sign should be removed or covered when the condition or activity does not exist.</p>
</div>

## 2C.02 Design of Warning Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.02 &middot; manual page 147 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c02">CA amended: 2C.02</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.02/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 2 of this Section or unless specifically designated otherwise, all warning signs shall be diamond-shaped (square with one diagonal vertical) with a black legend and border on a yellow background. Warning signs shall be designed in accordance with the sizes, shapes, colors, and legends contained in the “Standard Highway Signs” publication (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/1a/#1a05">Section 1A.05</a>).</p>

## 2C.04 Placement of Warning Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.04 &middot; manual page 152 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c04">CA MUTCD 2026: 2C.04</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.04/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Information on the placement of warning signs is contained in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2a/#2a13">Sections 2A.13</a> through 2A.18.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The time needed for detection, recognition, decision, and reaction is called the Perception-Response Time (PRT). <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a> is provided as an aid for determining warning sign location. The distances shown in <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a> can be adjusted for roadway features, other signing, and to improve visibility.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Warning signs should be placed so that they provide an adequate PRT. The distances contained in <a href="#fig-2c-3">Table 2C-3</a> should be applied with engineering judgment.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Minimum spacing between warning signs with different messages should be based on the estimated PRT for driver comprehension of and reaction to the second sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The effectiveness of the placement of warning signs should be periodically evaluated under both day and night conditions.</p>
</div>

## 2C.05 Horizontal Alignment Warning Signs – General


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.05 &middot; manual page 153 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c05">CA MUTCD 2026: 2C.05</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.05/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A variety of horizontal alignment warning signs (see <a href="#fig-2c-1">Figure 2C-1</a>), pavement markings (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/">Chapter 3B</a>), and delineation (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3g/">Chapter 3G</a>) can be used to advise motorists of a change in the roadway alignment. Uniform application of these traffic control devices with respect to the amount of change in the roadway alignment conveys a consistent message establishing driver expectancy and promoting effective roadway operations. The design and application of horizontal alignment warning signs to meet those requirements are addressed in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c05">Sections 2C.05</a> through 2C.12.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The following list identifies treatments that might be used in advance of or within a change in horizontal alignment: A. Horizontal alignment (Turn (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-1.svg">W1-1</a>), Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-2.svg">W1-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-10/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-10.svg">W1-10</a> series, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-11.svg">W1-11</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-13/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-13.svg">W1-13</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-15.svg">W1-15</a>), Reverse Turn (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-3.svg">W1-3</a>), Reverse Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-4.svg">W1-4</a>), Winding Road (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-5.svg">W1-5</a>), Exit Speed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-2.svg">W13-2</a>), Ramp Speed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-3.svg">W13-3</a>), and Combination Horizontal Alignment (Advisory Exit or Ramp Speed <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-6.svg">W13-6</a> through <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-11.svg">W13-11</a>)) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c07">Sections 2C.07</a>, 2C.09, and 2C.12) B. Advisory Speed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-1p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-1P.svg">W13-1P</a>) plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c59">Section 2C.59</a>) C. Chevron Alignment (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-8.svg">W1-8</a>) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c08">Section 2C.08</a>) D. Delineators (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3g/">Chapter 3G</a>) E. One-Direction Large Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-6.svg">W1-6</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c10">Section 2C.10</a>) F. Raised Retroreflective Pavement Markers (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b15">Sections 3B.15</a> through 3B.17) G. Sign or marking conspicuity enhancements (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2a/#2a11">Section 2A.11</a>) H. Wide edge lines (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3a/#3a04">Section 3A.04</a>) I. Pavement word, symbol and arrow markings (symbol or words) (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b20">Sections 3B.20</a> through 3B.22) J. Rumble strips (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3k/">Chapter 3K</a>) K. Vehicle Speed Feedback Sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c13">Section 2C.13</a>) L. Speed reduction markings (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b28">Section 3B.28</a>)</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> In addition, considerations other than traffic control devices, such as improved surface friction (high friction surface treatments), pavement edge treatments, lighting improvements, increased superelevation, and rumble strips, might be used in advance of or within a change in horizontal alignment.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c06">Section 2C.06</a>, the selection of traffic control devices used to warn road users of a change in horizontal alignment or to provide guidance in navigating the change in horizontal alignment should be based on consideration of one or more of the following factors: A. The speed of traffic on the approach to the change in horizontal alignment B. The recommended advisory speed for the change in horizontal alignment C. The difference between the speed limit and the advisory speed, or the speed differential for the change in horizontal alignment D. Daily traffic volumes on the roadway E. The typical mix of vehicle types on the roadway F. Sight distance throughout the change in horizontal alignment G. Other types of traffic control devices that are used in advance of and within the change in horizontal alignment on the same roadway segment H. The crash history of the change in horizontal alignment I. The presence of driveways or intersections within the curve radius</p>
</div>

## 2C.06 Device Selection for Changes in Horizontal Alignment


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.06 &middot; manual page 154 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c06">CA MUTCD 2026: 2C.06</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.06/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The criteria shown in Chart A of <a href="#fig-2c-4">Table 2C-4</a> shall be used to determine the need for devices for changes in horizontal alignment. If the use of a device or devices is indicated by Chart A of <a href="#fig-2c-4">Table 2C-4</a>, then Chart B of <a href="#fig-2c-4">Table 2C-4</a> shall be used to specify the type(s) of devices to be used in advance of, and/or along, a horizontal curve, except as provided in Paragraphs 3, 5, and 6 of this Section. The speed differential in Chart B of <a href="#fig-2c-4">Table 2C-4</a> shall be the difference between the horizontal curve’s advisory speed and the roadway’s posted speed limit, statutory speed limit, or the 85th-percentile speed on the approach to the curve. (See <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c59">Section 2C.59</a> for the use of the Advisory Speed Plaque.)</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Chart A of <a href="#fig-2c-4">Table 2C-4</a> represents existing AADT, type of roadway, and whether or not there are existing markings.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Additional or supplemental devices may be used for a change in horizontal alignment on the basis of engineering judgment.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Devices for changes in horizontal alignment may be omitted when the speed limit on the approach to an alignment change is 20 mph or less.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Devices for changes in horizontal alignment may be omitted on urban streets with an AADT of 1,000 vehicles per day or less.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> For purposes of selecting traffic control devices for changes in horizontal alignment, an arterial or collector is considered to have pavement markings when either a center line, edge lines, or both are present. Warrants for center lines and edge lines are provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b02">Sections 3B.02</a> and 3B.10, respectively.</p>
</div>

<p><span class="pn">03</span> Where there are two changes in roadway alignment in opposite directions that are separated by a tangent distance of less than 600 feet, the Reverse Turn (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-3.svg">W1-3</a>) sign should be used instead of multiple Turn (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-1.svg">W1-1</a>) signs or the Reverse Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-4/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-4.svg">W1-4</a>) sign should be used instead of multiple Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-2.svg">W1-2</a>) signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <a href="#fig-2c-2">Figure 2C-2</a> provides examples of warning signs used for turns and curves.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A Winding Road (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-5.svg">W1-5</a>) sign may be used instead of multiple Turn (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-1.svg">W1-1</a>) or Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-2.svg">W1-2</a>) signs where there are three or more changes in roadway alignment each separated by a tangent distance of less than 600 feet.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> A NEXT XX MILES (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w7-3ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W7-3aP.svg">W7-3aP</a>) supplemental distance plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c61">Section 2C.61</a>) may be installed below the Winding Road sign where continuous roadway curves exist for a specific distance.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> If the curve has a change in horizontal alignment of 135 degrees or more, the Hairpin Curve (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-11/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-11.svg">W1-11</a>) sign may be used instead of a Turn or Curve sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If the curve has a change of direction of approximately 270 degrees, such as on a cloverleaf interchange ramp, the 270-degree Loop (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-15.svg">W1-15</a>) sign may be used instead of a Turn or Curve sign.</p>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>

## 2C.08 Chevron Alignment Sign (W1-8)


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.08 &middot; manual page 158 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c08">CA MUTCD 2026: 2C.08</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.08/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The use of the Chevron Alignment (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-8/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-8.svg">W1-8</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-1">Figures 2C-1</a> and 2C-2) to provide additional emphasis and guidance for a change in horizontal alignment shall be in accordance with the information shown in <a href="#fig-2c-4">Table 2C-4</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Chevron Alignment signs may be used instead of or in addition to standard delineators.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Chevron Alignment sign shall be a vertical rectangle. No border shall be used on the Chevron Alignment sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If used, Chevron Alignment signs shall be installed on the outside of a turn or curve, in line with and at approximately a right angle to approaching traffic. Chevron Alignment signs shall be installed at a minimum height of 4 feet, measured vertically from the bottom of the sign to the elevation of the near edge of the traveled way.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The approximate spacing of Chevron Alignment signs on the turn or curve measured from the point of curvature (PC) should be as shown in <a href="#fig-2c-5">Table 2C-5</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The Chevron Alignment signs should be visible for a sufficient distance to provide the road user with adequate time to react to the change in alignment.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> LEDs may be used to enhance the conspicuity of Chevron Alignment signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2a/#2a12">Section 2A.12</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The LEDs used in the Chevron Alignment sign shall consist of yellow LEDs outlining the chevron symbol.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Chevron Alignment signs shall not be placed on the far side of a T-intersection facing traffic on the stem approach to warn drivers that a through movement is not physically possible, as this is the function of a Two-Direction (or One-Direction) Large Arrow sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Chevron Alignment signs shall not be used to mark obstructions within or adjacent to the roadway, including the beginning of guardrails or barriers, as this is the function of an object marker (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c70">Section 2C.70</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Chevron Alignment signs directing traffic to the right shall not be used in the central island of a roundabout or a neighborhood traffic circle.</p>
</div>

## 2C.25 Low Clearance Signs (W12-2, W12-2a, and W12-2b)


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.25 &middot; manual page 172 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c25">CA amended: 2C.25</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.25/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Low Clearance Advance (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2.svg">W12-2</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-6">Figure 2C-6</a>) shall be used to warn road users of vertical clearances less than 14 feet 6 inches, or vertical clearances less than 12 inches above the statutory maximum vehicle height, whichever is greater.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The actual clearance should be displayed on the Low Clearance (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2.svg">W12-2</a>, <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2a.svg">W12-2a</a>, and <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2b.svg">W12-2b</a>) sign to the nearest 1 inch not exceeding the actual clearance. However, in areas that experience changes in temperature causing frost action, a reduction, not exceeding 3 inches, should be used for this condition.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Clearances should be evaluated periodically to determine if additional low clearance signing is necessary, particularly when resurfacing operations have occurred, on routes onto which over-height vehicles are normally directed under the permit process, and structures that are susceptible to catastrophic failure when struck by over- height vehicles.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2.svg">W12-2</a> sign with a supplemental distance plaque should also be placed at the nearest intersecting road or wide point in the road at which a vehicle can detour or turn around.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Where there is a need to warn of a low clearance on an intersecting road or off a freeway or expressway exit, a rectangular warning sign with an appropriate word legend should be used rather than a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2.svg">W12-2</a> sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The Low Clearance Overhead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2a.svg">W12-2a</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2b.svg">W12-2b</a>)sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-6">Figure 2C-6</a>) may be installed on the structure to supplement the advance warning sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> In cases where physical conditions on a structure limit the width such that the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2a.svg">W12-2a</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2b.svg">W12-2b</a> signs are physically unable to fit, a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2.svg">W12-2</a> sign may be installed overhead on the structure or post-mounted in front of the structure, in addition to the required <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2.svg">W12-2</a> sign at the advance location.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> In the case of an arch, or other structure under which the clearance varies greatly, two or more Low Clearance Overhead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2a.svg">W12-2a</a> or 12-2b) signs should be installed on the structure itself to give information as to the clearances over the low clearance portions of the roadway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> If used, the Low Clearance Overhead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2b.svg">W12-2b</a>) sign shall be placed over a lane or shoulder to indicate the portion of the structure with low clearance if the posted clearance does not apply to the entire structure.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The clearance shown on the Low Clearance Advance sign should match the clearance on the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2a.svg">W12-2a</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2b.svg">W12-2b</a> sign or, if there are multiple <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w12-2b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W12-2b.svg">W12-2b</a> signs, should match the lowest clearance.</p>
</div>

<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The signs and plaques described in this Section are intended to give motorcyclists advance notice of surface conditions that might adversely affect their ability to maintain control of their motorcycle under wet or dry conditions. The use of some of the advance surface condition warning signs described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c30">Section 2C.30</a>, such as Slippery When Wet, LOOSE GRAVEL, or ROUGH ROAD, can also be helpful to motorcyclists if those conditions exist.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If a portion of a street or highway features a roadway pavement surface that is grooved or textured instead of smooth, such as a grooved skid resistance treatment for a horizontal curve or a brick pavement surface, a GROOVED PAVEMENT (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-15.svg">W8-15</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-7">Figure 2C-7</a>) may be used to provide advance warning of this condition to motorcyclists, bicyclists, and other road users. Alternate legends such as TEXTURED PAVEMENT or BRICK PAVEMENT may also be used on the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-15.svg">W8-15</a> sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If a bridge or a portion of a bridge includes a metal or grated surface, a METAL BRIDGE DECK (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-16/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-16.svg">W8-16</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-7">Figure 2C-7</a>) may be used to provide advance warning of this condition to motorcyclists, bicyclists, and other road users.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A Motorcycle (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-15ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-15aP.svg">W8-15aP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-2c-7">Figure 2C-7</a>) may be mounted below or above a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-15/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-15.svg">W8-15</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w8-16/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W8-16.svg">W8-16</a> sign if the warning is intended to be directed primarily to motorcyclists.</p>
</div>

## 2C.45 Merge Signs and Plaque (W4-1, W4-5, and W4-5aP)


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.45 &middot; manual page 182 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c45">CA amended: 2C.45</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.45/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A Merge (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-11">Figure 2C-11</a>) may be used to warn road users on the major roadway that merging movements might be encountered in advance of a point where lanes from two separate roadways converge as a single traffic lane and no turning conflict occurs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> A Merge sign may also be installed on the side of the entering roadway to warn road users on the entering roadway of the merge condition.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Merge sign should be installed on the side of the major roadway where merging traffic will be encountered and in such a position as to not obstruct the road user’s view of entering traffic.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> When a Merge sign is installed on a major roadway, the symbol should be oriented right or left as appropriate to depict the side from which the merge occurs, with the arrow representing the major roadway and the curved stem representing the entering roadway (see <a href="#fig-2c-11">Figure 2C-11</a>)</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When a Merge sign is installed on an entering roadway that curves before merging with the major roadway, such as a ramp with a curving horizontal alignment as it approaches the major roadway, the Entering Roadway Merge (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-5.svg">W4-5</a>) sign (see <a href="#fig-2c-11">Figure 2C-11</a>) should be used to better portray the actual geometric conditions to road users on the entering roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Where two roadways of approximately equal importance converge and merging movements are required, a Merge sign should be placed on each roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The Merge sign should not be used where two roadways converge and merging movements are not required.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The Merge sign shall not be used in place of a Lane Ends (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-2.svg">W4-2</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c47">Section 2C.47</a>) where lanes of traffic moving on a single roadway must merge because of a reduction in the actual or usable pavement width.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> An Entering Roadway Merge (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-5/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-5.svg">W4-5</a>) sign with a NO MERGE AREA (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-5aP.svg">W4-5aP</a>) supplemental plaque (see <a href="#fig-2c-11">Figure 2C-11</a>) mounted below it may be used to warn road users on an entering roadway that they will encounter an abrupt merging situation without an acceleration lane at the downstream end of the ramp.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> A Merge (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a>) sign with a NO MERGE AREA (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-5aP.svg">W4-5aP</a>) supplemental plaque mounted below it may be used to warn road users on the major roadway that traffic on an entering roadway will encounter an abrupt merging situation without an acceleration lane at the downstream end of the ramp.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> For a yield-controlled channelized right-turn movement onto a roadway without an acceleration lane, a NO MERGE AREA (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-5ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-5aP.svg">W4-5aP</a>) supplemental plaque may be mounted below a Yield Ahead (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w3-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W3-2.svg">W3-2</a>) sign and/or below a YIELD (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-2.svg">R1-2</a>) sign when engineering judgment indicates that road users would expect an acceleration lane to be present.</p>

<p><span class="pn">12</span> Examples of the use of Merge (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W4-1.svg">W4-1</a>) signs are shown in Drawing A in <a href="#fig-2c-12">Figure 2C-12</a>.</p>

## 2C.59 Advisory Speed Plaque (W13-1P) and Confirmation Advisory Speed Plaque (W13-1aP)


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.59 &middot; manual page 196 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c59">CA amended: 2C.59</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.59/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Advisory Speed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-1p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-1P.svg">W13-1P</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-2c-1">Figure 2C-1</a>) may be used to supplement an advance warning sign to indicate the advisory speed for a condition.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The Confirmation Advisory Speed (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w13-1ap/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W13-1aP.svg">W13-1aP</a>) plaque (see <a href="#fig-2c-1">Figure 2C-1</a>) may be used to supplement a One- Direction Large Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-6.svg">W1-6</a>) sign on the outside of a turn or curve in line with and at approximately a right angle to approaching traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The use of the Advisory Speed and Confirmation Advisory Speed plaques for horizontal curves shall be in accordance with <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c06">Section 2C.06</a> and <a href="#fig-2c-6">Table 2C-6</a>. The speed differential in <a href="#fig-2c-6">Table 2C-6</a> shall be the difference between the advisory speed for the horizontal curve and the posted speed limit, statutory speed limit, or the 85th percentile speed on the approach to the curve. The Advisory Speed plaque shall also be used where an engineering study indicates a need to advise road users of the advisory speed for other roadway conditions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The speed displayed on the Advisory Speed and Confirmation Advisory Speed plaques shall be a multiple of 5 mph.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Except in emergencies or when the condition is temporary, an Advisory Speed or Confirmation Advisory Speed plaque shall not be installed until the advisory speed has been determined by an engineering study.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The Advisory Speed plaque shall only be used to supplement an advance warning sign. The Advisory Speed plaque or the Confirmation Advisory Speed plaque shall not be installed as a separate sign installation.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The Advisory Speed plaque, if used with a sign that is also supplemented with another plaque, such as an Advance Street Name plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c65">Section 2C.65</a>), should be mounted immediately below the primary warning sign with any other plaque mounted below the Advisory Speed plaque.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The Confirmation Advisory Speed plaque shall only be used to supplement a One-Direction Large Arrow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W1-6.svg">W1-6</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2c/#2c10">Section 2C.10</a>) or an Exit Gore (E5-1 series) (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2e/#2e26">Section 2E.26</a>) sign and shall not be installed as a separate sign installation.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> The advisory speed shall be determined by an engineering study that follows established engineering practices.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The advisory speed should be determined based on free-flowing traffic conditions.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> Because changes in conditions, such as roadway geometrics, surface characteristics, or sight distance, might affect the advisory speed, each location should be evaluated periodically or when conditions change.</p>
</div>

<p><span class="pn">12</span> The 2nd Edition of the “Traffic Control Devices Handbook,” 2013, Institute of Transportation Engineers contains engineering practices for the determination of the recommended advisory speed for a horizontal curve including following: A. Compass method B. Safety-based method C. Accelerometer method D. Design equation method E. Ball-bank method using the following criteria: 1. 16 degrees of ball-bank for speeds of 20 mph or less 2. 14 degrees of ball-bank for speeds of 25 to 30 mph 3. 12 degrees of ball-bank for speeds of 35 mph and higher</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The 16, 14, and 12 degrees of ball-bank criteria are comparable to the current AASHTO horizontal curve design guidance. Research has shown that drivers often exceed existing posted advisory curve speeds by 7 to 10 mph.</p>

## 2C.70 Object Marker Design and Placement Height


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.70 &middot; manual page 199 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c70">CA amended: 2C.70</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.70/">short link</a></div>

<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> When used, object markers (see <a href="#fig-2c-17">Figure 2C-17</a>) shall not have a border and shall consist of an arrangement of one or more of the following types: Type 1—a diamond-shaped sign, at least 18 inches on a side, consisting of either a yellow (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-1.svg">OM1-1</a>) or black (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-2.svg">OM1-2</a>) sign with nine yellow retroreflective devices, each with a minimum diameter of 3 inches, mounted symmetrically on the sign, or an all-yellow retroreflective sign (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om1-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM1-3.svg">OM1-3</a>). Type 2—either a marker (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om2-1v/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM2-1V.svg">OM2-1V</a> or OM2- 1H) consisting of three yellow retroreflective devices, each with a minimum diameter of 3 inches, arranged either horizontally or vertically on a white sign measuring at least 6 x 12 inches; or an all-yellow horizontal or vertical retroreflective sign (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om2-2v/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM2-2V.svg">OM2-2V</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om2-2h/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM2-2H.svg">OM2-2H</a>), measuring at least 6 x 12 inches. Type 3—a striped marker, 12 x 36 inches, consisting of a vertical rectangle with alternating black and retroreflective yellow stripes sloping downward at an angle of 45 degrees toward the side of the obstruction on which traffic is to pass. The minimum width of the yellow and black stripes shall be 3 inches. Type 4—a diamond-shaped sign, at least 18 inches on a side, consisting of either a red (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-1/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-1.svg">OM4-1</a>) or black (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-2/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-2.svg">OM4-2</a>) sign with nine red retroreflective devices, each with a minimum diameter of 3 inches, mounted symmetrically on the sign, or an all-red retroreflective sign (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/om4-3/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/OM4-3.svg">OM4-3</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Type 3 object markers with stripes that begin at the upper right side and slope downward to the lower left side are designated as right object markers (OM3-R). Object markers with stripes that begin at the upper left side and slope downward to the lower right side are designated as left object markers (OM3-L). Object markers with chevron stripes that slope downward to both the lower left and lower right sides are designated as center object markers (OM3-C).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> When used for marking obstructions within the roadway or obstructions that are 8 feet or less from the shoulder or curb, the minimum mounting height, measured from the bottom of the object marker to the elevation of the near edge of the traveled way, should be 4 feet.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When used to mark obstructions more than 8 feet from the shoulder or curb, the clearance from the ground to the bottom of the object marker should be at least 4 feet.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Object markers should not present a vertical or horizontal clearance obstacle for pedestrians.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> When object markers or markings are applied to an obstruction that by its nature requires a lower or higher mounting, the vertical mounting height may vary according to need.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/9c/#9c09">Section 9C.09</a> contains information regarding the use of object markers on shared-use paths.</p>
</div>

<span id="2c71" class="anchor"></span>

## 2C.71 Object Markers for Obstructions within the Roadway


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.71 &middot; manual page 200 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c71">CA amended: 2C.71</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.71/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Obstructions within the roadway shall be marked with a Type 1 or Type 3 object marker. In addition to markers on the face of the obstruction, warning of approach to the obstruction shall be given by appropriate pavement markings (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b13">Section 3B.13</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> To provide additional emphasis, a Type 1 or Type 3 object marker may be installed at or near the approach end of a median island.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> To provide additional emphasis, large surfaces such as bridge piers may be painted with diagonal stripes, 12 inches or greater in width, similar in design to the Type 3 object marker.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The alternating black and retroreflective yellow stripes (OM3-L, OM3-R) shall be sloped down at an angle of 45 degrees toward the side on which traffic is to pass the obstruction. If traffic can pass to either side of the obstruction, the alternating black and retroreflective yellow stripes (OM3-C) shall form chevrons that point upwards.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Appropriate signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b39">Sections 2B.39</a> and 2C.23) directing traffic to one or both sides of the obstruction may be used instead of the object marker.</p>
</div>

<span id="2c72" class="anchor"></span>

## 2C.72 Object Markers for Obstructions Adjacent to the Roadway


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.72 &middot; manual page 200 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c72">CA amended: 2C.72</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.72/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Obstructions not actually within the roadway are sometimes so close to the edge of the road that they need a marker. These include underpass piers, bridge abutments, handrails, ends of traffic barriers, utility poles, and culvert headwalls. In other cases there might not be a physical object involved, but other roadside conditions exist, such as narrow shoulders, drop-offs, gores, small islands, and abrupt changes in the roadway alignment, that might make it undesirable for a road user to leave the roadway, and therefore would create a need for a marker.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Type 2 or Type 3 object markers may be used to mark an obstruction adjacent to the roadway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If a Type 2 or Type 3 object marker is used to mark an obstruction adjacent to the roadway, the edge of the object marker that is closest to the road user should be installed in line with the closest edge of the obstruction.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> When a marker is applied to the approach ends of guardrail or crash cushion terminals it should have the appearance of a Type 3 object marker and should be directly affixed, without a substrate, to the approach end of the guardrail or crash cushion and generally conform to the size and shape of the approach end of the guardrail or crash cushion.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Type 1 and Type 4 object markers shall not be used to mark obstructions adjacent to the roadway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Standard warning signs in this Chapter should also be used where applicable.</p>
</div>

<span id="2c73" class="anchor"></span>

## 2C.73 Object Markers for Ends of Roadways


<div class="secmeta">Section 2C.73 &middot; manual page 200 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2c/#2c73">CA amended: 2C.73</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2C.73/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> The Type 4 object marker is used to warn and alert road users of the end of a roadway in other than construction or maintenance areas.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If an object marker is used to mark the end of a roadway, a Type 4 object marker shall be used.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Type 4 object marker may be used in instances where there are no alternate vehicular paths.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Where conditions warrant, more than one marker, or a larger marker with or without a Type 3 Barricade (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b75">Section 2B.75</a>), may be used at the end of the roadway.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The minimum mounting height, measured vertically from the bottom of a Type 4 object marker to the elevation of the near edge of the traveled way, shall be 4 feet.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Appropriate advance warning signs in this Chapter should be used.</p>
</div>
