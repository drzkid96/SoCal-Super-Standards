## 2F.01 Scope


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.01 &middot; manual page 380 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f01">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Toll highways are typically limited-access freeway or expressway facilities. A portion of or an entire route might be a toll highway, or a bridge, tunnel, or other crossing point might be the only toll portion of a highway at which a toll is collected. A toll highway might be a conventional road. The general signing requirements for toll roads will depend on the type of facility and access (freeway, expressway, or conventional road). The provisions of Chapters 2D and 2E will generally apply for guide signs along the toll facility that direct road users within and off the facility where exit points and geometric configurations are not dependent specifically on the collection of tolls. The aspect of tolling and the presence of toll plazas or collection points necessitate additional considerations in the typical signing needs. The notification of the collection of tolls in advance of and at entry points to the toll highway also necessitates additional modifications to the typical signing.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The scope of this Section applies to a route or facility on which all lanes are tolled. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2g/">Chapter 2G</a> contains provisions for the signing of managed lanes within an otherwise non-toll facility that employ tolling or pricing as an operational strategy to manage congestion levels.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Except where specifically provided in this Chapter, the provisions of other Chapters in Part 2 shall apply to toll roads.</p>
</div>

<p><span class="pn">04</span> The ETC system pictograph (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2a/#2a04">Section 2A.04</a>) shall be of a size that makes it a prominent feature of the sign legend as necessary for conspicuity for those road users with registered ETC accounts seeking such direction, as well as for those road users who do not have ETC accounts so that it is clear to them to avoid such direction when applicable.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 6 of this Section, an ETC pictograph that is in the shape of a horizontally- oriented rectangle should have a minimum height of 1.5 times the upper-case letter height of the principal legend on the sign. The width of an ETC pictograph in the shape of a horizontal rectangle should be between approximately 2 and 3 times the height of the pictograph.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> When the pictograph is the principal legend on the sign, such as for advance guide signs for open-road tolling lanes (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f15">Section 2F.15</a>), the minimum height of a horizontally-oriented rectangular ETC pictograph should be consistent with that of a route shield prescribed for the particular application and type of sign.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> For ETC pictographs whose shape is square, circular, or otherwise similar in height and width, or is a vertically-oriented rectangle, the same basic principles for conspicuity and placement should be followed. ETC pictographs whose shape is not in that of a horizontally-oriented rectangle should be suitably sized to facilitate conspicuity as described in Paragraph 4 of this Section and should be of a similar approximate area as the horizontally-oriented rectangular pictographs designed in accordance with the height and width as provided in Paragraph 5 of this Section.</p>
</div>

## 2F.03 Use of Color on Toll Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.03 &middot; manual page 380 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f03">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.03</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.03/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Use of the color purple on any sign shall comply with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/1d/#1d05">Sections 1D.05</a> and 2A.06. Except as provided in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f05">Sections 2F.05</a> and 2F.16, purple as a background color shall be used only when the information associated with the appropriate ETC account is displayed on that portion of the sign. The background color of the remaining portion of such signs shall comply with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/1d/#1d05">Sections 1D.05</a> and 2A.06 as appropriate for a regulatory, warning, or guide sign. Purple shall not be used as a background color to display a destination, action message, or other legend that is not a display of the requirement for all vehicles to have a registered ETC account.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If only vehicles with registered ETC accounts are allowed to use a highway lane, a toll plaza lane, an open-road tolling lane, or all lanes of a toll highway or connection, the guide signs for such lanes or highways shall incorporate the pictograph (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2a/#2a04">Section 2A.04</a>) adopted by the toll facility’s ETC payment system and the regulatory message ONLY. Except for ETC pictographs whose predominant background color is purple, if incorporated within the green background of a guide sign, the ETC pictograph shall be on a white rectangular or square panel set on a purple underlay panel with a white border. For rectangular ETC pictographs whose predominant background color is purple, a white border shall be used at the outer edges of the purple rectangle to provide contrast between the pictograph and the sign background color.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If an ETC pictograph is used on a separate plaque in a route sign assembly (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f05">Section 2F.05</a>) or on a header panel within a guide sign, the plaque or the header panel shall have a purple background with a white border and the ETC pictograph shall have a white border to provide contrast between the pictograph and the background of the plaque or header panel.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Purple underlay panels for ETC pictographs or purple backgrounds for plaques and header panels shall only be used in the manner described in Paragraphs 1 through 3 of this Section to convey the requirement of a registered ETC account on signs for lanes reserved exclusively for vehicles with such an account and on directional signs to an ETC Account-Only facility from a non-toll facility or from a toll facility that accepts multiple payment forms.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="#fig-2f-1">Figure 2F-1</a> shows examples of ETC account pictographs, their use with various background colors, and modifications involving underlay panels.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f02">Section 2F.02</a> contains provisions regarding the size of pictographs for ETC accounts.</p>
</div>

## 2F.04 Regulatory Signs for Toll Plazas


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.04 &middot; manual page 383 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f04">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.04</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.04/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Toll plaza operations often include lane-specific restrictions on vehicle type, forms of payment accepted, and speed limits or required stops. Vehicles are typically required to come to a stop to pay the toll or receive a toll ticket in the attended and exact change or automatic lanes. Electronic toll collection (ETC) lanes with favorable geometrics typically allow vehicles to move through the toll plaza without stopping, but usually within a set regulatory speed limit or advisory speed. In some ETC lanes and in most lanes that accommodate non-ETC vehicles, a stop might be required while the ETC payment is processed because of geometric or other conditions.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Regulatory signs applicable only to a particular lane or lanes should be located in a position that makes their lane applicability clear to road users approaching the toll plaza.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Regulatory signs, or regulatory panels within guide signs, indicating restrictions on vehicle type and forms of toll payment accepted at a specific toll plaza lane should be installed over the applicable lane either on the toll plaza canopy or on a separate structure immediately in advance of the canopy located in a manner such that each sign is clearly related to an individual toll lane.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f12">Section 2F.12</a> contains information regarding the incorporation of regulatory messages into guide signs for toll plazas.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f16">Section 2F.16</a> contains information regarding the design and use of toll plaza canopy signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>

## 2F.12 Toll Facility and Toll Plaza Guide Signs – General


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.12 &middot; manual page 388 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f12">CA amended: 2F.12</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.12/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Toll plazas are used on many toll highways, bridges, and tunnels for collection of tolls from road users. Electronic toll collection and/or open-road tolling might also be used on such facilities, either in addition to or in place of collecting toll payments at toll plazas.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2g/">Chapter 2G</a> contains information regarding signs for preferential and managed lanes that are applicable to toll roads.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3e/">Chapter 3E</a> contains information regarding pavement markings for certain toll plaza applications.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Directional assemblies for entrances to a toll highway, or to a road leading directly to a toll highway with no opportunity to exit before paying or being charged a toll, shall clearly indicate that the facility is a toll facility. Except where the State Toll Route sign (see Paragraph 8 of this Section) is used, the TOLL (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-17p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-17P.svg">W16-17P</a>) warning plaque (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f11">Section 2F.11</a>) shall be used above the route sign of a numbered toll facility in any route sign assembly that provides directions to the toll route from another highway (see <a href="#fig-2f-5">Figure 2F-5</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Except where the State Toll Route sign (see Paragraph 8 of this Section) is used and on Exit Gore signs or destination guide (D1 series) signs, a rectangular panel with the black legend TOLL on a yellow background shall be incorporated into the guide signs leading road users to a tolled highway (see <a href="#fig-2f-6">Figures 2F-6</a> through 2F-8).</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Guide signs for toll highways, toll plazas, and tolled or priced managed lanes (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2g/">Chapter 2G</a>) shall have white legends and borders on green backgrounds, except as specifically provided by <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f12">Sections 2F.12</a> through 2F.16.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A State Toll Route sign (see Paragraph 8 of this Section) may be used in lieu of the State Route (M1-5) sign in combination with the TOLL (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-17p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-17P.svg">W16-17P</a>) warning plaque or the TOLL panel (see Paragraphs 10 and 11 of this Section).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> A State Toll Route sign shall incorporate into its design the word TOLL using the same letter height, legend, background colors, and overall plaque dimensions specified for the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/w16-17p/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/W16-17P.svg">W16-17P</a> warning plaque.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> The Interstate, Off-Interstate, and U.S. Route signs shall not be modified for tolled facilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Where conditions do not accommodate separate signs, or where it is important to associate a particular regulatory or warning message with specific guidance information, regulatory and/or warning messages may be combined with guide signs for toll plazas using plaques, header panels, or rectangular regulatory or warning panels incorporated within the guide signs, as long as the proper legend and background colors are preserved.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> When regulatory messages are incorporated within a guide sign, they shall be on a rectangular panel with a black legend on a white background. When warning messages are incorporated within a guide sign, they shall be on a rectangular panel with a black legend on a yellow background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> Guide signs for toll plazas should be designed in accordance with the general principles of guide signs and the specific provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2e/">Chapter 2E</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Signs for toll plazas should systematically provide road users with advance and toll plaza lane-specific information regarding: A. The amount of the toll, the types of payment accepted, and the type(s) of registered ETC accounts accepted for payment; B. Which lane or lanes are required or allowed to be used for each available payment type; and C. Restrictions on the use of a toll plaza lane or lanes by certain types of vehicles (such as cars only or no trucks).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> Signs for attended lanes at toll plazas shall incorporate the Toll Collector (M4-17) symbol panel (see <a href="#fig-2f-9">Figure 2F-9</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> Signs for attended lanes at toll plazas may also display word legends, such as FULL SERVICE, CASH, CHANGE, or RECEIPTS (see <a href="#fig-2f-9">Figure 2F-9</a>), to supplement the required symbol panel when lanes have different services available through them.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> Signs for Exact Change lanes at toll plazas shall incorporate the Exact Change (M4-18) symbol panel and, except for ticketed systems, display the amount of the toll for passenger vehicles (see <a href="#fig-2f-9">Figure 2F-9</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">17</span> Signs for Exact Change lanes at toll plazas may include an appropriate word legend, such as EXACT CHANGE (see <a href="#fig-2f-9">Figure 2F-9</a>), to supplement the required symbol panel.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">18</span> When used, the M4-17 and M4-18 symbol panels shall be used only as panels within guide signs. The M4-17 and M4-18 symbols or panels shall not be used as an independent sign or within a sign assembly.</p>
<p><span class="pn">19</span> If only vehicles with registered ETC accounts are allowed to use a toll plaza lane, the signs for such lanes shall incorporate the pictograph adopted by the toll facility’s ETC payment system and the regulatory message ONLY (see <a href="#fig-2f-1">Figures 2F-1</a> and 2F-8 through 2F-11). The use, size, and placement of the ETC pictograph shall comply with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f02">Sections 2F.02</a> and 2F.03.</p>
<p><span class="pn">20</span> An Overhead Arrow-per-Lane guide sign (see <a href="#fig-2f-10">Figure 2F-10</a>) shall be used in advance of a location where the mainline lanes split to separate traffic entering Open-Road ETC lanes from lanes entering a toll plaza where other methods of payment are accepted and an option lane is provided at the split (see <a href="#fig-2f-11">Figure 2F-11</a>). An Overhead Arrow-per-Lane guide sign shall not be used if there is no option lane at the split.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">21</span> The ETC payment system’s pictograph, without a purple underlay or purple header panel, may be used on signs for Exact Change or attended lanes at toll plazas to indicate that vehicles with registered ETC accounts may also use those lanes.</p>
</div>

## 2F.14 Advance Signs for Conventional Toll Plazas


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.14 &middot; manual page 399 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f14">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.14</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.14/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> For conventional toll plazas (those without a divergence onto a separate alignment from mainline-aligned open-road tolling or ETC-Only lanes), one or more sets of overhead advance guide signs complying with the provisions of this Section should be provided. The advance guide signs for multi-lane toll plazas should provide information regarding which lanes to use for all of the toll payment methods accepted at the toll plaza. These signs should include toll plaza lane numbers (if used), or action messages or lane-use information such as LEFT LANE(S), CENTER LANE(S), RIGHT LANE(S), or down arrows over the approximate center of each applicable lane. These signs should also incorporate regulatory messages indicating any restrictions or prohibitions on the use of the lanes associated with the various types of payment methods by certain types of vehicles. For mainline toll plazas, these signs should be at least ½ mile in advance of the toll plaza, and farther if practical.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Additional guide signs with lane information for the toll payment types should be provided between approxi- mately ¼ mile and 800 feet in advance of the toll plaza at a location that avoids or minimizes obstruction of toll plaza canopy signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f16">Section 2F.16</a>) and lane-use control signals.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The number, mounting, and/or spacing of sets of advance signs for approaches to toll plazas on ramps, toll bridges, or tunnels, to accommodate a limited distance to the plaza from an intersection or from the start of the approach road to the bridge or tunnel, should be based on an engineering study or engineering judgment.</p>
</div>

## 2F.15 Advance Signs for Toll Plazas on Diverging Alignments from Open-Road ETC Account-Only Lanes


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.15 &middot; manual page 399 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f15">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.15</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.15/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Open-Road ETC lanes are sometimes located on the normal mainline alignment while the lanes for other toll payment methods are located at a toll plaza on a separate alignment (see <a href="#fig-2f-15">Figure 2F-15</a>). Since road users paying cash tolls must diverge from the mainline alignment, similar to a movement for an exit, it is important that the guide signs in advance of and at the point of divergence clearly indicate the required lane use and/or movements.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> For toll plazas located on a separate alignment that diverges from mainline-aligned Open-Road ETC lanes where vehicles are required to have a registered ETC account to use the Open-Road Tolling lanes, overhead advance signs should be provided at approximately 1 mile and ½ mile in advance of the divergence point. Both the 1-mile and ½-mile advance signs should include: A. The ETC (pictograph) Account-Only guide sign (see <a href="#fig-2f-9">Figures 2F-9</a> and 2F-15) with a down arrow over the approximate center of each lane that will become an Open-Road ETC lane; B. For the lane or lanes which will diverge to a toll plaza, guide signs conforming to the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f12">Section 2F.12</a>, indicating which lane or lanes will diverge to the toll plaza for the various cash toll payment methods; and C. Regulatory signs, plaques, or panels within the guide signs, indicating any restrictions or prohibitions of certain types of vehicles from toll plaza lanes associated with the various types of payment methods.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> At or near the theoretical gore of the divergence point, an additional set of overhead guide signs should be provided and should include: A. The ETC (pictograph) Account-Only guide sign (see <a href="#fig-2f-9">Figures 2F-9</a> and 2F-15) with a down arrow over the approximate center of each Open-Road ETC lane; B. Guide signs conforming to the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f12">Section 2F.12</a> and 2F.13, with diagonally upward-pointing directional arrow(s) over the approximate center of each lane indicating the direction of the divergence, and providing lane information for all types of payment methods accepted at the toll plaza; and C. Regulatory signs, plaques, or panels within the guide signs, indicating any restrictions or prohibitions on the use of the toll plaza lanes associated with the various types of payment methods by certain types of vehicles.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Approximately 800 feet in advance of the toll plaza at a location that avoids or minimizes any obstruction of the toll plaza canopy signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f16">Section 2F.16</a>) and lane-use control signals, an additional set of overhead advance signs with lane information for the toll payment types should be provided.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The use of down and directional arrows on the signs at the locations described in Paragraphs 2 through 4 of this Section shall comply with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2d/#2d08">Section 2D.08</a>.</p>
</div>

## 2F.16 Toll Plaza Canopy Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.16 &middot; manual page 402 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f16">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.16</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.16/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> A sign complying with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f12">Section 2F.12</a> shall be provided above the approximate center of each lane that is not an Open-Road ETC lane, mounted on or suspended from the toll plaza canopy, or on a separate structure immediately in advance of the plaza located such that each sign is clearly related to an individual toll lane, indicating the payment type(s) accepted in the lane and any restrictions or prohibitions of certain types of vehicles that apply to the lane. Except for toll-ticket systems, the toll for passenger or 2-axle vehicles shall be included on the canopy sign or on a separate sign mounted on the upstream side of the tollbooth.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The background color of a canopy sign for an ETC Account-Only toll plaza lane shall be purple (see <a href="#fig-2f-16">Figure 2F-16</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Where vehicles are required to have a registered ETC account to use the lane, one or two flashing yellow beacons (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4r/#4r03">Section 4R.03</a>) may supplement a canopy sign over an ETC Account-Only lane to call special attention to the location of the ETC Account-Only lane within the plaza.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The canopy sign for an ETC Account-Only toll plaza lane in which a regulatory speed limit is not posted and in which vehicles are not required to stop may display an advisory speed within a horizontal rectangular panel with a black legend and yellow background within the bottom portion of the canopy sign.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Flashing beacons supplementing a canopy sign over an ETC Account-Only lane shall be mounted directly above or alongside the sign in a manner that is separated from any lane-use control signals for that lane (see <a href="#fig-2f-16">Figure 2F-16</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> For multi-lane toll plazas, lane-use control signals (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4r/#4r02">Section 4R.02</a>) shall be provided above the approximate center of each toll plaza lane that is not an Open-Road ETC lane to indicate the open or closed status of each lane. Lane-use control signals shall not be used to call attention to a lane for a specific toll payment type such as ETC Account-Only lanes.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Part 6 contains information regarding the closing of a lane for temporary traffic control purposes.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> <a href="#fig-2f-16">Figure 2F-16</a> shows examples of toll plaza canopy signs.</p>
</div>

## 2F.17 Guide Signs for Entrances to Electronic Toll Collection (ETC) Only Facilities


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.17 &middot; manual page 402 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f17">CA amended: 2F.17</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.17/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Some toll highways, bridges, and tunnels are restricted to use only by vehicles with a specific registered ETC account, referred to as ETC Account-Only facilities. Other facilities collect tolls electronically using license plate character recognition in which the registered vehicle owner is then billed by postal mail and registration in an ETC account program is not required but could be an optional payment method. These facilities are commonly referred to as All-Electronic Tolling (AET) or Cashless Tolling.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Guide signs for facilities that only collect tolls electronically shall comply with the applicable provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2e/">Chapter 2E</a> and <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f13">Section 2F.13</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Guide signs for the entrance ramps to ETC Account-Only facilities shall incorporate the pictograph of the toll facility’s primary ETC payment system and the word ONLY in a header panel or plaque designed in accordance with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f13">Section 2F.13</a> (see <a href="#fig-2f-8">Figure 2F-8</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> A separate information sign displaying the route number, the TOLL warning panel (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f11">Section 2F.11</a>), and the legend NO CASH may be located within the sequence of the Advance guide signs on the approach to the entrance to an ETC Account-Only facility.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> Exit Gore signs for entrance ramps to such ETC Account-Only facilities may incorporate the pictograph of the toll facility’s ETC payment system and the word ONLY in a header panel or plaque designed in accordance with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f13">Section 2F.13</a> (see <a href="#fig-2f-13">Figure 2F-13</a> and Drawing B in <a href="#fig-2f-12">Figure 2F-12</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If more than one ETC account program is accepted for toll payments on an ETC Account-Only facility, the additional accepted ETC account program pictographs may be displayed on a separate informational guide sign with the legend, ALSO ACCEPTED, within the sequence of advance guide signs for the entrance to the facility.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f05">Section 2F.05</a> contains information regarding ETC Account-Only signs and plaques for use with route signs in route sign assemblies.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Where vehicles are not required to have a registered ETC account to use an ETC-Only facility, guide signs for the facility shall comply with the applicable provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2e/">Chapter 2E</a> and specifically with the applicable provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f13">Section 2F.13</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Advance and Exit Direction guide signs for the entrances to facilities that do not require registration in an ETC toll account program shall not display a pictograph of an accepted ETC payment system or use purple as a background color on any portion of the signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Information on accepted toll payment methods for a facility that does not require registration in an ETC toll account program shall only be provided on a separate informational guide sign, if used, that displays one of the following legends (see Drawing C in <a href="#fig-2f-12">Figure 2F-12</a>): A. TOLL BILLED BY MAIL ONLY, if there is no alternative payment method; or B. TOLL BILLED BY MAIL OR [ETC Account Pictograph], if the facility also accepts payments from registered users of an ETC account program.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> If there is more than one ETC toll account program accepted, all ETC account program pictographs of the accepted ETC accounts may be displayed on the separate informational guide sign below the TOLL BILLED BY MAIL OR legend. A plaque with the legend NO CASH may be added below the signs described in Paragraph 10 of this Section.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The signs described in Paragraph 10 of this Section should be located within the sequence of Advance Guide signs for the entrance to the facility and/or at a location along the facility itself (see Drawing C in <a href="#fig-2f-12">Figure 2F-12</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> If the ETC-Only facility also accepts payments from multiple ETC account programs, but does not require registration in the primary ETC account program associated with the facility in order to use the facility, then the pictographs of the other accepted ETC account programs may be displayed on the separate information sign beneath the legend TOLL BILLED BY MAIL and the word OR.</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> If, in addition to a toll, a nominal surcharge (not a fine, penalty, or violation) is assessed road users not registered in the toll account program, or registered toll account users are assessed a discounted toll, such information may be displayed on a separate information sign on the approach to the entrance to the facility.</p>
</div>

## 2F.18 Guide Signs for ETC-Only Entrance Ramps to Non-Toll Highways


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.18 &middot; manual page 403 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f18">CA amended: 2F.18</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.18/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In some cases, access to or from a non-toll route might be provided by a ramp on which a toll is charged in order to manage congestion, limit access, or for other reasons. The toll ramp might be provided as an alternative to or in lieu of a ramp providing similar access without charging a toll. <a href="#fig-2f-8">Figures 2F-8</a> and 2F-13 show examples of guide signs for a ramp on which a toll is charged to enter a non-toll route.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Guide signs for ETC-Only Entrance Ramps to non-toll highways shall comply with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2f/#2f17">Section 2F.17</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> A NO TOLL panel with a black legend and a yellow background may be included on the top section of the Exit Gore sign for an exit that provides access to the facility without charging a toll.</p>
</div>

## 2F.19 ETC Account Program Information Signs


<div class="secmeta">Section 2F.19 &middot; manual page 404 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2f/#2f19">CA MUTCD 2026: 2F.19</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/2F.19/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 2 of this Section, signs that inform road users of telephone numbers, Internet addresses, including domain names and uniform resource locators (URLs), or e-mail addresses for enrolling in an ETC account program of a toll facility or managed lane, obtaining an ETC transponder, and/or obtaining ETC account program information shall only be installed in rest areas, parking areas, or similar roadside facilities where the signs are viewed only by pedestrians or occupants of parked vehicles.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> ETC account program information signs displaying telephone numbers that have no more than four characters may be installed on roadways in locations where they will not obscure the road user’s view of higher priority traffic control devices and that are removed from key decision points where the road user’s view is more appropriately focused on other traffic control devices, roadway geometry, or traffic conditions, including exit and entrance ramps, intersections, toll plazas, temporary traffic control zones, and areas of limited sight distance.</p>
</div>
