## 4N.01 Application of Emergency-Vehicle Hybrid Beacons


<div class="secmeta">Section 4N.01 &middot; manual page 742 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4n/#4n01">CA MUTCD 2026: 4N.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/4N.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons shall be used only in conjunction with signs to warn and control traffic at an unsignalized location where emergency vehicles enter or cross a street or highway. Emergency- vehicle hybrid beacons shall be actuated only by authorized emergency or maintenance personnel.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons should only be used when all of the following criteria are satisfied: A. The conditions justifying an emergency-vehicle traffic control signal (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4m/#4m01">Section 4M.01</a>) are met; B. An engineering study, considering the road width, approach speeds, and other pertinent factors, determines that emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons can be designed and located in compliance with the requirements contained in this Chapter and in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4s/#4s01">Section 4S.01</a>, such that they effectively warn and control traffic at the location; and C. The location is not at or within 100 feet from an intersection or driveway where the side road or driveway is controlled by a STOP or YIELD sign.</p>
</div>

## 4N.02 Design of Emergency-Vehicle Hybrid Beacons


<div class="secmeta">Section 4N.02 &middot; manual page 742 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4n/#4n02">CA MUTCD 2026: 4N.02</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/4N.02/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Except as otherwise provided in this Section, an emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon shall meet the requirements of this Manual.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> An emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon face shall consist of three signal sections, with a CIRCULAR YELLOW signal indication centered below two horizontally-aligned CIRCULAR RED signal indications (see <a href="#fig-4n-1">Figure 4N-1</a>).</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> At least two emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon faces shall be installed for each approach of the major street.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> On approaches having posted or statutory speed limits or 85th-percentile speeds in excess of 40 mph, and on approaches having traffic or operating conditions that would tend to obscure visibility of roadside beacon faces, both of the minimum of two emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon faces should be installed over the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> On multi-lane approaches having posted or statutory speed limits or 85th-percentile speeds of 40 mph or less, either an emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon face should be installed on each side of the approach (if a median of sufficient width exists) or at least one of the emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon faces should be installed over the roadway.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> An emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon should comply with the signal face location provisions described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4d/#4d05">Sections 4D.05</a> through 4D.10.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> Stop lines and EMERGENCY SIGNAL—STOP ON FLASHING RED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-14/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-14.svg">R10-14</a> or <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-14a/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-14a.svg">R10-14a</a>) signs (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b59">Section 2B.59</a>) shall be used with emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons for each approach of the major street.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If needed for extra emphasis, a STOP HERE ON FLASHING RED (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-14b/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-14b.svg">R10-14b</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b59">Section 2B.59</a>) may be installed with an emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons may be equipped with a light or other display visible to the operator of the egressing emergency vehicle to provide confirmation that the beacons are operating.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> Emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons may be supplemented with an advance warning sign, which may also be supplemented with a Warning Beacon (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4s/#4s03">Section 4S.03</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> If a Warning Beacon is used to supplement the advance warning sign, it should be programmed to flash only when the emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon is not in the dark mode.</p>
</div>

## 4N.03 Operation of Emergency-Vehicle Hybrid Beacons


<div class="secmeta">Section 4N.03 &middot; manual page 743 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4n/#4n03">CA MUTCD 2026: 4N.03</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/4N.03/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons shall be placed in a dark mode (no indications displayed) during periods between actuations.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Upon actuation by authorized emergency personnel, the emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon faces shall each display a flashing yellow signal indication, followed by a steady yellow change interval, prior to displaying two CIRCULAR RED signal indications in an alternating flashing array for a duration of time adequate for egress of the emergency vehicles (see <a href="#fig-4n-1">Figure 4N-1</a>). The alternating flashing red signal indications shall only be displayed when it is required that drivers on the major street stop and then proceed subject to the rules applicable after making a stop at a STOP sign. Upon termination of the flashing red signal indications, the emergency-vehicle hybrid beacons shall revert to a dark mode (no indications displayed) condition.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The duration of the flashing yellow interval should be determined by engineering judgment.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The duration of the steady yellow change interval shall be determined using engineering practices in accordance with the provisions in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4f/#4f17">Section 4F.17</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> A yellow change interval should have a minimum duration of 3 seconds and a maximum duration of 6 seconds (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4f/#4f17">Section 4F.17</a>). The longer intervals should be reserved for use on approaches with higher speeds.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> A steady red clearance interval may be used after the steady yellow change interval.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> An emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon that is located 200 feet or less from an active grade crossing should be preempted in accordance with the applicable provisions in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4f/#4f19">Sections 4F.19</a> and 8D.09.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> If an emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon is placed into a flashing mode by a conflict monitor (malfunction management unit) or by a manual switch, the emergency-vehicle hybrid beacon faces shall display flashing yellow signal indications to each approach of the major street.</p>
</div>
