## 6I.01 Guide Signs – General


<div class="secmeta">Section 6I.01 &middot; manual page 811 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6i/#6i01">CA amended: 6I.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6I.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Guide signs along highways provide road users with information to help them along their way through the TTC zone. The design of guide signs is presented in Part 2.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> The following guide signs should be used in TTC zones as needed: A. Standard route markings where temporary route changes are necessary, B. Directional signs and street name signs, and C. Special guide signs relating to the condition or work being done.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If additional temporary guide signs are used in TTC zones, they shall have a black legend and border on an orange background.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Guide signs used in TTC incident management situations may have a black legend and border on a fluorescent pink background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When temporary directional signs and temporary street name signs are used in conjunction with detour routing, these signs may have a black legend and border on an orange background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> When permanent directional signs or permanent street name signs are used in conjunction with detour signing, they may have a white legend on a green background (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2d/#2d35">Sections 2D.35</a> and 2D.45).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> The sizes for TTC guide signs shall be as shown in <a href="#fig-6i-1">Table 6I-1</a>.</p>
</div>

## 6I.02 Detour Signs and Plaques (M4-8P, M4-8a, M4-8bP, M4-9, M4-9a, M4-9b, M4-9c, and M4-10)


<div class="secmeta">Section 6I.02 &middot; manual page 811 &middot; <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/6i/#6i02">CA amended: 6I.02</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/6I.02/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Each detour shall be adequately marked with standard temporary route signs and destination signs.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Detour signs in TTC incident management situations may have a black legend and border on a fluorescent pink background.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> The Detour Arrow (M4-10) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be used where a detour route has been established.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The DETOUR (M4-8P) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be mounted at the top of a route sign assembly to mark a temporary route that detours from a highway, bypasses a section closed by a TTC zone, and rejoins the highway beyond the TTC zone.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The Detour Arrow (M4-10) sign should normally be mounted just below the ROAD CLOSED (R11-2, R11-3a, or R11-4) sign. The Detour Arrow sign should include a horizontal arrow pointed to the right or left as required.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The DETOUR (M4-9) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) should be used for unnumbered highways, for emergency situations, for periods of short durations, or where, over relatively short distances, road users are guided along the detour and back to the desired highway without route signs.</p>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> A Street Name sign should be placed above, or the street name should be incorporated into, a DETOUR (M4-9) sign to indicate the name of the street being detoured.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> The END DETOUR (M4-8a) sign or the END (M4-8bP) plaque (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be used to indicate that the detour has ended.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> When the END DETOUR sign is used on a numbered highway, the sign should be mounted above a route sign after the downstream end of the detour.</p>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> The Pedestrian/Bicyclist Detour (M4-9a) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) should be used where a pedestrian/bicyclist detour route has been established because of the closing of a pedestrian/bicycle facility to through traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> If used, the Pedestrian/Bicyclist Detour sign shall have an arrow pointing in the appropriate direction.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> The arrow on a Pedestrian/Bicyclist Detour sign may be on the sign face or on a supplemental plaque.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> The Pedestrian Detour (M4-9b) sign or Bicyclist Detour (M4-9c) sign (see <a href="#fig-6i-1">Figure 6I-1</a>) may be used where a pedestrian or a bicyclist detour route (not both) has been established because of the closing of the pedestrian or bicycle facility to through traffic.</p>
</div>
