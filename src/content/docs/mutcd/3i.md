## 3I.01 Channelizing Devices


<div class="secmeta">Section 3I.01 &middot; manual page 632 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3i/#3i01">CA amended: 3I.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/3I.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Channelizing devices (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6k/#6k01">Sections 6K.01</a> through 6K.07 and <a href="#fig-6k-1">Figure 6K-1</a>) such as cones, tubular markers, vertical panels, lane separators, drums, and barricades may be used for general traffic control purposes such as adding emphasis to reversible lane delineation, channelizing lines, islands, pedestrian facilities, or bicycle facilities. Channelizing devices may also be used along a center line to preclude turns or along lane lines to preclude lane changing, as determined by engineering judgment.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Although they are not considered to be traffic control devices, raised islands (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3j/">Chapter 3J</a>) are also sometimes used to channelize traffic.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Except for color, the design of channelizing devices, including, but not limited to, retroreflectivity, minimum dimensions, and mounting height, shall comply with the provisions of <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6k/">Chapter 6K</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Except as provided in Paragraph 5 of this Section, the color of channelizing devices used outside of temporary traffic control zones shall be the same color as the pavement marking that they supplement, or for which they are substituted, in accordance with <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3a/#3a03">Section 3A.03</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The color of channelizing devices used to emphasize pavement marking patterns outside of temporary traffic control zones may be orange provided that the application of the orange-colored channelizing device is not permanent.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> Emergency incidents and planned special events are the most common temporary traffic control zones that would justify orange channelizing devices to emphasize standard pavement marking colors. These events do not necessitate police officers or other authorized personnel to obtain and deploy channelizing devices that match the color of the existing pavement marking.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> For nighttime use, channelizing devices shall be retroreflective (as described in Part 6) or internally illuminated. On channelizing devices used outside of temporary traffic control zones, retroreflective sheeting or bands shall be white if the devices separate traffic flows in the same direction and shall be yellow if the devices separate traffic flows in the opposite direction or are placed along the left-hand edge line of a one-way roadway or ramp.</p>
</div>

## 3I.02 Tubular Markers


<div class="secmeta">Section 3I.02 &middot; manual page 632 &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3i/#3i02">CA MUTCD 2026: 3I.02</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/3I.02/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Tubular markers for permanent installations shall be a minimum of 28 inches in height and shall be a minimum of 2 inches wide facing road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Tubular markers should be affixed to the pavement or other surface either directly or by means of an attachment system that is affixed to the pavement or other surface. Tubular markers should normally be spaced no greater than N as cited in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b14">Section 3B.14</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Other spacing may be used based on engineering judgment.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Tubular markers are sometimes used to provide additional emphasis or improve lane discipline in advance of an unsignalized crosswalk (see <a href="#fig-3i-1">Figure 3I-1</a>).</p>
</div>

<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> When tubular markers are used to supplement a <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-6.svg">R1-6</a> series sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b20">Section 2B.20</a>) that is either on the center line, lane line, or median island, they should not be used on the same pavement marking line where the <a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-6/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R1-6.svg">R1-6</a> series sign is installed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6k/#6k04">Section 6K.04</a> contains information for temporary installations of tubular markers.</p>
</div>
