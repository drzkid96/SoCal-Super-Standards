<div class="srcbar">FHWA, Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices for Streets and Highways, 11th Edition with Revision 1, December 2025. Final rule effective March 5, 2026.</div>

This is the current national standard. In California, the legal standard is the [CA MUTCD 2026](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/), which adopted the original 11th Edition with California amendments. Caltrans has until March 2028 to adopt Revision 1.

Every section here shows:

- <span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> when the section sits on a page FHWA re-issued in Revision 1 (dated December 2025). Check those against the CA text.
- <span class="badge ca-amend">CA amended</span> when California's version of the section adds or changes text, or <span class="badge ca-same">CA MUTCD 2026</span> when it matches. Either badge links straight to the CA section.
