## 7D.01 Adult Crossing Guards


<div class="secmeta">Section 7D.01 &middot; manual page 985 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7d/#7d01">CA amended: 7D.01</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/7D.01/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Adult crossing guards may be used to provide gaps in traffic at school crossings where an engineering study has shown that adequate gaps need to be created, and where authorized by law.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Adult crossing guards can also add conspicuity at the crossing where children, who are typically smaller in stature, might not be as visible.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> High standards for selection of adult crossing guards are essential because they are responsible for the safety of and the efficient crossing of the street by schoolchildren within and in the immediate vicinity of school crosswalks.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Jurisdictions should have policies and procedures for the qualifications, selection, and training of adult crossing guards.</p>
</div>

## 7D.02 Operating Procedures for Adult Crossing Guards


<div class="secmeta">Section 7D.02 &middot; manual page 985 &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/7d/#7d02">CA amended: 7D.02</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/us/7D.02/">short link</a></div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> Law enforcement officers performing school crossing supervision and adult crossing guards shall wear high‑visibility retroreflective safety apparel labeled as ANSI 107‑2020 standard performance for Class 2, Type R, as described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6c/#6c05">Section 6C.05</a>.</p>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> Adult crossing guards shall not direct traffic in the usual law enforcement regulatory sense. In the control of traffic, they shall pick opportune times to create a sufficient gap in the traffic flow. At these times, they shall stand in the roadway to indicate that pedestrians are about to use or are using the crosswalk, and that all vehicular traffic must stop.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> Adult crossing guards shall use a STOP paddle. The STOP paddle shall be the primary hand‑signaling device.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> The STOP paddle shall comply with the provisions for a STOP/SLOW paddle (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/6d/#6d02">Section 6D.02</a>) except both sides shall be a STOP face.</p>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> The paddle shall be retroreflective or illuminated when used during hours of darkness.</p>
</div>
