## 4U.01 Application of In-Roadway Warning Lights


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-same" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4u/#4u01">CA MUTCD 2026: 4U.01</a></div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In-Roadway Warning Lights are special types of highway traffic signals installed in the roadway surface to warn road users that they are approaching a condition on or adjacent to the roadway that might not be readily apparent and might require the road users to reduce their speed and/or come to a stop. This includes situations warning of marked school crosswalks, marked midblock crosswalks, marked crosswalks on uncontrolled approaches, marked crosswalks in advance of roundabouts as described in <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3d/">Chapter 3D</a>, and other roadway situations involving pedestrian crossings.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> In-Roadway Warning Lights shall not be used for any application that is not described in this Chapter.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> When used, In-Roadway Warning Lights shall be flashed and shall not be steadily illuminated.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> Steadily illuminated lights installed in the roadway surface are considered to be internally illuminated raised pavement markers (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/3b/#3b14">Section 3B.14</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> In-Roadway Warning Lights may be flashed in a manner that includes a continuous flash of varying intensity and time duration that is repeated to provide a flickering effect (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4u/#4u02">Section 4U.02</a>).</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> If used, In-Roadway Warning Lights should not exceed a height of ¾ inch above the roadway surface.</p>
</div>

## 4U.02 In-Roadway Warning Lights at Crosswalks


<div class="secmeta"><span class="badge rev">Revision 1 page</span> &middot; <a class="badge ca-amend" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/4u/#4u02">CA amended: 4U.02</a></div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">01</span> In-Roadway Warning Lights may be installed at certain marked crosswalks, based on an engineering study or engineering judgment, to provide additional warning to road users.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">02</span> If used, In-Roadway Warning Lights at crosswalks shall be installed only at marked crosswalks with applicable warning signs. They shall not be used at crosswalks controlled by YIELD signs, STOP signs, traffic control signals, or pedestrian hybrid beacons.</p>
<p><span class="pn">03</span> If In-Roadway Warning Lights are used at a crosswalk, the following requirements shall apply: A. Except as provided in Paragraphs 7 and 8 of this Section, they shall be installed along both sides of the crosswalk and shall span its entire length. B. They shall initiate operation based on pedestrian actuation and shall cease operation at a predetermined time after the pedestrian actuation or, with passive detection, after the pedestrian clears the crosswalk. C. They shall display a flashing yellow light when actuated. The flash rate shall be at least 50, but not more than 60, flash periods per minute. If they are flashed in a manner that includes a continuous flash of varying intensity and time duration that is repeated to provide a flickering effect, the flickers or pulses shall not repeat at a rate that is between 5 and 30 per second to avoid frequencies that might cause seizures. D. They shall be installed in the area between the outside edge of the crosswalk line and 10 feet from the outside edge of the crosswalk. E. They shall face away from the crosswalk if unidirectional, or shall face away from and across the crosswalk if bidirectional.</p>
<p><span class="pn">04</span> If used on one-lane, one-way roadways, a minimum of two In-Roadway Warning Lights shall be installed on the approach side of the crosswalk. If used on two-lane roadways, a minimum of three In- Roadway Warning Lights shall be installed along both sides of the crosswalk. If used on roadways with more than two lanes, a minimum of one In-Roadway Warning Light per lane shall be installed along both sides of the crosswalk.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">05</span> If used, In-Roadway Warning Lights should be installed in the center of each travel lane, at the center line of the roadway, at each edge of the roadway or parking lanes, or at other suitable locations away from the normal tire track paths.</p>
<p><span class="pn">06</span> The location of the In-Roadway Warning Lights within the lanes should be based on engineering judgment.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov opt"><div class="prov-label">Option</div>
<p><span class="pn">07</span> On one-way streets, In-Roadway Warning Lights may be omitted on the departure side of the crosswalk.</p>
<p><span class="pn">08</span> Based on engineering judgment, the In-Roadway Warning Lights on the departure side of the crosswalk on the left-hand side of a median may be omitted.</p>
<p><span class="pn">09</span> Unidirectional In-Roadway Warning Lights installed at crosswalk locations may have an optional, additional yellow light indication in each unit that is visible to pedestrians in the crosswalk to indicate to pedestrians in the crosswalk that the In-Roadway Warning Lights are in fact flashing as they cross the street. These yellow lights may flash with and at the same flash rate as the light module in which each is installed.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">10</span> If used, the period of operation of the In-Roadway Warning Lights following each actuation should be sufficient to allow a pedestrian crossing in the crosswalk to leave the curb or shoulder and travel at a walking speed of 3.5 feet per second to at least the far side of the traveled way or to a median of sufficient width for pedestrians to wait. Where pedestrians who walk slower than 3.5 feet per second, or pedestrians who use wheelchairs, routinely use the crosswalk, a walking speed of less than 3.5 feet per second should be considered in determining the period of operation.</p>
<p><span class="pn">11</span> An audible information device should be used with In-Roadway Warning Lights to provide assistance for pedestrians with vision disabilities.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov std"><div class="prov-label">Standard</div>
<p><span class="pn">12</span> If pedestrian push buttons (rather than passive detection) are used to actuate the In-Roadway Warning Lights, a PUSH BUTTON FOR WARNING LIGHTS/WAIT FOR GAP IN TRAFFIC (<a class="sref" href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r10-25/" data-svg="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/R10-25.svg">R10-25</a>) sign (see <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/2b/#2b58">Section 2B.58</a>) shall be installed explaining the purpose and use of the pedestrian push button detector.</p>
<p><span class="pn">13</span> Where the period of operation is sufficient only for crossing from a curb or shoulder to a median of sufficient width for pedestrians to wait, median-mounted pedestrian actuators shall be provided.</p>
<p><span class="pn">14</span> If an audible information device is used in conjunction with In-Roadway Warning Lights, the audible information device shall not use vibrotactile indications or percussive indications.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov gui"><div class="prov-label">Guidance</div>
<p><span class="pn">15</span> If an audible information device is used in conjunction with In-Roadway Warning Lights, the audible message during the time that the lights are flashing should be a speech message that says, “Warning lights are flashing.” The audible message should be spoken twice.</p>
</div>
<div class="prov sup"><div class="prov-label">Support</div>
<p><span class="pn">16</span> <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4i/#4i05">Section 4I.05</a> contains further information about pedestrian push button detector location criteria and <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/mutcd/4k/">Chapter 4K</a> contains further information on push button locator tones.</p>
</div>
