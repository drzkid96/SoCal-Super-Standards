## Active West Hollywood Mobility &amp; Traffic Standards

| Policy / Standard | Requirement | Effective / Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Green Class II bike lanes (citywide)** | City Council unanimously approved installing **green-colored pavement on all Class II bike lanes** citywide (construction began Jan 2026 on Santa Monica Blvd and corridors) | Council Policy April 7, 2025; [CA MUTCD 9E.02](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/9e/) |
| **Protected bike lanes directive** | City Council directed staff to evaluate and include **Class IV protected bike lanes** on roadway capital projects | Council Directive Nov 2023; [Caltrans DIB 89-02](/SoCal-Super-Standards/bulletins/caltrans-dib-89-02/) |
| **Pavement restoration** | All roadway cuts must restore pavement per **City Standard Pavement Restoration Details** (Public Works Development Services) | [WeHo Pavement Restoration Details](https://www.weho.org/city-government/city-departments/public-works/engineering/development-services/pavement-restoration-details) |
| **Speed limits &amp; E&amp;TS** | Engineering &amp; Traffic Survey updated every **5 years** for streets &gt; 40 ft wide; local streets carry **25 mph** prima facie speed limit | CVC §§ 627, 22348–22413, 40802; [WeHo E&amp;TS](https://www.weho.org/city-government/city-departments/public-works/engineering/engineering-and-traffic-survey) |
| **Daylighting (AB 413)** | Statewide **20 ft** approach prohibition (15 ft with curb extension) at marked and unmarked crosswalks | CVC § 22500(n); [LADOT/CA Daylighting Guide](/SoCal-Super-Standards/ladot/curb-paint/) |
| **Pedestrian &amp; Bicycle Mobility Plan** | Guides all-ages-and-abilities Complete Network corridor projects (Willoughby, Vista/Gardner, Kings, Fountain Ave, Design District) | Adopted 2017; ongoing corridor concept plans |

## Neighborhood Traffic Management Program (NTMP) Toolbox

West Hollywood's [NTMP Traffic Safety Measures](https://www.weho.org/city-government/city-departments/public-works/engineering/neighborhood-traffic-management/traffic-safety-measures) standardizes six primary engineering treatments on residential/local streets, all conforming to CA MUTCD and state/county standards:

<table class="dwtab"><thead><tr><th>NTMP Device</th><th>Primary Objective</th><th>Matching Detail / Standard on This Site</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td><strong>Bulb-Out (Curb Extension)</strong></td><td>Shorten crossing distance, improve daylighting &amp; slow turning speeds</td><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/crosswalks/">Crosswalks Topic</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/fhwa/step-uncontrolled-crossings/">FHWA STEP Guide</a></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Marked Crosswalk</strong></td><td>Channelize pedestrian crossings at uncontrolled &amp; signalized intersections</td><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3c/">CA MUTCD Ch. 3C</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/caltrans/standard-plans-2026/">Caltrans A24F</a></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Pedestrian Refuge Island</strong></td><td>Two-stage crossing on wider multi-lane streets</td><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/fhwa/step-uncontrolled-crossings/">FHWA STEP</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/crosswalks/">Crosswalks</a></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Speed Cushions (Speed Lumps)</strong></td><td>Reduce residential vehicle speeds while allowing emergency-vehicle wheelbase passage</td><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/speed-and-calming/">Speed &amp; Calming</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/details/burbank/burbank-std-plans-bt-417/">Burbank BT-417</a></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Traffic Circle</strong></td><td>Slow intersection approaches &amp; reduce right-angle conflicts on local grids</td><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/topics/speed-and-calming/">Speed &amp; Calming</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/3d/">CA MUTCD Ch. 3D</a></td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Stop Sign</strong></td><td>Right-of-way assignment where CA MUTCD warrants are met (not as standalone speed control)</td><td><a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/signs/r1-1/">R1-1 STOP</a> &middot; <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/ca-mutcd/2b/">CA MUTCD 2B.04–2B.17</a></td></tr>
</tbody></table>
