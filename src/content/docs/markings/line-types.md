## Rules that apply to every detail

- **Line width:** 6 in for lane lines, edge lines and centerlines on State highways; at least 4 in on local agency roadways, where 6 in is an option. A wide line is at least twice the normal width. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3A.04/">CA MUTCD 3A.04</a>
- **Federal patterns, for comparison:**
  - broken lines of 10 ft segments and 30 ft gaps (or a similar ratio)
  - dotted lane lines of 3 ft and 9 ft
  - dotted extensions of 2 ft with 2 to 6 ft gaps

  California requires the patterns in Figures 3A-101(CA) to 3A-114(CA), shown above. <a href="/SoCal-Super-Standards/s/ca/3A.04/">CA MUTCD 3A.04 paragraphs 06, 07 and 07a</a>
- **Paint centerlines:** where the centerline material is paint, a 3 in black line is placed between the yellow lines on State highways, and may be placed on local streets. (Figure 3A-107(CA), Note 2)
