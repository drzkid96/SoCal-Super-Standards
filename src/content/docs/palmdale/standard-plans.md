The City of Palmdale Public Works Engineering Division maintains the **Section X Standard Drawings** book alongside its **Engineering Design Standards (Section 4 — Street Improvement Plans)** and **Traffic Control Requirements (Rev. 07-2025)**. Where Palmdale does not publish a city-specific sheet (including `S-3.1`/`S-3.2` curbs, which are superseded), the City defers to **APWA/SPPWC**, **LA County DPW**, and **Caltrans / CA MUTCD**.

:::tip[Governing Hierarchy & Local Practice]
**Governing hierarchy & key local rules:**
- **Traffic Control Plans (Rev. 07-2025):** A TCP signed and stamped by a registered Traffic Engineer is mandatory whenever work moves traffic across the centerline, reduces travel lanes, closes a sidewalk/bike lane, or affects a signalized intersection; notify Public Works Inspection **48 hours** prior to work.
- **Street geometry (`Sec. 4` & `S-1.2`):** Minimum centerline radius is **300 ft** on collectors (64 ft R/W) and **250 ft** on local streets; curb returns use the **plane method (`G-4`)**; standard pavement cross-fall is **1.5% (`S-2`)**.
- **Curb & gutter note:** Palmdale sheets `S-3.1` and `S-3.2` are stamped VOID — construct curb & gutter per **APWA Std. Plan 120-1**.
:::
